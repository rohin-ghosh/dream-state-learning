## 0. One screen

**Claim.** One lineage, existence proof: a child raised in reasoning-gym classrooms under parents teaching six moves in varied modes, whose sleep writes its own frequent thoughts and perceptions into two adapter blocks, learns the unseen compiler gym faster than the identical mechanism untaught, by ≥ +0.020 with interval excluding zero — a demonstration with internal controls, not a population claim [IDEAS 09-10 "one-lineage claim"].

**Mechanism.** Parents teach six moves in varied modes under rulebook v2 (§4) [IDEAS 09-11; SURVEY 3.3], the leak rule (CANON 2a) the hard shell; every thought and perception kept, sleep trains two child-text blocks — behaviour (rank 8), memory (rank 32, bare frames read by completion) [IDEAS 09-11]; gate as patience (§3); clones never wait (§2); final test = the unseen compiler gym (§5).

**Thesis (Rohin, 09-11 evening; `THESIS_PARENTING_AS_MECHANISM_MATCHING.md`).** Parenting teaches thought forms the write can store; the write regime is adopted from Physics-of-LLMs / TMEM, not this paper's contribution; the bridge experiment (child-authored vs synthetic renderings, same cues) is the first measure of the perception skill and precedes the lineage. Sleep/compile is infrastructure.

## 2. The write

**Eligible.** Child-authored thought, reflection and perception rows only; gym lines masked; parent text stripped and refused by the row scan (adopted recipe's `ACT:` line: C13); no training without a scan report [CANON §1; Codex fixes 2, 4]. Every row kept, no cap [IDEAS 09-10 late]; the polarity-tag arm (D15) runs in item 5 [Codex fix 6].

**Behaviour block — rank 8, attention projections.** Multi-scale sequences (three scales, a token budget per scale), neighbourhood packing by family and time [IDEAS 09-11; v7 §2.2]. Phase A cumulative from base for K = 3 sleeps; phase B incremental with use-and-recency replay only through P9 [IDEAS 09-10].

**Memory block — rank 32, MLP projections.** Bare declarative frames from the child's own perceptions and outcomes, every occurrence kept, each ending in its canonical sentence; no chat template or question form [IDEAS 09-11 "Retrieval by completion"; SEQ-033]. Retrieval is a taught move (SURVEY rule 25 v2); dose fixed by cell F.

**Serving and release.** The two deltas add; one adapter served; each block switchable (both / behaviour-only / memory-only / OFF). **Default = interim merge**: non-blocking coordinator plus the ≈ 4-minute engine restart at batch boundaries [v7 §2.4], labelled; hot-swap [Astra §3] only after the P6 fixtures pass. Release blockers on every write: canary and row scan (Codex re-checks [fix 3]). Rank growth by zero-padding only if P9 qualifies (C1) [IDEAS 09-11 early]; SVD initialisation only if write cost binds [IDEAS 09-10].

**Budget.** One writer cycle ≤ 1.25 GPU-h training at 1,107 tok/s [SEQ-028]: T_max = 5 M token-passes, 3.5 M behaviour + 1.5 M memory, frames packed against the short-item regime (389 tok/s, SEQ-028); newest round in full, the rest sampled. Cycle ≈ 1.25 train + 0.1 canary + 0.45 gate exam + 0.07 engine start ≈ 1.9 h [v7 §2.4] = T_round (§7).

## 3. The gate as patience

Per trained adapter [IDEAS 09-10]:
- **Brake** (never a score): canary fails, or median tokens per unsolved exam problem < 0.5 × frozen (≥ 6 paired observations; missing support = hold) → `REJECTED`.
- **Provisional activation** at the next batch boundary, re-examined at the next probe (never averaged); three consecutive confirmed strikes (score < F_g − tol_g) → roll back to the best committed adapter (reason code to parents).
- **Severe deficit** (≤ F_g − 5 tol_g; R4 601@800's 0.114, SEQ-015): committed like any adapter (ruling: three sleeps for everything); the hold is D6, off until P8.
- **Floor and tolerance**: F_g per CANON §1 [ruling 8 via v7 §3.3]; compiler tol 0.01 (v7 §3.4); reasoning gym max(quantum, 2 × paired SD, 0.02) after recalibration [Astra §3]. Rollback never touches the record (D7).
- **Build dependency.** None of this exists (`run_life_v2.py`: fixed score floor plus brevity ratio); the gate v7 state machine is on the 09-11/12 build list (Fable; cut line 09-13 06:00 UTC); fallback: birth under "gate v6", listed as a deviation.

## 4. Parents

**Room parents (Astra + Astra, two per gym).** Score-blind, gate-informed: redacted record, instruments as counts until 6b passes, then trends, gate reason codes, parental ledger. **Rulebook v2** [SURVEY 3.3; CONSTITUENTS §3; q8 §4–5; IDEAS 09-11]: six moves (decide, associate, goal, build, prune, perceive), one per nudge at the frontier, in varied **modes** — question the default from Stage 1, stated then asked in Stage 0; suggestion, demonstration, worked pattern (past or unscored problems only), recipe, created event, probe — mode-tagged and rotated [rule 31; tag built in 6a]. A recipe: one of at least two options, the parent's opinion, never required or verbatim twice, withheld while dependence rises [rule 24]. Every brief suggestion carries **four parts** (scope, varying confidence, reason, refutation condition) after a "write your plan first" line; the one-line `[PARENT]` slot is exempt [rules 28–29]; the parent closes the loop on its last suggestion, then asks the **weighing question** ("follow, test once, or decline — and why?"), never graded [rule 30; IDEAS 09-11 item 2]. **Calibration feedback** on stated confidences, incident-anchored: mode S, to A/B (D22, C12). Brief ≤ 10 lines, allocated per SURVEY rule 28, ending in a `[REHEARSAL]`-tagged restatement request, excluded from the echo count [IDEAS 09-10]. Every mode is judged parent-absent on a changed gym two sleeps later [rule 32]. **Content = the ten constituents of CONSTITUENTS §0** (wanting split into curiosity and appraisal, weighing into others and self: twelve teaching targets; appraisal: item 9). The six moves are taught from Stage 0 (Rohin's 09-11 ruling); content beyond them waits for item C (D21; Astra q8 §4 advisory).

**Central parent (Fable; Codex reviewer).** Asynchronous, never blocks a sleep [IDEAS 09-10]; asks "is this new intelligence or a script", proposes experiments, re-ranks curriculum [IDEAS 09-11 early]; writes only from a fresh allow-listed context [v7 §1.5.5].
