## Canonical topology sentence

> One reset, frozen, target-blind parent teaches one child process-level
> thinking through target-blind tasks that force thought into action; public
> outcomes admit or reject lessons. Parenting then ends and every parental or
> nursery artifact disappears. In a fresh deployment, the resulting child runs
> Think--Dream--Sleep with per-life LoRA writes and is compared with a regular
> strong frozen-parameter active-text agent under the same ordinary deployment
> affordances. Learning is judged from parent-absent lifetime curves.

Never describe independent statistical roots as a classroom, cohort,
population-learning mechanism, peer group, or multiple children interacting.
They are isolated reruns of one fixed parent-policy/child-architecture pair and
never share state or observations.

## Result insertion rule

The final paper is written from the immutable confirmation manifest, never
from logs or narrative notes. Insert, in fixed order:

1. `D` with its registered confidence interval and practical margin;
2. `W_P` only if `D` passes;
3. `L_terminal` only if `W_P` passes;
4. `C_public=P1-R0` only if the causal learning rungs pass; and
5. `T_R0`/local-plateau language only if the active-text certificate and
   registered plateau tests pass.

If a rung fails, use the strongest surviving lower-rung statement and report
the complete failure table. A positive `P1-R0` cannot rescue a failed `D`; a
positive `D` cannot rescue harmful parented writes; a higher entry checkpoint
cannot be described as learning ability.
