# Full-claim closure ladder v2

Date: 2026-09-11

Status: evidence synthesis and experiment ordering only. It authorizes no
implementation, model/tokenizer call, training, adapter/GPU use, parenting,
C11 execution, claim, release, or submission.

## Why this ladder exists

The end state is not a writer canary. It is a valid demonstration that one
agent converts its own action--outcome life into connected experiential
knowledge, traverses and expands it under goals, improves later action over an
increasing lifetime, and eventually exceeds a strong evolving text-memory
agent. The current synthetic and CompilerGym results establish useful
precursors and failure modes, but not that end state.

The ordered ladder prevents a narrow passing component from silently
substituting for the organism. Each rung has one estimand, one causal contrast,
and a stop rule. Downstream GPU work is justified only when its prerequisite
passes.

## Noncompensatory order

1. **E1 now.** It is the cheapest falsifier and costs at most 3 A40-hours.
   Its result decides whether the present writer deserves any more childhood or
   lifetime compute.
2. **E2 and E3 after an E1 pass.** Persistence and authentic source are
   separate causes. Do not let one stand in for the other.
3. **E0 certification and E4 can run while E2/E3 close.** Both are small and
   needed before a clean childhood.
4. **E6--E8 are the PCFL mechanism relay.** Connectedness, goal traversal and
   prospective expansion are three interventions, not three readings of one
   score.
5. **E5 and E10 are independent-root developmental experiments.** A fixed
   child cloned across environments is not multiple childhoods.
6. **E9 is CPU-first and parallel.** It must never delay the writer gate or
   inflate a transport result into a compression claim.

## GPU-hour policy

- Do not spend more GPU time on rank rescue, negative-frame exposure,
  additional legacy writer lives, or broad parenting until E1 resolves.
- A failing E1 is already a decisive scientific result: this writer installs
  broad habits but cannot carry the tested conditional policy. Switch the
  paper to prospective writer-failure science and redesign the writer before
  more lineage scale.
- A passing E1 triggers exactly one E2 write and one matched E3 source test,
  not a seven-day child.
- Only E1--E4 plus the active-text certificate permit a clean childhood.
- Full C11 custody is finished and enforced only when the paper-grade C11
  mechanism run is actually next. It is not a prerequisite for development
  scouts.

## Claim discipline

The highest passing rung controls the title, abstract and conclusion:

- E1: supervised conditional policy carriage.
- E2: repeated-write retention.
- E3: grounded child-experience compilation.
- E4/E5: bounded parenting persistence / parenting of learning.
- E6: connected experiential knowledge.
- E7: goal-conditioned traversal.
- E8: prospective expansion through action.
- E9: measured semantic or physical compression.
- E10: bounded continual improvement beyond a certified active-text plateau.

No lower rung implies a higher one. In particular, V7 is one gate in the
full programme, not the paper's desired end state.
