# R106 — branching definition and composition supplement

Directive received from Rohin through Fable, message 106, reported 2026-09-15
06:08 UTC; acted on by Main after receipt at approximately 06:33 UTC.

> BRANCHING does NOT mean multiple hypotheses or two worked methods under the same givens.
> It means the chain of thought leaves the main line for other forms and comes back.

The operational unit is an evidenced departure and return: side-observation,
check, judgment, aside, or what-if followed by resumption of the solution.
Count worked methods separately. A grounded counterfactual check can be a
branch without being another solution method. An incorrect check can still
have branching shape: report grounding, usefulness, coherence and errors
separately. Mere connective words, repeated arithmetic, or an ordinary next
step do not by themselves establish a departure. Return to the final answer
can close a branch. A side-path without resumption is separately incomplete.

This supersedes the **definition**, not the preserved source evidence, in
`orch_branching_method_correction_20260915_0556.md`. Its dismissal of checks
as non-branching was wrong under R106. Neither the old 9/12 author labels nor
the correction is a new validated R106 population rate. Previously published
method and rejection counts remain method and rejection counts; zero of
either must not be reported as zero R106 branches.
