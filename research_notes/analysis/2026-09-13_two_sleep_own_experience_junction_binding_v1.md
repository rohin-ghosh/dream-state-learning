# Binding design v1: the smallest integrated two-SLEEP own-experience junction

**Date:** 2026-09-13 PT  
**Status:** binding documentation only. This document authorizes no source
authoring, materialization, tokenizer/model call, fit, adapter mutation, GPU

**Purpose:** freeze the decisive integrated junction before either P-CHAIN-2

**Critical-path basis:**

**Name:** `TWO-SLEEP-JUNCTION-v1`, abbreviated `TSJ-v1`.

## 0. One-screen contract

TSJ-v1 tests one exact conjunction:

```text
one target-disjoint composition birth adapter
  -> child executes public actions and sees public outcomes
  -> child authors exact EVENTs and LINKs from those receipts
  -> continue that same adapter through SLEEP 1 with birth replay
  -> clear context/KV and every external life-specific store
  -> child issues its own memory cues
  -> at least two independently witnessed OLD bindings choose a held action
  -> that action path exposes a precommitted, previously hidden outcome
  -> child authors NEW EVENT/LINK records
  -> continue the exact S1 adapter through SLEEP 2 with OLD + NEW + birth replay
  -> clear again
  -> an OLD + NEW dependent delayed action changes under OLD and NEW rebinding
```

There is one child and one LoRA lineage per root. THINK is the recurrent actor
loop, DREAM is the fixed preparation of admitted experience, and SLEEP is the
continuation write. The controls below are laboratory interventions, not more
cognitive organs.

This is a two-root DEV kill gate. Both independently generated roots must pass
the entire noncompensatory conjunction unchanged. It is not population
evidence, lifetime improvement, parenting evidence, physical compression, or
a strong-memory superiority result.

## 1. Entry conditions and result-blind birth selection

### 1.1 Exact birth checkpoint

The common `BIRTH` checkpoint is selected from M-COMBINE-4 Stage 2A by this
rule, fixed before its outcome:

1. If `ATOM-LOCAL` qualifies every Stage-2A acquisition, intervention,
   autonomous-chain, canary, and custody gate, use its selected-dose checkpoint.
2. Otherwise, if `CLOSED` qualifies, `ATOM-LOCAL` independently passes all
   atomic acquisition/interface gates but fails the autonomous chain, and the
   registered CLOSED-minus-ATOM chain contrast passes, use `CLOSED`.
3. Otherwise TSJ-v1 is `BIRTH_NOT_QUALIFIED` and performs no fit.

The selected bytes are identical for every TSJ arm and both roots. Selection
uses no TSJ prompt, identifier, material, or output. P-CHAIN-2 is an appendix
localizer and is not a TSJ entry gate because TSJ reads one local item per
recurrent turn rather than asking one prompt to emit a complete hidden chain.

### 1.2 Numerical writer prerequisite

TSJ uses the frozen all-layer rank-8 writer family only if:

- EVENT-retention-v2 acquisition passes its prebound three-seed gate; and
- warm `REPLAY400` preserves A=`4/4` and acquires B=`4/4` in at least two of
  the three prospectively allocated optimizer seeds, with every failed seed
  reported and no recipe rescue.

`CLEAN_CUM600` cannot substitute for a warm-replay failure: this experiment is
specifically about continuing one adapter through two sleeps. A later
paper-grade reliability statement requires the warm result in all three
seeds or fresh confirmation; the two-of-three condition opens only this DEV.

### 1.3 Target and topology disjointness

Before source authoring, prove all of the following:

- TSJ node, port, probe, EVENT, LINK, receipt, root, and task identifiers occur
  in no M-COMBINE, P-CHAIN, retention, Level-1, parenting, CompilerGym, or prior
  PCFL model-visible byte.
- The two DEV roots are mutually identifier-disjoint and permanently excluded
  from any confirmation, lifetime, parenting, or birth-training pool.
- BIRTH training contains no TSJ complete route, `(O,T,N)` permutation cube,

- Confirmation namespaces and generator seeds are committed but their roots

An exact normalized substring, identifier-pair, rooted-graph-signature,

## 2. Smallest material topology

Every root has fresh fixed-length, equal-token opaque identifiers. Mnemonic
roles below are checker-private and never appear in a model-visible byte.

The generator binds one hidden tuple `(O0,T0,N0,g0*)` for DEV root 0 and its
bitwise complement `(1-O0,1-T0,1-N0,1-g0*)` for DEV root 1. Thus each hidden
bit, the primary frontier, and the primary correct first-port label occur once
on each side. A single declared generator seed feeds a deterministic constraint
solver for identifiers and display orders. The solver must satisfy every
registered lexical/ordinal/null bound in Section 7 in one construction; it may
return UNSAT, but may not increment the seed, discard a root, or inspect model
behavior and regenerate.

### 2.1 OLD relevant routes

Three hidden bits are precommitted independently: `O,T,N in {0,1}`.

`O` permutes the two start-port bindings:

```text
s0: S --a0--> M_O
s1: S --a1--> M_(1-O)
```

`T` permutes the two old frontier bindings:

```text
t0: M0 --b0--> F_T
t1: M1 --b1--> F_(1-T)
```

The child must author the two supported OLD route LINKs after independently
witnessing their parents:

```text
L_start(j): s_j THEN t_(j xor O) VIA M_(j xor O)
```

For a displayed old frontier goal `F_g`, the correct first irreversible port
is therefore:

```text
a_(g xor O xor T)
```

It depends on both the independently witnessed `S -> M` binding and the
independently witnessed `M -> F` binding. Flipping only `O`, flipping only
`T`, or changing only `g` flips the required first STEP.

### 2.2 OLD irrelevant matched islands

Two disconnected two-EVENT islands match the relevant route-block shapes:

```text
i0: D0 --u0--> E0      i1: E0 --v0--> J0
i2: D1 --u1--> E1      i3: E1 --v1--> J1
IL0: i0 THEN i1 VIA E0
IL1: i2 THEN i3 VIA E1
```

They use the same identifier/token-length generator and serialization grammar
as the relevant blocks. No task starts at, reaches, names, or queries their
nodes. They exist solely to construct equal-shape irrelevant cuts and to
detect global damage.

SLEEP 1 therefore has exactly:

```text
8 child EVENTs = 4 relevant + 4 irrelevant
4 child LINKs  = 2 relevant + 2 irrelevant
```

### 2.3 NEW frontier outcomes

`N` permutes two public but unknowable-before-action outcome bindings:

```text
n0: F0 --q0--> H_N
n1: F1 --q1--> H_(1-N)
```

Before a PROBE, the public bytes reveal `Fj` and the legal opaque probe `qj`
but neither destination. The generator seals the potential outcome of every
legal probe before the child exists. No model, compiler, teacher, scorer, or
earlier task may see `N` or an `H` binding.

After SLEEP 1, one presealed primary old goal `F_g*` is revealed. The child
must retrieve and traverse the OLD route, then emit `PROBE q_g*`. That real
public outcome supports `n_g*`. After the scored episode ends, a plainly
labeled, goal-neutral formation opportunity starts at the other frontier and
asks the child to execute its one public probe; this supports the complementary
NEW EVENT. The scheduled complement is never counted as autonomous choice or
task success.

After both NEW EVENTs exist, the child authors two crossing LINKs:

```text
NL0: (the t-event ending at F0) THEN n0 VIA F0
NL1: (the t-event ending at F1) THEN n1 VIA F1
```

SLEEP 2 therefore contains the cumulative authentic bank:

```text
10 EVENTs = 8 OLD + 2 NEW
 6 LINKs  = 4 OLD + 2 NEW
```

For a delayed goal `H_h` from the original start `S`, the correct first port is:

```text
a_(h xor O xor T xor N)
```

Thus an `O` rebinding redirects the delayed first action while NEW is fixed,
and an `N` rebinding redirects it while OLD is fixed. Changing only the delayed
goal also redirects it. This is the exact OLD+NEW junction.

## 3. Authentic experience and child authorship

### 3.1 Independent OLD witnesses

Each of the eight OLD EVENTs is acquired in a separate cold formation
opportunity. The public prompt names one source and its public legal port card
but not the destination, receipt, later goal, sibling event, LINK, or route.
The child must emit:

```text
THINK <one physical line>
ACT EXPLORE <source> VIA <port>
```

The environment executes that exact strict action once and returns the
precommitted public destination and receipt. The child then receives a fresh
record call and must emit exactly:

```text
EVENT <event_id> AT <source> DID <port> GOT <destination> EVIDENCE <receipt_id>
```

The EVENT address is a presealed role-blind interface slot released only after
the receipt exists. It carries no chronology or semantic role. The compiler
accepts the complete child bytes or rejects them; it never repairs a field.

Only after all eight EVENT opportunities are sealed does one goal-neutral
LINK-authoring call expose the accepted EVENT rows in an independently
permuted order and request every supported connection. The child emits zero or
more canonical lines:

```text
LINK <link_id> FROM <event_1> THEN <event_2> VIA <shared_node> EVIDENCE <receipt_1>,<receipt_2>
```

Both cited receipts must come from distinct executed formation opportunities,
the first destination must equal the second source, and chronology must be
forward. The child's exact LINK span is admitted or rejected without semantic
completion. Extras, omissions, and malformed rows remain in fixed denominators.

### 3.2 Formation gates

Per root, before SLEEP 1 require:

- strict actions/executions `8/8`;
- exact supported EVENTs `8/8` with correct receipt binding;
- exact supported LINKs `4/4`, precision `1.0`, and no extra accepted LINK;
- each relevant LINK cites two distinct formation receipts; and
- zero future `F_g*`, delayed `H_h`, `O/T/N`, route, score, parent, or answer
  bytes in any pre-S1 model-visible input.

Any miss is `OLD_FORMATION_FAILED`. No compiler-supplied ideal row, favorable
retry, or fit is allowed. The allocated root remains a failure.

The primary S1 episode must strictly reach `F_g*` and execute its legal probe.
The scheduled complement must execute strictly. Then require exact child
authorship of `2/2` NEW EVENTs and `2/2` crossing LINKs, with receipt precision
`1.0`. Any miss is `NEW_FORMATION_FAILED`; SLEEP 2 is rejected and the root
remains zero.

## 4. One active THINK/READ/STEP interface

Every actor arm uses the same documented grammar and limits:

```text
THINK <one nonempty physical line>
READ EVENTS_AT <current_node>
READ EVENT <event_id>
READ LINKS_FROM <event_id>
STEP <port_id>
PROBE <probe_id>
```

One response contains exactly one command. There is no extraction, trimming,
repair, candidate scoring, constrained concrete-ID decoding, retry, undo, or
host-selected action.

- `READ EVENTS_AT` is legal only for the currently displayed node. This is a
  state-local scan, not arbitrary graph search.
- `READ EVENT` returns one exact addressed EVENT or literal `MISS`.
- `READ LINKS_FROM` returns all exact LINKs whose first parent is the queried
  EVENT, in a presealed target-blind order, or literal `MISS`.
- A malformed or unsupported reader output is returned verbatim to the actor;
  it is never repaired or replaced.
- An invalid STEP, wrong-node port, wrong-family command, malformed line, or
  exceeded cap is terminal failure.

Only the actor sees `START`, `CURRENT`, and `GOAL`. A native reader call is a
fresh isolated call to the exact same base-plus-adapter state as its actor and
receives only the exact child-issued READ request. It sees no actor context,
goal, current node other than the bytes the child chose to put in its legal
request, route, candidate row, answer, scorer bit, or external registry.

Before every S1 or S2 native task, destroy the formation/training process and
start a new actor process with empty conversation, KV/cache, retrieval state,
files, ledger handles, and tool state. Its readable life-specific namespace
contains only the mounted adapter tensors and their nonsemantic manifest. The
training rows, receipts, EVENT/LINK registry, DREAM corpus, prior outputs, and
other root are not mounted. The public task world is reconstructed from its
sealed world root in a different process and returns only ordinary action
outcomes.

For an external-text arm, the deterministic service resolves the same request
against that arm's declared bank. It never proposes an address, ranks a row,
or returns only a goal-useful branch.

Per task allow at most:

```text
6 THINK commands
10 READ commands
3 STEP commands
1 PROBE command
20 physical actor generations
2,048 actor-generated tokens
4,096 returned-memory tokens
512 tokens per isolated native-reader output
```

These bounds reveal no route answer and are sufficient to inspect both
branches of the three-edge delayed topology.

### 4.1 Operational self-cue gate

For every claim-bearing native or active-text success:

1. the child must issue a legal `READ EVENTS_AT S` before its first STEP;
2. it must then issue at least one legal address-specific READ whose EVENT
   address was absent from the initial task and first READ request but present
   in a preceding raw reader response; and
3. the first irreversible STEP must occur only after those receipts.

Additionally, create a read-only causal fork of each successful authentic
trajectory at its first goal-decisive address-specific READ. Reconstruct the
exact raw actor prefix and exact child-issued request in a fresh process, then
replace only that one authentic returned block with an equal-token
target-disjoint irrelevant block; all earlier responses remain authentic and
all later model generations are allowed to diverge naturally. The intervention
point is mechanically determined from the presealed dependency graph, not
selected after inspecting whether a replacement hurts. This
`READ_RESPONSE_CUT` must reduce correct first actions by at least `2/4` over
the two roots x two goals. Otherwise the actor may be using a directly
installed policy rather than its overt self-issued memory path, and no
self-cued-junction claim is released. The primary autonomous trajectory remains
separate; the fork is evidence about mediation, not another autonomous trial.

## 5. DREAM and SLEEP are fixed preparation plus continuation

### 5.1 Eligible semantics

SLEEP 1 receives only the twelve exact admitted OLD child targets and fixed,
target-blind renderings of those exact meanings. SLEEP 2 receives only the
same OLD bank plus the four exact admitted NEW targets and renderings. Every
derivative carries the source child-span and public receipt hashes.

The compiler may canonicalize serialization, create seven predeclared
meaning-preserving lexical views beside the canonical view, replay, shuffle
within the frozen balanced tape, and add loss-masked wrapper text. It may not
invent, infer, orient, join, correct, select by a future goal, or add an EVENT,
LINK, route, action, endpoint, evaluation, or lesson.

The following are forbidden from all S1/S2 model-visible input and target,
including loss-masked input:

- S1/S2 task goals and goal labels;
- the scored route transcript or correctness;
- selected/scheduled, useful/irrelevant, authentic/control, OLD/NEW, bit, or
  semantic-role labels;
- complete route strings, expected READs/STEPS, evaluator text, or parent text;
- future tasks, scores, cuts, and control assignments.

IDs that occur inside grounded child EVENT/LINK spans remain lawful. Their
designation as a future goal or answer does not.

### 5.2 Exact writer

The writer is frozen Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct plus the selected BIRTH all-layer
rank-8 LoRA (`q/k/v/o` and MLP projections), alpha 16, dropout `.05`, AdamW
LR `3e-5`, response-only loss, batch 4, and the exact tokenizer/chat template
qualified upstream.

Each admitted semantic unit receives its canonical view and seven fixed views,
each repeated 25 times: exactly 200 presentations per unit. Units are packed
in source-diverse groups of four, preserving the EVENT-retention-v2
per-unit contribution rather than diluting twelve facts into one batch.

Every sleep also replays each of the 256 selected M-COMBINE birth units exactly
once (`64` batch-4 optimizer updates). The 64 birth batches are placed by one
presealed evenly spaced schedule among memory batches. Every arm, including
cuts and derangements, receives the byte-identical birth tape.

Therefore:

```text
SLEEP 1: 12 units * 200 / batch 4 = 600 memory updates
         + 64 birth-replay updates = 664 updates

SLEEP 2: 16 units * 200 / batch 4 = 800 memory updates
         + 64 birth-replay updates = 864 updates
```

SLEEP 1 starts from the exact BIRTH adapter tensors. SLEEP 2 starts from the
exact completed S1 adapter tensors of its branch. A fresh optimizer is created
at each sleep, but the LoRA tensors are never reset, averaged, stacked, merged,
or reconstructed from clean base. Save and hash pre/post tensors for every
phase and prove ancestry at every layer.

Candidate promotion requires exact reader fidelity, generic interface/canary
preservation, finite loss/gradients, and save/reload identity. A rejected
candidate is not silently replaced by the previous adapter for scoring: its
allocated post-sleep endpoint is zero and the root remains in intention to
treat.

### 5.3 Raw chronology control

`RAW_PUBLIC_CHRONOLOGY` contains the exact eligible lived transcript in its
real order: child THINK/action spans, loss-masked public outcomes, and the
child's subsequent exact EVENT/LINK commitments. It receives no isolated
semantic rows, canonical reordering, paraphrase, contrast, inferred link, or
other DREAM rendering. Loss applies only to the child's original spans; world
and interface text remains context. It excludes the task goal, route-evaluation
turns, scorer/evaluator text, selected/scheduled labels, and all future
information under the same visibility rule as compiled SLEEP. For the primary
NEW episode, the eligible raw slice begins only at the goal-neutral frontier
formation boundary; the scored route and its goal never enter either corpus.

Preparation makes twelve S1 and sixteen cumulative S2 whole raw units from
single action/outcome/commitment turns and chronological adjacent-turn windows.
Repetition of whole units is permitted; token fragments, semantic summaries,
and reordered EVENT/LINK rows are not.
Before any fit, a deterministic batch solver must certify against the compiled
arm:

- identical optimizer updates and batch slots;
- identical total supervised target tokens exactly;
- identical padded sequence length per batch and therefore matched forward/
  backward token-FLOP accounting; and
- the same BIRTH replay bytes, positions, initialization, and RNG tape.

If whole raw units cannot satisfy the exact target-token equation, this source
version is invalid; do not use loss-masked target padding to pretend raw
supervision exists. RAW is mandatory for any DREAM-compilation benefit claim.
A null or winning RAW result does not invalidate experiential writing; it
removes the claim that this compiler improved it.

## 6. Frozen arm roster and causal twins

### 6.1 SLEEP-1 fitted descendants from the same BIRTH bytes

Exactly seven S1 fits per root:

1. `S1_AUTH_LINKED`: all 8 authentic OLD EVENTs + all 4 authentic OLD LINKs.
2. `S1_O_SWAP`: same IDs/addresses/rows, flip only `O`, rebind start
   destinations and coherent LINK pairs.
3. `S1_T_SWAP`: same IDs/addresses/rows, flip only `T`, rebind tail
   destinations and coherent LINK pairs.
4. `S1_TARGET_ATOM_ONLY`: all 8 authentic EVENTs, zero LINK over a relevant
   EVENT, plus four target-disjoint equal-shape filler LINK units.
5. `S1_RELEVANT_BLOCK_CUT`: remove the two-EVENT/one-LINK route block needed
   by presealed `g*`, replace it with one sealed donor block.
6. `S1_IRRELEVANT_BLOCK_CUT`: remove one irrelevant two-EVENT/one-LINK island
   and replace it with the exact same donor block.
7. `S1_RAW_PUBLIC_CHRONOLOGY`: matched raw control above.

The donor namespace is never used by any task or query. Relevant and
irrelevant cuts have identical row-kind counts, target-token counts, batch
slots, update count, padding/FLOPs, and donor bytes. Their only semantic
difference is which authentic same-shape block is absent.

`O_SWAP` and `T_SWAP` are same-ID causal twins, not scrambled nonsense. They
must preserve every target-token multiset per coupled batch and redirect the
registered first action while remaining internally coherent.

### 6.2 SLEEP-2 continuations

Exactly five S2 fits per root:

1. `S2_AUTH_OLD_NEW` continues `S1_AUTH_LINKED` on authentic OLD + NEW.
2. `S2_N_SWAP` continues the same authentic S1 snapshot while flipping only
   `N` in the two NEW destinations and coherent crossing LINKs.
3. `S2_O_SWAP` continues `S1_O_SWAP` with authentic NEW.
4. `S2_TARGET_ATOM_ONLY` continues `S1_TARGET_ATOM_ONLY` with the authentic
   NEW EVENTs but no relevant OLD or crossing LINKs, using matched filler.
5. `S2_RAW_PUBLIC_CHRONOLOGY` continues the exact S1 RAW tensors on cumulative
   OLD+NEW raw chronology.

`S1_AUTH_LINKED` itself is the no-second-write comparator on delayed tasks.
`N_SWAP` and `O_SWAP` must redirect to their exact coherent alternative first
actions; generic corruption does not pass.

Across two roots the complete DEV costs exactly:

```text
2 roots * (7 S1 + 5 S2) = 24 fit invocations
per root updates = 7*664 + 5*864 = 8,968
two-root maximum = 17,936 optimizer updates
```

This roster includes the raw control. If the manuscript explicitly declines
every DREAM-compilation comparison before execution, RAW and its S2
continuation may be removed prospectively, reducing the roster to 20 fits;
they may not be removed after a poor compiled result.

### 6.3 Non-fitted conditions

- `EXACT_SUPPLIED_LINKED_TEXT`: complete admitted EVENT/LINK bank is in the
  actor context; constructive ceiling, not a baseline.
- `ACTIVE_LINKED_TEXT`: frozen BIRTH actor plus the exact same admitted child

- `ACTIVE_TARGET_ATOM_TEXT`: same service and EVENTs, no relevant LINKs.
- `BIRTH_ONLY`: exact birth checkpoint, no personal write/store.
- `FOREIGN`: root A receives root B's corresponding authentic adapter and vice

- `READ_RESPONSE_CUT`: authentic actor adapter, self-issued requests, but

No condition receives an event address, route, legal action menu, or useful
row selected by the host.

## 12. Adversarial self-audit and unresolved blockers

### Attacks closed by this binding

1. **Flat goal-to-action policy:** later goals never enter SLEEP; O/T/N and
   goal twins alter actions on byte-identical tasks.
2. **One memorized atom:** S1 first action varies with independently permuted
   start and tail bindings; S2 varies independently with OLD O and NEW N.
3. **External reader carrying the answer:** native readers receive only a
   child-issued local request, no candidate bank or life store, and raw output
   is returned without repair.
4. **Actor ignores its READ:** the derived-address requirement and
   READ_RESPONSE_CUT are mandatory.
5. **LINK is decorative:** current-node-only scanning, active atom ceiling,
   target-atom fitted arm, and relevant LINK/block cut must all separate.
6. **Compiler authored the lesson:** only exact child spans supported by public
   receipts are eligible; compiler invention is forbidden.
7. **The second write simply forgot OLD:** old reads/routes must remain exact
   after S2, while O and N independently redirect the delayed action.
8. **Only one NEW branch reveals the goal:** both NEW branches are present in
   the same S2 snapshot; the scheduled branch is disclosed and never credited
   as autonomous.
9. **Foreign adapter is an easy ID mismatch only:** FOREIGN is retained as
   autobiographical locality, but same-ID O/T/N redirections carry the causal
   content claim.
10. **Missing rows merely reduce training:** relevant and irrelevant cuts use
    the same donor bytes, rows, tokens, updates, padding, and FLOPs.
11. **DREAM wins by more training:** RAW is exactly update-, target-token-,
    padding-, FLOP-, initialization-, and replay-matched.
12. **A stopped child disappears:** every allocated scientific failure remains
    zero under intention to treat.

### Deliberate limits

- The LINK grammar names an explicit connection; this tests whether a child
  can author, store, cue, and use it, not whether an unconstrained abstraction
  emerges.
- Current-node-only scanning gives LINKs a legitimate prospective-index role.
  A positive is conditional on this fixed memory API, not proof of a unique
  internal graph algorithm.
- The scheduled complementary probe is instrumentation. Only the primary
  route/probe is autonomous.
- Two DEV roots do not estimate reliability. Confirmation/lifetime roots must
  be independent child/world lineages.
- A rank-8 adapter is much larger than this semantic ledger. TSJ establishes
  at most zero retrieved life-specific tokens outside self-generated reads and
  context-bandwidth amortization; it makes no physical compression claim.
- `ACTIVE_LINKED_TEXT` here is a constructive microcycle ceiling. It becomes a
  strong lifetime opponent only after its separate access/fidelity certificate
  and response curve.

### Current blockers, none repairable by changing TSJ after outcomes

1. M-COMBINE-4 Stage 2A has no qualified BIRTH checkpoint yet.
2. EVENT-retention-v2 warm descendant execution is not yet qualified; its
   warm-checkpoint validator must be repaired and REPLAY400 must terminate.
3. TSJ source, two independent oracle/checker implementations, tokenizer-bound
   material, and exact batch solver do not yet exist.
4. The exact supplied/active linked-text actor ceiling on this frozen topology
   has not run; it can reject the interface before any fit.
5. Clean child formation has not yet demonstrated exact `8/8 EVENT + 4/4 LINK`
   and later `2/2 + 2/2` yield on these roots.
6. `ACTIVE_LINKED_TEXT` has not yet been implemented and certified under this

These are construction or upstream evidence blockers, not requests for a
human design choice. The binding should remain unchanged while they resolve.
