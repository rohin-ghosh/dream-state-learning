## Bound definitions

1. `actual_route_depth` is total physically executed STEP count on the complete
   designated effective START-to-GOAL path, including an initial mismatching
   STEP. Complete an unresolved birth prefix through its registered directory,
   uniquely matching relation and effective world transition, for evaluator
   metadata only. Never append that completion to a training target or actor
   prefix. Derive the count by replay, not descriptor arithmetic. Reject cycles,
   missing service/transition, ambiguous matches, or uncompleted paths rather
   than claiming a depth. No arbitrary depth cutoff is a substitute for proof
   of completion. The source-derived expected birth depths are 1/2 for
   reached/unresolved ordinary, MISS and irrelevant cases; 2/3 for mismatch
   cases. Held complete chains retain depth 2. Preserve separately the existing
   semantic route-depth matching: the failed prelude increases physical depth,
   not the productive suffix-depth matching factor.
2. For mismatch recovery SEEK, relevant candidate position is the rendered
   index of the implicated EVENT whose RECOVER operand is the target query.
   Derive it from the authentic preceding EVENTS response and failed STEP,
   and verify RECOVER ownership. Do not add a directory to ATOM or copy the
   original directory's unrelated position. Existing non-mismatch positions
   and nullability remain unchanged.
3. `predicted_actual_match` uses only the latest STEP outcome retained in the
   exact arm prefix, comparing the retained owning EVENT's GOT to the observed
   WORLD outcome. It is null if no STEP outcome is retained. In particular,
   task-only ATOM CONTINUE and corrective ATOM PROSPECT have null, even when
   original CLOSED history contains a STEP. Missing ownership for a retained
   STEP is an error, not an invented prediction. Private original trace history
   must never add public graph edges or create an ATOM observation.
