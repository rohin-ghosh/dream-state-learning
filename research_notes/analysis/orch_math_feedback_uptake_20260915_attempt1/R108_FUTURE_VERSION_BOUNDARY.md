## Prospective intervention record

Next separately frozen versions use the intervention classes requested by R108:
perception, persistence, metacognition, curiosity, goal and meta-goal regulation,
reflection, action steering, and affective-value. These are descriptive axes,
not forced branch counts, quotas, answer templates, or synthetic SFT targets.
Parents respond to sourced difficulties/failures and reasoning behavior, not by
supplying the task answer. Affective/value language targets the task process,
not personal worth or pressure to continue indefinitely.

Keep every original attempt, including negative outcomes, truthfully tagged.
Interventions do not relabel wrong attempts as correct. Parent-visible inputs
remain limited to authorized TRAIN history and feedback; held scores, sealed
answers, evaluator instructions, and future tests stay inaccessible.

Each episode preserves a child-state / parent-intervention / subsequent-behavior
triple with these bindings:

- Child state: lineage/epoch, actual mounted base and adapter/optimizer identity
  or explicit absence, cycle/episode/source ID, original trace and context hashes,
  outcome provenance, caps, tokens, process identity, and exact timestamps.
- Parent intervention: provider's actual model identity, request/response/plan
  hashes, declared intervention classes, authorized visible-source manifest,
  raw node-local transcript, and queue/provider/delivery timestamps.
- Subsequent behavior: exact child check/revision source hashes, individual
  prompt/token/ending metadata, subsequent-cycle source links, and reviewer
  observations with counterevidence. Linkage alone is not a causal-effect claim.

All raw traces/transcripts stay node-local with verified hashes; repository
publication is limited to code, tests, manifests, compact tables and reductions.
No teacher lesson words become training targets. Any future learning objective
must retain the existing source/mask contract and be described accurately;
outcome-conditioned SFT is not negative-gradient or unlikelihood training.
