## Agreement and the important distinction

Yes: choose the developmental behavior by asking what later activity needs,
and what the actual writer can acquire from the resulting experience. The
learner should not have to emit a training-perfect document during every
action. But richer text and more repetition are not themselves evidence of
better learnability. A useful compiler makes the connection measurable.

Separate two products from an episode:

1. **Experience records:** what was observed, attempted and actually happened.
   A failed action can yield an excellent factual record. Preserve its failure
   status; do not teach the failed action as an unqualified successful policy.
2. **Behavior examples:** a model's actual decisions under their actual public
   evidence, selected for verified utility. Recoveries and useful subsegments
   may contribute even when a full episode fails, but credit assignment must
   be explicit rather than labeling every token in a lucky success as correct.

Our strict full-route collector selects only the second product, narrowly.
Zero selected routes does NOT imply zero usable experience. Preserve that
original result and selector; a new compiler can prospectively extract factual
records or independently verified recovery decisions without changing it.

## Minimal compiler boundary

- **Select with provenance:** retain observed outcomes, including failures;
  separately tag candidates for successful decision imitation or contrasts.
- **Render without inventing meaning:** an address-only question can supervise
  the exact sourced answer. Keep the answer out of the input when testing
  recall. Deduplicate identifiers and track parent/child/environment origin.
- **Preserve child-authored connections:** atomizing and formatting a connection
  is not permission to invent a new LINK or infer an unseen transition. Any
  semantic extraction or new relation needed for the authentic loop must be
  produced by the learner from available experience, then validated. A helper
  that does this reasoning instead is an explicitly exogenous DEV treatment.
- **Rehearse:** schedule selected new material and retained old material under
  measured budgets. Different query wrappers are different presentations, not
  additional independent experiences or permission to embellish the event.

Public task and environment observations remain in the student's masked
context. Elicitation guidance is removed entirely. Removing environment context
along with teacher guidance would destroy the evidence-conditioned behavior
we are trying to teach. Loss masking alone does not remove teacher information.

## What counts as good data

Count sourced, nonduplicate facts; usable decisions and recoveries; provenance
coverage; and actually supervised tokens as diagnostics. Do not reward mere
verbosity or the number of fabricated connections. The decisive tests remain:
after writing and removing helpers, is the fact recalled, is a relevant action
improved, and is older learning retained compared with no-write/matched controls?
Report generation, compilation and update cost separately.

The next minimal causal test should use one new observed event, compile a
query-to-observed-answer example, write with the existing tested recipe plus
old-memory replay, reload, and test both recall and a decision that requires
that fact in a new episode. A matched no-write clone separates prompt ability
from the effect of sleep. No requirement to solve every two-hop controller
panel first; those panels are separate diagnostics, not this definition.

## Corrections to the present interpretation

PCHAIN's failed copy arm presented **each atomic relation 40 times**, not eight:
1,280 atomic presentations / 32 relations. The 192 identifiers include other
roles and domains, not 192 independently trained facts. Its context contained
the answer; recall omitted it. Format and dose are unresolved alternatives.
SEQ-194/195 supports a successful recipe at its tested four-record scale; it
does not establish a universal 200-presentation law. A fixed-dose query-only
precursor isolates the format change before deciding whether more dose is
needed. Neither low training loss nor a supplied-fact ceiling proves memory.

## Literature pointers: inherited checks, not a fresh primary-source pass

**TMEM**, *Scaling Self-Evolving Agents via Parametric Memory*, arXiv:2606.04536,
as summarized in `research_notes/16_tmem_deepread.md`: distills current experience
into grounded QA supervision for online LoRA and evaluates later behavior with
adapted weights. This inherited reading motivates testing a query-aligned writer,
not assuming our parameters or lifetime work. I have not freshly verified an
ablation here and do not use one as evidence for the present experiment.
I have not verified the watcher's blanket claim that every episode resets its
state and do not use that as our novelty boundary.

**SEAL**, *Self-Adapting Language Models*, arXiv:2506.10943v2, sections 3, 5 and
appendix B, as checked in the existing
`research_notes/analysis/2026-09-13_seal_claim_boundary_primary_check.md`:
reinforces generated self-edits using downstream adaptation utility, with
filtered SFT/ReST-EM. It also measures sequential-edit forgetting. That is
closer to judging a compiler by what its outputs teach than by polished prose;
it does not establish our parent-free lifetime retention.

**Mattar and Daw (2018)**, *Prioritized memory access explains planning and
hippocampal replay*, DOI:10.1038/s41593-018-0232-z, is a primary-source lead for
the replay-prioritization analogy, not a freshly verified source in this note.
No claim that a brain has our literal compiler or that one deterministic
schedule reproduces biology is supported by this comparison.
