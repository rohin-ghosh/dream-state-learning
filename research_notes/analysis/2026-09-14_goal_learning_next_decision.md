# Next decision after the fixed goal-learning comparison

Written while both 400-update fits run, before any AFTER outcome. This is a
decision map, not a second experiment authorization packet or a result claim.
The current protocol and all its denominators remain unchanged.

## What is being learned

The child already knows a READ/ROUTE habit and sometimes chooses between
opposite goals correctly. SEQ255's unchanged-child baseline solves 2/4 paired
cases across two new identifier worlds. New teaching expands actual successful
experience across worlds and both display orders. The student targets are the
child's captured commands, supervised with parent instructions masked away;
this is not an autonomous curriculum or a compiler that invents new memories.

The running control retains all old trajectory targets and masks only the 48
new targets. A difference therefore estimates the incremental contribution of
these targets under this recipe. It is not the lifetime effect of parenting.
Loss scaling matches the full-reference denominator, not active-token budgets
or achieved retention. Compare those outcomes explicitly.

## Decision order

1. Finish both fixed fits and fresh readouts. No score-based early stopping,
   extra dose, case deletion, or best-checkpoint selection.
2. Compare TRAIN acquisition, PROBE paired success, UNAVAILABLE, and retention.
   FULL success with a control tie is shared improvement, not target superiority.
   Good TRAIN and weak PROBE means limited transfer; failed TRAIN means this
   teaching/write recipe has not established acquisition. Changed aggregate
   retention counts must be resolved by case identity before causal claims.
3. If the declared engineering target passes, preserve the resulting actor and
   test one fresh parent-free memory-write/action continuation. Use new adult
   identifiers, not either scored PROBE world as training material. Source
   records must be the child's actual responses to experienced outcomes.
   Record the pre-write text/parametric behavior, one finite write, fresh reload,
   and post-write text/parametric/no-memory behavior plus retention. Keeping
   text behavior while improving parametric access separates acquisition from
   a new routing-policy failure. Opposite-goal pairs remain necessary: a
   goal-blind first-port baseline cannot pass them.
4. If the target fails, close this declared recipe and inspect the actual
   failure location before selecting a successor. More goal/order coverage,
   explicit child-produced evidence-use trajectories, and richer contextual
   demonstrations are alternatives, not a preapproved hyperparameter sweep.
   The current data do not establish that a new planning architecture is needed.

## Relation to the thesis

These tests connect experiential data, persistent writing, and memory-using
action. They do not yet show a learner that independently selects better data,
changes its own future learning policy, or improves autonomous learning rate.
A successful continuation is the next useful small loop, not the full H2 claim.
The eventual comparison must keep parent removal, frozen-base/LoRA-only
learning, replay, provenance and child-state isolation intact. Replications and
larger breadth follow a usable connected recipe, consistent with message63;
the present one-lineage case counts stay descriptive.
