# Fresh audit: does the proposed Level-1 birth teach learning, or only habits?

**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Scope:** read-only audit of Rohin message 23 (and the clarifying message 24),
`astra_level1_fading_sequence_design_20260912.md`, and the accepted Level-0
writer evidence through SEQ-118. No builder source, run, model, GPU, or result
was changed or inspected.

## Verdict

**The fading-sequence design is useful Level-0 writer evidence, but it is not a
Level-1 birth design and cannot test a learned learning disposition.** It begins
from an adapter already taught one explicit convention, gives it four blocks of
authored `ACT: sum / COMPUTED: sum` targets, and asks whether the old
`PREDICT`-before-`ACT` convention survives. Its own interpretation correctly
limits the result to competing-objective habit preservation/replacement. The
deferred long-sequence variant would change how repeated demonstrations are
packaged; it still would not ask the child to decide *whether*, *how*, or *how
long* to think from its state.

This is now supported, rather than merely semantic, by the completed Level-0
line. Authored training can install and replace an output convention; distinct
source interleaving plus varied views can carry facts and an old task together;
and SEQ-118 shows replay can preserve three successive 16-fact banks at one
seed. Those are the ingredients for a birth write. None shows that the adapter
has acquired the disposition to turn new feedback into better subsequent
learning.

Rohin message 23 changes the estimand. Level 1 is a high-plasticity, authored
birth that supplies the initial flywheel; its efficacy is tested by a *small
sample of Level 2*: give the born child new teaching and experience, then ask
whether it learns more usefully than an otherwise matched child. Message 24
makes the connection explicit. Direct adherence at birth is necessary, but the
Level-2 learning delta is what separates an installed learning disposition from
several installed routines.

## What would merely install fixed habits

An interleaved corpus containing `PREDICT`, `INPUT`, fact recall, `PROSPECT`, and
`REVISE` can still be five lookup tables or response templates. The model can
pass by always emitting reflective language, always taking more steps, or
memorizing a finite condition-to-label map. The earlier conditional attempt
demonstrated both hazards: REVISE had a public-identifier shortcut, and the
adapters carried their supplied maps while damaging unrelated addition/copy
behavior. More metacognitive text, more repetition, or a longer context does not
by itself repair this.

The smallest defensible Level-1 target is therefore one **selective learning
policy**, expressed across several task skins:

> When evidence is insufficient but an informative check is available, state
> the live uncertainty, predict, and choose the check; when evidence is already
> sufficient, commit and stop; after a genuine surprise, revise the implicated
> belief rather than repeating the same routine.

This retains Rohin's broader birth vision while making the first assay
falsifiable. “Think more” is not the target. *Allocate thought and action from
the information state* is.

## Minimum birth controls before scaling

Use one predeclared development root first. Do not call the candidate corpus
“perfect” until it passes; that is the design target, not a measured property.

1. **Common clean birth.** Every arm starts from the same authenticated frozen
   Qwen base, empty personal context, and no prior task/gym exposure. Preserve a
   source/evaluation exposure registry and an immutable pre-birth receipt.
2. **Authentic birth versus active routine control and OFF.** The authentic
   corpus contains fully crossed information states and the appropriate
   check/commit/revise continuations. The active control must match tasks,
   answers/facts, response inventory, native target-token mass, update schedule,
   and style, while flattening or coherently permuting the state-to-cognitive-
   action relation. OFF establishes the base response. The already-demonstrated
   `PREDICT` habit is a writer positive control, not the primary Level-1 arm.
3. **Contrasts, not slogans.** Every taught operation needs paired cases in
   which it should and should not occur: uncertain/resolved, surprising/expected,
   informative/uninformative action, continue/stop. Cross all causal factors
   within opaque public instances; a projection that omits the deciding evidence
   must score at chance. No ID, template, label frequency, or target length may
   reveal the answer.
4. **Known-good write organization.** Mix distinct sources within updates and
   retain varied views and replay; SEQ-113/116/118 justify these as current
   engineering defaults. Fix rank, learning rate, views, replay ratio, and dose
   before the Level-2 sample. Do not select them from downstream parenting
   outcomes.
5. **Locality/non-harm.** Reuse strict native action, arithmetic, copy, and tag-
   spill panels. A birth that learns the right labels but loses ordinary action
   or emits training tags has failed, as SEQ-108 did.

The direct birth gate must count both sides separately: correct check/revise on
the positive cases **and correct omission/commit on the negative cases**, on
held wording and at least one held task skin. Aggregate “reflection rate” cannot
compensate for indiscriminate reflection. Also report exact behavior, semantic
behavior, task correctness, invalidity, spill, and complete raw outputs.

## Minimum Level-2 sample: the actual disposition test

After the direct gate, fork each sealed birth arm into a tiny, fixed curriculum
from a task family absent from the birth corpus. Give authentic-birth and
routine-control children the same task opportunities, world feedback, parent
policy, turn/token budget, sleep compiler, and write dose. The parent may point
to process but must not disclose hidden answers; all differences in observed
evidence and advice are logged rather than silently forced equal.

Measure three times: before Level-2 teaching, during the learning episodes, and
after one qualified write with the parent, lesson text, active context, and
external memory removed.

The minimum primary readouts are:

1. **Prospective learning behavior:** on fresh uncertain states, does the child
   choose an informative action, make a falsifiable prediction, and change the
   implicated belief after feedback? Score correct omission on resolved/control
   states too.
2. **Task learning curve:** improvement from early to late encounters with new
   rules, keeping missing/invalid actions as failures. Eloquence and restatement
   are not substitutes.
3. **Grounded self-authorship:** proportion of committed child records whose
   condition, action, prediction, outcome, and revision are supported by the
   actual event; plus omission on non-informative events. This is a mediator,
   not the headline outcome.
4. **Parent/context-free persistence:** after the write, performance on fresh
   cases versus that arm's sealed pre-Level-2 checkpoint. The causal quantity is
   the root-level difference in learning gains:

   `[(authentic post - authentic pre) - (routine post - routine pre)]`.

For a claim that SLEEP caused persistence, add promotion versus shadow within
both birth arms: every branch compiles and trains, but shadow preserves the
pre-write mounted adapter. Without this split, the small Level-2 sample can test
whether birth improves learning, but not isolate the contribution of promoting
the new LoRA.

## Scale decision

One root is feasibility only. Expand to independent birth roots only if:

- authentic birth passes both sides of conditional selectivity on held skins;
- it preserves task/action/interface behavior;
- its Level-2 sample improves either the task-learning curve or grounded
  prospective behavior over the active routine control; and
- the context-cleared result moves in the same direction, with no hidden parent
  or evaluation exposure.

A failure of direct selectivity means repair the birth material. Direct
selectivity with no Level-2 advantage means the corpus installed a policy but
did not yet bootstrap learning; increasing the number of birth slogans is not a
licensed rescue. Only a favorable Level-2 learning delta licenses GPU scaling
and a broader core curriculum.

## Bottom line

Keep the fading/repetition work as Level-0 evidence about plasticity,
interference, and replay. Build Level 1 as a conditional birth curriculum, but
judge it by what happens when the born child receives *new* evidence and
teaching. That is the minimum experiment that can distinguish “the LoRA learned
several routines” from “the LoRA learned how to begin learning.”
