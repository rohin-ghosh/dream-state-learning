## Three separate objects

1. **Complete shared custody.** Retain complete task, construction/registry/
   services/effective edges, original trace/facts/all four targets, role bindings,
   paired mutation, allocation/master provenance and route basis once per
   case/pair. Content hashes address retained objects; they never replace their
   values. Reconstruct independently and verify source counts and pins. Do not
   re-encode/atomize this entire object for every boundary.
2. **Small decision boundary.** Bind shared source identities/hashes; unit, arm,
   phase and decision; exact retained indices; exact public projection/messages/
   spans and evidence; target bytes/hash/parsed action; START/GOAL/latest CURRENT;
   implicated query/event and contradiction; references and hashes for canonical
   disclosed/future lists; core/public-graph/route-basis hashes. At native
   preparation also bind the actual template/tokenization receipt. The checker
   reconstructs public bytes independently from shared custody and requires
   exact equality—not just endpoint or supplied hash agreement.
3. **Typed finite leak basis.** Derive exact next action/operand, complete
   undisclosed future IDs, full private categorical values and full private
   role/case/unit keys, six forbidden cross-system labels plus forbidden
   LINK/OLD/NEW graph-edge labels, and the unchanged finite effective-route
   automaton. Keep source paths/ownership for every item. Do not generate
   needles from pointers, structural field names, array indices, standalone
   numbers, booleans/null, tagged pointer forms, or opaque-ID substrings/atoms.

Private categorical/key inventories must be source-derived and explicit, not
caller-declared allowlists or selected after scan outcomes. Full keys are not
their slash-separated fragments. Exact identifiers are not suffixes. An
unhandled source category must be diagnosed, not silently omitted.
