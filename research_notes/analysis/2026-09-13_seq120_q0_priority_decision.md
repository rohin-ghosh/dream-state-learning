# SEQ-120 aftermath: Q0 remains the deadline-critical selective-writer test

**Date:** 2026-09-12 PDT / 2026-09-13 UTC

**Evidence cut:** repository `247141aa`, including the complete SEQ-120 result.

**Scope:** decision audit only. No builder source, adapter, job, GPU process,
threshold, experiment, or claim was changed.

## Decision

**Implement and execute the already closed pairwise Q0 before the September 15
paper freeze. Do not substitute either an unchanged replication or a repaired
version of the birth component.**

SEQ-120 makes Q0 more informative, not redundant. It shows that the current
full-response birth recipe can install strong complementary conditional
responses without the earlier tag/copy collapse, while still failing the
predeclared paired-change and addition conjunction. Q0 isolates whether the
remaining bottleneck is the selective decision writer rather than a mixture of
full-response acquisition, reasoning, formatting, and unrelated anchors.

The Q0 contract was closed before SEQ-120. Its root, objective, schedule,
thresholds, controls, and classifier must remain unchanged by this result.
Prioritization may use SEQ-120; Q0 design choices may not.

## Why birth cannot substitute without weakening the paper

SEQ-120 is a valid exploratory component result, but not the paper's missing
necessary-condition result:

- it is one source-authored root/seed, explicitly declared exploratory;
- both trained maps fail the frozen conjunction: AUTH revision twins are
  `26/32`, DERANGED `24/32`, versus `29/32`; DERANGED REVISE is `56/64`
  versus `58/64`; and both addition panels are `15/16` versus `16/16`;
- it has no exact-training-form panel, so residual error cannot be localized
  to storage versus held-form extraction;
- full-response target training does not isolate the natural-prefix branch
  choice, absolute legal-action mass, or the first-step XOR tangent;
- its locality surface is only addition/copy compliance and tag spill, not
  Q0's missing-mode, unsupported-mode, neighboring-ID, wrong-root, itemwise
  `q`, absolute legal-mass `M`, action-identity, and complementary-map gates;
- OFF hits the 64-token cap on `96/128` calls, so its zero strict answers are
  not a clean base-competence result; and
- one root cannot replace the minimum memo's three independent root/seed
  units. Prompt and twin rows are repeated measurements, not replications.

An unchanged three-root birth replay could estimate the reproducibility of
this *multitarget birth recipe's* terminal failure, but it would still not
classify the selective natural-action writer cleanly. A repaired birth chosen
after `26/32`, `24/32`, and `15/16` were observed is development. It cannot
count SEQ-120 as confirmation, and it would need a frozen repaired recipe plus
three new failure-inclusive roots.

For a birth assay to substitute without weakening the selective-writer claim,
it would have to import Q0's target-independent common prefix, pair-balanced
quartets, complementary fits, exact/held separation, absolute branch-mass and
generation readout, full locality panels, immutable reducer, and three-root
conjunction. That is Q0 embedded inside a noisier multivariate birth fit, not a
cheaper alternative.

## Information and calendar choice

Q0 costs at most `0.75` A40-hour per root, or `2.25` A40-hours for the required
three-root classification. SEQ-120's fit-plus-readout interval was about
`0.40` A40-hour for one root; repeating it three times would save roughly one
GPU-hour but answer the less identifiable question above. GPU time is not the
bottleneck; implementation and paper calendar are.

The highest-information schedule is therefore:

1. Implement only the closed Q0 executor and focused tests. The required files
   are absent at this evidence cut. Do not use any SEQ-120 adapter, output,
   failure location, template, seed, or threshold to choose its bytes.
2. Require one valid, immutable, replayable Q0 terminal by **September 13,
   23:59 PDT**. A valid scientific failure counts as a terminal; an integrity
   abort does not.
3. Freeze the complete recipe after that first valid terminal and run exactly
   two fresh opaque-root/optimizer-seed repetitions, retaining every failure.
   Require all three raw-audited terminals by **September 15, 12:00 PDT**.
4. Use the registered failure-inclusive subtype vector. No favorable root,
   checkpoint, retry, replacement seed, threshold change, or majority vote.
5. Treat additional birth repair/replication and the running born-parenting
   participation sample as research-program work, not deadline-paper evidence.
   They must not delay or substitute for Q0.

If Q0 cannot meet the dates, take the minimum memo's ICLR no-go branch. Do not
rescue the submission by changing `29/32` to the observed `26/32` or `24/32`,
changing addition `16/16` to `15/16`, relabeling DERANGED `56/64` as a pass, or
calling a post-result birth repair confirmation. The honest surviving statement
is that supplied storage and rehearsed coexistence are feasible while selective,
cumulative personal learning remains open.

## Claim boundary

Even a three-root Q0 pass establishes only a finite, supervised, one-root-per-
unit complementary `(opaque tool, mode) -> action` writer under the registered
locality surface. It does not establish birth efficacy, improved subsequent
learning, child-authored SLEEP, parenting, connected knowledge, compression,
general continual learning, or lifetime improvement. SEQ-120 establishes none
of those claims either.
