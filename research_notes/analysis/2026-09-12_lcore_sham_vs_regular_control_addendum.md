# L-core control addendum: matched sham versus an ordinary agent

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Status: watcher-side design audit only. This note changes no builder source,
model, task, adapter, job, resource, or claim authority. It authorizes no GPU
work, childhood generation, release, or submission.

## Decision

Use **no-teacher as the final childhood comparator**, and retain the active
matched sham as an upstream lesson-qualification assay.

The minimum lifetime block remains five branches:

```text
same attested fresh birth
    |
    +-- adaptive targeted parent --> sealed adult P
    |       +-- P-RUN
    |       +-- P-FROZEN
    |       +-- P-TEXT
    |
    +-- no teacher ---------------> sealed adult N
            +-- N-RUN
            +-- N-FROZEN
```

During childhood, `P` and `N` receive the same task tape, child-generated-token
budget, legal action opportunities, THINK/DREAM/SLEEP mechanism, writer,
SLEEP opportunities, exams, and saved ages. `P` additionally receives the
frozen adaptive-parent policy and its root-local teaching state. Parent calls,
tokens, and latency are treatment costs and are reported, not fictitiously
matched away.

This is the smallest block that both matches the user's intended **parented
adult versus regular unparented agent** comparison and identifies the finite-
horizon parenting-by-SLEEP interaction:

```text
B_P = AUC(P-RUN) - AUC(P-FROZEN)
B_N = AUC(N-RUN) - AUC(N-FROZEN)
D   = B_P - B_N
```

Require both `B_P > 0` and `D > 0`. The first prevents an interaction from
passing only because promoted SLEEP harms the no-teacher child.

The supported claim is deliberately total-package:

> Under the exact classroom, teacher, and SLEEP policy, adaptive parenting
> changed the later benefit of promoted SLEEP relative to an otherwise matched
> no-teacher developmental agent.

It does not identify the effect of one sentence, emotional tone, answer
awareness, teacher tokens, or correction content separately. Those are parts
of the exact parenting package.

## Why sham should not be the headline adult

An active sham is the right control for asking whether **one specific teaching
distinction** is useful. It is not the ordinary agent the user wants in the
final comparison. It has a teacher, receives extra text, and may learn from the
sham's support or be harmed by it.

SEQ-075 demonstrated the danger: the lesson fit looked better than sham only
because both harmed the child and sham harmed more. SEQ-077 then found equal
first-action solves (`1/16` versus `1/16`) despite a secondary format shift.
The current `FORM_CHECK -> CONSTRAINT_LEDGER` card therefore has not passed the
visible-lesson utility gate and must not seed an expensive childhood.

For every candidate competency, first run a small no-write three-way assay:

```text
targeted lesson     matched active sham     no teacher
```

Freeze a useful action/constraint endpoint. The lesson must beat both sham and
no-teacher, and sham must stay within a no-harm margin of no-teacher. This
qualifies the material; it does not become a confirmation population and does
not support retention or parenting-by-SLEEP.

If a bounded one-SLEEP lesson canary is still wanted, it may compare lesson
versus sham after parent deletion as an excluded diagnostic. A positive result
supports only persistence of that exact taught distinction. The paper does not
need to carry sham through the full lifetime unless it wants the stronger
content-specific interaction claim.

## What each no-teacher placement can and cannot say

### 1. Upstream no-teacher anchor only

Use: qualify lesson/sham material and detect sham toxicity before childhood.

Supports: immediate visible utility of one exact lesson and no-harm of one
exact sham.

Does not support: a parented-adult versus ordinary-adult result, persistence,
lifetime curves, or parenting-by-SLEEP. A different upstream child cannot be
used as the final comparator.

### 2. One concurrent sixth no-teacher branch

Adding only `N-FROZEN` gives a contemporaneous ordinary reference for entry or
terminal value. Adding only `N-RUN` gives a total-system curve against a
no-parent learner. Either can be a useful secondary anchor.

One branch cannot identify `B_N`, so it cannot participate in the
parenting-by-SLEEP interaction. A full sham factorial **plus** a full no-teacher
factorial needs two no-teacher branches, producing seven lifetime branches
once `P-TEXT` is retained—not six. That cost is justified only if both a
content-specific teaching interaction and a total no-teacher interaction are
headline claims.

### 3. No-teacher replaces sham in confirmation — recommended

Use the five branches shown above. This directly answers the user's total-
package question without adding a confirmation arm. Keep sham upstream.

Supports:

- retained difference between parented and no-teacher adults after parent and
  context deletion (`P-FROZEN` versus `N-FROZEN` on fresh trigger and
  contraindication cases);
- relative autonomous lifetime curves (`P-RUN` versus `N-RUN`);
- parenting-by-SLEEP through `B_P > 0` and `D > 0`; and
- strong evolving-text comparison through the concurrent `P-TEXT` branch.

Does not support: correction-content causality. Use the words **total adaptive
parenting package**, not “the process lesson caused” or “answer-free correction
alone caused.”

## What “regular agent” means

The clean scientific comparator is `N`: a no-teacher child with the same
architecture, experience opportunities, and childhood SLEEP. It is an
**unparented experiential agent**, not necessarily the untouched raw base.
That match is what makes the parenting contrast interpretable.

`N-FROZEN` is the regular **adult** with no deployment weight promotion; it may
still contain whatever the no-teacher child learned during its matched
childhood. Do not call it an untouched base model.

If the paper also wants the familiar off-the-shelf frozen-agent number, add a
secondary `RAW-BASE` exam anchor on the same frozen forms. When the ordinary
agent has no cross-episode state and each evaluation is sterile, this can be a
repeated fixed-checkpoint exam anchor rather than a sixth on-policy lifetime.
It supports only level/context: “all developed systems were above/below the
raw base on these exams.” It does not replace `N-RUN/N-FROZEN` and does not
enter `D`.

If the ordinary agent is allowed persistent context, retrieval, or other
on-policy state, it needs a real concurrent lifetime branch; an exam-only raw
checkpoint is then not opportunity-matched.

## Preserve the user's parent-present 2x2 without another lifetime factorial

The user's other 2x2—adult type by parent presence during deployment—is a
different tutoring estimand:

```text
                         parent absent      parent present
parented adult P              P0                 P1
no-teacher adult N            N0                 N1
```

It does not identify SLEEP promotion. Run it, if retained, as a bounded set of
sterile terminal clones from the same sealed `P/N` adults, with parent
present/absent task tapes and budgets fixed in advance. No clone output,
teacher state, score, or weight update returns to L-core. This reuses the same
adults and adds bounded evaluator calls, not another set of lifelong children.

The tutoring 2x2 can support immediate audited tutoring response and whether
the parented adult uses the same teacher differently from the no-teacher adult.
Retention after tutoring would require a separately frozen post-removal test;
continued parent-present lifetime learning would be a new experiment.

## Minimum frozen block

Before expensive confirmation, bind:

1. one exact fresh birth and no-teacher childhood policy;
2. the strongest frozen parent model/policy/pedagogy snapshot, with adaptive
   state persistent within a root and reset between roots;
3. the qualified competency and upstream three-way lesson/sham/no-teacher
   receipt;
4. identical child/world/SLEEP opportunities for `P` and `N`;
5. parent/context deletion and sealed `P/N` adult receipts;
6. all five concurrent lifetime branches and root-level contrasts;
7. `P-TEXT` implementation and launch regardless of observed P-RUN direction;
8. an optional raw-base anchor labeled secondary; and
9. any terminal parent-presence 2x2 as a separate, no-return tutoring endpoint.

No current teaching card clears item 3. Until one does, the right result is
`CHILDHOOD_NOT_RUN`, not a full sham or no-teacher lifetime using unqualified
material.
