# Fresh adversarial audit of M-core v2

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Scope: independent read-only scientific review of
`2026-09-12_m_core_minimal_exact_two_cycle_design_v2.md`. I read that note in
full before consulting the directly linked predecessor/audit and the current
coordination record. Prior verdicts were treated as provenance, not authority.
No builder code, job, model, adapter, or GPU was touched.

## Verdict: REWORK

V2 repairs the previous source shuffle, truthful-outcome twin, nuisance
admission, independent-unit, and fit-count failures. It is not yet an exact
executable identification contract. The crossed source subdesign is basically
valid, and the six-fit allocation is sensible. However, the published graph and
READ API do not entail the claimed goal-conditioned traces; the DREAM candidates
are not shown to be frequency-indistinguishable on the evidence that actually
distinguishes them; several mandatory reader interventions are not acceptance
conditions; and the old-plus-pad arm is schedule-matched but not yet an exact
equal-work control. Those are release-fatal for the requested M-core sentence,
but repairable without adding trained arms.

## Fatal issues and exact minimum repairs

### F1. The path theorem and goal-conditioned READ trace are missing

The displayed graph makes **every** lane a route to both terminals:
`S -> U_i -> B -> C -> T_A/T_B`. The prose then asserts an unspecified
“ordinary public constraint” that uniquely selects `u_A` or `u_B`. No node,
edge, constraint predicate, action precondition, or alternative path encoding
that fact is given. Consequently the advertised unique proof is not derivable
from the graph.

The byte-level API compounds the gap. `READ` contains only `query_type` and one
`anchor_id`. Both B goals start at the same `S` with the same FULL S1 carrier.
V2 does not enumerate query types, state which public goal bytes enter the
recognition scorer, or give an A/B request trace whose first differing request
legitimately carries the goal distinction. A request anchored only at `S`
cannot deterministically select `a_u` from eight symmetric outgoing atoms; a
reverse read from `T_A/T_B` reaches shared `C` and still does not select a lane.
The asserted three-read FULL success and four-read atoms ceiling therefore are
tests-to-be-written, not consequences of this specification.

Minimum repair:

1. Publish the complete typed graph, including the public goal constraint,
   action preconditions, decoy alternatives, and D action semantics.
2. Enumerate every legal `query_type` and every byte passed to recognition.
   If public goal context is a scorer input, put it in the serialized request;
   do not rely on process-global prompt state.
3. Give exact A, B, D-h0, and D-h1 request/return/action traces and mark the
   first goal/outcome-dependent byte.
4. Exhaustively prove unique FULL traces, no three-read atoms trace, a valid
   four-read atoms trace, and old/new necessity while retaining the pre-DREAM
   candidate equivalences.

### F2. The DREAM “frequency” exclusion is defined around the counterexample

Useful lanes have joint success plus two failing matched single-component
ablations; decoys are explained by a single component or a registered nuisance
route. V2 proves equality only for schedule counts and **joint** rewards. A
policy that counts left-only and right-only outcomes per lane, or applies a
small truth table to those records, can distinguish the useful lanes. Calling
the latter `NECESSITY_ORACLE_CPU` does not make the information non-frequency:
the child and oracle receive the same public evidence, while the registered
non-oracle frequency policy is artificially restricted to joint counts.

This does not negate that the child emitted the pair IDs before the blueprint.
It does negate the broader claim that the organization was not mechanically
recoverable from syntax/frequency, and it makes “child-specific DREAM
intelligence” depend on excluding the strongest obvious mechanical baseline.

Minimum repair: choose one of two honest contracts.

- Narrow the claim to “not recoverable from endpoint syntax, exposure,
  recency, joint co-use, or joint-reward count,” register the ablation-count
  rule as a non-oracle mechanical opponent, and do not claim child-specific
  advantage if the child only ties it; or
- redesign the evidence so all per-lane/per-action outcome marginals,
  token/field bags, recency, and local record counts match, while only a
  registered relational alignment across contexts identifies necessity. Then
  publish the exhaustive feature table and run the strongest prospectively
  registered public-evidence policies, including the current ablation rule.

No wording should say the organization is mechanically unrecoverable without
specifying the algorithm/feature class: an exact public-evidence necessity rule
already recovers it by construction.

### F3. Reader visibility is a promise, not yet a closed acceptance object

The observability line
`I(catalog/order/status/length; correct row | public query)=0` is not defined
well enough to evaluate. `order` is backend-visible rather than actor-visible;
“public query” is unspecified and may or may not contain the goal; `FOUND`
versus `MISS` intentionally reveals presence; and the origin of the 32 candidate
payloads is not stated. A blanket proxy ceiling of `.25` is also not tied to
endpoint-specific chance levels (eight-way lane choice, binary source choice,
and complete-trace success are different events).

More importantly, section 7 calls `SLEEP1_OFF`, `CATALOG_PERMUTE`,
`READ_BINDING_SWAP`, and `GOAL_ONLY/CATALOG_ONLY` required controls, but the
noncompensatory definition of `R_r` never explicitly requires their model-level
failures/invariances. As written, a root can be `R_r=1` even if the S1 carrier
is unnecessary, candidate order drives retrieval, or the actor ignores the
returned semantic payload. CPU transform checks do not substitute for those
fitted-carrier inference cells.

Minimum repair:

1. Specify the public candidate superset and show it is materialized without
   truth, theorem, expected-trace, or post-outcome access; serialize all scorer
   inputs and outputs.
2. Split actor-observability from reader-order invariance. Define each random
   variable, conditioning set, endpoint, chance value, and exact finite-root
   calculation instead of using one `.25` ceiling.
3. Bind every claimed “required” inference control into `R_r`: S1 OFF must
   fail the dependent B/C trace; catalog permutation must preserve FULL;
   binding swaps must redirect the next query/action or yield the registered
   content-specific wrong action without parser/interface damage; carrier-free
   cells must fail the corresponding trace.
4. Require hit-, status-, length-, query-count-, and legal-action-matched false
   DREAM returns. If a swapped link merely proposes an illegal action from the
   current node and is rejected mechanically, call it an anchor-binding failure,
   not evidence for higher-level organization.

### F4. `FULL_OLD_PLUS_PAD` is not yet an exact equal-work S2 control

The S2 table matches initialization, deck row count, dose, optimizer schedule,
and slot. It does not state that the pad has the same tokenized input/target
lengths, supervised-token count, loss mask, render multiplicity, visit count,
deck position, or target-bearing training objective as `n0/n1`. “Canonical
pad; no new target” could mean masked padding or an easier repeated target,
either of which changes gradient-bearing work. Old-row retention/non-harm does
not repair that contrast. Thus NEW versus PAD is not yet an exact test that the
new grounded row, rather than a different second-fit workload, caused D.

Minimum repair: define one semantically dead, root-specific target-like row
whose serialized input/target token counts, supervised mask, renderer visits,
deck position, batch exposure, optimizer steps, and reader envelope match each
truthful row; preseal it before outcomes; show it cannot satisfy any B/C/D
predicate; publish token/deck/step diffs for all three S2 builds. If `n0` and
`n1` differ in token length, equalize their aliases or use presealed matched pad
variants without adding fits. Keep the same old-row retention and non-harm
tests.

Also define the root statistic over both branches. Current `W_r` contains an
unbound `h`; use a predeclared vector, mean, or conservative `min_h` reduction.

### F5. The release statistics and DEV root flow are incomplete

The reliability calculation is correct, but it is not a causal-analysis plan.
“Exact sign/randomization intervals” and “registered paired exact test” do not
name the root reducer, null, test statistic, alpha, confidence level, treatment
assignment/randomization set, or multiplicity handling. Child-specific DREAM
must beat two policies, and more policies are required by F2. The four C forks
are repeated measures, correctly not roots, but SOURCE_DERANGED “accuracy” has
no intrinsic correct action under its `.5/.5` row; it must be scored against a
predeclared label from the authentic counterfactual, not redefined after its
choice.

The stage language is contradictory: “all stages are source-disjoint” conflicts
with Stage 3 “add six” to the two Stage-2 roots and the `48`-fit eight-root DEV
total. It also leaves open which two Stage-1 successes become the fitted kill
roots. Likewise, the descriptive `Binomial(16,1/28)` law requires the useful
pair to be sampled uniformly from all 28 pairs, independently per root and of
the frozen tie policies. “Presealed useful lanes” and symmetry do not by
themselves state that generator law. Exact balancing of root cells would also
need a conditional/randomization analysis rather than an iid binomial label.

Minimum repair:

1. Declare DEV as either a nested, presealed eight-root prefix (first two fixed
   before Stage 1) or use genuinely disjoint stage cohorts and correct the fit
   totals. Confirmation remains sixteen fresh source-disjoint roots.
2. For source, DREAM, S1-OFF, and NEW-versus-PAD, specify the root-level vector,
   adverse fill, primary paired statistic, exact null distribution/test,
   interval, alpha, and familywise correction or one max-statistic family test.
3. Freeze the SOURCE_DERANGED scoring label and tie policy prospectively.
4. Publish the root generator/randomization law for `(b,z,u_A,u_B,u_D)`, aliases,
   orders, and fit RNG; align the binomial or stratified test with that law.
5. State that `12/16 R` is the reliability test only and cannot replace any of
   the four component causal tests.

## Nonfatal corrections

- Write the source numbers as empirical event frequencies (`3/4`, `1/4`,
  `2/4`), not environmental probabilities, unless an actual stochastic
  sampling law is added. The present fixed occurrence schedule still supports
  a crossed experiential association.
- One pad carrier may be evaluated against both `h` twins because the twins are
  repeated measures, but all h-specific inference bytes surviving the reset
  must be listed. Raw C outcome/history must remain absent as section 9 requires.
- `LINK_CUT` and `OLD_CUT` at D appear redundant. Give them distinct semantics
  or keep only one in the root endpoint.
- The writer gate is appropriately conditional: the inspected coordination
  record did not establish an actual W0-qualified selective writer for M. No M
  LoRA fit is ready merely because this design note exists.

## Truth table, independence, stopping, and arithmetic checks

The Phase-C admission table is correct under its stated deterministic world:
target-direct gives `y=h`, target-inverse gives `y=1-h`, and either nuisance
experiment leaves both `h` values possible even when `z==h`. Admission must
continue to depend on a singleton **public** posterior, with
`DECLARATION_FAITHFUL` and `WORLD_TRUE` separate.

The independent unit is correctly the source-disjoint root. The two h branches,
two goals, four C probes, calls, renders, and checkpoints are repeated measures.
The confirmation rule has no early success stop, keeps skipped upstream failures
as zeros, and the stated exact binomial tail is correct:

```text
P[X >= 12 | X ~ Binomial(16, .5)]
  = 2517 / 65536
  = 0.0384063720703125.
```

The chance of selecting both useful lanes is also correctly
`1 / choose(8,2) = 1/28`. Neither calculation fixes the missing paired component
tests.

The fit arithmetic is exact **if** the two Stage-2 roots are included in the
eight-root DEV total:

```text
S1/root = 3; S2/surviving root = 3; maximum/root = 6
2-root S1 kill = 6
2-root full kill = 12
8-root DEV maximum = 48
16-root confirmation maximum = 96
DEV + confirmation maximum = 144.
```

At the assumed `7.5--15` A40-minutes per fit, `144*t_fit` is exactly `18--36`
A40-hours and six S1 fits are `.75--1.5` A40-hours. Adding the asserted
`10--24` gives `28--60`, and dividing by eight gives `3.5--7.5` ideal device
hours. Those multiplications are correct. The `10--24` control allowance and
“below roughly three A40-hours” kill-gate total are unsupported planning guesses,
not conservative bounds. Report cost as `N_fit*t_fit` plus measured inference,
action, and reset overhead until the exact deck and controls are timed; keep
serial critical path and device packing separate from aggregate A40-hours.

## Minimum acceptance boundary after repair

No extra trained arm is required. Before any M fit, v3 must supply the full
typed graph/query theorem, frequency-clean DREAM table or narrowed claim,
byte-exact reader/bank observability contract, exact S2 pad token/work receipts,
and frozen root/test statistics. Then Stage 0 must falsify every named shortcut,
and the noncompensatory endpoint must literally include every inference control
used in the release sentence.

If those repairs pass, the source and two-cycle claims can use v2's cautious
phrasing: **crossed experiential binding**, **child-emitted pre-blueprint
organization** (with the repaired mechanical qualifier), **typed functional
traversal**, and **two-cycle compiler-mediated cumulative reconstruction**.
Until then, M-core v2 is a strong design sketch, not a launch-ready exact test.
