# Clean one-parent childhood and five-branch L-core: watcher audit

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Status: watcher-side design and contamination audit only. This note changes no
builder-owned source, model, task, adapter, job, resource, or claim authority.
It does not authorize childhood generation, GPU work, release, or submission.

## Verdict

The minimum clean study is simpler than the surrounding governance documents
make it sound:

```text
one attested fresh birth
          |
          +-- targeted adaptive parenting --> sealed parented adult P
          |
          +-- active matched sham ----------> sealed matched adult S

P --> P-RUN, P-FROZEN, P-TEXT
S --> S-RUN, S-FROZEN
```

The first four deployment branches identify whether targeted childhood changes
the later benefit of promoted SLEEP. `P-TEXT` is the fifth branch needed only
for the current strong evolving-text comparison. A branch is not an independent
learner: the independent unit is one fresh birth block containing the matched
`P/S` childhood pair and all five terminal descendants.

No historical adult qualifies as the source of this clean study. R2/R3/R4/R5,
RP, bootstrap-v1--v3, `lineage3_*`, `parent_scout_*`, and the CompilerGym
preschool are development evidence only. The current static
`FORM_CHECK -> CONSTRAINT_LEDGER` scout can establish whether one visible card
improves an immediate action. It cannot become a clean adult, and it cannot
establish adaptive parenting, persistence, or SLEEP.

## Four objects that must not be conflated

### Bootstrap

A bootstrap is a one-time weight intervention made **before** child-specific
interaction from an immutable, target-blind demonstration corpus. It installs
an entry habit; it is neither the child's lived memory nor parenting.

The clean minimum should omit it unless an excluded feasibility test shows the
base child cannot express the taught competency at all. If a bootstrap is
necessary, use the exact same bootstrapped birth for `P` and `S`, freeze it
before randomization, and state that L-core estimates parenting conditional on
that bootstrap. Testing whether bootstrap helps requires another factorial and
does not belong in the minimum five-branch study.

A birth brief shown only in context is instruction, not a parametric bootstrap.
A fixed lesson card is static teaching, not adaptive parenting.

### Parenting

Parenting is a child-conditioned intervention during a target-blind classroom.
The strongest bound teacher may inspect the child's allowed classroom history,
identify a process error, choose what to repeat or fade, and give an
answer-withholding intervention before the child tries again. The teacher may
know the answer or verifier for the **classroom** task in order to diagnose the
process, but it has no access to deployment tasks, answers, identities, scores,
or descendants.

Parent words are visible during wake but receive zero training loss. Only the
child's committed thought/action, the public outcome, and child-authored
grounded continuations may become SLEEP targets. Exact or paraphrastic copying
of a lesson is not itself uptake and must never be the behavioral endpoint.

### Personal learning

Personal learning is the child's own SLEEP update from grounded lived
experience. It is what changes the child's LoRA. The parent weights do not
learn. The parent and SLEEP can both influence the child, but they remain
separate causal objects.

### Development versus deployment

Classroom tasks may update the clean child under the frozen childhood policy.
Disposable exams and every deployment descendant are terminal: none of their
text, outcomes, scores, caches, selections, parent notes, or learned weights may
return to the clean child, its canonical parent state, or a later root.

## Strong adaptive parent without cross-root leakage

The user's strongest-parent vision and independent experimental roots are
compatible if adaptation happens at the right level:

1. **Before confirmation**, Rohin, Codex, Fable, and development children may
   improve a reusable `pedagogy_core`. Every promoted teacher model, prompt,
   tool, skill, curriculum, or core revision gets a new version and forks the
   scientific lineage.
2. **At confirmation freeze**, bind the strongest available teacher model and
   exact revision, prompt, tools, files, decoding, and one mature
   `pedagogy_core` snapshot.
3. **Inside one childhood root**, do not reset the parent every lesson. Give it
   a private root-local `child_record`; it may remember the child's prior
   attempts, update teaching hypotheses, repeat lessons, and fade support under
   the frozen state-transition rule.
4. **Between independent roots**, reset to the same frozen pedagogy snapshot,
   create a fresh provider session and empty private `child_record`, and deny
   all sibling/root visibility. Root-local parent state is discarded when that
   root ends.

Thus the *teacher policy* is the same across roots while its teaching adapts to
each child. Letting one live parent learn across confirmation roots would make
later treatment depend on earlier children's outcomes and launch order; the
parent, not the child block, would become the effective replication unit. Such
a continuing parent is useful exploratory development, but not clean L-core
confirmation.

Developmental exam feedback should default to no return. If one finite
aggregate ever informs the parent, its alphabet, destination field, and one-use
rule must be frozen in advance; raw exam behavior never returns. Sealed
deployment feedback has no exception.

## The one taught competency

Use the already specified narrow disposition rather than a broad instruction
to "think better":

> After an informative and verifiable action outcome, form a grounded
> first-person condition--action--outcome--belief-update record; use it when
> its condition genuinely recurs, and withhold it on non-informative or
> contraindication cases.

This is one competency with both a positive trigger and a negative boundary.
It connects THINK to lived evidence and gives SLEEP something behavioral to
amortize without teaching deployment answers.

Score it through consequences, not words:

- correct first useful/informative action on fresh homologous trigger cases;
- fewer invalid or constraint-violating first actions;
- correct non-use on matched contraindication cases;
- later acquisition value under the five-branch deployment; and
- interface and unrelated-skill noninferiority.

Lexical restatement, longer thought, lesson-token overlap, NOTE frequency, or a
teacher-style answer is not success. The static
`FORM_CHECK -> CONSTRAINT_LEDGER` package is an upstream visible-instruction
material-fit probe, not a substitute for this childhood competency.

## Matched sham

`S` is an **active matched sham**, not an unparented child. Match the targeted
condition on:

- teacher model/revision and number of calls;
- timing, turn count, tools, visible history, and opportunity to respond;
- tokenizer-counted parent tokens and context occupancy;
- child thought/action budget, task tape, outcomes, and SLEEP opportunities;
- writer, rank, fit schedule, canaries, and saved ages.

Only the targeted process distinction changes. The sham must be plausible,
same-format support that neither names the registered competency nor gives
conflicting advice. Before L-core, compare both visible treatments with a
separate frozen no-teacher DEV anchor. Require the targeted card to improve a
useful action and the sham not to cause material harm. Otherwise
`targeted > sham` can mean only that the sham was more damaging, as happened in
SEQ-075.

The no-teacher anchor is an upstream qualification cell; it need not become a
sixth confirmation branch. Confirmation estimates the total exact targeted
adaptive-parenting package relative to the qualified matched sham, not the
effect of one sentence or parenting in general.

## Sealing and deleting childhood before deployment

Seal both adults at a predeclared age or classroom-only graduation rule, never
because one later scores better on deployment. Each sealed-adult receipt must
bind the birth, base and tokenizer, optional common bootstrap, mechanism and
writer, parent version and root-local session, classroom source set, every
accepted/rejected SLEEP transaction, final adapter bytes, and deletion result.

Deployment starts in a fresh process with:

- the exact sealed adult adapter and frozen THINK/DREAM/SLEEP mechanism;
- empty active context and empty deployment text store;
- no parent process, callable parent credential, lesson, parent notebook,
  classroom transcript, waking brief, retrieval index, embeddings, cache,
  query state, or society/sibling state; and
- no path by which a disposable descendant can update the adult or parent.

The learned skill is allowed to remain in the LoRA—that is the treatment.
Continued deployment SLEEP must extend the exact adult adapter (or reproduce it
under an explicitly tested cumulative reconstruction), not silently fit a new
adapter from clean base and erase childhood. The minimum clean design should
not retain a hidden childhood text corpus merely to rebuild the adult; if one
is retained, it is an additional nonparametric learning state and must be
matched, exposed in the estimand, and separately audited.

`P-TEXT` starts from the same sealed parented adult, keeps its childhood LoRA
frozen, and begins deployment with an empty, qualified public-only evolving
text store. It is a total-system opponent, not a pure carrier comparison.

## Minimum gates before expensive childhood generation

These are the smallest gates; the full C11-style sealed-final apparatus need
not block current mechanism scouts.

1. **Fresh-birth gate.** Fail closed before any model/parent call. Accept only
   an attested empty fresh-base birth (or one exact common bootstrap, if
   predeclared); reject every unknown/inherited adapter, corpus, brief, cache,
   society file, and quarantined ancestry.
2. **Split/visibility gate.** Prove the classroom source/generator and parent
   read set contain no deployment-family task bytes, identifiers, answers,
   scores, transformed derivatives, or global sibling state. Bind the exact
   teacher and allowed tools.
3. **Visible-lesson utility gate.** On fixed no-write tasks, run targeted,
   token/opportunity-matched sham, and no-teacher. Useful first-action behavior
   must improve; sham harm cannot manufacture the result. This is the current
   static competency scout's only valid role.
4. **Parent-isolation gate.** Demonstrate one persistent parent notebook within
   a root, fresh empty private state and provider session between roots, and
   hard refusal of sibling, exam, deployment, and report-panel reads.
5. **Loss/provenance gate.** Verify tokenizer-bound zero loss on parent,
   harness, and outcome text; admit only child continuations joined to a real
   executed action and public outcome; preserve all sources for derivatives;
   reject orphan, fabricated, copied-dirty, and favorable-only rows.
6. **One-SLEEP persistence gate.** In one excluded 32-episode childhood canary,
   show absorption after a fresh reload, correct trigger **and** non-trigger
   behavior after parent deletion, previous-child interface/no-harm, and atomic
   accept-or-rollback. A merely lower training loss does not pass.
7. **Adult-extension gate.** Prove a zero-new-data operation leaves the sealed
   adult behavior/adapter unchanged and a later accepted SLEEP extends rather
   than replaces the adult. A rejected candidate leaves adapter, corpus,
   optimizer, brief, and all future-readable state unchanged.
8. **Five-fork gate.** Hash-load the same `P` adult into `P-RUN`, `P-FROZEN`,
   and `P-TEXT`, and the same `S` adult into `S-RUN` and `S-FROZEN`.
   `FROZEN` performs complete shadow SLEEP but never mounts the candidate;
   RUN/FROZEN are identical before first promotion. All five roots are
   terminal and same-block scheduling is recorded.
9. **Endpoint/freeze gate.** Freeze one competency, sham, childhood dose,
   seven deployment cuts/forms, exogenous exposure clock, actor budgets,
   writer/rank, retention margins, failed-write handling, root-level analysis,
   and resource-based sample-size rule before confirmation identities exist.

After gates 1--5 pass, a cheap excluded childhood canary is justified. After
gates 6--8 pass directionally, expensive independently raised childhood roots
are justified. A failed gate means `NOT_RUN` or a new versioned development
lineage, not reuse of a contaminated adult.

## Narrow release order

1. Parent-deleted persistence: targeted versus sham on fresh trigger and
   contraindication cases.
2. Parenting-by-SLEEP: require both `P-RUN > P-FROZEN` over the lifetime AUC
   and `(P-RUN-P-FROZEN) > (S-RUN-S-FROZEN)`.
3. Increasing-lifetime learning: separately require positive absolute late
   `P-RUN` slope/gain and childhood plus early-deployment retention.
4. Strong-text advantage: only if the concurrent qualified `P-TEXT` branch
   loses on the predeclared slope and terminal margins.

Passing this study would show that one exact targeted adaptive childhood made
one exact SLEEP mechanism more useful over a finite later lifetime, relative
to one qualified sham, and possibly outperformed one qualified evolving-text
system. It would not show that pedagogy is solved, that parent wording alone is
causal, that one adult generalizes universally, or that a parent may learn
across confirmatory roots without changing the experiment.
