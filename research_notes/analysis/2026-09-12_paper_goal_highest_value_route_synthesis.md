# Paper-goal route after the writer sign canary

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Role:** fresh independent read-only synthesis  
**Cut:** the specified sprint abstract, first-eleven writer audit, RuleGame-v2
terminal audit, fresh-behavior causal audit, and the final 250 coordination
lines. This memo launches nothing and changes no scientific system.

## Executive decision

The paper has several useful prerequisite results but still lacks one closed
causal chain. The shortest high-value route is: first require the already
recommended two-input/opposite-action canary to show that one writer can bind
equal-format inputs to opposite decisions; if it passes, run one presealed
**level-1 teaching-corpus adherence-by-plasticity experiment** before another
parented lifetime or H2 deployment. That experiment should establish whether a
fresh rank-8 LoRA can retain simple taught learning behaviours and taught
content outside their teaching form, versus both frozen-child and equal-dose
shuffled/wrong-binding controls. Only after that result should scarce effort
return to child-authored material and the full H1/H2 chain.

## 1. Maximum claims already proved

1. **The frozen-base LoRA substrate can change behaviour and carry supplied
   material, but not selectively enough for the thesis.** Oracle correctly
   bound Sudoku material yielded sparse exact solves (2/3/5 of 16 across three
   optimizer seeds) versus wrong-bound material (0/1/0) and adapter-OFF zero on
   a reused development panel. Synthetic assays also install cue-compatible
   completions. These establish supervised parametric carriage and material
   sensitivity, not experiential memory, an algorithm, or parenting.
2. **The current writer is unstable and spill is binding.** In the first eleven
   heterogeneous lives, whole-text fitting cleared the descriptive report band
   in 4/11 and the source-disjoint band in 2/11, but mean deltas were only
   +.0045 and -.0045. Target-masked frames were likewise null/unstable;
   deterministic QA destroyed the measured interface in 11/11; the coupled
   TMEM-style bundle was usually harmful. Strong synthetic acquisition changes
   unsupported cues, and OLD+NEW is fresh-base simultaneous replay with
   attenuation, not sequential retention.
3. **Some generation-side process contrasts are real but no useful chain is
   closed.** Worked examples improved source grounding (2/8 versus 0/8) and
   application (1/8 versus 0/8), but the only application followed an invalid
   source and remained note-present. No fit or parent-free test followed.
4. **RuleGame-v2 qualifies only a narrow execution boundary.** It produced 8/8
   valid quizzes, 24 real TRYs, and replayable action/world custody, improving
   on v1's 8/8 protocol-invalid tasks. Material nevertheless failed: faithful
   records were P 1/6 versus A 3/6, controls violated acknowledgment-only
   contact, all observed apply outcomes were True, and no corpus, fit, reload,
   or readout occurred. This is interface repair, not learning or teaching.
5. **The fresh-behavior attempt-2 panel cannot carry a freshness claim.** Four
   selected IDs had prior committed model exposure. Even a repaired panel would
   be an exploratory exact-output-disjoint transport diagnostic, not H1/H2.

Thus the maximum paper-level statement is that controlled post-training can
carry supplied routines/associations, while present experience-to-corpus
recipes are unstable, nonselective, or interface-destructive; a repaired
interaction harness can now execute grounded events but has not generated
qualified teaching material. H1 and H2 remain proposed questions.

## 2. Exact missing causal link to H1/H2

No experiment has shown, within one prospectively controlled chain, that
**developmental teaching causes the child to produce useful, externally
grounded post-training material; sleeping on that child-authored material
causes a selective parameter change; and the change improves task-disjoint
learning-process behaviour after the parent, lesson, transcript, notes, and
other temporary teaching state are removed.** That missing teaching -> artifact
-> write -> parent-free behaviour edge is H1's necessary bridge. Current work
has separately shown teaching-like text, material formation failures, synthetic
writes, and sparse downstream utility, but never their causal composition.

Without a qualified H1 child, H2 is not merely underpowered; its estimand has
not been instantiated. H2 additionally requires the registered deployment
interaction on unseen verifiable tasks:

`(parented running-sleep - parented frozen-sleep) - (unparented running-sleep - unparented frozen-sleep)`

measured as improvement/slope from new self-generated experience, not a higher
starting level or terminal-score difference.

## 3. Highest-information experiment after the opposite-action canary

Run one **open-loop level-1 teaching-corpus adherence-by-plasticity test**. It
should be a deliberately favorable test of the post-training substrate and
corpus, with no child-generated training text and no parent model, so a failure
cannot be blamed on DREAM formation.

- Preseal a coherent corpus that teaches a few fundamental, mechanically
  scorable learning behaviours (for example: commit a prediction before an
  action; cite the observed evidence; choose a grounded lesson or explicit
  null) plus nonce factual content. Include definitions, varied worked
  examples, corrective examples, and repetition.
- From the same frozen base, compare fresh rank-8 LoRAs trained on the correct
  corpus against an equal-token, equal-target-marginal shuffled/wrong-binding
  corpus and adapter OFF. Start at the highest safe plasticity, then vary only
  learning rate/epochs/repetition on a predeclared small ladder; keep seeds,
  token dose, modules, prompts, and evaluation fixed.
- Measure both exact trained-form acquisition and parent-free held-form
  adherence/content recall on task-disjoint situations after reload. Require
  correct conditional action selection, not slogans or interface conformity;
  report unsupported-cue spill and destructive interface changes.

This is the highest-information successor because the sign canary answers
whether conditional writing is possible at all, while this experiment answers
the next live uncertainty: whether a sufficiently explicit teaching corpus and
plasticity regime can install the simple behaviours/content that H1 needs. A
positive result qualifies a level-1 mechanism and supplies a target distribution
for the later child-authored DREAM experiment; it is still not H1 because no
think-then-sleep child generated the corpus. A failure under this favorable
design redirects the project to the writer/substrate rather than parenting or
large adult deployments.

## 4. Work to stop as low information

- Stop adding whole-text/frame/QA/TMEM-style lifetime bakeoffs or coupled
  optimizer/rank/format bundles before conditional carriage and level-1
  teaching are qualified. Eleven lives already identify instability and harm.
- Stop action-syntax, sampler-seed, citation-prefix, and verbose relation-label
  rescues as scientific targets. Keep relation transcription only as the tiny
  interface diagnostic already scoped; mechanically bind trusted event
  relations thereafter and spend model calls on the child's lesson-or-null
  judgment.
- Do not promote or repeat the contaminated fresh-behavior attempt as fresh
  evidence. A registry-repaired rerun is lower priority than the level-1 test
  and remains only a transport diagnostic.
- Do not launch the expensive adult H2 2x2, more parented RuleGame formations,
  or mechanism-freeze/manuscript promotion until there is a qualified writer,
  an explicit teaching corpus that survives parent-free held-form tests, and a
  prospectively valid child-authored H1 bridge. Otherwise a null cannot localize
  parenting, corpus formation, writing, retention, or adult learning.
- Stop treating more repeated-puzzle cells, optimizer seeds, tasks, tokens, or
  completed convenience lives as independent replication. The learner/life or
  matched block remains the causal unit.

The route is therefore a ladder, not a broader sweep: conditional sign canary
-> favorable level-1 teaching/write qualification -> strongest-teacher paired
child-authored DREAM-or-null plus sleep/OFF parent-free H1 bridge -> only then
the registered parented/unparented x running/frozen adult slope interaction.
