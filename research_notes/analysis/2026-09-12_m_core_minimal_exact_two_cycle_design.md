# M-core: minimum exact two-cycle THINK--DREAM--SLEEP relay

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Status: independent watcher design only. This note changes no benchmark,
builder source, child, model, adapter, job, GPU allocation, claim, or release.
It is a proposed minimum for Astra to attack or implement under its own builder
authority. It does not supersede a bound contract.

## Verdict

The smallest experiment that still tests all three organism operations is one
finite two-cycle root with **three trained memory conditions and two writes per
condition**:

1. `FULL_AUTH`: the child's real public action--outcome events, its own
   pre-blueprint DREAM links, and the later truthful outcome row;
2. `SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED`: the same path atoms, child DREAM decision,
   support events, Phase-C world/compiler, immutable source-event identities,
   outcome-donor multiset, surfaces, marginals, row count, dose, and optimizer
   work, with **only the Phase-A cue--family action/outcome binding** deranged
   in derived compiler rows (later action/outcome differences are causal
   descendants of that intervention); and
3. `DREAM_NULL`: truthful action--outcome atoms and the truthful later outcome
   row, but the child-authored link slots replaced by byte- and dose-matched
   `NULL` rows.

Every other causal question can be asked by unmounting one of those adapters,
reusing the first-sleep adapter at the delayed test, or intervening on reads.
No fourth trained arm is needed.

The experiment can establish a **bounded compiler-mediated causal relay**:
the child acted and observed; before seeing the later action blueprint it
organized its observations; a conservative SLEEP carried that organization;
old memory changed an information-seeking action; a public outcome selected a
precommitted new row; and a cumulative second SLEEP changed a delayed action
that required both old and new content. It does not establish native graph
learning, learned search, open-world expansion, physical compression, or a
sequential in-place weight update.

## 1. Why this is the minimum rather than a smaller proxy

Each of the following is indispensable:

- Two SLEEPs are required to distinguish old experience from a newly acquired
  row and to test their delayed conjunction.
- An authentic child action followed by a later public outcome is required;
  oracle statements and harness-selected actions cannot stand in for THINK.
- One child-authored organization object must be sealed before later support
  actions, goals, or answers are visible; a mechanical event join alone cannot
  stand in for DREAM.
- A truthful semantic twin must change the selected experiment. Otherwise the
  child may merely react to the presence of memory.
- `NO_SLEEP_2`, `OLD_CUT`, and `NEW_CUT` must each destroy delayed success.
  Otherwise the second write or old--new conjunction is unnecessary.
- `SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED` and `DREAM_NULL` must be separately trained. One
  corrupt control cannot distinguish authentic event binding from
  organization.

Removing any one of these narrows the experiment to storage, factual recall,
in-context reasoning, or supplied-memory traversal rather than the requested
THINK--DREAM--SLEEP loop.

## 2. Exact root geometry

### 2.1 Hidden roles and public aliases

Each independent root contains opaque aliases of these hidden roles:

```text
path states:       S, X, Y, B, C, TA, TB, D
hypothesis roles:  H0, H1, J0, J1
experiments:       E0, E1, E2, E3
outcome bit:       h in {0,1}
signal family:     f in {H,J}
```

All actor-visible node, action, relation, candidate, row, and event IDs are
derived only from `(root_nonce, object_class, slot)` and then independently
permuted. IDs, byte length, list position, and padding carry no role, truth,
goal, arm, score, or root-seed information. A root includes two counterfactual
twin axes: `h` flips the later outcome/new action, while `f` switches which
experiment family old memory says is relevant.

### 2.2 Old path atoms

The seven old path relations are:

```text
p0: S -> X       p1: X -> B
p2: S -> Y       p3: Y -> B
p4: B -> C       p5: C -> TA       p6: C -> TB
```

The two same-start Phase-B goals are `S -> TA` and `S -> TB`. Each has two
legal four-action paths, but their terminal action differs. A later Phase-D
goal starts at `X` or `Y` as presealed by the root and targets `D`. Its unique
minimal proof requires one old two-atom link (`p1+p4` or `p3+p4`) followed by
the new row. Thus neither an old-only nor a new-only carrier can close D.

Every `p` atom is admitted only from an actually executed child `ACT` and the
causally later ordinary public environment outcome. A source opportunity
offers `ACT` and `SKIP`; the harness never executes on the child's behalf,
repairs a missing action, or substitutes an oracle result. Missing source
coverage is an adverse root failure.

### 2.3 The one DREAM decision

After the old discovery events, DREAM receives only the public event/atom
records and a fixed roster of twelve candidate atom pairs. Exactly six pairs
are composable:

```text
(p0,p1), (p2,p3), (p1,p4), (p3,p4), (p4,p5), (p4,p6).
```

The other six are fixed noncomposable pairings drawn from the same seven atoms.
All twelve candidates have the same schema, padding, occurrence count, age
stratum, and presentation treatment. Candidate order is independently
permuted. DREAM must publish one `ORGANIZE_SET` containing exactly the six
pair IDs it judges connected. The decision is sealed before the child can see:

- any support-action handle, menu, arguments, or expected result;
- either Phase-B goal;
- the Phase-C cue, hypotheses, action menu, or outcome;
- the Phase-D goal or either possible new row; or
- any compiler, theorem, score, hidden role, or expected trace.

Only after that seal does the world reveal six public co-use opportunities.
The child executes the support actions. Each proposed link is admitted only
when later public evidence independently verifies that both cited atoms are
necessary under matched single-atom ablations. Validation cannot create a
proposal or change its endpoints. In particular, the two central links
`(p1,p4)` and `(p3,p4)` are necessary for the scored B and D traces under the
read budget.

This is intentionally a modest DREAM claim: the child organized explicit
events before the benchmark exposed their later use. It is not evidence that
the LoRA internally learned an arbitrary graph representation.

### 2.4 Old content that selects an experiment

Phase A also contains sixteen public calibration action--outcome events for
the following balanced table:

```text
             E0      E1      E2      E3
H0           0       1       {0,1}   {0,1}
H1           1       0       {0,1}   {0,1}
J0           {0,1}   {0,1}   0       1
J1           {0,1}   {0,1}   1       0
```

Two matched authentic old signal events map two opaque cues to opposite
families. The cue later used in Phase C maps to `f`; a matched decoy cue maps
to the other family. Under `H`, `E0/E1` uniquely identify `h` while `E2/E3`
identify only a balanced nuisance bit; under `J`, `E2/E3` identify `h` while
`E0/E1` identify only the nuisance bit. The truthful `f` twin changes the two
supported old signal relations and the matching hidden experiment equations,
and must redirect the child's first experiment into the other experiment
pair. Both twin signals are equally frequent, supported, and target-relevant,
but they demand different actions.

`SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED` swaps only the two signal-event outcome donors in
the compiled S1 rows. It does **not** alter any `p` atom, calibration row,
candidate pair, `ORGANIZE_SET`, public support event, or admitted DREAM link.
The main cue therefore retrieves the wrong family while every path/DREAM byte
remains held fixed. The Phase-C experiment and its public outcome are then
allowed to unfold naturally and are compiled truthfully; the control never
performs a second shuffle at the new-row seam.

Before acting in Phase C, the child must publish:

```text
signal row used
-> two live hypotheses
-> predicted outcomes for the chosen experiment
-> outcome-to-new-row map
-> selected experiment
```

For `E0/E2`, outcome `y` maps to row `n_y`; for `E1/E3`, outcome `y` maps to
`n_(1-y)`. The environment then returns the public bit. A total target-blind
compiler may select the already declared row, but it cannot invent, repair, or
reinterpret a declaration. Missing or ambiguous maps remain adverse.

The new row is:

```text
n_h: C -R(7+h)-> D
```

Both relation labels reach the same public target but require different final
actions. The `h` twin is byte-identical through experiment dispatch, then
flips the public outcome, admitted new row, and correct delayed action.

### 2.5 Read geometry

The clean base actor uses a frozen typed one-hop reader. LoRA is mounted only
during `READ`, never while the actor plans or acts. Every READ chooses one
candidate explicitly; batch reads are illegal.

- There are eight exchangeable first-read candidates and at most three reads
  in Phase B. A valid old link compresses the two middle atoms, so a path uses
  one prefix atom, one two-atom link, and one terminal atom. `DREAM_NULL`
  requires four atomic reads and therefore cannot produce a valid trace.
- Phase C permits two reads and exactly one experiment action.
- Phase D permits two reads: the required old link and the new row.
- Goal-only, catalog-only, ID/order, fixed-first, and exhaustive-suffix proxy
  policies must be `<= .25` in exhaustive CPU closure.

The exact-text carrier uses the identical candidate roster, query API, read
budget, clean actor, and trace scorer. It is the assay ceiling, not the paper's
strong evolving-text lifetime baseline.

## 3. Exact chronology and visibility

```text
A0  seal root, twins, public opportunity tape, candidate permutations,
    all future goals, all interventions, and all RNG counters
A1  child ACT -> public outcomes -> immutable Phase-A event ledger
A2  child ORGANIZE_SET sealed (no blueprint/goals/C/D visible)
A3  child support ACTs -> public co-use outcomes -> link admission
S1  compile old FULL/source-binding-shuffled/DREAM-null decks; fit three
    SLEEP-1 adapters
B   sterile forks: same-start goal A and goal B; no writes; destroy forks
C0  restore common post-S1 state with B workspaces/caches deleted
C1  old carrier is read; child declares map; child ACTs; world emits outcome
C2  compiler admits exactly the declaration-selected public new row
S2  cumulative clean-base rebuild for each condition over old + one new row
D0  fresh process; all C prose, workspaces, caches, raw outcome text, and
    temporary stores deleted; then reveal delayed goal
D1  child reads and ACTs; seal terminal public result
```

Future goals are presealed but capability-inaccessible before their reveal.
The compiler has the public event, declaration, and schema only. The actor and
reader process cannot import or open the generator, hidden world, theorem,
compiler receipt, scorer, oracle policy, or expected trace.

SLEEP-2 is a clean-base cumulative reconstruction using immutable SLEEP-1
rows plus the new row. It is not an in-place update of the SLEEP-1 weights.
The experiment therefore supports “two-cycle cumulative reconstruction,” not
“persistent sequential adapter expansion.”

## 4. The only three trained conditions

Each root uses one predeclared optimizer seed and **exactly the writer recipe
that actually passes W0 plus its old/new coexistence canary**. If canonical
`Q0` passes, M inherits canonical `Q0`; if W0 instead qualifies a registered
cross-view or preservation repair, M inherits that exact frozen repair. M does
not assume cross-view rendering, add paraphrases, or select a writer recipe of
its own. There is no fit-seed search, best-candidate selection, or condition-
specific dose. A rejected/failed candidate gives that root zero.

| condition | SLEEP-1 deck | SLEEP-2 deck | causal role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FULL_AUTH` | truthful child action--outcome atoms, both truthful cue--family rows, and six admitted child-proposed links | exact S1 deck + truthful declaration-selected `n_h` | complete chain |
| `SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED` | byte-identical truthful path/calibration atoms and the exact same six DREAM links; only the two cue--family compiler rows swap outcome donors | exact shuffled S1 deck + the **truthful, unshuffled** Phase-C declaration-selected `n_h`, if one was validly acquired | authentic old action--outcome binding |
| `DREAM_NULL` | all truthful atoms/cue rows + six padded `NULL` link rows in the same slots | exact null S1 deck + truthful `n_h` if validly acquired, otherwise the common `NO_ADMISSION` pad | child-authored organization |

Every semantic row is rendered with the exact W0-qualified renderer, loss
mask, dose, and replay schedule—whatever prospectively registered recipe
qualified. Old and new strata receive the same registered minimum supervised
dose; null/pad rows occupy the same token-length class. Prompts are zero-loss
context and only the canonical child memory continuation is a target. Corpus
rows retain exact event/link/declaration provenance.

The shuffle never rewrites a public event under its old ID. Each of the two
shuffled signal rows is a new canonical synthetic object that cites the
unchanged action event and its distinct outcome-donor event plus the frozen
swap receipt. Equal source identities means equal immutable source objects,
not “same ID, changed payload.” Every non-signal S1 row is byte-identical
between FULL and SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED; every DREAM row and support receipt
is byte-identical; the S2 new row, when admitted, is derived truthfully from
that arm's actual Phase-C action/outcome rather than transformed again.

Every S2 deck has one reserved new-row slot. A validly acquired row occupies
it; otherwise a canonical matched `NO_ADMISSION` pad occupies it. This keeps
row count and dose exact without turning a failed acquisition into a row.

### 4.1 Orthogonality re-audit

Before any model call, an exact deck-diff receipt must prove:

```text
FULL S1 vs SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED S1:
    exactly two derived cue--family target rows differ;
    every path atom, calibration row, DREAM row, support citation,
    slot, rendering multiplicity, and loss mask is byte-identical.

FULL S1 vs DREAM_NULL S1:
    exactly six DREAM link slots differ;
    every action--outcome atom, cue/calibration row, slot,
    rendering multiplicity, and loss mask is byte-identical.

All three S2 writers:
    same truthful G_new compiler and reserved-slot rule;
    any action/outcome/new-row difference is downstream behavior,
    never a second intervention.
```

This restores the intended factorial isolation without a fourth trained arm.
FULL versus SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED identifies the old cue--family
action/outcome binding while holding DREAM fixed. FULL versus DREAM_NULL
identifies the DREAM link content while holding every source binding fixed.
The added check changes neither the six-fit-per-root count nor the inference-
only control roster.

There are exactly six fits per valid root: three at S1 and three at S2. Public
source and DREAM model calls occur once and are forked identically into the
three material conditions.

## 5. Inference-only controls

No additional training is needed for these cells:

| control | construction | necessary interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `SLEEP_OFF` | unmount all adapters with identical visible state | S1-dependent B/C and total parametric contribution |
| `NO_SLEEP_2` | use the frozen `FULL_AUTH` S1 adapter at D | the new write is required |
| `WRONG_ROOT` | mount the next sealed root's same-condition adapter through the frozen slot mapping | life-specific rather than generic carrier behavior |
| `LINK_CUT` | mask only `(p1,p4)` or `(p3,p4)` in the raw read return | necessary child-organized connection |
| `OLD_CUT` | suppress the required old link only at D | delayed old necessity |
| `NEW_CUT` | suppress `n_h` only at D | delayed new necessity |
| `GOAL_TWIN` | same non-goal state/RNG, swap A/B goal bytes | query, path, and terminal action redirection |
| `SIGNAL_TWIN` | truthful `H<->J` old-signal world/carrier twin | old content redirects the selected experiment |
| `OUTCOME_VALUE_TWIN` | flip `h`, keeping prefix equal through dispatch | outcome, new row, and delayed action redirection |
| `CATALOG_PERMUTE` | semantics-preserving candidate/list permutation | no order/ID policy |
| `READ_PERMUTE` | same build, redirect one critical returned row | content rather than adapter presence |
| `GOAL_ONLY/CATALOG_ONLY` | matched padding, READs return `MISS` | direct prompt/menu shortcut ceiling |
| `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS` | exact FULL rows through the same reader, no LoRA | instrument/actor ceiling and text transport reference |

Every positive LoRA read needs one byte-exact receipt:

```text
public child event
-> admitted canonical semantic row
-> exact training target bytes
-> published adapter manifest
-> raw reader output
-> actor-visible bytes
-> cited action dependency
```

Semantic return substitution is forbidden in positive cells. Interventions
may alter bytes only in their named negative or truthful-redirection cell.

## 6. Noncompensatory root gates

For root `r`, set `R_r=1` only if **every** item below passes. A high final
score cannot compensate for a failed source, DREAM, read, or redirection seam.

1. **Root validity:** theorem, prefix-equality, visibility, alias balance,
   unique delayed proof, derangement, air-gap, and proxy audits pass.
2. **Authentic THINK source:** every critical atom is backed by a child ACT
   and causally later public outcome; no action/result is synthesized or
   success-selected.
3. **Pre-blueprint DREAM:** `ORGANIZE_SET` was sealed at A2, has no future
   taint, and includes the two critical links; later independent co-use
   events validate them. Missing/false proposals are adverse, not repaired.
4. **Writer/interface:** critical S1/S2 rows are extractable under their
   registered held views; canonical ACT validity and the unrelated-action
   panel stay within the W0 adverse bound; no control adapter causes generic
   interface collapse that could counterfeit specificity.
5. **Goal-conditioned traversal:** FULL solves both B goals with valid public
   dependency traces; `GOAL_TWIN` redirects first query/path/final action;
   `LINK_CUT`, DREAM_NULL, SLEEP_OFF, and wrong-root do not produce a valid
   trace. SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED must match FULL on the B path/link gates;
   otherwise the supposedly source-only control caused generic interference
   and cannot identify the source binding.
6. **Content-specific experiment:** FULL reads the raw mounted old signal,
   publishes the complete pre-outcome map, selects a maximum-information
   experiment, and obtains a public separating outcome. `SIGNAL_TWIN`
   redirects the experiment family. DREAM_NULL must match FULL through this
   experiment/new-row gate; otherwise DREAM removal also damaged the source
   channel. SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED must instead retrieve the swapped family
   and redirect toward its corresponding non-target experiment pair, while
   retaining a valid interface. The scorer/posterior is offline only.
7. **Truthful new row:** the compiler emits exactly the public declaration-
   selected row; `OUTCOME_VALUE_TWIN` redirects the row. No hidden truth or
   answer lookup is available to the compiler.
8. **Cumulative retention:** FULL S2 retains the registered critical old rows
   while adding the new row and still passes the B old-goal probe.
9. **Delayed conjunction:** FULL S2 produces the correct D trace/action;
   `NO_SLEEP_2`, `OLD_CUT`, `NEW_CUT`, DREAM_NULL, SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED,
   SLEEP_OFF, and wrong-root score zero; both truthful twins redirect rather
   than merely reduce behavior.
10. **Text ceiling:** `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS` completes the same chain with no
    LoRA and matches the LoRA-visible semantics exactly. If it cannot, the
    assay is invalid rather than evidence against LoRA.

The useful diagnostic contrasts are:

```text
S_r = relevant_experiment_value_C(FULL_AUTH)
      - relevant_experiment_value_C(SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED)
M_r = B(FULL_AUTH)      - B(DREAM_NULL)
W_r = D(FULL_AUTH_S2)   - D(NO_SLEEP_2)
F_r = D(FULL_AUTH_S2)   - max(D(OLD_CUT), D(NEW_CUT),
                               D(SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED), D(DREAM_NULL))
```

But the main datum is the conjunction `R_r`, not the largest component. Later
success never rescues an earlier zero.

## 7. Fatal shortcuts

Any one of these invalidates the affected root or, when structural, the whole
instrument:

1. selecting/regenerating roots after seeing the child's DREAM proposal;
2. exposing a support action/menu/arguments or later goal before A2;
3. allowing the compiler/renderer to add, repair, rank, or choose a link;
4. training on oracle, hidden-truth, score, future-goal, or answer bytes;
5. using a harness/evaluator action instead of the child's executed action;
6. placing the outcome before the action or admitting an unexecuted draft;
7. substituting an answer-bearing semantic row at READ in a positive cell;
8. deriving IDs/order/length/hit patterns from semantic roles or truth;
9. reusing one semantic ID for different bytes in a shuffle/twin/control;
10. allowing the read budget to enumerate every exchangeable first choice;
11. letting goal/action-menu text solve the task without carrier content;
12. mounting LoRA during planning/action and then attributing changed policy
    prose to memory carriage;
13. carrying raw events, DREAM prose, C outcome text, workspaces, caches, or
    query state across a sterile reset;
14. changing any path atom, calibration row, DREAM decision/link/support byte,
    Phase-C public outcome compiler, marginal, dose, row count, token-length
    class, or optimizer work in SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED—or changing anything
    except the DREAM link slots in DREAM_NULL;
15. interpreting generic damage by a corrupt adapter as binding specificity;
16. tuning fit seeds, rank, LR, render forms, prompts, or thresholds on M
    outcomes;
17. dropping, retrying, reparsing, replacing, or complete-case filtering a
    failed root;
18. counting twins, goals, calls, samples, paths, or checkpoints as independent
    roots;
19. calling a clean-base cumulative rebuild an in-place sequential expansion;
20. calling typed one-hop resolver use native search or internal graph
    traversal; or
21. allowing any parenting/final-gym descendant, report result, or other-root
    store to return into the fixed child or another scientific root.

## 8. Root stages and stopping rules

The stages are source-disjoint. No root moves from DEV into confirmation.

### Stage 0 — CPU closure, zero fits

Exhaustively instantiate at least 64 alias/twin fixtures and require all root
theorems, prefix laws, transforms, unique old/new cuts, proxy bounds, strict
parsers, carrier-origin mutations, and oracle-air-gap tests to pass. Run the
scripted explicit-memory positive and every fatal-shortcut mutant. Any
structural failure stops M.

### Stage 1 — exact-text model DEV, 4 then at most 8 roots

Run four fixed roots through the real child/DREAM/actor interface using
`TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS`. Stop if fewer than `2/4` complete the source + DREAM +
goal-redirection + experiment-redirection + delayed chain. If `2/4` or `3/4`
pass, extend to eight total; require at least `6/8`. If `4/4` pass, the builder
may advance directly. Any carrier-origin, future-taint, or proxy leak stops
regardless of count.

This stage diagnoses the task/interface and child-authored proposal before
paying for a LoRA. It is not paper evidence.

### Stage 2 — two-root LoRA kill gate

On two presealed DEV roots, make exactly six fixed-seed fits per root. Reuse
them for every inference-only cell. Both roots must have the expected positive
direction for authentic source, DREAM organization, first SLEEP, signal-twin
experiment redirection, second SLEEP, retention, and delayed old+new cuts.
One structural leak or two-root direction failure stops confirmation.

### Stage 3 — eight-root LoRA DEV ceiling

Add six disjoint DEV roots, for eight total. Freeze all rendering, fit,
interface, thresholds, root reduction, and resource arithmetic. Require at
least `6/8` joint `R_r=1`, zero fatal shortcuts, and a measured upper resource
bound. This is the last point at which mechanics may change.

### Stage 4 — 16-root confirmation

Generate sixteen fresh iid roots after the complete freeze and run all three
trained conditions and all mandatory inference controls. No extension,
replacement, or early success stop is allowed. Require at least `12/16`
joint-positive roots. This is the smallest binomial cohort that permits a 25%
root failure rate while rejecting `Pr(R_r=1) <= .5` with the exact one-sided
test (`P[X>=12 | n=16,p=.5] = .0384064`). Report the exact confidence interval,
all root vectors, all failures, and component contrasts regardless of pass.

Passing supports repeatable directional causal relay over the frozen root
distribution. It does not estimate an open-world population or validate the
lifetime flywheel.

## 9. Fit count and rough GPU cost

Let `t_fit` be the measured A40 time for one fixed-seed build using the actual
W0-qualified recipe on this exact M deck. The design uses:

```text
2-root kill gate:          2 roots * 3 conditions * 2 sleeps = 12 fits
8-root DEV total:          8 roots * 3 conditions * 2 sleeps = 48 fits
16-root confirmation:     16 roots * 3 conditions * 2 sleeps = 96 fits
total through confirmation                              = 144 fits
```

The current audited writer estimate is roughly `7.5--15 A40-minutes/fit` for
this scale. Training therefore costs about `18--36 A40-hours`. Source/DREAM,
exact-text, read/action probes, twins, cuts, clean resets, and artifact checks
plausibly add `8--20 A40-hours`, for a conservative all-in range of
**26--56 A40-hours** after CPU closure. On eight A40s this is roughly 3.5--7
hours of perfectly packed device time, but process startup and serial phase
dependencies will increase wall time. The two-root kill gate costs only about
`1.5--3` A40-hours of training and should cap total failed-design spend below
about five A40-hours.

If the qualified writer's measured `t_fit` lies outside this range, replace the
estimate mechanically with `144*t_fit`; do not shrink controls or roots to fit
the prose estimate. A writer that requires multiple candidate seeds multiplies
the cost and introduces a selection problem; M therefore uses exactly one
predeclared fit seed per root/condition and adverse-fills a rejection.

## 10. Claim boundary and handoff

If all gates pass, the strongest accurate sentence is:

> In a finite relational benchmark, a child's public actions and outcomes were
> compiled with a child-authored pre-blueprint organization into a per-root
> LoRA; a clean actor used the carried old content to redirect a later
> information-seeking action, and after a second cumulative SLEEP used one old
> connection plus the newly grounded row to redirect a delayed action.

Use “connected carriage,” “typed functional traversal,” and “two-cycle
compiler-mediated cumulative reconstruction.” Do not use “native graph
learning,” “persistent adapter expansion,” “autonomous scientific discovery,”
“compression,” or “self-improving lifetime” for M-core alone.

M-core closes the mechanism seam of the user's objective. It does not replace
the independently raised-child lifetime experiment. If M passes, the next
scientific GPU-hour belongs to L-core rather than more M forms, ranks, readers,
or writer recipes.
