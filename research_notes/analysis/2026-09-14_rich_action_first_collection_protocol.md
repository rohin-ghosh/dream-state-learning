## Separate execution, candidate collection, and fit admission

V1 remains zero admitted candidates and64failed episodes. No retrospective
relabeling or invented continuations. Its56literal-prediction-label stops do
not establish inability to complete the intended actions. Successor keeps
terminal/context/token checks, exact RATIONALE/ACTION envelope, a single exact
extracted command, legal environment transitions and six-turn/four-read/two-
route limits. Malformed or nonterminal output cannot execute.

Execute a valid action independently of rationale findings. Record missing
prediction label, unseen identifiers and generated guidance echoes as separate
content diagnostics; none certifies semantic correctness. Actual private
prompt contamination remains a source-integrity error, not merely a finding.
Request an explicit next-feedback expectation in natural language, optional
PREDICTION heading, uncertainty before reading and evidence-grounded routing.
No synonym whitelist or automatic truth classifier.

Action-complete means all six source-planned actions, actual four reads/two
routes and final goal. Retain raw candidate rows from complete episodes only;
all three views share exactly the same actual episode/call IDs. These are
UNREVIEWED CANDIDATES, never fit-ready. Content findings do not erase execution
denominators. Failed attempts and their real public histories remain preserved.

Before any fit, separately review every retained turn under the fixed floor:
- READ: a listed uninspected address, what is unknown, how inspection helps
  choose a path, and an expectation of retrieval rather than an invented edge.
- First ROUTE: connect observed CURRENT/chosen port/intermediate edge toward
  GOAL, distinguish alternatives, and predict the actual next hop, not final
  success prematurely.
- Final ROUTE: use actual preceding transition and the observed remaining
  edge to predict arrival at GOAL without inventing receipt bytes.
- All stages: no unsupported factual relation, hidden teacher echo or padding;
  a heading, length or first-person phrasing is not sufficient. Review against
  the pre-action public prefix, never using future feedback to justify a claim.

Pass/fail/unresolved with raw supporting spans; unresolved is excluded.
One failed turn excludes all six rows of that episode from ALL three fit
views. Content selection is shared, not a richer-arm-only advantage. Separate
content-review and fit protocol required; no automatic fitting in this run.
