## Decision

The smallest defensible ICLR paper is now a **necessary-condition paper about
the boundary between supplied parametric storage and selective personal
learning**. It is not the full Experience Models paper.

The only new experiment that is manuscript-critical is one prospectively
valid conditional-writer `Q0` terminal, followed without recipe search by the
same frozen terminal on two fresh independent roots/seeds. This is six
mandatory fits: two complementary-map fits per root across three roots. At
most one already-prebound diagnostic fit may be used on the first root; it
does not create a new recipe or an independent unit.

Everything downstream is optional for this deadline paper:

- the complete Level-1 birth and birth-disjoint Level-2 sample;
- equal-new-dose OLD/NEW replay;
- M-core zero-fit closure and every M fit;
- the five-branch lifetime study;
- the active-text comparison; and
- semantic or physical compression.

Rohin's birth-first steer remains the builder's research priority. It does not
change manuscript claim accounting: a direct birth adherence pass is still a
feasibility result unless its conditional Q0 slice is valid and the born child
shows a birth-disjoint Level-2 learning-gain advantage. The builder may embed
Q0 inside the birth sequence, but the paper must preserve Q0's paired controls,
independent roots, locality gates, and separate terminal classification.

This is a contraction of the submitted claim, not of the research program.
SEQ-118 improves the lower-rung paper but does not change this decision. It is
a carefully audited one-lineage fixed-total-budget allocation result. It
cannot replace a valid selective-writer result, a causal replay experiment, or
independent-root confirmation.

If a valid three-root Q0 classification is unavailable by the internal claim
freeze, the existing evidence still supports a scientifically honest
lower-rung abstract. It does not, in my judgment, support the intended ICLR
empirical submission strongly enough; the default should then be no-go rather
than presenting the architecture plan as a result.

## The one critical experimental path

### Q0 root 0: validity and classification

Use the already closed pair-balanced common-prefix Q0 contract. The two fits
start from identical clean initialization and differ only by the complete
complementary target map. A global action bias, mode-only rule, or tool-only
rule must be unable to satisfy the signed quartet.

The terminal must classify, in order and without compensation:

1. exact-train storage;
2. held-form extraction;
3. both complementary native-action maps under ordinary generation and
   candidate scoring;
4. absolute legal-action mass, not only within-pair preference;
5. non-cancelling itemwise legality and common-mode drift;
6. wrong-root, missing-mode, neighboring-ID, copy, arithmetic, and all frozen
   locality/spill panels; and
7. immutable replay from raw calls to the terminal reducer.

An invalid carrier, mutable seal, missing denominator, reducer loophole, or
unreplayable terminal is **no result**, not a failed writer.

### Immediate confirmation: two fresh roots

Once root 0 is valid, freeze its complete recipe and run exactly two fresh
root/optimizer-seed units with new opaque identities. Retain every failure;
do not replace a root, select a favorable seed, change the threshold, or carry
forward a repair chosen from confirmation outcomes.

The paper-facing classification is:

- **Selective supplied writer:** all three roots pass both complementary maps
  and every acquisition, extraction, locality, interface, and wrong-root
  conjunct.
- **Replicated storage-with-spill barrier:** all three roots pass exact and
  held acquisition but fail the same predeclared locality/interface conjunct.
- **Stored, not extractable:** exact storage passes but held extraction fails;
  locality is then not interpreted as the binding barrier.
- **Conditional acquisition failure:** exact-map acquisition itself fails;
  do not recast it as a selectivity null.
- **Heterogeneous recipe:** terminal subtypes differ across roots, or fewer
  than all roots pass the full conjunction. Report the failure-inclusive
  pattern; do not majority-vote a writer qualification.

The first valid failure subtype determines the paper branch. The prebound
relation-view or hard functional-null diagnostics may remain future mechanism
work, but they are not needed to make the frozen Q0 result interpretable and
must not delay the manuscript.

## Minimum units and statistics

### Narrow deadline paper

- Q0 uses **three independent root/optimizer-seed units**, two paired map fits
  per root. Map cells and prompt rows are nested repeated measurements.
- Report every per-root terminal subtype and all exact numerators and
  denominators. The primary release is the all-root noncompensatory
  conjunction, not an average score.
- Report an exact binomial interval for the descriptive root pass proportion
  and root-paired acquisition/locality deltas with their full range. With
  `n=3`, even `3/3` has a two-sided 95% Clopper--Pearson lower bound of about
  `.292`, and the smallest one-sided sign-test probability is `.125`.
  Therefore the result is replication over three frozen roots, not a
  population reliability or equivalence claim.
- Do not compute inferential uncertainty over prompts, facts, lexical views,
  maps, or checkpoints. They may support exact finite-panel gates only.
- Keep Sudoku and interleaving results at their existing seed-level `n=3` and
  SEQ-118 at lineage-level `n=1`. SEQ-118 receives no `p`-value or confidence
  interval suggesting independent fact trials.

### Level 1, if pursued later

One authored birth root is feasibility only. Direct adherence does not show a
learning disposition. The minimum distinguishing experiment is a
birth-disjoint Level-2 sample in which authentic-birth and matched routine-
control children receive identical task opportunities, feedback, parent
policy, compiler, and write dose. The root-level estimand is

```text
(AUTH post - AUTH pre) - (CONTROL post - CONTROL pre).
```

Promotion versus shadow within both birth arms is additionally required to
attribute parent/context-free persistence to SLEEP. Children/root blocks are
the independent units. No confirmation `N` is presently defensible; it must
be sized from blinded root-level DEV variance. Consequently Level 1 cannot be
a manuscript claim merely because one birth corpus passes its direct labels.

### M-core, if the full mechanism claim is retained later

The current candidate-free checklist is a gate, not evidence. Before any fit,
`Z0--Z6` must jointly close: deterministic packets, role visibility,
endogenous/copy-only source compilers, exact causal carrier arms,
candidate-free generative LoRA reads, shortcut/noninterference tests, and four
fixed exact-text roots. Q0 and an identity-disjoint OLD/NEW receipt must bind
the same renderer, grammar, and runtime.

The minimum staged M program is two DEV roots, S1-first (`6` fits); only if
both pass, S2 completes the two roots (`12` cumulative fits). A paper-facing M
claim then requires the fixed sixteen fresh confirmation roots (`96` maximum
fits), with failed roots retained as adverse zeros. Roots, not outcome twins,
goals, fits, queries, or checkpoints, are independent. Use the predeclared
failure-inclusive root conjunction, exact paired inference under the hidden
assignment, and an exact interval; DEV is never pooled with confirmation.

This scale and the still-open zero-fit contract make M noncritical for the
September paper. A two-root kill result is never confirmation.

### Lifetime

There is no defensible fixed confirmation root count yet. Repeated checkpoints
do not increase learner `N`. Any L claim needs five concurrent branches per
root, a root-level paired AUC interaction, absolute post-anchor slope,
retention, and a sample size selected from blinded mean-free DEV covariance
under a frozen cap. That cannot be reduced to a one-child deadline result.

## Strongest memory baseline

The strongest specified agent-memory baseline is `ACTIVE_TEXT_NATIVE`, not the
historical static brief, raw recency, or an exact lookup oracle. It starts
empty per root/branch, stores only lossless eligible public events and the
same admitted child-authored records, and uses target-blind BM25 followed by
declared-link personalized PageRank. It receives four charged reads, at most
1,024 returned tokens, the same native actor query/action surface, and no
parameter update.

Its prospective certificate uses exactly four source-disjoint roots and
requires perfect source/citation fidelity, no hidden/future/parent/wrong-root
rows, byte-identical restart and insertion-order invariance, donor-store
redirection, necessary-bridge drop at least `.40`, irrelevant-store effect at
most `.05`, strict legality at least `.95`, and complete resource receipts.
For a lifetime comparison it becomes the concurrent `P-TEXT` branch from the
same parented checkpoint with the childhood LoRA frozen and an empty text
store.

`TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS` is an assay-validity/exact-carrier ceiling, not a strong
competitor. The static brief is historical diagnostic evidence, not a strong
memory baseline.

No memory-system superiority sentence is needed in the narrow paper.
Therefore `ACTIVE_TEXT_NATIVE` is optional. If a text-baseline comparison is
added, its full implementation, certificate, roots, retrieval budget, and
endpoints must be frozen before seeing the paired LoRA outcomes and it must run
on the same confirmation roots. Otherwise omit the comparison.

## Abstract that survives each branch

Use the branch-stable title:

> **Beyond Parametric Storage: Causal Tests of Per-Life Learning in Acting
> Agents**

The stable empirical core is already supported:

> Per-life parametric learning requires more than fitting experience text: a
> writer must extract learned content under held forms, preserve prior behavior
> across writes, and alter only intended actions. We introduce a
> necessary-condition assay ladder for rank-8 adapters over a frozen
> instruction-tuned model, separating supplied-material transfer, fact
> storage, sequential coexistence, conditional native-action acquisition, and
> locality. Correct mini-Sudoku material exceeded an equal-marginal corrupt
> corpus by `+2,+2,+5` exact solves across three adapter-training seeds. With
> distinct-source interleaving and arithmetic rehearsal, a four-view 16-fact
> condition reached `15--16/16` exact and development recall, `45--48/48`
> held lexical probes, and `32/32` old-action accuracy in three optimizer
> lineages. In one prospectively interpreted sequence, replay retained `64/64`
> old-bank decisions versus `25/64` without replay while both learned the new
> bank at `32/32`; because current-new exposure and batch composition differed,
> this is fixed-budget allocation evidence rather than a pure replay effect.

Append exactly one terminal branch:

### A. All three Q0 roots pass

> Across three fresh roots, the prospectively frozen pair-balanced writer
> learned both complementary native-action maps under held renderings while
> satisfying every predeclared legal-action, wrong-root, copy, unrelated-task,
> and spill gate [manifest counts]. These finite results establish selective
> supplied-policy writing and replay-supported supplied-memory coexistence in
> the tested model and assays, not endogenous experience or lifetime learning.

### B. All three store and extract, but fail the same locality/interface gate

> Across three fresh roots, both complementary maps met exact and held
> acquisition criteria, but every root failed the same non-cancelling
> locality/interface conjunction [manifest counts]. Thus storage and
> extraction can coexist with an unsafe acting-agent write; selective,
> coexistence-preserving policy change is the binding failure of this recipe.

### C. Exact storage passes but held extraction fails

> Across three fresh roots, the frozen writer acquired the conditional map on
> exact training forms but failed the predeclared held-form extraction gate
> [manifest counts]. Arbitrary fact recall and training-form policy storage did
> not imply relation extraction under new renderings; locality is not
> interpreted as the limiting mechanism in this branch.

### D. Exact conditional acquisition fails

> Across three fresh roots, the frozen pair-balanced writer failed its exact
> conditional native-action acquisition gate [manifest counts], despite the
> separate supplied-fact storage positives above. The result localizes a gap
> between small arbitrary-fact storage and conditional action acquisition for
> this writer recipe; it is not a selectivity or lifetime null.

### E. Valid roots have mixed terminal subtypes

> Failure-inclusive evaluation across three fresh roots produced [exact
> subtype vector and counts], so the frozen recipe did not support a robust
> selective-writer qualification. We report the root heterogeneity directly;
> prompt cells are repeated measurements and no majority vote is used.

### F. Q0 is invalid, incomplete, or has fewer than three roots

Only the stable empirical core survives, followed by:

> These results separate feasible supplied storage and rehearsed coexistence
> from the still-open problem of selective, cumulative personal learning.

That is an honest lower-rung abstract, but it is the recommended **ICLR no-go**
branch because the prospective central writer result is absent. It must not be
filled with the one-root conditional stress diagnostic, a Level-1 feasibility
root, an M kill root, or historical lifetime data as if they were confirmation.

## Claim firewall

Under every branch above, omit parenting efficacy, learning-to-learn,
child-authored SLEEP, THINK/DREAM causal efficacy, connected traversal,
agentic expansion, general continual learning, increasing-lifetime
improvement, retention, text-memory superiority, saturation, and compression.
Add one only after its own prospectively frozen root-level confirmation gate
passes.

The clean scientific distinction for this deadline is:

```text
supplied material can matter
  + supplied facts can be stored/extracted
  + replay is useful under one fixed-budget allocation
  != selective personal learning
  != endogenous THINK--DREAM--SLEEP
  != a self-improving lifetime
```
