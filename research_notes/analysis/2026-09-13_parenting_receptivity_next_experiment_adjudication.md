## Evidence-bound verdict

The terminal fixed-coaching run already answers two upstream questions:

1. the lesson changes immediate child output in its named direction
   (`48/48` coached versus `38/48` neutral on prediction and relation); and
2. training child-authored faithful records teaches the narrow record-forming
   skill (`45/48` after either P or N write versus `27/48` before writing).

It does **not** show that the lesson-specific process became durable. P and N
were identical after parent removal in every root. That null is not a clean
negative: the postwrite panel was nearly saturated, admitted dose was unequal
(`344` versus `304` updates), and the record-only target erased the main
treatment difference. Every admitted P or N target had the same correct record
schema; the model was never trained on the coached pre-action operation itself.
Seed 2 also showed why field effects must remain separate: coaching repaired
all prediction/relation fields but produced five incorrect copied actions.

The cheapest next experiment is therefore the already-audited crossover in
`2026-09-13_minimum_level1_parenting_receptivity.md`, not another P/N record
fit. Make only one scientific change: promote its currently optional fresh
parent-free `PROCESS_USE` readout to a required durability gate. The result
then distinguishes:

```text
immediate ALIGNED steering only                         -> (a)
record carriage / record-form skill, no process reuse  -> (b)
fresh parent-free use of both taught operations        -> (c)
```

Category (b) already has direct support from the terminal P/N run; the new
run's source-withdrawn record reads retest carriage under the repaired writer.
Do not spend another fit merely to reproduce the saturated generic record
endpoint.

## Why not modify the current P/N design

Equalizing P/N to eight admitted records, adding replay, or lowering heat
would repair dose and retention, but would not repair identifiability. Its
SLEEP target contains only the faithful post-outcome record. Once admission
filters errors, coached and neutral targets express the same generic behavior;
the lesson, restatement, and coached processing are absent. A second P/N run
could qualify a safer record writer, but cannot tell whether a particular
lesson became a particular durable thinking operation.

ALIGNED / SWAPPED is the necessary immediate control because both siblings
receive the exact same P and C lesson bytes twice and solve identical task
bytes in identical order; only whether the lesson matches the next task
changes. NO_PARENT establishes that ALIGNED helped instead of SWAPPED merely
hurting. Its task-bound checker requires values absent from the lesson, so
copying warm prose cannot pass.

Do not train SWAPPED in this minimum test. It exists to identify immediate
lesson specificity. The causal bridge is then: matched lesson alignment
changes the rate of authentic child process continuations; only the first
eight prospectively ordered ALIGNED continuations are written; and the
written child is tested against its unchanged tensor-identical root. A
trained SWAPPED control would double fits while its likely material shortage
would reintroduce unequal dose.

## Frozen experiment, with only the required change

Keep the repaired prospective design unchanged:

- three original pre-memory Level-1 perception roots; all branches and
  descendants quarantined from final children and every later parenting or
  deployment corpus;
- two answer-free skills: prior/outcome separation (P) and latest
  same-receipt chronology (C);
- identical task bytes/order and identical repeated lesson multiset for
  ALIGNED and SWAPPED, plus the ordinary NO_PARENT anchor;
- parent removed before application; only a child-authored restatement NOTE
  enters the next task;
- exact deterministic `PROCESS_USE`, strict-action, and faithful-record
  checks; no repaired action or substituted row;
- exactly the first eight qualifying ALIGNED rows/root, requiring eight
  addresses and at least four semantic targets;
- two exact child targets per row: the pre-action process/action continuation
  and the post-outcome record continuation;
- parent and restatement bytes never receive loss;
- rank 8, LR `3e-5`, eight epochs, batch 4, and all 96 original perception
  rows replayed beside the 16 new child continuations: exactly 224 updates per
  eligible root; and
- fresh-process source-withdrawn exact/paraphrase reads, itemwise old-skill
  retention, and generic canary after SLEEP.

This directly addresses all terminal defects: fixed new-row dose removes the
P/N admission imbalance; process targets preserve the treatment's meaningful
child-owned consequence; replay addresses the lower-LR result's residual
`3/143` inherited-skill losses; strict action admission prevents seed-2-style
wrong actions from becoming targets; and clean original roots prevent gym or
prior-writer contamination.

## Exact stage gates and terminal labels

### Gate 1 — immediate specific receptivity (no fit before it passes)

Keep the existing prewrite gate exactly:

- correct ALIGNED restatement after at least `3/4` contacts in at least two
  roots;
- `PROCESS_USE(ALIGNED)-PROCESS_USE(SWAPPED) >= 4/16` in at least two roots,
  with no root `<= -4/16`;
- `PROCESS_USE(ALIGNED)-PROCESS_USE(NO_PARENT) >= 2/16` in at least two roots,
  with no root below NO_PARENT by more than `2/16`;
- second delivery nondegrading in at least two roots; and
- at least two roots yield `>=8/16` ALIGNED `FULL_MATERIAL` rows.

Failure stops all fits and is reported as `IMMEDIATE_SPECIFIC_RECEPTIVITY_FAIL`.
No writer can rescue missing teaching uptake.

### Gate 2 — replay-protected one-SLEEP carriage

Keep the existing per-root gate: exact and paraphrase semantic recall each
`>=4/8`, unchanged root `<=1/8` on each; at least four distinct addressed
targets recovered; at most two inherited C0-correct items lost; and zero
generic-canary losses. Require at least two roots.

If Gate 1 passes but Gate 2 fails, report
`IMMEDIATE_ONLY__WRITER_OR_CARRIAGE_FAIL`. Do not change dose or select a seed.

### Gate 3 — lesson-specific durability (promoted from optional)

Use the already budgeted eight fresh parent-free homologous inputs for the
written child and unchanged root: four P and four C, prebound before formation.
Each gets one pre-action generation, no lesson, restatement, source transcript,
ledger, retrieval state, or outcome feedback. The ordinary instruction asks
only for a relevant-state NOTE and action; it does not name comparison or
recency. Score the same deterministic task-bound `PROCESS_USE` predicates.

Call a root `DURABLE_PROCESS_USE` only if:

- written minus root is at least `+2/8` overall;
- written is not worse in either four-item family; and
- at least one additional exact success occurs in **each** of P and C.

Require `DURABLE_PROCESS_USE` in at least two roots, with no root at
`written-root <= -2/8`. This is a small DEV gate, not a population estimate.

Interpret the complete ladder noncompensatorily:

- Gate 1 only: `(a) coaching changes immediate output`;
- Gates 1-2 but not 3: `(b) child-owned lesson-use records can be carried,
  but only generic/record-level persistence is shown`;
- Gates 1-3: `(c) two distinct lesson-specific processes survive parent
  removal and one SLEEP sufficiently to change fresh child processing`.

Even Gate 3 does not establish better final actions, adaptive parenting,
connected memory, recurrence, or lifetime learning. Those remain PCFL/H2.

## Cost and stop ruling

The existing design already includes the 48 calls used by promoted Gate 3, so
its cap remains **636 child calls, at most three fits, 672 updates, zero parent
model calls, and <=5 aggregate A40-hours**. Run all inference-only ALIGNED /
SWAPPED / NO_PARENT material first; only then decide whether any fit opens.

Stop and preserve on any clean-lineage/hash mismatch, parent/world-value leak,
task ambiguity, parser/world disagreement, parent or restatement bytes in a
supervised label, target truncation, nonfinite fit, replay schedule mismatch,
retention failure, ownership/release failure, or resource-cap breach. Never
replace a failed root or action, choose later favorable rows, loosen a gate,
increase dose, or feed post-SLEEP results back into the child or parent.

**Single recommendation:** execute the repaired crossover with Gate 3 made
mandatory. Do not rerun a cosmetically balanced version of P/N; it cannot
carry the semantic distinction the next experiment is meant to test.
