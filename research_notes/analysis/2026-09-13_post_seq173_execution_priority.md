## Scope firewall

The EVENT-only result must not replace the failed LINK question.  SEQ-171--173
show that this child can emit admitted EVENT rows under an external format
constraint but does not yet reliably serialize a child-selected relation
between them.  The next authentic formation must therefore accept every
publicly valid LINK pair in any child-chosen order and bind the bank actually
formed.  It must not disclose a chosen pair or require one hidden order.  Only
that repair can feed the two-root connected vertical.

Likewise, C0 is a researcher-supplied ceiling.  A C0 pass establishes that the
actor/interface can traverse supplied connected memory; it does not establish
own-experience writing or LoRA learning.  The paper's central chain still
requires both branches to meet later: authentic child EVENT/LINK formation ->
compiled write -> cold use/cuts/retention -> increasing-lifetime comparison
against evolving text and final-batch controls.

## Stop rules

- Do not fit the old incomplete twelve-row/full-bank target.
- Do not count imported EVENT addresses as independent lives.
- Do not launch native C0 from the current partial core if exact v2 bytes,
  joint gates, A4 eligibility, prebinding, or custody joins are absent.
- A failure on either branch is a localized result; it cannot be rescued by a
  positive result on the other branch.

This ordering maximizes information per GPU-hour without narrowing the final
objective: one branch tests whether the actor can *use* connected knowledge,
the other whether the writer can *carry* authentic atomic experience, while
the dynamic-LINK repair remains the bridge required by the paper.
