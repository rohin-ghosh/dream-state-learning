# Next-24-hour gate order: one plasticity sentinel, then conditional writing

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** independent design adjudication; no code change or launch.  
**Decision horizon:** the 24 hours after the active level-zero short-row versus
long-context repetition sentinel becomes terminal.

## Decision

Do **not** run the full 8-A40-hour dose/persistence protocol before the
input-selective writer qualification. Stage exactly one new material root:
its four phase-A trajectories and, only if acquisition qualifies, its seven
predeclared phase-B histories. Do not expand to roots 2--3 before W-H1.

The reason is causal, not merely economical. Phase B estimates survival of an
already acquired global response-order habit through later updates. H1 is
currently blocked one rung earlier: arbitrary family x mode binding has not
been shown at all. The one-exposure fact bank was at chance and collapsed to
priors even on its training surface, while the global `PREDICT` ritual was
perfect. Persistence of that ritual cannot qualify a conditional writer. The
exact W-H1 AUTH/SWAP assay can. One within-root continuation sentinel is still
worth buying first because it directly tests whether the installed behavior
remains plastic while another behavior is learned, without paying for
population inference about a writer that may fail conditional binding.

The highest-information order is therefore:

```text
terminal short/long sentinel
  -> one-root phase-A acquisition slice
  -> one-root phase-B plasticity sentinel, conditional on acquisition
  -> qualified level-1 birth state
  -> W-H1 seed-0 sentinel
  -> W-H1 seeds 1 and 2
  -> excluded H1 executor sentinel
  -> 16-root H1 confirmation
  -> roots 2--3 persistence expansion before repeated-SLEEP/H2 claims
```

No downstream pass may compensate for an upstream failure.

## Stage 0 — close the active sentinel

Treat the four short/long cells as valid only if all expected rows, updates,
loss masks, adapter hashes, reloads, and readout calls are present; loss is
finite; no target is truncated; valid single-action rate is at least `.95`;
and correct arithmetic action remains at least `30/32` in every cell.

For the taught response habit, a usable cell additionally requires taught
adherence at least `30/32` and its matched task-only control at most `2/32`.
For the 16 balanced fact bindings, call a state acquired only at `>=12/16`,
with every colour represented in its predictions and no interface score more
than `.05` below OFF. These are noncompensatory; a perfect habit cannot rescue
failed binding.

Interpret the outcomes prospectively:

- If short and long both meet the same endpoints, choose short rows as the
  simpler candidate and do not spend GPU time resolving a null packaging
  contrast.
- If only one layout meets the fact and interface gates, carry that layout
  forward as a *development candidate*. It does not qualify conditional
  writing.
- If neither layout acquires facts, record failure at 64 identical-copy
  presentations. This is evidence against repetition alone, not evidence
  against the exact H1 surface.
- Any invalid/missing cell makes the sentinel operationally inconclusive.
  Repair and rerun only that frozen sentinel version; do not use the surviving
  favorable cells.

## Stage 1 — one-root acquisition and plasticity sentinel

Use exactly one new material root and the four phase-A trajectories already
specified: `TEACH` and `TASK_ONLY` at `3e-4` and `1e-4`, with nested A1/A4/A16
checkpoints. This root is needed even if Stage 0 is favorable: the active
sentinel changes context packaging on old development material, whereas this
root supplies an independently generated start and an explicit dose curve for
the continuation comparison.

A recipe is usable for later development only if, at A16 in that root:

- habit: `TEACH >=15/16`, `TASK_ONLY <=1/16`, and correct action `>=15/16`;
- binding: `>=12/16` on both canonical and unseen wording, with each
  colour recall `>=.50` and valid single actions `>=.95`;
- interface: no native/interface endpoint is worse than OFF by more than
  `.05`; and
- all token, step, initialization, reload, and scorer receipts pass.

Phase B launches only from the fixed `TEACH, 3e-4, A4` checkpoint and only if
that state has habit adherence `>=15/16`, correct action `>=15/16`, validity
`>=.95`, and no interface endpoint more than `.05` below OFF. Facts enter its
retention analysis only if A4 already scores `>=12/16` on canonical and unseen
wording. A16 is still completed for the registered dose curve, but cannot be
substituted as the phase-B start after outcomes.

If the A4 habit gate passes, run exactly that root's seven registered phase-B
histories: `QUIET`, plus `SAME`, `UNRELATED`, and `MIXED` at both `1e-4` and
`3e-5`, through B1/B4/B16. Do not prune an active history for an unfavorable
scientific score; stop it only at the registered action/interface validity
failure below `.95`. The one-root phase-B sentinel is interpretable only if:

- QUIET weights are byte-identical and no more than one old habit item changes;
- in at least one UNRELATED setting, the new habit is `>=13/16`, new facts are
  `>=12/16`, and action/interface validity is `>=.95`; and
- exact update, target-token, replay-share, reload, and scorer receipts pass.

Among settings that actually learn the new material, a continuation recipe is
usable only if old habit adherence at B16 is at least `.90` of its A4 value,
with action/interface validity `>=.95`. If facts qualified at A4, they also
require `>=12/16` held accuracy and at least `.75` of their starting
correct-colour probability gain. Prefer `3e-5` only if it preserves more old
signal while satisfying the same new-learning gate; failure to learn is not
stability.

If qualified UNRELATED learning destroys the habit at both rates and both
MIXED histories fail the retention/interface gates, the current recipe is a
no-go for repeated SLEEP. This does **not** block the one-cycle W-H1/H1 assay;
it blocks recurrence/H2 and records that later updates erase the write. If no
UNRELATED history learns the new material, the plasticity sentinel is
uninformative and no continuation setting is promoted.

If both phase-A rates pass, the lower acquisition rate is the default only
when it meets every endpoint; no `2/16` or larger benefit from the higher rate
on either binding form and no-harm endpoint may be surrendered. If no state
reaches even `8/16` canonical facts at A16, close generic fact acquisition as
null at this registered dose. This redirects later level-1 design to the
conditional objective or representation; it does not license extra epochs or
post-hoc phrasings.

Hard-cap the complete one-root phase-A/phase-B sentinel at **3 aggregate
A40-hours**. If result-blind profiling cannot fit it, re-freeze rather than
drop a history, checkpoint, control, or readout. It selects a development
recipe and signs; it is not root-level replication, a persistence claim, or
the H1 release gate.

## Stage 2 — W-H1 is the next decisive GPU experiment

W-H1 must use the exact proposed H1 action tokens, renderer, objective, dose,
initialization, replay mixture, birth state, and held readout. First run the
registered four-update opposite-sign canary. It passes only if both inputs
move their correct action log odds in opposite intended directions, neither
wrong action moves more in the same direction, and all four updates and
reload receipts are exact. A canary failure stops W-H1.

Then fit one disposable AUTH/SWAP pair at optimizer seed 0. Expand to seeds 1
and 2 only if seed 0 independently satisfies every W-H1 criterion:

- exact-training balanced accuracy `>=.90` for AUTH and SWAP;
- held balanced accuracy `>=.80` for each write;
- each mode recall and each action recall `>=.75`;
- valid single actions `>=.95`;
- paired own-map redirection `B >= .40`;
- TV from OFF `<=.05` for every unsupported/unexposed control family, and
  unrelated/interface performance no worse than OFF by `.05`; and
- exact target-token, initialization, step, reload, corpus, scorer, and world
  receipts all pass.

Seeds 1 and 2 use unchanged bytes and gates. W-H1 passes only if **all three
seeds** pass every item. No averaging, majority vote, favorable seed, or
strong training-surface score can compensate for a held, binding, locality,
or interface failure. Use a result-blind measured budget; impose a **2
aggregate A40-hour ceiling** for all six fits and their readouts. Exceeding
that ceiling is a design/profile failure, not permission to omit controls.

Failure localization changes the next work directly:

- training-surface failure -> objective, optimizer, or effective-dose repair;
- training pass but held failure -> renderer/cue-generalization repair;
- held accuracy without `B` -> action-prior/global-habit failure;
- `B` pass with spill/interface failure -> locality/replay-mixture repair;
- all three seeds pass -> freeze this exact writer version; no H1 tuning.

## Stage 3 — H1 only after both independent dependencies close

W-H1 is necessary but not sufficient. H1 still requires the independently
qualified, clean, task-naive level-1 birth state and the complete CPU
action-to-receipt-to-child-artifact-to-loss-target-to-fresh-readout fixture.
If either is absent, spend the remaining 24-hour window closing it; do not run
a base-only H1 under the existing name.

Once both are terminal, run exactly one permanently excluded H1 executor
sentinel, capped at 6 A40-hours. It is an operational gate only: all calls,
blindness, grounding joins, child-only source spans, AUTH/SWAP collation,
state deletion, adapter reconstruction, and fresh-process readouts must pass.
Its behavioral direction must not decide whether confirmation launches. An
infrastructure repair creates a new version and a new excluded sentinel.

Only then launch the 16 untouched paired roots. The H1 intersection claim
passes only if all original gates plus the adversarial schema gate pass:

- schema validity in both P and S is `>=.90`, with absolute P-S gap `<=.05`;
- the paired 95% root interval for `q` is above zero, mean `q>=.10`, P STORE
  coverage `>=.70`, P-S coverage `>=.10`, and P NULL accuracy `>=.75` and no
  more than `.05` below S;
- the paired 95% interval for `g` is above zero, mean `g>=.10`, P-AUTH
  balanced accuracy `>=.75`, each mode `>=.70`, and OFF `<=.625`;
- the paired 95% interval for `theta` is above zero, mean `theta>=.10`, mean
  P binding `B>=.20`, AUTH and SWAP each gain over OFF on their own map, and
  at least 12/16 roots have `q>0`, `g>0`, and `B[P]>0`;
- valid P-AUTH actions are `>=.95`, unrelated/interface loss is at most `.05`,
  every spill-family mean TV is `<=.05`, and all fit/custody gates pass.

Result consequences are fixed: `q` failure redirects to parenting/artifact
formation; `q` pass with P-TEXT failure redirects to artifact usability;
P-TEXT pass with `g/B` failure redirects to rendering/SLEEP; gain without
redirection or with spill is a broad habit; equal P and S success establishes
child-authored writing but not causal parenting; only the full intersection
closes the bounded H1 bridge.

## Where the 8-hour persistence study belongs

The complete three-root acquisition/persistence protocol remains well
designed, but its remaining roots become high-value only after conditional
writing exists. Expand roots 2--3 before any population claim about repeated
SLEEP, aging/plasticity, or H2 continual learning, preferably using the writer
recipe frozen by W-H1. The one-root signs may choose a candidate; they cannot
replace the three-root analysis. Its decisive failure condition remains:
qualified unrelated learning destroys the old habit even at `3e-5`, while
MIXED fails to preserve at least `.90` of starting habit adherence through
B16 without more than `.05` interface harm. That is a no-go for repeated
SLEEP, not for the one-cycle H1 bridge.

Thus the next 24 hours should buy one plasticity sign, then the missing
conditional edge. Spending the full 8 A40-hours to estimate persistence of a
global ritual before proving input-selective binding would be precise
measurement of the wrong bottleneck.
