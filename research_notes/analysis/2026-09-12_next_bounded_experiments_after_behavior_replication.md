## Verdict

The new evidence separates **semantic usefulness** from **memory
selectivity**:

- Correct mini-Sudoku material beat equal-format corrupted material at every
  one of three optimizer seeds. Exact-solve difference-in-differences were
  `+2/16`, `+2/16`, and `+5/16`; useful adapters solved `2/16`, `3/16`, and
  `5/16`, versus corrupt `0/16`, `1/16`, and `0/16`, with every OFF at
  `0/16`. Thus content—not merely the `ACT` surface—can affect a later first
  action. Absolute success remains weak, the data are external-oracle, and
  this is not SLEEP or selective memory.
- On the fixed oracle-frame corpus, reducing learning rate from `1e-4` to
  `3e-5` or `1e-5` did not create locality. Frame spill remained `.394` and
  `.296` against the `.03` gate, and both fits remained `frame-habit` writers.
  Thus the bottleneck is not simply excessive heat.

The highest-information order is therefore:

1. finish and reduce the already-running prefix-mask diagnostic, with no
   automatic follow-on;
2. run semantic W0's fresh carrier gate and, only if it passes, its four
   conditional writer fits;
3. construct and test authentic S from mechanically joined child
   action--public-outcome events, first without a write, then as one

This is a dependency order, not a serial schedule. S source acquisition and

## 3. Authentic S is the next thesis-bearing arrow

The source must be the ledger's authoritative join, not a child's prose claim:

```text
raw child output -> one strict parsed action -> engine input
                 -> causally later public outcome
```

The failed first-person NOTE route and its coached replay establish that
narration syntax is a poor source prerequisite. The raw-wake exporter is a
useful package-level diagnostic, but its targets contain unverified prose and
lesson/sham token-dose differences. It must not displace S or be called
action--outcome learning.

The bounded S sequence should be:

1. Import one immutable W0 action-surface specification. Generate two fresh
   source roots without a parent, reward, answer, or evaluator action. Require
   the child to produce both actions twice in each of eight opaque situations
   under a fixed 16-opportunity schedule. If either root lacks the exact 32
   replayable joined events, stop as `S_SOURCE_INVALID`; never force, top up,
   or replace the root.
2. After sealing events, derive TRUE and a within-situation derangement whose
   action/outcome marginals, event IDs, renderings, masks, target counts, and
   dose match while their conditional relation is destroyed. Prove TRUE joint
   oracles are `1.0`, SHUFFLED joint oracles `.50`, and every registered
   nuisance shortcut `.50`.
3. Run the no-write GOLD/TEXT forward and inverse assay. This tests whether the
   world, join, renderer, corruption, retrieval, and native action surface are
   usable before blaming LoRA. It can complete provisionally while W0 runs.
4. Only after the exact pair carrier **and W0 writer** pass, fit TRUE and
   SHUFFLED for the predeclared primary root. Require later goal-conditioned
   inverse action and forward outcome prediction to favor TRUE, alongside
   legality, deletion, wrong-root, spill, interface, provenance, and replay
   gates. One root is a kill gate; three roots are feasibility; eight frozen
   roots are needed for the proposed source-facing claim.

This is the first experiment that directly tests whether **the child's own
action--public-outcome relation**, rather than an oracle dataset or generic
format, later changes an action.
