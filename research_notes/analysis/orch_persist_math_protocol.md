joint answer+valid-nontrivial-record successes, corrected joint successes,
tasks with prior records, realized prompt/generated/prose tokens and native
calls. Rich win is a **measured screen gap**, not installed behavior. This

reasons for factual grounding, goal connection and checkable expectation.

on heldL1validation, not more dose on the null. W1 alone cannot establish
memory causality, retention, transfer, or a learning slope.
