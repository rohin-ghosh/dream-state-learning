## Bounded interpretation

The positive result is infrastructural: this root proved that exact,
publicly-supported child record bytes can be admitted, response-only trained
into finite LoRA candidates, mounted through the declared native route, and
carried through two complete cumulative clean-base refits without corrupting
the interface.

The scientific readout is null. Despite large loss reduction, no mounted
candidate changed a greedy report response, old-key recall, second-wake action,
or subsequent semantic training material. The SLEEP2 branches therefore
collapsed back to the same effective training problem and the same final
tensor bytes. At the measured interface, this is **fit without extractable
conditional memory**.

This does not prove that the adapters stored no probability-level signal:
logits were not retained, and the report wording was a small paraphrase of the
training/wake wording rather than an exact-view readback. It does prove that
any such signal was too weak or too poorly transferable to alter the frozen
greedy interface. One root and one deterministic seed cannot establish a
population effect.

## Consequence for the authentic vertical

L2 should not be used as evidence that the learning loop works, and it does
not justify changing or delaying the already independently passed vertical
protocol. Its failure mode instead reinforces choices already present there:
separate atom/link extraction from native use, use candidate-free controlled
readouts, retain atom-only and link-structure controls, and demand a real
mounted-versus-cut behavioral gap before making a mediation claim. An exact-
training-view recognition diagnostic may be useful as an absorption localizer,
but it must remain diagnostic and cannot rescue a failed task-relevant native
endpoint.
