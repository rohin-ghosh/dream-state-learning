# ICLR area-chair audit: evidence ceiling and shortest decisive sequence

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Role:** skeptical, independent paper-evidence judge  
**Evidence cut:** repository state through the 17:45 UTC level-1 red-team
entry in `research_loop/COORDINATION.md`  
**Scope:** read-only scientific judgment. This memo changes no builder source,
experiment, model, tokenizer, adapter, job, GPU allocation, claim, or release.

## Area-chair verdict

The repository does not yet support a Dream--LoRA--Think or developmental
flywheel claim. It supports a narrower prerequisite story: LoRA writes can
alter behavior and carry a supplied routine or binding under some controlled
conditions, but the current experience writers are unstable, commonly spill
to unsupported cues, and sometimes destroy the action interface. The repaired
RuleGame harness can now execute and preserve real action/outcome events, but
it has not produced enough qualified child-authored teaching material to fit
or evaluate. No experiment yet closes the required causal path

```text
adaptive teaching
  -> better child-authored, grounded DREAM material
  -> selective SLEEP write
  -> parent-free behavior on task-disjoint cases
  -> faster later learning because SLEEP continues.
```

That missing mediation, rather than another lifetime-format comparison or a
larger childhood, is the paper bottleneck.

The current open-loop level-1 corpus is a useful operational scout, not H1. It
can become the *calibration immediately before H1* if it is upgraded to the
predeclared true-versus-complementary-binding design. It cannot become H1 by
adding seeds, epochs, or a stronger score: it contains no parent, no child-
authored experience, no DREAM decision, and no SLEEP cycle.

The shortest defensible route is therefore one gated sequence:

1. exact-surface opposing-input writer canary;
2. true-versus-complementary open-loop level-1 teaching and plasticity;
3. one parent-versus-yoked-control, child-authored H1 bridge with material
   swaps, SLEEP-OFF, and sterile parent removal;
4. only if H1 passes, one childhood-by-adult-SLEEP five-arm lifetime study
   containing the strong evolving-text arm;
5. only if the abstract retains connected/delayed experiential knowledge,
   release the already preserved M-core kill gate after the writer passes.

An H1 result can make a focused developmental-internalization paper. H2 and a
strong-text comparison make the flywheel paper. Connected traversal requires
the separate authenticated delayed-goal evidence. Semantic or physical
compression is not presently measured and must be omitted unless a specific
rate--distortion test is added.

The table distinguishes an implemented mechanism from evidence that the
mechanism produces the intended scientific effect.

The present maximum paper sentence is therefore:

> Controlled per-life LoRA updates can carry supplied behaviors and
> associations, but the tested experience-to-training recipes are unstable,
> nonselective, or interface-destructive; a grounded interaction layer now
> executes correctly, while developmental internalization and faster
> self-learning remain open.

Anything stronger currently collapses separate component observations into a
causal chain that was never executed.

## 2. Evidence-quality constraints that cannot be wished away

### Development is not confirmation

The CompilerGym report panel is repeatedly used and, for gated R4 lives,
lineage-selecting. The disjoint panel is source-disjoint but was researcher-
selected for headroom and repeatedly inspected. The Mini-Sudoku attempt-2
panel contains four IDs with prior model readout exposure. These are useful
development diagnostics, not fresh confirmation.

Optimizer seeds on one corpus and one panel measure fit instability. They are
not independent tasks, learners, or experimental replications. Prompt items,
episodes, checkpoints, generations, and actions cannot be pooled to inflate
`n`; the independent child/root is the unit for developmental claims.

### The exposure registry is now a mandatory input

The pre-attempt-2 Mini-Sudoku model-exposure union is 153 IDs: 137 with model
generation and 16 with fit-only exposure. Attempt 2 adds 12 new IDs, for a
successor union of 165; `1900070..1900087` must be excluded. CPU-only and
metadata-only IDs must remain distinguished from model exposure even if a
conservative selector excludes them too.

A final panel requires a hash-bound semantic exposure ledger and the exact
bare selector list consumed by preparation. Missing or default registry input
must fail closed. Replacing favorable exposed items after outcomes would not
repair the old root; it creates a new development root.

### Clean lineage is directional

A classroom child can be copied into a disposable deployment evaluation. A
deployment-exposed child, its rows, its parent state, and any selection choice
informed by final-gym outcomes cannot return to childhood. Mechanism changes
fork a new lineage; they do not rewrite the child already evaluated. This is
especially important for an adaptive parent: the parent may improve through
its permitted developmental history, but its update policy and information
boundary must be frozen before confirmation, and it must never see sealed
scores or answers.

### Content sensitivity is not useful learning

A positive useful-versus-corrupt difference can arise because corrupt material
causes more harm. A claim of useful carriage requires all three directions:

1. useful ON beats its own OFF;
2. useful ON beats corrupt ON;
3. the difference of ON--OFF gains is positive.

The attempt-2 result has that descriptive direction on a mixed-exposure panel.
Future tests must preregister it and use fresh roots.

### Fluent thought is not mediation

More `PREDICT`, `REFLECT`, or `LESSON` words can be a global ritual. A valid
bridge needs a condition-selective decision and downstream action. Child prose
is an artifact to test, not privileged access to cognition. The environment
may verify cited events and arithmetic truth; it must not author the lesson or
silently choose the only useful memory for the child.

## 4. The exact missing mediation

The paper needs four independently visible edges in the same prospective root:

```text
PARENTING EDGE
P versus dose-/contact-matched yoked control
    -> rate and quality of the CHILD'S grounded lesson-or-null decisions

WRITER EDGE
same childhood state and same child artifact
    -> SLEEP ON versus SLEEP OFF after clean reload

CONTENT EDGE
authentic child artifact versus binding-deranged artifact at matched dose
    -> trigger-correct and contraindication-correct behavior

OWNERSHIP EDGE
parent, lesson, transcript, notes, text store, caches, and retrieval deleted
    -> child produces/uses the behavior on fresh task-disjoint cases
```

H1 is the conjunction of these edges, not any one of them. H2 adds:

```text
LIFETIME EDGE
[(parented RUN - parented FROZEN)
 - (unparented RUN - unparented FROZEN)] in learning-curve area/slope > 0.
```

The current project has observations adjacent to each edge, but none binds all
of them within one treatment lineage.

## 5. Shortest decisive experiment sequence

### Stage 0 — exact-surface opposing-input writer canary

Use the exact native chat prefix, exact two legal action strings, tokenizer,
pairwise scorer, loss mask, LoRA target modules/rank family, and optimizer that
the next test will use. Preseal two equal-format target-independent inputs
whose correct actions are opposite. Starting from bound identical tensors and
RNG state, execute four real updates and record FP32 common-prefix raw
log-odds after each update and after clean reload.

**Pass:** both inputs move in their respective target-correct, opposite
directions on the jointly normalized score; the effect survives reload; no
constant-action/common-mode shift can satisfy the rule; receipts bind input
bytes, token IDs, initial/final tensors, optimizer state, RNG, and scorer.

**No-go:** failure stops this writer family before any larger lifetime,
parenting, M-core, or H2 run. A generation-only or different-surface pass does
not release it.

This is a few updates, not a benchmark. It buys the right to interpret later
binding results.

### Stage 1 — favorable open-loop level-1 writer/plasticity qualification

Use the independent red-team protocol in
`2026-09-12_level1_teaching_corpus_plasticity_redteam_protocol.md`, not the
current addition-only arm as the decisive test:

- 32 operand pairs, each rendered under two nonce modes;
- one mode requires ADD and the other SUB, so paired inputs require opposite
  actions;
- T contains the true mode and 16 device/color bindings;
- W swaps mode meanings and deranges colors within quartets while preserving
  prompts, response/token marginals, dose, and batch geometry;
- OFF is the frozen base;
- rank 8, four epochs, batch 4, fixed order; LR `{3e-4,1e-4}` crossed with
  optimizer seeds `{0,1,2}`;
- a four-adapter seed-0 sentinel, then 8 or 12 total adapters only for
  predeclared eligible rates; confirmation remains sealed until expansion.

The current `fundamental_teaching_corpus.py` is deterministic and its 11 CPU
tests independently pass. It remains a low-information scout because both
arithmetic arms use addition, all actions are the correct sum, the only main
difference is global PREDICT-before-ACT order, and the color bank is identical.
Even perfect token equality would not repair those scientific shortcuts.

**Development promotion:** retain an LR only when T and W acquire their own
maps, redirect opposite-action margins in the registered directions, preserve
the interface, and show held-form behavior. Development may select the
predeclared LR; it may not change corpus bytes, forms, dose, thresholds, or
confirmation cases.

**Paper-grade level-1 pass:** each of the three seed blocks satisfies the
predeclared red-team gates: train-map acquisition at least `.90`; held
arithmetic balanced accuracy at least `.80`, at least `.75` within key/form,
at least 12/16 opposite-action pairs, and T-minus-W true-map advantage at least
`.25`; own-map color recall at least 12/16 with T-minus-W true-binding
advantage at least `.25`; at least 15/16 unknowns, 8/8 exact action copies,
unrelated validity at least `.95`, zero multiple-ACT outputs, and mean
unsupported-family TV/legal-action drift at most `.05`.

**Interpretation:** a full pass proves that this authored corpus and writer can
install a narrow conditional routine and nonce facts outside the teaching
form. It selects the writer/plasticity operating point for H1. It is not DREAM,
SLEEP over experience, parenting, or H1.

**Can level 1 become H1?** Yes only operationally: keep its exact scorer,
surface, writer, and selected plasticity, then replace the teacher-authored
training rows in Stage 2 with grounded child-authored rows caused by a parent.
The causal claim begins there. Do not count Stage 1 itself as part of the H1
treatment estimate or seed the H1 treatment arm alone with its corpus. If a
birth adapter is necessary, give the identical frozen birth adapter to every
H1 arm and teach a distinct H1 competency.

### Stage 2 — the one H1 bridge worth running

Use one simple verifiable nursery family and one competency: after a real
prediction, action, and outcome, decide whether there is a reusable scoped
lesson; either cite the event and write the lesson in the child's own words,
or explicitly choose `NULL`. The task family must contain both triggers and
contraindications so a global ritual cannot pass.

Run independent matched childhood blocks. In each block:

- **P:** the strongest available adaptive parent sees the child's permitted
  executed history and gives one process-level question or correction. It may
  use training-task answers to diagnose process, but must withhold the answer
  and never see sealed evaluation scores.
- **Y:** a yoked/contact- and native-token-matched process message drawn by a
  presealed rule from a different matched event/child. It preserves attention,
  language, and compute but breaks contingency with the present mistake.
- Both children begin from identical base/birth tensors, receive identical
  task distributions and budgets, act before feedback, and get the same world
  outcomes for the actions they take. Parent wording is visible in childhood
  but loss-masked and absent from SLEEP targets.
- After outcome, the child receives an unforced THINK/DREAM opportunity and
  emits `LESSON(event_ids, scope, claim)` or `NULL`. The harness authenticates
  event IDs, action, outcome, and mechanical truth. It never writes the
  lesson. Invalid or unsupported text does not become a silently repaired
  target.
- Every episode keeps a fixed record slot, including authenticated `NULL`, so
  formation denominators remain visible. Track environmental encounters,
  child decisions, admitted artifacts, and optimizer presentations
  separately.

At each scheduled write, create evaluation-only forks from the same
pre-write state:

1. `AUTH_ON`: authentic child artifacts compiled and written;
2. `BINDING_DERANGED`: complete child artifacts permuted across incompatible
   situations with target marginals and dose preserved;
3. `SLEEP_OFF`: identical chronology and shadow compilation, no promotion;
4. `TEXT`: adapter OFF plus the same authentic artifacts under a fixed,
   query-conditioned public-only retriever (diagnostic carrier ceiling).

The continuing childhood follows only the prespecified authentic branch;
evaluation forks and their outcomes never return to the parent or lineage.
No deployment-exposed fork re-enters parenting.

After the final childhood sleep, delete parent access, parent state, lesson
text, childhood transcript, notes, indexes, caches, text memory, and temporary
prompts. Fresh contexts contain only the ordinary task interface. Evaluate:

- parent-free lesson-or-null decisions on fresh task-disjoint events;
- trigger and contraindication action accuracy;
- exact source/event citation and scope;
- unrelated-task/interface validity and unsupported-cue spill;
- adapter ON/OFF and authentic/deranged effects under common tasks/seeds.

The key estimands are root-level, never prompt-level:

```text
formation = valid_grounded_rate(P) - valid_grounded_rate(Y)

write_P = utility(P, AUTH_ON) - utility(P, SLEEP_OFF)

content_P = utility(P, AUTH_ON) - utility(P, BINDING_DERANGED)

H1 = [utility(P, AUTH_ON) - utility(P, SLEEP_OFF)]
   - [utility(Y, AUTH_ON) - utility(Y, SLEEP_OFF)].
```

Require formation and H1 to be positive on development before confirmation;
require authentic utility to beat both its own OFF and deranged writer, rather
than permitting deranged harm to create the headline. For a development
promotion with three blocks, require the correct sign in all three, a mean
root-level H1 of at least `+.05` on a normalized primary score, and no
interface/unrelated-family decrease below `-.05`. These are a scale decision,
not a paper p-value.

For a paper claim, determine the fresh confirmation-root count from blinded
OFF/control variance under a hard resource cap. Predeclare a smallest effect
of interest of `.05`; H1 passes only if the confirmation interval's lower
bound exceeds zero, the point estimate exceeds `.05`, authentic P is
absolutely above its own SLEEP_OFF, authentic P beats its deranged control,
and no adverse/interface gate fails. Report the total parenting effect and the
formation/content mediation separately; do not claim complete mediation from
correlation alone.

This one experiment closes H1 if positive. It simultaneously localizes a
failure:

- P does not improve formation: teaching policy/curriculum failed;
- formation improves but AUTH_ON does not: writer/readout failed;
- AUTH_ON and deranged move together: global ritual/spill;
- behavior improves only with TEXT: persistence/write failure;
- ON improves record prose but not actions: articulation without utility.

### Stage 3 — one adult lifetime study for H2 and the strong-memory baseline

Only an H1-qualified, pre-evaluation child checkpoint proceeds. Each
independent childhood root forks, before adult outcomes, into:

```text
P-RUN       parented childhood; adult SLEEP promotes
P-FROZEN    parented childhood; shadow SLEEP only
Y-RUN       yoked childhood; adult SLEEP promotes
Y-FROZEN    yoked childhood; shadow SLEEP only
P-TEXT      parented checkpoint frozen; same adult experience enters a
            qualified query-conditioned evolving text-memory system
```

Parents and childhood state are absent from every adult arm. Use new task
families or source-disjoint generator roots, paired exogenous exposure clocks,
fixed generous inference budgets, identical scheduled cuts, and independent
children as units. Scores at adult entry are reported separately from learning
after entry.

Primary H2 estimand:

```text
D_H2 = [W-AUC(P-RUN) - W-AUC(P-FROZEN)]
     - [W-AUC(Y-RUN) - W-AUC(Y-FROZEN)].
```

Also require `P-RUN - P-FROZEN > 0`, a positive absolute late P-RUN slope,
anchor-to-terminal improvement, retention of childhood/early-adult items, and
no increasing action/interface failure. Interactive curves estimate the total
organism effect; a secondary common-trajectory replay fork may isolate write
efficiency but cannot replace the interactive result.

**Development promotion:** three roots, all registered directions correct,
mean `D_H2 >= .05`, no ceiling/floor/adverse explanation. **Paper claim:** a
fresh confirmation cohort sized from blinded nuisance variance, lower interval
bound above zero, point estimate at least `.05`, and absolute P-RUN growth.
With only three roots, call H2 exploratory regardless of prompt count.

The strong-memory claim is separate. `P-RUN` must beat `P-TEXT` on predeclared
late-life W-AUC/terminal utility under the same source information and reported
interaction, inference-token, write-token, GPU, and storage budgets. If it does
not, retain H1/H2 if they pass but make no superiority or saturation claim.
The current static brief is not an acceptable substitute.

### Stage 4 — connected traversal/expansion only if retained in the paper

M-core v8 is internally explicit enough to preserve, and its HOLD is correct.
Do not materialize its 32-root package or spend its larger fit budget before
Stages 0--1 qualify input-conditioned writing. It remains a supplied-memory
ceiling rather than proof of child intelligence.

For a connected experiential claim, the authentic condition must additionally
show a child-authored organization decision over grounded atoms and a delayed
goal that mechanically requires both an old link and a new action-derived row.
Use the minimal registered conditions:

- `FULL_AUTH`;
- `OUTCOME_SHUFFLED` with authentic marginals/dose but broken action--outcome
  binding;
- `DREAM_NULL` with source facts preserved but child organization removed;
- `SLEEP_OFF` with shadow work and no promotion;
- `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS` as the finite-reader ceiling/baseline.

The primary delayed action must be better in FULL_AUTH than both shuffled and
DREAM_NULL and disappear under SLEEP_OFF. Goal swaps, outcome twins, old/new
cuts, read-order permutations, wrong-root reads, retention, and interface/
locality gates must redirect exactly as preregistered. Two development roots
are only a kill gate; a fresh root-level confirmation cohort is required.

This supports bounded connection, traversal, and action-driven expansion in a
finite world. It still does not justify "open-ended", native graph learning,
or general agent intelligence. If no explicit rate--distortion/fidelity test
is run, call the operation *compilation*, not compression.

## 6. Explicit full-claim and fallback decisions

### GO: full bounded Dream--LoRA--Think claim

Use this label only if all are true:

1. Stage 0 passes on the exact downstream surface.
2. Stage 1 passes conditional carriage, held-form use, reload, locality, and
   interface gates across all registered seed blocks.
3. Stage 2 passes fresh-confirmation formation, authentic-vs-OFF,
   authentic-vs-deranged, parent-vs-yoked H1, parent deletion, and no-harm.
4. Stage 3 passes the adult-SLEEP interaction and absolute lifetime growth on
   fresh roots.
5. Stage 4 passes if connection/traversal/expansion remains in the claim.
6. `P-RUN` beats the qualified evolving-text baseline if superiority remains
   in the claim.
7. Every confirmation lineage and panel is clean, sealed before model output,
   and analyzed at the child/root level with failures included.

Only then may the paper say that developmental process teaching caused a child
to author grounded learning material, its own SLEEP write preserved and used
that material after parent removal, and continued consolidation improved later
learning; add connected traversal/expansion and text superiority only for the
separately passed components.

### GO: focused H1 paper, H2 exploratory or null

If Stages 0--2 pass but Stage 3 is inconclusive, the paper can still center the
novel result:

> A learning-process disposition taught through interaction was expressed by
> a parent-deleted child, produced grounded child-authored training material,
> and selectively changed later behavior through a LoRA write.

Report H2 as the preregistered next question or an honest bounded null. Omit
increasing-intelligence, flywheel, strong-memory superiority, connected-
knowledge, and compression claims unless their own evidence passes.

### NO-GO for developmental claims; characterization fallback

If Stage 0 fails, or Stage 1 cannot produce conditional carriage without spill
or interface damage, stop parenting-scale and lifetime runs with this writer.
The honest fallback is a prospective characterization paper:

- per-life LoRA writes can carry global routines but are unreliable;
- current self-experience renderings are null/unstable;
- exact QA formatting can destroy the motor interface;
- strong acquisition often spills to unsupported cues;
- simple format canaries miss semantic/action collapse;
- query-conditioned text memory is the necessary comparator.

That fallback is not paper-ready from the heterogeneous retrospective lives
alone. It needs one presealed fresh confirmation suite across independent
writers/roots, exact conditional-binding and interface probes, and the strong
text baseline. Do not market contaminated or post-hoc panels as confirmation.

If Stage 1 passes but Stage 2 fails, the result localizes the failure to
developmental material formation/parenting rather than LoRA capacity. A strong
negative paper is possible only if the parent, yoked control, child-authorship,
sterile reset, and downstream utility assay were all valid; eloquent but unused
lessons are not enough.

If H1/H2 pass but `P-TEXT` wins, publish the learning result without parametric-
memory superiority. If the connected assay fails, publish H1/H2 without a
connected-knowledge claim. These are claim removals, not experiment failures.

## 7. Final recommendation to the builder

Do not let abundant GPUs turn the causal ladder into a factorial sweep. Spend
the next model hour on the exact writer sign canary, then the red-team T/W
level-1 sentinel. Preserve the current addition-only cell as a small global-
habit/fact-storage scout if already operationally useful, but do not open its
held confirmation or call it plasticity evidence merely because it runs.

Once Stage 1 qualifies one writer/LR, stop writer-format churn. Implement one
simple H1 nursery around the exact learned decision that the repository has
measured missing: after outcome, author a grounded reusable lesson or choose
NULL. Use the strongest context-adaptive parent, a yoked noncontingent control,
parent-text loss masking, child-only targets, authentic/deranged/OFF/text
forks, and irreversible parent deletion. This is the first result that can
materially change the abstract.

M-core v8 should remain preserved until that writer is qualified; its contract
closure is not a substitute for H1. The adult H2 five-arm study should remain
unlaunched until an H1 child exists. The paper should not claim compression
unless the team elects to measure it.
