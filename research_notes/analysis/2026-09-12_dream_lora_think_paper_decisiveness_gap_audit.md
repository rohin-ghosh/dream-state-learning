# Dream--LoRA--Think paper decisiveness-gap audit

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC
Evidence cut: canonical paper through SEQ-086, plus sprint state and the
terminal SEQ-087 exact-training-row diagnostic
Scope: fresh paper-level audit only. The actively edited M-core v6 file was
not read. M-core judgments below use the superseded v5 design, its two fresh
audits, and the explicit fact that no M-core scientific result yet exists.
No builder source, active design, model, GPU job, coordination record, or
canonical paper file was changed.

## Executive verdict

The current paper is an evidence-backed **writer-characterization and failure
paper**, not yet a Dream--LoRA--Think developmental paper. Its central bounded
claims are defensible: per-life LoRA writes change behavior, frequently lock
to routines, sometimes cause severe late harm, and do not beat a static text
brief on average in the historical matched diagnostic. The September 12
component assays add two narrow facts: useful oracle material transfers a
small amount, and one selected child's action span is a better training target
than its whole Scratchpad under the tested recipe.

The intended headline remains unproved:

> Developmental parenting changes how a child turns its own future experience
> into useful DREAM/SLEEP material; after the parent and childhood state are
> removed, the child retains that learning disposition (H1) and improves
> faster from new experience specifically when adult consolidation continues
> (H2).

Several current implementations give evidence **against their own positive
versions**. The historical writer's apparent gain is dominated by fixed
routine reproduction and deteriorates at late disjoint endpoints. Every
tested canonical-frame writer fails the selectivity gate. Most decisively,
SEQ-087 shows the current semantic full-response writer is near chance even on
its exact training prompts: it learned global action preference, not a usable
key-conditioned mapping. The strict current P0 compiler found zero qualifying
grounded records; the raw-wake fork degraded both arms; tested process-parent
packages tied their active controls on primary solves; and whole Scratchpad
replay never beat action-only replay.

These are implementation-bounded contradictions, not impossibility results
for LoRA, reflection, or parenting. They do, however, forbid treating the
semantic writer and M-core as two positive links that can simply be narrated
into one causal chain. The writer is not qualified, M-core has no result, and
even hypothetical passes of both would establish only a controlled mechanism.
The first experiment capable of supporting the headline is still a fresh,
matched, parent-deleted adult `parenting x SLEEP` factorial with learning
curves across independent children.

## 1. The objective against which this audit judges the paper

The governing objective is the narrow synthesis in the thesis and launch
prompt, not the current CompilerGym manuscript title:

```text
THINK
  child acts -> environment supplies a public, causally later outcome

DREAM
  child decides what relation/lesson/link is worth forming or how it should be
  represented, without seeing the later answer-bearing blueprint

SLEEP
  provenance-bound material changes the persistent adapter; old useful content
  survives later writes; fresh-context action depends selectively on the write

PARENTING / H1
  matched developmental teaching changes this learning behavior, and the
  difference persists after parent, reminders, childhood stores, and caches
  are removed

ADULT FLYWHEEL / H2
  on fresh verifiable experience, continued SLEEP adds more learning value for
  parented than unparented children; the estimand is a gain/slope interaction,
  not a starting-level difference
```

This demands a connected execution receipt, not merely compatible component
results. Adult updates must change parameters used on later experience; the
child's own material must enter those updates; the parent treatment must be
matched; parent removal must be real; and independent child/root blocks, not
prompts, fits, or episodes, are the replication units. Superiority to external
memory is an additional claim and requires a qualified evolving-text agent,
not a static brief.

**objective mismatch**: it truthfully proves a narrower and mostly negative
characterization while the project-level thesis asks whether parenting builds
a later self-learning flywheel. Calling the present components the full paper
would quietly replace that thesis.

One update is now scientifically due in any later authorized manuscript pass:
the paper's SEQ-086 phrase “semantic writer inconclusive” is conservative but
stale. SEQ-087 establishes a bounded negative for this exact full-response
recipe: exact-training generated accuracy is `68/68/64/63 of 128` versus OFF
`64/64/65/63`, with near-constant action emission. It remains wrong to extend
that negative to LoRA or all writers.

## 4. Why the semantic writer and M-core do not add up to the headline

### 4.1 The current semantic writer is a negative component result

The no-fit semantic carrier proves that the base model can follow the direct
row surface. The four fits then optimize heavily and retain a valid action
interface, but they learn the native action **class** and a map-specific global
preference rather than key binding. Exact-row failure means M-core cannot treat
the present writer as an already qualified substrate. Positive average
conditional score gain is not a rescue: generation is near chance, many keys
move the wrong way, and off-target distributions move broadly.

The proposed full-response-versus-first-choice mask diagnostic is therefore
well targeted. It can identify whether common syntax and teacher-forced suffix
tokens dilute the sole key-dependent decision token. A positive result would
select a candidate objective; it would not by itself prove locality, held-form
extraction, cumulative retention, authentic experience use, or parenting.

### 4.2 M-core is an unexecuted controlled mechanism, not a developmental study

V5's own claim ceiling is deliberately narrow: compiler-mediated experiential
binding, child-selected evidence-indicated pairs, typed functional traversal,
and two-cycle clean-base reconstruction. Its causal and statistics audits both
required rework before Stage 0 or fits. The file is now superseded by an active
v6 that this task explicitly excluded, so no assertion about v6 closure is
warranted.

Even a flawless future M pass would still use a fixed finite world, registered
action grammar, compiler, typed queries, exact read budget, child-selection
test, and endpoint checker. That could be strong evidence for a bounded
THINK--DREAM--SLEEP relay. It would not show that parenting installed the
selection behavior, that the behavior persists after parent deletion, that
adult consolidation improves learning slope, that native free-form planning
emerged, or that weights beat evolving text.

### 4.3 Component conjunction is not causal-chain evidence

The present writer and M-core fail the minimum composition rules:

- no shared executed root, checkpoint lineage, objective, row renderer, or
  evaluation surface;
- no qualified selective writer artifact for M to consume;
- no joined child-action -> public-outcome -> child-DREAM -> compiled-row ->
  accepted-write -> sterile-reload -> later-action trace;
- no cumulative second write with isolated OLD and NEW necessity;
- no matched parenting treatment, parent deletion, or adult running/frozen
  contrast; and
- no independent child blocks or strong evolving-text comparator.

Therefore the results cannot be combined as “writer works” plus “M works” plus
“parenting works.” At this cut the accurate synthesis is: **useful material can
sometimes alter action; the tested writer does not bind selectively; the
two-cycle relay is a design; parenting efficacy and the adult flywheel are
unmeasured.**

## 5. Ranked next experiments by expected scientific information per GPU-hour

The ranking uses expected ability to change a major claim status, divided by
device time, with penalties for immature execution contracts. It is a gated
sequence: a cheap failure prevents expensive downstream runs.

### 1. Decision-token versus full-response objective ablation

Run the already specified two-fit root1/W+ comparison: identical initialization,
128 exact rows, seed, order, inputs, optimizer, rank, LR, precision, dropout,
and 256 updates; change only the loss mask from the full response to the first
divergent action token. Keep the registered full-response arm as the control.

- **Budget:** two fits, 512 total updates, 384 exact-prompt generations,
  `<=1800 s` total wall cap as specified.
- **Primary information:** whether the failure is caused by the response
  objective's supervision geometry rather than absent capacity or a bad action
  surface.
- **Decision:** if decision-only does not materially improve per-key/template
  conditional generation over both full-response and OFF without another
  one-action collapse, stop this objective family. If it does, nominate the
  objective for fresh qualification; do not call it a writer pass.

This is the highest-value immediate fit because SEQ-087 has already eliminated
the held-extraction-only explanation and the training audit identifies one
key-dependent token among mostly shared supervised tokens.

### 2. Fresh two-root selective-writer qualification of the winning objective

Only after experiment 1 shows a real conditional advantage, freeze fresh
source-disjoint roots and rerun the full complementary-map writer gate. Test
exact forms, held renderings, strict greedy action, per-key decision margins,
missing/unsupported/neighbour/wrong-root locality, unrelated interface, and
replay. Use absolute gates; improvement relative to the failed objective is
not sufficient.

- **Budget anchor:** the original four-fit semantic run plus 1,712 requests
  reported 1,414.7 runner seconds (`~0.39` A40-hour), but the new manifest must
  bind its own measured cap; this number is only a scale reference.
- **Primary information:** whether any current small writer can carry opposing
  condition-action bindings selectively and reproducibly.
- **Decision:** require every root/map cell to pass. A failure ends semantic
  writer micro-sweeps and forces a literature-backed writer/objective pivot or
  a narrower paper. A pass releases cumulative and integrated work.

Do not spend first on another rank, LR, mask, or preservation coefficient
sweep: completed evidence already shows the acquisition--spill tradeoff under
those one-factor changes.

### 3. Zero-fit M-core closure, then a two-root integrated two-cycle kill gate

After active v6 bytes are frozen and independently audited, first run the CPU
theorem/shortcut/provenance suite and exact same-semantics text actor on four
predeclared DEV roots. This stage uses no optimizer GPU and must establish that
the world, child-selection seam, read machine, goal swap, source derangement,
cuts, and delayed OLD+NEW endpoint are behaviorally exposed.

Only if exact text passes and experiment 2 supplies a qualified writer, run two
LoRA roots. Each root should contain the minimum noncompensatory trained
conditions: authentic full chain, matched outcome/source derangement, and
matched DREAM-link derangement, with SLEEP-off, cuts/swaps, and exact text as
inference-only controls. Include the clean-base cumulative second write and a
delayed action that requires one OLD relation plus NEW.

- **Budget:** zero training fits for the theorem/text stop; at most the v5
  envelope of six carriers per completed root (`<=12` fits for the two-root
  kill gate), rebound to measured writer throughput before launch.
- **Primary information:** whether the writer and finite relay actually compose
  in one causal execution, and whether authentic source and child organization
  each add necessary value.
- **Decision:** require both roots to have the preregistered direction and all
  validity gates. Otherwise do not open a powered M cohort. A pass licenses a
  bounded mechanism claim only.

This joined experiment is more informative than separately powering S, DREAM,
connected traversal, and W1 populations. One root can reuse each trained
artifact for all read-time interventions.

### 4. Fresh parent-deleted adult `parenting x SLEEP` pilot

This is the first headline-bearing experiment and should run only after the
selective writer and integrated two-cycle kill gate pass. Raise independently
seeded sibling children under one targeted process-parent treatment versus a
dose-matched, target-blind active sham. Freeze the adult task generator before
child outcomes. Delete the parent, teaching cards, childhood prompts, text
stores, indexes, caches, and query state. From each root checkpoint fork:

```text
P-RUN       parented; adult SLEEP promotes
P-FROZEN    parented; shadow SLEEP, no weight promotion
U-RUN       sham; adult SLEEP promotes
U-FROZEN    sham; shadow SLEEP, no weight promotion
```

Measure entry competence separately. The primary adult estimand is the
root-block interaction in gain/AUC or late-minus-early score, not terminal
level. Require absolute positive late slope and OLD/early-cohort retention in
`P-RUN`. Add `P-TEXT` only if an evolving public-only text system has already
passed its own certificate and the paper retains a superiority claim.

- **Budget:** profile one excluded four-branch canary, then bind a finite root
  count and cap from measured branch cost. Do not reuse the historical
  223-GPU-hour CompilerGym estimate as if it applied to a new PCFL design.
- **Primary information:** H1 at entry after deletion, H2 as the marginal value
  of continued consolidation, and absolute finite-lifetime improvement.
- **Decision:** no favorable starting level can compensate for a null H2
  interaction. Low-seed output remains exploratory.

### Parallel probe that should not displace the four above

The planned eight-board process-card versus active-format constraint-check
production diagnostic is cheap and sensible on otherwise idle inference
capacity. It asks whether coaching improves a publicly checkable artifact.
Even a clean win is live prompt influence with no fit, parent removal, or
downstream utility. Treat it as curriculum localization, not evidence for H1
or a reason to skip experiments 1--4.

## 6. Paper decision at this evidence cut

- **Current defensible paper:** the canonical Experience Models
  characterization, updated eventually with the bounded semantic exact-train
  negative. Its contribution is failure science and measurement discipline.
- **Not yet defensible:** a headline claiming that DREAM, LoRA, and THINK form
  a child-built self-learning flywheel, that parenting is amortized into later
  learning, that connected experience expands over a life, or that LoRA beats
  strong external memory.
- **Mechanism promotion point:** experiments 1--3 pass with one joined trace.
  This supports a finite typed THINK--DREAM--SLEEP mechanism paper, still not
  the developmental headline.
- **Headline promotion point:** experiment 4 yields a parent-deleted H1 effect,
  a positive `parenting x SLEEP` adult gain interaction, absolute late
  improvement, retention, and uncertainty across independent roots. Only then
  should the canonical paper's spine move from characterization to the
  developmental claim.
