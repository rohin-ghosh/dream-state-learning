# Full-objective paper claim coverage audit

**Date:** 2026-09-13

**Role:** independent fresh-context paper/evidence audit

**Repository snapshot:** `df6565ff` (`Close PCFL C0 interface repair v2`) at
initial inspection

**Scope:** paper prototype, THESIS/IDEAS, PCFL contracts, the newest C0 and
own-write/interface records, and prior mechanistic/baseline results

**Constraint:** read-only scientific review; no runtime, model, tokenizer, GPU,
benchmark, adapter, or checkpoint changes

## Executive verdict

The full objective is not yet demonstrated. The repository contains several
real and useful bounded results:

1. child-produced action--outcome text can sometimes be written into a LoRA
   and recalled after the source is withdrawn;
2. replay can prevent some previously observed retention loss;
3. supplied or mechanically compiled connected memories can support strong
   downstream behavior under heavily scaffolded read protocols; and
4. the current instruments expose semantic collapse that generic format
   canaries miss.

Those results do **not** yet join into the paper's intended causal chain:

> the child acts -> records the outcome -> compiles connected experience ->
> retrieves/traverses it for a new goal -> acts to close a missing edge ->
> improves over a lifetime more than frozen, strong active-text, and terminal
> batch learners.

The present full-objective claim ladder is therefore approximately:

| Requirement | Current status | Short reading |
|---|---:|---|
| (a) Own action--outcome write | **WEAK / bounded partial** | Cold recall exists in another controlled domain, but the first native PCFL formation never reached a fit and safe/selective/downstream use is unproved. |
| (b) Connected/compiled knowledge | **MISSING in the current organism** | Legacy supplied/mechanical ceilings are positive; the recent two-SLEEP learned conditional-memory test is exactly null. |
| (c) Goal-conditioned traversal/expansion | **MISSING** | The first PCFL C0 run was interface-invalid; the corrected typed THINK/READ ceiling is frozen but unrun. No parametric causal link use or learned expansion exists. |
| (d) Lifetime improvement | **CONTRADICTED for reliability; missing for the intended claim** | Some exploratory lives improve, others collapse, and the matched nine-life aggregate is negative. No novelty-growing causal lifetime result exists. |
| (e) Strong external-memory and batch baselines | **MISSING** | Designs exist; the prior active-text run did not serve a single valid read. The only matched textual brief is competitive or better. |
| (f) Compression | **CONTRADICTED physically; semantic form missing** | Current adapters are larger than the ledgers. Zero returned memory tokens is not storage compression. A predictive-reuse assay is designed but unrun. |

The highest honest current paper is a writer/behavior-interference and
interface-failure characterization. The intended Dream--LoRA--Think paper
becomes available only if the corrected supplied-memory ceiling and the
stage-gated PCFL vertical both pass, followed by a qualified lifetime
comparison.

## Status vocabulary

- **PROVEN (bounded):** directly supported under a named protocol, without
  importing a larger claim.
- **WEAK:** a directional or one-domain result exists, but a necessary causal,
  replication, retention, selectivity, or lineage condition is absent.
- **CONTRADICTED:** existing evidence goes against the proposed general claim;
  this does not prove impossibility under a repaired mechanism.
- **MISSING:** no admissible result tests the requirement.

## Requirement matrix

LoRA can retain some exact and paraphrased content from a child's own grounded
record after source withdrawal.” It is not fair to say that the organism has a
working, safe, selective own-experience write or that the written experience is
used prospectively.

**Legacy feasibility, not current proof.** The G4h/K L0 pipeline reached about
0.949/0.882/0.923 across three worlds using a real dreamer, verified-claim
graph, rank-64 transport, atomic reads, and clean-base composition. G4k also
noticed a missing family connection and executed a world action to resolve it.

verifier, clustering, emission, and read plan were mechanical; it lacked modern
child provenance, matched raw compilation, and lifetime controls. Semantic

**Bottom line for (b):** connected experiential knowledge is a target, not a
paper result. “Compiled” may describe the fixed data transformation. It should
not imply that the model discovered abstractions or that the resulting relation
is causally used until AUTH beats atom/no-link and survives the causal cuts.

collapse. The lifetime flywheel remains a hypothesis.

Two historical facts constrain the narrative. First, Alchemy v2 retrieval
reached near-perfect seen recall while LoRA failed the registered comparison;
that is a negative substrate result, not evidence that the new system beats
RAG. Second, the matched CompilerGym static brief averaged +.0041 while the
adapter averaged -.0146 in the common-random diagnostic. The present record
does not merely lack external-memory superiority; the strongest roughly
matched textual evidence is directionally against it.

The external-memory comparison should be split into three explicit roles:

1. **supplied exact service ceiling** — can the actor use correct memory at all?
2. **same-semantics carrier comparison** — parameters versus text holding
   semantics fixed;
3. **native evolving comparator** — each system must form and maintain its own
   memory from the same stream.

Collapsing these roles into one “RAG baseline” would make the result
uninterpretable.

### F. Compression

| Claim form | Status | Evidence | Honest interpretation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| F1. Physical storage compression | **CONTRADICTED at current scale** | A rank-8 adapter contains about 20,185,088 parameters: about 40.37 MB even at bf16; the observed file is about 80.8 MB at fp32. Current semantic ledgers are smaller. | The current adapter expands rather than compresses the represented records. |
| F2. In-context bandwidth reduction | **Trivially possible but not yet a scientific result** | Parametric memory can return zero explicit memory tokens at inference. | This is context-bandwidth amortization, not physical compression; the parameter bytes and training compute remain charged. |
| F3. Lossless semantic compression | **MISSING and unlikely under present carrier** | No codec comparison. | Need exact cold reconstruction across at least two loads, zero false rows, total byte accounting, and an ordinary packed graph/codec baseline. |
| F4. Lossy task-useful compression / rate--distortion | **MISSING** | A minimal predictive-reuse assay is designed. | Need a predeclared semantic distortion metric, action utility, byte/token/training cost, and multiple carrier rates. |
| F5. Parametric predictive reuse | **MISSING but plausible as the right near-term claim** | A zero-new-fit continuation assay can ask whether old weights shorten or improve predictions on related new structure. | Must include an independent/unrelated continuation null, adapter-off, shorter predictive code, and downstream utility. It depends on M and L passing first. |

The manuscript already records that fewer rows and longer rows late in a life
could mean abstraction, ritual-induced novelty loss, or verbosity. It is not a
compression metric. “Tokens per episode covered” is also too upstream and
annotator-dependent for the current paper. It may be a daydream diagnostic,
but it cannot support a storage/compression claim.

**Bottom line for (f):** remove “compression” from the empirical contribution
and title. If the later reuse assay passes, use **parametric predictive reuse**
or **context-bandwidth amortization**, with physical size and training cost
reported explicitly.

## Current paper and abstract audit

`paper_prototype/main.tex` is a 2,500+-line append-only research chronology,
not a submission manuscript. Its strength is unusually honest claim hygiene;
its present limitation is that the evidence timeline has swallowed the paper.
The explicit “What we do not claim” paragraph correctly excludes connected
knowledge, traversal, expansion, compression, lifetime improvement, and strong
active-memory superiority. That exclusion also means the current document does
not yet carry the intended thesis.

`paper_prototype/ABSTRACT_CANDIDATE_v3_flywheel.md` is now stale. Its “zero
late-stage articulation across 28 lives” diagnosis predates the post-outcome
formation repairs and bounded own-record write results. Its high-level H1/H2
separation remains useful, but its current-evidence paragraph must be rebuilt,
not patched.

There are two coherent submission modes:

1. **Full objective mode (conditional):** only after typed supplied-memory C0,
   authentic two-root PCFL M, causal link use, and qualified lifetime L.
2. **Fallback mode (available now):** a rigorous study of why per-life LoRA
   writers fail: prompt/interface mismatch, action-dialect loss, conditional
   selectivity failure, fit-without-extractability, replay/retention tradeoffs,
   and one bounded own-record cold-write repair.

Do not blend the fallback's evidence with the full objective's title. A paper
cannot disclaim every phrase in its headline contribution.

## The next three experiments, ranked by scientific information per GPU-hour

The ranking deliberately preserves hard baselines, exact semantic scoring and
causal controls. It does not make the benchmark easier for the adapter.

### 1. Corrected supplied-memory C0 interface qualification

**Question:** can this 7B actor traverse and expand an exact connected memory
when the information is supplied and every legal THINK/READ/action operation is
actually disclosed?

**Protocol:** run the frozen v2 ladder in order: READ handshake -> one-shot
exact graph -> typed recurrent THINK -> conditional disjoint generic scaffold.
Use joint per-task read-plus-correct-action gates, exact whole-turn bytes, fixed
counters, pre-materialized blinded confirmation catalogs, and identical
FULL/NATIVE access. Reuse aliases; never retry a selected successful stage.

**Cost ceiling:** 13,952 logical / 13,056 unique generation calls, about 34,200
device seconds or **9.5 A40-hours**, with no fits.

**Why first:** it answers the absolute feasibility question. If the ceiling
fails, another writer fit cannot explain whether memory or the actor interface
is broken. If it passes, every later miss localizes to formation/write/use
rather than task impossibility. Stop if the final generic scaffold fails; do
not weaken the graph or scorer.

### 2. Full stage-gated two-root PCFL vertical DEV, including authentic own write

**Question:** can one fixed pipeline form grounded child EVENT/LINK records,
write them selectively, causally use the connection, survive a second write,
and take an informative action?

**Protocol:** repair only the already diagnosed record boundary (native EOS
child payload plus explicitly receipted harness LF) and curriculum disclosure;
then run LOW, conditionally HIGH, S1 AUTH/ATOMS/TWIN/PERMUTE, cuts, reachout,
and S2 OLD+NEW. Keep roots, records, wrappers, token targets, update count,
model, and scorer frozen. No template substitution after seeing failures.

**Cost ceiling:** depending on the exact closed version, roughly **14--16 fits,
2,800--3,200 updates, and 17--20 A40-hours** including formation/readout.

**Why second:** this is the highest-density experiment in the repository. One
run touches authentic formation, cold transport, selectivity, connected causal
use, expansion, and retention. Stage gates give scientifically interpretable
stops instead of spending through a broken layer. A cheap standalone exact
address write can run in spare capacity, but it should not displace this: the
project already has bounded own-record cold recall in another domain.

### 3. Baseline-qualified novelty-growing lifetime campaign

**Question:** after M works, does periodic consolidation improve learning on
new opportunities over a lifetime, and does it beat both a strong evolving
text memory and equal-dose terminal batch learning?

**Mandatory gateway before scale:** on DEV, add the same-history,
same-target-token/FLOP **raw-chronological LoRA** arm (previous estimate: four
fits, about 2 A40-hours) and certify `ACTIVE_TEXT_NATIVE` through the corrected
interface. If PCFL-L offers no reusable structure or external-memory bandwidth
pressure, redesign L before spending; do not handicap text retrieval or brute
force an opaque exact-route domain that intrinsically favors lossless memory.

**Confirmation arms:** at minimum `DLT_PERIODIC`, `SLEEP_FROZEN`, certified
`ACTIVE_TEXT_NATIVE`, and `FINAL_BATCH` if recurrence is claimed. Use independent
roots as the inferential unit, common-random panels, an exogenous opportunity
clock, parent-free cuts, old/new retention, action utility, and intention-to-
treat accounting for rejected writes. Parent-present cells belong to a later
or piggybacked factorial; they must not replace the writer controls.

**Cost:** the existing one-epoch N=16 estimate is about **142.4 A40-hours plus
inference** after writer qualification. This is much more expensive than the
first two experiments, but it is the first spend that can close the central
lifetime and baseline claims. Its information per GPU-hour remains high only
after the two gateways pass.

## What not to spend next

- Do not repeat the exact-LF elicitation. Preserve SEQ-169/170 as failures and
  separate semantic child bytes from storage framing.
- Do not run another storage-only fact assay as the headline next result. The
  repository already shows fit, exact recall, and paraphrase recall can diverge
  from behavior.
- Do not tune the external-memory budget downward until LoRA wins.
- Do not claim DREAM value without raw chronological LoRA.
- Do not claim recurrence without FINAL_BATCH.
- Do not claim compression from reduced context tokens or denser dream prose.
- Do not scale parenting until the child record/write/use chain is qualified;
  otherwise teacher effort is spent against an unmeasured substrate.

## Submission decision rule

The full paper title and abstract should remain conditional on this ordered
chain:

1. **C0 supplied-memory ceiling passes.**
2. **PCFL M passes authentic formation, selective connected write, causal link
   use, and one retention/expansion step on two roots.**
3. **Raw-LoRA and strong native text controls are certified without outcome-
   adaptive weakening.**
4. **Lifetime confirmation shows a positive root-level interaction with
   retention and action utility.**

If step 1 fails, stop and report an actor/interface boundary. If step 2 fails,
the full-objective paper is not ready; use the exact failed rung as the main
scientific finding. If steps 1--2 pass but the lifetime run does not, the paper
can support a bounded connected parametric-memory mechanism, not a flywheel.
Only step 4 licenses “lifetime improvement.” External-memory superiority and
compression remain separate conjunctions, not automatic consequences.
