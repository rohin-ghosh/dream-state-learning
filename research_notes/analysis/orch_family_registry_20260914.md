generated, that a child qualified, or that a native loop ran. Each executable
protocol binds exact roster/generator, oracle, visibility, parent-free readouts,
controls, retention and provenance before use. No labels or real task contents

qualification, including mathematics and incremental dependency work. Two
reserved families are a starting inventory, not the requested eventual breadth.

coverage, meaningful reasoning opportunity and a documented level boundary.

registry. Existing global scientific gates remain in force. Parent presence in
