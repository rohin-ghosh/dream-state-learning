## Ruling

The causal order remains right:

```text
functional target-disjoint controller
  -> own action/outcome + connected write/use across two sleeps
  -> powered novelty-growing lifetime
  -> certified strong evolving text-memory comparison
```

But **terminal Stage2A (5,040 calls) followed by terminal TSJ (6,460 calls) is
not the highest-information-per-GPU-hour execution plan**. It front-loads a
curriculum comparison, 22 counterfactual fits, and a compiler-attribution
sidecar before establishing that the authentic same-adapter path works.
Logical staging should be retained while model work is released in
predeclared, root-frozen tranches.

SEQ-195 changes one decision only: the writer recipe is now qualified enough
for integration on one exposed same bank. New-only writes lost A 12/12 pooled
learner cells while learning B; replay and clean cumulative retained A and B
12/12 at both views. This ends singleton writer scouting. It does **not**
qualify LINK use, controller coexistence, unseen banks, action utility,
lifetime retention, or either optimizer lineage as generally superior.

## Cells to remove, combine, or defer

### Remove from the serial gate

- Tiny Stage-1 model run, P-CHAIN as a prerequisite, and all further
  singleton storage/rank/heat/syntax scouts. CPU checks and P-CHAIN's optional
  appendix status remain.
- P70 `S1_NOWRITE` after an S2 `OLD_FILLER` result exists. It is a cheap early
  kill control, but `OLD_FILLER` is the stronger matched-work no-new-content
  control; reporting both as necessary evidence is redundant.

### Combine by holding the authentic adapter fixed

The current TSJ spends separate fits on S1 `O`, `T`, `REL_CUT`, `IRR_CUT` and
S2 `O`, `N`. The existing `RESP_T`, `RESP_O`, and `RESP_N` fork design gives a
cleaner first causal test: preserve the AUTH actor, address, prefix, syntax,
and adapter, replace the earliest affected response, then switch to the
complete coherent transformed registry. Extend that same fork construction
to one indispensable LINK cut and one equal-token irrelevant cut. This tests
whether retrieved content, rather than treatment-correlated optimization,
controls action.

Retain fitted `ATOM` at S1 and S2 (link-added value) and fitted S2
`OLD_FILLER` (matched write heat without NEW semantics). With full text
ceilings, AUTH/ATOM/OLD_FILLER fits, fixed-actor response transformations,
foreign/birth controls, old retention, and both preservation checks, the
regenerated cap is **4,504 calls and 10 fits per paired TSJ**, versus 6,460
calls and 26 fits. This requires a versioned ledger/checker amendment before
execution; it is not authority to reinterpret v4.

### Defer, do not delete

- A fitted same-ID whole-bank derangement is claim-hardening after
  AUTH+ATOM+forks pass; it is not a rational pre-AUTH fit. If needed, select
  prospectively one OLD and one NEW binding, not every redundant bit/stage.
- S1/S2 `RAW` (four paired fits; **516 calls**) is required only for the claim
  that DREAM compilation adds value over chronological LoRA. Run it after
  connected utility exists; a RAW result cannot rescue an AUTH failure.
- The full Stage2A panel and `CLOSED` curriculum contrast are robustness/
  curriculum evidence after integration, not admission tickets to it.
- Independent-bank retention belongs inside the novelty-growing lifetime,
  where retained items must change action. Do not add a separate SEQ-195
  confirmation bank ahead of TSJ.
