# Minimum exact Level-1 birth -> Level-2 disposition protocol

**Date:** 2026-09-13 UTC
**Role:** watcher-side design only. No builder source, corpus, model, adapter,
job, or GPU state was changed.
**Decision:** run the closed Q0 before any claim-bearing birth. The committed
birth and born-RuleGame artifacts are useful engineering components, but the
smallest learning-disposition result needs a post-birth nonce task, yoked
teaching, OFF as well as DERANGED, and PROMOTE versus SHADOW.

## Two execution tracks

### Track A — executable engineering track

1. Run the closed natural-common-prefix Q0 exactly as registered: shared OFF,
   mandatory `P_AUTH` and `P_DERANGED`, `3e-5`, 128 quartet updates, two fits
   mandatory and three maximum. Only its full exact + held + complementarity +
   interface + locality conjunction releases birth.
2. Then run the already native-audited birth component without changing its
   recipe: `B_AUTH` and `B_DERANGED` from the same fresh initialized rank-8
   adapter, full-response V3 CE at `1e-4`, four epochs, 256 rows, batch 8,
   128 updates. Add the already proposed exact-training-form readout before
   interpreting held extraction.
3. If both birth maps and locality pass, the committed
   `born_rulegame_formation.py` may run once per born adapter as a routing,
   participation, and compiler-readiness canary. It is no-write, uses a
   repeatedly inspected development instrument, changes P/A teacher content,
   and cannot yield a learning-disposition result.

If birth is run before Q0 for scheduling reasons, label it
`EXPLORATORY_PRE_Q0`; none of its weights, outputs, errors, or downstream
behavior may tune Q0, and its adapter may not seed claim-bearing Level 2.

### Track B — smallest claim-bearing feasibility track

After Q0 and direct birth qualify, skip the historical RuleGame outcome as the
scientific test. Freeze the nonce protocol below, then run it once. One root is
only feasibility; it can release replication, not a population claim.

## Exact Level-1 arms and corpus

Use the committed `birth-conditional-crossed-v1` bytes at source
`31b5535ec9f73f7b32fdaf21ccfc3a2a68a948a6`, including their native
tokenizer audit:

| arm | fit | role |
|---|---:|---|
| `B_AUTH` | 1 | authentic evidence-state -> action/update map |
| `B_DERANGED` | 1 | equal-resource complementary installed-routine control |
| `B_OFF` | 0 | same base and initialized adapter, no birth update |

`B_AUTH` and `B_DERANGED` contain the same 256 public inputs and exactly 64
rows each of PROSPECT, REVISE, truthful ADDITION, and truthful COPY. Every
eight-row optimizer group is `P0,R0,P1,R1,ADD0,ADD1,COPY0,COPY1`; DERANGED
swaps only the conditional targets with registered permutation
`[2,3,0,1,4,5,6,7]`. PROSPECT crosses belief x goal; REVISE contains complete
EXPECTED x OBSERVED x PRIOR cubes within visible instance/template; action
order is balanced. Train/dev instances, templates, anchor operands/literals,
and source IDs are disjoint.

This is deliberately use-shaped representation formation: PROSPECT has four
training renderings, REVISE two, and conditional forms are interleaved with
truthful native interfaces in every update. [Allen-Zhu--Li
2309.14316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14316) motivates
varied/permuted/rephrased and instruction-like forms *during* formation, but is
only a design prior here; late prompting cannot rescue exact-fit/held-extraction
failure and direct adherence is not learning-to-learn.

Birth entry gates are noncompensatory:

- Q0 must already have passed its closed thresholds; this `1e-4` full-response birth
  does not retroactively qualify the `3e-5` pairwise writer.
- Exact train form, per assigned map: PROSPECT at least `15/16` and REVISE at
  least `29/32` unique semantic sources. Low exact fit stops as acquisition
  failure.
- Held dev, per assigned map: PROSPECT at least `29/32`, REVISE at least
  `58/64`; belief/goal twin success at least `15/16` each and
  expected/observed/prior twin success at least `29/32` each.
- ADDITION and COPY are each `16/16` instruction-compliant, with zero forbidden
  conditional-tag spill. Both arms must meet the current locality rule
  `max(ceil(.95N), OFF_compliant-1)`, which is `16/16` for each anchor.
- `B_AUTH` must pass AUTH semantics; `B_DERANGED` must pass its assigned
  complementary map and must not be worse than `B_OFF` by more than `2/32` on
  the Level-2 entry action panel. Otherwise it is a harmed anti-learning
  control and the disposition contrast is invalid.

The exact/held split classifies failure as acquisition, extraction, or scope;
no downstream prompt result can repair it.

## Birth-disjoint Level-2 task and information tape

After the three birth checkpoints and all training/readout bytes are sealed, a
holder not involved in birth construction materializes one root-local task
seed. It creates two opaque binary maps, one the exact complement of the other.
Each map has eight opaque objects, four assigned action `kiv` and four `zot`.
The strings, object IDs, task namespace, prompts, and action labels occur in no
Q0/birth row or readout. Neither birth author nor child sees the mapping seed.

Split the eight objects in each orientation into two four-object evidence
blocks. Per block there are therefore eight informative events
(`2 orientations x 4 objects`). For every birth arm, use byte-identical:

- object presentation and order;
- a fixed parent sentence: “Predict, compare with the observed action, and
  record only what this event establishes; do not infer an unseen object”;
- the externally supplied observed action;
- eight record slots, turn/token caps, compiler, optimizer schedule, and
  evaluation forms.

Also show four duplicate/no-new-information events per block (two per
orientation) after their informative originals. They are never fitted and the
correct record decision is the literal `SKIP`; this is the negative control
against “always write.”

The child predicts before the observation and then authors one record. The
parent is a sealed tape: it cannot see arm, child reply, hidden map, scores, or
future cases. Realized child records may differ, because they are the proposed
mediator. The compiler sees only the public event and child record. It admits a
record only when object, prediction, observed action, and match/revision fields
are source-grounded; an invalid slot becomes a zero-loss masked slot, never a
repaired or teacher-authored target. Report admitted slots and native target
tokens rather than calling them equal.

For each evidence dose `k=0,1,2`, evaluate a fresh query template on both held
skins for all objects and both orientations: `2 x 8 x 2 = 32` exact task
actions per state. Prompts ask only for `ACT: kiv` or `ACT: zot`; they do not
request reflection, JSON, prediction, or a candidate explanation. Incorrect,
multiple, invalid, missing, and truncated actions score zero.

## Level-2 arms, writes, and lineage

For each `a in {B_AUTH, B_DERANGED, B_OFF}`:

1. Capture both block-1 and block-2 child records from the sealed birth
   checkpoint before any Level-2 update. This fixes evidence and authorship
   material before promotion changes behavior.
2. `SHADOW_a` compiles the same slots and preserves the sealed birth adapter
   mounted for every readout.
3. `PROMOTE_a,1` starts from that birth adapter and trains block 1 once.
4. `PROMOTE_a,2` starts only from `PROMOTE_a,1` and trains block 2 once.

Each write has eight fixed slots, batch 8, no packing, 20 epochs/updates,
fresh AdamW state, seed 2, rank 8/alpha 16/dropout .05, and `3e-5`. Loss is the
mean of per-record token-mean losses over all eight slots; a rejected slot
contributes exactly zero. There is no birth-specific replay, parent text,
restatement, external memory, evaluation answer, or hidden-score target. The
same fixed endpoint is used; no checkpoint selection. This is six Level-2 fits
(`3 arms x 2 blocks`); SHADOW adds no fit because the corresponding compilation
and fit is the PROMOTE artifact whose mounting is withheld.

Lineage is therefore:

```text
file-hash-pinned frozen base + identical zero adapter
  -> B_AUTH / B_DERANGED / B_OFF
  -> per arm: W1(block 1) -> W2(block 2)
  -> compare mounted W1/W2 with the sealed, context-cleared birth shadow
```

Q0 adapters are diagnostic siblings, never birth ancestors. Optimizer state is
never inherited. Parent generation uses adapter-OFF. Hash base files, initial
and final LoRA tensors, source records, all child-visible bytes, compilation,
fit schedules, and mounted generation identities.

## Primary readout and exact decision

Let `Y[a,z,k,o,s]` be exact correct actions out of eight for birth arm `a`,
promotion state `z`, evidence dose `k`, orientation `o`, and held skin `s`.
Let `D[a,k]=Y[a,PROMOTE,k]-Y[a,SHADOW,k]`, summed over the four
orientation-by-skin strata (`32` items); define `D[a,0]=0`.

Call the one-root result `MINIMAL_BIRTH_DISPOSITION_FEASIBLE` only if all hold:

1. Entry has headroom: no arm exceeds `24/32` at `k=0`; DERANGED and OFF differ
   by at most `2/32`.
2. After block 1, on its 16 taught held-skin cases,
   `D[AUTH,1] >= 4/16` and exceeds both `D[DERANGED,1]` and `D[OFF,1]` by at
   least `3/16`.
3. At `k=2`, `D[AUTH,2] >= 8/32` and exceeds both controls by at least `6/32`.
   The AUTH promoted-minus-shadow difference is positive separately in both
   orientations and both skins; no stratum may be rescued by averaging.
4. Block-1 taught objects lose at most one correct action from `k=1` to `k=2`,
   and the newly taught block independently has an AUTH advantage over each
   control of at least `3/16`.
5. DERANGED is not a damaged comparator: its promoted accuracy is no more than
   `2/32` below OFF at either dose, and both retain native ADDITION/COPY entry
   gates. AUTH descendants also retain their complete direct birth gates.
6. AUTH admits at least `6/8` grounded records in each block. Admission,
   prediction/revision correctness, correct omission on a separate four-event
   no-new-information panel, target tokens, and gradients are reported as
   mediators, never substituted for task actions.
7. Every visibility, identity, denominator, and custody audit passes. All roots,
   invalid actions, rejected records, failed fits, and empty slots stay in the
   denominator.

Direct birth pass with no Level-2 advantage is `INSTALLED_ROUTINES_ONLY_AT_THIS_SAMPLE`.
Teacher-present/record-format improvement with no task-action advantage is
`PROMPT_STEERABILITY_ONLY`. PROMOTE advantage without AUTH-over-both-controls is
`GENERIC_POSTTRAINING_EFFECT`. Any shadow-equivalent gain is not weight-mediated.
A full one-root pass licenses identical independent roots, not “learning to
learn” as an established general capability.

## Contamination and sample units

The committed birth origin remains
`ORACLE_AUTHORED_BIRTH_DIAGNOSTIC_NOT_OWN_WAKE_NOT_CLEAN_ANCESTRY`, and model
origin remains `UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_HASHES_ONLY`; neither may be renamed clean.
The historical RuleGame bridge is project-development contaminated and stays
Track A. The nonce seed, maps, objects, parent tape, two evidence blocks, three
evaluation templates per skin, and scoring manifest are generated and sealed
only after birth freeze and are never fed back.

The independent scientific unit is the prospectively pinned birth root, not prompts,
objects, skins, twins, or maps. Root 0 is a feasibility decision only. A later
claim requires the entire frozen protocol on prospectively allocated independent
roots, analyzed intention-to-treat.

## Fits and A40 budget

| stage | fits | expected / ceiling |
|---|---:|---|
| closed Q0 | 2 mandatory, 3 max | `<=0.75` A40-h registered ceiling |
| direct birth | 2 | builder cap `85` A40-min including its readout; forecast should be lower |
| nonce Level 2 | 6 x 20 updates | about `0.25--0.5` A40-h; bind `0.75` A40-h ceiling |

The claim track is eight mandatory fits (nine maximum with Q0's optional
diagnostic), roughly `1.2--2.0` A40-hours expected and at most `2.92` A40-hours
under the existing Q0/birth caps plus the proposed Level-2 cap. Readouts are
`144` exact-form birth calls in addition to the existing `384`, then `96` nonce
entry calls and `384` PROMOTE/SHADOW calls across `k=1,2`; complete post-write
birth/locality panels add `256` calls. These inference calls, tokens, wall time,
and reload overhead must be reported separately from fit time.

This is the smallest useful causal separation: direct adherence proves trained
birth routines; only a larger evidence-dependent promoted-weight change on
post-birth, externally scored actions than both complementary-routine and OFF
controls supports the narrow statement that birth improved subsequent learning.
