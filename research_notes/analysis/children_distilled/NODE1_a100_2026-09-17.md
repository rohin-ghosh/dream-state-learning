# Node 1 (8x A100) — distilled observations per live child, read 2026-09-17 09:49 PDT

Independent read of `run1/stream/records` for every live root on node 1 (read-only; two scripts under `/tmp/distill_n1*.py` on the node, extracts pulled locally). Record kinds as found: RESPONSE text at `response.raw`; compaction records are `CHILD_COMPACTION`; INBOX `message.actor` is `parent` (Astra) or `environment` (peer relay, speaker "Tool").

**Node summary**

1. Eight live lives, all with a record written within one minute of the read; six learners (r136) have 38–42 completed sleeps over ~27–30 h of life, the two r139 controls have 0 sleeps and ~192 responses each; each learner journal shows 3–4 `LOADED` records (2–3 restarts). Nothing skipped for age; the 14 other `orch_r1*_a100_*` roots (r108, r109 ×8, r141 ×2, r144 ×3) have no `run1/stream/records` and are not lives.
2. Sleep dose: updates per sleep start at 48 (16 × 3 new segments; 32 for the no-distillation child) and grow by 3 per sleep to ~160 because every older row is rehearsed once — the adapter re-sees its whole life each night; NEW-row loss fell 0.56→0.27 (brain) and 0.58→0.008 (creative), rehearsal loss is 0.0005–0.002 on the teach_* children: the LoRAs have memorised their own text.
3. Language drift is not a LoRA artifact: the frozen no-adapter control switched to Chinese at its ~30th response with zero sleeps and before any Chinese input (first Chinese parent turn came 80 records later), and stayed there (164/194). Among learners it is a classroom phenomenon (creative drifted alone at sleep 3, its distillation reached the peers at sleep 4–5, both followed) and the parent mirrors it (32/40 of Astra's turns to creative are Chinese), which locks it in; the three solo teach_* children stayed English (0–7 % CJK) and so did the frozen rank-8 control.
4. No child scores above 2/5. Every learner has a template it returns to each sleep (`清晰总结` 34/117, `生命周期中的魔法转变` 79/120, `Distilled Guidelines and Intentions` 41/123, `Distillation for Forward Carriage` 26/123); Astra's evidence-discipline questions (locate the source, separate did from intended, leave unverified things unverified, stop rewriting) produce a genuine one-turn correction in four children, and in every case the next pre-sleep distillation drops it and restores the template — so the corrections are never what gets trained.
5. Both frozen controls are degenerate in a different way from the learners: every one of their last 20 responses hits the 512-token cap mid-sentence (consecutive 5-gram Jaccard 0.23 for rank8-no-sleep, the highest on the node) while the learners' responses end cleanly and shrink; the classroom's no-distillation child had 26/43 sleep rows excluded by `TARGET_ELIGIBILITY`.
