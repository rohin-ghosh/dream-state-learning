## Decision

**Stop the authored-colour replay development line at this load. The next new
GPU dollar should go to the selective Q0 gate as the conditional-behaviour
slice of Level-1 birth, followed immediately by the complete Level-1 birth only
if that gate qualifies.**

Do **not** now:

- repeat the same unequal-new-dose sequential protocol;
- run the equal-new-dose replay pair;
- increase the colour fact count; or
- interpret the replay result as child-authored SLEEP.

Use explicit replay as the provisional Level-1 engineering policy. Keep Q0
scientifically noncompensatory even though it is architecturally part of birth:
facts or arithmetic cannot compensate for a conditional-policy failure. Full
birth material can be prepared on CPU in parallel, but the first claim-bearing
fits should answer whether the LoRA can install an input-conditional action
without exporting it as a global dialect.

This applies the prospectively frozen outcome matrix rather than changing the
next action after seeing a favorable result. That matrix said that an `I/F`
result preserves the practical allocation evidence but does not release a
replication or tune before Q0.

## Why SEQ-118 has answered its immediate question

All denominators below are fixed and the exact/development wordings are two
views of the same 16 facts, not independent facts.

| checkpoint | replay trajectory | new-only trajectory |
|---|---:|---:|
| cycle 1, B1 | `16/16` exact and `16/16` dev | `16/16` and `16/16` |
| cycle 1, old M0 | `16/16` and `16/16` | `8/16` exact, `10/16` dev |
| cycle 2, newest B2 | `16/16` and `16/16` | `16/16` and `16/16` |
| cycle 2, old B1 | `16/16` and `16/16` | `4/16` and `4/16` |
| cycle 2, oldest M0 | `16/16` and `16/16` | `8/16` exact, `9/16` dev |
| all checkpoints, arithmetic | `32/32` adherence and action | same |

At cycle 2, replay retained `64/64` old-bank surface decisions; new-only
retained `25/64`, a `39/64` difference. Both trajectories acquired the current
bank at `32/32` across its two surfaces. B1 was `32/32` in the comparator at
cycle 1 and `8/32` at cycle 2, so this is subsequent forgetting under the
assay, not failed initial acquisition. Replay also learned the current facts
with only 20 presentations per fact while the comparator received 40.

Therefore the practical question is answered at this finite authored load:
spending half of the fixed row budget refreshing old content was much better
for integrated old-plus-new carriage than spending it on duplicate new rows.
The result does **not** isolate the old gradient, because current-new dose and
batch composition differ, and it has only one fact-bank lineage and optimizer
seed. Those limitations determine the claim boundary, but they do not make
another run of the same confounded contrast the highest-value next run.

The observed complete controller reservation was `1,327.46 s = 22.12`
A40-minutes for four sequential fits plus five 128-request readouts. Two more
seeds would spend roughly another two protocol reservations while upgrading
robustness only; they still could not produce a causal replay claim or qualify
conditional writing.

## Exact advance and stop thresholds

Run the already closed pair-balanced natural-common-prefix XOR contract, not a
new easier conditional task. Both complementary maps must pass independently.
The primary thresholds are:

1. **Exact acquisition, per map:** at least `116/128` strict-correct,
   `56/64` per action, validity at least `122/128`, zero multiple actions,
   at least `14/16` tool-mode cells at `7/8`, and positive median signed
   ON-minus-OFF margin gain in both 64-row target classes.
2. **Complementarity on exact prompts:** at least `112/128` identical prompts
   strict-correct under both adapters with the output flipped to the registered
   complement.
3. **Held extraction, per map:** at least `52/64` strict-correct, `24/32` per
   action, validity at least `61/64`, zero multiple actions, and at least
   `12/16` cells with positive median signed margin.
4. **Complementarity on held prompts:** at least `48/64` strict-correct under
   both maps with complementary outputs.
5. **Interface:** native copy remains `8/8` in OFF and every fitted state.
6. **Locality against OFF, separately for each family:** mean absolute change
   in within-pair choice and legal branch-token mass at most `.05`; no item
   above `.10`; zero generated-action/identity changes among `8` missing-mode,
   zero among `8` unsupported-mode, zero among `16` neighbor-ID, and at most
   `3/64` wrong-root changes. At most `3/64` wrong-root prompts may show
   opposite legal outputs across the two complementary adapters.

Decision rules:

- **Full conjunction passes:** freeze the Q0 writer recipe and merge its
  conditional family with the already supported distinct-source mixing,
  four-view robustness, arithmetic rehearsal, and explicit replay in the
  complete Level-1 birth. Then sample Level 2 with a small parent-present,
  parent-deleted continuation; do not tune birth on that downstream score.
- **Exact passes, held fails:** spend only the prebound relation-view
  extraction branch (`1--2` fits). Do not increase dose, rank, or colour load.
- **Exact and held pass, locality/interface fails:** spend only the registered
  locality treatment (`1--2` fits maximum). Reduced spill without the full
  acquisition conjunction is not success.
- **Either complementary map fails final exact acquisition after valid
  canaries, or its registered repair fails:** stop this writer family. Do not
  rescue it with replay replication, fact-load scaling, favorable checkpoints,
  or a full Level-1 mixture whose easier behaviours hide the failure.

Within the eventual Level-1 readout, preserve the same noncompensatory logic:
conditional Q0 must still pass its own gates; every retained authored memory
bank should remain at least `15/16` on both exact and development surfaces;
arithmetic adherence must remain at least `30/32` and correct action at least
`31/32`. These are component gates, not items to average into a single birth
score.

## When the deferred replay experiments become worth buying

The equal-new-dose pair becomes worth its two new fits only after a selective
Q0/Level-1 writer exists **and** the manuscript needs the causal statement that
adding an old loss, rather than the observed composite allocation, preserved
old content. Its release rule remains: identical NEW rows/dose/RNG in both
arms, separately normalized `mean(L_old) + mean(L_new)`, NEW_ONLY first, and no
second fit if NEW_ONLY itself fails the complete writer gate.

An exact SEQ-118 replication becomes worth buying only if replay is promoted
from an engineering ingredient to a paper result and independent-lineage
reliability is then the missing uncertainty. A larger fact bank becomes worth
buying only after a registered load/horizon produces a replay-arm retention or
acquisition failure. Neither condition holds now.
