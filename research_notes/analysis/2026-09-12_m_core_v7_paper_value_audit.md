# M-core v7 paper-value audit

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Scope: independent paper-strategy audit of
`2026-09-12_m_core_exact_two_cycle_design_v7.md`, read in full against the
Dream--LoRA--Think thesis and current writer evidence. I did not inspect or
change builder code, run tests or models, use a GPU, or treat any v7 package,
qualification, TEXT root, fit, receipt, or endpoint as existing evidence.

## Recommendation

**HOLD implementation promotion. Preserve v7 as a high-integrity mechanism
specification, but do not put it on the paper critical path until one writer
recipe has passed input-conditioned binding and locality on fresh roots.**

If that prerequisite passes, seal a v7-derived two-root scout and run 12 fits
as the composition kill gate; do not mislabel that subset as a completed v7
program. Decide then whether the paper needs a finite typed-relay result
strongly enough to justify the rest. The full 144-fit
ceiling is statistically coherent for v7's chosen finite-generator reliability
claim, but it is not presently justified by the paper objective or by measured
writer yield. The dominant near-term cost is likely the contract's engineering
and audit surface, not its GPU fits.

## Contract correctness is not strategic value

These are separate judgments.

**Contract judgment.** V7 is unusually careful about custody, matched work,
visibility, negative controls, independent roots, adverse failure handling,
and exact statistics. Its six-fit root is a coherent attempt to isolate source
information, link organization, carrier necessity, and old-plus-new writing in
one joined relay. It is not yet ready to freeze as executable bytes: the same-cut
fresh causal audit identifies three material gaps--admission-to-training-deck
lineage, an evidenced rather than asserted process capability boundary, and
literal permitted tensor-difference closures. Those are contract blockers,
not requests for more trained arms.

**Strategy judgment.** Even after those repairs and a flawless run, v7 answers
a narrow mechanism question. Most of its 2,365-line surface--JCS/Merkle law,
typed handles, fixed-size RPCs, exhaustive failure precedence, interval-union
cost accounting, and receipt custody--raises confidence in that answer but
does not enlarge the paper claim. This rigor is valuable if the finite relay is
a main paper result or reusable benchmark artifact. It is poor critical-path
spend while the project cannot yet write two opposing input-conditioned
actions without collapsing to a global output habit.

## What a full pass would materially establish

Conditional on the contract repairs, qualified bindings, and all stated gates,
v7 would provide strong bounded evidence for the following:

- A model-turn-custodied child can act, observe public outcomes, and recover
  the preassigned lane pair uniquely indicated by the supplied ablation
  evidence before support and row-template reveal.
- Compiled truthful 3/1 source evidence changes a later source choice relative
  to a matched truthful 2/2 presentation; an authenticated selected link works
  where a preassigned deranged link does not.
- A fresh actor's later B/C actions depend on the mounted trained carrier, and
  cuts plus same-handle payload swaps localize the required source/link rows.
- After an authenticated public experimental outcome, a second clean-base
  cumulative fit can carry the exact old deck plus the corresponding new row,
  preserve the required old link, and solve a delayed D trace that needs both;
  old/new cuts and swaps localize both dependencies.
- Across the sealed finite root/noise generator, each tested binary component
  succeeds with probability greater than one half if at least 12 of 16 CONF
  roots pass under the stated iid premise.

That is a respectable **finite typed THINK--DREAM--SLEEP relay result**. It
would materially strengthen the mechanism/substrate section of a paper and
close the present “two successive cumulative writes with old-plus-new use”
gap. It would also be much stronger than separately narrating unjoined writer,
source, and retrieval assays.

The wording must remain narrow. The DREAM-like choice is recovery of a
preassigned evidence-indicated pair, not discovery of a representation or a
causal benefit of a learned selection policy. The second SLEEP is a fresh
clean-base cumulative reconstruction, not an in-place update of the S1
adapter. The reader, compiler, action grammar, row templates, query types, and
success functions are fixed parts of a synthetic benchmark.

## Central paper claims it would not establish

Even a perfect v7 result leaves the launch objective's central contribution
absent:

- no developmental parent or matched active-sham childhood treatment;
- no evidence that parenting changes what the child notices, diagnoses,
  selects, writes, or rehearses;
- no H1 test after parent, lesson, prompt, childhood text store, cache, and
  scaffold removal;
- no H2 `parenting x adult-SLEEP` gain/AUC/slope interaction;
- no independently raised child roots, so no population claim over learners;
- no autonomous free-form DREAM policy, native search, planning improvement,
  or learned decision about what merits storage;
- no more-than-two-cycle stability, broad retention, interference curve, or
  absolute improvement over an adult lifetime; and
- no comparison with a qualified evolving text-memory agent, hence no LoRA
  advantage, baseline saturation, or amortized-compute claim.

V7 therefore cannot support the headline that parenting builds a self-learning
flywheel. It can support one necessary mechanism link beneath that headline.
Calling its preassigned selection “parented learning-to-learn” or its
two-cycle reconstruction “continual improvement” would be a category error.

## Is 144 fits scientifically justified?

The arithmetic is clear: 8 DEV roots plus 16 CONF roots, at six fits per
complete root, gives 144. Six fits **within one root** are defensible for v7's
factorization: three S1 carriers isolate truthful source and selected-link
organization; two outcome-specific S2 carriers plus a grounded equal-work PAD
control isolate the new write while checking old preservation. Inference-only
cuts and swaps efficiently reuse those fits.

The **number of roots** is not yet comparably justified. Sixteen CONF roots
with cutoff 12 give the displayed exact one-sided binomial p-value `.0384`
for `H0: p <= .5`, but v7 provides no target power, practically relevant
success probability, measured writer-yield estimate, or argument that
`p > .5` on this synthetic generator is the paper's scarce claim. The eight
DEV roots are a reasonable reliability/debugging filter, but powering five
component prevalences separately is less paper-relevant than one joint relay
endpoint. Root replication also varies benchmark/noise roots around one fixed
child; it cannot substitute for independent child replication in H1/H2.

So the ceiling is a valid **certification plan conditional on choosing this
benchmark claim**, not a current scientific necessity. It should not be read
as 144 fits worth buying merely because the protocol can account for them.

## Smallest information-efficient ladder and kill rule

### Prerequisite ladder before v7 promotion

1. **Two-input writer sign canary.** Use two opposing, target-independent
   prompts and the budgeted first two real updates per objective arm. Require
   the same raw action log-odds to move in opposite target-correct directions,
   with bit-stable pre-update reads. If it does not, stop that objective family
   after four total updates. A common logit shift is a kill, not partial
   success.
2. **Fresh selective-writer qualification.** Run one frozen recipe on fresh
   complementary maps/roots. Require exact and held-form conditional action,
   both-label recall, opposite-label pair success, wrong-root/locality/spill,
   interface, save/reload, and cumulative old+new cases. Any constant-action
   basin, failed root/map, or spill failure kills M promotion; do not rescue it
   with rank/LR/dose sweeps selected on the same outcomes.
3. **Only then pay the v7 engineering cost.** Repair the three contract gaps,
   generate schemas, rematerialize independently, and run the independent
   checker. Failure here stops before model work.

### Smallest faithful v7 execution

Follow v7's own state machine; do not call an informal subset a v7 pass:

1. Package/checker and both eight-case qualifications pass exactly.
2. Run TEXT T1--T4, extending to T5--T8 only under v7's 2/4 or 3/4 rule. A
   failed final TEXT gate stops all fits.
3. Run D1/D2 S1: six fits. If either `Q_S1=0`, stop.
4. Run D1/D2 S2: six more fits. If either root has `I_R=0`, v7 skips late DEV
   and cannot reach confirmation. This **12-fit point is the first decisive
   integrated composition result**; no root-specific repair or replacement is
   allowed.
5. If both pass, v7 opens D3--D8 (up to 36 more fits) and requires at least 6/8
   joint successes.
6. A DEV pass opens the 96 CONF fits on the frozen 16 roots, with no
   replacement and v7's fixed rule.

A favorable v7 run has no discretionary paper-value pause after D1/D2 or DEV;
its state machine continues. Stopping there would make an incomplete
exploratory execution, not a v7 result. Therefore the decision to fund the
whole possible 144-fit program belongs before exact v7 promotion, or the
state machine must be changed and resealed as a successor.

An information-efficient **successor protocol**, if Rohin wants a smaller
paper experiment rather than v7 certification, would make joint `I_R` the one
primary endpoint: two complete development roots (12 fits), then eight fresh
confirmation roots (48 fits), requiring at least 7/8. Under `p=.5`, the exact
one-sided tail is `9/256=.0352`. This cuts the trained ceiling to 60 fits and
asks the paper-relevant question--does the whole bounded relay work?--while
leaving component contrasts diagnostic. It has lower power than 16 roots and
would require a newly sealed contract; it must not be relabelled as v7.

## Promotion decision

Do not promote v7 now. The current evidence cut still shows global action
habits rather than input-conditioned binding, cumulative coexistence with
large spill rather than a selective writer, and zero usable output from the
latest partial-prefix record write. V7's own qualification would therefore be
expected to stop before its science fits, after substantial implementation
work.

Revisit promotion only when all three conditions hold: a writer passes the
fresh binding/locality gateway; the three contract gaps are closed; and the
paper owner explicitly chooses a finite typed-relay result over spending the
same engineering time on the first parent-deleted H1/H2 pilot. Until then, v7
is a valuable specification and falsification map, not the highest-value next
experiment.
