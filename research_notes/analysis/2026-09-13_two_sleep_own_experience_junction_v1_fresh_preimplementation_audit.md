## Verdict

**REWORK before source authoring. NO-GO for materialization, model calls,
fits, or GPU execution under these bytes.**

TSJ-v1 has the right high-level causal skeleton and is substantially closer to
the active objective than the component tests. In particular, the authentic
path prospectively demands child-executed action/outcome receipts, exact child
EVENT and LINK spans, continued tensors rather than a rebuilt adapter, a clean
actor reset, child-issued addresses, a primary post-S1 action that reveals a
hidden outcome, a cumulative S2 write, OLD and NEW content redirections, and
old-route retention. The XOR route law is internally correct, and the maximum
claim is commendably restricted to two DEV worlds.

But the current binding cannot yet prove its advertised controlled
conjunction. There are six claim-fatal contradictions and several missing
execution laws. Most importantly, the design requires S2 rows and N-swapped
text ceilings before the authentic child can lawfully create or see those
rows; it forbids the compiler from inventing or rebinding semantics while
simultaneously requiring synthetic rebindings and donor/filler rows; and it
replays a new one-pass birth curriculum rather than the selected
M-COMBINE-4 presentation tape. A positive produced by an implementation that
silently resolves those contradictions would be implementation-selected
science.

This is a **design REWORK**, not evidence that the intended mechanism is
impossible and not a request for another storage, prompt, rank, or heat scout.

## What is already scientifically sound

The following parts should be preserved through a successor:

1. The longitudinal authentic lineage is correctly defined as
   `BIRTH -> S1_AUTH_LINKED -> S2_AUTH_OLD_NEW`; LoRA tensors are continued,
   and fresh optimizers do not falsely create fresh children.
2. OLD parents are witnessed in distinct cold opportunities, LINK admission
   requires both receipt-bearing parents, and malformed/omitted child rows
   remain failures.
3. The later goal and hidden N binding are excluded from authentic S1
   training; the primary probe exposes an ordinary precommitted public
   outcome.
4. Native READ is candidate-free ordinary generation from the mounted
   adapter. Only the actor sees goal/current state, and the host is not allowed
   to repair, rank, or select a memory row.
5. O, T, N, and goal twins are coherent redirections rather than merely
   corrupt banks. The atom-only and relevant/irrelevant-cut concepts are the
   correct kinds of controls for stored-LINK value.
6. S2 separately requires exact OLD carriage, exact NEW carriage, delayed
   behavior, O and N redirection, and re-passing OLD tasks. That is much
   stronger than treating a second fit or a final endpoint alone as evidence.
7. RAW is correctly made mandatory only for a DREAM-compilation advantage,
   and the document refuses reliability, lifetime, parenting, emergence,
   physical-compression, and strong-memory-superiority claims from two DEV
   worlds.

## P0 findings: claim-fatal until repaired

### P0.1 — The S2 zero-fit gate is temporally impossible and leaks the future

Section 2.3 says no model or earlier task may see N or an H binding before the
child's public probe. Section 3 says the exact NEW EVENT/LINK rows do not exist
until the authentic S1 primary episode and scheduled complementary formation.
Nevertheless Section 7 requires, **before any TSJ adapter is trained**:

- S2 exact-supplied and active-linked-text performance on the complete
  admitted OLD+NEW bank; and
- exact text/service N_SWAP redirection.

Before S1 fitting and action, there are no admitted child NEW spans or public
NEW receipts to put in those conditions. Substituting generator-authored ideal
rows violates the “exact same admitted child rows” law. Showing N/H rows to a
model before the probe violates the hidden-outcome law even if that model call
is not allowed to update weights. Silently postponing the calls violates the
single global zero-fit gate.

**Required repair:** split closure into two immutable stages:

1. `PRE_S1_ZERO_FIT`, after authentic OLD formation but before any S1 fit,
   may run only OLD ceilings, OLD swaps/cuts, interface checks, and source
   oracles.
2. `POST_NEW_PRE_S2_ZERO_FIT`, after the authentic primary and complementary
   outcomes and exact NEW child admissions, but before any S2 fit, runs S2
   supplied/active text, N twins, and S2 cut oracles using those actual rows.

Checker-private potential outcomes may be mechanically certified before the
probe, but no model-visible fixture containing N/H may be exposed before the
authentic disclosure boundary. Bind an execution order proving that the
authentic primary actor is the first model-visible path that can reveal its
N outcome and that non-primary S1 rollouts cannot PROBE into a hidden outcome.

### P0.2 — The control banks violate the authentic-only compiler contract

Section 5.1 says each sleep receives only exact admitted child targets and
that the compiler may not invent, infer, orient, join, correct, or add an
EVENT or LINK. Yet Section 6 requires:

- O_SWAP and T_SWAP to change destinations and coherent LINK pairings;
- N_SWAP to change the two new destinations and crossing LINKs;
- atom-only arms to insert target-disjoint filler LINK units; and
- cut arms to insert a donor route block.

Those changed/filler/donor rows were neither emitted by the authentic child
nor supported by its actual receipts. They cannot simultaneously be exact
authentic child targets and counterfactual laboratory interventions. Fake
receipt citations would be worse: they would turn provenance into a false
claim.

**Required repair:** define two disjoint source laws.

- `AUTHENTIC_CORPUS` alone is restricted to exact admitted child bytes and
  receipt-grounded meaning-preserving views.
- `CONTROL_TRANSFORM` is a deterministic, presealed, checker-authored
  intervention over an authenticated source bank. It must be labeled
  synthetic, use its own non-factual provenance type, preserve the promised
  ID/token/batch constraints, and never enter the authentic lineage or claim
  of child authorship.

Alternatively, separately execute truthful counterfactual worlds and obtain
new child records, but then they are additional lived branches with a larger
formation/cost/independence design. The successor must choose one route. It
cannot retain the present universal “compiler never rebinds” sentence and
silently exempt most controls in code.

### P0.3 — Birth replay is not the bound M-COMBINE presentation tape, and
controller preservation has no actual gate

M-COMBINE-4 Section 11 expressly requires a later successor to name the
selected composition presentation tape/checkpoint exactly, define replay over
that exact tape, and recalculate all counts after the D1/D2 branch. TSJ-v1
instead replays each of the 256 unique birth units once: 256 presentations and
64 updates in both sleeps. The selected M-COMBINE tape is 1,024 presentations
at D1 or 2,048 cumulative presentations at D2. The new one-pass replay is an
untested dose/treatment, not the selected tape.

The problem is substantive, not only accounting. `COMPLETE_TSJ` item 3 claims
BIRTH acquisition/preservation through both sleeps, but Sections 8.1/8.2 test
only generic interface/copy/action canaries. They do not re-run a frozen
M-COMBINE intervention/chain panel after S1 and S2. A write could erase the
selected controller while teaching a task-specific read/route surface; tensor
ancestry alone would still pass the current “same adapter” story.

**Required repair:** bind the exact replay branch conditional on the selected
D1 or D2 endpoint, including example multiplicities, batch order, masks, RNG,
and optimizer updates. Either replay the selected tape or prospectively name a
reduced replay treatment and qualify it before TSJ. Add a frozen,
target-disjoint M-COMBINE preservation panel after authentic S1 and S2 (and
the corresponding control states where claimed). Recalculate every update,
presentation, padded-token/FLOP, time cap, and fit-total derivative.

### P0.4 — The two roots are paired complements, not independently generated,
and the “every root passes” rule is not implemented

Section 2 makes root 1 the bitwise complement of root 0 under one declared
generator seed. That produces useful exact balance, but it is dependence by
construction. It is false to call the pair “two independently generated
roots,” as Sections 0/9 and the critical-path prerequisite do. Both also begin
from the same selected one-seed BIRTH checkpoint. This can support a paired,
fixed-child two-world DEV kill gate; it is not two independent child/world
lineages or a reliability sample.

The pass rule is also compensatory despite saying otherwise. Several controls
are gated only over pooled 2-root panels:

- atom-only can be `2/4` with both successes concentrated in one root;
- BIRTH_ONLY or FOREIGN can be `2/4` in one root;
- READ_RESPONSE_CUT need only cost `2/4`, potentially both on one root; and
- the relevant cut may score `1/2`, allowing one root's sole primary cut task
  to remain correct.

There is then no root-local predicate that determines whether that root passed
items 5, 7, or 8 of `COMPLETE_TSJ`, despite the instruction that every root
passes individually.

**Required repair:** choose and state one estimand.

- For the smallest DEV, rename these as **two paired complementary worlds
  conditional on one BIRTH child**, remove every independence phrase, and
  define the conjunction at the pair level; or
- allocate genuinely independent world/learner root seeds and accept that
  exact O/T/N balance is no longer guaranteed with n=2 (or use a larger
  prospectively blocked set).

In either case write a complete root-by-condition truth table. If each root is
to pass, every indispensable/irrelevant cut and response intervention must
have a root-local required direction, and no root's missing causal effect may
be rescued by the other. Nested goals, cuts, arms, and decodes are never extra
independent n. Two roots remain a DEV falsifier only.

### P0.5 — READ_RESPONSE_CUT is an invalid-return alarm, not a clean memory
content intervention

At the decisive address-specific READ, Section 4.1 replaces the authentic
returned block with an equal-token, target-disjoint irrelevant block. If the
child requested `READ EVENT <id>` or `READ LINKS_FROM <event_id>`, an unrelated
block ordinarily changes the requested ID, support status, row type, parent,
or evidence citations. The actor can fail because the memory service broke
its public contract, not because the authentic binding content mediated its
action. Equal token count does not create same-surface identity.

**Required repair:** prebind a request-valid response twin that preserves the
exact request, returned address/row type, syntax, line count, token count,
evidence-status surface, and all non-treatment bytes while changing only the
named destination/parent binding coherently. It must remain a legal response
under the public interface. Pair the fork on exact prior actor bytes, decode
configuration, and RNG stream. Keep a malformed/unrelated response only as a
separate error-handling diagnostic; it cannot license self-cued semantic
mediation.

### P0.6 — Native READ is not implementation-bound and does not match the
“one local item per recurrent turn” rationale

The critical-path ruling makes P-CHAIN-2 non-gating because the junction is
supposed to retrieve one local atom per recurrent turn. TSJ's mandatory first
cue is instead `READ EVENTS_AT S`, which returns all events at S—two relevant
start events in this topology. `READ LINKS_FROM` is likewise specified as
returning all matches. This is a multi-record parametric aggregation/index
operation, not the already qualified single-address reader. The 10-READ S2
budget appears to rely on this fan-out; fetching two start addresses and then
both rows separately would exceed the bound for a complete two-branch scan.

The document also does not freeze the model-visible templates for any native
READ, the seven memory views, aggregate response ordering, duplicate/MISS
semantics, exact LINK-address release, or the NEW EVENT/LINK record prompts.
The cold reader arithmetic tests direct EVENT/LINK blocks and misses but does
not separately require exact `EVENTS_AT` aggregate/index outputs. Source code
would therefore decide a central learned interface after the binding.

**Required repair:** render and bind every exact actor, native-reader, record,
and multi-LINK prompt/target before source implementation. State whether
`EVENTS_AT` returns addresses or complete rows, prove the S1/S2 read budgets
under that choice, and add direct cold aggregate/index gates at every required
node for every native arm. If fan-out aggregation remains, call it an
additional unqualified junction seam; do not justify P-CHAIN's scheduling by
saying every turn reads one item.

## P1 findings: required for the advertised controls or exact execution

### P1.1 — ACTIVE_LINKED_TEXT is a carrier ceiling, not a strong on-policy
memory opponent

The active text condition mounts the exact rows authored in the authentic
DLT life and gives them to the frozen BIRTH actor. It does not begin with its
own empty store, choose its own primary action, receive its own potential
outcome, author/admit its own NEW rows, and update its own memory branch. It is
a valuable conditional same-semantics service ceiling. It is not an
independent active-text agent completing the own-action -> outcome -> update
-> delayed-action chain, and it cannot support a whole-system superiority,
noninferiority, or “strong opponent” statement.

**Required repair:** choose the claim before implementation. If TSJ is only a
mechanism junction, rename ACTIVE_LINKED_TEXT everywhere as a
`SAME_AUTH_HISTORY_ACTIVE_TEXT_CEILING`, require and report it, and explicitly
defer strong-baseline comparison. If the active goal requires a strong text
baseline in this experiment, fork a concurrent on-policy text branch from the
same pre-life opportunity: its own actions generate its own eligible store and
second outcome, with prospectively matched opportunity, query/returned-token
curves, persistent-store accounting, and no post-result root selection. Do not
require DLT to beat a constructive exact-text ceiling unless superiority is a
separately powered estimand.

### P1.2 — The second-write comparison is not update/dose matched

`S2_AUTH_OLD_NEW` receives 800 memory updates plus birth replay, including a
second 200 presentations of every OLD unit. Its “no-second-write comparator”
is the earlier S1 checkpoint, which receives none of those additional OLD or
birth updates. Exact native NEW generation and N_SWAP redirection can still
show that NEW content entered the continued adapter, but S2-versus-S1 behavior
is not a pure second-write/add-NEW contrast. Extra OLD rehearsal, birth
rehearsal, optimizer exposure, or generic training effects are confounded.

**Required repair:** add an `S2_OLD_PLUS_MATCHED_FILLER` continuation from the
same S1 snapshot with the exact S2 updates, replay, target-token count,
padding/FLOPs, RNG schedule, and no NEW meaning. Require authentic S2 to beat
or redirect relative to it on the delayed endpoint while both preserve OLD.
If this arm is declined, retain S1 only as an untrained no-NEW reference and
do not describe the S2-minus-S1 difference as the causal effect of the second
write.

### P1.3 — The RAW S2 corpus cannot currently contain the promised authentic
NEW meanings

RAW is forbidden from using isolated semantic rows and is supposed to contain
the exact lived chronology. But Section 5.3 says the eligible raw slice for the
primary NEW episode begins only at the goal-neutral frontier formation
boundary, which Section 2.3 places after the scored primary route/probe. Read
literally, that excludes the primary NEW action/outcome/record while S2 RAW is
also asserted to contain four NEW units and the same two NEW EVENT/two crossing
LINK meanings. The exact 16-unit construction is not given.

**Required repair:** enumerate every RAW S1/S2 unit byte-for-byte and show how
the primary NEW public PROBE/outcome/EVENT span is retained while goal, scored
route, evaluator text, and future information remain absent. Then run the
whole-unit exact target-token/padded-FLOP solver. If no such chronology-only
solution exists, RAW is infeasible for this source version and every
DREAM-benefit claim must be removed prospectively rather than after results.

### P1.4 — Decode, RNG, arm coupling, and hidden-outcome execution order are
underbound

Actor generation has call/token caps but no exact decoding algorithm,
temperature/top-p, seed law, stop-token handling, or common-random pairing.
O/T/N fits promise target-token multisets but do not fully bind unit-slot,
token-position, dropout, data-order, and optimizer RNG coupling. These choices
can change a four-task DEV result or turn the response-cut fork into an
uncoupled resample.

The binding also does not state a complete rollout order or forbid every
non-primary S1 condition from issuing a legal PROBE before authentic NEW
formation. A control/ceiling actor must not be the first process to expose a
sealed N outcome.

**Required repair:** bind greedy decoding or an exact per-root common-random
tape, termination parsing, all arm-coupled training streams, rollout order,
and action-family permissions. Record and gate raw bytes; never use best-of,
retry, or a favorable decode.

### P1.5 — The infrastructure replacement rule leaves ITT and the two-root cap
open

Section 9 allows replacement of an INVALID root under a “prospectively written
infrastructure-failure rule,” but that rule, its authority, cutoff, replacement
seed law, and maximum count are absent. Section 10 simultaneously calls two
roots and 24 fits the immutable logical maximum. A later rule could exclude an
unfavorable run as infrastructure and allocate an extra root.

**Required repair:** either permit no replacement in v1—global instrument
failure stops the version—or freeze a closed infrastructure taxonomy,
independent adjudication, replacement seed queue, attempt cap, and resource
arithmetic before any root is materialized. Scientific failure, malformed
child behavior, failed formation, rejected write, timeout caused by the model,
and custody uncertainty remain zero or stop the experiment; they are never
replacement-eligible.

### P1.6 — “No external life-specific store” and “own action” need narrower
wording

The reset actor has no external autobiographical text/vector/event store, but
the public world is reconstructed from a sealed root containing life-specific
topology and potential outcomes. That is necessary environment state. The
absolute phrase “every external life-specific store” is therefore false.

Likewise, all eight OLD actions are scheduled formation actions: the prompt
names the source and legal port card and the child executes the required
EXPLORE command. They are child-executed and outcome-bearing, not autonomous
goal choices. Only the post-S1 primary route/probe is the claimed autonomous
new action.

**Required repair:** say “no external autobiographical/content memory store is
mounted after reset; the precommitted public environment persists,” and say
“child-executed scheduled OLD formation actions” wherever “own action” could be
read as autonomous exploration.
