# Fresh red-team: the smallest defensible lifetime and parenting-by-learning study

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Status: independent watcher-side statistical and experimental audit.  This
changes no builder source, task, model, adapter, job, GPU allocation, claim
authority, release, or submission.  It is not a launch authorization.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The proposed deployment factorial has the right causal spine, but the paper
needs **one five-branch root block**, not two overlapping 2x2s:

```text
fresh root -> parented adult P -> P-RUN, P-FROZEN, P-TEXT
           -> no-teacher adult N -> N-RUN, N-FROZEN
```

One independently raised root block is one replicate.  `P-RUN/P-FROZEN` and
`N-RUN/N-FROZEN` identify whether promoted deployment SLEEP helps and whether
that help differs after parenting.  `P-TEXT` is the paired strong evolving
text-memory opponent.  A separate adult-type by parent-present 2x2 is a
tutoring diagnostic and must not be folded into this lifetime estimand.  The
smallest confirmation is 16 fresh blocks only if a blinded variance audit
supports it; otherwise expand prospectively to 24 or 32.  The cleanest
longitudinal measurement is one root-specific, untouched exam deck reused in
sterile disposable clones at every cut, not seven imperfectly equated forms.

## What the current lifetime evidence actually says

Current CompilerGym lives are developmental diagnostics, not estimates for
the proposed study:

- Across the nine completed ungated R2 lives, the reported mean ON-minus-OFF
  advantage over all repeated checkpoints is `+0.0194` (`SD=.0334`,
  `SE=.0111`); its ordinary descriptive 95% t interval crosses zero.
- A fresh calculation from the two preserved
  `analysis_tables_2026-09-10_*.json` files gives a mean late linear trend in
  ON-minus-OFF advantage from episode 384 through 1024 of `-0.0180` per 1,000
  episodes (`SD=.0778`, `SE=.0259`; only 3/9 positive).
- More sharply, the episode-1024 minus episode-384 change in ON-minus-OFF is
  `-0.0316` on average (`SD=.0372`, `SE=.0124`; 0/9 positive, one exactly
  zero after rounding; descriptive 95% t interval about
  `[-.060,-.003]`).  This is a post-hoc repeated-panel contrast, not a paper
  test, but it points against increasingly useful late writes.
- On the identifier-disjoint historical panel, ungated R2 adapters moved from
  about `+.005` at mid-life to `-.017` at the terminal checkpoint; final minus
  mid-life was negative in 6/9 lives and zero in 3/9.  The historical static
  brief was approximately tied once its experience horizon was matched.
- Seed 606's adapter advantage existed before its first parent brief, and the
  brief mainly changed wording while visible.  The existing RP/R2 comparison
  therefore does not estimate parenting.

The new calculation regresses each life's stored paired difference on the
exogenous episode index over cuts 384--1024 and then summarizes the nine
life-level slopes.  It does not turn the 99 checkpoint values into `n=99`.
The result is diagnostic because the panel is researcher-used, the tasks
repeat, the writer is unqualified, and the historical lives do not contain a
randomized childhood treatment.

## Exact confirmation topology

For each independent root `r`:

1. Draw a new sealed classroom/world seed, deployment-world seed, optimizer
   seed schedule, and actor/parent randomness tape from a pre-enumerated root
   registry.
2. Start two byte-identical children from the same attested birth.  Give `P`
   the frozen strongest adaptive-parent policy and give `N` no teacher.  Both
   receive the same child-generated-token budget, task opportunities,
   THINK/DREAM/SLEEP mechanism, writer opportunities, and developmental age.
   Parent calls, tokens, and latency are treatment costs rather than fake
   matching variables.
3. Delete the parent, its root-local record, nursery prompts, childhood text
   stores, indexes, caches, and query state.  Seal the two adults.
4. Fork `P` into `P-RUN`, `P-FROZEN`, and `P-TEXT`; fork `N` into `N-RUN`
   and `N-FROZEN`.  Keep the five descendants of a root on one node and
   randomize their GPU and launch positions.
5. `RUN` and `FROZEN` execute identical SLEEP compiler/trainer/canary work.
   Only `RUN` transactionally promotes a technically accepted candidate.
   Candidate rejection keeps the previous adapter and the life continues.
   `P-TEXT` keeps the sealed childhood LoRA fixed, begins with an empty
   deployment store, and evolves only its prequalified public text memory.

This estimates the total exact parenting package relative to a regular
**unparented experiential agent**.  It does not isolate the words in one
correction.  A targeted-lesson / active-sham / no-teacher no-write assay
belongs upstream to qualify teaching material and rule out a toxic sham; it
does not need seven full lifetime branches.

## Independent unit, worlds, seeds, and common randomness

The independent unit is the complete fresh root block.  Branches,
checkpoints, exam items, tasks, decode samples, sleeps, and optimizer updates
are nested measurements.

Within a root, all branches receive the same exogenous deployment task at a
given cohort/occurrence index.  Their later actions and outcomes may diverge;
that divergence is part of the recursive treatment effect.  Confirmation
roots independently sample generator worlds and task identities from the
sealed distribution.  Every exposure cohort has new identities and a frozen
novelty/difficulty balance; the lifetime clock is scheduled exposures, never
accepted writes.

Randomness addresses must omit arm, adapter, device, cache, process order,
restart, and wall time.  At minimum they include:

```text
(study, root, phase, cohort, task-occurrence, decision,
 continuation, technical-replicate)
```

Use the same address across matched arms.  Key fit RNG by `(root, sleep,
fit-slot)` and use the same frozen schedule in every branch; never search
optimizer seeds until one scores well.  Remove wall-clock text from prompts.
Before first promotion, paired RUN/FROZEN traces must be byte-identical or the
root fails preflight.  Common random numbers reduce within-root noise; they do
not create additional independent samples.

Primary inference should be a root-level Student interval on the registered
contrasts.  A sign-flip test is only exact if a real randomized assignment
scheme makes its sign random; CRN and randomized GPU positions alone do not.
Report a root bootstrap and sign/win count only as sensitivity analyses.

## Checkpoints and measurement

Use seven fixed cuts `T0,...,T6`, with `T0` at adult deployment entry.  Choose
one horizon from `{128,192,256}` on DEV using only arm-blinded reliability,
headroom, runtime, and baseline behavior.  Freeze the cuts before confirmation.
The anchor must leave at least two earlier increments and at least three later
checkpoints; with a 32-episode SLEEP cadence, a clean 192-episode candidate is
`0,32,64,96,128,160,192`, with `96` as the only candidate late anchor.

The existing proposal uses seven parallel exam forms and rotates them across
roots.  That is valid only if their difficulty is demonstrably equated, and
seven-way balance is awkward at `N=16/24/32`.  The smaller and more precise
choice is:

- generate one source-disjoint report deck per root before any life begins;
- hold that same deck fixed across all seven cuts and all five branches;
- run every item in a sterile disposable clone whose outputs, outcomes,
  scores, caches, and memory never return to any live branch, parent, writer,
  gate, or later root; and
- use the same fixed decode seeds at every cut and arm.

This is a longitudinal test panel, not repeated experience: the child never
receives it.  It removes form-difficulty drift and improves within-root
precision.  A separate DEV/gate deck is mandatory, and confirmation outputs
remain concealed until all root receipts close.  If sterile no-return cannot
be proven, fall back to rotated parallel forms and include the frozen form
schedule in the root summaries.

Start DEV with 24 balanced cases and two fixed decode seeds per case; no
best-of-two selection.  Increase the deck or fixed `K` before confirmation
only if duplicate-score measurement error exceeds the predeclared reliability
limit.  Invalid, missing, over-budget, and illegal outputs score zero.  Keep
task families equal-weighted and report interface failure separately.  The
historical unbounded best-of-trajectory score, which hid invalid late actions,
is not an acceptable paper endpoint.

## Frozen root-level estimands

Let `V_rc(Tj)` be the registered equal-weight value for root `r`, condition
`c`, at cut `Tj`.  Define entry-centered, time-normalized trapezoidal AUC:

```text
A_rc = trapz_j[V_rc(Tj) - V_rc(T0)] / (T6 - T0)
B_Pr = A_r,P-RUN - A_r,P-FROZEN
B_Nr = A_r,N-RUN - A_r,N-FROZEN
D_r  = B_Pr - B_Nr
```

`DEVxSLEEP` passes only when one-sided lower confidence bounds for both
`mean(B_P)` and `mean(D)` exceed their frozen bounds.  Requiring `B_P>0`
prevents an apparent interaction created only by SLEEP harming `N`.

AUC measures sustained finite-horizon treatment benefit.  It does **not**
prove improvement with increasing lifetime.  Define the late estimands
separately:

```text
beta_rc = OLS slope of V_rc(Tj) on exogenous Tj for j >= anchor
G_rc    = V_rc(T6) - V_rc(anchor)
```

Release `LATE` only if every noncompensatory component passes:

1. absolute `P-RUN` late slope and anchor-to-terminal gain are positive by
   prebound practical margins;
2. `P-RUN - P-TEXT` has a positive late-slope advantage and positive terminal
   practical margin;
3. the untouched childhood-skill panel is noninferior at terminal; and
4. a fixed early-deployment information cohort first clears an acquisition
   floor and remains noninferior after later identity-disjoint writes.

Use a fixed sequence `PERSIST -> DEVxSLEEP -> LATE`; later results are
descriptive if an earlier claim gate fails.  The predicates within one claim
form an intersection-union test: no strong endpoint may compensate for a
failed retention or absolute-growth endpoint.  Freeze all superiority,
positive-growth, acquisition, and noninferiority margins from task utility
and DEV noise before confirmation, rather than inheriting `.02` merely because
it appeared in an old plan.

## Baseline strength and the word “saturation”

The minimum paper claim needs finite-horizon superiority over one frozen,
prequalified `P-TEXT` system; it does not need “text memory saturated.”  Seal
the updater, retrieval policy, query budget, context packing, and cost
accounting before any confirmation `P-RUN` output is opened.  Access, store
swap, necessary-record deletion, future/hidden-answer rejection, and
candidate-blind update tests must pass on separate DEV material.

If the paper nevertheless uses **saturation**, add a strictly optional
baseline-only conjunction:

- a DEV-selected late anchor with at least three later increments;
- continued scheduled novelty and at least `.10` oracle/task headroom;
- equivalence, not nonsignificance, of the baseline's late slope and registered
  late gains inside frozen bands;
- no negative decline masquerading as a plateau; and
- a `2,048`-returned-token sensitivity fork whose late slope is also locally
  flat and whose terminal benefit over the native `1,024`-token budget is
  equivalent to less than the frozen margin.

The doubled-read check can be run from saved `P-TEXT` snapshots as sterile
inference forks; it need not become a sixth online life.  If this conjunction
is unpowered or fails, report the two finite curves and omit “saturation.”

## Stopping, attrition, and failures

- No efficacy-based stopping, horizon extension, endpoint switch, root
  deletion, or optimizer retry is allowed in confirmation.
- One excluded executor canary may stop on a mechanical failure.  DEV may
  choose horizon and measurement resolution only under its predeclared,
  outcome-blind rules.
- A rejected writer candidate is an adverse treatment event, not attrition:
  keep the previous adapter, preserve the candidate, count the rejection, and
  continue.
- A treatment-dependent crash, overflow, invalid action, timeout, or missing
  scheduled output stays in the intention-to-treat denominator as zero under
  the frozen rule.
- A whole root may be administratively restarted/excluded only for a
  treatment-blind external failure proven from receipts (wrong bytes, node
  loss, corrupted common tape) before outcome labels are opened.  Use the next
  pre-enumerated reserve root; preserve and disclose the failed root.  Partial
  post-divergence loss is not replaceable.
- Exact checkpoint resume is allowed only when ancestry, optimizer, cursor,
  RNG, task frontier, parent state, and mounted adapter hashes all verify.  A
  resumed root keeps its original identity.
- Analyze all valid randomized root blocks.  Never turn tasks, checkpoints,
  branches, or decoder seeds into extra `n`, and never remove a heavy-tailed
  root after seeing its effect.

## Smallest staged study and sample size

1. **Developmental prerequisites (not lifetime evidence):** selective writer,
   identity-disjoint cumulative retention, authentic action/outcome source,
   qualified evolving-text access, and the no-write lesson/sham/no-teacher
   utility assay.
2. **One excluded executor canary:** one complete four-branch root for 128
   episodes.  It proves cloning, parent deletion, shadow SLEEP, common tapes,
   transactional promotion, and analysis only.
3. **L-DEV:** four fresh five-branch blocks, initially 128 episodes and
   extended only by the frozen arm-blind/headroom rule.  Use them to freeze the
   128/192/256 horizon, anchor, deck/K, practical margins, baseline, and cost.
   Four roots cannot establish efficacy or a trustworthy variance; if the
   operating characteristics remain unstable, DEV expands rather than
   borrowing confirmation outcomes.
4. **Fresh confirmation:** use the first eight blocks as a retained,
   concealed, mean-free internal variance pilot.  An independent reducer may
   release only the covariance/required-N decision.  Select total
   `N in {16,24,32}` from a frozen upper-confidence variance rule sized for the
   most demanding root-level member of the registered conjunction.  If more
   than 32 are required, that claim is `NOT_ESTABLISHED` under the resource
   cap.

The old `SD=.027` is from a different endpoint and only four gated lives.  If
used as a rough planning value with a `.020` effect, the normal approximation
gives about 15 roots; hence 16 is a structural floor, not a guaranteed powered
sample.  `N=24` and `N=32` roughly cover interaction SDs of `.035` and `.040`
under the same simplified calculation.  Re-estimation must use the actual
vector of `B_P`, `D`, late slopes/gains, text contrasts, and retention changes,
not `D` alone.

## Compute envelope

The repository's measured planning numbers imply:

- one 128-episode four-branch executor root: about `50--80 A40-hours`;
- one 256-episode wake branch: about `17.9 A40-hours` before all evaluation
  overhead; and
- one five-branch root at a 192--256 horizon: roughly `90--150 A40-hours`
  after childhood, shadow fits, checkpoints, and probes are included.

Therefore four L-DEV roots are roughly `360--600 A40-hours`, and confirmation
at 16/24/32 roots is approximately `1,440--2,400` /
`2,160--3,600` / `2,880--4,800 A40-hours`.  These are planning ranges, not
reservations or A100 conversions.  Before launch, replace them with a measured
one-root profile split into childhood, wake inference, parent calls, SLEEP
compilation, fitting/shadow fitting, evaluation, retrieval, serialization,
and cold start.  Report both total GPU-hours and the longest sequential
root path; 32 devices do not shorten one child's causal history by 32x.

The active-text branch should run concurrently on the same confirmation roots.
Deferring it until favorable learner roots are known loses pairing and creates
selection/calendar confounding.  The optional parent-present tutoring 2x2 and
the optional doubled-read saturation assay are inference-only descendants and
should not consume lifelong confirmation branches.

## Developmental diagnostics versus confirmation

| evidence | valid use | invalid promotion |
|---|---|---|
| historical R2/RP/R3/R4 lives | writer/noise/failure diagnosis | lifetime or parenting efficacy |
| lesson/sham/no-teacher no-write test | qualify one visible intervention | durable parenting |
| one root executor canary | prove plumbing and direction | causal effect or uncertainty |
| four L-DEV blocks | freeze horizon, endpoints, baseline, margins, cost | contribute to final `n` |
| first eight concealed confirmation blocks | variance-only N selection; retained in confirmation | treatment-direction adaptation |
| 16--32 fresh valid confirmation blocks | `PERSIST`, `DEVxSLEEP`, and, if powered, `LATE` | universal learning, indefinite growth, or connectedness mediation |
| terminal adult x tutor-presence clones | immediate audited tutoring response | parenting-by-SLEEP or lifetime improvement |

Even a complete positive L-core does not establish that graph connectedness
mediates the lifetime slope; that requires the separate M-core bridge/cut
experiment.  It also does not establish physical compression, sentience,
unbounded self-improvement, or superiority to every external-memory system.
