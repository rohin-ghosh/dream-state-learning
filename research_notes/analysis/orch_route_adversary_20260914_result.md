the entire public task stays constant but source destinations change, or when
only root port display is reversed. FULL instead selects the correct changed
first port on every source-switch pair and retains it under the nuisance-only
display reversal. This falsifies these narrow pure-display / source-insensitive
accounts on the six fixed comparisons, including all five with complete positive
route-source support. The substantially weaker matched controls travel with
this observation; source-free first-port performance drops to3/6 for every state.

It does **not** establish general planning, universal shortcut resistance,
autonomous retrieval, unseen-family transfer, H1, H2, or acceptance-gate passage.
The routing continuation sees explicitly scripted reads; source syntax and graph
family remain familiar. The three read-order seeds are not three independent
training seeds, and paired goals within a world are not independent observations.
No inferential p-value or population claim is made.
