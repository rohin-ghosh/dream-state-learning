# Stage2A held-core binding v1

Builder prospective convention, September14,2026. No held signature-comparison
result or native outcome has been inspected to choose this convention.

This closes two local implementation definitions; it does not modify birth
constructors, actor inputs, goals, intervention outcomes, targets, controls,
optimizer, parser, scoring, or the reduced280BASE+280D1 reserved slots. It is
within standing Builder scope, not a new human ratification or C11 process.

## Source-limited depth

Keep birth actual_route_depth exactly as previously bound: complete designated
effective START-to-GOAL path, with missing continuation an error. Keep complete
autonomous held chains at their source-replayed complete depth2.

Intervention constructors register only the start-state transition system.
For interventions only, the legacy integer core field actual_route_depth means
the designated registered path-span STEP count:1 for a non-reached intervention,
0 for initially reached CONTINUE. Derive the one registered step and verify its
EVENT/DID ownership and actual effective destination; do not invent an absent
continuation. This is not the full distance to GOAL. Store depth_basis as
REGISTERED_DESIGNATED_SPAN and continuation_status as UNREGISTERED_AFTER_SPAN
(or ALREADY_REACHED) in an evaluator-only versioned receipt outside the core.
Do not interpret held and birth depth fields as directly comparable full-route
distances. The value1 overlaps existing valid birth depths; it is not a reserved
separation sentinel.

## Initially reached CONTINUE

The unchanged core/signature schema requires an actual registered ROOT_EVENT
and its ROOT_PORT and a LEFT/RIGHT side. For initially reached CONTINUE, select
the pair's actual source-registered left-reference EVENT and its DID PORT.
This is a structural comparison anchor, not the task's first irreversible
action. Bind root_basis=PAIR_REFERENCE_EVENT and goal_side_basis=PAIR_REFERENCE
outside the core, and use goal_side=LEFT only with that explicit interpretation.
GOAL still equals START/CURRENT in the public and complete source graphs.

For other intervention records use the actual designated source event and the
side derived from the goal's bound role, not member index (PROSPECT and CHECK
members share a goal). Bind ROOT_EVENT/DID to authentic registered source rows.
For reached CONTINUE: phaseCONTINUE, flowORDINARY, recoveryNONE, terminalREACHED,
positionnull, no observed STEP and predicted_actual_matchnull. All other phase,
position, outcome, flow and terminal fields derive from the exact held source
and retained public prefix, using the existing checker nullability rules.

## Required evidence

- Reconstruct all64 intervention members and verify unchanged source, paired
  allowed differences, targets and public bytes. Checker acceptance is necessary,
  not proof that the binding alone opens native execution.
- Retain complete graph/core/envelope/receipt bytes, explicit depth/root/side
  basis, source ownership and contract pins. Never expose these to the actor.
- Keep the existing four-radius EVENT/PORT signature algorithm unchanged. No
  depth, side, domain/version salt or status marker may enter its hashing.
- Compare every complete held signature against every birth signature; also
  compare complete core hashes. No partial hash or prefix-length shortcut.
  Missing cases or any intersection block qualification. Do not reselect anchors,
  change labels, change goals or omit rows after seeing an intersection.
- Test forged source/selected events/effective outcomes, foreign roles, missing
  registered transitions and improper retained-outcome/nullability claims.

This is an explicitly versioned evaluator convention. Neither a CPU pass nor
topological separation establishes learning, parenting, retention, or H1/H2.
Native tokenizer/runtime/initialization/resource gates remain separate.
