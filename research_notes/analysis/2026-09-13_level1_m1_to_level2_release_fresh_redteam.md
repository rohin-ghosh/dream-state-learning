# Fresh red-team: M1 correction-to-reuse and immediate Level-2 release

**Date:** 2026-09-13 UTC
**Role:** independent watcher-side scientific red-team
**Scope:** analysis only. I did not edit builder-owned source, corpora, tests,
models, adapters, runners, jobs, hosts, or GPU state, and I ran no model or GPU
work. Repository HEAD at review start was `68cef9f4`.

## Verdict

**REWORK before `L1_M1_RELEASE` or a claim-bearing Level-2 DEV launch.** The
proposal is close to a high-information engineering test, and its central
choice is sound: externally scored parent-free actions, a fresh downstream
family, exact child-span provenance, and mounted versus unmounted writes are
the right spine. It is materially better than the earlier combined birth and
teacher-present RuleGame paths.

It does not yet distinguish a bounded evidence-conditioned correction policy
from task/evaluator alignment strongly enough to release the adapter. Four
issues are decisive:

1. the same 32-item Level-1 DEV panel selects the corpus route and then serves
   as seed-1/seed-2 “confirmation”; new optimizer seeds do not make an exposed,
   selection-used task panel independent;
2. balanced strata and a held family do not require the action to depend on
   the correction-bearing evidence; no registered collision-twin endpoint
   rules out a direct current-state, label-position, or family-template policy;
3. the target-permuted controls are not guaranteed to be legal, semantically
   wrong, non-collapsing controls, and the Level-2 shuffle can change the wake-2
   sources it is supposed to control; and
4. final `PROMOTE - SHADOW` can be entirely a persistent SLEEP-1 effect. The
   present thresholds do not require SLEEP 1 to change block-2 material or
   SLEEP 2 to add any action benefit.

These can be repaired without a new trainer, larger LoRA, new model, broad
sweep, or long life. The minimum repair adds sealed readout panels and nested
checkpoint comparisons, not more claim-bearing fits. With fit de-duplication,
the Level-2 root can fall from the stated ceiling of eight fits to five.

The strongest defensible eventual wording is bounded:

> A rank-8 birth acquired a source-conditioned correction-to-next-action
> policy across sealed microdomain families, and under a fixed scaffold its
> own public, child-authored records caused two successive mounted writes to
> change parent-free actions.

This would still be scaffolded, curated learning on one task grammar. It would
not establish a general learning disposition, autonomous self-learning,
connected memory, long-life improvement, or superiority to text memory.

## Evidence boundary

The current evidence supports the proposal's caution, not immediate release:

- the three-seed perception result is an interface result: OFF already has
  `11/12` supported fenced records, ordinary practice makes `11/12` strict,
  anchor training is worse, and no fitted cell reaches `12/12`;
- the two-wake correction path ties sham on solves (`1/32` then `2/32`) and
  yields fewer qualifying corrections in the process arm;
- the selected Scratchpad-versus-ACT follow-up produces only `0--2/32` solves
  and mainly changes strict format;
- the 256-row AUTH birth installs conspicuous finite routines but fails the
  stronger revision-locality conjunction and introduces an arithmetic error;
- the teacher-free protocol audit shows prompt grammar can move the same
  comparison from parser failure to apparent revision benefit; and
- Q0 attempt 2 exposed an objective-incompatible first-update gate, while the
  full-dose conditional endpoint remains unmeasured. Q0 therefore neither
  blocks this independent M1 recipe nor supplies missing evidence for it.

The lesson across these results is consistent: exact form and direct routine
expression are easy to move; correct conditional use and preservation are the
unresolved quantities.

## What should be preserved

No redesign is needed for these parts:

- Level 1 is correctly called **trained**, including CD and parent-steered
  rejection sampling; Level 2 is the first **learned** rung only when exact
  child-authored post-receipt spans enter a mounted update.
- The Level-1 primary endpoint is an action-only, parent-free prompt. Process
  fields and syntax remain secondary mediators.
- A-SFT, CD-STRIP, and P-RS start from the same clean base, use the same source
  identities and fixed trainer, and never inherit the perception, Q0,
  CompilerGym, or inspected development adapters.
- A missing CD/P-RS target is a formation failure; no resampling past four,
  hard-source replacement, or authored backfill is permitted.
- Level-2 identifiers, mappings, reports, and vocabulary are generated only
  after the released birth bytes are sealed.
- Parent text is fixed and answer-blind in Level 2; it is absent from training
  targets and readouts.
- Invalid child records are not repaired, child semantic spans are hash-bound
  after their public receipt, and birth-corpus replay is absent from SLEEP.
- Block 1 is collected once before the first mount fork. That is the correct
  causal boundary for SLEEP 1.
- The no-birth `PROMOTE/SHADOW` pair and the birth-by-publication interaction
  are necessary. Process prose or admission counts cannot replace action
  effects.

## 1. Does M1 test correction rather than task memorization?

### What it can identify

With appropriate collisions, this assay can identify acquisition of a small
operational policy:

```text
same action situation + different warranted evidence
    -> different next action
same surface demand + irrelevant/unsupported evidence
    -> preserve or test rather than revise
```

That is more than memorizing the 48 source rows because an entire family is
held out and Level 2 uses a later generator. It is less than “learning to
learn”: the four strata and the three-field grammar directly define the policy
researchers want the adapter to acquire.

### What can pass the current panel

As written, a model may pass by learning one or more simpler rules:

- map `MISMATCH`-like wording to switch and `MATCH`-like wording to keep;
- use the new situation alone when it already determines `NEXT`;
- exploit family-specific action vocabulary or response position;
- always revise in the revise half and always keep in the pooled non-revise
  half while sacrificing one of unsupported versus irrelevant cases;
- learn the common three-field ritual and let pretrained task competence solve
  the action panel; or
- use a conjunction of ID shape, template, action order, length, and outcome
  wording that survives marginal balance and string disjointness.

The current `13/16` revise and do-not-revise floors pool semantically distinct
negative cases. They do not make source use noncompensatory.

### Minimum construct repair

Build the 32-item action panel as 16 registered counterfactual collision pairs.
Within a pair, action vocabulary, new situation, ID/token lengths, rendering,
and action marginals are fixed; only the public prediction/outcome or the
relevance of the correction changes, and the correct next action flips. Cover
all four strata. In addition to `26/32`, require:

- at least `6/8` correct in each of the four strata, replacing the pooled
  revise/non-revise-only protection;
- a prospectively fixed paired-collision floor (recommended pilot floor:
  `12/16` pairs with both twins correct); and
- zero cases in which the action is already identified after deleting the
  registered correction-bearing field.

The two CPU solvers should also enumerate prohibited public-feature
projections. Marginal balance is not enough: for every registered shortcut
projection, either construct target-collision groups or show that its best
deterministic accuracy is below the release floor. A shallow decision tree or
finite lookup over IDs/templates is a diagnostic, not the certificate; the
certificate comes from generator-level collisions.

This is the necessary defense against task memorization. More prose variants,
more rows, or another learner seed do not substitute for it.

## 2. The Level-1 confirmation panel currently leaks selection

The proposal uses the same DEV situations for:

1. the no-fit temporary-help headroom test;
2. seed-0 route selection among A-SFT/CD-STRIP/P-RS;
3. seed-1 confirmation; and
4. seed-2 release and comparison with the permuted fit.

Seed 1 and seed 2 replicate optimization from the same base; they do not
replicate transfer to an unselected task sample. The lexicographic route choice
explicitly conditions on those 32 outcomes. Calling later fits on that panel
confirmation overstates the evidence even if nobody changes a byte afterward.

The cheap repair is panel separation:

- `DEV_SELECT32`: the present held family, used for headroom and seed-0 route
  selection only;
- `RELEASE32` (or two sealed 32-item roots): generated from another family or
  independently authored generator skin after every corpus, trainer,
  threshold, and route-selection rule is frozen; it remains unopened until the
  selected route is fixed;
- seed-1 and seed-2 authentic fits must pass on `RELEASE32`; seed 2 remains the
  deployed artifact; and
- Level 2 then uses a further generator family created after the seed-2 release
  hashes, as already required.

This adds generations, not fits. If the team wants a task-transfer claim rather
than only an engineering release, use two sealed release roots and report them
as task roots, not learner replications.

Also add a zero-cost feasibility rule: if contemporary OFF exceeds `24/32`,
the registered `+8/32` net-correction gate is arithmetically impossible even
for a perfect fit. Redesign the panel before training rather than spend a fit
against an unreachable conjunction.

## 3. Source matching across the arsenal is useful but not causal matching

Using the same 48 source identities prevents easy-route families from getting
more situations. It does not make the three trained corpora equivalent:

- A-SFT has deterministic authored continuations;
- CD-STRIP is selected from up to four helped child samples;
- P-RS is selected from up to four two-wake, parent-steered trajectories;
- accepted surfaces, lengths, entropy, and model-native priors can differ even
  when all three semantic targets are valid; and
- formation inference and parent-token cost differ sharply before any fit.

Therefore the three-way comparison is an operational **recipe tournament**,
not a causal estimate of authorship, context distillation, or lived data. That
is acceptable for choosing a launch recipe, but the claim must stay at that
level.

Before fitting, hash and compare the exact response targets:

- if two route corpora are byte-identical after masking, do not spend two fits;
  their fitted treatment is identical and only formation cost differs;
- if they differ, report exact supervised IDs, tokens, unique surface forms,
  candidate success rate, inference/parent tokens, and source-bearing batch
  placement; and
- if the selected route wins with materially different target dose or batch
  composition, call it a recipe-bundle win. Add the proposed dose-yoked fit
  only if a narrower route-mechanism claim would change a decision.

For information per GPU-hour, run the cheap A-SFT feasibility fit first. Form
the expensive P-RS corpus only after the action task and trainer are shown able
to pass, unless a predeclared hypothesis specifically predicts that native
targets rescue authored-target failure. During formation, once any registered
source exhausts all four candidates the route cannot meet its 48/48 gate; stop
claim-bearing collection for that route and preserve the partial formation
rate as its result.

## 4. The target-permuted controls need semantic validity

### Level 1

A fixed-point-free row permutation within stratum is insufficient. Rows in a
stratum may share the same action, so a different row can preserve the correct
semantic target. Conversely, moving a target across microdomain families can
produce an illegal action vocabulary or an obviously malformed continuation.
Either defect makes authentic-minus-permuted uninterpretable.

Construct a **semantic derangement**, not merely a row derangement:

- permute only within family, stratum, legal action grammar, and matched target
  token length (or within prebuilt collision blocks);
- require every assigned `NEXT` to be legal for the recipient source and wrong
  under authentic scoring;
- preserve exact target-text and action-frequency multisets;
- couple initial tensors, optimizer state, row/batch order, dropout seeds,
  updates, and decoding settings to the authentic seed-2 fit;
- after reload, require the permuted adapter to reproduce its assigned TRAIN
  targets at a predeclared rate comparable to AUTH, with zero malformed
  collapse; and
- retain authentic-versus-OFF as the primary gain. The permuted gap is evidence
  about source binding only after the control demonstrably learned its assigned
  targets.

The current one-sided condition “permuted no more than `2/32` above OFF” does
not validate the control; an inert or badly damaged adapter passes it. Direct
assigned-target acquisition and generic preservation are the economical
validity checks.

### Level 2

Do not let BIRTH-SHUFFLE become active ancestry before wake 2. If it is mounted
after SLEEP 1, it changes block-2 actions, outcomes, admitted rows, and dose;
the final comparison no longer isolates semantic target binding.

Instead, create the shuffle as a terminal sibling from the exact authentic
birth-PROMOTE old+new source rows. Derange only legal semantic targets, train
from the same frozen birth with the same cumulative schedule, never mount it
into a wake, and require direct assigned-map/read acquisition plus generic
preservation. Then authentic versus shuffle changes target binding while
holding the experience sources fixed.

## 5. PROMOTE/SHADOW needs a nested second-write contrast

The first fork is valid:

```text
common block 1 and child records -> one candidate C1
                              |-> mount C1: P1
                              `-> retain starting child: S0
```

The proposed final contrast `P2 - S0` is a valid **total flywheel effect**. It
bundles the SLEEP-1 write, the experience/material it changes, and SLEEP 2.
It is not a causal SLEEP-2 effect. The current gates can pass if C1 alone causes
all final gains and the cumulative C2 write adds zero.

Use a nested fork after the P1 wake-2 records close:

```text
P1 -> block 2 -> exact P1 child records -> one cumulative candidate C2
                                      |-> mount C2: P1P2
                                      `-> retain P1: P1S2
```

`P1S2` is just the saved SLEEP-1 checkpoint evaluated with cleared context; it
requires no additional fit. Do the same for the base start. Preserve `S0` for
the total effect and for observing whether C1 changed wake-2 material.

The minimum estimands become:

```text
D1_a = Y[a,P1,1]   - Y[a,S0,1]
D2_a = Y[a,P1P2,2] - Y[a,S0,2]       # total two-sleep effect
J2_a = Y[a,P1P2,2] - Y[a,P1S2,2]     # incremental SLEEP-2 mount
I2   = D2_birth - D2_base             # total birth interaction
```

Keep the proposed `D1_birth >= 4/16`, `D2_birth >= 8/32`, final
birth-P1P2 `>=24/32`, and `I2 >= 6/32`. Change the new-block condition from
P1P2 versus never-promoted S0 to an incremental condition:

- `J2_birth >= 3/16` on the new block; and
- birth-P1P2 loses at most `1/16` on old items relative to birth-P1S2/P1.

For the words **closed loop**, additionally require at least one certified
block-2 semantic target to differ between P1 and S0 because of post-SLEEP-1
behavior/public evidence, and require the cumulative C2 corpus to contain that
new child-authored target. Formation rate alone is not evidence that the loop
changed its own material.

This decomposition separates three useful outcomes:

- `D1 > 0`, `J2 = 0`: one installed child-authored write, not a two-write loop;
- P1 changes block-2 material but `J2 = 0`: closed-loop formation failure;
- P1 changes block-2 material and `J2 > 0`: the minimum two-SLEEP closed-loop
  behavior.

### Evidence yoking across birth and base

Within each starting child, block 1 is already shared across P/S. Across birth
and base, the present design still lets actions and outcomes differ. Then
`I2` may reflect direct birth-enabled evidence acquisition rather than better
correction/absorption from comparable evidence.

M1 is correction-to-reuse, not experiment allocation. In the primary root,
fix or yoke the executed block actions and public receipts across birth/base,
while allowing child predictions, records, admission, and later policy to
differ. This preserves authentic public experience and makes the birth
interaction about preparation/use of matched evidence. A free-action sibling
is optional; if only it wins, the supported result is better information
acquisition, not better absorption of matched corrections.

## 6. Threshold and statistical audit

Most proposed thresholds are reasonable engineering screens, but their role
must remain explicit.

1. `26/32`, `+8/32`, regression `<=2`, and locality bounds are minimum effects
   of interest, not confidence statements. The three learner seeds are useful
   robustness checks, but seed 0 is selected and must not enter a confirmatory
   learner-level p-value.
2. Report semantic addition/copy correctness separately from exact native
   syntax. The repository already contains cases where a format score hid
   correct content and cases where trained syntax hid an arithmetic error.
3. Reconcile Level-2 formation `>=6/8` with the pre-mount requirement of
   `>=7/8` valid direct reads. If a missing record cannot be read correctly,
   the effective mount gate is already `>=7/8`; state that directly and use one
   denominator for every root.
4. Convert every `.05` bound to an exact count for its panel size before
   launch. Do not alternate between continuous and rounded discrete rules.
5. `7/8` complete root passes is a defensible replication rule, but
   `9/256=.0352` is not a valid p-value for the composite pass conjunction.
   A null probability of `.5` for that conjunction has not been derived.
   Remove the p-value, or apply a sign/randomization test to a prospectively
   defined root-level signed contrast under an exchangeable mount label.
6. Eight downstream roots share one released M1 adapter. They support
   robustness across generated task roots conditional on that adapter, not
   eight independent birth-training replications. Keep that scope in the paper.

Do not solve these issues by increasing prompt counts and treating them as
independent samples. The independent units are learner fits for birth
robustness and generated roots for downstream task robustness.

## 7. Minimum corrected release ladder

### Level 1

1. Freeze the collision-certified sources, two independent semantic solvers,
   forbidden-feature audit, trainer, target masks, thresholds, and separate
   SELECT/RELEASE panels.
2. Check OFF arithmetic feasibility and temporary-help headroom.
3. Run A-SFT seed 0 first; form and fit only eligible complete CD/P-RS routes.
4. Select on `DEV_SELECT32`, with every route failure reported.
5. Train the selected authentic corpus at seeds 1 and 2 and evaluate on the
   unopened `RELEASE32` root(s).
6. Run one coupled, legal semantic-derangement fit at seed 2; verify assigned
   target acquisition before interpreting its held score.
7. Release seed 2 only if the action, four-stratum, collision, preservation,
   semantic-control, custody, and empty-reload gates all pass.

### Level 2 DEV

Per starting child, train C1 once and use mount/no-mount branches. From the P1
trajectory, train C2 once and use the nested mount/no-mount branches. Use one
terminal source-matched shuffled C2 for the birth. The essential fits are:

| fit | source material | readout use |
|---|---|---|
| birth C1 | common birth block 1 | birth P1 versus birth S0 |
| base C1 | common base block 1 | base P1 versus base S0 |
| birth C2 | authentic birth-P1 old+new union | birth P1P2 versus saved P1S2 |
| base C2 | authentic base-P1 old+new union | base P1P2 versus saved P1S2 |
| birth C2 semantic derangement | same authentic birth-P1 sources/dose | terminal control only |

That is five fits, not eight. Training unused S0-derived SLEEP-2 candidates
does not affect any child and is scientifically unnecessary after the DEV
pipeline has established that compile/train itself cannot mutate the unmounted
checkpoint. If the project retains them as infrastructure audits, exclude them
from the minimum scientific budget and never count them as independent
evidence.

Only a corrected DEV pass freezes the eight-root confirmation protocol. One
DEV root releases engineering replication, not a general claim. Confirmation
uses the same frozen bytes, no root replacement, full intention-to-treat
denominators, and root-level effects; `7/8` is reported as an engineering
replication conjunction unless a valid randomization statistic is separately
predeclared.

## Information per GPU-hour

The priority order should be:

1. CPU collision/projection certificates and discrete threshold arithmetic;
2. no-fit OFF/help readouts;
3. one A-SFT seed-0 feasibility fit;
4. only then CD/P-RS formation and complete-route fits;
5. selected-route seed 1, seed 2, and one valid semantic derangement;
6. one five-fit Level-2 DEV root with the nested readouts; and
7. eight confirmation roots only after the complete DEV conjunction.

Do not spend GPU time on more perception fits, unchanged full Scratchpads,
multiple random shuffles, broad rank/LR/replay sweeps, or separate duplicate
fits for byte-identical route corpora. The highest-value new information is
whether correction-bearing evidence is necessary for the action and whether
the second child-authored write adds behavior beyond the first.

## Disposition

The proposed M1 grammar remains the right smallest module, and rapid movement
to a small Level-2 root is justified after the repairs above. The release is
not scientifically safe as currently written because route selection reuses
the confirmation exam, the semantic control may be malformed or inert, and
the two-sleep gate does not require a second-sleep effect.

Do not broaden the program. Repair the source collisions, split the panel,
make the permutation a learned legal derangement, yoke primary evidence across
birth/base, and add the saved-P1 nested contrast. Those changes directly test
the intended process while reducing, rather than increasing, claim-bearing
GPU fits.
