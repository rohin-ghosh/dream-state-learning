## Hypothesis and exact change

The existing documentary replay reconstructs a historical-utterance prompt.
That tests learning to reproduce attributed records, not directly replaying the
runtime decision conditional on its actual prompt. The proposed treatment uses
the child's exact recorded runtime messages and own generated continuation.
Parent guidance may remain in its causal source prefix; it is never substituted
as the target. Existing teacher-target-copy checks remain in force.

All original attempts, checks and own reflections remain outcome-tagged records,
including incorrect attempts. The objective is ordinary likelihood training,
NOT unlikelihood or negative-gradient training; the tags do not make that claim.
Nonterminal generated prefixes retain their actual tokens and do not acquire a
synthetic EOS. Prefix masking, target token identity, no silent cropping and
source-call joins are tested explicitly. No branch count, minimum prose length,
template slot or outcome-based sample selection is introduced.

Candidate: `organism_v6/orch_r107_parented_replay.py`. Fourteen focused tests;
41combined tests plus9subtests pass with existing feedback-uptake tests. The first
fixture incorrectly put FINAL on a prose line; corrected to the unchanged
parser's standalone final line. No production parser/scoring change.
