# Smallest decisive path from L0 coexistence to parent--DREAM--SLEEP H1

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** fresh-context scientific review; advice only. No builder code or
job was changed or launched.  
**Evidence cutoff:** `research_loop/COORDINATION.md` through the 19:20 UTC L0
coexistence audit, plus the current Level-1 and H1 design memos.

## Decision

Use four noncompensatory gates, in this order:

```text
L0 coexistence (one disposable root)
  -> L1 input-selective PROSPECT + REVISE (one root, then two replications)
  -> W-H1 exact arbitrary family x mode writer (one seed, then two)
  -> parent--DREAM--SLEEP H1 (excluded executor root, then 16 clean roots)
```

L0 asks only whether explicit replay lets one adapter express two compatible
global conventions. L1 asks whether two *input-dependent operations* can be
installed and then composed. W-H1 asks the different question H1 actually
depends on: whether the exact writer can carry arbitrary family--mode
bindings. H1 alone asks whether parenting changes what a child authors from
its own public action outcomes and whether SLEEP carries those child-authored
artifacts into parent-free action.

Do not merge these claims. In particular, direct authored SFT may qualify L1
and W-H1, but can never count as experiential compilation. That provenance
edge first exists in H1.

## Gate 0: L0 coexistence is a plumbing sentinel, not a paper rung

Run only Astra's root-0 `INPUT -> PREDICT -> ACT` versus
`PREDICT -> ACT -> INPUT` pair after the symmetric scorer repair already
specified in `2026-09-12_two_habit_coexistence_adversarial_audit.md`.

Advance only if both descendants:

- score at least `28/32` on their **own** exact operand-bearing order and at
  most `4/32` on the other's order;
- retain PREDICT-before-ACT on at least `30/32` and correct ACT on at least
  `31/32`;
- copy both source operands exactly on every counted own-map pass; and
- add no more than `1/16` tag spill versus the inherited adapter and lose no
  more than `.05` interface validity.

If it passes, stop L0 after root 0 and spend the next GPU-hour on conditional
behavior. Three-root replication would only make a more precise claim about
tag ordering. If it fails, repair replay/collation before L1: a system that
cannot preserve one explicitly replayed compatible convention while adding a
second has not earned a two-operation corpus.

This result cannot establish that INPUT informed PREDICT, that an outcome
changed a policy, memory, parenting, or DREAM/SLEEP. Also preserve the new
dose correction: SEQ-101 compared reset versus long-context packaging, but
its repeated copies were averaged into the same 80 optimizer updates. It did
not falsify repetition across distinct gradient encounters.

## Gate 1: make PROSPECT and REVISE genuinely input-selective

Keep the proposed single interleaved 128-row AUTH corpus, paired DERANGED
corpus, one rank-8 adapter, target-only loss, and OFF read. Strengthen the
panel so that syntax, prompt meaning, label marginals, and one global inverse
rule cannot be mistaken for evidence of the **AUTH** operation.

### Required counterfactual panel

For **PROSPECT**, every logical case belongs to two independently scored twin
families:

1. **goal twin:** the belief card and action order are byte-identical; only
   the goal outcome changes, so the correct predicted consequence and action
   must change;
2. **belief twin:** the goal is byte-identical; only the two action--outcome
   bindings exchange, so the correct action must change.

For **REVISE**, every case has:

1. an **outcome twin** whose bytes are identical through the prior
   prediction/action and differ only in `OBSERVED`; `COMPARE`, `POLICY`, and
   `NEXT` must all flip; and
2. a **prior-action twin** with the logical expected/unexpected status held
   fixed while the physical action names exchange; the correct NEXT action
   must exchange.

Cross card order, goal position, physical action names, outcome names, and
surface form by a balanced Latin-square schedule. Choose native-token-length-
matched opaque labels and independently permute their external spellings by
root. Before fitting, enumerate every constant-output, first-position,
goal-only, observed-token-only, action-name-only, template, and row-position
policy; each must score exactly `.50` on each applicable family. AUTH and
DERANGED must have identical target-token multisets, per-batch length and EOS
multisets, row/update counts, and label marginals.

DERANGED must be scored on its own registered rule as well as AUTH's rule.
Its role is not to model a good thinker. It tests whether gradients can
redirect the same input-relative operation against the frozen base's semantic
preference. A compact global inversion rule is allowed to explain DERANGED;
that is why DERANGED is a controllability control, not evidence of good
reasoning. AUTH success with DERANGED failure is **prompt-semantic competence**,
not qualified carriage, while DERANGED success cannot rescue a failed AUTH
twin family.

### Noncompensatory L1 gate

Run AUTH and DERANGED at root 0 first. Expand unchanged to roots 1--2 only if
root 0 satisfies every item:

- each arm is at least `.90` on its own exact assigned training map;
- AUTH surface carriage is at least `30/32` separately for PROSPECT and
  REVISE;
- AUTH semantic correctness is at least `28/32` separately for both;
- at least `14/16` goal twins and `14/16` belief twins flip the complete
  PROSPECT decision correctly;
- at least `14/16` outcome twins flip `COMPARE + POLICY + NEXT` completely,
  and at least `14/16` prior-action twins exchange NEXT correctly;
- each physical action, outcome, branch, and template stratum is at least
  `.75`;
- the paired teacher-forced conditional interaction is at least `1.0` nat
  for each operation; and
- strict validity is at least `.95`, no-phase tag spill at most `.05`, and no
  unrelated/interface endpoint is more than `.05` below OFF.

All three roots must meet the same gates; averages cannot rescue a failed
root, operation, twin family, label stratum, spill family, or interface test.
Report AUTH-minus-OFF separately. If OFF is already at ceiling, the result
may establish *installed conditional form* through AUTH/DERANGED redirection,
but not increased reasoning competence. Claim increased competence only if
AUTH improves the registered semantic endpoint over OFF by at least `.10`.

This closes the main shortcut routes:

- a global ritual may pass surface carriage but fails the twin flips;
- first-position, goal-token, observed-token, and physical-label policies
  fail one of the crossed twin families;
- label-marginal or optimizer asymmetry fails the byte/token parity receipt;
- pretrained prompt semantics may help AUTH but cannot make DERANGED follow
  its own registered map;
- direct SFT is explicitly the mechanism being qualified here, not evidence
  of lived experience.

### Intertwining is a readout before it is another fit

If both operations qualify, immediately run the registered three-turn
`PROSPECT -> public outcome -> REVISE -> new PROSPECT` dialogues with **zero
new training**. Require at least `14/16` whole-chain passes in every root, each
outcome branch at least `.75`, and all interface gates preserved.

If that passes, stop: explicit chain examples add no information. If the
parts pass but the chain fails, fit one AUTH/DERANGED chain pair at root 0
from the frozen base while replaying the complete isolated corpus. Expand to
roots 1--2 only on a root-0 pass. The isolated scores may fall by at most
`.05`. Failure after explicit chain training means only two isolated
procedures were stored; do not call them intertwined and do not birth H1 from
that state.

## Gate 2: W-H1 remains necessary and should use the exact H1 stack

L1 does not replace W-H1. L1's answers are computable from public prompt
relations; H1 requires 32 arbitrary family x mode action bindings to reside in
weights. Run the exact oracle-authored W-H1 AUTH/SWAP pair at seed 0 using the
selected Level-1 AUTH birth corpus, H1 renderer, action tokens, rank, LR,
replay share, loss mask, initialization, and readout. The birth corpus must
contain no H1 family/mode/object/STORE schema or task identifier.

Advance to seeds 1--2 only if seed 0 has, for both AUTH and SWAP:

- exact-training balanced accuracy at least `.90`;
- held-object/held-wording balanced accuracy at least `.80`;
- each mode and action recall at least `.75` and valid single actions at
  least `.95`;
- symmetric own-map redirection `B >= .40`;
- every unsupported and unexposed family's action-distribution TV from OFF
  at most `.05`, with unrelated/interface loss at most `.05`; and
- exact corpus, token, initialization, update, reload, scorer/world, and
  state-isolation receipts.

Every seed must pass every item. Training-surface failure means objective or
dose; training pass plus held failure means cue/rendering; held accuracy
without B means a global action prior; B with spill means nonlocal writing.
None licenses more parenting. Only a three-seed pass freezes the writer for
H1.

## Gate 3: H1 is where experiential compilation begins

After Gate 2, run one permanently excluded executor root, then the existing
16-root P-versus-active-sham confirmation unchanged except for the adversarial
schema-parity amendment. H1's three causal components remain
noncompensatory:

1. `q`: parenting improves grounded child-authored STORE/NULL artifacts;
2. `g`: an authentic personal SLEEP improves parent-free held first actions
   over the common birth state; and
3. `theta`: AUTH/SWAP redirection is stronger for the parented child than for
   the sham child.

Retain all existing root-level intervals and practical margins. Additionally
require P and S schema validity each at least `.90`, absolute P--S schema gap
at most `.05`, and report before admission: valid STORE attempt rate,
supported precision conditional on STORE, supported coverage, correct NULL,
and every rejection class. More parseable guesses cannot substitute for a
semantic gain. The answer key remains the immutable world map, never the
admitted artifact.

The H1 compiler may diversify contexts, but every supervised target must be
an exact byte span from the child's committed action and every derived row
must cite its earlier immutable action/outcome receipt. Parent prose,
classroom notes, verifier reasons, hidden maps, and rejected artifacts remain
outside every target and training byte. Delete all temporary state before a
fresh-process read. These are what distinguish H1 from another authored SFT
experiment.

An H1 pass permits only:

> In one bounded two-condition world, process parenting increased grounded
> child-authored artifacts, and a qualified LoRA write of exact continuations
> traceable to the child's own earlier action outcomes selectively changed
> parent-free held-instance first actions; equal-dose swapped writes redirected
> the behavior.

It does not establish broad metacognition, multiple sleeps, lifetime slope,
connected knowledge, graph traversal, active expansion, compression, or
memory-baseline superiority.

## Spend and stop table

| Stage | Maximum useful spend now | Advance | Stop / redirect |
|---|---:|---|---|
| L0 coexistence | one T/C root; two fits | symmetric own-order, old habit, source-copy, spill gates all pass | repair replay/collation; do not replicate tag order |
| L1 isolated | root-0 AUTH/DERANGED; then roots 1--2, six fits total | every operation, twin family, arm, root, locality and interface gate passes | localize surface vs conditional vs semantics vs spill |
| L1 chain | zero-fit read first; at most one fit pair then two replications | whole chain passes with isolated behavior retained | no H1 birth from merely isolated procedures |
| W-H1 | seed-0 AUTH/SWAP, then seeds 1--2; six fits total, `<=2` A40-hours | exact + held arbitrary binding, B, locality, interface pass in every seed | repair writer/representation, not parenting |
| H1 | one excluded root `<=6` A40-hours; then 16 roots `<=96` A40-hours | full `q AND g AND theta AND safety` intersection | use the predeclared failure localization; no H2 promotion |

The economical principle is simple: replicate only the first *conditional*
result, not another global ritual. L0 is a cheap compatibility check. L1
proves conditional operations under authored supervision. W-H1 proves the
exact arbitrary-binding carrier. H1 then spends clean children only after
both prerequisites exist and supplies the first causal experiential edge.
