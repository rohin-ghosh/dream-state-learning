oracle outcomes and provenance, separating private guidance from student data.

## Proposed repeated-cycle comparison

- Prospectively select one qualifying child using the existing transfer,
  control, deterministic-reference, retention and independent-evidence gates.
  Do not select it from the repeated-cycle sealed evaluations.
- Three sleeps, with evaluation before sleep0 and after each sleep1–3.
  Freeze all episode rosters, family splits and evaluation cohorts before runs.
- GUIDED+SLEEP: parent present only during experience/reflection. Child alone
  generates training targets, admitted by outcome and substantive rubric.
- GUIDED+FROZEN: same starting child, comparable guided interaction budget,
  no parameter update. This isolates continued consolidation from guidance
  available during collection; the child's evaluation is still parent-free.
- UNPARENTED+SLEEP: same start and task/budget schedule, no parent in collection
  or reflection. Sleep only on its genuinely qualifying own outputs, never
  fill failed collection with guided-arm targets. Report unequal actual yield.
- Fresh processes evaluate all twins without parent, sleep prompt, transient
  conversation, or arm-specific guidance. Old W0/W8/audit each>=15/16.
  Compare matched task denominators, first-port reference for routes, and
  interval-by-interval changes, not just final scores or selected best sleep.
- Distinguish common-input consolidation comparisons from naturalistic twins
  that collect different experience. Do not describe the latter as isolating
  parenting alone when data quantities or realized compute differ.
- Before each experience tranche, a small fixed parent-absent collection probe
  can measure dependence on guidance. It is a measurement, not a prerequisite
  for admission or a reason to reject guided developmental learning.
- Independent reader re-derives result counts; no paper promotion until all
  gates and independent replication are satisfied. Main never runs the reader.
