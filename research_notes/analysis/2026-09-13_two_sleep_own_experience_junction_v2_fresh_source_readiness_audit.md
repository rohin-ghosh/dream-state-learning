## Exact source blockers

### P0.1 — The inherited BIRTH treatment is not frozen at this commit

V2 says to import the selected serialized units, masks, slots, padding plan,
decode baseline, and preservation tasks byte-for-byte, but it names no
M-COMBINE path, commit, manifest schema, or artifact hash. At the audited
commit, the applicable Stage-2A v1 binding is
`ac0a61fbbf907cc8dd463e4138ba268c4a44daded01926f298d759c6e9bf064a`,
and its already-present implementation-readiness audit
`9ab0932e8dc41a3bc9efa35966a3d7b58674c75457dd9d10b03fe5a0d73a23dc`
is explicitly **REWORK**: topology, prompts, seeds, nulls, canaries, masks,
decode and execution pins are not frozen. Thus the “selected tape” and the
ordinal preservation panel do not yet denote reproducible bytes.

Repair by importing one adopted source-ready M-COMBINE contract by exact
path/SHA, then define accepted checkpoint/tape/manifest hashes and the exact
decode used for both its frozen baseline and TSJ preservation reruns. Do not
let a later M-COMBINE implementation retroactively choose TSJ's treatment.

### P0.2 — Pre-fit calls, gates, and order do not form one executable truth table

Sections 3.1 and 3.3 require OLD atom/O/T/cut ceilings and post-NEW atom/O/N/
filler ceilings plus O/N response twins. Section 9 supplies no acceptance
predicate for those zero-fit control ceilings. Section 11 counts atom/N/filler
post-NEW calls but omits O and both response twins; it later counts response
O/N only after S2. `GO-FIT` nevertheless requires “all” PRE-S1 and POST-NEW
ceilings. Source therefore cannot determine which calls gate S1/S2, what each
must score, or whether the pre-S2 response twins are model executions at all.

Repair with one phase-by-phase ledger containing every condition, mounted
actor state, memory service, goals, expected action/route, gate/diagnostic
status, and exact call ordinal. The truth table and resource ledger must be
generated from that same ledger.

### P0.3 — Several causal twins are not uniquely implementable

For a response O intervention, coherently swapping O entails changes to both
start EVENT destinations and their LINK pairing. A single changed return can
conflict with authentic rows already in the actor prefix or returned later.
V2 does not say whether the treatment is one row only or a switch to a fully
transformed service from the first affected READ onward. The exact decisive
request/address is also not registered. N has the analogous multirow issue.

Separately, Sections 1.2/10 say every causal twin shares exact initialization
tensors, while the ancestry map starts S2 O, ATOM, and RAW from their distinct
treated S1 snapshots. Those statements cannot both mean S2 initialization.

Repair by binding, for every goal/world, the fork prefix hash, first affected
request, returned row and all subsequent service state, with an exact allowed
byte diff. Say explicitly that S2 arms share the BIRTH ancestor and named RNG
schedule, while only N_SWAP and OLD+filler share the exact S1 AUTH tensors (or
change the ancestry design).

### P0.4 — The supposedly exact source still contains designer choices

The construction seed and two writer seeds have no values or derivation. Nor
are the identifier alphabets/lengths, W0 tuple, slot coin, primary frontier,
display/call permutations, indispensable READ registry, filler inventory,
transform algorithms, task/world outcome messages, per-call actor cap, or
training/collation contract frozen. V2 supersedes v1 but does not preserve or
restate v1's AdamW/LR/dropout/LoRA-module recipe; “fixed max tokens” and
“predeclared seed” are not pins.

The active-text conditions are also ambiguous: `EXACT_SUPPLIED_TEXT_CEILING`
and `SAME_AUTH_HISTORY_ACTIVE_TEXT_CEILING` are both described as the same
perfect one-row service over the admitted rows, and neither says whether the
actor is BIRTH or the stage's authentic fitted adapter. Post-NEW use of S1
AUTH would not be a clean supplied-text ceiling.

Repair with literal seed derivations, complete public/private material and
wire schemas, exact model/checkpoint/optimizer/collator/decode hashes, and one
unambiguous ceiling definition (normally frozen BIRTH), or define the two
conditions' real treatment difference. A source author must not choose these.

### P0.5 — RAW is not yet an exact feasible ledger

R0--R7 and R12--R13 merge an action response and a later EVENT-record response
into one “formation segment,” although those are separate calls with separate
system/user messages. The record-call prompt, chat-role boundaries, host
outcome bytes, separators and loss spans are not enumerated. Consequently the
claimed whole-unit target-token and padded-FLOP equations do not yet have
defined operands. Calling the section “RAW is feasible” is premature: the
real-tokenizer integer solution has not been exhibited, though fail-closed
`RAW_UNSAT` is a valid fallback.

Repair by rendering every R0--R15 byte/role/mask sequence exactly and either
publishing a tokenizer-bound witness vector/batch assignment before source
approval or retitling this as conditional feasibility and making `RAW_UNSAT`
a prospective whole-version stop, as the body already requires.

### P0.6 — The hard resource cap is arithmetically incomplete

The 60-rollout ledger omits the required two-goal `S2 AUTH old-goal retention`
row, so it is at least 62 before resolving the missing pre-S2 calls above.
The `440` cold-reader/canary count treats S1/S2 as only `12/16 registered + 4
MISS` calls per fit. That cannot execute Section 5.3's requirement to query
both slots at every required EVENT source and every LINK source, in addition
to covering the direct `READ EVENT` request used by the route. For the stated
topology, slot coverage alone is already 14 EVENT-slot + 16 LINK-slot calls at
S1 and 18 + 20 at S2, before four fixed MISSes, direct EVENT-address checks,
or canaries. The `380` route-reader count also omits the two native old-goal
retention rollouts. Hence `2,694` calls and `449,472` generated tokens/world
are not hard maxima.

Repair by deriving fit, rollout, actor-call, reader-call, output-token,
training-token/FLOP, retry and wall-time caps from the completed execution and
cold-query ledgers. Include every partial attempt and the single permitted
pre-side-effect infrastructure restart.
