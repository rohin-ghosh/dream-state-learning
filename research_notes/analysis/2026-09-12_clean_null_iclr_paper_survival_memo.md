## Verdict

A real paper can survive a clean null, but it is a different empirical centre
from the current manuscript. The strongest honest contribution is:

> **Parametric storage and even correct supplied-map behavior are not evidence
> of selective per-life learning. In this frozen-base/rank-8 system, the
> binding failure is the transition from stored supervision to conditionally
> appropriate native action without global output-dialect spill or damage to
> unrelated behavior; suppressing that spill can remove the acquisition. The
> historical repeated-write lives show the same problem at system scale:
> finite early/by-identifier advantage does not compound and declines with
> lifetime.**

This is potentially an ICLR paper if the storage/selectivity result is made a
valid, prospectively frozen and independently repeated result before the
abstract freeze. It is not a paper about a functioning Experience Model,
successful parenting, connected memory, or lifetime improvement.

If no valid Q0-style result exists by the September 18 abstract freeze, do
not submit the architecture promise as an empirical abstract. The current
collection of useful but invalidated or post-hoc diagnostics is then a strong
internal engineering record, not yet a paper result.

## The scientific object under the null

The paper should test a necessary-condition ladder rather than present another
agent architecture:

```text
surface can express both native actions
  -> supervised relation is stored
  -> relation is extracted under held views
  -> extraction changes the right action only
  -> old/new behaviors coexist across writes
  -> endogenous action/outcome experience is useful
  -> utility compounds with lifetime
```

The evidence already shows why these arrows must be separated.

- **Material can matter.** Correct oracle mini-Sudoku material beat an
  equal-target-marginal corrupt corpus in all three training seeds, by
  `+2,+2,+5` exact solves. This is a small supplied-material positive, not
  self-learning.
- **Capacity is not the immediate issue.** A supplied conditional stress test
  made AUTH and DERANGED adapters express their opposite PROSPECT maps on
  `32/32` development cases each. Earlier exact-text and owner-binding assays
  also show large train-loss/storage effects.
- **Selectivity is the failure.** In that same conditional stress test,
  exact-copy fell from `14/16` adapter-OFF to `0/16` in both fitted arms and
  learned-tag spill reached `13/16--16/16`. Lower learning rate weakened but
  did not localize the broad habit. Prefix masking reduced frame spill from
  `.416` to `.202` while erasing acquisition. A matched frozen-OFF preservation
  treatment reduced frame spill by 91.2% (`.4155 -> .0367`) but reduced
  `I_d_frame` from `1.921` to `.152` with an interval crossing zero and still
  missed the `.03` spill limit. This is an acquisition--preservation tradeoff,
  not a selective writer.
- **Finite behavior change is not a flywheel.** Across the nine historical
  1,024-episode R2 lives, the mean within-panel ON--OFF advantage was about
  `+.019`, but `advantage(1024)-advantage(384)` was negative in `9/9` lives
  (mean `-.0316`, SD `.0372`, SE `.0124`). Terminal disjoint-panel adapters
  averaged `-.017` relative to OFF and regressed from their mid-life value.
  Static brief-only execution matched or beat the adapter in `5/8` inspected
  lives. These are retrospective system diagnostics, not confirmation-grade
  lifetime causality.
- **The tested teaching moves are null, not parenting in general.** A visible
  process card and matched sham each solved `1/16`; a fresh same-episode
  correction test moved both process and sham from `1/32` first-wake solves to
  `2/32` second-wake solves. They validate interfaces and feedback plumbing,
  but they neither establish useful teaching nor a precise equivalence result.

If Q0 fails because storage is present but locality fails, downstream M and L
nulls do not independently falsify DREAM, parenting, or lifetime learning:
the required writer intervention was never qualified. Under that branch, M/L
are stopped or explicitly labeled downstream-uninterpretable. If Q0 passes and
M or L then cleanly fails, the paper becomes stronger because it can localize
the next broken arrow. Do not collapse those branches into “the full organism
was null.”

## Why this can be science rather than a diary

The closest work already occupies online LoRA memory (TMEM), self-adapting
weight updates (SEAL), selective/isolated parametric consolidation (EVAF and
PEAM), wake--sleep consolidation, batch reflection internalization (LEAFE and
Early Experience), and active external memory (MemoPilot, Evo-Memory/ReMem,
Spark). “We tried sleep LoRA and it was unstable” is therefore not novel.
`Understanding LoRA as Knowledge Memory` also makes generic LoRA capacity and
composability an occupied topic.

The defensible delta is a **native-action causal stress test** that measures,
on the same immutable fitted states, exact storage, held-view extraction,
conditional action redirection, full-vocabulary/common-mode drift, itemwise
interface changes, wrong-root use, unrelated-task locality, and subsequent
lifetime utility. The result is interesting if it demonstrates that the
commonly reported lower-loss/recall or task-gain endpoints can coexist with a
failed policy-locality endpoint.

| Publishable scientific evidence | Engineering diary / appendix only |
|---|---|
| Prospectively fixed valid carrier, immutable denominators, exact-train versus held-view scores, non-cancelling locality measures, and a terminal failure subtype | Triton/Python-header failures, empty-then-mutated seals, timeout handling, hostname/runtime text, and cleanup incidents |
| Same writer and thresholds repeated on fresh independent roots/seeds, with all failures retained | A sequence of learning-rate, masking, prompt, target-alphabet, or parser scouts selected after outcomes |
| A controlled acquisition--locality frontier or a clean no-anchor versus preservation contrast with confidence intervals | One convenient coefficient that reduces both spill and acquisition |
| Root-level historical lifetime analysis clearly labeled retrospective, plus a prospective saved-checkpoint re-read if feasible | Treating checkpoints/programs/decodes as independent `n`, or calling a repeated-panel advantage increasing lifetime learning |
| A specific lesson's effect bounded against sham and no-teacher with a predeclared SESOI | “Parenting is null” from two underpowered lesson implementations |
| Releaseable benchmark, reducer, manifests, and negative outcomes | The chronology of every invalid oracle, shortcut, rerun, and protocol repair |

## Cheapest evidence before the abstract deadline

Priority is to turn one failure into a valid result, not to run more of the
organism.

1. **Finish one corrected prospective Q0 terminal.** Use the already specified
   pairwise/native-action Q0 with the passed surface control, exact-train
   storage, held-view extraction, headroom-aware acquisition, common-mode
   legal-action mass, itemwise non-cancelling legality, wrong-root, copy, and
   four locality families. The current preflight budgets at most three fits
   and `0.75` A40-hour. A terminal label such as
   `STORED_NOT_EXTRACTABLE` or
   `CONDITIONAL_BINDING_WITH_SPILL_OR_INTERFACE_FAILURE` is the minimum
   abstract-bearing result. Invalid oracle/replay/reducer output is no result.
2. **Repeat the frozen terminal recipe, not the search, on two fresh
   independent roots/seeds.** Two additional small roots are far more valuable
   for the null paper than M-core, a new writer family, or another lesson. A
   replicated storage-with-spill classification supports a bounded recipe/
   model conclusion; one root supports only a case study.
3. **Freeze a zero-new-training lifetime manifest.** Recompute the nine-life
   episode-384-to-1024 contrast, disjoint mid/final contrast, harm counts,
   static-text comparison, intervals, and exclusions from the immutable raw
   tables in one script and one result manifest. If saved checkpoints and time
   permit, prospectively re-read episode-384 and terminal states on one frozen
   source-disjoint deck with fixed inference seeds. This adds cheap inference,
   not another life; keep the result retrospective.
4. **Only if parenting remains in the main text, run one larger three-arm
   no-write lesson assay.** Freeze one already motivated lesson, sham,
   no-teacher, a task-utility endpoint, SESOI, and enough items to bound the
   effect. This can establish “this lesson did not help by at least X,” never
   “parenting does not work.” Otherwise move the existing teaching nulls to a
   short secondary section and spend nothing more on them before abstract
   freeze.
5. **Prepare the paper evidence objects now.** Figure 1 should be the
   storage-to-lifetime causal ladder; Figure 2 the acquisition/locality result
   with the retrospective lifetime decline as a separate panel; Table 1 should
   list every gate, independent unit, denominator, and disposition. Populate
   all three only from the frozen manifest.

Do not spend the pre-abstract window on M/L after a valid Q0 failure, semantic
compression, a new broad hyperparameter sweep, or polishing the current
CompilerGym-first positive manuscript. The checklist's September 17 claim
freeze is the real go/no-go: valid replicated Q0 null gives a bounded negative
submission; no valid Q0 gives no empirical submission.

## Branch-stable title and abstract

Recommended title:

> **Beyond Parametric Storage: Causal Tests of Per-Life Learning in Acting
> Agents**

It remains accurate if Q0 fails, if Q0 passes and M/L fail, or if one later
arrow succeeds. It does not claim parenting, meta-intelligence, continual
improvement, or superiority.

The following is a genuine null-branch abstract now, not a promise or result
placeholder. A valid Q0 terminal can replace the conditional-stress sentence
with its prospective numbers without changing the question, contribution, or
paper identity.

> Online low-rank updates are increasingly used as parametric memory for
> agents, but lower loss on experience-derived text does not show that an
> acting agent learned the right behavior without changing unrelated behavior,
> nor that successive writes improve a life. We separate this claim into
> controlled tests of supplied-material transfer, exact storage, held-view
> extraction, native-action redirection, policy locality, interface
> preservation, and utility across lifetime. Correct supplied mini-Sudoku
> material beat an equal-marginal corrupt corpus across three adapter-training
> seeds (`+2,+2,+5` exact solves). In a complementary-map stress test, fitted
> adapters followed opposite supplied PROSPECT maps on `32/32` development
> cases per map, yet exact copying fell from `14/16` without the adapter to
> `0/16`, with trained-dialect spill on `13/16--16/16` unrelated outputs. A
> matched preservation objective reduced spill by 91.2% but nearly eliminated
> conditional acquisition. In nine exploratory 1,024-episode lives, the
> within-panel adapter advantage declined from episode 384 to 1024 in every
> life (mean change `-0.0316`), while terminal disjoint performance also
> regressed. These results identify selective, coexistence-preserving writing,
> rather than raw storage capacity, as the bottleneck between parametric memory
> and autonomous lifetime learning in this system. We release the causal
> assays, failure-inclusive manifests, and controls needed to distinguish
> storage, extraction, policy spill, and compounding utility.

The abstract must continue to label the nine lives exploratory, bound all
claims to this writer/model/benchmark, and omit parenting unless a properly
bounded three-arm result exists. If the prospective Q0 evidence contradicts
the development diagnostics, report that; do not preserve the null story by
selecting historical runs.

## Required manuscript pivot if this branch is taken

The current `main.tex` cannot merely receive a negative final sentence. Its
headline is parenting plus a `2x2` lifetime study and it still contains
`TBD`/`UNPROVEN` slots. The null paper's order should instead be:

1. distinction between parametric storage and selective personal learning;
2. the causal assay ladder and exact claim boundaries;
3. supplied-material positive controls;
4. prospective writer result and acquisition--locality diagnosis;
5. retrospective repeated-write lifetime consequences;
6. bounded teaching nulls as secondary evidence;
7. limitations, especially one model/rank/writer family and non-prospective
   historical lives.

That is a coherent negative study. A chronology of failed implementations,
parenting attempts, CompilerGym lives, and future M/L plans is not.
