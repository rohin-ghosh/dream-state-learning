## We do not need implicit multi-hop composition inside the weights

Yes, for the intended architecture: learn usable memories and cues, retrieve the
relevant material into context, and make decisions there. Frequently useful
combinations may also be written as supported composite records. A latent
multi-hop diagnostic is not a prerequisite for the loop, and I am not extending
that diagnostic with more fits now.

But extraction is an operation performed by the running learner on available
experience; it is not something the optimizer can be assumed to do correctly.
For “Shelly was in the car” and “the car was red,” first establish that both
refer to the same car at the relevant time. A supported compilation can then
record “Shelly was in the red car,” retaining the source references. This does
not establish that Shelly owns the car. Otherwise keep separate facts or a
hypothesis rather than silently making a binding.

The joint statement need not be a literal sentence in the wake stream. It can
be generated during consolidation, and learning need not in general require
the exact future answer sentence in training. For our controlled path, however,
an explicit grounded record makes the intended binding inspectable. We must
test what an update actually makes retrievable rather than infer that the
weights extracted every useful relation. “Conscious space” here means the
operational context/scratchpad, not evidence of consciousness or of a particular
internal reasoning mechanism.

## Deterministic safeguards and a learned compilation policy are different layers

The deterministic part can preserve raw records, validate references, serialize,
count presentations, and execute a bounded replay schedule. It does not invent
semantic links. The learner can propose connections, summaries, questions,
contrasts and replay priorities while operating in consolidation mode with the
same adapter. Preserve those proposals and their outcomes so a later version
can learn from the process. No separate sleep adapter is needed for this design.

An actual thought is evidence that the learner generated that thought, not
evidence that its claim about the world is true. Keep an unverified idea as an
idea; do not relabel it as an observed fact to get it past a provenance check.
Keeping raw experience does not require training on every token at equal dose.
Selection and representation can change without deleting the original record.

One self-review pass is a reasonable bounded proposal. Training a compilation
policy with supervised examples is possible as an implementation choice; whether
it improves later learning remains the experiment. A good score after a sleep
does not by itself assign credit to its compilation thoughts: task difficulty,
replay volume, initial state and stochastic updates are alternative explanations.

Use separate, explicitly permitted learning feedback if compilation is to learn
from outcomes. Do not return sealed report-panel, gate-exam, final-test or held
canary scores to the parent/learner as teaching data. We must not turn the
evaluation battery into a covert teacher. Existing teacher-only guidance is
removed from student inputs, not merely given zero loss weight.

## Immediate work

The first outcome-generated SFT has four complete new routes out of eight and
reproduces exactly after adapter reload, but all four successes are recovery
routes. Three normal paths wrongly enter recovery; a fourth chooses a wrong-AT
event. I have started a fresh training-only collection with the existing stronger
public strategy to obtain genuine normal/KEEP successes before selecting a
branch-balanced corpus. No larger dose or automatic fit is attached to it.

The small own-EVENT sleep test separately achieved cold recall of all four
experienced facts. Native action choices did not improve; supplying the model's
own isolated reads yielded correct ports in a post-hoc content check, but its
strict newline endpoint failed and unknown-address abstention deteriorated.
This is a narrow access diagnostic, not the flywheel. It argues for connecting
the read interface to contextual decisions, not requiring implicit latent hops.

The next useful loop alternates outcome collection, one bounded update,
helper-free use and preservation checks. It need not wait for open-loop
“saturation,” a general-purpose compiler, or perfect autonomous free thought.
