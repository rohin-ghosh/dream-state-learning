   and superseded. Its bracketed success sentence is unusable; its current
   failure paragraph may be mined only with the stated exploratory boundary.

   describes a common `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` system. The v2 paper core and current
   audits instead make PCFL the headline M/L environment, CompilerGym
   secondary failure/transfer evidence, exact text a W/S/M carrier ceiling,
   and `ACTIVE_TEXT_NATIVE` a separate conditional fifth L branch. Donating a
   derived active-memory agent to all four parenting-by-SLEEP cells changes the
   estimand.
3. `main.tex` may describe the native response-only transactional writer only
   as the intended tested mechanism until an exact promoted implementation has
   a paper-grade execution receipt. The completed long-life evidence still
   uses legacy/bundled writers.
4. Use object names rather than overloaded H1/H2: `PERSIST`, `DEVxSLEEP`,
   M-E2/E4/E5, and `LATE`. A positive interaction alone is not increasing-
   lifetime improvement, and a fixed exact-text ceiling is not the strong
   evolving-text opponent.
