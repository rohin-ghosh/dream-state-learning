## Fixed question and sample

Does an actual child critique of its own public episode improve a second
attempt compared with an equally budgeted independent repeat-planning call?
This is prompted inference/candidate collection, not learned self-reflection.
Use all four TRAIN worlds of original rich shard0 in fixed order, tasks0–3
(16 episodes, eight same-display opposite-goal pairs). Reuse original EXPOSE
captures, unchanged37ec and frozen base. No PROBE model inputs, goal scores,
hidden teacher plan, corrected action, reward, or canonical answer is supplied.
There is no selection by initial success, retry, or world replacement.

## Three separate, captured phases

1. COMMON_INITIAL: one uncoached action-first rollout per planned episode,
   <=6 calls each. Use the rich public system, amended explicit V3/plain
   projection described above, and immutable
   turnbound hop runtime, exact captured-text memory, actual public responses.
   Do not call the rich teacher coach or source planner to form actor inputs.
   Execute exact extracted actions independently of rationale diagnostics.
   Stop normally on goal/error/cap. Preserve every raw response and failure.
2. SELF_CRITIQUE_REVISE: for every initial episode, one actual child call
   requesting a grounded critique of its public action/outcome history and
   a proposed improvement; then one fresh root-state rollout with that raw
   critique supplied as explicitly fallible child advice, never as authority.
3. REPEAT_NO_FEEDBACK: same initial task and same public history, one actual
   child call requesting an independent next-attempt plan, without critique,
   verdict, correction hint or researcher feedback. Then the same fresh
   rollout interface, supplying its raw plan as equally fallible child advice.

Here NO_FEEDBACK means no added critique/evaluative feedback: ordinary public
environment outcomes are IDENTICAL inputs to both intervention calls. The
intervention instruction is the sole planned input difference. This estimates
critique-prompting versus repeat-planning, not feedback access versus blindness
or critique against zero extra thinking. Both arms reuse byte-identical initial
captures/task/source IDs. Each later actor prefix contains only its current
action/public-outcome history plus the fixed intervention; prior rationale is
not recurrently injected. The next public observation is never supplied early.
Critique/plan is not repaired or filtered into a better answer. A failed
intervention remains captured and yields an explicitly unavailable advice
marker, not a fabricated critique; the final attempt still runs.
