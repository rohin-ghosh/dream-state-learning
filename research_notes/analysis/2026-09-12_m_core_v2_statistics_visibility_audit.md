# Statistical-identification and visibility audit of M-core v2

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Status: independent watcher review only. I changed no builder source, benchmark,
child, model, adapter, job, GPU state, coordination file, or scientific claim.

## Verdict

**REWORK before any M fit.** The six-fit allocation, root/twin unit, crossed
source intervention, truthful outcome-twin builds, and matched S2 pad repair
the principal arithmetic and causal collisions in v1. The design can identify
a narrow compiler-mediated, typed two-cycle relay after the visibility and
inference repairs below.

It cannot presently identify genuine child organization or child-specific
`DREAM intelligence`. The two useful lanes are exactly the two lanes marked by
a complete public joint-versus-single-component necessity experiment. The
so-called `NECESSITY_ORACLE_CPU` uses no hidden truth: it is the obvious frozen
algorithm on the same evidence and roster shown to the child. Beating endpoint
join, frequency, and chance while merely matching that algorithm does not
distinguish organization from a hardcoded necessity calculation.

The reader gate is also not closed. Conditioning mutual information on a raw,
root-unique opaque query makes the proposed equality vacuous, and a 32-slot
bank being larger than the read budget does not prove a read ceiling because
the backend scans all 32 slots on every READ.

## Items that check out

- Fit arithmetic is exact: three S1 fits per root; three S2 fits only after the
  upstream gate; six fits per successful root. The two-root kills cost 6 S1
  fits and then 6 S2 fits, or 12 total. Eight-root DEV is at most `8*6=48`,
  confirmation is `16*6=96`, and their worst-case total is 144. At 7.5--15
  minutes per fit, training alone is 18--36 A40-hours.
- One source-disjoint presealed root, not its two `h` twins, two goals, four C
  probes, calls, or checkpoints, is the independent unit. Twin/probe outcomes
  may only form one root-level statistic. The v2 text states this correctly.
- `P[Binomial(16,.5) >= 12] = 2517/65536 = 0.03840637`; the stated reliability
  arithmetic is correct. Fixed 16-root confirmation with no replacement,
  extension, or early-success stop is valid for the bounded `Pr(R=1)>.5`
  claim, conditional on iid root generation. DEV stopping is acceptable only
  as mechanics selection and has no inferential standing.
- The authentic `3:1` versus `1:3` source block and the within-situation
  `2:2`/`2:2` donor intervention destroy action--outcome association while
  preserving the stated marginals. FULL versus source-deranged can therefore
  identify carriage of the compiled contingency content at C. It does not
  identify storage of raw experience outside the declared compiler.
- FULL versus hit-matched DREAM derangement can identify correct link-binding
  content under the three-read assay. It does not identify child authorship:
  a compiler that applied the public necessity calculation would supply the
  same FULL bytes.
- `FULL_NEW_h0`, `FULL_NEW_h1`, and `FULL_OLD_PLUS_PAD` are the minimum three
  S2 fits for the claimed outcome redirection plus an equal-work second-write
  contrast. The pad comparison identifies new-target training versus another
  clean cumulative fit. Semantic specificity comes from the two FOUND twins,
  carrier-origin receipts, distinct returned rows/actions, and binding-swap
  and cut controls--not from FULL-versus-pad alone, whose status also changes.

## Required repairs

### 1. Narrow or redesign the DREAM claim

The minimum no-new-fit repair is to replace every substantive use of
`child-emitted organization` with **child-selected pre-blueprint pair set
identified by the registered public ablation signal**, and delete the
`child-specific DREAM intelligence` release option. The protocol may retain
`DREAM` as a stage name and may report that the child's sealed output was the
provenance of the trained rows.

If genuine organization is required, the task itself must change: the public
necessity statistic cannot uniquely determine the requested set, and a
registered mechanical policy using all actor-visible evidence--including the
necessity rule--must be a real comparator. Endpoint/frequency straw policies
are insufficient. Under the present deterministic evidence schedule the
necessity policy is a 100% ceiling, so no result can show that stronger claim.

### 2. Replace the mutual-information receipt with a noninterference contract

`I(catalog/order/status/length; correct row | public query)=0` is not usable as
written. Exact opaque query bytes are normally unique per root, so the
conditional cells are singletons; `correct row` is also often a function of
the query. Conversely, FOUND/MISS is intentionally informative about row
availability in pad and cut cells. Sixty-four fixtures cannot establish exact
zero mutual information under an unenumerated root/alias generator.

Freeze the complete side-channel vector: catalog fields and anchor IDs,
`query_fingerprint`, status, pad bytes, serialized length, reads remaining,
errors/retries, latency, candidate index/order, threshold/score, and process
exit behavior. Then require:

1. catalog, fingerprint, and counter fields are pure functions of allowed
   public history/query/budget; fingerprint adds no bytes derived from hidden
   role or carrier score;
2. score, winning index, candidate order, and timing are either inaccessible
   or constant-time/padded;
3. in every hit-matched answer-changing twin/derangement cell, the
   alpha-renamed metadata transcript is byte-identical and only the intended
   row payload changes;
4. MISS/status differences are analyzed separately as intended availability
   interventions, never included in an asserted global independence equality;
5. opaque IDs are sampled by a role-independent label assignment whose
   independence is proved from the generator construction. If MI is still
   reported, compute it over canonical finite factor cells rather than unique
   raw aliases, and prove the full-support equality analytically or
   exhaustively. A fixture sample may regression-test that proof but cannot
   prove `I=0`.

The universal proxy ceiling of `.25` must also be removed or defined. Chance
is endpoint-specific: inverse source action is binary (`.5`), informative
experiment family is binary (`.5`), one lane among eight is `.125`, and exact
two-of-eight selection is `1/28`. A `.25` law applies only to a separately
defined unique one-of-four target; the present Phase-C table makes two of four
experiments informative. Every proxy needs a named target, denominator,
counterbalancing table, and exact null law.

### 3. Prove traversal depth, not bank cardinality

Because each READ internally scores the full bank, `32 > reads_remaining`
does not prevent candidate enumeration and is not the atoms-versus-link
ceiling. Publish all legal query types, initial anchors, row-returned anchors,
and the exact state transition for FOUND/MISS/BLOCKED. Exhaustively model-check
every adaptive legal policy, including goal-first, reverse, repeated,
suffix/ID, MISS-branching, and returned-endpoint policies, and prove:

- one READ exposes at most one row payload and no raw score/index;
- no atoms-only policy completes either B trace in three reads;
- at least one atoms-only policy completes each B trace in exactly four;
- FULL completes each trace in exactly three; and
- no other legal anchor or query type bypasses these minima.

The 32-slot invariant remains useful for fixed compute/hit geometry, but it is
not evidence for the read lower bound.

### 4. Freeze root selection, estimands, and exact tests

- Before any Stage-2 root outcome, seal one ordered eight-root DEV manifest.
  Stage 2 must use entries 1--2 without replacement or success filtering;
  Stage 3 uses entries 3--8. The adaptively sized Stage-1 exact-text cohort is
  separate and descriptive only. Also freeze a condition/device/inference
  order schedule balanced independently of root truth and arm.
- Define source-control accuracy against the authentic root orientation even
  though the deranged arm has a tie, and publish the four-probe table. Those
  probes contribute one `S_r`, never four observations.
- Bind every diagnostic to a scalar. In particular, the current `W_r` has a
  free `h`; use
  `W_r = min_h[D(FULL_NEW_h)-D(FULL_OLD_PLUS_PAD)]`. Define whether the two B
  goals in `M_r` use a minimum or a prespecified mean. Keep the existing
  minimum over twins/cuts for `F_r`.
- Replace the undefined phrase `exact sign/randomization intervals`. With both
  semantic conditions observed rather than randomly label-assigned, a
  sign-flip test is not automatically exact. For each frozen root-level paired
  contrast, count `delta_r>0` with zero/missing/failed roots as non-successes,
  and use the exact binomial test and Clopper--Pearson interval for
  `Pr(delta>0)`; report all 16 magnitudes descriptively. Use randomization
  inference only if a genuine treatment-label randomization is constructed.
- For child versus a deterministic mechanical policy, specify the one-sided
  exact paired McNemar test on root-level selection-success indicators. A
  claim of superiority to every policy is an intersection--union claim; every
  component must pass the frozen alpha. Chance `1/28` remains a separate
  binomial construct check.

### 5. Close remaining contrast-matching channels

Source donor receipts and derivation kind must be audit-only. The source FULL
and deranged training views must match citation count, visible provenance
schema, serialized-length distribution, ID distribution, row/view count,
padding, render multiplicity, loss masks, and optimizer work; otherwise C can
classify synthetic provenance rather than contingency content. Apply the same
byte-level transcript checks to DREAM FULL/deranged and to the two truthful S2
twins. Preserve the current unrelated-action/non-harm invalidation rule.

## Minimal disposition

No additional standing fit is required. The exact minimum is: narrow the
DREAM language (or redesign that task before claiming organization), replace
the MI/proxy gate with the noninterference proof above, model-check the legal
READ transition system, prebind the Stage-2 root IDs/order schedule, close the
free-`h` estimand and exact tests, and make source derivation provenance
invisible and matched. With those changes, the six-fit design is acceptable
for the narrow finite typed-relay sentence; without them, confirmation would
quantify a partly leaked capacity assay and a mechanically prescribed pair
selection, not the advertised child-organization effect.
