## Scientific disposition and verification limits

The result supports **partial zero-new-fit contextual transfer to fresh
identifiers within the same interface/topology**: TRAINED executes three
source-grounded paths that ORIGINAL does not under matched supplied text.
Exact memorization of the original graph's identifiers alone cannot account
for successful routes using these disjoint new identifiers. Dependence on
usable context and a remaining intermediate-step failure are both visible.

It does not establish general planning, robust display-order invariance,
fresh-graph parametric storage, autonomous exploration/extraction, a new task
family, or H1/H2. Four cases on one fresh graph and one lineage are not four
independent population samples. The contrast is a **mixed-sleep trained
snapshot versus its pre-sleep adapter**, not trajectory gradients versus a
matched rehearsal/loss-off control. It cannot isolate which component of the
prior mixed update caused the gain; any future matched control is a separate
experiment, not retroactive evidence for this result.

No unresolved primary replay, stimulus, namespace, or state-join mismatch was
found. Behavioral failures and the unavailable remote-only failure log/weight
recomputation boundaries remain explicit above. No further hardening or
ancestry review is needed to complete this assigned bounded review.
