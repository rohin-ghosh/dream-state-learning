## Governing bytes and scope

The pre-outcome RECORD protocol says
`checkable_expectation (actual concrete check or falsifiable numerical expectation)`
and `first_person (singular OR plural substantive own account, not literal-I test)`.
It explicitly says headings, length, fluent text, correct gold and pronouns
alone never qualify. The original RICH protocol independently says
`checkable expectation or concrete consistency check`.

SCALE's pre-outcome instructions also explicitly allow a concrete numerical
expectation/check, without requiring a separate inverse check. That permission
is not introduced in response to the audit or to reach1000. An operation tied
to its problem-imposed meaning can satisfy expectation while deriving an answer;
an ungrounded equation or a generic 'checked it' assertion cannot. Outcome alone
cannot prove either ownership or expectation.

Gauss's addendum acknowledges that requiring verification distinct from
derivation cannot be defended from the expectation alternative. I agree with
that self-critique, not with replacing his frozen labels en masse. Main's
conservative43NEW/8OLD/3rich conjunction is a separately identified sensitivity
result; original55/9/28 and83NEW-pipeline official counts remain unchanged.
