# THINK--DREAM--SLEEP minimum decisive program audit after SEQ-073/074

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Status: independent watcher recommendation only. This note changes no builder
source, experiment, adapter, job, GPU allocation, architecture, or claim. It
does not pause Astra under `AGENTS.md`.

## Verdict

The current synthesis is scientifically careful but too large for the core
paper. It has turned three organism operations into seven independently
releasable ladders (`W0/W1/S/M/L/B/C`), then split M and L again. Most of those
labels are useful failure localization; they are not seven claims the paper
needs.

The minimum full paper needs only:

1. one cheap, selective writer qualification;
2. one end-to-end mechanism benchmark in which THINK supplies an authentic
   action/outcome, DREAM contributes one child-authored organization decision,
   and SLEEP writes it before a delayed old-plus-new action; and
3. one clean childhood-by-adult-SLEEP lifetime study, with a single evolving-
   text opponent if text superiority remains in the abstract.

Compression, broad external-memory saturation, scaled THINK branching, and a
separate persistent-sequential-update claim are optional. Their failure must
not prevent the organism or H1/H2 paper.

SEQ-073 and SEQ-074 strengthen this ruling. Correct external-oracle Sudoku
material beat matched corrupt material at all three optimizer seeds, so useful
content can alter a later first action. Prefix masking then reduced spill only
from `.416` to `.202` against the `.03` ceiling while eliminating acquisition
(`I_d_frame=.015`, interval crossing zero). More behavior-transfer seeds,
learning-rate sweeps, rank sweeps, and mask variants on the old frame corpus
now have very low information value. The live uncertainty is conditional
selectivity and the closed experience-to-action loop.

## Audit against the actual architecture

| operation | what must appear in the decisive benchmark | what does not count |
|---|---|---|
| **THINK** | The child chooses a public action; the environment, not the child or evaluator, supplies its causally later outcome; a later goal changes the child's query/path/action. | External-oracle targets, success-selected final actions, compiler-authored experiments, or evaluator replacement actions. |
| **DREAM** | Before an answer-bearing blueprint is visible, the child selects or proposes at least one memory/link/schema organization that is later publicly checkable. A matched null/derangement removes its value. | A mechanically joined ledger alone. That is an authentic source for SLEEP, but it does not show that DREAM contributed intelligence. Shorter prose is not compression. |
| **SLEEP** | A frozen, provenance-gated write from the public material changes a sterile descendant's delayed action; SLEEP-off, wrong-root, and matched derangement remove the effect; old content survives the new write. | Lower training loss, high candidate mass, broad action habits, or an in-context reader doing the reasoning. |

The current S design is a good source falsifier, but by itself it contains no
necessary child-authored DREAM decision. Conversely, M contains the needed
pre-blueprint link proposal and old-to-new experiment, but its source arrow
must use the ledger's real action/outcome bytes. The paper gets more evidence
per fit by joining these properties in one M confirmation than by powering S
and M as separate populations.

## Complexity that does not buy another main-paper claim

- **Eight-root standalone S confirmation.** Keep one root, then at most three,
  as a kill/feasibility gate. Put the final `AUTH` versus
  `OUTCOME_SHUFFLED` contrast into the same roots as the end-to-end mechanism
  confirmation. This avoids up to the planned sixteen separate S fits and
  still identifies the authentic-source arrow.
- **W1 as a paper rung.** Replay-supported two-bank reconstruction is an
  engineering prerequisite, not a headline. After W0, one root-level OLD/NEW
  coexistence canary is enough to avoid wasting M fits. Require old/new
  retention again inside M and L; do not separately power W1.
- **Separate powered confirmations for M-E2, M-E4, and M-E5.** Use one primary
  delayed old-plus-new action contrast. Connected carriage, goal redirection,
  and informative action are noncompensatory trace gates on the same root, not
  three populations with three independently selected sample sizes.
- **A second native-policy M campaign.** Either use the ordinary legal API in
  the decisive endpoint or keep the exact four-read endpoint and call it
  `typed functional traversal`. A second campaign buys only the adjective
  “agentic.”
- **Separate PERSIST and H1 campaigns.** Parent/nursery deletion plus the
  parented-versus-sham frozen branches in the final root block already test
  parent-absent expression. Add fresh trigger/contraindication items at entry;
  do not raise another child population.
- **Every text candidate as a lifetime arm.** Recency, raw BM25/RAG, static
  brief, and evolving RAG belong in DEV/certification. Only the frozen winner
  enters confirmation.
- **The word “saturated.”** Finite-horizon `P-RUN>P-TEXT` does not need it. A
  doubled-read-budget branch and equivalence test are justified only if that
  word remains in the headline.
- **Compression in the critical path.** The current 80.8 MB rank-8 package
  cannot establish physical compression at these loads. Run the semantic-code
  assay after connected utility, preferably for the appendix.
- **Scaled THINK, repeated CompilerGym lives, and another Sudoku replication.**
  They are useful secondary characterization, but none closes H1/H2 or the
  full three-operation relay. Use the safe reference THINK and the existing
  SEQ-073 result.
- **Further first-person NOTE rescue.** Authentic public action/outcome joins
  are already available. Child-authored organization should be measured at the
  link/selection seam, not made contingent on one brittle prose register.

The one writer-recipe 2x2 remains worthwhile because its cap is about three
A40-hours and it directly separates storage, extraction, interface, and
locality. It is the last justified writer screen: if neither cross-view
rendering nor the OFF-KL anchor yields an absolute selective pass, stop recipe
micro-sweeps and narrow the paper rather than multiplying them.

## Minimum decisive benchmark family

Use one finite PCFL-style generator with mechanically provable shortcuts and
two **source-disjoint studies**, not a collection of unrelated headline gyms.

### Study M-core: one end-to-end two-cycle relay

One root should contain:

```text
THINK: act -> public outcome -> authentic old atoms
DREAM: propose one link before its action blueprint is visible
public co-use verifies the proposal
SLEEP-1 -> sterile reset -> goal swap redirects query/path/action
old memory selects between two genuinely different experiments
THINK: act -> public outcome selects a precommitted new row
SLEEP-2 cumulative write -> sterile reset
delayed goal requires exactly one old relation and the new row
```

Minimum scientific conditions per root are:

| condition | extra fit cost | purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| `FULL_AUTH` | yes | Complete child-action, child-DREAM, SLEEP chain. |
| `OUTCOME_SHUFFLED` | yes | Same event IDs, surfaces, marginals, dose, and write work; breaks the authentic action--outcome binding. |
| `DREAM_NULL` | yes | Same authentic source and matched write dose; removes or deranges the child-authored link/organization. |
| `SLEEP_OFF` | no new fit | Same chronology and shadow artifacts, no promotion; identifies dependence on the write. |
| `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS` | no LoRA fit | Exact finite-reader ceiling and assay-validity control. |

Adapter-off, cyclic wrong-root, bridge/old/new cuts, truthful goal and outcome
twins, catalog permutation, and read permutation should be inference-only
interventions on these artifacts wherever possible, not new training arms.
Every positive read needs the existing row-to-carrier-to-visible-byte receipt.

The one primary root endpoint is strict delayed utility in `FULL_AUTH` relative
to both `OUTCOME_SHUFFLED` and `DREAM_NULL`, with `SLEEP_OFF` removing it.
Release it only when the same root also passes: pre-blueprint authorship, goal
swap redirection of query/path/action, content-specific experiment redirection,
outcome-twin redirection of the new row and delayed action, old/new necessity,
interface/locality, and retention. These are conjunction components, not
separate winners.

Run CPU theorem/leakage/oracle-air-gap proofs and four exact-text roots first.
Then use two LoRA roots as a kill gate. Only a surviving frozen design proceeds
to one fresh, jointly powered confirmation cohort. A separate eight-root S
campaign, full six-build M deck, and endpoint-specific confirmations are not
needed.

### Study L-core: the actual H1/H2 and lifetime test

Raise one matched childhood factor: targeted process parenting versus a
dose-matched target-blind sham, teaching one predeclared competency. Delete
parent, nursery, childhood text stores, indexes, caches, and query state. At
deployment, one independent root block contains exactly:

```text
P-RUN       parented, SLEEP promotes
P-FROZEN    parented, shadow SLEEP only
U-RUN       sham, SLEEP promotes
U-FROZEN    sham, shadow SLEEP only
P-TEXT      same parented checkpoint, childhood LoRA frozen,
            one qualified public-only evolving text memory
```

The first four branches are the minimum H2 factorial. `P-TEXT` is the fifth
and final arm only because the current full objective includes a strong-text
comparison; run it concurrently for clean paired estimation. If the paper
drops text superiority, omit the fifth branch rather than replacing it with a
weak static brief.

Use source-disjoint parallel PCFL information cohorts on an exogenous exposure
clock and the same seven cuts. The minimum root-level releases are:

- **H1 / parent-absent expression:** `P-FROZEN-U-FROZEN` on fresh entry trigger
  and contraindication items after deletion;
- **H2 / parenting-by-SLEEP:** require both
  `AUC(P-RUN)-AUC(P-FROZEN)>0` and the interaction
  `(P-RUN-P-FROZEN)-(U-RUN-U-FROZEN)>0`;
- **increasing lifetime:** positive absolute `P-RUN` late slope and
  anchor-to-terminal gain, plus childhood and acquired-early-cohort retention;
  and
- **strong-text advantage, if retained:** paired positive late-slope advantage
  and terminal practical superiority over the one qualified `P-TEXT`.

One excluded end-to-end canary, one small source-disjoint DEV cohort, and one
fresh confirmation cohort are enough. DEV selects mechanics, horizon, forms,
and nuisance variance, never the largest treatment effect. Confirmation N
comes from a sealed blinded/mean-free root-level variance rule under a hard
resource ceiling. Do not create separate confirmation cohorts for H1, H2,
retention, and text advantage.

## Information-per-GPU-hour funnel

1. **Zero fits:** close CPU theorem/shortcut, pre-blueprint authorship, and
   public-carrier proofs first; then spend inference only on the fresh exact-row
   interface, exact-text M, and the text-memory certificate.
2. **About 0.5 A40-hour if the inherited recipe works:** run fresh W0's four
   fits and score-only gates. If it fails after a valid carrier, run the one
   capped writer 2x2 and no further recipe family.
3. **One root only:** run the minimal W1 coexistence canary; stop if old/new
   cannot coexist. It is not a release.
4. **Two roots:** run the three trained M-core conditions, reusing each build
   for all read-time cuts/twins. Stop before confirmation if authentic source,
   DREAM organization, or SLEEP promotion does not have the preregistered
   direction in both roots.
5. **Spend the remaining scientific budget on independent L root blocks.**
   Lifetime slopes and the parenting interaction are the user-defined paper,
   and independent children—not extra templates, probes, endpoints, or
   adapters—buy their precision.
6. **Only after M/L:** run semantic rate--distortion. Add doubled text access
   or powered LoRA transport only if the corresponding sentence still matters.

This funnel preserves diagnostics but stops treating them as destinations. It
also concentrates GPU-hours where they change the abstract: selective closed-
loop behavior, the H2 interaction, absolute late improvement, and comparison
to one credible alternative.

## Claim ceiling

If M-core and the five-branch L-core pass, the paper can say that, in a finite
benchmark, a parent-deleted child used a child-organized, publicly grounded
THINK--DREAM--SLEEP write to alter delayed old-plus-new action, and that
parenting increased the value of continued adult consolidation over a bounded
lifetime; the running parented learner improved absolutely and exceeded one
qualified evolving-text system at the frozen resource point.

Do not add `physical compression`, `external-memory saturation`, `native graph
learning`, `open-ended`, or `general agentic expansion` unless their optional
tests pass. Those adjectives are not needed for the core contribution.
