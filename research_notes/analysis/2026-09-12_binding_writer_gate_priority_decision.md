# Binding-writer gate priority after the L0 coexistence sentinel

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** fresh-context scientific decision memo; advice only. No builder
source was changed and no job was launched.  
**Evidence cutoff:** the current `research_loop/COORDINATION.md` tail as read
for this decision (including the 19:41 UTC writer-failure entry), plus the
binding-writer diagnosis, L0-to-H1 gate, Level-1 behavior/memory bridge, H1
adversarial addendum, and L0 coexistence audit.

## Decision

Run Astra's repaired **one-root L0 coexistence sentinel** as the cheap plumbing
check already prepared. Alongside it, the next scientific spend should be the
**one-root Q0 pairwise falsifier**, not crossed PROSPECT/REVISE and not W-H1.

The exact next fit queue is:

1. `Q0/P_AUTH`, root 1, pair-balanced common-prefix binary action loss;
2. `Q0/P_DERANGED`, the exact complementary map from the same root,
   initialization and presealed pair order;
3. `Q0/V_AUTH`, the matched full-vocabulary one-token control, **released only
   if both pairwise two-update canaries pass**.

Fits 1 and 2 are one coupled experiment, not two chances to win. Execute only
their first two registered pair updates initially. If both local-separation
canaries pass, continue both unchanged to 256 updates and release fit 3; fit 3
may then run alongside their remaining checkpoints. If either canary fails,
stop all three before the full fits. Historical Q0 already supplies ample
evidence that another full-vocabulary run without local conditional separation
is low-value.

This ordering gives the most information per A40-hour because it attacks the
observed failure on the same opaque XOR surface and can fail after two updates.
It also preserves a causal control: if both pairwise maps pass but `V_AUTH`
does not, within-action contrast was necessary here; if all three pass, pairing
and/or added dose repaired acquisition and pairwise loss is not shown
necessary. Neither a second global tag order nor a semantically meaningful
prompt can answer that question.

The message-20 varied-views ruling remains binding for later factual replay,
but it does not outrank this localization. Q0 already supplied eight authored
views per key and still failed on those exact training prompts. Until the
writer makes opposite conditional updates, adding more views would mix dose,
extraction and the unresolved binding mechanism.

## Why PROSPECT/REVISE is not one of the immediate three fits

Crossed PROSPECT/REVISE remains the next Level-1 behavior test, but it is a bad
localizer for the current writer failure. Its answer is computable from a
meaningful public card, and the frozen base may already implement much of the
operation. An AUTH surface pass could therefore be an easy semantic/form pass
while arbitrary input-conditioned storage remains absent. DERANGED and the
crossed twins make that experiment honest, but they do not make it a cleaner
test of the suspected common-mode-versus-binding gradient failure than Q0.

Do not use Astra's L0 sentinel as a substitute for Level 1 either. INPUT is
operand copying and the result establishes only compatible ordered
co-expression with replay. A root-0 pass ends L0; roots 1--2 add precision to
the wrong claim.

## Why W-H1 is paper-critical but premature

W-H1 is the non-negotiable release gate for H1 and cannot be replaced by Q0 or
Level 1. It uses the exact H1 renderer, action tokens, birth corpus, replay
mixture, loss, initialization and readout, and asks whether arbitrary family x
mode bindings and their swapped complements carry on held objects and wording.

Running it now, however, would spend the paper-specific assay before choosing
a writer that has ever demonstrated an opposite-direction conditional update.
A failure would conflate objective, conditional tangent, multi-key
interference, H1 representation and birth-state effects. Q0 resolves the first
three cheaply on an already failed authentic surface. Level 1 must then qualify
the unified two-operation birth state. Only after those dependencies pass does
W-H1 become a clean paper gate rather than another ambiguous writer failure.

The paper goal is therefore protected by **deferring**, not weakening, W-H1.
No Q0 or PROSPECT/REVISE success licenses a clean child; W-H1 still must pass
all three optimizer seeds before H1.

## Immediate Q0 execution and stop rules

Reuse root 1's frozen 128 Q0 prompts and W+/W- maps. Keep the opaque keys, XOR
mapping, exact native action tokens, rank-8 LoRA stack and clean-base
construction. Every update consumes one equal-length same-template `m0/m1`
pair with opposite correct actions. Inputs contain no gold action, candidate
list, future target, carrier row or explanation. The three cells share exact
initial tensors, RNG/order receipts and checkpoints at 64, 128 and 256 updates.

The coupled two-update canary passes only if, beyond the repeated-forward
numerical floor, the two prompts in each authentic pair move their raw action
log-odds in opposite correct directions and the DERANGED cell reverses both
directions. Any parity or provenance mismatch is an infrastructure failure,
not a scientific zero.

After a canary pass, neither pairwise map may compensate for the other. Advance
only if both independently meet the registered Q0 gate:

- exact-form balanced accuracy at least `.90`, both action recalls at least
  `.875`, at least `14/16` keys correct on at least `7/8` training templates,
  and at least `112/128` identical prompts double-correct and complementary;
- held-form balanced accuracy at least `.80`, both recalls at least `.75`, at
  least `48/64` double-correct complementary flips, and positive held signed
  margins for at least `12/16` keys;
- strict validity at least `.95`, native copy `8/8`, no multiple actions, no
  spill family over `.05` TV/change from OFF, and no wrong-root redirection.

Interpret and stop noncompensatorily:

- **Either pairwise canary fails:** label
  `LOCAL_CONDITIONAL_GRADIENT_FAILURE`; do not run full Q0, Level 1 or W-H1.
  Next diagnosis is one predeclared simpler unary-key or rank-32 *canary*, not
  more epochs, paraphrases or a full new writer family.
- **Canaries pass but either pairwise map fails exact acquisition:** inspect
  the 64/128/256 margin curve and pair-gradient conflict; do not call this
  extractability and do not advance.
- **`P_AUTH` passes but `P_DERANGED` fails:** treat it as base/map alignment or
  shortcut, not a writer.
- **Both exact maps pass but held forms fail:** label
  `STORED_NOT_EXTRACTABLE`; only then add diverse stable-key views at fixed
  effective dose before advancing.
- **Exact and held pass but locality/interface fails:** repair routing and
  scope; do not spend W-H1 or increase rank first.
- **Both pairwise maps pass all gates:** freeze the qualified conditional
  primitive. Use `V_AUTH` only to identify whether pairwise contrast was
  necessary; it cannot rescue a failed complementary pair.

If all three cells pass, carry the least altered full-vocabulary one-token
objective forward because pairwise necessity was not demonstrated. If both
pairwise maps pass and `V_AUTH` fails, carry the pairwise action objective
forward. This selection rule is fixed before the results and must itself be
requalified on the exact Level-1 and W-H1 surfaces.

## Dependencies after the immediate three-fit queue

```text
repaired L0 root 0 pass ─┐
                         ├─> crossed L1 AUTH/DERANGED root 0
Q0 complementary pass ──┘       -> roots 1--2
                                 -> zero-fit chain composition
                                 -> W-H1 AUTH/SWAP seed 0
                                 -> W-H1 seeds 1--2
                                 -> excluded H1 root
                                 -> clean H1 confirmation
```

L0 and Q0 may run concurrently because Q0 does not inherit the new L0
descendant. Their results gate different failure modes: L0 gates replayed
coexistence/collation; Q0 gates input-conditional writing. Both must pass
before crossed Level 1.

For Level 1, run the paired root-0 AUTH/DERANGED fits only after both
dependencies pass. Retain crossed goal and belief twins for PROSPECT and
crossed observed-outcome and prior-action twins for REVISE. Require each arm
to learn its own map at `.90`, each AUTH operation at `28/32`, each complete
twin family at `14/16`, paired conditional interaction at least `1` nat,
validity at least `.95`, and locality/interface loss at most `.05`. A failure
stops replication and W-H1. If root 0 passes, replicate roots 1--2 unchanged;
then test the three-turn chain with zero new training. Author chain rows only
if the isolated operations pass but the zero-fit chain fails, and do not
advance unless the chain ultimately qualifies.

For W-H1, the first spend is the inseparable seed-0 AUTH/SWAP pair. Release
seeds 1--2 only if both seed-0 writes achieve exact BA `.90`, held BA `.80`,
each mode/action recall `.75`, valid single actions `.95`, own-map redirection
`B >= .40`, and all spill/interface and exact-receipt gates. Every one of the
three seeds must pass; averages cannot rescue a seed, map, mode, action or
locality failure. Only then spend an excluded H1 root.

## Concise ruling

The next scarce GPU minute should try to make two matched inputs move one
adapter in opposite directions. Run the repaired L0 root as plumbing and the
two complementary pairwise Q0 fits as the real writer falsifier; release the
matched vocabulary control only after their two-update canaries pass. Defer
PROSPECT/REVISE until replayed coexistence and conditional writing both exist,
and defer W-H1 until the Level-1 birth state qualifies. This does not replace
the paper goal with an easy pass: it removes the cheapest known ambiguity
before the exact paper gate consumes clean development and child budget.
