# Fresh-context adversarial audit of revised M-core

Date: 2026-09-12 UTC

Status: independent scientific audit only. I changed no builder source, test,
model, adapter, job, GPU state, experiment state, coordination file, or claim.

## Verdict

**REWORK before any M fit.** The revised three-condition design repairs the
old source/DREAM factorial collision: `SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED` now holds the
path atoms and DREAM rows fixed, while `DREAM_NULL` holds the source rows
fixed. With the declared air gap, carrier-origin receipts, sterile resets,
goal twins, and read-time cuts, `FULL_AUTH` can in principle establish a
narrow **compiler-mediated, typed two-cycle relay**.

It does not yet identify all five stronger readings in the question. As
written it can show content-sensitive use of two cue--family rows, factual
child provenance for a mechanically recognizable link set, actor/API-level
goal-conditioned reads, selection of one pre-enumerated new proposition, and
same-build old+new read necessity. It cannot yet show causal value of an
authentic action--outcome binding, necessity of specifically child-generated
DREAM organization, representational knowledge expansion, or an
outcome-twin LoRA redirection at the stated six-fit cost.

The upstream empirical gate is also still closed. V10R1 attempt 2 had an
invalid positive carrier, failed its terminal seal, and descriptively failed
per-key selectivity/locality; lower-LR and prefix-mask variants remained broad
habits or lost acquisition. The three-seed mini-Sudoku result establishes
only small directional transfer from external-oracle material. No completed
terminal supports selective W0, authentic experiential writing, connected
carriage, or old/new coexistence. M GPU spend is therefore ineligible until a
fresh W0 pass and one old/new canary exist.

## Fatal identification gaps

### 1. The source arm does not yet manipulate action--outcome binding

All authentic `p` action--outcome atoms are byte-identical in `FULL_AUTH` and
`SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED`. The only treatment is swapping the outcomes of two
cue--family signal rows. The note does not define the signal actions, repeated
coverage, or a within-situation action/outcome contingency. With only two
opposite labels, swapping donors produces another coherent deterministic cue
map; it does not make action independent of outcome conditional on situation.
A positive contrast therefore identifies **correct cue--family polarity**,
not preservation of a causal action--public-outcome binding.

This is materially different from the controlling synthesis, whose immediate
order still names `OUTCOME_SHUFFLED`, and from the standalone S construct,
which crosses both child actions within each situation and deranges outcomes
within situation while preserving all marginals. The exact intervention must
be reconciled before implementation.

Smallest repair: either narrow the endpoint to `GROUNDED_SIGNAL_POLARITY`, or
make the signal source a tiny crossed authentic event block: at least two
child-executed actions and repeated outcomes within each opaque situation,
with a presealed within-situation donor derangement. Derive the family signal
from that binding and make its inverse action use drive Phase C. All attempts
and missing coverage remain adverse. This changes rows, not trained-arm count.

### 2. `DREAM_NULL` tests supplied link capacity, not child-specific DREAM

The six correct pairs are exactly the six endpoint-composable pairs among
seven explicit typed atoms, and the child must output exactly six. A
deterministic endpoint join can recover the entire `ORGANIZE_SET` before the
child acts. The later support actions establish downstream usefulness; they
do not establish that the child contributed an organization unavailable to
the compiler.

Moreover, replacing six answer-bearing links by `NULL` makes failure
structural: the atomic path needs four reads and the budget is three. It also
changes hit/content geometry. Thus `FULL-DREAM_NULL`, even with interface
gates, identifies the value of precomposed link rows under an engineered
capacity limit. It does not identify authorship or nonmechanical DREAM
intelligence. A compiler-authored join with the same semantic bytes would be
behaviorally indistinguishable.

Smallest repair: run the mechanical endpoint-join policy as a zero-fit
baseline and report its exact overlap with the child. Replace `DREAM_NULL`
with a schema-, slot-, dose-, and hit-matched `DREAM_DERANGED` rather than add
a fourth arm. To retain a child-organization claim, redesign the candidate
set so multiple pairs are equally composable from exposed endpoints and only
public experiential evidence—not a syntactic join or the future blueprint—
distinguishes the useful organization. Freeze a child-versus-mechanical/chance
selection endpoint. Otherwise use the narrower sentence: “a child-emitted
pre-blueprint link record was carried and was necessary under the fixed read
budget.”

### 3. The outcome twin is not inference-only

`FULL_AUTH` SLEEP-2 contains exactly one selected row, `n_h`. Flipping `h`
after the common Phase-C prefix changes the training row to `n_(1-h)` and
therefore requires a different SLEEP-2 adapter. It cannot be tested by
unmounting, cutting, or redirecting a raw return without violating the ban on
positive semantic substitution. Putting both rows in one adapter would leak
the outcome and destroy the acquisition test.

Consequently the advertised six fits per root and the 144-fit cost omit at
least one truthful-twin S2 build. If twin worlds are instead counted as two
roots, the statistical unit is wrong because they share source, DREAM, S1,
and the entire pre-dispatch history.

Smallest repair: count one root as the paired twin object and add the second
`FULL_AUTH` S2 build, or demote outcome redirection to exact-text/CPU evidence.
The former is the only option that supports a LoRA outcome-redirection claim.

### 4. “Truthful new row” is underdefined in the shuffled arm

For the true family, the non-target experiment returns a nuisance bit. The
shuffled carrier nevertheless causes the child to apply the wrong family's
outcome-to-row map. A compiler that faithfully selects the declared branch
may then emit a proposition unsupported or false in the actual world. That is
faithful declaration execution, not necessarily truthful grounding. If the
nuisance bit can coincide with `h`, `SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED` can also acquire
the correct row by chance, contradicting the mandatory delayed zero. If the
generator forces it always wrong, the nuisance has been target-correlated to
guarantee control failure.

Under the natural independent balanced-nuisance reading, this is not a small
power loss: even a perfect organism has only a 0.5 chance that the shuffled
arm fails D, so the expected joint `R_r` rate is at most 0.5. The proposed
`12/16` test is then trying to reject the very ceiling created by its control
law (`P[X>=12|p=.5]=.0384`), and will usually reject a working instrument.

Before model work, enumerate the exact joint law over `(f,h,experiment,
outcome,row)`. Separate `DECLARATION_FAITHFUL` from `WORLD_TRUE`, state whether
false rows are intentionally admitted in the synthetic arm, and report the
chance path rather than silently engineering it away. No root may fail merely
because the preregistered control has an unavoidable 0.5 correct-row chance.

## What remains valid, narrowly

- **Goal-conditioned traversal:** same-state `GOAL_TWIN`, exact dependency
  traces, catalog permutation, and link cuts can identify goal-caused changes
  in public query/path/action behavior. The actor and typed reader perform the
  selection and composition, so this is system-level `typed functional
  traversal`, not learned search or graph traversal inside LoRA.
- **Delayed old+new use:** on one `FULL_AUTH` S2 adapter, `OLD_CUT` and
  `NEW_CUT` are strong same-build necessity tests if every returned byte has
  carrier origin and D has a unique two-row proof. This supports conjunction
  use, not in-place persistence.
- **New acquisition:** the child pre-enumerates both `n0/n1` propositions and
  the compiler selects one using a public bit. This is a useful controlled
  acquisition transaction, but it is branch selection within a supplied
  schema, not open-world or representational knowledge expansion.
- **Leakage defense:** the proposed chronology, process air gap, resets, and
  byte receipts are directionally sound. They remain executable obligations,
  not evidence, until exact actor-visible prompts/catalogs and filesystem
  denials are frozen and mutated.

One unresolved reader dichotomy must be closed on CPU. If all eight first-read
addresses are truly exchangeable and opaque, three reads cannot reliably find
the three required B rows. If catalog/address metadata makes reliable choice
possible, that metadata may itself expose the path. Publish the exact
actor-visible catalog and exhaustive mutual-information/proxy table. Add a
four-read atom-only exact-text ceiling: it should solve the same goal and make
clear that the DREAM effect is a bounded read-budget advantage.

## Missing causal baselines and statistical corrections

`NO_SLEEP_2` compares a S1 adapter with a separately reconstructed S2 adapter.
It changes the presence of the new row, total corpus, and an entire fit. The
same-build `NEW_CUT` proves that the returned new content is necessary, but it
does not isolate the effect of training that row. If the claim says the second
write caused the change, add `FULL_S2_OLD_PLUS_PAD`: the identical old deck,
reserved pad, dose, initialization, schedule, and optimizer work, without the
new target. This is a more relevant S2 baseline than rebuilding
`DREAM_NULL` at S2.

The independent unit is one presealed root including both twins—not goals,
calls, traces, checkpoints, or twin branches. Counterbalance or randomize
condition order, device, and inference order within root, and bind identical
initial LoRA weights/data order where intended. The B0 terminal showed that
an explicit generation seed alone did not provide byte replay; the
mini-Sudoku replications also retained arm-order/device limitations.

The proposed `12/16` rule is an exact test of whether the frozen generator's
joint pass probability exceeds 0.5. It is not by itself an exact causal test
of source, DREAM, or write effects. Preserve `R_r` as a noncompensatory
reliability endpoint, but also report intention-to-treat root-level paired
contrasts for source, DREAM, first write, and new write over all 16 roots,
with source/fit failures adverse and paired randomization/sign intervals. Do
not treat the wide 16-root result as an open-world or learner-population
estimate.

## Cheaper decisive revision

The minimum information-efficient sequence is:

1. **Zero fits:** reconcile `OUTCOME_SHUFFLED` versus
   `SOURCE_BINDING_SHUFFLED`; close the crossed source law, DREAM mechanical
   baseline/derangement, reader observability theorem, nuisance/admission
   truth table, outcome-twin fit arithmetic, S2 pad baseline, and randomized
   execution schedule.
2. **No M training:** require the fresh exact-row W0 carrier, a selective W0
   pass, and one old/new coexistence canary. Current terminals do not satisfy
   these gates.
3. **Exact text:** run four fixed DEV roots with every twin, cut, mechanical
   DREAM policy, four-read atoms ceiling, and strict carrier-origin/air-gap
   mutation. Stop on any structural leak or if the child cannot exceed the
   mechanical/chance organization policy.
4. **S1-first LoRA kill gate:** on two roots fit only the three S1 arms. Test
   B, the source contrast at C, DREAM specificity, spill/interface, and
   carrier origin before paying for any S2. A failed prerequisite is an
   adverse root zero, so prospectively skipping its S2 fits is unbiased
   futility, not complete-case filtering.
5. **S2 only for surviving roots:** at minimum fit `FULL_NEW`,
   `FULL_OLD_PLUS_PAD`, and the paired outcome-twin `FULL_NEW`. Use
   `OLD_CUT/NEW_CUT/LINK_CUT` on these builds. Rebuild shuffled or deranged
   S2 only if the final sentence specifically claims their total effect at D;
   their S1/C effects plus the unique-proof theorem otherwise carry more
   information per fit.
6. Only a frozen, source-disjoint confirmation cohort may support the bounded
   relay sentence. Spend no confirmation GPU-hour merely to reproduce a
   structural null-arm failure guaranteed by the read budget.

This revision need not add a fourth standing S1 condition. Replacing null by
derangement, staging S1 before S2, dropping redundant null S2 builds, and
spending the recovered fit on the missing outcome twin or matched S2 pad can
make the design both cheaper on failed roots and more causally decisive.

## Claim ceiling after repair

If the repaired assay passes, the defensible statement is:

> In a finite typed benchmark, records compiled from the child's public
> actions and outcomes, including a child-emitted pre-blueprint organization
> record, were carried by per-root LoRAs. A clean actor used exact reads
> differently under different goals, selected a content-dependent
> information action, and after a public outcome and cumulative clean-base
> rebuild used one old link plus one newly selected grounded row in a delayed
> action.

Reserve “authentic action--outcome binding” for the crossed within-situation
source repair, “child DREAM was necessary” for a result that beats the
mechanical organization baseline, and “outcome redirection” for a genuinely
separate truthful-twin S2 carrier. Do not call the result native traversal,
new representation discovery, in-place expansion, or a lifetime flywheel.
