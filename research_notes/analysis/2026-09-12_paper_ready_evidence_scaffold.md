## Abstract A — positive writer-ingredient branch

The body below is fully supported at this evidence cut. The gated sentence is
optional and must remain absent from a submitted abstract until released.

> Per-life parametric learning requires more than storing a training set: a
> writer must extract learned content under new wording, coexist with prior
> behavior, and change only the intended policy. We separate these properties
> with supplied-material, native-action, locality, and repeated-write assays
> for rank-8 adapters over a frozen instruction-tuned model. Correct
> mini-Sudoku material beat equal-marginal corrupt material across three
> adapter-training seeds, by `+2,+2,+5` exact first-action solves. In a
> 16-fact assay, distinct-source interleaving with explicit arithmetic
> rehearsal let `FOUR_VIEW` adapters score `15--16/16` on both development and
> exact fact probes, `45--48/48` across three held lexical families, and
> `32/32` on the old action in each of three optimizer/warm-start lineages.
> `SINGLE_VIEW` met the joint criterion in two lineages; in the third it scored
> `4/16`, `5/16`, `12/48`, and `0/32`, despite valid output syntax. This is a
> positive small supplied-writer/coexistence ingredient and a directional
> robustness result, not evidence that lexical diversity is universally
> superior. A separate conditional-map stress test carried opposite supplied
> maps but changed unrelated outputs: exact copy fell from `14/16` without the
> adapter to `0/16`, with trained-dialect spill on `13/16--16/16` controls.
> Historical repeated-write lives likewise showed finite average behavior
> change without compounding late utility. These results separate feasible
> supplied storage and rehearsed coexistence from the still-open problem of
> selective, cumulative personal learning.
>
> **[GATE: W0+]** *Only after release, add one manifest-generated sentence
> reporting the number of independent roots and the exact all-cell storage,
> held extraction, native generation, locality, and interface conjunction.*

## Abstract B — replicated selectivity-null branch

The current positive controls and historical clauses are SEALED. Both bold
bracketed clauses are unsupported today and must be generated from the final
Q0 manifest, not completed from development diagnostics.

> Online low-rank updates are often evaluated by training loss or recall, but
> an acting-agent writer must also preserve unrelated behavior. We introduce
> a causal ladder that separates surface expressibility, exact storage,
> held-view extraction, native-action redirection, policy locality,
> coexistence, and lifetime utility. The frozen model passed the inference-only
> surface control (`64/64` valid generations and scores; `32/32` complementary
> binding redirects in each modality), and supplied-material positives showed
> that this rank-8 substrate can change behavior: useful mini-Sudoku material
> beat matched corrupt material by `+2,+2,+5` exact solves, while
> distinct-source interleaving stored and extracted `15--16/16` supplied facts
> and preserved a rehearsed `32/32` old action in three optimizer lineages.
> **[GATE: W0-NULL-R] Across _N_ prospectively frozen independent writer
> roots, exact storage and held extraction met every acquisition criterion,
> but all _N_ failed the same non-cancelling locality/interface conjunction;
> insert the exact per-root counts and interval from the immutable manifest.**
> Matched development interventions already traced the mechanism boundary:
> lowering learning rate left spill at `.296--.394`, prefix masking suppressed
> acquisition while leaving `.202` spill, and a preservation objective reduced
> spill by 91.2% but reduced `I_d_frame` from `1.921` to `.152`, with its
> interval crossing zero. In nine exploratory 1,024-episode lives, the
> within-panel adapter advantage fell from episode 384 to 1024 in `9/9` lives
> (mean change `-.03158`), and terminal disjoint performance regressed.
> **[GATE: W0-NULL-R] Together, these results identify
> selective/coexistence-preserving policy change—not raw storage capacity—as
> the binding failure of this writer under the tested model and tasks.**
