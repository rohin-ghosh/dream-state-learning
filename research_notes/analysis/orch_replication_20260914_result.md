captured EVENT validity rules, loaded checkpoint state hashes and transition
semantics are the intended contract. World/source collection and old audit APIs
are reused; the route readout/scorer is independently coded, not the entire
software stack. Same-family fixed topology, supplied text, greedy inference,
one exposed development lineage and the existing saved fit pair only.

exceed loss-off and the deterministic reference. It did exceed both here, and
no tested old-fact/audit retention was lost. This does not establish causal
necessity of reading the supplied text (no no-text/shuffle condition), isolate
training dose, demonstrate a new family, or establish robustness across
training seeds. No latent-hop, H2, unseen-family, general-planning, clean-ancestry,
or new parametric-memory acquisition claim. No new H1/H2 thesis claim.

training-seed replication, with the same retention and reference controls and
fresh cohort construction before results. That is a recommendation, not an
