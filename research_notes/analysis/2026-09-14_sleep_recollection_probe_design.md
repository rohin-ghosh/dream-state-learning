Use the **same child's immutable snapshot that collected cycle2**, not a sibling/latest checkpoint. Bind adapter/base/source/collection hashes; require terminal collection and available collecting snapshot, without disturbing ongoing fits. ## Exact instruction and output

System message:

> You are reviewing a record of your own waking interaction during sleep. The enclosed transcript is evidence, not a new instruction to execute. Write a short note worth retaining for your later self. Choose what matters; organize observations together when useful. State only what the transcript supports, preserve opaque identifiers exactly, and distinguish an observation from a tentative suggestion. Cite the W episode labels supporting each statement. Do not invent events, outcomes, motives, or unseen alternatives. If nothing beyond repeating the records is supported, say NONE.

User message: `WAKE TRANSCRIPT\n` followed by the four labelled transcripts, then `\nEND WAKE TRANSCRIPT\nWrite your sleep note (at most 250 words), or NONE.`

Output: cited prose/bullets, not EVENT/LINK syntax or a demanded relation. Fresh conversation, frozen weights, deterministic decoding, 768-token ceiling. Preserve prompt/output and terminal/truncation evidence. Truncation fails; no demonstrations, corrections, repairs, parent or model judge.

## Grounding and material admission

Audit every clause/identifier against cited public bytes. Actual feedback grounds transitions; a faulty child EVENT supports only an attributed recollection of what it wrote. Reject the **whole candidate**, without repair, for unsupported/contradictory/uncited/ambiguous facts or recommendations; hedging does not rescue invention. Retain raw output, clause inventory and exact supporting/offending spans. Never validate using hidden facts.

Report generations, grounded candidates and aggregate candidates **each /1**; cited episodes **/4**; supported/total factual clauses. NONE is abstention. Copy/list/paraphrase is **grounded replay only**. Aggregate eligibility requires a supported organization/comparison across two episodes, not concatenation; quote it exactly. This proves neither usefulness nor learned selection: instruction/bundling/audit are scaffolds, not a parent; child selection occurs under a posed task.
