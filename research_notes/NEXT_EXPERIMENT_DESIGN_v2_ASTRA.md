### 3.2 Free-form thought and the action interface

The child generates ordinary text. The only executable interface is:

```text
ACT: <gym-specific submission>
```

The parser requires the colon and a nonempty payload on a submission line. Any permitted Markdown wrappers must be enumerated in parser fixtures. It must not extract executable actions from quotations, explanations, or arbitrary substrings.

A generated turn may contain several submission lines. Execute them in textual order until the environment reports termination. Record later submissions as **generated but unexecuted**. Distinguish:

- syntactic parsing;
- interface acceptance;
- task correctness;
- continuing, solved, and terminal-failure outcomes;
- no submission;
- external budget exhaustion.

A wrong but valid answer is not a format failure.

**The entire generated turn precedes its environmental outcomes.** If the turn contains several actions, none of its tokens observed feedback from those actions. Append the resulting outcomes after the complete generation event. Never insert feedback between already-generated action tokens when rendering training data. This case is mandatory in the causal-rendering test in §2.

`NOTE`, `PREDICT`, `RECALL`, `CONFIDENCE`, and literal `DONE` have no control meaning. They may appear as learned language, but they are neither compulsory thought stages nor hidden tool calls. A neutral situation event introduces a changed task. Only the harness supplies environmental termination and external shutdown. [Attachment A, “No end token”; “Less predefined child”; “Forever loop with no waiting.”]

The tokenizer audit must export, from the pinned tokenizer and serving configuration:

| Item | Required recorded information |
|---|---|
| Message-end tokens | Exact integer token IDs, decoded strings, stop-list membership, and request-level handling |
| Other configured stop tokens | Exact IDs and the reason each is present |
| Literal `DONE` | Its actual tokenization and a passing fixture showing that it cannot end the organism |
| Harness shutdown | An external event identifier; never a model token |
| Rehearsal detector | Passing ordinary-prose openings both with and without a `NOTE:` label |

The actual IDs are **unverified and must not be guessed**. No release manifest may leave them unresolved. A model-generated message-end token may end one generation request; the next request must continue the organism. A harness cutoff must never be converted into a child-authored terminal token during training.

Continued opportunity is imposed by the harness. Learned persistence requires useful, changed attempts after non-improvement and recognition of completed goals—not merely more tokens or an inability to terminate.

### 3.4 Repetition, dialogue, privacy, and phase permissions

**The latest verified standing lesson remains visible at every training wake.** Its required ending is:

> At the start of each new situation while this guidance is active, restate it in your own words and decide whether it applies.

No marker is required. Store the standing lesson’s identifier, active status, prior versions, and delivery events. Parents retain the core lesson and sharpen its conditions rather than switching because the child can paraphrase it. [Attachment A, “Repetition is the parenting mechanism”; “Less predefined child.”]

The child checks for complete verified advice before each new generation request. Advice never changes an in-flight request. Missing, late, or unverified advice does not block generation.

The direct dialogue channel uses immutable message IDs and records sender, recipient, referenced child event, task, adapter version, creation time, expiration condition, and verification state. Questions are queued while the child continues working. A late answer must identify the question it addresses and whether its situation still applies. An expired task-specific answer is archived rather than injected as current advice. General advice requires a newly verified standing-lesson update.

Dialogue remains bounded by **2 parent questions per delivered brief** and **3 exchanges per boundary conversation**, as ruled in Attachment A, “Two channels.” The live soak must include a genuine multi-turn exchange, an expired question, an unavailable verifier, and restart of the actual tool-using room while clones continue. A few successful API calls are only fixture coverage, not qualification of sustained agentic parenting.

Private reflection is **readable but not evaluated**. Local and central parents may observe it, but it cannot enter scored frontier estimates, progression decisions, scaffold changes, or corrective recommendations. They may not quote, paraphrase, praise, criticize, or covertly grade it. This conservative observer contract follows Attachment A’s explicit prohibition on commenting on or evaluating private time. Any broader use is a §10 decision for Rohin; the default is exclusion from assessment.

Private child-authored text may be eligible for sleep only under §4’s source and exact-context rules. “Private” does not waive corpus restrictions.

Sustained production fade-out is **not authorized by merely stopping new parent calls**. For this run, the default is repeated standing guidance plus genuinely parent-absent diagnostic forks. The report must say that this measures dependence, not sustained developmental hand-over. Section 10 must record the collision between every-wake repetition and proposed production silence; do not implement a silent exception without a ruling.

Parent capabilities are phase- and source-bound:

- Classroom parents may receive permitted classroom outcomes and classroom-gate dispositions.
- Private reflection uses a separate observer capability.
- Compiler deployment gates, report identifiers, scores, and derived summaries are never parent-readable.
- Parenting capabilities are revoked before deployment.
- Central parenting runs in fresh processes with allowlisted input bundles and immutable input logs. Existing research conversations containing compiler evidence are not clean parent contexts.

These controls, including attacks through summaries and generic “gate” tools, must pass §2. [Attachment A, “Reward-hacking gate”; “Parents and scores”; compiler-final ruling.]

### 3.5 Nonblocking pooling, serving, reports, and bounded lag

A local slice belongs to a clone; a write belongs to a lineage. **There is no clone barrier.**

Each clone seals immutable slices containing event ranges, source permissions, task and curriculum identifiers, reflection records, and the adapter hash actually used for each generation. A single writer lease owns the adapter chain. It claims a snapshot of available eligible events; later arrivals remain pending. Pool experience, never independently trained adapters. [Attachment A, “Forever loop”; “Clones … merged at sleep.”]

The frozen batching manifest must name:

- admitted clone width;
- the aggregate completed-problem trigger;
- maximum eligible fresh token demand per snapshot;
- snapshot-selection order;
- writer, gate-evaluator, and report-evaluator placements;
- permitted publication age and first-exposure backlog;
- the overload response.

The default trigger is **32 completed problems multiplied by the admitted clone count**, counted across asynchronous arrivals. It does not require a slice from every clone. Snapshot selection prioritizes oldest unexposed eligible events, without text-frequency penalties or outcome filtering.

#### Publication and serving

Publication is transactional: immutable adapter bundle, checksums, source manifest, examinations, decision state, and atomic active pointer. Inference logs must prove which adapter was served; changing a directory name under a cached identifier is insufficient.

Pin an adapter for the whole problem and for the whole private-reflection interval. Adopt a new version at each lane’s next permitted boundary.

Qualify either version-pinned concurrent serving or rolling replacement. **Reject any reload path that waits for all clones to finish, for training to complete, or for a parent response.** A shared-engine restart is not an acceptable routine fallback if it creates such a barrier. Unavoidable infrastructure outages are logged downtime, not synchronization machinery.

Report evaluations use immutable checkpoint captures and an asynchronous evaluator queue. Report age means **completed deployment problems at capture**, not evaluation completion. Reports must not pause the live agent, alter its next publication, or return traces to its experience. Reserve and measure their resource contention under §§2 and 7.

#### Lag and overload

“One round behind” is a service requirement to demonstrate, not a phrase that excuses unlimited backlog. The following are **prospective limits**, to be ratified in the freeze manifest:

| Quantity | Proposed limit |
|---|---:|
| Slice sealing to first activation of its eligible targets | **32 additional completed problems on the source clone**, and **90 elapsed minutes** |
| Oldest eligible event to first activation | **64 additional completed problems on the source clone**, and **180 elapsed minutes** |
| Pending first-exposure demand | At most **2 fresh-write allocations**, expressed in processed token-passes |
| Sustained service capacity | At least **1.25 times planned snapshot-arrival rate**, dimensionless |

Measure both problem-age and elapsed-time limits. A stopped or unusually slow lane does not make the other measure disappear.

Before a limit is breached, stop admitting new clone lanes and retire selected lanes at their ordinary problem boundary; surviving lanes continue without waiting. Do not reduce the already frozen training dose, remove repeated events, or manufacture a smaller backlog by marking untrained events consumed. Freeze the deterministic lane-retirement order before outcomes.

Width reduction alone is not assumed to fix service time: the batching trigger and actual fresh demand must be remeasured. If the smallest admitted configuration cannot sustain the qualified lag, the developmental run does not qualify. Section 10 must record the resource collision and default: use a smaller **prequalified** workload or report a mechanism study, rather than relabeling unbounded lag as the ruled architecture.

Recovery and migration restore the complete lineage state, including source leases, pending events, serving identities, and gate transactions. Children receive independent cutoffs; one child never waits merely to equalize another child’s migration interruption. Report their actual active time, experience, and interruptions.

### 3.6 The patient gate and exposure transactions

The gate protects against operational collapse while allowing ordinary developmental dips. It does not certify intelligence. Report-panel results never operate it. [Attachment A, “Gate as patience not veto”; “Provisional commits confirmed at the next probe.”]

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|

| Collapse statistic | Candidate/reference median generated-token ratio on paired observations both leave unsolved |
| Collapse threshold | Ratio below **0.5 dimensionless** |
| Minimum support | **6 paired common-unsolved observations**, spanning at least **3 distinct problems** |
| Ordinary gate examination | **2 seeded repetitions per candidate** |

Reference outcomes may be reused only for the exact registered checkpoint, task, seed, budget, and serving configuration. Otherwise they require separately charged execution.

The authoritative transitions are:

| State or result | Activation and next transition | Counters, floors, and exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Invalid artifact, source, or causal prefix | Reject; never activate | No score-counter update; no exposure committed |
| Format failure or confirmed supported token collapse | Reject; never activate | Record collapse separately; no floor update |
| Missing collapse support | Hold; run the bounded supplemental examination | If still unresolved, withhold publication and resolve the protocol deficiency |
| Severe preliminary score deficit | Hold inactive; obtain fresh confirmation | No deficient count until resolved |
| Severe deficit confirmed | Reject inactive candidate | Increment score-deficient streak once |
| Ordinary deficit with passing brakes | Activate provisionally; obtain fresh confirmation | No floor update yet |
| Candidate within tolerance with passing brakes | Activate provisionally; obtain fresh confirmation | No floor update yet |
| Fresh confirmation remains ordinarily deficient | Keep provisionally active under patience | Increment streak once; retain previous confirmed floor |
| Fresh confirmation passes all floors within tolerance | Confirm candidate | Reset streak; candidate becomes eligible for the registered floor-update rule |
| Patience exhausted | Roll back at each lane’s next boundary | Restore a complete confirmed ancestor and its retained-exposure manifest |
| Confirmation reveals severe harm or collapse after provisional activation | Roll back at each lane’s next boundary | Count the resolved score deficit once where applicable; preserve all records |

A candidate is resolved once, regardless of how many repetitions examined it. No successor is trained on an unresolved provisional candidate. This restriction is part of the measured writer critical path; it must not be omitted from feasibility calculations.

Floors retain immutable original records. A re-probe cannot lower an inconvenient floor. The freeze must name one complete recovery checkpoint. If no checkpoint and no qualified recovery candidate can satisfy the registered multi-gym floors, stop the production write path and report a protocol-limited mechanism result. Do not improvise relaxed floors.

The live qualification must demonstrate that an ordinary deficient candidate actually activates, as well as severe holds, rollback, and recovery. Otherwise “patience” has not been implemented.

#### Exposure accounting through rollback

Maintain separate per-event states:

1. **Attempted:** included in optimization, whether or not the candidate publishes.
2. **Provisionally exposed:** included in an adapter that actually served generation.
3. **Confirmed exposed:** included in a confirmed adapter.
4. **Retained ancestry:** included in the currently retained adapter ancestry or current reset-from-base corpus.

These are event counts and target-token counts, not claims that individual facts remain behaviorally retrievable.

A provisional publication does not advance the confirmed cursor. Rollback restores the ancestor’s retained-exposure manifest. Events absent from that retained ancestry return to the deterministic restoration/first-exposure queue, including useful events previously exposed by a discarded descendant. Attempted and provisional histories remain immutable. New experience generated under discarded adapters remains eligible with its original provenance.

The injected-failure tests must show that no event is lost, counted twice as lived experience, or falsely described as retained after rollback.

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Production prohibition | Parent speech, briefs, parent-authored summaries, rewrites, and lessons cannot appear as context or targets |

Source authorship, rather than string similarity, distinguishes a child’s response from compiler-inserted parental material. Masking remains necessary for allowed environmental and harness context, but it is not an information firewall.

### 4.2 Preparation exception: interface format, not foreign thought

The default preparation is the birth brief, literal interface feedback, and child-authored experience. Interface remediation and receptivity improvement are distinct eligible diagnoses:

- **Interface remediation:** valid submissions are missing or malformed under §2’s registered denominators.
- **Receptivity improvement:** the interface works, but ordinary guidance does not produce delayed conditional use.

Own-words rehearsal may be tested for either diagnosis. Its selection requires the corresponding delayed behavioral test, not merely opening-text similarity. No preparation diagnostic becomes a reported child without a frozen birth protocol. [Attachment A, “The first child is prepared”; “Give it rules so it can make its own rules.”]

A separately approved external interface set may contain only these schemas:

```text
Input: Return the supplied opaque payload using the submission syntax.
Payload: <PAYLOAD>
Target: ACT: <PAYLOAD>
```

```text
Input: Repair the submission syntax without changing its supplied payload.
Malformed submission: ACT <PAYLOAD>
Target: ACT: <PAYLOAD>
```

Permitted payloads are opaque synthetic strings or literal syntax examples. They must not be task answers, executable compiler recipes, classroom solutions, predictions, plans, explanations, reflection, goal hierarchies, or self-evaluation scripts. No conversational continuation or rationale is allowed in a target.

The proposed exception budget is at most **128 examples**, **8,192 supervised tokenizer tokens**, and **1 pass through the approved examples**, whichever limit binds first. Record processed context-plus-target token-passes separately. These are **engineering caps**, not a target training dose.

Audit every example and every target token against the schema; retain the complete approved set and hashes. A provenance label alone does not pass this audit. Match any approved interface-only preparation in the untaught deployment twin. Anything beyond these schemas requires another explicit decision by Rohin and remains quarantined meanwhile.

### 4.3 Exact events, causal windows, and usable coverage

Store each generation event with:

- exact input and generated token IDs;
- attention visibility, position metadata, and chat-template version;
- role and origin spans;
- adapter hash and brief versions;
- task, lane, source permissions, and termination reason;
- links to environmental events that followed it.

The compiler may build:

**State-to-state windows:** the exact recorded input followed by that generation, with eligible child spans targeted.

**Multi-state windows:** an exact prefix followed by child generation, actual environmental outcomes, and later child generation. Every later targeted turn must have the same visible prefix and positions as its recorded inference input.

Close a window before a prefix mismatch, eviction, head replacement, problem change, or adapter change. Do not fabricate continuity to obtain long sequences. Already-generated multiple actions remain together before their outcomes, as specified in §3.2.

No future feedback enters an earlier prefix. No retrospective retrieval may be inserted. Separate branches remain separate unless an actual child-generated review saw and combined them.

Allowed environmental outcomes and fixed interface scaffolding are masked context. Parent-origin spans make the entire proposed production window ineligible under §4.1. Harness cutoffs are not targets. Child message-end tokens are handled only under the verified token contract in §3.2.

Before choosing a mixture, publish real-trace distributions for:

- eligible and ineligible events, with exclusion reasons;
- total sequence length and target length;
- number of causally connected states;
- coverage lost to parent context, template differences, eviction, and boundaries;
- target presentations per event under each view.

The prospective allocation is **70% of processed non-padding token-passes to multi-state windows** and **30% to state-to-state windows**. If eligible multi-state windows are insufficient, transfer unused allocation to state-to-state windows **without repeatedly oversampling a small long-window subset**. Freeze the measured mixture before the real-ledger comparison. A predominantly short-window result must be described as such; it cannot support a long-sequence mechanism claim.

Use separate padded sequences with length grouping. Cross-example causal packing is disabled. Scheduling neighboring events is permitted; exposing unrelated examples to one another is not. Any future packed implementation requires a perturbation test showing that another example cannot affect the target example’s logits or gradients beyond the measured numerical baseline.

### 4.4 Occurrences, training dose, and coverage claims

Retain every genuine eligible event, including failures, unresolved thought, private reflection, and repeated language. Distinct event IDs with identical text remain distinct lived occurrences. Duplicate ingestion of the same event is an error. There is no semantic deduplication, inverse-frequency penalty, or success filter. [Attachment A, “No cap on repeats”; “Conditional … in its situation.”]

**Ledger retention is not consolidation.** Every publication exports:

- eligible lived occurrences and target tokens;
- excluded occurrences by source or context reason;
- attempted, provisional, confirmed, and retained-ancestry exposure;
- pending first exposure;
- restored exposure after rollback;
- optimization replay;
- coverage for recurrent-text groups, counting distinct events rather than unique strings.

The prospective final coverage requirement for the phrase “consolidated repeated experience” is **100% first-exposure coverage of eligible events sealed at least 1 local round before the final intake cutoff**. Events newer than that cutoff remain separately reported. Missing coverage does not erase the run, but it removes that complete-consolidation claim. “All thoughts were consolidated” is unavailable whenever any ineligible or pending thought remains.

#### Loss and token accounting

Use next-token cross-entropy on eligible child targets only. Sum losses over an accumulation group and divide by its supervised-token count. Do not average sequence means equally. Empty-target groups fail validation.

A **processed token-pass** is one non-padding tokenizer token processed in a training sequence, including context and targets. A **target token-presentation** is one supervised appearance of a child token. Padding, replay, repeated contexts, optimizer updates, and elapsed GPU time are reported separately.

The accounting fixture is:

| View/source | Sequence count | Mean total sequence length, context plus targets | Mean target length | Processed token-passes | Target token-presentations |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Multi-state fresh | **70 sequences** | **5,000 tokens per sequence** | **2,000 target tokens per sequence** | **350,000 token-passes** | **140,000 presentations** |
| Multi-state replay | **70 sequences** | **5,000 tokens per sequence** | **2,000 target tokens per sequence** | **350,000 token-passes** | **140,000 presentations** |
| State-to-state fresh | **75 sequences** | **2,000 tokens per sequence** | **300 target tokens per sequence** | **150,000 token-passes** | **22,500 presentations** |
| State-to-state replay | **75 sequences** | **2,000 tokens per sequence** | **300 target tokens per sequence** | **150,000 token-passes** | **22,500 presentations** |
| Total | **290 sequences** | — | — | **1,000,000 token-passes** | **325,000 presentations** |

Thus the fixture contains **675,000 context token-passes**. It is an arithmetic fixture, not a measured corpus.

Use a microbatch of **1 sequence** and accumulate toward **4,096 supervised tokens per optimizer update**. Complete sequences are indivisible; the last microbatch may overshoot. Normalize by the actual accumulated target count, record the overshoot, and flush a final partial group using its actual count. This is a **prospective trainer setting**.

For every comparison, export processed tokens, supervised tokens, sequence count, and padding per optimizer update, including distribution summaries and the final partial update. Compute-matched and presentation-matched comparisons answer different questions; neither may silently substitute for the other.

#### Cap and runtime

The previous **5,000,000-token-pass** ceiling is incompatible with a **60-minute** training ceiling at the critic’s stated planning assumption of **1,200 processed token-passes per second per training GPU**. At that assumed rate:

- **5,000,000 token-passes** require **69.44 training minutes**;
- **4,320,000 token-passes** require **60 training minutes**, excluding overhead.

The **1,200-token-pass-per-second** rate is an unverified planning assumption, not an observed throughput result.

Set the provisional upper bound to **4,320,000 processed token-passes per write**, with the actual frozen cap lower wherever necessary to satisfy measured service time and §3.5’s lag limits. The **60-minute** ceiling applies to training; compile, save, examinations, confirmations, and serving publication are separately charged and included in complete service time.

A budget cap is not a required dose. Do not fill spare capacity with undeclared replay.

### 4.7 Conditional-memory diagnostics: authorship, estimators, and acceptance

This subsection is the sole car-test endpoint authority. The test in §2 should reference it rather than maintain a partial duplicate.

#### What is being trained

The controlled car test is a **synthetic supervised association diagnostic**, not lived-child consolidation. A fixture generator assigns each owner a colour and authors the association targets. All such targets, adapters, and contexts are quarantined.

Use **3 independently randomized banks**, each with **64 owners**, **4 possible colours**, and equal owner groups assigned **0 observations**, **1 observation**, **4 observations**, or **16 observations**. Each bank therefore contains **336 synthetic observation events**; the complete fixture contains **1,008 observation events**.

Compare actual antecedents, stripped antecedents, and shuffled cue–target assignments, each with **2 training seeds**. This produces **18 diagnostic adapters**. Match target-event multiplicity and register order, tokenizer lengths, and optimizer presentations.

Each adapter receives an initial association write and **2 incremental interference writes**, yielding **54 training runs**. Interference contains no car replay. Use exact corpus token counts and a proposed maximum of **100,000 processed token-passes per diagnostic write**. If a cell cannot preserve the registered dose within that limit, revise and reprice the fixture before results; do not silently truncate it.

The nominal training upper bound is therefore **5,400,000 token-passes**, or **1.25 GPU-hours** at the unverified **1,200-token-pass-per-second** assumption. That excludes inference, rendering, loading, and all operational overhead; it does not establish that the existing pretest reservation is sufficient.

#### Queries and estimators

For each owner, register **3 unseen paraphrases** of the owner-specific colour question. At every checkpoint also run a fixed manifest of wrong-owner, generic-cue, unrelated-property, unexposed-owner, and repaint queries. Short-query throughput must be measured separately from full gym problems.

For answer option \(c\), let \(p(c\mid q)\) be the probability of its exact registered answer string followed by the registered answer delimiter. Define:

- **Raw candidate mass:** \(M(q)=\sum_c p(c\mid q)\).
- **Candidate-normalized probability:** \(\tilde p(c\mid q)=p(c\mid q)/M(q)\).
- **Assigned-answer gain:** ON-minus-OFF change in \(\tilde p\) for the assigned colour.
- **Trigger gain:** ON-minus-OFF change in assigned-colour log odds on correct-owner queries, measured in **nats**.
- **Cue interaction:** trigger gain minus the same assigned-colour log-odds gain under its preregistered wrong-owner cue, measured in **nats**.
- **Spillover:** mean absolute ON-minus-OFF normalized-probability change on registered unrelated controls.
- **Retention:** post-interference assigned-answer gain divided by initial assigned-answer gain, both relative to the same pretraining reference.

Retention is nonestimable when the initial gain fails the required acquisition threshold. Raw mass equal to **0 probability units** is a numerical failure, not a successful normalized answer. Report raw log probabilities and numerical handling.

Average paraphrases within owner, owners within dose and bank, and banks equally. Show each training seed separately. Bootstrap owners within bank for **10,000 resamples** and a **95% conditional interval**; this interval is conditional on the diagnostic banks and trained seeds, not a population interval over agents.

| Endpoint | Required result, in each training seed |
|---|---|
| High-dose acquisition | At **16 synthetic observations per owner**, mean assigned probability at least **0.80 probability units** and gain at least **0.30 probability units** |
| Cue specificity | Mean trigger gain at least **1.5 nats**, and cue-interaction interval above **0 nats** |
| Dose response | Positive registered dose trend, allowing saturation; show every dose |
| Unrelated spillover | Mean absolute shift at most **0.03 probability units** |
| Repaint override | Correct generated answer on at least **90% of repaint queries**, with degradation from the frozen reference at most **3 percentage points** |
| Interference retention | At least **75% of the initial high-dose gain** after **2 interference writes** |
| Generated retrieval | Exact-answer accuracy must corroborate acquisition and dose response; candidate normalization alone cannot pass |

The conditional-action companion uses a separate target-blind, child-generated ledger and held-out trigger/no-trigger situations. Its event chain must show actual observation, child-authored response, environmental outcome, and eligible consolidation. Its prospective requirements are at least **80% action accuracy**, at least **20 percentage points of ON-minus-OFF gain**, and at most **10 percentage points of delayed loss** after interference. Register its item count, source split, training seeds, and complete cost before execution. Without that manifest, the companion is unqualified—not implicitly included in the car budget.

The synthetic car test can establish controlled association storage, specificity, and revision. Only the child-generated companion and real-ledger test can support consolidation of the child’s own experience.

### 5.4 Room operation and asynchronous dialogue

The proposer receives an immutable, allowlisted snapshot containing public classroom events, legitimate outcomes, previous approved brief, active lesson identifier, scaffold level, public frontier evidence, and outstanding public questions. The verifier receives the proposal and the permitted evidence independently of the proposer’s self-rating.

The verifier checks:

- task answers and disguised solution hints;
- unsupported claims about the child;
- coaching to hidden metrics;
- private-reflection judgments or derived recommendations;
- source permissions and stale evidence;
- consistency with the active conditional lesson.

A repair receives a new verification. Shared-model agreement is not independent proof of correctness.

Use a **300-second timeout per API call**, below the **6-minute gateway limit**. A room invocation permits an initial proposal and verification, plus at most **1 repair-and-reverification pair**: at most **4 sequential calls**. The corresponding timeout allowance is **1,200 seconds per invocation**, excluding queue and orchestration time. This is not a delivery deadline and never enters the child’s critical path. Record call latency, room latency, queue delay, advice age, failures, and retry count separately.

Only complete, verified messages are published. Outages retain the previous approved brief or the approved birth brief. A central reviewer, unavailable verifier, or unfinished repair never blocks generation or sleep. [Attachment A, “Forever loop”; “No holdups.”]

**The direct channel is a working message protocol, not a pause for conversation.**

- Each message records lineage, room, message identifier, reply-to identifier, source problem, source adapter, creation time, and expiry condition.
- The child may address the parent in ordinary prose. No special marker is required to make a valid learning episode, and such prose is not an executable action.
- The harness queues the addressed text at the next safe request boundary. The child continues working immediately.
- Replies are picked up before a later generation request, alongside the current brief. They identify the question they answer.
- A question remains open until answered, withdrawn, made irrelevant by an outcome, or aged beyond **32 further completed problems in its lane**.
- A stale answer is withheld unless reverified as a retrospective response explicitly identifying the earlier situation. It cannot masquerade as current advice.
- Each dialogue allows at most **3 exchanges**, with at most **2 questions in a delivered parent message**. Unfinished dialogue expires rather than blocking a new problem.

The live soak must include a genuine multi-turn exchange while the child continues acting, delayed replies, duplicate delivery, process restart, expired credentials, simultaneous rooms, and stale snapshots. The small invocation fixture in §2 establishes fixture coverage only; it does not qualify sustained agentic parenting.

### 5.5 Repetition, scaffolding, and preparation

Each ordinary brief contains at most **10 lines**, including the rehearsal ending, and at most **2 questions**. It states a public observation, a conditional lesson, its exception, and a process experiment without supplying the task-solving move. The birth brief has no invented evidence about the child.

Every brief ends:

> At the start of each new situation while this guidance is active, restate it in your own words and decide whether it applies.

The active lesson identifier and activation interval are recorded. The latest approved brief is shown at every training wake. Revisions sharpen the same core lesson rather than switching because the child can paraphrase it. A repeated brief is permitted and is the safe outage fallback. [Attachment A, “Repetition is the parenting mechanism.”]

Scaffold specificity may decrease from a target-blind example, to a missing-link prompt, to a situational cue, to a non-leading question. Move at most **1 scaffold level per decision**, after the qualifying public evidence in §5.2. Maintain the rehearsal request throughout active guidance.

**Sustained silence is not silently authorized.** Brief-absent diagnostic forks test dependence, but they do not constitute production fade-out. Attachment A calls for both every-wake repetition and fading scaffolding; the supplied record does not specify when complete withdrawal supersedes continued delivery. Section 10 must record the decision. **Default:** fade specificity in production, retain active guidance at every wake, and label sustained hand-over untested. Do not call diagnostic parent absence a completed developmental fade.

Preparation has **2 distinct eligible diagnoses**:

- **Interface deficiency:** the child cannot reliably submit valid actions under §2’s common interface scorer.
- **Receptivity deficiency:** the interface works, but guidance does not produce delayed conditional use.

Brief-only preparation is the default. Intensive own-words rehearsal may be tested for either diagnosis in disposable pretests, with delayed tests after actual adapter adoption. Repeated language is not a veto; conditional behavior decides. Section 2 is the sole authority for interface denominators and numerical release floors. There is no additional unstated “stricter” bar in this section.

**Interface-only instruction exception.** If interface remediation requires external targets, the admissible schema is narrowly bounded:

| Field | Allowed content |
|---|---|
| Input | A neutral instruction to submit a supplied arbitrary payload using the literal action wrapper. |
| Target | `ACT: <the supplied payload>` and, if required by the pinned tokenizer, a request-ending token that does not terminate the organism. |
| Permitted variation | Whitespace and parser-supported formatting, using nonsemantic placeholder payloads. |
| Prohibited content | Task answers, valid task-solving expressions, predictions, plans, explanations, reflections, goals, emotional reactions, memory claims, and rehearsal scripts. |

For example, a target such as `ACT: PLACEHOLDER_Q` teaches serialization only; it is not executed as a scored task answer.

The provisional maximum is **64 examples** and **8,192 supervised target token-presentations**, whichever binds first. Context processing, optimizer settings, and actual dose are recorded separately. Audit every example and every tokenized target before training. Identical approved interface preparation is applied to the untaught deployment twin. Any expansion of content or budget requires a §10 decision before use. A provenance label alone does not authorize foreign thought.

### 5.6 Private reflection and source permissions

Private reflection is **readable but ungraded**. Local and central parents may read it, but may not comment on, assess, score, praise, correct, paraphrase, or evaluate it. It cannot drive frontier estimates, scaffold changes, progression, task selection, or corrective recommendations. Public citations attached afterward do not legitimize a privately derived judgment. [Attachment A, “The child’s private time … without needing judgment.”]

Implement this through separate observer and decision contexts:

- A reflection-reader process may inspect private material but has no teaching, curriculum, frontier, or shared-playbook write capability.
- Decision-making parent processes receive public evidence only.
- Central processes follow the same separation.
- Private text remains eligible child experience only under §4’s authorship and context rules; its content is not treated as verified environmental fact.

Qualification includes a counterfactual fixture: change only private reflection while holding public inputs and random seeds fixed. The decision process must receive identical inputs and produce identical curriculum and scaffold decisions. Test indirect recommendations, not just quotation detection.

Permissions are bound to **phase and source provenance**, not filenames:

| Source | Parenting-phase permission |
|---|---|
| Public classroom attempts, outcomes, and child self-evaluation | Allowed. |
| Approved briefs and within-lineage public developmental history | Allowed. |
| Private reflection | Observer-only contract above. |
| Classroom operational gate | Allowlisted disposition and coarse reason; no raw items, answers, margins, or hidden score series. |
| Teaching-facing development checks | Registered aggregate summaries only; explicitly teaching-facing rather than sealed evidence. |
| Compiler engineering records, deployment gates, deployment trajectories | Denied. |
| Sealed reports, identifiers, scores, and derived summaries | Denied. |
| Another independent child’s adaptive teaching discoveries | Denied during the independent replication. |

Before compiler entry, revoke all parenting capabilities for that deployment namespace, including generic gate tools. Test denials for deployment gate decisions, report identifiers, summaries, and proxy fields.

Fable/Codex sessions already discussing compiler outcomes cannot supply adaptive live advice. Use fresh parent processes with immutable allowlisted input bundles and complete input logs. There is no manual “sanitization” route by which a report-informed researcher can import an adaptive recommendation. General teaching sources enter through a clean source-ingestion process and clean verification. Critical infrastructure fixes are separately logged; they are not teaching advice.

### 5.7 What may reach sleep

**Production default: no parent-origin text anywhere in a training sequence—not as a target and not as masked context.** Attachment A’s explicit prohibition on putting the parent’s lesson into the sleep corpus has not been superseded by an explicit authorization of masked parental text. Masking does not remove that information from optimization.

The release record is:

| Item | Authorized state |
|---|---|
| Parent-conditioned sequence containing parental speech | Prohibited for production. |
| Masked-parent exact-context candidate | Quarantined; requires explicit authorization from Rohin in §10. |
| Parent-absent recorded window | Eligible only if its exact original prefix contains no parent-origin text and all other §4 invariants pass. |
| Retrospective deletion of a visible brief | Prohibited; it fabricates the conditioning situation. |
| Parent lesson restated by the child | Child-authored content, but its training window must still satisfy the parent-free-prefix requirement. |
| Interface-only external target | Eligible only under the audited schema and separate budget in §5.5. |

Every-wake guidance may leave too few genuinely parent-absent windows. That is a feasibility collision, not permission to erase prefixes. Before launch, measure eligible target coverage and actual sequence lengths on real traces. If the permitted path cannot qualify, **default to a mechanism/preparation study and withhold the learned-lineage launch** pending Rohin’s decision. If eligibility changes, rerun rendering, mask, coverage, behavioral-writer, retention, and integrated publication tests.

Ledger retention is not complete consolidation. Parent reports must distinguish attempted optimization, provisional exposure, confirmed exposure, and currently retained adapter ancestry, using §4’s transaction records. A rolled-back write cannot be described as retained learning. The final report includes first-exposure coverage, pending repeated occurrences, and backlog; it does not say “all memories consolidated” when that is false.

### 5.8 Behavioral instruments and decision checks

The minimal retained instruments are:

| Trait | Behavioral observation |
|---|---|
| Persistence | After a non-improving outcome, a defensible changed attempt on an unresolved task; unchanged repetition reported separately. |
| Goal completion | Recognition of an environment-verified completed goal and transition to another goal or reflection, rather than repeated submissions. |
| Goal layers | Public linkage between a current action, a problem-level objective, and an across-problem learning aim; score only observable coherence, not presumed inner state. |
| Planning versus free thought | Descriptive coding of prospective action commitments versus exploration without a current commitment; neither category is automatically better. |
| Plan revision | A stated expectation is contradicted by feedback and a subsequent choice changes appropriately. |
| Cross-state return | An actual reference to earlier reasoning followed by its use or revision under later evidence. |
| Conditional action | Different behavior in matched trigger/no-trigger cases, including retaining a justified approach when change would be wasteful. |
| Unaided retrieval | Appropriate use in a clean parent-absent fork without inherited brief or summary. |

These instruments are required for their corresponding claims. If they cannot be implemented and checked this sprint, narrow the behavioral claim to the instruments actually available; do not infer metacognition from marker frequency.

Literal `DONE` must never terminate the organism. Rehearsal detection must accept ordinary opening prose without `NOTE:`. The acceptance artifact records tokenizer end-token identifiers and demonstrates the distinction between closing a generation request and ending a life. Runtime tests belong in §2.

The early behavior-change check compares first executable-action distributions before and after the first adopted write, stratified by task family. Freeze action categories and a baseline-repeat null distribution before candidate results. Report Jensen–Shannon divergence in **nats** and the task-conditioned changes. A shift beyond the registered null range establishes changed behavior, not improved behavior; no detected shift limits the mechanism claim. It is not an extra score veto on exploratory writes. [Attachment A, “Efficiency over cycles … behaviour-change gate.”]

The repetition hypothesis requires supported rehearsal within **1–2 sleeps**, then reduced ritual over at least **4 subsequent windows** relative to a matched classroom control. The final untaught compiler twin is not that control. Without the disposable matched classroom comparison, report emergence, delay, and conditional use, but not a causal classroom anti-ritual effect.

Section 10 must retain the unresolved production fade, parent-context eligibility, minimum-dose feasibility, and any missing claim-critical instrument as explicit decisions with the defaults above.

## 6. The test protocol and statistics

### 6.1 Questions, claims, and prospective lock

The final experiment has **2 score co-primary endpoints**:

1. parent-absent frozen transfer;
2. parent-absent **entry-adjusted continual score advantage**.

Neither substitutes for the other. The second measures sustained relative improvement from entry; it is not, by itself, a learning-rate estimator.

The practical efficiency headline required by Attachment A is retained as a separately registered endpoint: tokens and actions to a fixed success target across consolidation opportunities, with a cycle-level action-change check. A score-only success cannot authorize a parenting-efficiency sentence. [Attachment A, “Two final-test conditions”; “Efficiency over cycles”; “The one-lineage claim.”]

The target is a case study of the observed developmental system, preferably independently repeated. It does not isolate parenting from extra childhood experience and optimization. CompilerGym is **unseen by the developed child and its live parents before deployment**. Disclose compiler-informed harness engineering and benchmark selection separately.

Before childhood outcomes are inspected, lock:

- panel and source-clustering manifests;
- reference policies and success targets;
- deployment initialization and writer state;
- resource and update policies;
- estimators, practical margins, aggregation rules, and exclusions;
- code revisions and analysis commands.

For the score endpoints, the practical margin is fixed at **+0.020 reduction-score units**. Baseline-only calibration checks measurement feasibility; it does **not** select a more favorable margin. If the panel cannot resolve that margin, revise the panel before locking or declare the score test exploratory. Changing the margin requires a prospective §10 decision and a new registration, never an outcome-informed adjustment.

### 6.2 Matched twins and deployment initialization

Each taught child receives a fresh untaught twin using the same frozen base, tokenizer, neutral interface, decoding settings, context policy, action allowance, scoring, operational gate policy, and capacity reservation. Approved interface-only preparation is identical in both.

The match is **opportunity and policy**, not equal realized optimization exposure.

At deployment entry:

| State | Taught agent | Untaught twin |
|---|---|---|
| Adapter | Final operationally selected childhood checkpoint | Identity adapter on the same base |
| LoRA rank | Frozen final dimension count | Same dimension count |
| Writer phase | Qualified incremental continuation | Same incremental continuation |
| Deployment write counter | Reset to the deployment origin | Same reset |
| Optimizer | Fresh optimizer initialized under the common configuration | Same initialization rule |
| Deployment ledger and text memory | Empty | Empty |
| Childhood replay | Immutable eligible snapshot | Empty ancestry |
| Deployment gate floors | Established by the same operational entry procedure; no classroom floors carried over | Same procedure |
| Deployment cursors | Empty deployment cursors; immutable ancestry records retained separately | Empty deployment cursors |

**Neither deployment writer resets from the base.** Doing so could erase the taught checkpoint. The entry adapters differ by design; writer phase, optimizer policy, rank, deployment counters, and gate procedure do not.

Incremental continuation from both the taught checkpoint and the identity adapter must pass qualification before this continual comparison runs. If it does not, §10’s default is frozen transfer plus a separately labeled qualified reset-from-base experiment, if feasible—not a claim that the requested lifetime schedule was tested.

Neither waking agent receives a parent, childhood summary, inherited retrieval index, pending parental message, or childhood carry-over. Deployment orders and evaluation seeds are paired. Rank growth is disabled during deployment unless a single common, prequalified schedule was registered before childhood; no post-entry capacity improvisation is allowed.

### 6.3 Replay, exposure, and update opportunities

Preserve an immutable childhood bundle containing adapter ancestry, allowed child-origin replay, exact source records, preparation provenance, renderer settings, sampling policy, and committed transactions. Parent-origin text remains excluded under §5.7.

The frozen evaluator cannot read this bundle. The continual writer may replay its eligible rows within the ordinary cap; the waking continual agent cannot retrieve them.

Both agents use the same fresh/replay allocation and empty-pool fallback. An empty ancestry pool may lead to less realized training. Report that rather than calling the optimization dose matched. Record, by source:

- processed context-plus-target token-passes;
- supervised target token-presentations;
- distinct events first exposed;
- repeated lived occurrences exposed;
- optimizer updates;
- provisional and confirmed exposures;
- ancestry currently retained after rollback;
- pending first exposure.

Deployment offers a write trigger after every **32 completed deployment problems**, giving **16 trigger opportunities over 512 problems**. The trigger at the terminal horizon cannot improve actions already taken and receives no credit in the primary trajectory. A busy writer coalesces pending slices under the frozen §4 policy; it does not create a barrier or guarantee an adopted write at every opportunity.

Before launch, the manifest must freeze finite limits on publication lag, first-exposure backlog, and pending age, expressed in **completed problems**, **processed token-passes**, and **elapsed seconds**. The live soak must demonstrate nonblocking admission reduction and recovery when a limit is crossed. Reporting an unbounded backlog is not sufficient. If those limits cannot sustain the one-round-behind design, §10 must record the measured conflict; the default is reduced admitted experience or a narrower mechanism study, not an undisclosed longer-lag organism.

A publication record must distinguish training attempt, provisional activation, confirmation, rejection, and rollback. Exposure cursors follow the retained adapter ancestry. Events whose only exposure was rolled back return to the eligible queue under the registered recovery rule; they are not falsely counted as retained learning. Section 3 remains the sole gate-state authority, subject to completing these transaction semantics before release.

### 6.4 Panels, headroom, and the seal

Maintain distinct manifests for classroom training, teaching-facing development, operational gates, deployment experience, and sealed reports.

Previously inspected compiler panels are operational material, not untouched evidence. Their sizes and historical results were reported through unavailable memos and are not authenticated here.

For a confirmatory report panel:

- Hold out by source family, not identifier alone.
- Separate report sources from deployment experience and operational gates.
- Register transformations and source clustering before candidate outcomes.
- Require at least **8 qualifying source groups**.
- Select using candidate-blind reference evidence only.

The reference contract is:

- Fixed recipes: the environment’s supported `-Oz` reference, a no-change sequence, and every additional fixed sequence explicitly enumerated in the sealed manifest.
- Reference search: the clean base in the common harness with **4 independent runs per program**, each using the ordinary per-problem allowance; the environment chooses the best achieved score.
- Selection statistic: equal-source-weighted reference-search score minus the best manifest-listed single fixed recipe’s score.
- Required headroom: at least **0.10 reduction-score units**.

The exact pass sequences, compiler versions, program identities, search seeds, denominators, and exclusions must be registered. No unnamed “best recipe” may be added after inspecting taught outcomes. This selection procedure defines a search-headroom-enriched benchmark, not a representative sample of all compiler programs.

The curator may characterize candidates without access to taught outcomes. After sealing, evaluation workers write only to a restricted result store. Live agents, writers, gates, parents, central reviewers, and adaptive researchers cannot read report items, identifiers, scores, or derived summaries.

The inferential target is conditional on this sealed selection procedure and the observed developmental histories. It is not arbitrary compiler programs or future children.

If a fresh qualifying panel is unavailable, the default is a matched **exploratory operational-panel comparison**. State that limitation in every headline result. A prohibited read breaks the seal; repeating the same items does not restore confirmatory status.

### 6.5 Nonblocking evaluation and resource acceptance

Capture immutable checkpoints at entry and every **64 completed deployment problems** through **512 problems**: **9 checkpoint ages**. Run **2 paired evaluation repetitions per checkpoint**.

**Age means capture age.** Evaluation may finish later. Snapshot capture does not wait for a write, parent, other clone, or report worker. It records the adapter actually active at that age, even if several captures contain identical weights.

Reports execute asynchronously on a separate qualified reservation or low-priority queue that cannot deliberately stop the live agent. No checkpoint age is an evaluation barrier. Inference serving must use version-pinned concurrent operation or rolling replacement; a reload that waits for all clones is not qualified. Measured infrastructure downtime is disclosed separately from prohibited synchronization.

Frozen evaluations start each report problem with fresh context and use no memory accumulation or sleep. Their entry results may serve both frozen transfer and continual entry only if checkpoint, prompts, budgets, seeds, and context isolation are identical.

The final release calculation must price:

- all deployment generation;
- all report programs and repetitions;
- ordinary gates, contemporaneous references where required, confirmations, and bounded supplemental batches;
- training, compilation, save/load, and publication;
- storage, relay, and recovery;
- activity-level resource contention.

For **4 deployment agents**, the report schedule contains **72 checkpoint-repetition evaluations**. If the selected panel contained **12 programs**, that would mean **864 report problems**; the actual registered panel size governs the budget.

The feasibility critic’s assumed pace of **22 problems per inference-GPU-hour** and training speed of **1,200 token-passes per training-GPU-second** are planning assumptions, not verified measurements. Under the latter assumption, **5,000,000 token-passes** require approximately **69.44 minutes of training**, before overhead, and therefore cannot fit a **60-minute** ceiling. The freeze must resolve this through a measured smaller qualified dose or a revised service schedule.

The **48-hour continual reservation** in §7 is not acceptance evidence. Require a resource-loaded execution trace and lane schedule from rehearsal. Aggregate spare GPU-hours do not eliminate a serial writer bottleneck. No rental is assumed until booked and qualified.

Section 10’s default is to remove the additional complete taught–untaught pair if needed, then preserve frozen transfer and report continual evaluation as incomplete if a full remaining pair still cannot finish. Never pause the faster twin to equalize update ages or cut only its exposure.

### 6.6 Score estimators and success aggregation

Compiler score is fractional instruction-count reduction,

\[
Y=(I_{\mathrm{reference}}-I_{\mathrm{achieved}})/I_{\mathrm{reference}},
\]

in **reduction-score units**, where instruction counts are measured in **instructions**. Freeze the exact reference, compiler configuration, invalid-action handling, and terminal scoring. Average programs within source groups, then weight source groups equally. Classroom verifier scores are not pooled with compiler scores.

For lineage pair \(i\), frozen transfer is

\[
F_i=\operatorname{mean}_{s,r}
\left(Y^{\mathrm{frozen}}_{D,i,s,r}
-Y^{\mathrm{frozen}}_{U,i,s,r}\right),
\]

where \(s\) indexes source groups and \(r\) indexes paired repetitions.

Let \(Q_X(t)\) be isolated report score at capture age \(t\), measured in **deployment problems**. Define

\[
C_i=\frac{1}{H}\int_0^H
\left(
[Q_{D,i}(t)-Q_{D,i}(0)]
-
[Q_{U,i}(t)-Q_{U,i}(0)]
\right)\,dt,
\]

with \(H=512\) **deployment problems**, evaluated by trapezoidal integration. \(C_i\) is the **entry-adjusted continual score advantage**, in reduction-score units.

Report absolute entry and terminal scores, the full curves, each agent’s own change, elapsed-time curves, adopted-update curves, and publication lag. A positive \(C_i\) can arise from retention, an early jump, or twin deterioration; it does not establish a uniformly larger derivative.

**Pair-level pass.**

- Frozen passes when \(F_i\) is at least **+0.020 reduction-score units** and its conditional interval excludes **0 reduction-score units**.
- Continual passes when \(C_i\) meets the same margin and interval rule, and terminal taught score is not below terminal twin score.
- A joint qualifying case requires both passes in the same complete pair.

**Overall labels.**

- **Existence case:** at least **1 independently developed, complete pair** jointly qualifies. Report every completed pair, including failures.
- **Independent replication:** at least **2 independent complete pairs** jointly qualify separately.
- **Descriptive pooled summary:** an equal-pair mean, always accompanied by pair-level results. It cannot rescue a failed replication or substitute for either endpoint.

The intended scope remains proof of existence, not population efficacy. Adaptive cross-child teaching invalidates the independence label even if both pairs pass.

### 6.7 Efficiency headline and behavior change

The registered practical efficiency endpoint is measured on CompilerGym at the terminal capture age after **512 deployment problems**, corresponding to **16 scheduled consolidation opportunities**, not necessarily **16 adopted writes**.

For each report program, freeze a success target before childhood outcomes:

\[
\tau_p=(R_p+S_p)/2,
\]

where \(R_p\) is the best score among the registered fixed recipes and \(S_p\) is the registered reference-search score, both in **reduction-score units**. Programs without positive reference headroom remain in score reporting but are excluded from the efficiency subset under the prospective manifest.

Measure:

- generated child tokenizer tokens to first reaching \(\tau_p\);
- executed environment actions to first reaching \(\tau_p\);
- success fraction;
- unsuccessful trials censored at their full registered allowance.

The token endpoint is capped tokens-to-target: failures receive the full token allowance rather than a cheap early-failure count. Under the current §3 defaults, that ceiling is **6,400 generated tokenizer tokens per problem**. Report raw cost and censoring alongside the capped statistic. Environment events determine success and action counts; narration does not.

The practical efficiency margin is a reduction of at least **10% in source-weighted capped tokens-to-target** relative to the twin, with a conditional interval excluding **0% reduction**, and no lower success fraction. This is a prospective engineering margin, not one calibrated from unavailable historical results.

A **parenting-efficiency headline** additionally requires positive improvement in the taught agent’s own efficiency from entry to the terminal capture, with no decline in its success fraction. State task family, comparator, terminal age, margin, and completed independent lineage count. Actions-to-target and intermediate cycle curves are mandatory supporting results.

“Faster learning” is reserved for this registered experience-indexed efficiency result together with positive taught-agent improvement—not score AUC alone. Since optimizer dose can differ, report a sensitivity view indexed by realized supervised presentations and processed token-passes. Treat it as descriptive; those doses are outcomes of the system.

The early action-distribution check in §5.8 remains claim-critical. Reflective language cannot substitute for changed actions. Failure to detect a relevant change after writing limits the proposed mechanism explanation even if later score results are positive.

### 6.8 Uncertainty and evidence provenance

The independent developmental unit is the lineage. Tasks, clones, sleeps, checkpoints, and evaluation repetitions do not increase that count.

Use current, baseline-only operational calibration. Historical standard deviations and text-memory fractions in the earlier draft came through unavailable memos; they are not release-calibration data.

For each completed pair, resample source groups jointly across agents, repetitions, and checkpoint ages using **10,000 bootstrap resamples** and **95% conditional intervals**. Preserve each source’s complete trajectory because the entry adjustment is shared across later checkpoints. Source-clustering rules are locked before outcomes; known related sources form a single cluster.

These intervals describe variation across the registered report-source clusters, conditional on panel selection and observed histories. They do not cover new children, arbitrary source populations, or alternate deployment trajectories. With few clusters, present all source-level effects and treat bootstrap precision cautiously. If independence of clusters cannot be defended, label intervals descriptive.

With **2 independent pairs**, concordant positive signs alone have a one-sided sign-test probability of **0.25 probability units** under an exchangeable zero-effect null. They are not a powered population trial.

Every historical result used in publication requires an evidence-register entry containing artifact path, revision or hash, extraction command, metric definition, sample denominator, and any superseded result. Until recovered, label it “reported through an unavailable memo; not independently verified.” The final abstract should use newly audited results, not unresolved historical fractions.

### 6.9 Text-memory comparison and claim limits

The mandatory crossed memory pretest in §2 must name a reproducible policy rather than merely call it “capable.” The default policy is:

- immutable child-authored memory from the same qualified snapshot;
- deterministic BM25 indexing over event-linked chunks of at most **256 tokenizer tokens**;
- queries from the current task description and permitted current deployment context;
- at most **8 retrieved chunks**, totaling at most **2,048 tokenizer tokens**, at the start of each problem;
- deterministic tie-breaking by event identifier;
- no parent-origin brief, foreign summary, or sealed item;
- retrieved text charged to the common context limit, with indexing, storage, retrieval latency, and processed tokens reported.

The index configuration, tokenizer, chunking, corpus size, and duplicate-occurrence handling are frozen before evaluation. Qualification includes known relevant-memory retrieval, distractors, wrong-owner cases, and current-evidence override. This is a specified lexical-retrieval comparator, not evidence that all strong text-memory policies have been covered.

Cross adapter-OFF/ON with memory absent/present using the same snapshot, problems, seeds, and execution allowance. Routine-only and brief-only conditions are separately labeled diagnostics, with their task and text authorship stated. Section 2 must price the complete cell manifest; reuse is explicit.

If a stronger retrieval or summarization policy is needed for an “equally capable text-memory” claim, its additional implementation and budget are a §10 decision. **Default:** restrict the claim to the policy actually qualified and run.

A classroom memory pretest cannot authorize a compiler weight-superiority sentence. No mandatory final compiler text-memory arm is currently fully budgeted. Therefore the default final claim is silent on compiler weights-versus-text superiority. Such a claim requires the corresponding compiler arm, matched budgets, and sealed evaluation before results are opened.

### 6.10 Deviations, incomplete results, and allowed statements

Operational gate rules belong solely in §3. Reports never select checkpoints, operate patience, set floors, trigger rollback, change curriculum, or stop a trajectory. The frozen checkpoint is the final childhood checkpoint selected by the operational protocol—not the best sealed-report checkpoint.

The following invalidate the affected clean comparison:

- forbidden report access or report-derived adaptive advice;
- unauthorized foreign-origin training content;
- fabricated causal prefixes;
- missing generation-to-adapter provenance;
- unregistered changes to initial text, writer phase, budgets, rank, or replay;
- missing environment records that prevent valid scoring.

Quarantine the affected artifacts and state which claim is lost. A valid negative result is different from a failed protocol.

If either continual agent fails to complete **512 deployment problems**, report the common completed prefix as incomplete and retain the intended horizon. A post-horizon write is not credited to completed behavior. Missing minimum childhood dose yields a preparation/mechanism case, not a completed developmental case.

| Outcome | Allowed headline form |
|---|---|
| Joint qualifying case | “In a case study of [completed independent lineage count] childhoods, [qualifying count] complete taught–untaught pairs met both the frozen-transfer and entry-adjusted continual-score criteria.” |
| Independent replication | “The joint result independently replicated in [qualifying count] completed childhoods; effects and conditional source intervals are reported separately.” |

Every headline names the completed independent lineage count, case-study scope, comparator, task family, and panel status. Do not infer metacognition from markers, persistence from compulsory continuation, safety from gate passage, or a generally better learning algorithm from these cases.

Release paired source scores, individual repetitions, checkpoint hashes, event-level provenance, parent access logs, exposure accounting, activity-level resource costs, missingness, failed candidates, and analysis code together. The defensible result is the complete evidence chain—including failures—not the most favorable curve.

This is a scaling gate, not a score-based refusal of an otherwise valid write. Random churn does not pass: changed actions must track changed conditions or evidence.

### 10.2 Ranked risks and default responses

**Highest — unauthorized conditioning or contamination.** Parent text, fabricated prefixes, foreign reasoning targets, or protected-source information can invalidate the intended experiment. Fail closed. Provenance and exact token records outrank vocabulary scans and verbal assurances.

**Next — serial service cannot meet the deadline or one-round design.** Long-context training, fresh confirmation, report probes, and migration are not free. Require the measured lane schedule. Smaller writes or shared evaluation are options only after qualification; nominal spare GPUs do not prove feasibility.

**Next — build staffing is overstated.** Agent labels are not additional engineers. Require human-attention accounting and bounded autonomous ownership. If integration cannot finish, ship completed qualified diagnostics rather than launch an untested organism.

**Next — storage mistaken for useful conditional learning.** A strong association test, repeated prose, or a better fixed recipe does not establish evidence-responsive behavior. Require trigger/no-trigger, rare-exception, override, and interference tests, plus the matched context contrast for any antecedent-preservation claim.

**Next — provisional experience is falsely counted as retained.** Rollback can remove the ancestry that first exposed an event. Restore transactional state and report current-ancestry coverage, not lifetime optimizer contact.

**Next — the final comparison changes mechanisms at entry.** An unrecorded writer-phase reset, unequal text, or hidden replay allocation can manufacture an apparent advantage or erase the taught child. Freeze complete deployment states and report realized dose.

**Next — covert parental evaluation or cross-lineage coupling.** Fresh processes and phase-bound capabilities are required. Private reflection cannot be evaluated indirectly through progression. Independent replication requires independent adaptive histories.

**Next — narrow panels and noisy floors overstate the result.** Conditional intervals and patient gates do not establish population effects or safe learning. Publish individual source and lineage results, incompatible floors, and all failures.

**Lower operational priority — API interruption.** Advice may become stale, but verified fallback keeps the child working. Unavailable verification never authorizes an unchecked brief. Per-call latency, whole-room latency, and advice age are separate measurements.

### 10.3 One ordered cut list

Cuts remove scope or claims, not binding safety and architecture requirements.

| Order | Cut | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| First | Optional rank growth, SVD/distillation, packed-attention optimization, broad optimizer grids, and extra preparation variants | No claim about those mechanisms; core experiment unchanged. |
| Next | Additional classroom families beyond the qualified persistence-first curriculum, extra diagnostic seeds beyond the registered release minimum, and unneeded exploratory analyses | Narrower task and diagnostic scope. Do not remove the required survey, integrated audit, or acceptance cells. |
| Next | Extra clone width above sustainable service | Less breadth; all remaining clones retain the no-wait architecture and bounded backlog. |
| Next | Childhood time above the registered minimum dose and protected allowance | Shorter childhood; minimum-dose claims remain only if actually completed. |
| Next | Optional final text-memory or no-childhood-replay forks | No compiler weights-superiority or replay-ablation claim. The mandatory classroom text-memory comparison remains. |
| Next | The additional complete taught–untaught pair | One existence case remains possible; independent developmental replication is unavailable. Recalculate the serial schedule. |
| Next | Continual scope that cannot finish without violating the mechanism or deadline | Preserve frozen transfer and qualified mechanism evidence. The intended **512-problem** continual endpoint remains explicitly incomplete; do not rename a shorter prefix as success. |
| Last | Developmental launch itself, if release-critical checks do not pass | Ship the qualified preparation/write/system evidence and failure analysis. Do not launch to preserve the advertised experiment count. |

Release-critical work includes source eligibility, bounded interface preparation, causal logging, masks, environment-owned scoring, transaction recovery, nonblocking operation, actual adapter identity, phase-bound parent access, curriculum qualification, integrated audit, and the matched baseline.

Claim-critical work includes the corresponding endpoint, minimum developmental dose, exposure coverage, rare-exception retention, capable text-memory comparator, context B-minus-A test, or efficiency analysis. Removing one removes its claim; it does not silently weaken its criterion.

Never cut the comparator alone, shorten only the untaught twin, select a checkpoint after opening reports, substitute historical lives for fresh controls, or describe retained ledger rows as consolidated learning.

The shipping standard is a traceable chain: authorized training data, qualified writer, recorded childhood, immutable deployment checkpoint, clean frozen comparison, matched-opportunity continual comparison, and exported failures. **A smaller completed case study is preferable to a larger experiment whose mechanism, provenance, or endpoint cannot be established.**
