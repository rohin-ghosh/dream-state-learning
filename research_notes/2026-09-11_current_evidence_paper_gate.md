## Verdict

The current manuscript can support a rigorous writer/failure-characterization
paper. It does **not** yet support the intended Dream--LoRA--Think paper. The
architecture is coherent; the causal chain is not closed.

The strongest established result is narrower:

> Parametric writes can install routines and cue-conditioned associations, but
> the current child does not reliably author grounded post-outcome records, the
> writer is not yet selective or fit-stable, parenting has not shown durable
> parent-absent task learning, and static text remains competitive or better.

Do not describe H1 as verified. Do not claim compressed experiential knowledge;
say compiled, consolidated, or parametric transport until a rate--distortion
experiment exists.

## Noncompensatory sequence

1. Run V10R1. A failure stops long parenting and lifetime scaling through this
   writer and strengthens the characterization paper.
2. After a pass, run exactly one identity-disjoint retention write and one
   authentic child-source qualification.
3. In parallel, certify the evolving active-text baseline and, if parenting
   remains a headline, run the bounded targeted-parent versus active-sham test.
4. Establish the PCFL explicit-text ceiling, then separately close
   connectedness, traversal, and action-driven expansion.
5. Only then spend heavily on independent longitudinal lineages against the
   certified active-text baseline.
6. Treat compression as a separate experiment, not an inference from LoRA
   rank.

Causal units must stay distinct: environment roots for mechanism/PCFL claims;
independently raised child lineages for parenting and lifetime claims.
Episodes, goals, and checkpoints are repeated measures, not independent
samples.
