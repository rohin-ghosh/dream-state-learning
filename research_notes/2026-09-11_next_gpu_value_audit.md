## Recommendation

The single highest-information next GPU experiment is **V10R1, the compact
multi-key conditional-writer gateway**: four clean-base rank-8 fits, two
disjoint engineered roots, complementary `W+`/`W-` maps, sixteen seen
tool-by-mode keys per adapter, and a hard cap of 3.0 A40-hours.

Do not fill the next opening with another long life, an H1 childhood, a
retention campaign, or a large active-text comparison. The full
Dream--LoRA--Think objective requires a reusable parametric writer that can
carry situation-dependent native actions. Current evidence instead shows
routine amplification, owner-associated completion mixed with spill, and
interface damage. V10R1 is the cheapest test that directly separates the
needed conditional carriage from the observed global-habit failure mode.

## V10R1 stop and promotion rules

- Missing/corrupt provenance, tokenizer, mask, request, adapter, receipt, or
  sealed result: `NONREPORTABLE_ABORT`; repair the assay, infer no science.
- Failed explicit-map oracle: `ASSAY_INVALID`; repair the measurement.
- Failed per-key NLL movement: `OPTIMIZATION_INCONCLUSIVE`; stop downstream
  qualification. At most one separately approved dose diagnostic is
  defensible; do not retune V10R1 after opening results.
- Failed native output/interface gate: `INTERFACE_INVALID`; fix the dialect
  before any parenting or lifetime scale.
- Conditional binding with excess spill: `BINDING_WITH_SPILL`; do not call it
  usable memory and do not advance a childhood.
- Adequate optimization/interface but failed binding: `GATEWAY_NEGATIVE`;
  redesign representation/read policy, not rank, and stop new long lives
  through this writer.
- Only `MULTIKEY_BINDING_PASS` on both roots advances. Its maximum claim is
  supervised seen-key conditional policy carriage under the enumerated tests.

After a pass, freeze the exact representation and recipe and run one
equal-sized identity-disjoint cumulative write. Require acquisition of the new
mapping, all old mapping/interface/spill gates, and at least 80% of every old
adapter's mean NLL gain. Failure stops childhood scale and makes cumulative
retention the mechanism problem. Only after retention should GPU spending move
to an unrepaired, grounded child-source relay and then a clean P-versus-active-
sham H1 test. The strong evolving active-text certificate must be ready before
any direct carrier, late-life, or superiority claim.

## Fresh adversarial recheck (2026-09-12 UTC)

Two fresh independent reviewers rechecked this ordering after SEQ-055 and the
independent seed-1 child-frame audit. Both retained V10R1 as the next bounded
writer **kill-gate**, not as a paper-core result.

The more narrative-proximal lived-mirror alternative is weaker as the first
test: a null would mix source/articulation failure with writer failure, and a
pass could still be a small supervised situation table. A two-stage
acquisition/retention scout within the same four-fit budget would sacrifice the
second root precisely while fitting fragility is unresolved. V10R1 therefore
remains the cheapest way to ask the narrower prerequisite question: can the
fixed writer carry a genuinely joint conditional native-action policy without
turning it into a global habit?

The reviewers' shared boundary is non-negotiable. Even
`MULTIKEY_BINDING_PASS` is only E1 component qualification over four fitted
instances. It does not establish experience, DREAM, retention, parenting,
connectedness, traversal, expansion, compression, generalization, reliability,
or a learning lifetime. A pass advances immediately to one identity-disjoint
cumulative retention write and then an unrepaired lived-source relay; it does
not authorize parenting scale.
