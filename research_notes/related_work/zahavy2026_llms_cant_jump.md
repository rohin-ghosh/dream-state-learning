# LLMs can't jump (Zahavy, Google DeepMind, Jan 2026)

Position paper (tomzahavy.com/files/llms-cant-jump.pdf). Author is
AlphaProof / creative-chess RL lineage.

## Claims
LLMs have mechanized Induction (statistical compression) and Deduction
(formal proof, AlphaProof-class), but lack Abduction — Peirce's third
inference mode: inventing the explanation for a surprising result. Case
study: General Relativity was formulated with essentially NO error signal
(Newton verified to 1e-9; the one anomaly, Mercury, was attributed to planet
Vulcan), so compression-driven discovery (Schmidhuber) cannot explain it — a
compression engine would have patched Newton, not restructured spacetime.
Einstein's mechanism was "manipulative abduction": embodied thought
experiments (the elevator) generating imagined observations that ground new
axioms in simulated physical sensation. Proposed fix: physically consistent,
action-controllable world models (Genie-style, counterfactual intervention)
as a synthetic laboratory, plus strong priors/beliefs to test.

## Relation to Dream–LoRA–Think
Overlaps (real, citable):
- Hypothesis GENERATION as the scarce operation = our autonomy-spectrum
  measurement (exhaustive verify easy, propose scarce). Our dream stage IS
  an abduction engine over evidence: propose the hidden cause (parents/roles)
  of observed results under known rules, gate on public evidence.
- His "embodied simulation → abduce → verify later" maps one-to-one onto our
  one-system view: dreaming = thinking without external grounding; Einstein's
  elevator is a dream in our vocabulary (simulated experience → proposed
  axiom → external verification arrives later, Eddington).
- His closing point that invention needs strong PRIORS to test = our priors
  dissociation (proposing/composing is prior-scaffolded; storage is not).
- Both reject "reading is enough": symbols must be grounded in experience
  (his Chinese-Room critique ≈ our omniscient-generalist/amnesiac-particular
  framing — agents lack a LIFE, not knowledge).

Differences (do not conflate):
- Different bottleneck: he targets axiom invention with NO error signal
  (paradigm shifts). Our system operates precisely where evidence exists —
  the entire verified-write machinery presumes checkable observations. We
  mechanize evidence-gated abduction at lifetime scale; he points at
  evidence-free abduction. Complementary, not identical.
- Different fix: multimodal physical world models vs memory/consolidation
  architecture over symbolic experiential worlds.

RESOLVED (Rohin's rebuttal, 2026-09-04 — supersedes the earlier "scope the
claim" caution): do NOT retreat from compression-as-intelligence; generalize
the loss instead. Zahavy's argument only works if the loss is defined over
EXTERNAL observational data. Einstein's error signal existed — it lived in
INTERNAL COHERENCE: the clash between mechanics and field theory, and
between the felt equivalence of acceleration/gravity and their formal
separation. Zahavy concedes this in his own §4 ("his drive was ... a
conceptual inconsistency"), which is an error signal by another name. The
compression thesis survives when the compressed corpus is the agent's FULL
experience stream: external observations + simulated/imagined experiences
(thought experiments = dreams) + the mutual consistency of the theories
already held ("if your intuition and observed knowledge are strong, you
should be able to account for all your experiences"). Unification IS
compression — MDL over theories, not over data points; and the search path
temporarily increasing complexity (his non-Euclidean objection) is normal
annealing under a compression objective, not evidence against it. Loss comes
in forms: predictive (induction), coherence (Einstein), description-length
(unification) — different loss patterns, one principle. Cite him as
motivation: the ingredients he demands (internal simulation, strong learned
priors, first-class hypothesis generation) are the components our loop
trains; where he says "world models," our dream mode already is the
counterfactual simulator, at symbolic scale.
