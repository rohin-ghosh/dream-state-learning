# Hippocampal sharp wave-ripple: A cognitive biomarker for episodic memory and planning
Source: Hippocampus 25(10):1073-1188, 2015. DOI: 10.1002/hipo.22488 (not on arXiv)  
Authors: György Buzsáki

## Abstract
Sharp wave ripples (SPW-Rs) represent the most synchronous population pattern in the mammalian brain. Their excitatory output affects a wide area of the cortex and several subcortical nuclei. SPW-Rs occur during "off-line" states of the brain, associated with consummatory behaviors and non-REM sleep, and are influenced by numerous neurotransmitters and neuromodulators. They arise from the excitatory recurrent system of the CA3 region and the SPW-induced excitation brings about a fast network oscillation (ripple) in CA1. The spike content of SPW-Rs is temporally and spatially coordinated by a consortium of interneurons to replay fragments of waking neuronal sequences in a compressed format. SPW-Rs assist in transferring this compressed hippocampal representation to distributed circuits to support memory consolidation; selective disruption of SPW-Rs interferes with memory. Recently acquired and pre-existing information are combined during SPW-R replay to influence decisions, plan actions and, potentially, allow for creative thoughts.

## Our differentiation
Claims: SPW-Rs are the physical transfer channel of consolidation — ~10-20x time-compressed replays of fragments of waking sequences during offline states; causally necessary (selective ripple disruption impairs learning); replay mixes new with pre-existing content and also runs prospectively (planning/"preplay").
Prioritization: replay is biased, not uniform — novel, rewarded and task-relevant experiences replay more (e.g., Ambrose et al. 2016: reward modulates reverse replay), making replay a selection/scheduling policy over experiences.
Mechanism (biological): CA3 recurrent network emits sharp waves; CA1 ripples package compressed sequence fragments broadcast to neocortex during SWS and quiet rest.
Sleeper relevance: the sleeper's consolidation queue should be priority-ordered (novelty / reward / task relevance / uncertainty), operate on compressed fragments rather than full traces, and deliberately interleave old items with the new delta — replay-as-scheduler is the biological blueprint for the compile loop.
