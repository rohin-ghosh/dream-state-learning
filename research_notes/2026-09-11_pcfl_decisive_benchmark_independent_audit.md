# Independent audit: decisive PCFL benchmark after writer qualification

Date: 2026-09-11 PDT (written 2026-09-12 UTC)

Status: read-only scientific design audit incorporated into the canonical
planning layer. It authorizes no implementation, model/tokenizer call,
training, adapter operation, GPU use, parenting, claim, release or submission.

## Verdict

The scientific skeleton is correct, but Learned-PCFL V5 remains REWORK.
Full C11 is not on the critical path: its guarded supplied-memory ceiling is
optional and cannot establish LoRA writing, retention, self-experience,
recurrence, lifetime improvement or the organism.

After a selective writer exists, the smallest defensible ladder is:

| gate | question | minimum decisive evidence | failure disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| W | Is SLEEP reusable? | conditional multi-key carriage; one identity-disjoint cumulative write; exact PCFL-row read; locality, retention, interface and non-harm | stop PCFL LoRA work |
| M0 / M-TEXT | Is the benchmark solvable without leakage? | CPU theorem, mutations and scripted oracle; exact-text canary on four DEV roots, extending to eight only if at least two of four pass E2 and E4 | repair the interface if text fails while oracle passes |
| E2 | Are experiences connected? | child-authored atoms plus links for which both atoms are mechanically necessary; authentic versus truthful-null and same-build read permutation; adapter-off, wrong-life and spill controls | no connection claim |
| E4 | Does the goal control traversal? | same state and carrier under two incompatible goals; goal swap changes first relevant query, full path and action; bridge cut removes value and truthful twin redirects it | E2 may stand; no traversal claim |
| E5 | Does the system expand memory through action? | authentic versus matched sham carried through chosen action, public outcome, compilation/write, sterile reset and one delayed goal requiring old plus new knowledge; no-write, sham-write, reachout-off, binding and old-row cuts | E4 may stand; no expansion claim |
| L | Does the complete learner beat evolving text after its prospective plateau? | separately raised paired lineages; certified active text; at least three post-anchor cuts; positive learner slope and slope advantage; retention and terminal practical margin | no lifetime/saturation/superiority claim |

E2, E4 and E5 are separate nested releases. A failed higher endpoint cannot
erase a valid lower one. Their independent unit is the environment-root/twin
pair; goals, paths, queries, training candidates and checkpoints are repeated
measures. L uses the independently raised paired lineage as its unit.

## Text roles must stay separate

- Exact same-semantics text is an assay ceiling over the same payload bytes.
- The strong external-memory opponent is an independently evolving,
  candidate-blind system over eligible public experience, certified for
  record fidelity, recall, citation identity, store-swap sensitivity, actual
  use and sufficient budget.
- A failed strong-baseline integration blocks superiority only. It does not
  make LoRA win and does not erase a valid E2/E4/E5 carrier result.

The lifetime comparison is on-policy: paired systems share opportunity sets
and exogenous randomness, then choose actions and acquire their own outcomes.
Giving both systems an identical completed information stream would test
carrier accumulation, not the self-directed flywheel. The earlier one-cycle
E2--E5 interventions localize which mechanism produced a whole-system L
difference.

## Compute and stopping

V5's old eight-root bound of 48 LoRA fits assumes one fit per transaction.
With a frozen transactional writer of at most K candidates, the worst case is:

8 roots * 6 write transactions * K = 48K fits.

Candidate fits and checkpoints are not scientific replicates. Every
NO_COMMIT remains in the intention-to-treat denominator, and paired arms
share candidate-seed schedules.

Use at most eight learned-relay DEV roots. Freeze endpoint definitions and
estimate root-level variance there; then power a disjoint confirmation for
E2, E4 and E5 separately. Do not reuse the older omnibus N=96, 47/96 rule
after separating endpoints.

Full C11 would spend up to 24,072 calls and 7,120,896 generated tokens while
excluding the core learned-organism claims. Reuse its finite-world,
visibility and leakage-checking ideas for CPU M0, but keep the full supplied-
memory run optional.

Compression remains separate. No E2/E4/E5/L pass implies a rate--distortion or
physical-compression result.
