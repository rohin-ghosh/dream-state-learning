# NEXT EXPERIMENT DESIGN v1 — "taught to learn", the smallest honest test, revised

Synthesis of the design workflow (4 designers, 3 judges, 3 critics), 2026-09-10. Starting point: the winning design (MV-1, "minimum-viable"). Grafted: the judges' suggestions that survived the critics. Resolved: every fatal and major critic attack (section 10 states each attack and what changed). Nothing here enters the ICLR 2027 characterization paper (abstract 09-18, paper 09-25); this is the first experiment of Rohin's thesis programme. Read-only synthesis: no GPU work was done and no code was changed for this document; every "verified" claim below was re-checked against the repository or the analysis JSON on the laptop.

Plain-language glossary (used throughout): a **life** = one child (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct plus a rank-8 LoRA adapter) playing 1,024 program-optimization situations with a **sleep** (adapter retrain from the birth base on the compiled text corpus) every 32; a **cycle** = one sleep; a **situation** = one program instance with a fixed budget of 16 thinking chunks, ended only by the harness; a **state** = a segment of thinking inside a situation that the child closes with a review; a **paired probe** = the same fixed panel of programs played with the adapter ON and OFF at the same age with the same generation seeds; **L(t)** = ON minus OFF at cycle t, the life's learning gain; a **harmful pair** = ON minus OFF below −0.03; **recipe lock-in** = the adapter making the child apply one fixed pass sequence everywhere (measured: adapter-ON means repeat exactly at 0.4878 = the birth-prompt recipe); **ritual** = the frozen 09-09 detector for templated thinking; **JS shift** = how much the distribution of first actions changes between consecutive cycles (the "did behaviour change at all" gate); an **arm** = a set of lives sharing one recipe; a **lineage root** = a fresh child created because a mechanism changed.

---

## 0. One screen

- **Question.** Does a child that was *developed* before deployment — Stage 1: a structure bootstrap from a target-blind source; Stage 2: a 512-episode childhood in two persistence gyms with a critic that never gives answers — improve **faster per unit of experience** during autonomous continual learning in a gym held out **by source**, than an undeveloped child running the **identical** mechanism?
- **Primary comparison.** D (developed) vs B (regular), **K = 6 vs 6**, matched curriculum order, **co-located on the same node in pairs**, same v7 stream, same arm-neutral gate, same panels, parent absent at deployment. Readout at cycle 16 (deployment episode 512) on report panel v2, each arm against its own frozen reference (adapter OFF). Verdict rule F1 (section 7): L_D − L_B ≥ +0.02 in ≥ 5 of 6 pairs (false-positive rate 0.011 under the *measured* between-life SD of 0.027; the null is uninformative below a true effect of about +0.04 — said plainly in every write-up).
- **Baselines inside every life.** Adapter-OFF paired probe (the regular frozen agent); the 2×2 probe cells (adapter ON/OFF × text memory present/absent) at four ages, so weights effect, text effect and their interaction are read per life; the 8-program legacy panel at three checkpoints for continuity only.
- **Baseline arms.** A (same runner, v6 stream with DONE — the mechanism control, K = 2 in wave 1); E (text-memory agent living the whole life with no adapter, brief refreshed every wake — the ruled "always a baseline agent", K = 2); C (bootstrap only) and P (parenting only) complete Rohin's 2×2 {none, bootstrap, parent, both} at K = 3 each in wave 2.
- **One mechanism change (new lineage root, "v7a"):** no end token; harness-ended situations; child-ended states (`REVIEW:` closes a state, never the situation); the last review pinned into the next state and into the next situation; `RETURN: <state id>` brings an earlier state's plan and review back for revision. Everything else — writer, rank, budget, sleep cadence, RECALL — is unchanged; two organizational compile changes (child-authored review exemplars enter; model-written PRINCIPLE lines and exact-template duplicates leave the training corpus) apply to every new arm behind flags with a byte-identity test.
- **Not run.** Plasticity; conversations; clone merges and the evaluator classroom; k-branching (pilot only if a third node is granted); a contingent per-situation critic (sibling arm only if GPUs free); parenting-style or valence comparisons.
- **Budget and window.** ≈ 2,150 GPU-hours executed inside a 16-GPU lease of ≈ 4,000 GPU-hours held (two 8×A40 nodes, 09-18 evening → 09-29). Ten experiment days from Sat 09-19 (D1) to Mon 09-28 (D10); the build happens 09-11 → 09-18 in GPU-free slots. A third 8-GPU node is requested for 09-22 → 09-29; if granted it carries the replication wave (D×4 + B×4). The lease quota is a rolling 30-day window over booked hours (idle nodes count); the increase request goes in on 09-11 with the lease request.
- **Pre-registration.** PREREG v1 (arms, panels, probe cells, gate parameters, the D-admission rule, the wave-2 rule, every falsifier with cycle, metric, margin, K) is hashed and posted to the coordination log before the first launch on D2; v2 before wave 2. Thresholds move only through a new dated entry labelled post hoc.
- **What changed from the winning design (top ten).** (1) K raised from 4 to 6 on the primary and the null recomputed from the measured between-life SD; (2) matched pairs co-located on one node, critic on both nodes; (3) arm-neutral gate floor plus an entry-level admissibility band, so a schooled base cannot buy itself a looser gate or more headroom; (4) two distinct-seed reps at the decision checkpoints and the adapter's identity recorded in every probe file; (5) efficiency and backtrack instruments recomputed from the harness's action rows instead of the child's echoed outcome lines (verified defect); (6) Stage 2 dosed at 512 episodes across two gyms (LockGym first, rule game v2 with a Brier-scored quiz) with a 40-instance exam whose tolerance comes from a measured noise band, and a written "treatment delivered" rule; (7) the strongest available model (32B-AWQ) as critic, fed the child's reviews; (8) review quality measured by template-independent instruments (carry-over, calibration, counterfactual specificity), with the four-slot template conformance reported separately; (9) report panel v2 built from student-solution programs under a search-headroom rule with content-hash and shared-symbol checks, sealed by two sealers; (10) the text-memory agent's brief-refresh defect fixed and E promoted to a fixed part of the plan.
- **Sign-offs needed before D1 (section 14).** Rohin: rule game v2 and LockGym drafts; panel v2 criteria; recipe removal from the birth prompt; v7a (without k-branching) as the lineage root; PRINCIPLE lines out of the corpus (the 09-04 vs 09-07 reconciliation); the step-fade reading of repetition. Codex: provenance schema and negative tests (hard precondition of the D launch); gate form (provisional commit vs 2-rep); bootstrap writer; branch write unit (for the pilot only); co-sealing panel v2.

## 1. Hypothesis — in Rohin's words, then in operational form

**Rohin's words (IDEAS 2026-09-10, "the killer metric").** "A developed agent shows a higher rate of improvement per unit of experience than the baseline agent on task distributions the parent never showed — *did it acquire a better learning algorithm*, not *did the classroom pretrain it*." Parenting is "a curriculum of gyms that each instil a trait — persistence first — and return environmental feedback that makes the child learn on its own; the parent model is one feedback channel inside those gyms, the world's outcomes are the other, and the child's own reactions to those outcomes are what sleep writes." What we train is "state-to-state reasoning: plan, execute to a self-determined end, meta-review, and the review is remembered so it conditions the next state"; the model "should be taught recurrent chain-of-thought — reasoning that returns to and revises its own earlier reasoning". "The gym is the world; the benchmark is the protocol inside it; always have a baseline agent." Judge parenting "by what the child does afterwards in gyms it has not seen — first and second derivatives vs the baseline agent — not by how much the parent talked."

**Operational form (H1, minimal).** Let L_X(t) = ON(t) − OFF for arm X at deployment cycle t on report panel v2, where OFF is that arm's own frozen reference (clean base for A, B, E; bootstrap base B1 for C; schooled base S_i for D and P). Then, with the preconditions of section 7 holding, L_D(16) − L_B(16) ≥ +0.02 in at least 5 of 6 matched pairs, and the slope difference [L_D(16) − L_D(4)] − [L_B(16) − L_B(4)] is positive in the same pairs. This is the second derivative in its smallest measurable form: the *rate* of gain on a program family the parent never saw, entry level separated from acceleration.

**Mechanism sentence (Codex, kept as a hypothesis, never as a claim).** Bootstrap teaches the habit of making and revising rules; parenting teaches when to use that habit; lived outcomes supply the evidence; sleep makes the child's own successful use persistent.

**Alternatives killed by construction, not by argument.**

| alternative explanation | how the design kills it |
|---|---|
| the frozen model could already do it | adapter-OFF probe at every checkpoint, per arm, on the same seeds |
| the text memory explains it (review M4) | 2×2 probe cells at 256/512/768/1024 in every life; arm E lives the whole life on text only with the brief actually refreshed |
| the childhood pretrained the deployment task | Stage-1 source and Stage-2 gyms contain zero compiler content (leak-scanned for the full pass list at seal time); deployment is every arm's first contact with the gym; exposure domains are derived from the gyms actually instantiated, not declared |
| the stream change itself (v6 → v7) produced the effect | arm A (v6 stream, same birth prompt) vs arm B; the harness-only cell prices the birth-prompt change on the frozen model |
| entry-level offset masquerading as learning | OFF(0) per arm from 3 reps; the entry-level admissibility band; the claim is about L and the slope, never about OFF_D > OFF_B |
| a looser gate for the developed arm | arm-neutral floor (clean-base reference measured once with ≥ 6 reps); per-arm acceptance rate reported beside every curve |
| node or hardware differences | every D_i–B_i pair lives on one node; a node term is pre-registered and F1 is read within node |
| selection on the report panel (review F1) | gate on panel v1b, report on panel v2, legacy 8-panel reference only; overlap is a hard error |
| heavy-tailed single probes (one same-adapter pair differed by 0.065) | two distinct-seed reps at the decision checkpoints; readout = mean of four draws; adapter identity recorded; min/max reported |
| bootstrap or parent contamination (Codex STOP) | fail-closed provenance guard with negative tests, ratified by Codex before any D launch; every life carries a manifest |
| the developed arm was never actually developed | written "treatment delivered" rule executed by script from the Stage-2 logs before any deployment number is read |
| the review detector rediscovers the bootstrap's own template | template-independent review measures (carry-over, calibration, counterfactual specificity) are primary; template conformance is a separate descriptive column |

## 2. Measured state this design inherits, and what this synthesis verified

Cell counts from EVIDENCE_TABLES.md (with addenda) and the coordination log entries SEQ-001..008; every number names its unit.

- **Recipe lock-in.** Adapter-ON plateaus 0.4878 (= the birth recipe applied once) and 0.5291 (a six-pass routine) on the legacy 8-panel; -Oz 0.522, -O3 0.341; exploring frozen model 0.463–0.488; OFF on life probes 0.471–0.474. On disjoint panel v1 the birth recipe scores 0.2495; R2 seed2's final and mid-life adapters scored 0.2496/0.2495 there (identical reps); frozen model 0.257/0.254; -Oz 0.2745. The recipe-locked write reproduces the birth recipe on programs it never saw, and that recipe is worse than exploration there. Consequence kept from the winning design: the birth prompt of every new arm carries no recipe. Pre-registered prediction for the pending re-probe: every 0.4878-plateau adapter scores 0.2495 ± 0.003 on v1.
- **Efficiency and markers (SEQ-005).** Chunks-to-best doubles early → late (3–4 → 6–9) in every arm; contrast phrases and backtracks → ~0; first-action JS shift between cycles 0.02–0.13; taught vs untaught do not separate. **Caveat verified in this synthesis:** these numbers came from an instrument that reads `[OUTCOME]` lines out of the child's own text, so a window in which the child stops echoing outcomes has no chunks-to-best at all (27 of 32 windows undefined in R2 seed6; 12/32 in R3 500; 13/32 in R2 seed4). The doubling is confounded with the child ceasing to echo. All efficiency baselines are re-derived from the harness's action rows before pre-registration (section 3.10).
- **Late chunk saturation (Design 2's reading, verified).** Late-life chunks per situation sit at 15.4–16.0 in R2 seeds 0/1/2/4/7/8, R3 503, R4 604–606 and RP 402: late situations already end by budget, not by DONE. Removing DONE changes early life and the probes most; the late pathology is filling the budget without revising — which is what review states and RETURN target.
- **Harm at the life level.** Ungated R2: 4/9 lives with ≥ 1 harmful pair, 3/9 with ≥ 2; RP 1/3; gated R3 0/6 but post-selection (gate and report panel were the same eight programs); late-life candidates in R3 500 collapsed to 0.19–0.34 and the gate refused all six. Behavioural gate catches with passing scores: R3 500@64, 501@704, 505@288.
- **Noise (from disk, verified here).** SD of the adapter-OFF probe mean 0.0092/0.0087; same-adapter same-seed replicate SD 0.0065 (n = 70) with one 0.065 outlier; disjoint-v1 replicate SD 0.006–0.007. **Between-life SD of the design's own cycle-16 estimator** (mean of the paired ON−OFF at episodes 448 and 512): **0.027 across the four full gated R3 lives** (+0.051, +0.016, +0.066, +0.011), 0.072 across all nine R2 lives, 0.014 across the six R2 lives that did not collapse; SD of R2 life means 0.033 (early life 0.014). The gated figure, 0.027, is the ruler this design uses for the D-vs-B null.
- **Echo, ritual, parenting.** First-note overlap with the brief 0.454 after a brief vs 0.322 pre-brief vs 0.354 unbriefed controls; "rehearsal 0.50–1.00" withdrawn. Ritual onset 160–224 in 8/9 unparented lives; parented 288/192(+remission)/256, returns and persists under repeated briefs; a context-only lesson lasts ~2 sleeps. The 14B pattern parent's utterances are generic scientific-method advice (IDEAS 09-07).
- **Bootstraps.** v1/v2 task-exposed (quarantined); v3 target-blind but DEV_UNVERIFIED_PROVENANCE under Codex's STOP; no canonical clean child exists.
- **Code facts verified in this synthesis that the design must fix.** (a) A bootstrap adapter seeded into the first sleep directory is replaced at the first sleep because every sleep retrains a fresh LoRA from the base model — a bootstrap that is meant to persist must be merged into the birth base. (b) An adapter-free life never sees its waking briefs after the first wake (the refresh sits inside the adapter-arm block), so today's "arm A" is a frozen model with RECALL, not a text-memory agent. (c) Every probe uses one generation seed (777) at every checkpoint, so two probes of an unchanged adapter are the same draw. (d) The gate floor uses the arm's own base, measured once with a single 2-rep draw. (e) The format canary runs four compiler programs against every candidate, so a rule-game life on the shared runner would touch the deployment gym before deployment. (f) The masked bootstrap trainer left-truncates the stored context to about 1,600–1,750 tokens, cutting the head that would carry a pinned review. (g) The classroom commit rule computes a PREV exam but does not use it. (h) The chunk seed has no branch term, so k seeded branches from one context would be identical text. (i) Rule game QUIZ is repeatable with best-of scoring (a brute-force loophole), and the rule-game birth prompt still says "Write DONE when finished".

### 3.1 The v7a stream (one mechanism change; new lineage root)

Built as `organism_v6/run_life_v3.py` with `batch_loop`/`state`/`sleep_compile` edits behind a `--stream {v6,v7}` flag; arm A runs the same code with the v6 stream.

- **No end token.** `DONE` is removed from the marker regexes and the birth prompt. A situation (one program instance) ends only when the harness ends it at the 16-chunk budget. The CLOCK stays as information. Tokens after the last action are logged as `chunks_after_last_act` and are part of the persistence readout ("continued changing attempts" vs "idle repetition").
- **Child-ended states.** A state opens at the start of a situation and after each `REVIEW: <text>`. `REVIEW:` closes the current state and opens a new one **on the same program**; it never advances the situation (the harness budget does). The review text is stored as `kind="review"` with `state_id`, `forced=false`. If the child has written no review by chunk 15, the harness injects `[REVIEW] close this state: write your review now` and stores the next chunk as `forced=true`; forced reviews never count toward review-quality falsifiers. Collapse flags: `REVIEW:` within the first 3 chunks; ≥ 4 reviews per situation; consecutive-review Jaccard ≥ 0.5 (`templated_reviews`, reported separately, never folded into the frozen ritual definition).
- **Review pin.** The head of every state shows `LAST REVIEW (state k)`; the head of every situation shows the previous situation's last review in the same slot-stream (`LAST REVIEW (previous program)`). The last two reviews are pinned (≤ 600 characters each). One definition of "review" is used by the pin, the compile and the detectors: the `REVIEW:` text of a state (or the last NOTE after the final committed action in the v6 arm). A rehearsal-only state pins and compiles nothing as a review.
- **Recurrent chain of thought.** `RETURN: <state id>` pulls that state's plan (its first NOTE) and its review verbatim into the recalled block for revision. Counters: states per situation, reviews per situation, RETURNs per situation, "first action changed after a RETURN".
- **Birth prompt (all new arms, A included).** No concrete recipe: the example action line and the INVALID hint's four pass names are replaced by a neutral one-line pointer to the 124 valid pass names, kept out of the head (a RECALL-able ledger row) so the context budget is unchanged. One sentence: "each situation has a fixed number of chunks (CLOCK); close each stretch of work with REVIEW: — it is the first thing you will see next." Consequence: new-arm 8-panel numbers are not comparable to R2–R5 and are used as reference only.
- **Probes under v7a.** The primary probe runs 12 programs as 12 one-state situations (comparable ON vs OFF). A **chained probe** (3 slot-streams × 4 sequential programs with the pins active, seeded, same budget) runs at the decision checkpoints only, so a gain that lives in the review channel can register; single-state is pre-registered as primary for F1, chained is secondary.
- **Deliberately unchanged.** Wake budget 16 chunks × 400 tokens (the measured late-life v6 chunk count already saturates at 16, so more chunks per situation is a separate dose arm with its own probe budget and is future work with a trigger); wake batch 8; sleep every 32; probe every 64; temperature 0.7; v1 bare-text writer (lr 1e-4, 3 epochs, max length 512) with `--seed` and model/revision/seed in `train_meta.json`; rank 8 from birth; RECALL keyword retrieval; the waking brief.

### 3.2 Compile changes (organizational; constant across arms; flagged)

- For every state with ≥ 1 valid outcome, one review exemplar enters the corpus: `Program {eid}, state {k}. After {n} actions (best {best}), my review: {review}` — child-authored text grounded by a committed action (Codex's rule for what may enter SLEEP).
- PRINCIPLE lines (model-written, unlived) no longer enter the training corpus; the waking brief is still written and shown (context only). This is the 09-07 "compiler organizational, never smart" ruling applied; it needs Rohin's one-line reconciliation with the 09-04 "compiling is a model's job" ruling (section 14) and defaults to *drop*.
- Exact-template collapse: rows whose whitespace-normalized first 160 characters match are kept at most `cap = 3` times (count-capped), for all new arms; near-duplicate collapse waits for Codex's threshold/support-count protocol. Logged per sleep: rows collapsed, modal-row share before and after, review rows, principle rows suppressed.
- Test: with all v7 flags off, `compile_sleep_v7` reproduces the v6 corpus bytes on an archived R2 ledger; `run_life_v3 --stream v6` reproduces `run_life_v2`'s renders on a replayed ledger.

### 3.3 Gate hygiene (every gated arm; not a difference between arms)

- Gate on **panel v1b**; base measured once at birth (2 reps); **arm-neutral floor** = max(clean-base reference on v1b measured once with ≥ 6 reps on the utility GPU and shared by all arms, the arm's own base, best *confirmed* committed gate probe). No floor decay.
- **Commit form.** Default: provisional commit — the candidate mounts as PROVISIONAL after passing the canaries and scoring ≥ floor − 0.02; at the next sleep the same adapter is probed first on the gate panel and CONFIRMED (≥ floor − 0.02) or REJECTED_UNCONFIRMED (revert to the last confirmed adapter; wake rows generated under it remain experience). Adopted only if Codex ratifies by 09-17; otherwise the 2-rep gate (seeds 4242/5242, both ≥ floor − 0.02) is used and disclosed.
- **Behaviour canaries under no-DONE** (chunk count is constant, so "chunks per episode" is dead): actions per situation ≥ max(2, 0.5 × base median) AND ≥ 1 valid outcome per situation; distinct action strings per situation ≥ 0.5 × base; paired parseable-ACT rate ≥ base − 0.10 on the same gate probe. Calibrated on the D2 dry run so the gate does not penalize a candidate that spends half its chunks planning and reviewing.
- **Stage-2 gate** (trait gyms): exam = 40 held-out-family rule-game instances + 24 held-out LockGym codes; tolerance = 1 SD of the gym's measured frozen-model noise band (8 seeded reps, D1); floor = max(B1 on the exam, best confirmed); the collapse canary ON vs PREV on 8 held-out instances (HARM_TOL 0.10); the same acts canary.
- Rejections logged by reason (SCORE, UNCONFIRMED, ACTS, DISTINCT, FORMAT, PREV); a life that rejects its late writes stays on its last confirmed adapter — safe and informative.

### 3.4 Birth bases by merge (the bootstrap must live in the base)

`merge_birth_base.py`: PEFT merge-and-unload of a LoRA into Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (pinned snapshot), saved as a local bf16 checkpoint (~15 GB); the life's inference server and trainer point at it. One merge for the bootstrap (**B1**); for D and P a second merge of the Stage-2 final confirmed adapter (into B1 for D, into the clean base for P) → schooled base **S_i**, one per seed. Exact up to bf16 rounding (checked: next-token agreement between adapter-on-base and merged base on 20 stored prompts ≥ 0.99; disclosed). Adapter-OFF for a merged-base life = the merged base with no life LoRA, so ON − OFF is the deployment learning gain in every arm and OFF(0) is the arm's entry level. Deployment starts a new life directory and a new ledger: childhood carries over in the weights only; no waking brief and no critic text at deployment. Storage: ~15 GB per base, 7–10 bases per node (~150 GB) — checked on D1. For the follow-on lease only the LoRA adapters (≈ 100 MB each) are kept; bases are re-merged from them.

### 3.5 Fail-closed provenance guard (precondition for any birth; Codex STOP)

`provenance.py`: birth manifest with base id and snapshot hash; corpus sha and every source-ledger sha with the runner/renderer code shas that produced them; author model and revision; `mechanism_version` = sha over an **explicit runtime file list** (runner, batch loop, state, compile, trainers, model backend, gym backends, critic files — not the analysis scripts); writer/compiler path; `SCANNER_VERSION`; `exposure_domains` derived from the gyms instantiated; critic bytes hash (Stage 2); ancestor chain; merge receipt. `admit()` refuses launch on any missing field, any ancestor in the quarantine list, or any exposure domain other than the two trait gyms for a clean birth. Negative tests (copied directory without receipts; self-declared manifest; quarantined ancestor) must refuse. Hotfixes append a `code_change` event (old sha, new sha, sleep index, reason) and resume is allowed only if the new sha is on an allow-list posted to the coordination log. The shuffled curriculum and the runner arguments are persisted at launch and asserted on resume; the relaunch template carries every flag. **Codex ratification of the schema and the negative tests is a hard precondition of the D and P launches** (deadline 09-17; fallback in section 12).

### 3.6 Gym protocol and the split ledger

## 5. Gyms, the traits they instil, and split hygiene

**Deployment gym (the world): CompilerGym llvm-v0, instruction-count reduction.** Kept per Rohin's ruling; the protocol inside it is the benchmark. Traits it can register: persistence (actions after a non-improving outcome, distinct actions per situation, chunks after the last action), reflection (review detectors), situation-dependent choice (per-program panel-v2 table; the search-headroom rule guarantees a recipe-locked child scores HC = 0 by construction).

**Stage-2 gym 1 — LockGym (persistence first; re-planning after failure).** A hidden code of length 4 over symbols 1–6 (training tier) or length 5 over 1–8 (far-transfer exam tier); `ACT: GUESS s1 s2 s3 s4` returns `exact=k partial=m`. Every feedback contradicts hypotheses; payoff is monotone in continued, changing attempts (score = max over attempts of exact/L, 1.0 when the lock opens); quitting early cannot score and re-guessing the same code cannot score more. When the lock opens the harness seals the outcome (`[SITUATION] lock opened after n attempts`) but the situation runs to its budget — the white space is where the review is written; nothing here is an end token. Exam: 24 codes from a held-out seed range, 8 seeded frozen-model reps first (noise band). About 150 lines, target-blind, no descriptors that overlap compiler text.

**Stage-2 gym 2 — rule game v2 (reflection, self-evaluation, exploration).** Compound rules (AND / OR / A-and-not-B of two atomic primitives with parameterized thresholds) generated per instance and grouped into families; training and exam instances are split **by family**. `TRY a,b,c` returns True/False; the quiz asks T/F **with a confidence 0.5–1.0 per item** and is scored 1 − Brier (a proper scoring rule: confident wrong answers are punished, so self-evaluation is scored, not just persistence); one quiz per box, INVALID before 4 probes (an environmental rule, not parent text), sealed after it is answered, and the situation again runs to budget with the review in the white space. Disclosed limitation: the primitives are textbook rules; the compound families and the sealed Brier quiz are what make it a gym, and no score claim is made on it. Exam: 40 held-out-family instances (noise band re-measured for v2 with 8 seeded reps before any number is read).

**Persistence flags inside Stage 2** (ritual analogues, reported per window): modal guesses-before-quiz or guesses-before-abandon share ≥ 0.7; `REVIEW:` within the first 3 chunks; identical guess repeated after non-improving feedback.

**Stage-1 source — rule game v1, frozen model.** Not a gym for learning: a target-blind source of the child's real contexts and grounded episodes for the structure renderings (the ten atomic rules; the compound families of v2 never appear in the bootstrap).

**Trait → gym → instrument (per situation, averaged per cycle, ON vs OFF, D vs B):**

| trait | where instilled | deployment readout (action rows) | today's measured baseline (to be re-derived with instruments v2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| persistence | LockGym, then the deployment gym | backtracks per situation; distinct actions after the last improvement; chunks after the last action classified as continued attempts vs idle repetition | backtracks 0.3–0.9 early → ~0 late (echo-based; re-derive) |
| re-planning after failure | LockGym, rule game v2 | P(next action changes given feedback contradicted the stated hypothesis) | not measured |
| reflection | every REVIEW state | unforced review rate; carry-over; specificity; templated_reviews | contrast phrases 4.7–6.7 early → ~0 late (echo-based; re-derive) |
| self-evaluation | rule game v2 quiz; every PREDICT | calibration of reviews vs outcomes; quiz Brier (Stage 2 only) | PREDICT SD < 0.05 = a ritual flag |

**Split hygiene (Rohin's contamination rule; Codex's ratification pending).** Stage 1 and Stage 2 are target-blind by construction (two trait gyms, zero compiler content, verified by leak scan at seal); the deployment gym's training split is *not* used as a parenting environment in this experiment (that option is enabled for the next experiment once Codex ratifies split hygiene); the report panel is held out **by source** and content hash, the gate panel by source from a different family, the legacy 8-panel is reference only; the critic is target-blind (it never sees deployment programs and is absent at deployment); the bootstrap is gym-free; every compiled row carries `clone_id`, `env_id`, `situation_id`, `state_id` and exposure domain as contamination hygiene for the guard — **no gain-attribution-by-source analysis is planned** (Rohin: "thoughts carry the gain").

## 12. Risks and stop rules

Each risk names the mitigation already in the design and the rule that fires without judgment.

1. **Lease or provisioning slip.** Requests filed 09-11; provisioning from the environment freeze rehearsed on the surviving node before 09-21. *Stop rule:* if the nodes are not live by D1 noon, every launch shifts one day and the cut order applies from the bottom; if not live by D2, wave 2 is cancelled and B/D run at K = 5 vs 5.
2. **Codex does not ratify the guard in time.** *Stop rule:* not ratified by D4 noon → D and P run as development arms (no clean-child sentence; numbers reported as development evidence); B, A, C, E remain the scientific wave; the clean D wave moves to the follow-on lease. B and A are unaffected.
3. **Harness bug found late (the M-series pattern).** Smoke in every gym and a whole-chain rehearsal on D1; a 32-situation dry run with two sleeps, gate, probes and a kill-and-resume test on D2; marker-file resume; atomic writes; `code_change` events. *Stop rule:* a failed dry run is fixed and re-run; the launch never skips the dry run; two failed dry runs → the launch slides a day and E drops to K = 1.
4. **F7 fails (behaviour flat, as in every v6 arm).** Precondition A4: all score comparisons are declared uninterpretable in advance; the experiment still delivers the review gallery, ritual/marker contrasts and (with a third node) the diagnosis set. *Stop rule:* written, executed by the daemon on D6.
5. **Stage 2 does not deliver the treatment** (few confirmed writes, high fallback, quizzes never reached). Noise-band tolerances and reach-rate checks on D1; the A3 rule. *Stop rule:* if ≥ 2 of 6 D seeds are underdosed, D is reported as underdosed and F1 is not read as a parenting result; if the D1 smoke shows reach rates < 0.5, the rule-game probe requirement drops to 3 and the Stage-2 budget rises to 24 chunks (Stage 2 only, disclosed) before launch.
6. **Heavy-tailed probes.** Two-seed decision checkpoints, provisional commit, two-checkpoint harm definition, adapter identity in every probe file, min/max reported.
7. **Score headroom on panel v2 too small or the dataset unavailable.** Search-headroom rule; poj104-first; fallback disclosed as thin. *Stop rule:* if fewer than 8 programs qualify by 09-16, panel v2 = the qualifying set plus the best v1 programs by headroom, labelled mixed-source.
8. **Entry level or node term outside the band** (A1, A2). Read within node and in headroom-normalized form; no "learns faster" sentence unless both forms agree.
9. **Critic leaks or falls back.** Scoped scanner, whitelist, one rewrite, fallback rate as a treatment field. *Stop rule:* D1 smoke fallback ≥ 0.25 → scanner scope narrowed and re-smoked before the D4 launch; per-life fallback ≥ 0.5 → that seed is underdosed (A3).
10. **Disk.** ~15 GB per merged base, 7–10 bases per node; free space checked on D1 and D3. *Stop rule:* < 200 GB free → merged bases of finished lives deleted after their adapters are saved.
11. **Paper deadline collides with D5–D7.** Every decision in that window is a written rule executed by the daemon (D-admission, wave 2, third node); analysis begins D8.
12. **Reading the null as "no".** Childhood 512 situations, deployment read at 512: the write-up says "not at this dose/horizon" by construction, states the power (uninformative below ≈ +0.04), and shows the curves (the horizon warning, IDEAS 09-07).
13. **Two writers in one lineage** (masked writer for Stage 1, bare-text for Stages 2–3). Disclosed in every table; the alternative loses the context-conditioned structure and is the optional composition control's job.
14. **The author writes text that was never lived** (32B-authored flows over the child's real contexts). Accepted and described as such; the lived-heavy composition control runs only with a third node.
15. **Quota.** Booked hours count from creation; the two-node booking leaves ≈ 340 node-hours of headroom; a third node beyond 14 days needs the increase. *Stop rule:* increase refused and the window exceeded → the lease ends 09-27, P and E drop to K = 1, D is read at cycle 16 only.
16. **Data loss.** Nightly receipt mirrors to the laptop with hash checks; adapters (not bases) kept; nothing depends on a node surviving past the lease.
