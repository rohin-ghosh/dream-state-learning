# Dream--LoRA--Think full evidence stack

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: unbound, proposal-only program map. It authorizes no architecture
change, implementation, target generation, model/tokenizer call, benchmark or
adapter operation, external scientific execution, GPU use, or claim. It does
not modify the frozen one-parent v2 packet.

## Why this map exists

The one-parent/one-child CompilerGym headline can test whether parenting
changes the later benefit of per-life writes and whether the resulting system
beats a validated active-text agent. Even a clean positive result cannot by
itself establish that LoRA contains connected/compressed experiential
knowledge, that the agent traverses such knowledge under goals, or that its
knowledge drives actions which expand future knowledge.

The full project therefore requires a stack of separately falsifiable
results. Deadline pressure may determine which rungs fit in one paper, but it
must not turn an unmeasured architectural aspiration into a claim.

## E0 — writer truth and behavioral preservation

Question: can supported native thought/action/state continuations be written
without corrupting the executable agent?

Required evidence:

- exact public-state -> native child response -> dispatched typed action ->
  public outcome provenance;
- response-only supervision in the child's deployment dialect;
- clean-base cumulative adapter construction with reproducible mount/off
  receipts;
- parent prose, tool results, raw ledgers, and unsupported claims excluded as
  positive targets;
- free-routing quality and typed-forced proposal quality non-inferior after
  writing; and
- adapter-off, wrong-life, binding-shuffle, and format-canary diagnostics.

Failure means the experiment tested a bad writer, not experiential learning.

## E1 — one parent teaches later learning

Question: after the parent disappears, does one-to-one process parenting
increase the child's benefit from its own subsequent experience?

Carrier: the frozen one-parent v2 `U0/U1/P0/P1/R0` protocol.

Primary evidence:

```text
D = [gain_AUC(P1)-gain_AUC(P0)]
  - [gain_AUC(U1)-gain_AUC(U0)]
```

plus parent-absent entry-adjusted slopes, forward transfer, retention, and
information gained per generated token. `P1>R0` is a full-package system
contrast; it is not causal parenting evidence. A higher `P0` entry score with
nonpositive `D` is inherited competence, not learned meta-intelligence.

Because the parenting curriculum teaches behaviors that the deterministic
writer may preferentially admit, report the mediator rather than hiding it:
eligible/accepted scientific rows, rehearsal fills, target-type mix,
supported-outcome yield, labeled tokens, optimizer slots, fit/mount failures,
and subsequent typed behavior by arm and cut. A larger admitted corpus can be
a legitimate pathway through which parenting helps this architecture, but it
is not automatically evidence of a domain-general learning disposition.

This rung tests prospective learning and thought-to-action pedagogy. It does
not establish connected or compressed parametric knowledge.

## E2 — authentic connected experience is carried parametrically

Question: does a fixed-capacity LoRA carry and behaviorally expose a connected
experiential representation beyond storing isolated facts?

Use a separate controlled world with ground-truth dependency paths and a
prospectively fixed accepted semantic corpus. Hold the thinker, read protocol,
accepted semantics, exposure, and evaluation goals fixed while changing only
the carrier:

1. `ATOMS_TEXT`: accepted atomic experience in explicit text, no links;
2. `LINKED_TEXT`: the same atoms plus admitted use links/shortcuts;
3. `ATOMS_LORA`: matched atomic corpus in the fixed-rank adapter;
4. `LINKED_LORA`: matched linked corpus in the same fixed-rank adapter;
5. adapter-off, wrong-life, endpoint-binding-shuffled, and link-deranged
   controls.

Required endpoints:

- atomic retention remains above its preregistered floor;
- held-out goals require at least two authentic experiential relations and
  cannot be answered by one stored leaf or identifier cue;
- linked minus atoms improves path-composition at matched semantic exposure;
- adapter cut/swap removes the LoRA-carried effect;
- link/binding corruption removes the relevant effect without changing
  marginal record content; and
- explicit-text and LoRA conditions use the same read plan, so a substrate
  difference is not actually a protocol difference.

Passing supports useful parametric transport of connected experience. It does
not yet support *compression*.

## E3 — compression as a rate--distortion result

Question: does the system preserve useful structure while discarding detail
under a genuinely bounded representation, rather than merely storing another
copy?

Predeclare a total accounting boundary including adapter bytes, any retained
external state needed for reads, codebooks/grammars, prompts, indices, and
per-query candidate scans. The immutable raw ledger may be retained for audit
but cannot be counted as part of a supposedly compressed acting state.

Construct a deterministic `EXPANDED_EQUIVALENT` representation of the same
accepted semantics. Sweep lifetime/semantic load while holding LoRA rank and
bytes fixed. Report:

- retained task distortion versus total acting-state bits;
- atomic detail retention separately from structural/path utility;
- fixed-capacity crossover against the expanded text equivalent;
- candidate-scan and retrieval-compute growth; and
- at least three points beyond the text baseline's native-capacity boundary.

Compression requires useful distortion at a shorter total code or sublinear
retained growth under the registered accounting boundary. Fixed rank alone,
summarization, deleting details, or retaining a full searchable ledger is not
compression evidence. A fresh feasibility adjudication additionally shows that
the current all-layer rank-8 Qwen2.5-7B adapter has a 40.37 MB bf16 tensor floor
and the existing PEFT artifact is 80.79 MB fp32 before auxiliary state, so an
`8x` 16k-context expanded carrier cannot plausibly cross the proposed `<.50`
byte-rate threshold. Run the model-free physical crossover receipt in
`research_notes/56_pcfl_compression_feasibility_adjudication_v1.md` before any
E3 model/GPU spend. Until that crossover is feasible, keep semantic-code
compression and LoRA transport as separate claims.

The completed partial intercept is
`research_loop/advisory/20260907_pcfl_physical_intercept_receipt_v4.md`; its
fresh final audit passes the serialized-adapter numerator/intercept only. It
does not supply raw/canonical/expanded denominators, complete state accounting,
or actual rates, so the complete E3 gate and positive spend remain closed.

## E4 — goal-conditioned traversal

Question: does a new goal cause the agent to traverse and extend the relevant
portion of its own experiential structure?

At a frozen cut, give a fresh goal whose solution needs a previously unneeded
multi-edge path. Require public typed operations such as query, follow,
hypothesize, predict, revise, and backtrack; do not score private
chain-of-thought. Compare authentic links with preregistered degree/type/token-
matched derangements and with no-follow/atoms-only conditions.

Required evidence:

- goal-conditioned queries change which authentic nodes are returned;
- traversed cited paths precede the released action;
- breaking the necessary path lowers held-out action value while preserving
  marginal content and budgets; and
- success cannot be reconstructed by a fixed exhaustive controller, hidden
  verifier, or answer-bearing identifier.

This establishes functional traversal, not autonomous knowledge expansion.

## E5 — the expansion relay and flywheel

Question: can existing experience change which information the agent seeks,
can the resulting public outcome become new accepted knowledge, and can that
knowledge improve a still-later goal?

Register a three-time relay:

```text
t0: a goal exposes a specific knowledge gap
 -> existing memory/LoRA changes the chosen experiment at t1
 -> the public world returns a previously unavailable outcome
 -> DREAM/SLEEP admits and writes that outcome
 -> a disjoint goal at t2 benefits
```

Required controls:

- write-off: conduct the same t1 action but do not consolidate its outcome;
- sham gap: replace the memory signal while matching action opportunity;
- binding-shuffle: preserve outcome marginals but break action--outcome
  causality;
- reachout-off: preserve the old memory but deny the new experiment;
- wrong-life/cross-root adapter; and
- strong active-text memory with equal tools, token budget, and public
  outcomes.

The t2 target must be hidden at t0/t1, and the t1 action must not directly
contain its answer. The mediator chain is scored explicitly: gap detection,
experiment selection, valid new outcome, admitted write, later read/use, and
final value. Missing any link blocks the flywheel claim.

## E6 — saturation and increasing lifetime

Question: does the full learner continue improving where a strong active-text
agent reaches a local plateau?

Use at least three prospectively fixed checkpoints after `R0` first meets the
registered local-plateau definition. Report root-level slopes and uncertainty,
not noisy pointwise second differences. Match generated tokens and ordinary
deployment affordances; report model calls, accepted supervised tokens,
optimizer work, wall time, GPU time, storage, and retrieval compute.

Allowed language is *registered local plateau on this task distribution*.
Neither a finite horizon nor one saturated controller establishes intrinsic or
universal baseline saturation.

## Paper-level interpretations

| highest passing rung | defensible paper statement |
|---|---|
| E0 | writer/instrument and self-training failure study |
| E1 | one-to-one parenting improves parent-absent per-life learning |
| E2 | connected experiential semantics can be transported and used through a per-life LoRA |
| E3 | useful connected experience is compressed under the registered total-state accounting |
| E4 | the agent performs functional goal-conditioned experiential traversal |
| E5 | an experience-driven action expands knowledge that improves a later goal—the flywheel |
| E6 | the flywheel persists with lifetime beyond a strong baseline's registered local plateau |

The intended Dream--LoRA--Think thesis requires E0 through E6. The ICLR
one-parent paper can remain valuable at E1, but it must be titled and claimed
as parenting/per-life learning unless the later mechanism rungs actually run
and pass. Conversely, E2--E6 without E1 would test experiential memory and
self-learning but not the parenting contribution. The two arcs are related;
neither may stand in as evidence for the other.

E2, E4, and E5 need not be three unrelated gyms. A single controlled,
single-agent compositional-relay benchmark can contain four sealed phases:
own-action acquisition of atomic relations, fresh-goal traversal, a missing-
edge information-seeking action, and a still-later goal that needs the new
edge. Authentic/deranged/bridge-cut/write-off/same-corpus-text conditions can
then support the three distinct endpoints inside one benchmark family. E3
still needs its extra denotational-equivalence and total-code accounting; the
relay does not become a compression experiment merely because its memory is
small. The current causally repaired proposal is
`research_notes/58_one_child_pcfl_relay_v2_executable_contract.md`. Fresh
review passes it narrowly for entry into formal architecture deliberation but
rejects execution as written until its remaining timing, carrier, controller,
control, and statistics choices are adjudicated and ratified. It remains
proposal-only and cannot enlarge or delay the parenting headline.

## Information-gain order

1. Finish E0 writer truth before spending on any scientific life.
2. Run the smallest E1 parent-absent practice canary; stop if behavior does
   not transfer.
3. Run a spending pilot of the exact E1 root packet and validate `R0`.
4. Launch E1 fixed-N confirmation only after the pilot and resource gate.
5. In parallel on CPU, freeze E2/E3 semantics, code-length accounting, and
   interventions; do not put their GPU spend on the E1 critical path.
6. Execute E2/E3 only when E1's writer/carrier is stable.
7. Execute E4 before E5; if authentic traversal has no causal effect, the
   expansion relay lacks a tested mechanism.
8. Extend lifetime for E6 only if earlier curves show headroom and the strong
   baseline's local-plateau condition is met prospectively.
