# GYM SURVEY v1 — external environments that could serve the curriculum (2026-09-10, Fable)

Inputs: `CURRICULUM_DRAFT_v1.md` (stages 0–7, traits, exit criteria) and `NEXT_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN_v1.md` section 5 (deployment gym llvm-v0; childhood gyms LockGym and rule game v2; the A0 headroom check; split hygiene). Method: five searchers (text adventures; reasoning/puzzle; tool/web/software; optimisation/scientific; curriculum/meta-RL), 59 candidate entries (56 unique environments), two independent vetters scoring every entry 0–10 with a role (classroom / transfer gym / both / reject). This note adds a third pass: live PyPI metadata for 28 packages, three disputes settled by running code in the surviving Python 3.12 scratch venv, and a Monte Carlo of LockGym's chance floor. No WebFetch was used anywhere in the run.

Yardsticks used throughout: the runner does ~22 situations per GPU-hour at the 16-chunk budget (design section 8); <= 20k generated tokens per episode is comfortable, 50k+ roughly halves throughput. "A0 check" = the design's saturation guard: ceiling minus frozen-model mean >= 0.10 and >= 3 x noise-band SD over 8 seeded frozen reps. "Held out by source" = the exam draws from a generator family, item family or dataset the training split never touched, not merely from new seeds.

**Three flags for the in-house gyms (section 4):**
1. LockGym training tier (length 4 over 1–6) is Mastermind (c=4, n=6): MastermindEval solve rates within 12 guesses are Qwen2.5-7B 0.04, Llama-3.1-8B 0.07, R1-Distill-7B 0.03. The admission rule "lock-open rate >= 0.5 of situations" is at risk; measure it in the D1 band first.
2. The partial-credit score `max exact/L` has a chance floor of 0.50 at 12 guesses on the training tier and 0.40 on the exam tier (Monte Carlo below); a consistent guesser opens every lock in 4.6 / 5.9 guesses. Report lock-open rate and partial credit separately, against these two references, not against 0 and 1.
3. The A0 check itself is not at risk on either tier (the frozen model sits near the chance floor, far from the ceiling); the risk is a floor effect on the exam tier, where every 7B/8B in MastermindEval scored 0.00–0.03 at the easier (c=5, n=7).

**Install first (CPU, section 6):** TextWorld pool + Minesweeper hook ≈ 1 engineering day together and give the one-repeat rule a "changed gym" for Stage 1 and Stage 2; reasoning-gym probe ≈ 2 h; Alchemy + XLand ≈ 1.5 days; ScienceWorld + gcc-v0 ≈ 1 day. GPU banding: 8 seeded frozen reps x 24–40 exam instances per gym.

## 1. Criteria (as scored) and how the scoring worked

| # | criterion | operational test used here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | VERIFIABLE | exact ground-truth scoring from the environment; any LLM judge in the scoring path disqualifies the metric (a gym may keep judge-free metrics only) |
| 2 | HEADROOM for a 7B instruct model | a published 7B/8B zero-shot number well below a demonstrated ceiling (frontier or RL-trained 7B); "floor" (< ~5%) flagged as no learning signal; "saturated" flagged when scaffolded/RL 7B is near 100% |
| 3 | TEXT-INTERACTIVE, turn-based | one observation in, one action out, so PREDICT / ACT / NOTE / RECALL lines map onto turns; single-turn generators need a retry wrapper and are marked as such |
| 4 | TARGET-BLIND (classroom) or by-source split (transfer gym) | no compiler/LLVM/pass vocabulary for classrooms (leak scan at seal); transfer gyms must have low pretraining saturation and an exam drawn from a family/source the training split never used |
| 5 | TRAIT | which of persistence, re-planning after failure, calibration, exploration before commitment, contrast/generalisation, goal-setting the gym instils or measures, and which stage it serves |
| 6 | PROCEDURAL or large pool | seeded generation or >= thousands of instances, with a clean train/exam partition |
| 7 | INSTALL COST | Linux node, Python 3.10–3.12, CPU-only on the gym side; licence; maintenance (last release/commit) |
| 8 | EPISODE LENGTH | steps and tokens per episode against ~22 episodes/h/GPU |
| + | pedigree | prior use in curriculum, meta-RL or agent-learning papers (e.g. XLand-MiniGrid in Pappalardo, ICLR 2026) |

Vetter roles: V1 scored fit to the curriculum stages and instruments; V2 re-verified install claims, licences, dates and headline numbers (PyPI, raw LICENSE files, GitHub Atom feeds, paper PDFs, live installs in a Python 3.12 venv). Where they disagree by >= 2 points the reason is stated in the table; every disagreement I could settle by running code is settled in section 8.

Verification levels used in the tables: **[V]** verified by a vetter or in this pass (install run, file read, PDF table, PyPI JSON); **[S]** searcher-reported from a README/paper and not independently re-checked.

## 2. Recommended classroom set — detail

### C1. TextWorld: tw-cooking (levels 1–5) and tw-treasure_hunter — Stage 1 persistence

- **What.** Microsoft's procedural text-game generator (Inform7 backend). `tw-make tw-cooking --recipe 1..3 --take 1..2 --go {1,6,9,12} --split train|valid|test --seed S` generates a kitchen quest with a recipe to find and execute; wrong cooking method or dropped ingredient fails the game; `tw-treasure_hunter --level 1..30` adds locked doors/containers and keys. Gym-like API with admissible commands, `score`, `max_score`, `won/lost`, `max_episode_steps`.
- **Verifiable.** Engine-tracked score (one point per correctly processed ingredient), won/lost flags. No judge. [V]
- **7B headroom.** TALES (100-step cap, minimal scaffolding, cooking L1–10, normalised 0–100): Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct 27.7, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 29.7, Llama-3.1-70B 65.6, Claude-3.7-Sonnet 97.3 [V, vetter 2 read the rows]. BALROG TextWorld (treasure hunter/cooking/coin, 20 seeds each): GPT-4o 39.3%, Llama-3.1-70B ~15%, GPT-4o-mini 12.3% [S]. Ceiling demonstrably reachable; frozen 7B far from it.
- **Trait and instrument.** Persistence and re-planning: dead ends, locked doors, irreversible failures; dense exact score so quitting early cannot score. Exit readouts come straight from `efficiency_markers.py` v2 action rows: actions per situation >= 0.7 x frozen median, attempts after a non-improving outcome >= 2, backtracks; add a parser-loop flag (identical invalid command repeated) because invalid-command loops are the documented 7B failure mode (BALROG adds `feedback_on_invalid_action`).
- **Split.** Verified in `cooking.py`: `FOODS_SPLITS` and `FOOD_PREPARATIONS_SPLITS` partition food items (adjective-noun pairs) and preparations into train/valid/test, **but the test split also admits train foods**, so a strictly held-out exam pool must filter generated test games to those whose recipe contains >= 1 test-only item; add a disjoint seed range and, for treasure hunter, a held-out level band. This is a by-item-family holdout, weaker than a by-source split, and should be labelled as such.
- **Budget.** BALROG caps 80 steps (cooking) / 40 (treasure hunter); L1 needs ~7 optimal moves, L10 ~44. Observations 100–250 tokens. Estimated 5–15k tokens per L1–5 episode at a 150–300-token thinking block per step; 15–40k at L6–10 [S]. Keep L1–5 in the childhood.
- **Install.** `pip install textworld==1.7.0` (released 2026-01-30; manylinux + macOS wheels; Python >= 3.9; needs `build-essential libffi-dev` only if building) [V, PyPI]. MIT, bundling Inform7 (Artistic 2.0), Jericho (GPLv2), Fast Downward (GPLv3) — matters only for redistribution. Repo active (CI commits Sep 2026) [V]. Game compilation is seconds per game: pre-generate seeded pools offline.
- **Pedigree.** First TextWorld Problems competition (NeurIPS 2018); curriculum learning on cooking games — Yin & May 2019, arXiv 1908.04777 (vetter 1's correction: the searcher attributed it to Adolphs & Hofmann, which is LeDeepChef); TALES 2025; BALROG (ICLR 2025).
- **Caveats.** Cooking vocabulary is in pretraining (FTWP 2018–19) though every instance is fresh; strict parser; GPL components in the wheel; no published 7B numbers specifically for treasure hunter/coin collector.
- **Why it beats LockGym on persistence.** Dense graded score with a real ceiling, unlimited fresh instances, several difficulty families, and published 7B numbers to band against; LockGym remains the purest "no descriptors at all" gym and stays in the set.

### C2. Minesweeper (TextArena / GEM) — Stage 2 prediction and calibration, with monotone persistence

- **What.** Partially observable grid deduction: reveal/flag cells; numbers give adjacent mine counts; first click safe; fail on a mine. TextArena `Minesweeper-v0` defaults rows=8, cols=8, num_mines=10, max_turns=100 [V, source read today]; GEM `Minesweeper-v0-easy` 5x5 / 5 mines / 25 turns and `-hard` 8x8 / 12 mines / 64 turns with dense reward = share of safe cells newly revealed [V, searcher ran live].
- **Verifiable.** Exact against the hidden layout. TextArena stores the hidden grid in `state.game_state["grid"]` (−1 = mine) [V], so a harness hook can score a stated probability per turn with no change to the game.
- **7B headroom.** LaMer (ICLR 2026), Qwen3-4B non-thinking, 6x6 with 3 mines: zero-shot pass@1 4.5% / pass@3 8.6%; ReAct 6.3/10.9; PPO 29.7/35.5; GRPO 36.3/40.4; GiGPO 52.0/55.1; LaMer 44.1/74.4. Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct: GiGPO 39.1/42.2, LaMer 47.7/65.6 (zero-shot base not reported) [V]. Harder boards degrade every method.
- **Trait and instrument.** The same reveal action yields a different outcome by context, and the stated probability is exactly checkable — the rule-game quiz's 1 − Brier instrument transfers verbatim: `PREDICT P(mine)=p` before each reveal, scored per turn; exit = (1 − Brier) over the stage's last two sleeps exceeds the first two by >= 1 band SD and per-item confidence SD >= 0.05. Dense safe-reveal reward keeps the persistence payoff monotone over 25–64 turns. Report per-turn safe-reveal accuracy and invalid-action (coordinate/format) rate separately: forced guesses add irreducible luck and coordinate errors dominate small-model failures.
- **Split.** Procedural seeds; exam on a board size / mine density never trained (e.g. train 6x6/6 and 8x8/10, exam 7x7/9) plus a disjoint seed range.
- **Budget.** 8–64 turns; ~1k tokens of board per turn plus thoughts → 5–15k tokens per episode on 8x8 [S].
- **Install.** `pip install textarena==0.7.4` (2025-10-16; Python >= 3.10; MIT; repo commits through 2026-08-19) [V]; the `openai` dependency is used only for online play. GEM: install from source (`gem-llm` 0.1.0 on PyPI is stale and pins `reasoning-gym==0.1.23`, which conflicts with 0.1.25 — use a separate venv) [V]. Only 4 of TextArena's 111 envs call an LLM (Debate, GuessWho, ScenarioPlanning, TwentyQuestions) — not this one [S].
- **Pedigree.** LaMer (ICLR 2026, meta-RL exploration), ORBIT cross-episode meta-RL (arXiv 2602.04089, Feb 2026), Paprika (ICML 2025) task group, Li et al. NAACL 2024.
- **Caveats.** LaMer's numbers are Qwen3-4B, not 7B; first-click safety means mine placement depends on the first move in some implementations (Paprika notes this complicates preference data) — record the seed and first move.

### C3. DeepMind Alchemy, symbolic version — Stage 2/3 prediction under a hidden causal structure; explore then exploit

- **What.** Meta-RL benchmark (NeurIPS 2021 D&B). Each episode samples a latent "chemistry": 3 stones (3 perceptual features + a value) and 12 potions per trial; a potion moves a stone along a hidden graph with a hidden feature mapping; cauldroning a stone pays its value. Default 10 trials x 20 steps; the agent must experiment early and exploit late. Symbolic observation is a 39-dim vector; 40 discrete actions [V, vetter 2 ran 3 trials x 12 steps]. Level families: no_bottleneck, bottleneck1/2/3, perceptual_mapping_randomized_with_rotation.
- **Verifiable.** Exact reward; the harness can read the true chemistry (`see_chemistries`) to score transformation predictions exactly. The repo ships an **ideal observer** — a per-episode exact ceiling, which is precisely what the A0 check wants instead of a nominal 1.0 [S for the observer's API, V for its presence].
- **7B headroom.** No LLM baseline exists anywhere (searched). Random policy return 11.0 in the default level, seed 0 [S]. Band with 8 frozen reps; the ideal observer supplies the ceiling.
- **Trait and instrument.** "Same potion, different effect by chemistry" is the Stage 2 shape with no spatial text (3 stones x 3 named features, 12 potion colours — avoids the egocentric-grid weakness of small models). Instruments: `PREDICT` the stone's transformation before each potion → exact hit rate and Brier; per-trial return within an episode, with post-adaptation return (mean over the last trials) minus trial-1 return as the ULEE-style learning-to-learn readout; first-action JS shift between sleeps for Stage 3.
- **Split.** Chemistry resampled per seed within a level family; exam on a held-out level family (e.g. train no_bottleneck + bottleneck1, exam bottleneck3 with randomized perceptual mapping) plus a seed range.
- **Budget.** Recommended 3–5 trials x <= 12 steps = 40–60 turns, ~6–10k tokens [S]; default 200 steps is too long.
- **Install.** `git clone google-deepmind/dm_alchemy`; on Python 3.12 setup.py fails on `import imp` (removed in 3.12) — patch to `importlib.util` (two lines, diff in section 6); then `pip install grpcio-tools 'setuptools<70' wheel && pip install --no-build-isolation ./dm_alchemy` [V, wheel built in the scratch venv today]. Pulls dm-env, dm-env-rpc, dm-tree and the `docker` Python client (no daemon needed for the symbolic env). Apache-2.0 [V]. Repo archived 2024-06-08 (last commit 2023-04-05) [V]. Renderer ~100 lines.
- **Pedigree.** Alchemy (Wang et al. 2021); model-based meta-RL with transformers + tree search (arXiv 2208.11535). No LLM-agent paper.
- **Caveats.** Archived; patch must be vendored; no LLM numbers; flat-vector observation needs the renderer. Vetter split (7.5 vs 5) is entirely about maintenance and install, not fit — both call the fit excellent.

### C4. XLand-MiniGrid with an ECHO-style text wrapper — Stage 3/7 learning-to-learn under hidden rules (conditional)

- **What.** JAX meta-RL gridworld. A task = ruleset (goal + production rules + init tiles), hidden from the agent by default and discoverable only through trials; object positions re-randomised every reset; env ids `XLand-MiniGrid-R1-9x9` … `R9-25x25`; 6 actions; observation 5x5x2 (tile id, colour id) egocentric; `max_steps = 3*H*W` (243 at 9x9). Pre-sampled benchmarks trivial-21k, small-1m, medium-1m/3m, high-1m/3m (`xminigrid.registered_benchmarks()`), plus `scripts/ruleset_generator.py`; `benchmark.shuffle().split(prop)` for train/test [V, vetter 2 ran reset/step on CPU].
- **Verifiable.** Exact goal-predicate reward; ruleset id + seed determine the task; the hidden rules are readable by the harness for leak-free scoring of predictions [V].
- **7B headroom.** No LLM numbers on the standard benchmarks. ECHO (GPT-4o only) reports +80% relative reward over ReAct on its stateful variant [S]. RL2 recurrent PPO mean return ~0.6–0.7 on medium with a 20th percentile near 0–0.2 [S]. BALROG's text BabyAI analogue (Llama-3.1-8B 15–30%, Qwen2.5-7B 7.8–14%) says egocentric text navigation alone is hard for a 7B — floor risk, hence the condition.
- **Trait and instrument.** Hidden rules → hypothesis, prediction, surprise as evidence (Stage 2); exploration vs exploitation across K trials on one ruleset (Stage 3); contrast across ruleset families (Stage 4); in-context learning-to-learn (Stage 7 analogue). Instrument: return per trial k and post-adaptation return over the last trials of a K-trial meta-episode (ULEE uses the last 10 of 20–30 episodes; the XLand paper uses 25 trials); for a 7B, K = 3–5 trials x <= 40 steps.
- **Split.** By ruleset id range and by benchmark tier (train trivial-21k, exam small-1m) — the cleanest by-source split among the classrooms.
- **Budget.** 3–5 trials x 30–40 steps = 100–200 LLM turns at ~100–150 tokens each = 15–30k tokens per meta-episode — the upper end of the budget; only the low end fits 22 episodes/h [S].
- **Install.** `pip install xminigrid==0.9.3` (2025-12-16; Python >= 3.9; jax/jaxlib >= 0.4.27, flax, chex; CPU JAX wheel installs in 34 s on Python 3.12) [V]. Apache-2.0 [V]. Benchmarks download from HuggingFace (trivial-21k is 135 KB; larger tiers 38–579 MB) [V/S]. Text wrapper: port ECHO's `obs_to_text` / indexed valid actions / stateful reset (MIT; requires Python >= 3.11,< 3.13) [V]; ~300 lines; the goal must stay hidden for our use (ECHO verbalises it).
- **Pedigree.** XLand-MiniGrid (NeurIPS 2024 D&B); XLand-100B in-context RL dataset (ICLR 2025); **ULEE — Pappalardo, ICLR 2026** (unsupervised meta-learning with self-imposed-goal curricula on 4Rooms-Trivial/Small and 6Rooms-Small at 13x13; code MIT); ECHO hindsight-rewriting LM agents (arXiv 2510.10304).
- **Caveats.** No official language interface (roadmap issue #1 unfinished); spatial text is a 7B weak spot; repo quiet since 2025-12-16; meta-episodes must be capped hard.

### C5. reasoning-gym with a multi-attempt wrapper — Stage 4 contrast and the evaluator classroom

- **What.** 106 procedural generators with algorithmic verifiers in 11 categories, a coaching module (`BaseCurriculum`, `ScoreBoard`, per-task Curriculum classes) and difficulty parameters per task. NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight (arXiv 2505.24760). Angle-relevant tasks: sokoban, rush_hour, n_queens, futoshiki, kakurasu, mini_sudoku, tower_of_hanoi, knights_knaves, zebra_puzzles, acre (Blicket causal rule induction — the closest published analogue to rule game v2), arc_1d / rearc, countdown.
- **Verifiable.** Exact `score_answer`. Settled today: the four tasks searcher 2 called broken (rush_hour, rubiks_cube, boxnet, propositional_logic) have `answer=None` by design and verify a proposed solution by simulation; a wrong string scores 0.00–0.05 [V]. The other 32 probed tasks score the reference 1.00 [V, searcher 2].
- **7B headroom.** No 7B numbers published (smallest evaluated: Gemma-3-27B 20.3% on hard configs; o3-mini 63.5%); RL baselines on Qwen2.5-3B: Games 0.0 → 3.3%, Algebra 5.0 → 16.7 [S]. Same-genre 7B numbers exist elsewhere (Countdown 31.7%, K&K 0.19 avg, Sokoban ~6%, ZebraLogic 12.8%, section 5).
- **Trait and instrument.** Contrast/generalisation across families with natural by-family holdout; calibration via a confidence per answer (Brier); the paper shows curriculum beat fixed difficulty (+40.67% on spell_backward level 4, +13.33% mini_sudoku). Instrument: per-family accuracy on held-out families (the disjoint-panel Δ analogue), curriculum level reached per family, Brier.
- **Split.** Procedural, seeded, unlimited; hold out whole families.
- **Budget.** Measured mean prompt sizes: countdown ~105 tokens, sokoban/mini_sudoku/knights_knaves ~110, acre ~325, zebra ~335, rearc ~470 (max 3.2k chars), arc_agi ~680 (max 6k chars) [V, searcher 2 measured]; 10 attempts → 3–6k tokens for small tasks, 10–20k for ARC.
- **Install.** `pip install reasoning-gym==0.1.25` (2026-03-28; Python >= 3.10; pure Python; resolves on 3.12) [V]. Apache-2.0; 1.5k stars; last commit 2026-04-17 [V].
- **Pedigree.** NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight; GEM (ICLR 2026) wraps `rg:*` tasks; SynLogic and Reasoning Core interoperate; cited by curriculum-RL papers.
- **Caveats.** Single-turn by construction — the retry wrapper (attempt → verifier feedback → retry) is ~80 lines, and its persistence signal is blind retry rather than LockGym-style contradiction-driven re-planning unless tasks with partial credit or simulation feedback are chosen; exclude `codeio` / `list_functions` (program vocabulary) for target-blindness; several named genres (sudoku, hanoi, K&K) are pretraining-familiar though instances are fresh.

## 3. Transfer gyms — detail

### T1. ScienceWorld — far transfer to a different world

- **What.** Text simulator of elementary-science tasks: 30 tasks over 10 topics (boil/melt/freeze, thermometry, conductivity, living/non-living, plant growth, chemistry mixing, lifespan, life stages, inclined planes, Mendelian genetics), 7,207 parametric variations; score 0–100 from required plus optional subgoals; valid-action list; simplifications (`teleportAction`, `openDoors`, `openContainers`, `selfWateringFlowerPots`, `noElectricalAction` = the "easy" preset).
- **Verifiable.** Exact subgoal score and success flag [V].
- **7B headroom.** AgentGym-RL Table 5: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct 1.50 overall (Measure 1.89, Test-Cond 0, Find 0, Chem-Mix 0, Lifespan 13.33), Llama-3.1-8B 4.00, GPT-4o 21.0, o3 41.5; RL-trained 7B 50.5 (AgentGym-RL-7B) and 57.0 (ScalingInter-7B) [V, PDF]. TALES (100-step cap): Llama-3.1-8B 15.7, Qwen2.5-7B 12.6, Claude-3.5 82.3 [V]. ETO: Llama-2-7B-Chat 3.8/3.1 seen/unseen → SFT 67.4/53.0 → ETO 73.8/65.0 [S]. Original paper DRRN 0.17 test. Huge trainable headroom; the frozen floor is above zero only with the valid-action list and the easy preset — band it.
- **Trait and instrument.** Long-horizon persistence, re-planning after irreversible failures, contrast across families with unseen critical objects; CLIN (continual-learning language agent, +23 points over Reflexion, transfers to new tasks) is the published precedent for continual learning here. Instrument: subgoal-score learning curve on held-out variations, first and second derivatives vs adapter-OFF / text-memory / harness baselines (the finals' form), with an HC-style normalisation (score − frozen)/(reference − frozen) using the RL-7B 50–57 as the reference ceiling.
- **Split (by source).** The official variation split (50/25/25) holds out critical objects — unseen substances, animals, plants — in test [S, paper]; on top of that hold out whole task families (e.g. train on 20 tasks, exam on 10 never played), which is the by-source form the design requires. Verify identifier normalisation on both sides (task name + variation index) before calling the split clean.
- **Budget.** 100-step cap in the paper and TALES; observations 150–600 tokens → 30–80k tokens per full episode (halves throughput); with the easy preset and short tasks (find-*, boil, measure) 10–30 steps → 10–25k tokens [S].
- **Install.** `pip install scienceworld==1.2.3` (2025-07-13; pure-Python wheel + JAR; Python >= 3.7, 3.12 confirmed via TALES) + Java 1.8+ (`openjdk-8-jre-headless` or newer) + py4j [V, PyPI]. Apache-2.0. Repo active (commits Aug 2026) but **main carries an unreleased 1.3.0 fix that makes object enumeration deterministic and changes physics trajectories and gold paths vs 1.2.x** — pin 1.2.3 and the JVM version in the manifest [S].
- **Caveats.** ScienceWorld trajectories are in public SFT corpora (AgentGym AgentTraj, ETO data): exposure to the gym, not to the held-out variations — disclose under contamination rule (b). One JVM per environment instance; simulator is slow; long observations inflate tokens.

### T2. CompilerGym gcc-v0 with csmith-v0 — near transfer inside the domain (deployment only, never a classroom)

- **What.** GCC's command-line optimisation space as an RL environment: ~104,461 configurations (6 -O levels, 242 binary flags, 260 parametric options) as 2,281 discrete actions; observations include source/asm/obj and instruction counts; rewards `asm_size`, `obj_size`. |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

What follows for the design (each a check, not a change):
1. **Admission rule at risk.** "A seed deploys iff … the lock-open rate >= 0.5 of situations" (design 3.8). A frozen 7B opens ~4–7% of (4,6) locks in 12 guesses; a schooled 7B must reach 50% within 16. Read the lock-open rate in the D1 frozen band; if it is < 0.2, either soften the training tier to (3, 1–5) (Qwen2.5-7B 0.18, Llama 0.31) or lower the admission threshold before PREREG v1 — the reach-quiz rate has the same exposure but no published analogue.
2. **Report two numbers, against two references.** Partial credit sits at a 0.50 chance floor on the training tier (0.40 on the exam tier) and any consistent guesser hits 1.0 — so a schooled child's gain must be read as (score − chance) / (1 − chance), and lock-open rate reported beside it. A 30-line random-guess and consistent-guess reference on CPU is the cheapest A0 companion the design lacks.
3. **A0 is safe; the exam floor is not.** With the frozen model near chance and the ceiling 1.0, "ceiling − mean >= 0.10 and >= 3 SD" passes on both tiers. The exam tier (5, 1–8) is harder than (5,7), where every 7B/8B is 0.00–0.03: lock-opens will be ~0 events for frozen and schooled alike and the far-transfer exam will discriminate only on partial credit above 0.40. Consider (5, 1–6) or (4, 1–8) for the exam tier, or accept a partial-credit-only exam and say so.
4. **A published "learning across episodes" reference.** ORBIT (arXiv 2602.04089): Qwen3-8B on 3-digit / 6-symbol / 3-guess Mastermind, three episodes in context: ~0.27 → 0.59 after cross-episode meta-RL (single-episode RL 0.21). Useful as the external yardstick for the F-series' "second derivative".
5. **Repeated-guess and echo diagnostics.** RAGEN documents the "echo trap" (fixed phrasing, reward-variance collapse) on Sokoban — the same failure as SEQ-003/005 — so the identical-guess-after-non-improving-feedback flag should be reported per window, as the design already lists.

### Rule game v2 — closest published analogues (for "stage repeats with a changed gym")

- reasoning-gym `acre` (Blicket-detector causal rule induction; ~325-token prompts; exact) — the only maintained procedural analogue of compound-rule induction.
- Paprika Cellular Automata (infer a 1-D elementary CA rule from input/output pairs; hypothesised rule's outputs returned; 6 turns; Llama-3.1-8B best-of-20 73.7%) — rule prediction with exact feedback; reimplement (no licence file).
