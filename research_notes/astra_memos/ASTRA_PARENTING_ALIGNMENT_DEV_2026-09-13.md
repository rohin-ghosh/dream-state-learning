# Fixed-lesson alignment: inference-only developmental diagnostic

Prospective September 13, 2026. Status: design/CPU implementation; no native
outputs inspected for this assay. Main owns native authorization and allocation.
This adopts only the formation/steering question from
`research_notes/analysis/2026-09-13_minimum_level1_parenting_receptivity.md`.
Its proposed writer, replay recipe and one-SLEEP thresholds are NOT adopted.

## Decision and question

SEQ158 found no incremental held benefit from fixed coaching in three learners;
both arms improved record formation after writing, with retention losses.
SEQ159 finds a replay/recall trade-off, not an all-seed mechanism repair.
Do not run another unchanged coaching pair or increase write dose to rescue
these results. Instead ask whether an answer-free, task-aligned process lesson
changes immediate processing and produces faithful child-authored experience
records more than the same lessons swapped between task types.

This is a bounded diagnosis of responsiveness while the child's restatement
is available. It does not test amortization, persistent parenting, autonomous
learning, H1/H2, or a mechanism freeze. Zero fits and zero optimizer updates.

## Arms, parents and fixed budgets

Use the original pre-memory perception adapters, seeds 0/1/2. No coaching,
memory, replay-repair, compiler or deployment descendants may initialize this
assay. These are exposed exploratory development roots, not final clean children.
Bind existing parent tensor inventories, source/code/model/tokenizer identities,
and prior experiment namespace inventory before native execution.

Three independent inference states per seed: ALIGNED, SWAPPED and NO_PARENT.
Sixteen application tasks in four blocks of four, plus one restatement per
block in each lesson arm. At most one pre-action and one post-outcome record
call per task: 36/36/32 calls, 104 per seed and 312 across three seeds.
Invalid actions yield no invented world receipt or replacement record call.
Preserve failures, rejected records, missing fields, finish reasons and uncalled
slots. No resampling, selection of good roots, retries, dose escalation or fits.
Zero adaptive parent-model calls. Runtime at most 3600 controller seconds and
180 collection seconds per seed; aggregate allocation cap five A40-hours.

## Intervention and environmental evidence

Two fixed answer-free lessons: P separates an earlier prediction from its actual
outcome and compares them; C selects the latest executed public event and binds
action/outcome from that same receipt. Neither lesson contains task values,
task IDs, answers, hidden rules, evaluator labels or outputs from another arm.

Seeds 0/2 receive P-C-P-C task families; seed 1 C-P-C-P. Each lesson arm gets
the same two lesson strings twice, in the same task order with identical task
bytes. ALIGNED receives the relevant lesson; SWAPPED the other lesson.
NO_PARENT receives the same tasks without contact/restatement. It is a behavioral
anchor, not a token-matched treatment. Fixed lesson multisets match; realized
restatement lengths, contexts, trajectories and total inference tokens may differ
and must be reported. Counterbalance structurally matched first/second family
blocks for seed 1 before output inspection.

The child restates a lesson, then application receives that child's raw
restatement but not the fixed lesson itself. No evaluator corrects, filters or
rewrites a restatement. No generic task instruction names the relevant process
operation. Freeze the finite accepted NOTE/ACT/record schemas and all ordinary
format instructions in the CPU manifest before native generation; score
noncanonical valid serialization separately from content and canonical form.

P tasks expose one public earlier event with independently varied prior/outcome;
C tasks expose two public events with chronological order independent of display
order. All public outcomes are generated by the actual frozen CPU environment,
not fabricated as asserted successes. These harness-provided prior events are
not the current child's own actions. A fresh child action then executes once
in that environment; its returned receipt is distinct from the public context.
All child writes remain future candidates only, with immutable raw continuations
and exact experienced-source provenance. No hidden rule or sealed score enters
the child/parent context; no confirmation or deployment tasks are used.

## Readouts and feasibility vector

RESTATE: a deterministic, declared operation/scope checker, not prose approval.
PROCESS_USE: the pre-action NOTE binds task-specific public values: P prior,
observation and their relation; C latest receipt ID and same-receipt action/outcome.
EXECUTED: typed action dispatches once. RECORD_FAITHFUL: the post-outcome record
keeps those public bindings separate from the child's new prediction, action,
outcome and receipt ID. FULL_MATERIAL is their conjunction. Report per-field
errors, canonical form, raw diversity and all failure denominators.

Preserve the proposal's prospective feasibility vector, without any fit trigger:
- ALIGNED restatement correct at least 3/4 contacts in at least two roots.
- ALIGNED minus SWAPPED PROCESS_USE at least 4/16 in at least two roots;
  no root at or below -4/16.
- ALIGNED minus NO_PARENT at least 2/16 in at least two roots;
  no root worse than NO_PARENT by more than 2/16.
- ALIGNED second-delivery performance not lower than first in at least two roots.
- At least two ALIGNED roots produce at least 8/16 FULL_MATERIAL rows.

Report every component, not only the conjunction. Three learner roots are
replication units; correlated tasks are not independent learners. Failure means
this fixed-intervention assay did not establish specific responsiveness, not
that all possible parenting is ineffective. A pass supports only a later
prospective writer decision; it is not automatic qualification or launch.

## Implementation, hygiene and stopping

Reuse frozen world/parser/native-backend and capture lifecycle interfaces.
Beauvoir owns the new CPU core/tests; Parfit the new inference lifecycle/tests;
Main the protocol, integration, allocation, logging and result interpretation.
Pin their final bytes and fixture receipts before launch. Native preparation
must check actual tokenizer/context limits, original adapter/source identity,
GPU/process reservations, current boot, deadline and lease finish margin.
Three cold arm processes per seed with independent mutable state; no fits.
Collectors are once-only and cannot generate or rewrite evidence.

Stop on leakage, source/hash/lineage mismatch, invalid world/parser integration,
changed allocation/boot, deadline, duplicate collection or missing native custody.
Keep each failed attempt immutable. Simple hygiene remains in force; the formal
paper-grade C11 guard remains deferred. Preserve native roots and archive all
receipts before independent CPU reduction. The final code/plan manifests contain
exact prompts, schemas, IDs, token caps, native commands and pins; none is
invented in this prose before implementation.
