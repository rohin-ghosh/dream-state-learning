## Why replay rather than new rollouts

The existing diagnostic slot appends records to the ledger and clears pending
actions; it does not insert those records into later wake state. Re-executing
the long inference-only wake stream merely to test a slot-local coaching change
would duplicate work. Replay tests conversion of recorded experience, not the
interactive experience-formation estimand. Do not label copied source episodes
as newly executed episodes or fabricate existing formation certificates.
