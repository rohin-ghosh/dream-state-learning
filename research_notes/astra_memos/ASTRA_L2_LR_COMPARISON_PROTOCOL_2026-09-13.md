## Question and activation

If saved-adapter diagnostics show weak input-conditional discrimination even
at the exact training prompt, compare the existing LR 3e-5 with LR 1e-4 at
identical exposure. The recovered successful memory writer used 1e-4 but also
different data and other conditions: it motivates this contrast, not a causal
promise. If the diagnostic instead localizes an access-only mismatch, reconsider
this comparison before launch and retain this unexecuted specification.

Prediction: a larger learning rate may enable a usable conditional write at
the current dose. A common action bias, lower total loss, or larger parameter
norm alone does not satisfy that prediction. The null/negative branch remains
informative about this recipe and motivates material/representation diagnosis,
not a conclusion that the research thesis is false.

## Matched design

- Six new immutable roots: learner seeds 0, 1, 2 crossed with LR 3e-5/1e-4.
- Each root retains the original PROMOTE/SHADOW pair, shared first physical
  fit, two wakes, two cumulative sleeps, and original baseline/readout panels.
- Same frozen official Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct files, rank 8/alpha 16, dropout .05,
  seven target projections, AdamW, 20 epochs, batch 8, max length 1024, masks,
  exact admitted child bytes, EOS policy, world and evaluation settings.
- Every fit starts from frozen base with a fresh optimizer; later fits use
  cumulative old+new records. This is not warm-started sequential optimization.
- Vocabulary/truth seeds remain 2026091301/2026091302, inference seed 0. Only
  fit initialization/dropout/shuffling vary with learner seed. This is one
  already-inspected DEV world, not untouched task confirmation or task diversity.
- Both LRs use the same new runtime, differing only in the explicit LR field.
  The low-LR seed-0 rerun is a version/control bridge, not a new independent seed
  beyond the original seed-0 experiment. Do not pool it as a fourth learner.
- Later generated actions/material may differ because policies differ; preserve
  them. The LR contrast estimates the whole declared loop effect, not a pure
  identical-recipient material-quality estimand.
- No teacher text is a SLEEP target, no hidden score enters the child/parent,
  no old root or existing candidate is edited, and no clean ancestry is claimed.

## Metrics and interpretation

Primary: final PROMOTE minus SHADOW correct responses out of 16, reported per
learner seed and LR; then the paired difference of those differences across
LRs. Report all three seed-level values, their mean and range, without treating
the 16 within-learner responses as independent learner replicates.

Also report baseline, first and second reports split into old/new blocks of 8;
paired both/only/neither counts; legal/malformed/truncated output counts;
formation/admission denominators; fitted exposure, losses and elapsed cost.
Retention is reported descriptively, not inferred from unchanged chance scores.
No binary general-learning promotion threshold is added. All failed attempts,
protocol changes and seeds remain visible. Compare teacher-forced diagnostics
separately if later collected; do not replace native generation metrics with them.

## Budget, stop and placement

Per root: at most 128 generation calls, 3 fits, 100 optimizer updates;
5400-second controller including cleanup and separate 180-second collection.
Six-root ceiling: 768 calls, 18 fits, 600 updates, at most 9 controller GPU-hours.
The original single loop took about 23 minutes; approximately 25–40 minutes
per root is a planning estimate, not measured concurrent throughput.

Prospective node3 indices: seed0 low/high 0/1; seed1 low/high 2/4; seed2 low/high
5/6. UUIDs must be discovered and pinned before preparation. GPU3 remains for
the read-only diagnostic; faulty GPU7 is untouched. No allocation is presumed
from an idle-memory snapshot. A targeted compute and same-user environment
check precedes each launch, with bound process ownership and no foreign kills.
Node boot identity and all source/model/plan pins are checked. Finish at least
six hours before the verified node3 lease end; do not change any lease.

Stop on integrity/provenance/ownership failures, nonfinite training, invalid
metrics or controller budget exhaustion. Weak finite learning is an outcome,
not permission to stop a control arm or selectively increase dose. No retries
into an old directory; diagnose infrastructure failures and record new attempts.
Only original pinned runtime performs terminal collection, once per root.
