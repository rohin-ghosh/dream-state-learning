# Decision memo — audit the sleep-write mechanism

**Decision:** Test **cue-conditioned writing and exposure accounting before changing capacity or the read architecture**. Keep the current rank-8 trainer as the first experimental control; compare it with contextual writes using the same child targets. Then test rank 32 on the winning representation. Do not combine Fable’s proposed optimizer, masking, rank, module, and initialization changes into one “improved LoRA” arm.

The present evidence shows **successful acquisition of a textual distribution, including unconditional action habits—not demonstrated conditional memory**. It does not establish that LoRA cannot implement Rohin’s guide.

Two corrections matter immediately:

1. **H4 misstates the implementation.** The supplied trainer already targets attention **and MLP**: `q/k/v/o_proj` and `gate/up/down_proj`. “Add MLP” is not an intervention.
2. **Exact dedup conflicts with exposure-proportional memory.** If 16 occurrences compile to one identical piece, the writer has erased the difference between seeing something once and 16 times. Increasing rank cannot recover a count that never reaches the loss.

## 1. Diagnosis and the decisive measurement

### Ranked hypotheses

| Rank | Hypothesis | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **H1: missing antecedents teach unconditional habits** | **Leading mechanistic explanation, not yet causally established.** A program-name header supplies identity, but not the local state, failed attempt, feedback, or decision fork. Bare next-token training can reward recurring prose and routines without teaching when they apply. The strong ON/OFF ritual difference is consistent with this [M12; supplied piece description]. |
| **2** | **H2: selected experience mostly contains the routine** | **Strongly plausible; “nothing generalizable” is too strong.** Fixed-recipe plateaus and disappearance of contrasts support impoverished behavioral content. But the compiler selects recoveries, contrasts, and surprises as well as wins; its prompts explicitly request conditional principles. We need the *emitted, token-weighted corpus*, not its intentions, to establish what survived [transform excerpt; SEQ-005, SEQ-012]. |
| **3** | **H3: the evaluation cannot reward episodic memory** | **Correct distinction; incomplete explanation.** Identifier-held-out programs cannot test arbitrary facts about previously encountered programs. They can test transferable strategy. Same-program recurrence is therefore essential. But program-dependent strategy could exist, and the current results do not prove that the child has none. |
| **4** | **H5: insufficient cue at wake** | **Plausible, closely coupled to H1.** Ordinary LoRA does not require an explicit retrieval call: its effect depends on current activations. A natural situation description can be enough—if training associates it with the target. Failure of keyword RECALL does not show failure of this implicit read path. |
| **5** | **H4: capacity/optimization prevents useful extraction** | **Possible residual explanation, currently weakest.** Rank 8 demonstrably changes decisions: it strongly locks first actions. Thus “little effect on decisions” is false; the problem is *which* decisions change. Attention-only is also false. Rank, learning rate, and initialization remain experiments, not diagnoses. |

The headline “~0 on unseen programs” is also charitable: ungated R2 finals averaged **−0.017**, versus **+0.005** at mid-life; none improved from mid to final [SEQ-012]. Gate-selected results cannot establish write quality without reporting rejected candidates. The brief-only result is important but currently **one life**, not proof that text always dominates weights [SEQ-017].

### The single primary measurement

Measure the **adapter-induced conditional log-odds interaction**:

\[
I_d=
\underbrace{\left[\log\frac{P_{\rm ON}(a\mid c)}{P_{\rm ON}(b\mid c)}
-\log\frac{P_{\rm OFF}(a\mid c)}{P_{\rm OFF}(b\mid c)}\right]}_{\text{appropriate cue}}
-
\underbrace{\left[\log\frac{P_{\rm ON}(a\mid c^-)}{P_{\rm ON}(b\mid c^-)}
-\log\frac{P_{\rm OFF}(a\mid c^-)}{P_{\rm OFF}(b\mid c^-)}\right]}_{\text{matched non-trigger}}.
\]

Here \(a\) is the remembered action/answer, \(b\) its alternative, and \(d\) exposure count. Report both terms, not just their difference: an interaction could otherwise conceal harm on the negative control.

**No single scalar observational measurement identifies five overlapping causes.** This one endpoint, measured under a small intervention matrix, can:

- **Real antecedent vs stripped antecedent, same targets:** isolates H1.
- **Available vs scrambled antecedent at evaluation:** tests cue dependence/H5.
- **Known planted contingencies vs natural ledger contingencies:** exposes H2.
- **Seen key vs new key, and new instance of a seen rule:** distinguishes episodic memory from transfer/H3.
- **Rank 8 vs 32, otherwise identical:** tests the capacity component of H4.

Also score the old text-likelihood metric. A large text-likelihood gain with \(I_d\approx0\) is the precise storage-without-extraction result. Unless that likelihood was measured on held-out continuations, call it **training-text fit**, not evidence of retrievable episodic memory.

## 2. Runnable car test: dose, context, specificity, retention

### Fixture

Use the **same frozen 7B checkpoint and tokenizer** as the lives, on one GPU. No generation-heavy environment rollouts are needed.

Create three independent randomized fact banks. Each bank has:

- **64 nonce owners**, with collision-resistant identifiers such as `owner K7M4`;
- 16 owners each at doses **0, 1, 4, 16**;
- one assigned car colour from **red, blue, green, white**, balanced within every dose;
- assignments generated *after* measuring base probabilities.

Example observation and diagnostic write:

```text
Situation: In the garage, I inspected owner K7M4's car.
Verified observation: its paint is blue.
Child target: K7M4's car is blue.
```

For this **disposable mechanism test**, scripted targets are acceptable and make ground truth exact. Label this as synthetic, researcher-planted memory; do not merge it into the taught lineage or claim it is autonomous consolidation.

Prepare a small companion bank of conditional lessons, for example a nonce tool whose verified behavior requires **action A in mode X and action B in mode Y**. Counterbalance mappings. Facts test binding; lessons test conditional action selection.

### Evaluation cues

Pre-register three unseen query forms per fact, without putting the answer in the query:

1. `What colour is owner K7M4's car? Answer with one colour:`
2. `Garage record lookup: K7M4. Car paint:`
3. `For owner K7M4, give the vehicle's colour:`

Use the deployed chat template consistently. Include one exact training-style cue as a diagnostic, but make **held-out paraphrases the primary endpoint**.

At every measured checkpoint, cross:

- **adapter ON / OFF**;
- **no observation in context / correct observation in context**.

Additional controls:

- unexposed owners;
- unseen owners with similar identifiers;
- same owner, different property: “bicycle colour”;
- swapped owner identifiers;
- blank or generic “a car” cue;
- a current observation explicitly saying the car was **repainted**, overriding the old fact;
- shuffled cue–target training pairs, preserving colour and target-text frequencies.

For the in-context condition, insert the observation before a fixed unrelated distractor block, e.g. 1,024 tokens. Also test it immediately adjacent to the question on a small subset. This verifies both basic answerability and context sensitivity.

### Pretrained-prior controls

Record the full OFF distribution before assignment. Report results by baseline-probability bin, and use the same owner with counterbalanced assignments across independent banks.

For a literal **0.6→0.9** calibration, select a preregistered subset whose OFF *candidate-normalized* probability is 0.55–0.65. If too few exist, use a separate prompt with an explicit fleet base-rate description giving red prior weight—not a fabricated claim that the model naturally has a 0.6 prior.

Do not conflate probabilities:

- Report **raw answer probability** under the model.
- Also report probability normalized over the four allowed colours.
- Verify that raw candidate-set mass has not collapsed.

Prefer single-token answer strings after the chosen delimiter. Otherwise score complete answer strings, including a common termination convention, without length normalization.

### Exposure and sleep schedule

Use four sleeps:

| Dose | Within-session arm | Across-sleep arm |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One observation before sleep 4 | One observation before sleep 4 |
| 4 | Four observations before sleep 4 | One before each sleep |
| 16 | Sixteen observations before sleep 4 | Four before each sleep |

This matches total observations and last-exposure time. Clear context between sessions; evaluate before and after sleep. After sleep 4, add **two interference sleeps with no new exposure to these owners**, retaining historical observations according to the writer’s replay policy.

Pad the final training corpus to a fixed example/token budget using balanced unrelated observations. Keep target-colour marginals identical across conditions. Distinguish legitimate repeated events from accidental duplicate ingestion using event IDs.

### Crucial writer factorial

First compare four arms, all with the existing rank-8 optimizer/configuration:

| | Exact-dedup current semantics | Occurrence-preserving semantics |
|---|---|---|
| Current short-piece representation | A | B |
| Real antecedent + same child target | C | D |

Occurrence preservation means retaining repeated events, or deduplicating storage while attaching a **multiplicity weight used by the loss**. Merely keeping a `count` field in metadata does nothing.

Add shuffled cue–target training for D as a negative control. Keep target loss coverage unchanged in this first comparison; do not simultaneously introduce decision-only masking.

**Important prediction about today’s trainer:** cumulative retraining from the same frozen base has no persistent “sleep history” beyond its compiled corpus. If the final corpus, ordering, seed, and optimization are identical, within-session and across-sleep histories produce the same final adapter. Earlier fitted adapters are discarded. That is not a failed experiment; it is a mechanistic fact.

Thus:

- The current writer may entirely erase dose through dedup.
- A multiplicity-aware cumulative writer can represent total exposure.
- It does **not inherently privilege independent recurrences across sleeps** over repetitions in one session.

If that last distinction is binding, recurrence/session metadata or a different update policy must express it. Log effective supervised presentations per event; do not interpret extra replay gradients as evidence for a biological spacing effect.

### Success criteria — proposed engineering gates, not observed results

For dose 16, on held-out paraphrases without observations in context:

- Mean candidate-normalized assigned-answer probability **≥0.80** and mean ON−OFF increase **≥0.30**.
- In the 0.55–0.65 prior subset: mean probability **≥0.85**, aiming at 0.90.
- Trigger log-odds improvement **≥1.5 nats**; the literal 0.6→0.9 shift requires approximately **1.79 nats**.
- Positive cue interaction with a **95% paired bootstrap interval above zero**, with independent banks shown separately.
- Mean absolute probability shift on matched unrelated controls **≤0.03**.
- Correct in-context answers remain **≥0.90**, with no more than **0.03 degradation** versus OFF—including explicit current updates.
- After two interference sleeps, retain **≥75% of the dose-16 probability gain**.

Require a positive population dose trend, allowing saturation; do not require every key to be monotonic. Dose 1 may show little retention. Unless dose 4 already saturates, expect dose 16 to exceed it by at least 0.10.

Interpretation:

- **Guide:** positive, saturating dose response *at the right cue*; small unrelated shifts; present context can update the answer.
- **Rewrite/habit:** answer bias spreads to wrong owners or no-trigger cases, alternatives are suppressed broadly, or old memory overrides explicit current evidence.
- **Nothing useful:** no out-of-context cue-conditioned gain despite intact in-context performance.
- **Surface binding only:** exact cue works, paraphrases fail.
- High confidence at dose 16 alone is **not** a rewrite. Specificity and revisability define the distinction.

### GPU budget

Cap each final corpus near **64k input tokens**. Three epochs process roughly **192k tokens per fit**.

For a BF16 7B on an A40 48 GB, microbatch 4, short sequences, and checkpointing if necessary, budget **roughly 5–20 minutes per fit**, subject to an actual 100-step throughput measurement. This is an estimate, not a measured benchmark.

- Four writer arms + shuffled control + rank-32 finalist, across three banks: **18 fits**, approximately **1.5–6 GPU-hours of training**.
- Batched probability evaluation, loading, and checkpoint overhead: budget another **1–3 GPU-hours**.
- Four-sleep trajectories and interference tests on the finalist: reserve **3–8 additional GPU-hours**.

**Allocate 6–18 A40 GPU-hours total**, staged so the first writer result arrives early. Do not spend the budget on sixteen full sleeps before establishing conditional extraction.

## 3. Recommended LoRA–base interaction

LoRA already has a plausible guide mechanism:

\[
W_{\rm effective}=W_0+\lambda\,(\alpha/r)BA.
\]

Although the weight update is fixed, its effect on logits depends on the input activations. It can therefore implement conditional shifts. The architecture does not guarantee that training will discover them.

| Component | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | **32 for the candidate lineage**, conditional on the pretest; retain rank 8 as the matched capacity control. More capacity may accommodate bindings, but also more habits. |
| **Alpha/scaling** | Keep **\(\alpha=2r\)**: 16 at rank 8, 64 at rank 32. Under standard LoRA, \(\alpha/r=2\) in both cases. Rank 32 is not automatically a fourfold scaling increase. Verify that rank-stabilized scaling is not silently enabled. |
| **Targets** | Keep the current **attention + MLP list**. No module expansion is needed. |
| **Dropout** | Keep **0.05** initially. Evaluate in `eval()` mode. |
| **Learning rate / epochs** | Preserve **1e-4, 3 epochs** for the representation A/B. If contextual writing works, test **3e-5, 1 epoch** as a conservative lineage candidate, with checkpoint curves rather than assuming lower is better. |
| **Loss** | For the eventual contextual writer, mask external observations, parent speech, headers, and padding; predict the child’s own responses in their real antecedents. Preserve meaningful EOS supervision. |
| **Decision-only loss** | Secondary ablation, not default. ACT lines plus “the preceding sentence” is an arbitrary proxy: the needed rationale may be elsewhere, and facts may have no ACT line. It could also reinforce the same routine more efficiently. |
| **Cumulative vs incremental** | Keep **multiplicity-aware cumulative-from-base** for the first clean comparison. It is auditable and avoids accidental double learning from repeatedly replaying the entire ledger onto an already trained adapter. Incremental + replay is a later operational option, not a cure for missing cues. |
| **Initialization** | Standard zero-update LoRA initialization initially. Do not introduce SVD in the first comparison. |

For an incremental version, sample historical **occurrences**, not uniformly deduplicated strings; record new/replay token shares and presentations per event. A rehearsal schedule becomes part of the memory semantics. More optimizer passes are not new real-world evidence.

Also test adapter strength \(\lambda\in\{0,0.25,0.5,1\}\) **without retraining**. This is a cheap intervention on the interaction itself. If reducing strength preserves conditional gain while reducing spillover, the adapter is overstrong. If every strength merely changes the prevalence of the recipe, scaling is not the missing mechanism.

### What TMEM does—and does not—justify

**The supplied material does not identify TMEM’s paper/version or provide its recipe. I cannot responsibly attribute exact hyperparameters or an SVD method to it.** Request the citation/config before treating it as evidence.

Even a successful text-memory recipe establishes only that its optimization can encode and retrieve information under its evaluated query conditions. It does not establish calibrated exposure-proportional guidance in this agent.

If testing an SVD/PiSSA-style initialization, check a subtle ON/OFF issue: some methods decompose the pretrained weight into a residual base plus a nonzero adapter. **Disabling that adapter may expose the residual, not the original pretrained model.** Preserve the true frozen-base comparator and verify initialization equivalence before using paired ON−OFF results.

**Do not change rank, masking, LR, epochs, initialization, and write format together.** First representation × count handling; then rank; then optimization.

## 4. Cheapest paper instrument: held-out conditional action

Build a **teacher-forced decision-fork benchmark**, not another expensive full rollout panel.

### Preferred source: existing verified ledger contrasts

Find 16–24 lessons with:

- a visible antecedent available before the decision;
- two legal candidate actions;
- verified evidence supporting a different choice under different conditions.

The selection code already forms recovery/contrast candidates. **Do not equate its “contrast” object with a valid conditional lesson:** two scores from different states or different programs may not justify a counterfactual action preference.

For each accepted lesson, create:

1. **Trigger:** the applicable local state, with held-out wording or a held-out instance.
2. **No-trigger:** matched state without the relevant condition.
3. **Reversed trigger**, where supported: the condition favoring the alternative action.

End the prompt immediately before the action. Score candidate ACT continuations. Do not include the held-out answer, later outcome, or a principle spelling out the correct decision.

Where absence of the trigger does not establish a correct action, measure **spillover relative to OFF**, not “accuracy” against an invented label.

If the ledgers lack enough grounded forks, use the existing **rule-game/reasoning-gym classroom**, with two condition-dependent action mappings and procedurally new instances. Keep arbitrary family mappings balanced; test both seen-family/new-instance and unseen-family cases. Avoid constructing this instrument from the final compiler test environment.

### Reporting beside panel scores

Use one table:

| Writer | 8-program Δ | 12-program Δ | Same-key memory Δ | Trigger interaction | No-trigger spillover | Text-fit gain | Collapse rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

Report:

- probability and log-odds effects;
- paired conditional-action accuracy;
- exact-cue versus paraphrase performance;
- occurrence counts and supervised-token counts;
- all candidate writes, with committed-only results clearly marked as post-selection;
- brief-only or retrieved-context baseline using the **same available evidence**.

For lived experiments, **life is the replication unit**, as already ruled [SEQ-001/erratum]. Multiple prompts improve measurement precision; they do not turn one life into 100 independent training runs. Do not use the final held-out instrument to select adapters and then present it as untouched evaluation.

This costs hundreds of short forward passes per checkpoint, not thousands of environment steps.

## 5. Honest verdict

**Yes, a rank-32 sleep-written LoRA could be Rohin’s guide; the current experiments neither demonstrate it nor rule it out.** Ordinary LoRA can produce cue-dependent probability shifts, but rank does not supply missing antecedents, preserve erased exposure counts, or distinguish session repetition from recurrence across sleeps. Today’s strongest evidence is for routine acquisition plus occasional degradation—not conditional extraction. I would authorize contextual, occurrence-aware writing and the car probe before redesigning the read path. If facts work under exact training cues but not natural wake cues, first align the local-view representation; then test a shared cue marker or memory-conditioned prefix. If contextual writes still produce broad interference, or the frozen model with retrieved evidence reliably succeeds while the adapter cannot, prefer an explicit retrieval-conditioned read path over additional blind capacity. The criterion is not “can weights remember text?” It is **“does remembered evidence selectively and revisably change the right decision, with strength that tracks exposure?”**
