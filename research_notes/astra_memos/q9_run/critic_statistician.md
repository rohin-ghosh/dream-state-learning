### What happened to confidence intervals and noise rulers?

The draft **does not ignore its quoted B interval**: it correctly argues that a positive relative contrast can coexist with unusable recall. But that interval is unavailable for verification here.

The more important omission is elsewhere:

- No verified uncertainty accompanies the write-pretest score contrasts.
- No verified local noise ruler supports “nothing,” “plateau,” or “routine-equivalent.”
- No uncertainty or effect-magnitude distribution accompanies the endpoint brief tally.
- “Beyond local uncertainty” and “contrasts remain appropriate” are not executable gates until the estimand, resampling unit, decision margin, and contrast-harm criterion are specified.

**Do not invent a noise band.** Recover the notebook’s actual ruler and confirm that it applies to the same panel, horizon, node, and evaluation procedure. Distinguish generation variability, task-instance variability, and life-to-life variability.
