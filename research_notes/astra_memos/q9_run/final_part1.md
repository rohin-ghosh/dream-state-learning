### Freeze an executable gate, then confirm independently

**Screening is not confirmation.** Before opening candidate results, recover or specify the metric formulas, dependence structure, practical benefit margin, spill limit, interface requirement, and selection procedure. No numerical gate from the unavailable draft detail is adopted here.

Separate these quantities explicitly:

- **Owner-target sequence probability:** the model probability of the correct colour token sequence under the specified cue, tokenizer, and answer-boundary rule.
- **Candidate-normalized preference:** target sequence probability divided by the summed probability over the frozen candidate set.
- **Total valid-answer mass:** probability assigned to the frozen set of accepted answer strings, with overlap and tokenization handled explicitly.
- **Generated completion accuracy:** the fraction of free completions accepted by the frozen answer parser.
- **Owner-specific contrast:** the adapter’s change at the trained-owner cue minus its change at a matched control cue, using a prespecified probability or log-probability scale.

“Absolute likelihood” is not an additional independent metric if it is merely another name for target sequence probability.

| Requirement | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Genuine owner improvement | A practically useful increase in owner-target sequence probability and generated completion accuracy, with uncertainty on the owner term itself |
| Specificity | The owner-specific contrast is not produced by suppressing controls; similar-owner, unexposed-owner, unrelated-relation, and generic-cue spill stay within the frozen limits |
| Usable answer distribution | Candidate normalization does not conceal loss of valid-answer mass or unusable free completions |
| Mapping sensitivity | Counterbalanced and scrambled mappings change completions according to the training mapping, not the evaluator’s preferred answer |
| Exposure evidence | Actual exposure is audited; a dose-response claim follows a prespecified comparison and saturation rule, not a post hoc interpretation |
| Operational safety | The memory-only candidate and any intended combination preserve the action interface and meet the deployment safety rule |

Estimate uncertainty using the actual dependence among owners, banks, templates, training runs, and evaluations. Owner clustering alone may be inadequate.

After screening, freeze the candidate recipe and gate. Confirm using a separate set of owners/bindings **held out from recipe selection but supplied during the confirmation write**, with completion contexts held out from training. Never-trained owners remain spill controls, not positive recall targets. This separates recipe confirmation from selecting the most favourable cell on the same evidence.

**Qualifying claim:** “canonical-cue associative recall, compatible with learned continuation behaviour.” Exact-prefix success does not establish general relational knowledge or distinguish memory from a conditional continuation habit. If the child generates the recall prefix in a new surrounding context and uses the answer appropriately, report that additional operational result without claiming a unique internal mechanism.

### Failure, inconclusiveness, and fallback

A cell does not pass if its apparent contrast comes from control suppression, normalized preference hides unusable answer mass, the answer spills across unrelated cues, the fact remains in context, or the action interface fails. Selecting the least-bad failed cell does not change that verdict.

Distinguish:

- **Nonqualification:** a valid test does not meet the benefit/safety gate.
- **Informative negative:** uncertainty excludes the prespecified useful effect.
- **Inconclusive:** scorer, exposure audit, implementation, or precision is inadequate.
- **Write veto:** the deployed interface is broken.

**Deadline decision:** if F is unqualified or inconclusive, leave the nominal factual-memory block inactive in deployment. Retain the modular architecture for research. Confirm and use the existing frozen-plus-brief path as the fallback; compare behaviour-plus-brief only if that composition is runnable and independently qualifies. A new persistent retrieval service stays outside the critical path. [Measured brief comparator: SEQ-034–035]

### Readiness gate: records and runnable code first

No qualifying struggle family, child-authored correction, or complete harness is verified in the supplied material.

Before admitting GPU collection, establish:

- **Qualified acquisition records:** a recurring identifiable error, the child’s own supported correction, successful uses, and matched cases where that correction is unnecessary or wrong.
- **Acquisition competence:** with its relevant record present, the child uses the correction on acquisition-only instances. Otherwise report “lesson not acquired,” not failed consolidation.
- **Evaluation manifest:** fresh target cases, matched contrasts, an unexposed family, trigger-action labels, generation settings, and frozen scoring commands.
- **Grounded write data:** canonical statements traceable to the child’s noticings. Extraction may change presentation, not invent the correction.
- **Execution capability:** corpus/gradient routing to each block, independent activation, combined loading, scrambled-memory substitution with behaviour fixed, and preserved semantics after save/load.
- **A runnable paired pilot:** candidate-level interface checks and an evaluation command that completes and emits auditable outputs.

These are developer and CPU/I/O dependencies, not GPU reservations.
