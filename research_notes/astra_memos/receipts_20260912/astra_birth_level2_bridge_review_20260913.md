## Recommendation and the interface mismatch

Use **the existing interaction_v3 RuleGame teaching loop**, first to sample whether the born learner can participate, then—if Main chooses a write sample—to preserve its own eligible next-action outputs and test them after a warm-started write/reload. Keep birth map correctness, in-context teaching response, recording fidelity and durable decision utility as separate measurements. A larger conditional-format score cannot substitute for the latter three.

Conditional PROSPECT emits `PREDICT: <opaque-action> -> <opaque-outcome>` and `ACT: <opaque-action>`; REVISE emits COMPARE/POLICY/NEXT. RuleGame instead expects **one** `ACT: TRY x,y,z` (or the existing interaction_v3 TRY alias), an explicit preceding `PREDICT: T/F`, and a six-label QUIZ after reveal. The triples are integers, not necessarily Boolean bits. Copy/arithmetic ACT anchors do not teach TRY/QUIZ budgets, reveal sequencing, truthful outcome JSON, uncertainty, or informative probe selection. Do not relabel opaque actions as triples, equate NEXT with executed ACT, or synthesize a correct TRY from a REVISE answer. The conditional module's `composition_interface()` is explicitly `UNTRAINED_FUTURE_ASSAY`, with `cases=[]`: it is **not an existing executable level2 environment**.
