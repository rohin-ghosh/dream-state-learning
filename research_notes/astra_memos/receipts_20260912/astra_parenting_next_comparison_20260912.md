## 4. The ONE candidate comparison

**Hypothesis:** If these recorded process-conditioned outputs carry useful, learnable behavior rather than merely teacher-present formatting, an adapter trained only on those child bytes should show a positive first-ACT solve gain on the retained development canaries after teacher removal and process reload, exceeding the sham adapter's gain.
