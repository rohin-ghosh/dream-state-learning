## What can wait, and what cannot

**May defer for a Main-explicit zero-fit-only release:** future CAL/SLEEP batch
materialization and per-fit work expansion; optimizer/audit implementation;
W8/learned-memory refusal/NLL diagnostic literals before their applicable uses;
full future cold-load accounting; actual-child EVENT/LINK admission before
formation is implemented. Keep fixed scientific algorithms/constants unchanged
and do not condition later binding choices on earlier favorable outputs.

**Cannot defer for that release:** the world and visible projection semantics,
relevant versus distractor affordances, root/seed/tokenizer binding, exact
Stage0/zero-fit parsers and thresholds, complete current request inventory and
resource cap, public/private isolation and honest researcher-rendered labels.
Render choices are the experiment; serialization details that expose hidden
history can change identifiability. They are not a generic guard proxy.

Before CAL/SLEEP is enabled, a narrow `organism_v6/pcfl_vertical_train.py` is
still required: the existing trainer shuffles generic packs (`train_adapter_v3.py:351`),
skips nonfinite losses (807–819), lacks clipping (823) and can save a cold
checkpoint after skips (838). PCFL needs clean C0/fresh explicit AdamW,
presealed5x40batches of4,200finite clipped updates, exact child target+EOS masks
and final-only audited tensors. Neither its warm-start path nor the now-tested
additive batch1/summed-loss loop is a drop-in PCFL writer. This future engineering
fact does **not** prevent implementing the zero-fit path first.
