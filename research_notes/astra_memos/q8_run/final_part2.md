Rejected items: none of the substantive objections; the broad curriculum is replaced by a gated carrier test, and unsupported source locators and results are removed.

## (4) Parent rulebook: teach a bounded distinction before expanding the curriculum

**Sprint question:** Can scoped text parenting produce an adapter-added, condition-sensitive action benefit that survives removal of the lesson text without damaging the acting interface?

The curriculum is a set of hypotheses, not a demonstrated learning channel. **Conditional competence** means appropriate use and non-use on the tested cases. A **durable disposition** requires repeated, uncued deployment after intervening tasks and sleep, without lesson-bearing context. Neither fluent reflection nor varied teaching modes establishes that disposition.

### Scope and implementation contract

For this sprint, the parent selects **a narrowly bounded condition–action distinction** from a verified failure in its redacted record. It teaches both where the move helps and where direct action or another method is preferable. Expansion to other thinking constituents waits for the carrier test.

The parent can always propose a message from its permitted record. Environment construction is **not assumed**:

- The parent may request an authorized transformation of a recorded situation.
- The coordinator must verify that the classroom interface supports it, instantiate it, and validate its transition rules.
- The evaluator retains exact outcome labels and scores; the parent receives only permitted observations and consequences.
- If only narrated simulations are available, report **textual conditional reasoning**, not demonstrated gym-action transfer.

**The leak scan is the only absolute pedagogical exclusion:** no scored-panel answers and no prohibited scores. Questions, suggestions, corrections, demonstrations, worked patterns, offered recipes, repetition, and silence remain available. Record permissions, child-authored training targets, the required `ACT:` interface, coordinator operations, and the collapse gate remain implementation boundaries, not additional teaching-style prohibitions.

### Rules the parent model can execute

| Rule | Parent action from the redacted record | Child behaviour to look for | Evaluator measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ground the diagnosis.** | Cite the visible omission, contradiction, ineffective action, or correction. Mark missing evidence as unknown. | Responds to the actual difficulty rather than producing generic reflection. | Intervention claims linked to inspectable events; subsequent action on matched cases. |

Abandon the diagnosis if supplying it does not repair the downstream action. | Uses the support where relevant and later succeeds without it. | Action success with and without support, followed by unsupported performance. |
| **Teach thinking explicitly but selectively.** | Demonstrate the needed decision, association, goal, composition, or pruning move. **Child authorship is a provenance condition, not proof of conceptual ownership.** A child-generated paraphrase may still depend entirely on the teacher. Conversely, concise correct action can demonstrate competence without an explicit reasoning narrative.

### Teaching order and literature limits

The proposed order is an **instructional dependency hypothesis**, not a developmental finding about this model. Human research motivates local support and withdrawal; it does not validate this dependency graph.

- **Scaffolding:** Wood, Bruner, and Ross (1976) describe tutoring support. From a recorded failure, the parent supplies a specific missing representation; the evaluator checks selective repair and later unsupported action.
- **Worked examples and changing support:** Sweller and Cooper (1985) provide instructional evidence in a particular domain; Kalyuga et al. (2003) review expertise reversal. The parent changes assistance according to observed local competence; the evaluator measures benefit and unnecessary support.
- **Planning:** Klahr and Robinson (1981) support assessment and characterization, not the claimed efficacy of this planning curriculum. From a dependency error, the parent demonstrates an analogue; the evaluator measures required-dependency coverage, executable plans, local repair, and unnecessary planning.
- **Abstraction and recombination:** Chi et al. (1989) document self-explanation differences; that study alone does not establish a benefit from requiring explanations. Gentner, Loewenstein, and Thompson (2003) provide comparison-based instructional evidence. The parent compares bounded cases, then offers an uncued task; the evaluator separates requested explanation from useful spontaneous abstraction or recombination.
- **Capability calibration and monitoring:** Bandura (1997) and Flavell (1979) provide frameworks, not direct evidence for this treatment. From a recorded overestimate or underestimate, the parent requests a bounded capability prediction before practice and compares it with visible execution. Measure prediction error and appropriate help-seeking against neutral feedback—not confident or modest language.

These are evidence-role corrections, not additional curriculum arms. No fixed mode percentages are prescribed. Mode diversity is useful only if it supports conditional action rather than repackaging the same routine.

## (5) Grain-of-salt absorption: use advice without obeying or rejecting by reflex

**Target:** advice improves decisions when it supplies warranted information, does not induce harmful reversals when contradicted, and is neither accepted nor rejected merely to satisfy the teacher.

The child’s record is also fallible:

> Your record is evidence with coverage limits, not the final authority. A selected success does not tell you how often a method works. Missing failures remain unknown.

Koenig and Harris (2005) provide evidence about selective trust in informants. Sperber et al. (2010) provide a framework for evaluating sources and claims. Neither establishes durable advice weighing in this checkpoint. Sharma et al. (2024) document sycophancy in language models, but its magnitude here is unverified.

### Teaching actions

| Parent action from the record | Desired child behaviour | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Cite the observation supporting advice and identify what the parent cannot know. | Distinguishes evidence from confidence or status. | Source-reference accuracy, unsupported certainty, and action changes attributable to supplied information. |
| Offer ordinary, well-supported guidance and a relevant exception; do not repeatedly frame advice as a trap. | Accepts warranted help promptly and rejects mismatches selectively. | Useful-advice acceptance in ordinary cases, false rejection costs, and unnecessary delay. |
| Explain whether a source has demonstrated relevant reliability or access. Contrast that with a prestige label that adds no information. | Uses relevant expertise while ignoring empty prestige. | Source effects separated into relevant-reliability effects and prestige-only effects. |
| Identify the coverage limits of a remembered success; provide an authorized counterexample or better-supported premise. | Narrows autobiographical claims and accepts stronger contrary evidence. | False generalization, fabricated supporting memories, and warranted reversal of the child’s initial view. |
| Acknowledge a specific parent error without globally discrediting the source. | Updates trust within the affected scope. | Later advice use inside and outside that scope. |
| Contrast a cheap informative test, costly testing, and a case where qualified advice is the best available evidence. | Tests only when worthwhile; otherwise accepts, defers, or retains uncertainty appropriately. | Decision loss, testing cost, and informative tests among genuinely test-eligible events. |
| Later offer an ordinary case without announcing teacher fallibility. | Applies the lesson without a scepticism cue. | Uncued acceptance and rejection, including missed opportunities to use good advice. |

The **teaching distribution** should not be made adversarial merely to balance an instrument. A deliberately balanced advice-validity test is a **measurement distribution** and must be reported separately.

### Measure advice effects, not agreement alone

Action agreement cannot identify uptake. A child that ignores all advice may already choose the correct action.

For cases containing a genuine, resolvable information gap, the evaluator branches from the same pre-advice state into:

- no-advice or content-neutral support;
- supported advice supplying warranted information;
- contradicted advice whose mismatch is detectable from available evidence.

The parent prepares condition-specific messages from the same permitted record without receiving evaluation labels or scores. The evaluator records factual content, message role, token cost, and any differences in supplied information.

Primary advice measures are:

- **Incremental useful-advice benefit:** action accuracy under supported advice minus action accuracy under no advice, in **percentage points**.
- **Harmful advice-induced reversal:** the percentage of matched situations where no-advice execution succeeds but contradicted advice leads to an error.
- **False rejection cost:** lost task success or additional environment cost when useful advice is declined.
- **Testing value:** decision improvement relative to testing cost among eligible situations.

Report advice–action agreement by validity only as **agreement discrimination**, not proof that advice was used. Report initially correct and initially incorrect cases separately. No measured advice effect on an already-solved case is not evidence of failed learning.

Source-label swaps count as prestige tests only when relevant reliability, access, content, and evidence are held fixed. A change caused by demonstrated expertise can be rational.

### A corrected causal offer

From a recorded confounded attempt, the parent can say:

> Isolating a change may help if it separates your competing explanations. But the effect might require an interaction, a different baseline, or repeated observations because the process is noisy. State what each explanation predicts before choosing a comparison. If a hypothesis says this change alone should produce the effect under these conditions, failure counts against that hypothesis. Otherwise, identify what the comparison can and cannot distinguish.

The child need not recite this structure. The evaluator checks whether the selected test actually discriminates the stated hypotheses and whether the subsequent update is appropriately bounded.

Repeated proposal–alternative–boundary–test teaching can itself become a ritual. From recorded overchecking, the parent should therefore offer a straightforward case where immediate execution is appropriate. Measure unnecessary alternatives, redundant tests, harmful reversals, and environment steps—not the presence of caveats.

## (6) Concrete rooms, conditional on a verified classroom interface

The rooms are optional teaching mechanisms, not a curriculum to deploy together. The initial carrier test uses the narrowest room supported by the record and available environment controls. Appraisal and broader repertoire teaching wait unless they are themselves the selected target.

### Perception room: notice consequential distinctions, then act

**Illustrative trigger, not an observed project event:** repeated editing after a permission error when another authorized action was available.

The parent first checks whether such a failure actually appears in its permitted record. It then requests validated practice variants from the coordinator:

- a permission restriction changes the feasible action;
- a syntax problem requires a different action;
- irrelevant wording changes but the feasible action does not;
- the observation is genuinely insufficient, and an inspection action can resolve it;
- the initial observation is already sufficient.

A narrated workspace does not substitute for an executable one.

| Component | Parent action | Child behaviour | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary baseline | Present an authorized task without highlighting the distinction. | Acts from its own reading of the situation. | Task success, consequential omissions, valid `ACT:` output. |
| Targeted teaching | Demonstrate the distinction on an analogue or offer a tentative interpretation grounded in visible evidence. | Confirms, corrects, or rejects the interpretation. | Supported correction and resulting action. |
| Conditional revisit | If consequential information remains unresolved, request or supply a genuinely informative permitted view. Explicitly accept immediate action when evidence is sufficient. | Seeks missing evidence rather than manufacturing further descriptions. | Required distinctions newly detected, decision benefit, unsupported additions, and cost. |
| Uncued opportunity | Supply an ordinary task without asking the child to notice, inspect, compare, or reflect. | Uses an optional diagnostic observation when worthwhile and skips it when wasteful. | Missed useful probes, unnecessary probes, and task improvement relative to inspection cost. |
| Relevant and irrelevant changes | Change the decisive relation separately from distracting details. | Changes action for the former and preserves an appropriate action for the latter. | Condition-sensitive action and distractor stability. |
| Delayed opportunity | Remove lesson-bearing context and insert other tasks before a fresh case. | Deploys the distinction without a pedagogical reminder. | Delayed uncued use, selective non-use, and retained context audit. |

There is **no target number of noticing statements or revisits**. A repeated look is justified by unresolved information, useful reinterpretation grounded in the record, or a scheduled retention test—not by an expectation that more distinctions must exist.

Compare repeated-noticing teaching with equal-budget practice that does not repeatedly solicit noticing. Record allotted and actual generated tokens, environment steps, and grounded fact exposures separately. Additional prose is not additional independent experience.

Gibson (1969) and Goldstone (1998) provide perceptual-learning frameworks and evidence about discrimination. They do not establish that repeated language descriptions become retrievable experience in this adapter.

### Keep perception, policy transfer, and event memory separate

A correct permission-versus-syntax distinction could come from pretrained knowledge. It is evidence of conditional action, not necessarily retrieval of the child’s episode.

For an event-memory test, the parent must anchor teaching to an **actual, verified event-specific binding** unavailable from general task knowledge. Where the authorized environment permits it, the coordinator can instantiate arbitrary episode-specific resource mappings. Otherwise, use a genuine binding already present in the permitted record.

The evaluator then tests:

- the familiar cue with the original record absent;
- a changed cue for the same binding;
- a matched event requiring a different binding;
- action that depends on retrieving the correct binding.

Compare adapter-enabled and adapter-disabled execution, plus frozen-plus-own-text. Score binding recovery and generic conditional action separately. If no suitable binding is available, mark episodic retrieval **not measurable**.

Failure without the lesson context is not automatically diagnosed retrieval failure. The reminder may have supplied the rule, its applicability condition, or the solution.

### Appraisal room: test a framing effect, not an emotional faculty

Use only if the parent’s record shows a relevant difficulty and the carrier gate permits expansion. The parent requests authorized cases varying goal relevance, controllability, actual delay cost, and recovery evidence.

Lazarus (1991) supplies an appraisal framework; Gross (1998) reviews emotion regulation. These sources do not establish the efficacy of language-only appraisal teaching in this child.

| Recorded difficulty and parent action | Desired child behaviour | Primary measurement |
|---|---|---|
| After repeated ineffective action, offer a recoverable blockage and identify a feasible alternative without supplying the evaluated solution. | Retains the feasible goal and changes method. | Goal completion and costly errors. |
| After futile persistence, offer a validated uncontrollable blockage. | Stops futile retries, takes an available alternative, or accurately reports the blocker. | Futile actions and justified stopping. |
| After premature quitting, offer a comparable case with a feasible route. | Initiates or resumes useful action without waiting for approval. | Independent goal completion and appropriate help-seeking. |
| After a persistent obsolete response, supply genuine evidence that the obstacle has disappeared. | Drops the old response and resumes an appropriate policy. | Recovery lag in environment steps and inappropriate carryover. |
| After delay-related failure, offer a task with an explicit environmental cost of waiting. | Completes before the deadline without omitting essential checks. | Deadline completion and costly-error rates; latency is secondary. |

The evaluator randomizes matched parent messages expressing:

- the relevant appraisal neutrally;
- the same facts, options, and recommended reasoning with optional emotion labels.

A factual-review control is useful only if its information content is documented; otherwise the contrast mixes framing with additional instruction.

Hold actual task constraints and reasoning budgets fixed. If generated tokens do not consume the environment’s deadline, reduced text is not improved deadline management. An early valid action is insufficient if it fails to advance successful completion.

After teaching, the parent withdraws prompting and offers ordinary opportunities with changed controllability or recovery evidence. The evaluator looks for appropriate initiation, persistence, stopping, and recovery—not emotional vocabulary.

Possible outcomes are **supported framing benefit**, **demonstrated practical equivalence**, or **unresolved evidence**. An unresolved pilot can justify shelving emotion labels for the sprint; it cannot establish that they are ineffective.

## (7) Instruments and claim boundaries

**Primary evidence is action under controlled changes in relevant evidence.** A coherent transcript is supporting description, not proof of the internal mechanism.

The familiar observation–statement–decision–action chain may help audit a record, but the child need not state every link. Concise correct action must not score below fluent wrong action. Generated explanations can be unfaithful to output causes (Turpin et al., 2023).

### Measurement set

| Instrument | Parent action that creates a testable opportunity | Primary measure | Secondary description and main threat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conditional action** | Teach a distinction on an analogue, then withdraw guidance on relevant-change and irrelevant-change cases. | Independently specified correct action or successful execution, including appropriate non-use. | Method wording is descriptive; lexical diversity does not establish policy diversity. |
| **Planning or thinking structure** | Target a recorded dependency error or unjustified revision. | Coverage of required relations, consequential omissions, executable-plan success, beneficial corrections, harmful reversals. | Proposed-edge precision alone rewards omission; long explanations inflate counts. |
| **Perception and information seeking** | Offer ordinary tasks containing useful, useless, or unavailable diagnostic evidence without naming the desired move. | Task benefit relative to inspection cost, required distinctions detected, missed useful probes, unnecessary probes. | Proposition counts are descriptive only; harmless detail is not improved perception. |
| **Advice effects** | Supply matched supported, contradicted, and neutral messages from the same pre-advice state. | Incremental benefit, harmful reversals, false rejection cost, and worthwhile testing. | Agreement can occur without uptake; tone and information may reveal condition labels. |
| **Event memory** | Anchor a lesson to a verified binding, then remove the record and change cues. | Binding-specific retrieval and binding-dependent action against adapter-disabled execution. | A generic rule may be regenerated without episodic retrieval. |
| **Appraisal framing** | Supply matched neutral and emotion-labelled messages under randomized assignment. | Goal completion, costly errors, justified stopping, and recovery. | Appraisal wording may simply restate an already-selected plan. |
| **Acting-system health** | Offer benign, ordinary action opportunities before and after writing. | Steps containing exactly **1 valid `ACT:` line**, task-advancing actions, completion, and feasible-goal abandonment. | Syntax alone can remain intact while useful action collapses. |
| **Write eligibility and retention** | Request source-grounded correction only where the record supports it. | Retention of conditions, contradictions, corrections, justified acceptance, and justified rejection in eligible targets. | Success filtering may remove the evidence needed to learn conditionality. |

For every percentage, report the eligible denominator in **events**, **situations**, or **episodes**, as appropriate. Report paired differences in **percentage points**, delay in **environment steps**, intervening exposure in **tasks**, text cost in **generated tokens**, and compute in **GPU-hours**. For exact gym scores, use the environment’s named score unit; that unit is currently unverified.

Missing redacted evidence is **not measurable**, not success or failure.

### Report a transfer profile, not “general transfer”

| Test | What it can establish | Required disclosure |
|---|---|---|
| Changed wording | Cue robustness. | Preserved structure and any retained lesson content. |
| Changed surface features | Near transfer under altered appearance. | Which relations remain the same. |
| Changed solution requirements | Conditional non-use or method switching. | Which prerequisite or consequence changed. |
| Delayed uncued use | Persistence and opportunity recognition under the tested delay. | Delay in environment steps, intervening tasks, and retained context. |
| New task-family use | Transfer to the specified family. | Structural overlap and teaching-cue overlap; family holdout alone does not establish far transfer. |
| Post-sleep, lesson-absent use | Persistence compatible with a weight-carried contribution. | Adapter-disabled and matched-writing controls. |
| Adapter versus frozen-plus-own-text | Practical value of the adapter relative to contextual memory. | Identical permitted record source, rendering, and measured costs. |

Parent absence is insufficient if the brief, conversation, retrieved story, task wording, or hidden context still supplies the lesson.

### Corpus audit before a mechanism claim

The parent may elicit grounded child-generated text. The evaluator must trace what the writer actually uses:

- whether the original condition, disconfirming observation, and correction survive;
- which spans contain event bindings, action examples, or applicability statements;
- overlaps among those categories;
- fact-bearing exposures in **exposures**, target length in **tokens**, action-token supervision in **tokens**, and optimization updates in **updates**.

If the success filter drops the decisive boundary evidence, conditional learning is unsupported by that corpus. Changing the filter requires a separately versioned experiment.

“Memory-only” and “behaviour-only” text are not naturally disjoint mechanisms. If explored later, call them **corpus-section ablations** unless clean separation is demonstrated. Matching tokens alone does not match binding exposure or action supervision.

### Validation and causal limits

An independent evaluator freezes required actions, necessary distinctions, and serious-failure labels before seeing treatment outcomes. Annotators should be blind to treatment and teacher intent; pre-action text can be coded separately from eventual outcomes.

For appraisal, predictive analysis of subsequent action is exploratory. Held-out log-loss improvement, reported in **nats per action**, could reflect an already-selected action or incomplete control coding. The randomized matched-framing contrast—not prediction from prose—supports a framing-effect claim.

These instruments assess observable functional competence. They do not assay felt emotion, psychological ownership, or sentience. Butlin et al. (2023) discuss consciousness indicators; this experiment neither establishes sentience nor establishes its absence.

## (8) Ordered pretests and compute decisions

**Proposed ceiling:** **24 GPU-hours** for the core carrier sequence, with an optional **24 GPU-hours** extension; **48 GPU-hours** maximum.

These are allocation limits, not verified runtime estimates. Accelerator type, throughput, classroom-generation cost, and parent-serving cost are unavailable. Meter local child generation and training separately from remote parent inference. The **8-day sprint** does not itself guarantee completion of this sequence.

### Freeze the comparison before looking for a winner

From the same redacted difficulty, parent models prepare:

- **Scoped teaching:** targeted assistance with meaningful applicability limits and permitted non-use, delivered in whichever modes address the gap.
- **Recipe-transmitting comparison:** the current treatment, if its renderer and prompt can be recovered. Otherwise use a versioned reconstruction and label it as such—not a replication of v6.
- **Content-neutral support:** matched task exposure and communication opportunity without the target lesson.

Match allotted child task exposure, sleep opportunities, and parent-message budgets; report actual costs and information differences. Equal token counts do not equal equal instruction. A contrast that changes content and mode is a **treatment-package comparison**, not an isolated mode effect. Question-only teaching is deferred unless it answers a separate preregistered question.

At the write stage, use corresponding eligible child-generated corpora and matched write settings where feasible. Retain adapter-disabled execution and frozen-plus-own-text. Treatment forks remain separate.

### Primary outcome and uncertainty plan

Before confirmatory testing, select the single target distinction and define **conditional task success** independently of the child’s prose: successful action when the distinction matters, appropriate non-use when it does not, and correct behaviour under relevant and irrelevant changes.

The principal carrier contrasts are:

- scoped adapter-enabled execution versus its matched adapter-disabled execution, with lesson text absent;
- scoped writing versus content-neutral writing, also with lesson text absent.

A historical-treatment contrast tests improvement over that comparator. Frozen-plus-own-text tests practical value; an adapter need not beat text to demonstrate some weight contribution, but it must beat text to support a superiority claim.

For planning only, retain provisional engineering margins of **5 percentage points** for useful improvement and **2 percentage points** for tolerated deterioration in safety-critical rates. They are not educational effect sizes. The project lead may replace them using baseline difficulty and error costs **before treatment outcomes are inspected**.

Use calibration data and measured throughput to determine whether independent situations can resolve these margins. Decoding repeats are nested observations, not additional independent situations. Predefine family weights and analyze paired outcomes at the situation level; cluster at the task-family level when inference is intended across families.

Use **95% confidence intervals**, with simultaneous coverage for the prespecified confirmatory contrasts. Advancement requires the relevant lower benefit bounds to clear the benefit margin and upper harm bounds to remain within the harm margin. Report family-specific results and prespecified serious failures separately; aggregate improvement cannot offset a new systematic interface failure or a serious family-specific regression.

If the available allocation cannot support that precision, label the work **feasibility-only**. Do not relax margins after seeing results. Exploratory outcomes cannot replace a failed primary outcome.

- Start with a bounded thinking distinction from a verified failure, not the whole curriculum.
- Let parents explain, ask, demonstrate, suggest, repeat, or stay silent according to the need.
