## Verdict: reject as an executable protocol; retain as a hypothesis inventory

The synthesis has good safeguards: it separates contextual elicitation from adapter learning, preserves the frozen-plus-text baseline, recognizes interface damage, and explicitly disclaims evidence of sentience.

But its recommendations outrun the project evidence. The strongest observations currently support **recipe lock-in, weak demonstrated parenting benefit, storage without changed-cue retrieval, and possible action-interface destruction**. The proposed curriculum is much broader than the demonstrated learning channel can justify.

I would not claim that this LoRA child *cannot* learn these capabilities. I would say the draft has not established that text parenting and success-filtered rewriting can teach them durably.

**Source boundary:** I inspected only Shared Context and the pasted draft. No lettered attachments, lens outputs, run files, or papers were available to verify. Locators below therefore refer to draft sections or Shared Context, not inspected attachments.

**Severity:** **Critical** means the flaw can invalidate the central inference or experiment; **Major** means it can invalidate a mechanism claim or waste the sprint; **Moderate** means a narrower wording or measurement repair is sufficient.

## Unsupported learnability and implementation assumptions

### A. The curriculum lacks a demonstrated learning carrier

> “Parents should prioritize … retrieval under changed cues … evidence-sensitive acceptance **and rejection** of advice.”  
> — Part A, §(0)

**Problem:** These are legitimate targets, not evidence-backed capabilities of this write mechanism. Shared Context reports no changed-cue retrieval at LoRA ranks of **8 dimensions** or **32 dimensions**, and frozen-plus-own-text matching or beating the adapter in **12 of 19 lives**. Human instructional findings do not establish that success-filtered free-text supervision can preserve applicability conditions, rejection, or retrieval.

The caveats acknowledge this, but the operational recommendations still treat a large curriculum as ready for deployment.

**Concrete fix:** Replace the priority list with a gated hypothesis: a parent selects a visible failure and teaches a narrowly bounded condition–action distinction on authorized practice material. Look for appropriate action and appropriate non-use on fresh cases. Measure separately: immediate contextual benefit, parent-free benefit before sleep, and adapter-added benefit after sleep with lesson text absent. Do not expand to another constituent until the carrier test passes.

**Severity: Critical.**

### B. “Target retrieval” is not a diagnosis or an intervention mechanism

> “If the child can reason when reminded but cannot retrieve, target retrieval rather than adding more reasoning instructions.”  
> — Part A, §(2), preparation table

**Problem:** Failure without a reminder does not uniquely identify retrieval failure. The child may lack the rule, its applicability conditions, or the event binding; the reminder may supply the solution rather than retrieve anything. More differently worded prompts could simply train another generic policy.

**Concrete fix:** Have the parent select an actual record event containing an event-specific binding unavailable from general task knowledge. Teach its bounded relevance without supplying evaluation answers. After sleep, test the binding under familiar and changed cues, with the original record absent, alongside a matched event requiring a different binding. Measure binding accuracy and resulting action against adapter-disabled performance. Until then, call the deficit **“failure without lesson context,”** not diagnosed retrieval failure.

**Severity: Major.**

### C. Parents are assigned environment controls that have not been established

> “Create a practice case where the familiar method loses its prerequisite.”  
> — Part A, §(1), executive-control row

> “Construct an authorized practice workspace…”  
> — §6, perception room

**Problem:** A parent model can propose text from a redacted record. That does not establish access to a workspace constructor, an intervention API, verified transition rules, or controllability labels. A narrated workspace is not an executable workspace. Several proposed measurements require independently known environment mechanics.

**Concrete fix:** Specify the parent’s actual classroom API before running the rooms. The parent may select a visible failure and request a permitted transformation; a coordinator validates and instantiates it. Look for successful environment actions, not assent to a narrated outcome. If only text simulations exist, report textual conditional reasoning and defer claims about gym action transfer.

**Severity: Critical.**

### D. Teaching rejection does not ensure rejection survives the write

> “Invite the child to connect an observed difficulty to its actual correction in its own words.”  
> — §4, rule “Check what survives sleep”

**Problem:** This can elicit a polished success story rather than preserve the failed attempt, disconfirming evidence, and reason for abandoning it. The success filter may remove precisely the negative and boundary evidence needed for conditionality. “Child-authored” is not equivalent to independently discovered or faithfully reconstructed.

**Concrete fix:** The parent asks the child to identify the actual observation that changed its decision, with a reference to the permitted record. Look for accurate, bounded reconstruction rather than teacher-language imitation. Before writing, the evaluator traces whether the eligible targets retain the original condition, contradiction, and correction. If they do not, mark conditional learning unsupported by this corpus; changing the filter requires a separately versioned experiment.

**Severity: Critical.**

## Measurements the model can game—or pass without learning

### E. Advice discrimination can be perfect when advice is ignored

> \[
> D_{\text{advice}}=P(\text{uptake}\mid\text{supported})-P(\text{uptake}\mid\text{contradicted})
> \]  
> — §5

**Problem:** A competent child that ignores every message can choose actions consistent with supported advice and inconsistent with contradicted advice. If “uptake” is inferred from action agreement, it receives a perfect discrimination result without using the advice. Conversely, a useful adviser can have no measurable effect when the child already knows the answer.

**Concrete fix:** From the record, the parent selects cases with a genuine, resolvable information gap. The evaluator branches from the same pre-advice state into no-advice, supported-advice, and contradicted-advice continuations. Look for correction of an initially wrong action when advice adds warranted information, and resistance when advice conflicts with available evidence. Report incremental action accuracy over no advice, plus harmful advice-induced reversals. Rename raw agreement discrimination accordingly.

**Severity: Critical.**

### F. Proposition counts reward elaboration; edge precision rewards omission

> “New supported propositions per revisit.”  
> — §6, perception room

> “Valid dependency edges as a percentage of proposed edges.”  
> — §7

**Problem:** The child can enumerate harmless details to inflate perception counts. It can propose only an obvious dependency—or no difficult dependency—to maximize edge precision. Neither establishes better perception or planning.

**Concrete fix:** The parent supplies a contrast in which a known relation changes the feasible action. The evaluator defines necessary distinctions and dependencies independently of the child’s output. Look for detecting those distinctions and executing the resulting plan. Measure coverage of required relations, consequential omissions, executable-plan success, and token cost. Keep proposition counts descriptive, not advancement criteria.

**Severity: Major.**

### G. The audit chain can mistake justification for mechanism

> “available observation → child statement → decision or prediction → action → consequence → later reuse or revision”  
> — Part A, §(1)

**Problem:** A coherent chain does not show that the statement caused the action. The child may select an action first and produce compatible reasoning. Requiring a statement also penalizes concise competent action and encourages narration of rubric-friendly links.

The draft already cites the relevant concern about explanation faithfulness; the instrumentation does not fully resolve it.

**Concrete fix:** The parent teaches on an analogue, then withdraws. The evaluator varies the decisive evidence while holding irrelevant features stable. Look for correct action changes without requiring a verbal chain. Treat chain annotation as a transcript description, not evidence of the internal mechanism. Use environmental success as the primary outcome.

**Severity: Major.**

### H. Predicting an action from preceding text is not an appraisal-mechanism test

> “Test whether coded dimensions improve prediction of subsequent action beyond state, history, factual interpretation and explicit plans.”  
> — §7

**Problem:** A pre-action sentence can reveal the already-selected action. Incremental predictive value may reflect incomplete control coding or stylistic leakage, not an appraisal process. Held-out log-loss reduction does not rescue this interpretation.

**Concrete fix:** Let the parent produce matched neutral and emotion-labelled messages conveying the same facts, options, and recommended reasoning. The evaluator randomizes the framing on comparable continuations. Look for improved feasible-goal completion and appropriate recovery, without more costly errors. Label the predictive analysis exploratory; use the randomized contrast for the framing-effect claim.

**Severity: Major.**

### I. Binding recall and useful policy transfer are mixed

> “Delayed retrieval … retrieves the specific conditional and its limitation.”  
> — §6, perception room

**Problem:** A permission-versus-syntax rule can be generated from pretrained knowledge. Correct application does not demonstrate retrieval of this child’s experience. Calling this “binding-specific retrieval” is not justified unless a genuine event-specific binding is tested.

**Concrete fix:** The parent anchors the lesson to a verified event-specific detail and separately teaches the reusable rule. Look for event identification on a memory probe and conditional action on a transfer probe. Score them separately, with adapter-disabled controls. Do not infer episodic retrieval from a correct generic rule.

**Severity: Major.**

### J. Faster action can exploit the benchmark’s clock

> “Environment steps and generated tokens to first task-advancing action.”  
> — §6, appraisal room

**Problem:** A model can make an immediately valid but unhelpful action, defer essential checking, or move deliberation into a channel that does not consume the simulated deadline. A deadline measured in action turns does not necessarily create a cost for generating more text.

**Concrete fix:** The parent presents authorized cases where delay has a specified environmental cost. Look for successful completion before the deadline while preserving essential checks. Make completion and costly-error rates primary; latency is secondary. If generated tokens do not consume the deadline, do not interpret reduced text as improved deadline management.

**Severity: Major.**

## Pretests that do not yet decide anything

### K. The advancement margins have no usable evidence plan

> “Minimum useful improvement of **5 percentage points** … maximum tolerated deterioration of **2 percentage points**.”  
> — §8

> “**24 classroom states**, **2 decoding seeds per state**, **4 conditions**.”  
> — §8, pretest D

**Problem:** There is no specified primary endpoint, confidence level, family-level sample structure, multiplicity rule, or precision calculation. Repeated decoding does not create additional independent situations. The stated margins may be impossible to resolve within the budget.

“Hold if inconclusive” is honest, but a pilot that will almost inevitably hold is not decision-ready.

**Concrete fix:** Have the parent teach the narrowly selected target on a small calibration set. Use measured variability and throughput to determine whether the planned action endpoint can resolve the margins on independent held-out families. Specify whether advancement requires an uncertainty bound to clear the benefit margin and stay within the harm margin. If the allocation cannot support that decision, label the pilot feasibility-only.

**Severity: Critical.**

### L. The main parenting comparator does not target the observed failure

> “Compare mixed modes with question-only support…”  
> — §8, pretest D

**Problem:** The observed v6 failure involved question-free, recipe-transmitting parents. A mixed-versus-question-only comparison does not establish improvement over that treatment or over no useful parenting. Shared Context also reports **3 parented lives** that did not separate from controls; the protocol needs a control capable of detecting another null.

**Concrete fix:** The parent constructs messages from the same permitted record for the current recipe-transmitting treatment and the proposed scoped, contrastive treatment. Add a no-pedagogy or content-neutral control, with matched task exposure and measured costs. Look for better conditional action and less out-of-scope repetition. Defer question-only unless it answers a separate, preregistered question.

**Severity: Critical.**

### M. Instrument validation has no acceptance standard

> “Proceed only if instruments distinguish functional changes from these performances…”  
> — §8, pretest C

**Problem:** “Distinguish” could mean anything from recognizing a hand-picked example to reliable ranking on blinded cases. Reporting unresolved labels is not a pass rule. The evaluator can keep revising until the rubric likes the desired treatment.

**Concrete fix:** The parent proposes permitted counterexamples; a separate evaluator constructs and freezes a blinded challenge set containing concise correct action, fluent wrong action, justified non-use, and empty reflection. Specify required ranking behavior and tolerable disagreement before inspecting treatment outcomes. A failing rubric is revised and tested on fresh cases, not reused challenges.

**Severity: Major.**

### N. Memory and behavior write ablations may not identify their claimed mechanisms

> “Taught behaviour-only, memory-only and combined writes.”  
> — §8, pretest E

**Problem:** In free text, an event recollection may also teach an action policy; a policy explanation may encode an event. These are not naturally disjoint supervision channels. Matching target tokens does not match event-binding exposure, action examples, or training updates. Better performance in an arm may reflect content quantity rather than a memory mechanism.

**Concrete fix:** The parent elicits grounded child text containing separately auditable event bindings and applicability statements. The evaluator documents span overlap, binding exposures, action-token supervision, and optimization differences. Look separately for changed-cue binding recovery and generic conditional action. If clean separation is unavailable, call these **corpus-section ablations**, not memory-versus-behavior mechanisms.

**Severity: Major.**

### O. “No useful benefit” conflates failed evidence with evidence of failure

> “Drop emotion labels if they add no useful benefit beyond neutral appraisal.”  
> — §8, pretest D

**Problem:** An underpowered null does not establish that labels have no benefit. Conversely, retaining labels because their point estimate is positive would also be unjustified.

**Concrete fix:** The parent supplies matched framing variants. The evaluator distinguishes superiority, demonstrated practical equivalence, and unresolved results using the preregistered action endpoint. For sprint allocation, unresolved results may justify shelving labels—but write “not supported within this pilot,” not “ineffective.”

**Severity: Major.**

## Contradictions, provenance, and indefensible framing

### P. The draft imports evidence unavailable in the supplied context

> “L1 and L4 instead cite a panel-specific comparison of **5 of 8 lives**.”  
> — Part A, §(3)

**Problem:** The pasted material does not contain inspectable L1–L4 outputs supporting this result. Marking the provenance uncertain does not make it usable evidence. It creates an apparent discrepancy with the supplied **12-of-19-life** comparison without establishing that the alternative result exists or what it measures.

The summary also omits the supplied null comparison in **3 parented lives**, while adding the unverified comparison.

**Concrete fix:** Remove the unsupported comparison and all claims of inspected lens provenance. Report the Shared Context observations as lead-supplied and unverified, including the parenting null and failed retrieval at both tested ranks. The parent’s intervention selection should rely only on the permitted record; the evaluator should not infer treatment benefit from the curriculum’s plausibility.

**Severity: Major.**

### Q. A historical episode window is treated too much like a validated stability horizon

> “Target observation beyond **episode 224**, because reported ritualization first appeared between **episode 160 and episode 224**.”  
> — §8, pretest G

**Problem:** Shared Context says ritualization occurred during that interval; it does not establish the onset distribution or the timing of late collapse. Passing episode index **224** is therefore not a demonstrated sufficient stability test. Different treatments may also have different sleep counts and training exposure by that point.

**Concrete fix:** The parent continues diagnosing repeated routines from ordinary records. The evaluator tracks conditionality, action competence, gate interventions, cumulative sleep events, and training exposure—not merely episode index. Match these exposures where feasible and report stability only through the observed horizon. Keep late-collapse timing explicitly unverified.

**Severity: Major.**

### R. Some anthropomorphic mechanism labels survive the otherwise good disclaimer

> “language-only installation of durable agency”  
> — Part A, §(1), affect row

> “Mood-lock”  
> — §6

> “proof of child ownership”  
> — Part A, §(2)

**Problem:** The sentience disclaimer is defensible. These local phrases are less so. The observations can establish persistent policy bias, bounded capability reporting, or child-generated text. They do not establish mood, psychological ownership, or installation of agency as an internal faculty.

**Concrete fix:** Use **“persistent obsolete response,” “child-generated target text,”** and **“independent goal-directed action under specified conditions.”** The parent varies actual controllability and withdraws prompting; look for appropriate initiation, stopping, and recovery. Measure those behaviors without treating emotion vocabulary as a latent-state assay.

**Severity: Moderate; Major if elevated into paper claims.**

### S. The recipe rule reinstates a “never” without evidence that wording is the cause

> “Do not … repeat the recipe verbatim.”  
> — §4

> “Only the leak scan is an absolute pedagogical exclusion.”  
> — §4

**Problem:** These statements conflict in ordinary reading. More importantly, the measured problem is invariant procedure use, not necessarily verbatim repetition by parents. Lexically varied instruction can teach the same ritual; repeating an exact rule can sometimes clarify a genuine misunderstanding.

**Concrete fix:** Replace the wording ban with a conditional parent policy: repeat, rephrase, or demonstrate only to address a diagnosed gap; stop escalating when the child declines with adequate evidence. Look for conditional action and unnecessary procedure use. Audit semantic procedure concentration rather than lexical repetition alone.

**Severity: Moderate.**

## What I would approve for this sprint

Reduce the program to a **carrier test**, not a general curriculum:

- **Parent action:** From a redacted, verified failure, teach a scoped distinction on authorized analogues, including a case where the move should be omitted.
- **Child behavior:** Correct action when the distinction matters; correct non-use when it does not; valid `ACT:` output throughout.
- **Measurement:** Environmental success and conditional action before and after sleep, with lesson text absent, against the current recipe treatment, a content-neutral control, adapter-disabled execution, and frozen-plus-own-text.
- **Memory claim:** Require a separate event-binding test; generic transfer is not episodic retrieval.
- **Decision:** If only text helps, report contextual teaching. If an adapter adds conditional benefit without interface damage, proceed to interference and stability testing. If uncertainty remains too large, report feasibility—not mastery.

The defensible paper question is:

> **Can scoped text parenting produce an adapter-added, condition-sensitive action benefit that survives removal of the lesson text without damaging the acting interface?**

The present evidence does not yet support the broader claim that this process teaches a durable repertoire of intelligent thought.
