## (0) One screen: composition, instructional hypotheses, and sentience

**Decision: test a narrow learning carrier before expanding the curriculum.** Teach a conditional repertoire, not a longer routine. Appropriate use, omission, and evidence-sensitive action show **conditional competence on the tested cases**. Reserve **durable disposition** for repeated, uncued deployment after teaching context is removed, including after sleep. Neither establishes an internal psychological faculty.

**Source boundary.** Only Shared Context, the pasted draft, and the critic reports were supplied. No lettered attachments, underlying lens outputs, run records, classroom API specification, or cited papers were available for inspection. I therefore remove the draft’s L1–L4 “Basis” claims and unsupported alternative life comparison. Project observations below are **lead-supplied and independently unverified**. Literature citations identify established works from bibliographic knowledge, not a fresh full-text review; their evidential roles are stated where they matter.

### What intelligent thought is made of

A useful instructional inventory is:

- **Taking in a situation:** attention, perception, and state updating.
- **Making experience reusable:** event memory, retrieval, concepts, and analogy.
- **Working out what follows:** inference, causal testing, prediction, and revision.
- **Directing action:** goals, decisions, stopping, planning, and method selection.
- **Expanding and regulating available methods:** curiosity, creative composition, monitoring, selective social learning, bounded capability assessment, and appraisal.

These are overlapping functions—not separate brain modules, an exhaustive taxonomy, compulsory stages, or capabilities already shown to be teachable through this adapter. Psychometric classifications describe broad abilities; they are not elementary instruction sets for thinking (Carroll, 1993).

### Instructional order is a hypothesis, not a developmental finding

Human research motivates attention to representation, knowledge, control, social support, and metacognition. It does **not** establish the particular dependency graph proposed here, or its applicability to a pretrained language model (Vygotsky, 1978, theoretical account; Flavell, 1979, foundational framework; Diamond, 2013, review).

**Test dependencies locally.** From an inspectable redacted failure, the parent offers a suspected missing representation on an authorized analogue. The evaluator compares downstream action with and without that support, then tests unsupported action on a fresh case. If support does not selectively repair the failure, do not retain the prerequisite diagnosis.

### What should change during this sprint

The parent selects a visible failure and teaches a narrowly bounded condition–action distinction, including when the move is unnecessary. Teaching may use questions, suggestions, demonstrations, worked patterns, offered recipes, or silence. The evaluator looks for:

- correct action when the distinction matters;
- correct non-use when it does not;
- uncued use without semantic reminders;
- valid `ACT:` behaviour throughout.

Separate immediate contextual benefit, unsupported pre-sleep benefit, and adapter-added post-sleep benefit. Keep the adapter-disabled and frozen-plus-own-text comparisons.

**The table below is a hypothesis inventory, not an approved deployment curriculum.** Select the first carrier test from the permitted record. Do not expand sleep-written instruction across the inventory until that test supports useful conditional action without interface damage. Direct preparation needed to preserve the acting interface remains necessary.

### Honest boundary on sentience

Sentience concerns felt experience; intelligent behaviour concerns functional competence. Appraisal language, self-reference, uncertainty reports, or integrated information use do not by themselves establish feeling or consciousness (Nagel, 1974; Block, 1995; Butlin et al., 2023).

> **We teach and measure functional behaviour within one model lineage. Emotional language is an instructional variable, not evidence of felt emotion. This study establishes neither sentience nor its absence.**

**Evidence for human learning, contextual elicitation, and adapter-carried learning must remain separate.** A human intervention result supports a candidate teaching idea, not this write mechanism. A foundational framework or observational finding supports a distinction to investigate, not an intervention effect.

Every parent action starts from an **inspectable, permitted redacted record**. If the needed evidence is missing, the parent marks the diagnosis unresolved rather than reconstructing it.

### What counts as uptake

Use the following distinctions rather than a single “learned” label:

- **Restatement:** reproduces or paraphrases the lesson.
- **Supported execution:** acts appropriately while lesson-bearing context remains.
- **Unsupported conditional competence:** acts appropriately on fresh cases with parent messages, briefs, retrieved lessons, and semantic reminders removed; also omits the move where inappropriate.
- **Observed disposition:** repeatedly recognizes and uses eligible opportunities without pedagogical cues, while avoiding unnecessary use.
- **Post-sleep persistence:** the relevant behaviour remains after sleep with lesson-bearing context absent.
- **Adapter contribution:** the adapter improves the prespecified action outcome relative to matched adapter-disabled execution. Compare separately with the frozen child reading the same own-text brief.

Report a **transfer profile**, not “general transfer”: changed wording, changed surface features, changed solution requirements, delayed uncued use, and new task-family use. State retained context, delay in **environment steps**, and intervening exposure in **tasks**. New family labels do not automatically establish far transfer.

An observation–statement–action–consequence chain can describe a transcript, but does not establish the causal role of the statement. Do not require every link to be verbalized. Concise correct action counts as competence. Generated explanations may be unfaithful to the causes of answers (Turpin et al., 2023).

### Cross-table safeguards

**Audit semantic policies, not just teaching modes.** Before sending a message, each parent identifies the action policy it favours and evidence that would justify another policy. The central parent preserves genuinely unresolved alternatives rather than synthesizing a universal routine. The evaluator measures policy exposure across modes, out-of-scope use, and inappropriate action invariance under relevant changes. Keep this audit out of the child’s required output.

**Judge repetition by function.** Repeating wording can clarify a diagnosed gap; changing wording can still enforce the same ritual. Parents may repeat, rephrase, demonstrate, or withdraw. Look for repaired action and reduced dependence—not lexical novelty. No child response must contain an alternative, caveat, scope statement, or test.

**Separate teaching from diagnostic advice distributions.** Balanced misleading-advice probes are measurement devices, not a default classroom diet. Measure ordinary uptake of sound advice and false-rejection costs separately. Source sensitivity is appropriate when reliability or access is relevant; empty prestige effects are a different measurement.

**Treat autobiography as limited evidence.** A selected success does not estimate how often a method works. Parents identify missing coverage and offer authorized counterexamples or relevant premises. Measure false generalization, fabricated memories, and warranted changes away from the child’s initial view. Unavailable failures remain unknown.

## (2) Proposed instructional dependencies → preparation and classroom sequence

### Diagnose a local bottleneck rather than assign a developmental stage

A representation may help planning; a goal may guide perception; advice may supply missing knowledge. These are testable instructional possibilities, not an established developmental staircase for this model.

Scaffolding research motivates contingent support (Wood, Bruner & Ross, 1976). Worked examples and expertise-reversal research motivate adjusting assistance to local knowledge (Sweller & Cooper, 1985; Kalyuga et al., 2003). Neither validates the entire proposed sequence or its persistence through sleep.

### Classroom support: conditional parent actions, not a child script

**When representation is missing, teach the distinction.**  
The parent demonstrates or contrasts a grounded relation and then withdraws the hint. The evaluator checks required-relation coverage and resulting action, not description length. If the representation was already adequate, skip the intervention.

**When a method is missing, offer a bounded method.**  
The parent may explain, demonstrate, or offer a recipe on authorized practice material. Later cases include both genuine need and straightforward execution. Measure successful use and unnecessary alternatives, checks, or planning. Contrast teaching must not become a compulsory proposal–alternative–boundary–test routine.

**When another look could help, provide access to information.**  
From a recorded omission, the parent requests a genuinely informative view or probe. In a matched sufficient-information case, immediate action is acceptable. Measure consequential evidence gained, missed useful inspections, unsupported additions, and cost. Re-reading cannot reveal information absent from the observation.

**When causal explanations compete, bound the test’s meaning.**  
The parent states what the candidate hypotheses predict, including plausible interaction or noise alternatives. If a hypothesis predicts that an isolated change alone should produce an effect under the specified conditions, failed reproduction counts against that hypothesis. Otherwise, the parent identifies what the comparison leaves unresolved. Measure actual test discrimination and appropriately limited revision.

**After supported success, remove teaching cues.**  
The parent prepares permitted analogues; an independent evaluator reserves fresh uncued cases. Remove lesson-bearing conversation, briefs, retrieved stories, and task wording that names the move. Measure the transfer profile separately rather than treating renaming as broad transfer.

**When components work independently, test composition.**  
The parent requests a task that genuinely requires their combination and another that does not. Look for executable composition and local repair, with direct action on the simple case. Measure required dependency coverage, task completion, unnecessary branching, and cost.

**After failure, distinguish alternatives before reteaching.**  
The parent anchors possible explanations to the record: missing information, inadequate representation, absent event binding, bad rule scope, overchecking, context dependence, or interface damage. The evaluator checks whether the targeted support selectively repairs the predicted deficit. Do not label every unsupported failure “retrieval failure.”

### Appraisal is a separate instructional probe

Neutral goal relevance and controllability can accompany a narrowly selected goal-directed task. Added emotional language requires its own comparison:

- The parent produces matched neutral and emotion-labelled messages with the same facts, options, and recommended reasoning.
- The coordinator validates actual controllability and any environmental cost of delay.
- The evaluator randomizes framing and measures feasible completion, costly errors, justified persistence or stopping, and recovery after conditions change.

Do not infer an appraisal mechanism merely because preceding text predicts the action. Distinguish supported benefit, demonstrated practical equivalence, and unresolved results. An unresolved pilot may justify shelving emotional labels for this sprint; it does not establish ineffectiveness or absence of feeling.

### Sleep requires a corpus and carrier audit

Child authorship is a **training-data provenance condition**, not proof of independent discovery, conceptual ownership, or faithful explanation.

After a recorded correction, the parent may ask which actual observation changed the decision and request a permitted source reference. The evaluator checks the original record and traces whether eligible training spans retain:

- the relevant condition;
- the contradicted expectation or failed action;
- the disconfirming observation;
- the correction and its applicability limit.

Missing links must not be invented. If success filtering retains only polished success, the corpus may fail to support the proposed conditional-learning claim. Changing the filter requires a separately versioned experiment.

Later action tests should not request a recap. Measure correct action and selective non-use first; grounded explanation is secondary. If event and policy spans overlap, describe their removal as **corpus-section ablations**, not clean memory-versus-behaviour mechanisms. Report overlap, event-binding exposures, action-token supervision, target-token counts, and optimization differences.

### What the table adds

**Memory and policy are different claims.**  
Parents anchor event-specific bindings separately from reusable rules. The evaluator tests changed-cue binding recovery separately from generic conditional action. A correct permission rule does not show that the child retrieved its own experience.

**Monitoring and revision are different capabilities.**  
Parents contrast useful checks with cases where the decision should stand. Measure beneficial corrections and harmful reversals separately, including the cost of unnecessary checking.

**Goals, plans, and live state need different repairs.**  
Parents intervene on the observed failure—aimlessness, dependency error, or a stale constraint. Measure the corresponding action repair rather than rewarding more planning prose.

**Advice weighing includes warranted reliance.**  
Parents expose evidence, relevant source competence, and coverage limits in both advice and autobiography. The evaluator distinguishes causal advice benefit from mere agreement, useful trust from empty prestige, and justified rejection from costly contrarianism.

**Spontaneous deployment must be tested directly.**  
Parents can teach curiosity, alternatives, and repeated noticing explicitly during acquisition. Their decisive tests omit requests to be curious, generate alternatives, or notice more. Measure useful uncued deployment, missed opportunities, and unnecessary deployment.

**Different modes can transmit the same ritual.**  
Parents audit the policy each message favours across questions, demonstrations, stories, and recipes. Measure whether actions track relevant conditions, not teacher identity, repeated recommendations, or the classroom’s contrast format.

### What the supplied evidence supports

All observations below come from **Shared Context, “Evidence so far”**, and remain independently unverified:

- The question-free, recipe-transmitting v6 treatment reportedly ritualized in **8 lives out of 9 lives**, during **episode indices 160–224**, with late collapse in **4 lives out of 9 lives**. This motivates testing recipe dependence; it does not establish that recipes caused collapse, the onset distribution, or a sufficient stability horizon.
- **3 parented lives** reportedly did not separate from controls. The curriculum’s plausibility is therefore not evidence of parenting benefit.
- The frozen model reading the child’s own text brief reportedly matched or beat the adapter in **12 lives out of 19 lives**. The own-text baseline is a central alternative, not an optional control.
- A controlled memory test reportedly showed storage at **4.4 nats** without changed-cue retrieval at LoRA ranks **8** and **32**, both dimensionless ranks. The statistic’s normalization and full protocol are unavailable; storage cannot be equated with usable memory.
- A question–answer-form write reportedly produced **0 actions**. The protocol is unavailable, but the observation makes action-interface preservation an early requirement.

The unsupported alternative life denominator and second-hand attachment locators are excluded until their provenance is recovered.

### Decision for the sprint: a carrier test, not a broad curriculum trial

**Question:** Can scoped text parenting produce an adapter-added, condition-sensitive action benefit that survives removal of lesson text without damaging the acting interface?

**Parent action.** Select an inspectable redacted failure. Produce an authorized scoped lesson and non-use case. From the same permitted trigger, prepare messages for:

- the proposed conditional treatment;
- a reconstructed, versioned historical recipe-transmitting treatment;
- content-neutral support.

If the historical treatment cannot be reconstructed faithfully, label it an approximation. Record differences in instructional content and generated-token cost. A question-only arm is secondary and needs a separate question; it is not the historically implicated comparator.

**Child behaviour.** Correct action when the distinction matters, correct non-use otherwise, uncued deployment on fresh opportunities, and valid action production. Add a separate event-binding probe only if claiming episodic retrieval.

**Evaluator sequence.**

- **Validate feasibility and instruments first.** Freeze a blinded challenge set requiring concise correct action to outrank fluent wrong action, justified non-use to outrank empty reflection, and consequential omissions to count against performance. Prespecify tolerable disagreement before treatment outcomes; failed rubrics require fresh validation cases.
- **Calibrate before claiming benefit.** Use measured variability and throughput to decide whether independent held-out situations can resolve a useful action benefit and tolerable harm. Repeated decoding does not create independent situations. If precision is inadequate, label the pilot feasibility-only.
- **Reserve fresh confirmation cases.** Prespecify the primary action outcome, practical benefit and harm margins, uncertainty procedure, multiplicity handling, task-family breakdowns, and serious-failure events before choosing a winner. Parents must not see evaluator outcomes from those cases.
- **Separate carriers.** Compare contextual execution, unsupported pre-sleep execution, and post-sleep execution with lesson text absent. Include adapter-disabled execution and frozen-plus-own-text. Report binding recall separately from generic policy transfer.
- **Then test interference and stability.** After a supported carrier result, teach a distinct capability and retest the earlier one after another sleep. Track conditionality, action competence, rejected writes, gate interventions, cumulative sleep events, and training exposure. Report stability only through the observed horizon; passing episode index **224** is not a validated guarantee.

### Decision rules
