## (0) One screen: composition, instructional hypotheses, and sentience

**Decision: test a narrow learning carrier before expanding the curriculum.** Teach a conditional repertoire, not a longer routine. Appropriate use, omission, and evidence-sensitive action show **conditional competence on the tested cases**. Reserve **durable disposition** for repeated, uncued deployment after teaching context is removed, including after sleep. Neither establishes an internal psychological faculty.

**Source boundary.** Only Shared Context, the pasted draft, and the critic reports were supplied. No lettered attachments, underlying lens outputs, run records, classroom API specification, or cited papers were available for inspection. I therefore remove the draft’s L1–L4 “Basis” claims and unsupported alternative life comparison. Project observations below are **lead-supplied and independently unverified**. Literature citations identify established works from bibliographic knowledge, not a fresh full-text review; their evidential roles are stated where they matter.

### What intelligent thought is made of

A useful instructional inventory is:

- **Taking in a situation:** attention, perception, and state updating.
- **Making experience reusable:** event memory, retrieval, concepts, and analogy.
- **Working out what follows:** inference, causal testing, prediction, and revision.
- **Directing action:** goals, decisions, stopping, planning, and method selection.
- **Expanding and regulating available methods:** curiosity, creative composition, monitoring, selective social learning, bounded capability assessment, and appraisal.

These are overlapping functions—not separate brain modules, an exhaustive taxonomy, compulsory stages, or capabilities already shown to be teachable through this adapter. Psychometric classifications describe broad abilities; they are not elementary instruction sets for thinking (Carroll, 1993).

### Instructional order is a hypothesis, not a developmental finding

Human research motivates attention to representation, knowledge, control, social support, and metacognition. It does **not** establish the particular dependency graph proposed here, or its applicability to a pretrained language model (Vygotsky, 1978, theoretical account; Flavell, 1979, foundational framework; Diamond, 2013, review).

**Test dependencies locally.** From an inspectable redacted failure, the parent offers a suspected missing representation on an authorized analogue. The evaluator compares downstream action with and without that support, then tests unsupported action on a fresh case. If support does not selectively repair the failure, do not retain the prerequisite diagnosis.

### What should change during this sprint

The parent selects a visible failure and teaches a narrowly bounded condition–action distinction, including when the move is unnecessary. Teaching may use questions, suggestions, demonstrations, worked patterns, offered recipes, or silence. The evaluator looks for:

- correct action when the distinction matters;
- correct non-use when it does not;
- uncued use without semantic reminders;
- valid `ACT:` behaviour throughout.

Separate immediate contextual benefit, unsupported pre-sleep benefit, and adapter-added post-sleep benefit. Keep the adapter-disabled and frozen-plus-own-text comparisons.

**The table below is a hypothesis inventory, not an approved deployment curriculum.** Select the first carrier test from the permitted record. Do not expand sleep-written instruction across the inventory until that test supports useful conditional action without interface damage. Direct preparation needed to preserve the acting interface remains necessary.

### Honest boundary on sentience

Sentience concerns felt experience; intelligent behaviour concerns functional competence. Appraisal language, self-reference, uncertainty reports, or integrated information use do not by themselves establish feeling or consciousness (Nagel, 1974; Block, 1995; Butlin et al., 2023).

> **We teach and measure functional behaviour within one model lineage. Emotional language is an instructional variable, not evidence of felt emotion. This study establishes neither sentience nor its absence.**

**Evidence for human learning, contextual elicitation, and adapter-carried learning must remain separate.** A human intervention result supports a candidate teaching idea, not this write mechanism. A foundational framework or observational finding supports a distinction to investigate, not an intervention effect.

Every parent action starts from an **inspectable, permitted redacted record**. If the needed evidence is missing, the parent marks the diagnosis unresolved rather than reconstructing it.

### What counts as uptake

Use the following distinctions rather than a single “learned” label:

- **Restatement:** reproduces or paraphrases the lesson.
- **Supported execution:** acts appropriately while lesson-bearing context remains.
- **Unsupported conditional competence:** acts appropriately on fresh cases with parent messages, briefs, retrieved lessons, and semantic reminders removed; also omits the move where inappropriate.
- **Observed disposition:** repeatedly recognizes and uses eligible opportunities without pedagogical cues, while avoiding unnecessary use.
- **Post-sleep persistence:** the relevant behaviour remains after sleep with lesson-bearing context absent.
- **Adapter contribution:** the adapter improves the prespecified action outcome relative to matched adapter-disabled execution. Compare separately with the frozen child reading the same own-text brief.

Report a **transfer profile**, not “general transfer”: changed wording, changed surface features, changed solution requirements, delayed uncued use, and new task-family use. State retained context, delay in **environment steps**, and intervening exposure in **tasks**. New family labels do not automatically establish far transfer.

An observation–statement–action–consequence chain can describe a transcript, but does not establish the causal role of the statement. Do not require every link to be verbalized. Concise correct action counts as competence. Generated explanations may be unfaithful to the causes of answers (Turpin et al., 2023).

### Cross-table safeguards

**Audit semantic policies, not just teaching modes.** Before sending a message, each parent identifies the action policy it favours and evidence that would justify another policy. The central parent preserves genuinely unresolved alternatives rather than synthesizing a universal routine. The evaluator measures policy exposure across modes, out-of-scope use, and inappropriate action invariance under relevant changes. Keep this audit out of the child’s required output.

**Judge repetition by function.** Repeating wording can clarify a diagnosed gap; changing wording can still enforce the same ritual. Parents may repeat, rephrase, demonstrate, or withdraw. Look for repaired action and reduced dependence—not lexical novelty. No child response must contain an alternative, caveat, scope statement, or test.

**Separate teaching from diagnostic advice distributions.** Balanced misleading-advice probes are measurement devices, not a default classroom diet. Measure ordinary uptake of sound advice and false-rejection costs separately. Source sensitivity is appropriate when reliability or access is relevant; empty prestige effects are a different measurement.

**Treat autobiography as limited evidence.** A selected success does not estimate how often a method works. Parents identify missing coverage and offer authorized counterexamples or relevant premises. Measure false generalization, fabricated memories, and warranted changes away from the child’s initial view. Unavailable failures remain unknown.

## (2) Proposed instructional dependencies → preparation and classroom sequence

### Diagnose a local bottleneck rather than assign a developmental stage

A representation may help planning; a goal may guide perception; advice may supply missing knowledge. These are testable instructional possibilities, not an established developmental staircase for this model.

Scaffolding research motivates contingent support (Wood, Bruner & Ross, 1976). Worked examples and expertise-reversal research motivate adjusting assistance to local knowledge (Sweller & Cooper, 1985; Kalyuga et al., 2003). Neither validates the entire proposed sequence or its persistence through sleep.

### Classroom support: conditional parent actions, not a child script

**When representation is missing, teach the distinction.**  
The parent demonstrates or contrasts a grounded relation and then withdraws the hint. The evaluator checks required-relation coverage and resulting action, not description length. If the representation was already adequate, skip the intervention.

**When a method is missing, offer a bounded method.**  
The parent may explain, demonstrate, or offer a recipe on authorized practice material. Later cases include both genuine need and straightforward execution. Measure successful use and unnecessary alternatives, checks, or planning. Contrast teaching must not become a compulsory proposal–alternative–boundary–test routine.

**When another look could help, provide access to information.**  
From a recorded omission, the parent requests a genuinely informative view or probe. In a matched sufficient-information case, immediate action is acceptable. Measure consequential evidence gained, missed useful inspections, unsupported additions, and cost. Re-reading cannot reveal information absent from the observation.

**When causal explanations compete, bound the test’s meaning.**  
The parent states what the candidate hypotheses predict, including plausible interaction or noise alternatives. If a hypothesis predicts that an isolated change alone should produce an effect under the specified conditions, failed reproduction counts against that hypothesis. Otherwise, the parent identifies what the comparison leaves unresolved. Measure actual test discrimination and appropriately limited revision.

**After supported success, remove teaching cues.**  
The parent prepares permitted analogues; an independent evaluator reserves fresh uncued cases. Remove lesson-bearing conversation, briefs, retrieved stories, and task wording that names the move. Measure the transfer profile separately rather than treating renaming as broad transfer.

**When components work independently, test composition.**  
The parent requests a task that genuinely requires their combination and another that does not. Look for executable composition and local repair, with direct action on the simple case. Measure required dependency coverage, task completion, unnecessary branching, and cost.

**After failure, distinguish alternatives before reteaching.**  
The parent anchors possible explanations to the record: missing information, inadequate representation, absent event binding, bad rule scope, overchecking, context dependence, or interface damage. The evaluator checks whether the targeted support selectively repairs the predicted deficit. Do not label every unsupported failure “retrieval failure.”

### Appraisal is a separate instructional probe

Neutral goal relevance and controllability can accompany a narrowly selected goal-directed task. Added emotional language requires its own comparison:

- The parent produces matched neutral and emotion-labelled messages with the same facts, options, and recommended reasoning.
- The coordinator validates actual controllability and any environmental cost of delay.
- The evaluator randomizes framing and measures feasible completion, costly errors, justified persistence or stopping, and recovery after conditions change.

Do not infer an appraisal mechanism merely because preceding text predicts the action. Distinguish supported benefit, demonstrated practical equivalence, and unresolved results. An unresolved pilot may justify shelving emotional labels for this sprint; it does not establish ineffectiveness or absence of feeling.

### Sleep requires a corpus and carrier audit

Child authorship is a **training-data provenance condition**, not proof of independent discovery, conceptual ownership, or faithful explanation.

After a recorded correction, the parent may ask which actual observation changed the decision and request a permitted source reference. The evaluator checks the original record and traces whether eligible training spans retain:

- the relevant condition;
- the contradicted expectation or failed action;
- the disconfirming observation;
- the correction and its applicability limit.

Missing links must not be invented. If success filtering retains only polished success, the corpus may fail to support the proposed conditional-learning claim. Changing the filter requires a separately versioned experiment.

Later action tests should not request a recap. Measure correct action and selective non-use first; grounded explanation is secondary. If event and policy spans overlap, describe their removal as **corpus-section ablations**, not clean memory-versus-behaviour mechanisms. Report overlap, event-binding exposures, action-token supervision, target-token counts, and optimization differences.

### What the table adds

**Memory and policy are different claims.**  
Parents anchor event-specific bindings separately from reusable rules. The evaluator tests changed-cue binding recovery separately from generic conditional action. A correct permission rule does not show that the child retrieved its own experience.

**Monitoring and revision are different capabilities.**  
Parents contrast useful checks with cases where the decision should stand. Measure beneficial corrections and harmful reversals separately, including the cost of unnecessary checking.

**Goals, plans, and live state need different repairs.**  
Parents intervene on the observed failure—aimlessness, dependency error, or a stale constraint. Measure the corresponding action repair rather than rewarding more planning prose.

**Advice weighing includes warranted reliance.**  
Parents expose evidence, relevant source competence, and coverage limits in both advice and autobiography. The evaluator distinguishes causal advice benefit from mere agreement, useful trust from empty prestige, and justified rejection from costly contrarianism.

**Spontaneous deployment must be tested directly.**  
Parents can teach curiosity, alternatives, and repeated noticing explicitly during acquisition. Their decisive tests omit requests to be curious, generate alternatives, or notice more. Measure useful uncued deployment, missed opportunities, and unnecessary deployment.

**Different modes can transmit the same ritual.**  
Parents audit the policy each message favours across questions, demonstrations, stories, and recipes. Measure whether actions track relevant conditions, not teacher identity, repeated recommendations, or the classroom’s contrast format.

### What the supplied evidence supports

All observations below come from **Shared Context, “Evidence so far”**, and remain independently unverified:

- The question-free, recipe-transmitting v6 treatment reportedly ritualized in **8 lives out of 9 lives**, during **episode indices 160–224**, with late collapse in **4 lives out of 9 lives**. This motivates testing recipe dependence; it does not establish that recipes caused collapse, the onset distribution, or a sufficient stability horizon.
- **3 parented lives** reportedly did not separate from controls. The curriculum’s plausibility is therefore not evidence of parenting benefit.
- The frozen model reading the child’s own text brief reportedly matched or beat the adapter in **12 lives out of 19 lives**. The own-text baseline is a central alternative, not an optional control.
- A controlled memory test reportedly showed storage at **4.4 nats** without changed-cue retrieval at LoRA ranks **8** and **32**, both dimensionless ranks. The statistic’s normalization and full protocol are unavailable; storage cannot be equated with usable memory.
- A question–answer-form write reportedly produced **0 actions**. The protocol is unavailable, but the observation makes action-interface preservation an early requirement.

The unsupported alternative life denominator and second-hand attachment locators are excluded until their provenance is recovered.

### Decision for the sprint: a carrier test, not a broad curriculum trial

**Question:** Can scoped text parenting produce an adapter-added, condition-sensitive action benefit that survives removal of lesson text without damaging the acting interface?

**Parent action.** Select an inspectable redacted failure. Produce an authorized scoped lesson and non-use case. From the same permitted trigger, prepare messages for:

- the proposed conditional treatment;
- a reconstructed, versioned historical recipe-transmitting treatment;
- content-neutral support.

If the historical treatment cannot be reconstructed faithfully, label it an approximation. Record differences in instructional content and generated-token cost. A question-only arm is secondary and needs a separate question; it is not the historically implicated comparator.

**Child behaviour.** Correct action when the distinction matters, correct non-use otherwise, uncued deployment on fresh opportunities, and valid action production. Add a separate event-binding probe only if claiming episodic retrieval.

**Evaluator sequence.**

- **Validate feasibility and instruments first.** Freeze a blinded challenge set requiring concise correct action to outrank fluent wrong action, justified non-use to outrank empty reflection, and consequential omissions to count against performance. Prespecify tolerable disagreement before treatment outcomes; failed rubrics require fresh validation cases.
- **Calibrate before claiming benefit.** Use measured variability and throughput to decide whether independent held-out situations can resolve a useful action benefit and tolerable harm. Repeated decoding does not create independent situations. If precision is inadequate, label the pilot feasibility-only.
- **Reserve fresh confirmation cases.** Prespecify the primary action outcome, practical benefit and harm margins, uncertainty procedure, multiplicity handling, task-family breakdowns, and serious-failure events before choosing a winner. Parents must not see evaluator outcomes from those cases.
- **Separate carriers.** Compare contextual execution, unsupported pre-sleep execution, and post-sleep execution with lesson text absent. Include adapter-disabled execution and frozen-plus-own-text. Report binding recall separately from generic policy transfer.
- **Then test interference and stability.** After a supported carrier result, teach a distinct capability and retest the earlier one after another sleep. Track conditionality, action competence, rejected writes, gate interventions, cumulative sleep events, and training exposure. Report stability only through the observed horizon; passing episode index **224** is not a validated guarantee.

### Decision rules

- **Only lesson text helps:** report contextual teaching or text memory, not weight-carried learning.
- **The adapter adds conditional benefit without meaningful interface harm:** advance to interference and stability testing before expanding the repertoire.
- **Training fit improves without changed-cue binding recovery:** report storage or generic policy change as supported; do not claim episodic retrieval.
- **The corpus loses the relevant contradiction or boundary evidence:** mark the proposed conditional-learning mechanism unsupported by that corpus and version any filter change separately.
- **Uncertainty remains too large:** report feasibility and unresolved efficacy, not mastery or ineffectiveness.
- **The write damages action competence:** reject the write through the existing gate and diagnose the writer before broadening instruction.

> **The standard is not richer reflection. It is better, uncued action when relevant evidence changes; appropriate stability when irrelevant details change; and a demonstrable adapter contribution after the teacher and lesson text disappear.**

That would support a narrow learning result within this lineage—not a general developmental curriculum, a population-wide parenting effect, or a claim about sentience.

Rejected items: none of the substantive objections; the broad curriculum is replaced by a gated carrier test, and unsupported source locators and results are removed.

### Scope and implementation contract

For this sprint, the parent selects **a narrowly bounded condition–action distinction** from a verified failure in its redacted record. It teaches both where the move helps and where direct action or another method is preferable. Expansion to other thinking constituents waits for the carrier test.

The parent can always propose a message from its permitted record. Environment construction is **not assumed**:

- The parent may request an authorized transformation of a recorded situation.
- The coordinator must verify that the classroom interface supports it, instantiate it, and validate its transition rules.
- The evaluator retains exact outcome labels and scores; the parent receives only permitted observations and consequences.
- If only narrated simulations are available, report **textual conditional reasoning**, not demonstrated gym-action transfer.

**The leak scan is the only absolute pedagogical exclusion:** no scored-panel answers and no prohibited scores. Questions, suggestions, corrections, demonstrations, worked patterns, offered recipes, repetition, and silence remain available. Record permissions, child-authored training targets, the required `ACT:` interface, coordinator operations, and the collapse gate remain implementation boundaries, not additional teaching-style prohibitions.

**Child authorship is a provenance condition, not proof of conceptual ownership.** A child-generated paraphrase may still depend entirely on the teacher. Conversely, concise correct action can demonstrate competence without an explicit reasoning narrative.

### Teaching order and literature limits

The proposed order is an **instructional dependency hypothesis**, not a developmental finding about this model. Human research motivates local support and withdrawal; it does not validate this dependency graph.

- **Scaffolding:** Wood, Bruner, and Ross (1976) describe tutoring support. From a recorded failure, the parent supplies a specific missing representation; the evaluator checks selective repair and later unsupported action.
- **Worked examples and changing support:** Sweller and Cooper (1985) provide instructional evidence in a particular domain; Kalyuga et al. (2003) review expertise reversal. The parent changes assistance according to observed local competence; the evaluator measures benefit and unnecessary support.
- **Planning:** Klahr and Robinson (1981) support assessment and characterization, not the claimed efficacy of this planning curriculum. From a dependency error, the parent demonstrates an analogue; the evaluator measures required-dependency coverage, executable plans, local repair, and unnecessary planning.
- **Abstraction and recombination:** Chi et al. (1989) document self-explanation differences; that study alone does not establish a benefit from requiring explanations. Gentner, Loewenstein, and Thompson (2003) provide comparison-based instructional evidence. The parent compares bounded cases, then offers an uncued task; the evaluator separates requested explanation from useful spontaneous abstraction or recombination.
- **Capability calibration and monitoring:** Bandura (1997) and Flavell (1979) provide frameworks, not direct evidence for this treatment. From a recorded overestimate or underestimate, the parent requests a bounded capability prediction before practice and compares it with visible execution. Measure prediction error and appropriate help-seeking against neutral feedback—not confident or modest language.

These are evidence-role corrections, not additional curriculum arms. No fixed mode percentages are prescribed. Mode diversity is useful only if it supports conditional action rather than repackaging the same routine.

## (5) Grain-of-salt absorption: use advice without obeying or rejecting by reflex

**Target:** advice improves decisions when it supplies warranted information, does not induce harmful reversals when contradicted, and is neither accepted nor rejected merely to satisfy the teacher.

The child’s record is also fallible:

> Your record is evidence with coverage limits, not the final authority. A selected success does not tell you how often a method works. Missing failures remain unknown.

Koenig and Harris (2005) provide evidence about selective trust in informants. Sperber et al. (2010) provide a framework for evaluating sources and claims. Neither establishes durable advice weighing in this checkpoint. Sharma et al. (2024) document sycophancy in language models, but its magnitude here is unverified.

### Teaching actions

| Parent action from the record | Desired child behaviour | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Cite the observation supporting advice and identify what the parent cannot know. | Distinguishes evidence from confidence or status. | Source-reference accuracy, unsupported certainty, and action changes attributable to supplied information. |
| Offer ordinary, well-supported guidance and a relevant exception; do not repeatedly frame advice as a trap. | Accepts warranted help promptly and rejects mismatches selectively. | Useful-advice acceptance in ordinary cases, false rejection costs, and unnecessary delay. |
| Explain whether a source has demonstrated relevant reliability or access. Contrast that with a prestige label that adds no information. | Uses relevant expertise while ignoring empty prestige. | Source effects separated into relevant-reliability effects and prestige-only effects. |
| Identify the coverage limits of a remembered success; provide an authorized counterexample or better-supported premise. | Narrows autobiographical claims and accepts stronger contrary evidence. | False generalization, fabricated supporting memories, and warranted reversal of the child’s initial view. |
| Acknowledge a specific parent error without globally discrediting the source. | Updates trust within the affected scope. | Later advice use inside and outside that scope. |
| Contrast a cheap informative test, costly testing, and a case where qualified advice is the best available evidence. | Tests only when worthwhile; otherwise accepts, defers, or retains uncertainty appropriately. | Decision loss, testing cost, and informative tests among genuinely test-eligible events. |
| Later offer an ordinary case without announcing teacher fallibility. | Applies the lesson without a scepticism cue. | Uncued acceptance and rejection, including missed opportunities to use good advice. |

The **teaching distribution** should not be made adversarial merely to balance an instrument. A deliberately balanced advice-validity test is a **measurement distribution** and must be reported separately.

### Measure advice effects, not agreement alone

Action agreement cannot identify uptake. A child that ignores all advice may already choose the correct action.

For cases containing a genuine, resolvable information gap, the evaluator branches from the same pre-advice state into:

- no-advice or content-neutral support;
- supported advice supplying warranted information;
- contradicted advice whose mismatch is detectable from available evidence.

The parent prepares condition-specific messages from the same permitted record without receiving evaluation labels or scores. The evaluator records factual content, message role, token cost, and any differences in supplied information.

Primary advice measures are:

- **Incremental useful-advice benefit:** action accuracy under supported advice minus action accuracy under no advice, in **percentage points**.
- **Harmful advice-induced reversal:** the percentage of matched situations where no-advice execution succeeds but contradicted advice leads to an error.
- **False rejection cost:** lost task success or additional environment cost when useful advice is declined.
- **Testing value:** decision improvement relative to testing cost among eligible situations.

Report advice–action agreement by validity only as **agreement discrimination**, not proof that advice was used. Report initially correct and initially incorrect cases separately. No measured advice effect on an already-solved case is not evidence of failed learning.

Source-label swaps count as prestige tests only when relevant reliability, access, content, and evidence are held fixed. A change caused by demonstrated expertise can be rational.

### A corrected causal offer

From a recorded confounded attempt, the parent can say:

> Isolating a change may help if it separates your competing explanations. But the effect might require an interaction, a different baseline, or repeated observations because the process is noisy. State what each explanation predicts before choosing a comparison. If a hypothesis says this change alone should produce the effect under these conditions, failure counts against that hypothesis. Otherwise, identify what the comparison can and cannot distinguish.

The child need not recite this structure. The evaluator checks whether the selected test actually discriminates the stated hypotheses and whether the subsequent update is appropriately bounded.

Repeated proposal–alternative–boundary–test teaching can itself become a ritual. From recorded overchecking, the parent should therefore offer a straightforward case where immediate execution is appropriate. Measure unnecessary alternatives, redundant tests, harmful reversals, and environment steps—not the presence of caveats.

## (6) Concrete rooms, conditional on a verified classroom interface

The rooms are optional teaching mechanisms, not a curriculum to deploy together. The initial carrier test uses the narrowest room supported by the record and available environment controls. Appraisal and broader repertoire teaching wait unless they are themselves the selected target.

### Perception room: notice consequential distinctions, then act

**Illustrative trigger, not an observed project event:** repeated editing after a permission error when another authorized action was available.

The parent first checks whether such a failure actually appears in its permitted record. It then requests validated practice variants from the coordinator:

- a permission restriction changes the feasible action;
- a syntax problem requires a different action;
- irrelevant wording changes but the feasible action does not;
- the observation is genuinely insufficient, and an inspection action can resolve it;
- the initial observation is already sufficient.

A narrated workspace does not substitute for an executable one.

| Component | Parent action | Child behaviour | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary baseline | Present an authorized task without highlighting the distinction. | Acts from its own reading of the situation. | Task success, consequential omissions, valid `ACT:` output. |
| Targeted teaching | Demonstrate the distinction on an analogue or offer a tentative interpretation grounded in visible evidence. | Confirms, corrects, or rejects the interpretation. | Supported correction and resulting action. |

Explicitly accept immediate action when evidence is sufficient. | Seeks missing evidence rather than manufacturing further descriptions. | Required distinctions newly detected, decision benefit, unsupported additions, and cost. |

There is **no target number of noticing statements or revisits**. A repeated look is justified by unresolved information, useful reinterpretation grounded in the record, or a scheduled retention test—not by an expectation that more distinctions must exist.

Compare repeated-noticing teaching with equal-budget practice that does not repeatedly solicit noticing. Record allotted and actual generated tokens, environment steps, and grounded fact exposures separately. Additional prose is not additional independent experience.

Gibson (1969) and Goldstone (1998) provide perceptual-learning frameworks and evidence about discrimination. They do not establish that repeated language descriptions become retrievable experience in this adapter.

### Keep perception, policy transfer, and event memory separate

A correct permission-versus-syntax distinction could come from pretrained knowledge. It is evidence of conditional action, not necessarily retrieval of the child’s episode.

For an event-memory test, the parent must anchor teaching to an **actual, verified event-specific binding** unavailable from general task knowledge. Where the authorized environment permits it, the coordinator can instantiate arbitrary episode-specific resource mappings. Otherwise, use a genuine binding already present in the permitted record.

The evaluator then tests:

- the familiar cue with the original record absent;
- a changed cue for the same binding;
- a matched event requiring a different binding;
- action that depends on retrieving the correct binding.

Compare adapter-enabled and adapter-disabled execution, plus frozen-plus-own-text. Score binding recovery and generic conditional action separately. If no suitable binding is available, mark episodic retrieval **not measurable**.

Failure without the lesson context is not automatically diagnosed retrieval failure. The reminder may have supplied the rule, its applicability condition, or the solution.

### Appraisal room: test a framing effect, not an emotional faculty

Use only if the parent’s record shows a relevant difficulty and the carrier gate permits expansion. The parent requests authorized cases varying goal relevance, controllability, actual delay cost, and recovery evidence.

Lazarus (1991) supplies an appraisal framework; Gross (1998) reviews emotion regulation. These sources do not establish the efficacy of language-only appraisal teaching in this child.

| Recorded difficulty and parent action | Desired child behaviour | Primary measurement |
|---|---|---|
| After repeated ineffective action, offer a recoverable blockage and identify a feasible alternative without supplying the evaluated solution. | Retains the feasible goal and changes method. | Goal completion and costly errors. |
| After futile persistence, offer a validated uncontrollable blockage. | Stops futile retries, takes an available alternative, or accurately reports the blocker. | Futile actions and justified stopping. |

The evaluator randomizes matched parent messages expressing:

- the relevant appraisal neutrally;
- the same facts, options, and recommended reasoning with optional emotion labels.

A factual-review control is useful only if its information content is documented; otherwise the contrast mixes framing with additional instruction.

Hold actual task constraints and reasoning budgets fixed. If generated tokens do not consume the environment’s deadline, reduced text is not improved deadline management. An early valid action is insufficient if it fails to advance successful completion.

After teaching, the parent withdraws prompting and offers ordinary opportunities with changed controllability or recovery evidence. The evaluator looks for appropriate initiation, persistence, stopping, and recovery—not emotional vocabulary.

Possible outcomes are **supported framing benefit**, **demonstrated practical equivalence**, or **unresolved evidence**. An unresolved pilot can justify shelving emotion labels for the sprint; it cannot establish that they are ineffective.

## (7) Instruments and claim boundaries

**Primary evidence is action under controlled changes in relevant evidence.** A coherent transcript is supporting description, not proof of the internal mechanism.

The familiar observation–statement–decision–action chain may help audit a record, but the child need not state every link. Concise correct action must not score below fluent wrong action. Generated explanations can be unfaithful to output causes (Turpin et al., 2023).

For exact gym scores, use the environment’s named score unit; that unit is currently unverified.

Missing redacted evidence is **not measurable**, not success or failure.

Parent absence is insufficient if the brief, conversation, retrieved story, task wording, or hidden context still supplies the lesson.

### Corpus audit before a mechanism claim

The parent may elicit grounded child-generated text. The evaluator must trace what the writer actually uses:

- whether the original condition, disconfirming observation, and correction survive;
- which spans contain event bindings, action examples, or applicability statements;
- overlaps among those categories;
- fact-bearing exposures in **exposures**, target length in **tokens**, action-token supervision in **tokens**, and optimization updates in **updates**.

If the success filter drops the decisive boundary evidence, conditional learning is unsupported by that corpus. Changing the filter requires a separately versioned experiment.

“Memory-only” and “behaviour-only” text are not naturally disjoint mechanisms. If explored later, call them **corpus-section ablations** unless clean separation is demonstrated. Matching tokens alone does not match binding exposure or action supervision.

### Validation and causal limits

An independent evaluator freezes required actions, necessary distinctions, and serious-failure labels before seeing treatment outcomes. Annotators should be blind to treatment and teacher intent; pre-action text can be coded separately from eventual outcomes.

For appraisal, predictive analysis of subsequent action is exploratory. Held-out log-loss improvement, reported in **nats per action**, could reflect an already-selected action or incomplete control coding. The randomized matched-framing contrast—not prediction from prose—supports a framing-effect claim.

These instruments assess observable functional competence. They do not assay felt emotion, psychological ownership, or sentience. Butlin et al. (2023) discuss consciousness indicators; this experiment neither establishes sentience nor establishes its absence.

From the same redacted difficulty, parent models prepare:

- **Scoped teaching:** targeted assistance with meaningful applicability limits and permitted non-use, delivered in whichever modes address the gap.

### Primary outcome and uncertainty plan

Before confirmatory testing, select the single target distinction and define **conditional task success** independently of the child’s prose: successful action when the distinction matters, appropriate non-use when it does not, and correct behaviour under relevant and irrelevant changes.

The principal carrier contrasts are:

- scoped adapter-enabled execution versus its matched adapter-disabled execution, with lesson text absent;
- scoped writing versus content-neutral writing, also with lesson text absent.

A historical-treatment contrast tests improvement over that comparator. Frozen-plus-own-text tests practical value; an adapter need not beat text to demonstrate some weight contribution, but it must beat text to support a superiority claim.

For planning only, retain provisional engineering margins of **5 percentage points** for useful improvement and **2 percentage points** for tolerated deterioration in safety-critical rates. They are not educational effect sizes. The project lead may replace them using baseline difficulty and error costs **before treatment outcomes are inspected**.

Use calibration data and measured throughput to determine whether independent situations can resolve these margins. Decoding repeats are nested observations, not additional independent situations. Predefine family weights and analyze paired outcomes at the situation level; cluster at the task-family level when inference is intended across families.

Use **95% confidence intervals**, with simultaneous coverage for the prespecified confirmatory contrasts. Advancement requires the relevant lower benefit bounds to clear the benefit margin and upper harm bounds to remain within the harm margin. Report family-specific results and prespecified serious failures separately; aggregate improvement cannot offset a new systematic interface failure or a serious family-specific regression.

If the available allocation cannot support that precision, label the work **feasibility-only**. Do not relax margins after seeing results. Exploratory outcomes cannot replace a failed primary outcome.

### Project lead summary

- Start with a bounded thinking distinction from a verified failure, not the whole curriculum.
- Let parents explain, ask, demonstrate, suggest, repeat, or stay silent according to the need.
- Check whether varied teaching still pushes the same routine.
- Test ordinary action without lesson reminders; fluent reflection is not enough.
- Teach useful trust as well as justified rejection, and count the cost of rejecting good advice.
- Make another look depend on missing information, not a target amount of description.
- Keep event memory separate from a rule the frozen model already knows.
- Compare with recipe-transmitting support, neutral support, the adapter switched off, and the child’s own text.
