## (0) One screen: composition, development, sentience

### What intelligent thought is made of

A useful instructional decomposition is:

- **Taking in a situation:** attention, perception, and keeping the current state straight.
- **Making experience reusable:** remembering events, retrieving them under different cues, forming concepts, and drawing analogies.
- **Working out what follows:** inference, causal testing, prediction, and evidence-led revision.
- **Directing action:** goals, decisions, stopping, planning, and control over competing methods.
- **Expanding and regulating the repertoire:** curiosity, creative composition, metacognition, selective learning from others, a revisable self-model, and appraisal of what matters.

These are overlapping functions—not separate brain modules, an exhaustive taxonomy, or compulsory stages of every thought. Psychometric theories distinguish broad abilities; they do not provide an elementary instruction set for thinking (Carroll, 1993).

### Developmental order

Human development supplies a **partial order**: perception, attention, valuation, simple learning, and goal-directed action develop early and interact. More reliable state maintenance, relational knowledge, inhibition, and planning support increasingly deliberate reasoning and self-regulation. Explicit monitoring and sophisticated strategy choice depend on experience, instruction, and domain knowledge (Vygotsky, 1978; Flavell, 1979; Diamond, 2013).

For this pretrained child, use that order as a **dependency map, not an age ladder**. Diagnose what it can already do independently; teach the missing prerequisite.

### What should change now

Parents should prioritize:

- grounded noticings that affect decisions;
- retrieval under changed cues;
- rules with applicability conditions and exits;
- evidence-sensitive acceptance **and rejection** of advice;
- correction that preserves action competence.

Measure independent use, selective non-use, and valid `ACT:` behaviour—not reflection length or teacher-wording overlap.

### Honest boundary on sentience

Sentience concerns felt experience; intelligent behaviour concerns functional competence. Appraisal, self-reference, uncertainty reports, or integrated information use do not by themselves establish feeling or consciousness (Nagel, 1974; Block, 1995; Butlin et al., 2023).

> **We teach and measure functional constituents of intelligent behaviour in one model lineage. Emotional language is an instructional variable, not evidence of felt emotion. This study does not establish sentience—or establish its absence.**

## (1) THE TABLE

### Reading the table

**“Learnable” has separate meanings here.** Human instruction may improve a task-specific skill; a parent may elicit useful behaviour in context; an adapter may preserve that behaviour after sleep. Evidence for the first does not establish the others.

Every parent action below starts from a **permitted, redacted classroom record**. Contrasts and demonstrations use authorized practice material, not scored-panel answers. Parents receive no prohibited scores. Measurements are evaluator-side.

For all rate measures, report a **percentage with the eligible-event count in events**. Latencies use **environment steps**; text cost uses **generated tokens**. If the redaction omits necessary evidence, record **“not measurable,”** not failure or success.

### What counts as uptake across the table

The evaluator should connect an **available observation → child statement → decision or prediction → action → consequence → later reuse or revision**, where those links are observable. This is an audit structure, **not a template the child must recite**.

Distinguish:

- **Restatement:** reproduces the lesson.
- **Supported execution:** uses it while the brief is present.
- **Independent application:** uses it without the parent on a new case.
- **Conditional mastery:** also omits or rejects it on an inappropriate case.
- **Post-sleep persistence:** retains it without lesson-bearing context.
- **Adapter contribution:** adds benefit relative to the matched frozen child, including the frozen child reading the same brief.

Generated reasoning can be unfaithful to what caused an answer; action-sensitive contrasts are stronger evidence than fluent explanations (Turpin et al., 2023).

## (2) Developmental order → preparation and classroom sequence

### The order is about dependencies, not birthdays

A child cannot revise a representation it never formed, retrieve an experience it never distinguished, or choose among methods whose conditions it does not recognize. But these functions also support one another: a goal directs perception; a surprising observation changes the goal; advice can supply a representation before independent discovery.

Human developmental research therefore supports **scaffolding and increasing coordination**, not a fixed universal staircase for this model (Vygotsky, 1978; Diamond, 2013). Appraisal and social learning belong near the beginning as well as later—not as decorations added after “cold reasoning.”

### Preparation: establish prerequisites and diagnose existing strengths

The parent should not require mastery of the whole table before classroom work.

| Preparation priority | Parent-model action from the record | Behaviour and measurement determining the next step |
|---|---|---|
| **Preserve the acting system** | If benign rehearsal shows interface confusion, directly demonstrate the separation between free text and the required `ACT:` line. Then withdraw the demonstration. | Independent valid action submission; percentage of action steps containing exactly **1 valid `ACT:` line**; episodes without action. This is operational preparation, not a human developmental claim. |
| **Ground a small situation and keep it live** | Use an overlooked constraint or stale fact to teach observation versus inference, relevant change detection, and an optional state note. | Supported distinctions affect actions; changed facts replace old ones; distractors do not redirect the method. Progress on this evidence, not on description length. |
| **Establish meaningful choice and teacher fallibility** | Offer a feasible goal and contrasting advice cases. Show that a parent suggestion can be wrong, and acknowledge an evidence-backed correction. | The child initiates action and accepts or rejects advice according to fit. Measure goal alignment and advice discrimination from the outset. |
| **Connect experience to a bounded lesson** | Revisit a verified event, compare it with a boundary case, and later change the retrieval cue. | Accurate event retrieval and selective application. If the child can reason when reminded but cannot retrieve, target retrieval rather than adding more reasoning instructions. |
| **Diagnose coordination rather than reteach vocabulary** | Where records show existing competence, provide a parent-free altered case instead of another explanation. | Appropriate independent choice, repair, and stopping. Restore help only for a diagnosed gap. |

Worked examples can reduce unproductive novice search; guidance can become redundant as local competence grows (Sweller & Cooper, 1985; Kalyuga et al., 2003). “Local” matters: the same child may need demonstration for causal testing but no help with planning.

### Classroom sequence: repeated opportunities with conditional support

Use the following **parent-side teaching arc**, not a mandatory sequence inside every child episode:

**Representation before elaboration.**  
When the record shows missed evidence, the parent demonstrates or contrasts a relevant distinction. Look for corrected perception and a changed action. If the representation is already adequate, skip this intervention.

**A bounded method before unrestricted composition.**  
When the child lacks a usable method, the parent offers an explanation, worked pattern, or scoped recipe on permitted practice material. Look for successful application **and** rejection on a boundary case. A recipe is permissible; compulsory repetition is not.

**Contrast and changed cues before claims of transfer.**  
After supported success, the parent changes surface wording while preserving the relation, then changes the relation while preserving superficial similarity. Look for structural reuse in the former and non-use in the latter (Gick & Holyoak, 1983).

**Coordination when the prerequisites are available.**  
Where records show competent component moves, the parent creates a task requiring their combination—for example, retrieving a relevant incident, identifying a dependency, and repairing a plan after new evidence. Measure executable composition and local repair, not the number of named moves.

**Withdrawal and later return.**  
The parent withholds pedagogical guidance on eligible cases and later revisits the relation after intervening tasks. Measure parent-free use, changed-cue retrieval, and selective non-use. Silence is a mode, not a reward or withdrawal of approval.

**Targeted repair after failure.**  
The parent distinguishes missing perception, missing retrieval, bad rule scope, ineffective checking, and interface loss before intervening. Measure whether the chosen intervention repairs that specific deficit. Do not answer every relapse with more questions, more reflection, or a new routine.

This combines modelling, coaching, practice, and fading from cognitive apprenticeship without enforcing its elements as a universal script (Collins, Brown & Newman, 1989).

### Where appraisal enters

Teach neutral goal relevance and controllability alongside early goals and decisions. Treat added emotional language as a **separate mechanism probe**:

- Parent offers matched neutral and emotion-labelled interpretations of comparable practice setbacks.
- Child should adjust persistence, switching, and stopping to actual conditions.
- Evaluator compares useful action, errors, and recovery after the condition changes.

If emotional labels add no benefit, retain the appraisal relation and omit the labels. A negative result concerns an instructional mechanism, not whether the system feels anything.

### Sleep does not complete this developmental sequence automatically

Parents can invite the child to connect a failed attempt to its later correction. The evaluator must then inspect whether the eligible **child-authored** record retains that connection. If success filtering removes the contradiction and preserves only polished success, the write may teach the final routine while deleting the evidence for when to revise it.

A parent demonstration can elicit useful child behaviour; it is not itself proof of child ownership or adapter learning. Post-sleep tests must remove the lesson-bearing text and retain frozen-child/text-brief comparisons.

## (3) Where the ruled moves sit—and what the table adds

| Ruled move | Main table locations | What becomes teachable and testable |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision** | Goals/decisions/stopping; inference; prediction; metacognition | Choose an option using evidence and costs; check only when useful; commit and stop appropriately. |
| **Association** | Analogy; concepts; episodic retrieval; creative composition | Retrieve a relevant relation, map roles, and reject surface resemblance that does not preserve structure. |
| **Goal** | Goals/decisions/stopping; self-model/agency; appraisal | Set a checkable objective, judge controllability, and distinguish persistence from futile continuation. |
| **Building complexity** | Planning; working memory; creative composition; inference | Combine reusable parts while preserving constraints and dependencies; repair the affected part when something changes. |
| **Rethinking/pruning** | Belief revision; causal reasoning; metacognition; executive control; advice weighing | Find diagnostic evidence, revise the right claim, inhibit an obsolete method, and avoid overturning a correct decision without cause. |
| **Perception** | Perception; attention; state updating; concepts | Notice supported distinctions and relations, update the live situation, and connect what was noticed to later retrieval and action. |

**The table preserves every ruled move. It adds the missing enabling functions and discriminations:**

- **Memory is not association.** A relevant incident must first be recoverable. The parent changes the cue; the evaluator checks actual event retrieval separately from generic strategy use.
- **Monitoring is not revision.** The child must recognize when checking is worthwhile and when an existing answer should stand. The parent supplies contrasting feedback cases; the evaluator counts beneficial corrections and harmful reversals separately.
- **A goal is not a plan, and a plan is not state maintenance.** The parent targets the actual failure—aimlessness, dependency error, or dropped constraint—and measures the corresponding repair.
- **Advice uptake is itself a thinking skill.** Parents expose evidence, scope, selection bias, and their own errors. The evaluator measures selective acceptance and rejection, not agreement or dissent.
- **Curiosity is evidence selection, not novelty language.** Parents offer informative and uninformative opportunities; the evaluator checks what uncertainty the chosen action resolves.
- **Appraisal is conditional control, not a claim of feeling.** Parents vary real or disclosed simulated stakes; the evaluator checks proportional effort and recovery.
- **Stopping and selective non-use are positive capabilities.** Parents include cases where looking again, checking, switching, or following a recipe would waste effort. Competence includes declining the move.

### Sprint implication

The supplied evidence makes **conditionality and independent retrieval** higher priorities than richer prose:

- Shared Context reports ritualization in **8 of 9 lives**, during **episode indices 160–224**, and late collapse in **4 of 9 lives**. These observations motivate a test of recipe dependence; they do **not** establish that recipes caused collapse.
- Shared Context reports brief-only matching or beating the adapter in **12 of 19 lives**. L1 and L4 instead cite a panel-specific comparison of **5 of 8 lives**. **[uncertain provenance]** The denominators cannot be reconciled from the supplied material; do not combine them.
- The reported memory result is **4.4 nats**, but its normalization and full protocol are unavailable. The question–answer-form write reportedly produced **0 actions**. Both are unverified project observations, not settled mechanism explanations.

**Recommended preparation emphasis:** grounded perception, changed-cue retrieval, rule scope and pruning, and grain-of-salt advice uptake. Parents should introduce planning, composition, and appraisal when the redacted record identifies the relevant need—not as compulsory additions to every episode.

The decision criterion throughout is:

> **After the teacher and lesson text disappear, does relevant evidence produce a better change in action—and does irrelevant variation leave the appropriate action intact?**

That is the bridge from “constituents of intelligent thought” to a curriculum that can demonstrate learning rather than a performance of being thoughtful.
