You are asked to design the first 'level-3' (preschool) lives of a learning agent so that a single measurable behaviour moves. Ideas first; concrete; cite the evidence you rely on; mark what you cannot verify; no fetching. Up to ~1,800 words.

# Setting (as in q13; read as given)
Frozen 7B agent (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) in a compiler-optimisation gym: each episode = 16 ticks; each tick the child writes PREDICT (a number), NOTE (free text), ACT (a pass sequence), sometimes RECALL; the environment returns the measured outcome ('instructions 857 -> 470 (45.2% reduction)') AFTER the ACT. Every 32 episodes the child sleeps: notes/thoughts from successful episodes are compiled into a corpus (each item = 'Program <id>. My thinking: <child text> So I did: <passes> -> <outcome>', the outcome line appended by the harness) and a rank-8 LoRA is retrained (whole-text loss, 3 epochs), kept only if a fixed panel score does not fall. Parents (a 14B model, never seeing scores) talk to the child in a classroom between episodes. The project lead's rulings: parenting teaches how to think/perceive/reflect/judge/plan/execute; recipes may be offered, never enforced on the child; gates on the corpus are allowed; there is a progression — level 1 base model with prompting, level 2 a LoRA pre-trained on an instruction base ('birth'), level 3 preschool = parenting gyms with quick built tests where the systems are made to work with some actual learned effort, level 4 school = the same curriculum with the real world attached; then deployment (parented x sleep-running-vs-frozen 2x2 on unseen programs). The lead also said: teach the idea plus a numbered baseline ('do it 10x/20x/30x depending'), and let the gym add nuance; hardcoded rules (e.g. 'write 16 perceptions per event') are floors, not intelligence. Six days remain.

# Evidence
- Your q13 gate, measured retrospectively on 28 lives (25 finished + 3 running): admission (G and N and not D) 0.0-2.5% of new notes in every life; the articulation rate (first-person record with a measured outcome) in the final four sleeps is 0.000 in ALL 28 lives; 0-4.1% of any final corpus would survive the gate (best: a parented life, 2.5% admitted, 4.1% surviving).
- Rejection families and counts (per life, whole life): no-content (no pass run here and no result) up to 796; prediction-only (a pass named, only expected numbers) up to 1,214; no-result-number up to 1,177; practice-only (the slogan 'Form expectations before acting. Write down what I learn.') up to 1,026; result-numbers-unmatched (numbers that agree with no act of the episode) up to 294.
- A structural cause: in the tick format PREDICT and NOTE are written BEFORE the ACT and its outcome; there is no slot after the outcome, so 'what happened' has nowhere to be written by the child; the harness appends it. The lead has said the loop's tokens (goal/state start-end, plan/sub-plan/execute) are his to set and that behaviour compilation will cut on them.
- Earlier audits: the child's notes drift to recipes (pass list + expected %) in 17 lives and to first-person slogans in 8; the parented lives echo the parents' phrases ('be concrete'); the child does not invent facts (provenance >= 98.5%); the bridge experiment shows that REQUIRING the artifact form (end each perception with the canonical sentence) makes the child's prose about the fact 3x more often and the fact then stores.
- Cost: ~4.2 GPU-minutes per episode; a sleep ~10 min; 16 A40 GPUs on two nodes, most busy for the next ~24 h with other queued work; a full 1024-episode life takes ~3 days.
