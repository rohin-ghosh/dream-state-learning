## Execution and interpretation

Formation, write and readout are separately prepared, CPU tested, launched,
collected and released by Main; never automatically chained. Existing bounded
workers/cleanup and six-hour lease margin remain. Exact immutable source,
commands, process IDs, phase caps and native receipts must be logged before
each actual launch. The current document is a specification, not readiness.

Main chooses the first native sample after the birth component result; there
is no DERANGED parenting branch in this first sample. Consequently it cannot
identify the effect of birth versus no birth or a birth-by-parenting interaction.
Historical fresh-base runs are context, not matched contemporary controls.
One birth root and one descendant optimizer seed are exploratory, not the
three independent seeds required for numerical robustness claims.

Preserve selection failures and all tried conditions. A null result can
localize participation, source quality, write or readout failure; it does not
prove birth cannot help. Component syntax is not decision acquisition, and
record fidelity is not pre-action competence. No clean ancestry, mechanism
freeze, general G3/P1/G5/H1/H2 or completed self-improvement claim follows.

Simple hygiene applies now. The formal C11 guard remains deferred to the final
paper-grade C11 run. Agent implementation is disjoint: Arendt formation runner,
Einstein warm-writer binding, Ampere readout adapter; Main alone owns native
execution and integration. No third-party communication or extra paid capacity.
