## Final positions

These decisions rely on your supplied operational facts and Memo 3, not independently inspected code or logs.

**R1 — Accept co-primary; withdraw frozen-first.**  
Rohin’s thesis justifies equal billing:
- **Frozen transfer:** taught-minus-base mean score on matched report problems.
- **Continual deployment:** taught-minus-control normalized area under the 512-problem performance curve, with endpoint performance also reported.

Pre-register that the **joint claim requires both endpoints**; success on only one supports only that narrower claim. If either endpoint alone can declare overall success, specify multiplicity handling. The abstract should lead with the continual result, report frozen transfer alongside it, and call the former an **end-to-end system comparison**, not isolated learning efficiency when replay and compute differ. Co-primary means neither result can disappear when inconvenient.

**R2 — Prefer two short childhoods, conditional on completing both comparisons.**  
Given no supplied dose–response evidence that 40 sleeps crosses a threshold that 20 does not, two independent childhoods buy more evidence against a lucky lineage than doubling one lineage’s duration. They remain a small existence demonstration, not a reliable population estimate.

**There is no defensible minimum sleep count in the supplied evidence.** Twenty is a planning estimate, not a demonstrated sufficient dose; forty is not a guarantee. Count completed, activated writes and actual target exposure—not merely scheduled sleeps. Change to one long childhood only if *pre-freeze* evidence supports the longer dose or measured throughput makes two completed comparisons infeasible; do not choose after inspecting final outcomes.

**R3 — Accept, with an explicit gate/report distinction.**  
Panel v1 is child-unseen initially but **researcher-used and operationally reused**; once it gates updates, it also influences the deployed system. It is not an untouched report set. Seal v2 by 09-13 without delaying writer validation, freeze its selection and scoring before candidate inspection, and never use it for gating or selection. Twelve programs provide limited breadth; report per-program results and do not count repeated seeds as independent programs. If v2 cannot ship, use your exact exploratory label for all final compiler results.

**R4 — Reasoning-gym is sufficient for this run’s scope, not proof that all six values will transfer.**  
None of the six is inherently unavailable in multi-attempt, verifiable puzzles:

- Changed attempts test **persist-by-changing**.
- Explicit success recognition tests **know-when-done**.
- Pre-action forecasts test **predict-then-check**.
- Forecast-error-conditioned revisions test **surprise-as-evidence**.
- Counterexamples and family holdouts test **scope-the-rule**.
- Explicit alternative evaluation tests **compare**.

But the harness cannot teach or measure **autonomous stopping** if termination is imposed, nor **goal selection** if tasks are assigned. Brier scoring requires a defined event—for example, “this ACT will receive full credit”—not an ambiguous confidence number; partial credit is a separate outcome. Calibration alone does not demonstrate learning from surprise.

These omissions do **not** block compiler transfer under similarly fixed goals and termination. They do limit claims about general agency. Family count alone does not establish suitable teaching opportunities; select and instrument them.

**R5 — Accept operationally, but not as exam blindness.**  
Parents may receive training outcomes and a **coarse, predeclared gate decision/reason code from an operational gate panel**, including central parents. No scores, margins, item identities, or diagnostic detail. A decision bit still conveys evaluation information, so describe parents as **score-blind but gate-informed**. Final report outcomes must remain completely inaccessible. If the spec’s “exam” means the final report set, your proposal cannot use that exam to generate parental feedback; amend the terminology explicitly.

**R6 — Use node 1 GPU 6 + GPU 2 after the baseline, provided that meets the 09-11 smoke window.**  
Do not sacrifice legacy evidence merely to run identical code on node 2. However, code identity does not prove environment or reload equivalence: follow with a short **node-2 load/generate/hash check**, and retain the separate multi-clone fault-injection test.

If waiting for GPU 2 misses the smoke window, preempt **R4 601 first, R3 504 second**, after saving logs and restartable snapshots. That ordering is provisional on your supplied status, not unseen trajectory quality.
