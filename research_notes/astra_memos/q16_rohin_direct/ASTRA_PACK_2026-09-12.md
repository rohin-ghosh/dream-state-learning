"Kickstart self-learning" needs one operational meaning. The confound is that a parented agent might just start higher and then learn at the same rate. That would be an H1 result dressed up as H2. H2 has to be about slope: with the teacher gone, on a gym the teaching never touched, the parented agent climbs faster from its own experience. The clean test is a 2×2 of parented vs. not and sleep loop running vs. frozen at deployment. H1 is the parenting effect with sleep frozen. H2 is the interaction: parenting pays off more when the agent keeps consolidating. If parented+frozen matches parented+sleep, the skills are good but they aren't driving learning.

## 0. One screen

- **Mechanism statement.** Post-training changes behaviour (every chatbot) and creates memory (Physics of LLMs; any post-trained fact). Both are known. What is not known how to do is make an agent's *raw experience* into good post-training data. **The thing we teach is the behaviour of producing good post-training data about its own experience**, so that every input — an environment outcome, a parent, a person — flows through the model's thought into post-trainable, rememberable weight changes at sleep, and the next outputs can be post-trained too. "Everything it thinks turns into post-trainable data." The novelty moves from the learning algorithm to the data-generation disposition.
- **Diagnosis of the current bug.** The LoRA changes behaviour but does not remember; everything runs through thought. That is what you predict if parenting installed *reflection without articulation*: the model learned to reason and not to leave artifacts. The curriculum needs a second thing alongside self-evaluation — produce statements about what happened that are worth training on.
- **The flywheel.** Childhood (high plasticity): parenting builds the flywheel — the better the child learns how to learn, the more it learns from every reinforcement. Once built, childhood parenting is done; the post-childhood agent is deployed into a gym and keeps learning as an adult (plasticity still present, at a different rate; some frameworks stay, some keep breaking and re-forming). **The paper = parenting and birth create the childhood self-learning flywheel; deployment shows the adult flywheel still turning on the test gym.** Scope is finite: a flywheel good enough to learn better than a regular agent on our test gym, not everything in the world (a frontier lab's flywheel would run on ten layers of reflection and sub-agents; ours is scoped to the deadline).
- **Two hypotheses (the other assistant's split, adopted by Rohin).** *H1* (necessary condition, verified by us; TMEM and Allen-Zhu are the reason to expect it, not the evidence): skills taught through think-then-sleep are retained in the LoRA and expressed outside the context where they were taught. *H2* (the paper): an agent carrying those skills improves faster from self-generated experience on an unseen verifiable task than the same model without them, **and the gap depends on continued consolidation** — slope, not starting level. Clean test: a 2×2, parented vs not × sleep loop running vs frozen at deployment. H1 is the parenting effect with sleep frozen; H2 is the interaction. If parented+frozen matches parented+sleeping, the skills are good but they are not driving learning.
- **Not self-distillation.** "The model distills, but it doesn't originate." Every cycle's content traces to something outside — an environment outcome, a parent's correction, a person's response; the model contributes compression, not information. State this in the same paragraph as the mechanism or a reviewer supplies the collapse objection. Corollary: **a memory with no traceable source is confabulated** — a mechanical provenance gate, replacing the vocabulary leakage gate we lost when worlds became semantic.
- **The agent parent amortises a human teacher.** Rohin directs the agents and wrote the parenting corpus; there is one of him and finite hours, so the agents scale what he can teach. Humans would be the best parents (self-judgement, complex goal-setting, reaction to situations, learned values and patterns); the paper claims only what was run and the essay carries the rest.
- **Pointer principle (the other assistant's unification, adopted).** Semantic priors instead of built structure; emotional situations already in pretraining instead of an emotion system; descriptions of judgement and goal-setting already in pretraining instead of teaching them from scratch. The architecture works by routing latent pretrained structure into the decision loop rather than installing anything new — which is why a frozen base is right, not a limitation.
- **First person, procedural.** Pretraining holds "people evaluate their approach before committing" — third person, descriptive, about others. The LoRA needs "I evaluated my approach before committing, and here is what happened" — first person, procedural, with an outcome; only the second is a policy. Parenting is partly a register conversion, and the property is checkable in the corpus (person and tense).
- **What parents teach.** How to perceive strongly enough that things are remembered and connectable; how to think about ideas and extract them from the past; how to spend time thinking; how to plan, execute, meta-think, self-reflect; multilayer, long- and short-term planning; how to move between planning and execution and back.

# Can an agent be taught to turn its own experience into training data that makes its later learning better? Collaborator brief (v3 DRAFT, 2026-09-12)

## 1. The question and the spine

The question is not whether a small model can be trained on its own text. Post-training already changes behaviour and stores facts. The question is whether an agent can be **taught the disposition to turn externally grounded experience into good post-training data about itself**, so that every episode, every parent's correction and every environment outcome flows through its own thinking into weight changes at sleep, and its later learning gets better because of it. In Rohin's words: "everything it thinks turns into post-trainable data... that is the behavior that allows self learning. That is this entire paper." Childhood parenting builds this flywheel. Deployment removes the teacher, not the loop.

1. **Question.** Can taught experience-articulation skills kickstart consolidation-dependent self-learning on a task the teaching never touched?
2. **Mechanism chain.** External event or feedback, then learned attention, interpretation and articulation, then child-authored records, then sleep consolidation into an adapter, then changed behaviour and usable memory, then better learning from the next events.
3. **Scope.** The agent parent amortises a human teacher: Rohin writes the curriculum and there is one of him, so agents deliver it at scale. The model distills but does not originate: every factual item traces to an outcome or an external utterance, which is the defence against the self-distillation and collapse objection. ## 3. Definitions kept from v2

- **Life.** One 1,024-episode run of one agent from a fixed initial prompt in the compiler gym. Score is the fractional shrink in a program's instruction count.
- **Sleep.** Every 32 episodes, the agent's own spans from well-scored episodes are compiled into a corpus, and a fresh adapter is trained from the frozen base on that corpus, never on the previous adapter. The fresh-from-base rule determines what forgetting means here.
- **Committed adapter.** The last gate-accepted adapter. **Final adapter.** A life's last committed adapter.
- **Gate.** Whether the new adapter replaces the committed one. So far a score floor plus a brevity rule.
- **Collapse.** An adapter-on probe more than 0.03 below the same life's adapter-off probe.

## 4. The artifact contract and three separate gates

A record is a child-authored note that identifies the episode and program, the action actually taken, the observed result and its source, and any lesson written separately from the observation. Failed and null experiments are preserved. First person is a taught register (Rohin: pretraining holds third-person descriptions, the adapter needs first-person procedure), but it is not the validity condition, because "I form expectations" is first person and records nothing. The binding to a measured outcome is what is checked.

Three gates answer three questions and must not be merged:

- **Provenance.** Does every factual token trace to an outcome or an utterance? Fails closed on eligible results. Currently passes at 98.5% or better.
- **Record admission.** Does the note name a pass actually run in this episode together with that run's measured result, with numbers that agree with the ledger, and is it not an exact duplicate of the last 64 notes? Run in shadow mode first, because enforcing it today would skip every sleep. Promote to enforced only after two consecutive blocks with an articulation rate of at least 0.20 and at least 64 distinct admitted items per block.
- **Deployment safety.** Does the new adapter fall below the committed one on a fixed panel? The existing gate, with its known failures kept on record.

## 5. The staged causal test

1. **Preschool, three cells, two lives each, 128 episodes, four sleeps.** Cell A adds only a post-outcome writing slot. Cell B adds the parent's artifact lesson with outcome-varied examples. Cell C adds "aim for ten records per episode" as a starting practice. Corpus policy, training and panel held constant. Cell A runs first for 32 episodes as plumbing. Threshold in each of the final two blocks, in both lives of a cell: articulation rate at least 0.20, at least 24 of 32 episodes with a record, at least 98% factual precision on audit. About 61 GPU-hours.
2. **H1 probe inside the same lives.** One probe episode before and after each sleep on held-out programs, fresh context, no lesson or parent present, a neutral post-result field. Preliminary retention evidence only if both lives keep at least 20% articulation on the final post-sleep probes and exceed their pre-training baseline. If all three cells transfer equally, repairing the interaction enabled the skill and parenting did not teach it.
3. **The 2 by 2 on unseen programs.** Parented package versus plain package, sleep running versus frozen, three matched blocks each on one node.
