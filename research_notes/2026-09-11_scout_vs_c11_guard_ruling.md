# Scout hygiene versus the final C11 guard

Date: 2026-09-11

Rohin's ruling:

> Continue mechanism and parenting experiments with the simple hygiene rules
> now. Keep this guard specification ready, but only finish and enforce it for
> the final paper-grade C11 run.

## Current scouts

Mechanism and parenting scouts continue without waiting for the full C11
governance layer. Their minimum hygiene is:

- never parent from a child checkpoint that has entered the deployment gym;
- preserve immutable source-life, corpus, adapter, probe, and gate identities;
- keep parent text out of child training targets unless a separately approved
  experiment explicitly tests otherwise;
- keep numerical writer selection off the report panel where the current
  harness supports it, and disclose any remaining canary/report contact;
- commit a candidate write only after the applicable interface and non-harm
  checks, otherwise keep the previous adapter;
- label historical, one-life, same-panel, resumed, or otherwise confounded
  reads as exploratory and do not promote them into headline evidence; and
- preserve failed and rejected candidates rather than silently replacing
  their receipts.

These checks protect the current learning loop without pretending that a
scout is already a paper-grade causal experiment.

## Final paper-grade C11

The canonical C11 guard specification remains parked and unchanged. Before
the final run it must be finished, ratified, implemented, fault-tested, and
independently reviewed. That later gate covers the stricter evidence identity,
isolation, leakage, stopping-rule, exact-arithmetic, rollback, and release
requirements. Current exploratory runs neither satisfy nor claim to satisfy
that final contract.
