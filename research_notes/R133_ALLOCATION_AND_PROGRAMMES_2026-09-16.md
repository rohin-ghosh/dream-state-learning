## Runtime and programme boundaries

- New startup context is R127's factual orientation with correct local paths,
  actual resource bounds and one paragraph naming the programme. New lanes
  have no automatic shell/tool capability unless separately connected.
- Same rank8, AdamW, sampling, context16384,512-token calls, two experience
  segments per sleep,16 new-row presentations,1 rehearsal presentation and
  objective anchor weight0.25. Parent/environment prefixes remain masked.
- Free distillation is the current third child-generated summary. Re-read
  selects prior passages rather than adding a prescribed recurring format.
  No-distillation preserves available visible history and goes directly from
  two experience segments to sleep. Parent-guided distillation uses labelled
  parent guidance but trains only the child's continuation. Exact implementation
  and exposure differences are posted with tests before launch.
- Seed1 is a **new** lineage, never an alteration of a resumed seed0 child.
  Same prompts/readouts across checkpoints, separate fresh-process evaluation;
  held items/results never reach programme parents or training.
- One asynchronous Astra parent process per parented branch. Children continue
  while requests are pending. Every parent turn records speaker, programme,
  send/delivery time, source response IDs, model, and COMPLETE/MISSING/SILENT;
  no implicit retries or manufactured child replies.
- Emotional-support questions are a programme for examining a model's reported
  activity, judgments and uncertainty—not therapy for a human patient and not
  proof that the model has feelings. Do not transplant dataset speakers' lives,
  diagnoses, identities, or claimed emotions into the child.
- Brain teaching separates retrieved human-neuroscience findings from unproven
  LoRA analogies. Creative writing uses actual drafts, critique and revisions,
  not praise for length or mandatory introspective templates.
