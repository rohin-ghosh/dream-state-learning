# Thesis: parenting teaches the child to think in the form the write can store

Ruled by Rohin, 2026-09-11 (evening), after the completion-frame results (SEQ-038 to SEQ-042). Foundational for the paper. Supersedes any earlier framing in which the sleep/compile machinery is the contribution.

## 0. One screen

- **Parenting is teaching the model how to think: perceive, reflect, judge, plan, execute.** Remembering is one of those skills. The skill is *perceiving well enough that the memory gets written* — the child's thought output is its own training data.
- **The write/read mechanism is not our innovation and should not be a heavy lift.** Physics of Language Models (Allen-Zhu & Li: knowledge is extractable only when it was seen in many forms and many times) and TMEM (self-written canonical pairs absorbed into fast LoRA weights) already show how facts get into weights and out again. Our form differs — bare canonical frames from the child's own perceptions, retrieved by completing the sentence, repeated — but we are not reinventing storage. We adopt it.
- **We know that the write can install behaviour, but the demonstrated behaviour is mostly a supplied fixed routine.** Cell F establishes a narrower memory result: sixteen renderings per occurrence produce a replicated owner-associated canonical-completion component, including on fresh owners. It does **not** establish selective memory. The same adapters shift wrong owners and wrong relations by roughly 0.3–0.5; exposure-parity negatives leave abstention near zero; and increasing rank does not remove the spill (SEQ-039–044). The missing mechanism is conditional routing: can the writer carry several situation-dependent policies without turning them into one broad habit, and can those policies survive a later write?
- **Therefore parenting is not the only remaining problem.** Parenting may teach the child to produce better perceptions, reflections, judgements, plans, and training data, but those outputs are useful only if SLEEP can store and selectively recover their distinctions. The immediate branch gate is a small multi-key conditional writer assay. If it passes, the research question becomes whether parenting teaches the child to author the useful forms itself. If it fails, more parenting scale cannot repair this writer.
- **Sleep and compile are infrastructure rather than the intended novelty, but they are not yet qualified.** Broad recipe sweeps are no longer the critical path; the remaining writer work is the smallest conditional-carriage test and, only after a pass, one unrelated-write survival check. The larger research contribution remains the thought and teaching loop, but it cannot be evaluated causally through an unqualified writer.

## 1. What this changes in the programme

1. **Order of proof.** First prove that the writer can preserve more than one conditional distinction under changed surface forms and without broad spill. Cell F is evidence for an associative completion component, not that proof. Only after a multi-key conditional carrier and one unrelated-write survival test pass should the child-authored-perception comparison decide whether parenting closes the synthetic-to-lived data-quality gap.
2. **The bridge experiment ("child-authored frames").** Same planted events as the car test. Instead of templates, the frozen child (later: the taught child) is shown each event and asked to look at it several times and write what it notices, ending each look with the canonical sentence. Those renderings are the corpus. Compare to F_r16k16 on the same cues: completion, owner-specific contrast, spill, abstention. Variants: (a) the child writes the perceptions and the harness appends the canonical sentence; (b) the child writes the canonical sentence itself (the recall cue is part of the taught format); (c) the child also writes "not observed" for owners it has not met. Failure modes to measure: echo (the same sentence sixteen times), drift (perceptions that change the fact), missing canonical frame.
3. **Parenting content, restated.** The moves already ruled (decision, association, goal, building, rethinking/pruning, perception) are the list of thought forms; each has a storage form the write can consolidate. Parents teach the child to produce thoughts in those forms, by varied modes, never by enforcing a recipe. The thought-structure and perception instruments measure whether the forms appear unprompted.
4. **The paper's spine.** (i) Per-life writes strongly change behaviour, often by installing a global routine, and can also damage the native action interface (measured). (ii) Repetition plus a matching completion cue produces a replicated owner-associated component, but selective routing and abstention fail (measured). (iii) The child does not naturally produce reliably useful, varied thought forms (its notes are echo and recipe; SEQ-036: affect words are bootstrap echo; SEQ-003/005: reflective phrases rise while thinking narrows). (iv) A conditional writer gateway decides whether that bottleneck is representation or the writer itself; only a pass licenses the child-authored bridge and later causal parenting test. The lineage/final-test experiment remains the destination, not an established contribution.

## 2. Boundaries

- No claim that the mechanism is new. Cite Allen-Zhu & Li (Parts 3.1, 3.3) and TMEM for the storage regime; describe our completion-cue result only as owner-associated canonical completion mixed with broad spill.
- No claim about selective memory until a conditional writer passes binding, wrong-key/wrong-relation spill, interface, and later-write survival gates. "Canonical-cue associative recall" alone is insufficient.
- No claim about parenting until child-authored renderings are measured against synthetic ones.

## 3. Immediate consequences (2026-09-11)

- Run the small multi-key conditional writer gateway before the child-authored-frame bridge. It is the cheapest test of the unresolved selective-routing bottleneck.
- Stop the rank and abstention rescue branches: rank 32 did not improve specificity, and exposure-parity negatives did not teach abstention. Treat "THINK decides whether a memory applies" as an architectural hypothesis until directly tested, not as a result.
- Stop broad sleep/compile tuning. Keep only the conditional-carriage gateway, interface/leak checks, and a later-write survival test after a gateway pass on the critical path.
- The canon's EVIDENCE table gets the bridge experiment as the measurement for claim (iv).
