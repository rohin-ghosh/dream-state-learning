The 96 identities, order, potential-outcome graph, and within-root dependency

1. Complete every G0 operation for all roots on CPU.
2. After the disjoint DEV gate passes, execute TEXT on all 96 confirmation
   roots.
3. Execute LoRA only for TEXT-success roots in sealed order.
4. Inside an attempted root, once a required chain factor irreversibly fails,
