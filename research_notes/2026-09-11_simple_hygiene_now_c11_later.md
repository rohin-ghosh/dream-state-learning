# Simple hygiene now; C11 later

Date: 2026-09-11

Binding ruling: continue mechanism and parenting scouts under ordinary
development hygiene. Keep the canonical C11 guard ready, but finish and
enforce it only for the final paper-grade C11 run.

## Current boundary

No current `gpu/` or `organism_v6/` launcher imports or enforces the C11
guard. C11 is therefore not a technical prerequisite for ongoing scouts.

For current experiments:

- start in a fresh run root, or label and verify an exact continuation;
- never parent a checkpoint that has entered the deployment gym;
- freeze the recipe, seeds, panels, parser, thresholds, and parent version
  within a run;
- use matched dose/common seeds, and keep invalid outputs in denominators;
- record source-ledger, corpus, trainer, adapter, and probe identities;
- keep parent text out of the loss target; child-authored committed
  continuations may enter sleep;
- preserve rejected candidates, and retain the previous adapter when a write
  fails an interface or no-harm check;
- do not reuse an output directory unless code, configuration, corpus, and
  adapter identities prove it is the intended continuation;
- label all current results exploratory.

For final C11 only: full principal/object visibility matrices, separated
dispatchers, sealed evaluation-universe custody, four-commit barriers,
exhaustive lifecycle/fault products, exact-byte governance artifacts,
credential/network isolation, and paper-grade claim/release authorization.

## Immediate launcher cautions

1. `gpu/launch_bootstrapped_life.sh` can copy into an existing lineage and
   does not hash-bind `BOOTSTRAP_SOURCE`; do not use it for a clean child
   without independently proving destination freshness and source identity.
2. `organism_v6/classroom_round.py` appends live ledgers and overwrites its
   admission receipt on reuse; existing classroom/self arms remain
   exploratory and are not causal parenting evidence.
3. `gpu/memory_dose_childframes.sh` repairs missing canonical child answers.
   Raw generations are diagnostic; repaired fits cannot support a
   child-authorship or parenting claim and must not seed a clean lineage.
4. `gpu/write_ab.sh` has a known shared-output race and marker-only resume.
   Treat completed pretests as closed; never relaunch into their roots.
5. `gpu/queue_seed_node{1,2}_2026-09-11.sh` contains branches later closed by
   the terminal notes; do not reseed it.
6. `gpu/queue_runner.sh` is acceptable only for disposable scout jobs. It is
   not the C11 executor.
7. `gpu/launch_RP_lives.sh` may silently resume the same prefix/seed; use it
   only for an explicitly audited continuation, never a new clean arm.

The immediate safety problem is experiment mixing through path- or
marker-based resume, not missing C11 machinery. Enforce clean-lineage and
identity checks manually now; reserve the expensive custody system for C11.
