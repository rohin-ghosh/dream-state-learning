# H2 saved-adult deployment audit

Date: 2026-09-11

## Decision

Do **not** launch the proposed twelve-branch, nine-GPU-day RP-versus-R2
deployment as the paper-grade causal parenting experiment.

It can become a small **development-only consolidation pilot** after its
mechanics are repaired. It cannot show that parenting caused a child to learn
faster because the saved RP and R2 adults did not begin from common childhood
roots randomly assigned to parenting or a matched control.

Current mechanism and parenting scouts continue unchanged under the simple
hygiene ruling. The full C11 guard remains parked for the final paper-grade C11
run.

## What the historical comparison can answer

If each saved adult is cloned into sleep-running and sleep-frozen branches,
the within-adult contrast can estimate whether enabling weight consolidation
changes that adult's later adaptation.

The difference between RP and R2 contrasts is only descriptive heterogeneity
between two historical training packages. Parenting, parent model, prompts,
node, restarts, effective age, and prior selection differ between those
packages. The interaction is therefore not a causal parenting effect.

H1 is also not yet verified as a parenting claim. Existing results show that
supplied routines can persist and that instruction changes the child's prose
and subsequent corpus. They do not yet show that a parent-installed learning
disposition survives removal and transfers to fresh authoring or learning.

## Why the proposed launch is not ready mechanically

- The current life runner has no faithful deployment-panel schedule or bound
  initial-adapter path.
- A later sleep trains a new LoRA from the clean base, so it can replace rather
  than extend the saved childhood adapter.
- The current running branch changes both weights and waking briefing text;
  the frozen branch must perform an identical shadow sleep and differ only in
  whether the candidate weights are promoted.
- Repeated visits reuse generation seeds unless the visit index is included.
- The twelve-program panel is already researcher-used and cannot support an
  untouched-task generalization claim.
- The independent units are saved adults, not programs, visits, checkpoints,
  or decode repetitions. Three RP/R2 blocks give only two degrees of freedom
  and an approximate detectable interaction near 0.06, not 0.02.
- `256 = 21 x 12 + 4`; use 252 episodes for a balanced twenty-one visits per
  program.
- Best-achieved score is affected by action count. A first-valid-ACT or fixed-K
  score should be primary, with best score, action volume, invalid rate,
  register, provenance, and retention as diagnostics.

## Small development pilot

Use one same-node RP/R2 block selected by a prebound eligibility rule and hash
lottery, never by panel score. Exclude RP402 until its terminal receipt is
resolved.

For each adult, make sleep-running and shadow-sleep-frozen descendants:

- 252 episodes: twenty-one balanced rounds over twelve programs;
- occurrence-indexed common-random task tapes within the four branches;
- parent absent;
- identical active-text memory and sleep artifacts in running and frozen;
- only candidate-weight promotion differs;
- entry probe and fixed probes after each of eight sleep opportunities;
- descendants are disposable and never return to parenting lineage.

This is a feasibility and variance study, not evidence that parenting caused
faster learning. Estimated cost is about 73 GPU-hours before probe overhead.

## Paper-grade H2

Create new, response-masked, transactional lineages from byte-identical clean
birth roots. Randomize each root to authentic targeted parenting or a
classroom-, prompt-, turn-, and token-matched active sham. After childhood,
remove the parent and independently randomize each child to sleep-running or
shadow-sleep-frozen deployment.

The primary block contrast is:

`(parented_running - parented_frozen) -
 (unparented_running - unparented_frozen)`.

Use at least eight independent parenting/control root blocks as a structural
floor, then power the final count from development variance. Preserve a truly
untouched transfer family, keep every deployment descendant one-way, and use
the same non-weight state in all four cells.

A 252-episode run can test bounded early adaptation. The stronger flywheel
claim requires a longer horizon, positive learning-slope advantage, retained
old competence, an active-text baseline that has plateaued, and a terminal
practical margin.

## Scheduling consequence

Finish the already-running write, child-frame, and long-life scouts. Do not
spend nine GPU-days on the historical twelve-branch H2 until the initial-state,
shadow-sleep, visit-seeding, and measurement defects are fixed. Prefer the
small development pilot only if it will change the clean-lineage final design;
otherwise put those GPU-hours directly into the prospective randomized H2.
