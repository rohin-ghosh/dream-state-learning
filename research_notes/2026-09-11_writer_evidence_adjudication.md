# Writer / storage evidence adjudication

Date: 2026-09-11

Status: independent evidence review. This note authorizes no implementation,
model/tokenizer use, training, GPU work, parenting, C11 work, scientific
claim, release, or submission.

## Bottom line

The project has established **parametric assimilation and cue-conditioned
associative continuation**, not yet a selective experiential memory writer.
A rank-8 LoRA can fit supplied text, alter native behavior, and carry an
owner-conditioned component that is recoverable through a matching completion
prefix. The same writes also install broad habits, spill onto unsupported
owners/properties, and often fail under paraphrased question reads. No tested
condition simultaneously passes binding, specificity, interface preservation,
and non-harm.

The queued `t/u` cells cannot settle the missing writer-capacity question.
They test whether a fixed lesson immediately changes frozen-child prose into a
more write-compatible corpus. Even a positive `t` is not learned parenting.
Therefore V10R1 remains the next highest-information writer gate after the
already-queued cells finish. Its maximum result is deliberately narrow:
supervised carriage of several seen conditional native-action policies without
the enumerated simple shortcuts, spill, or interface damage.

## What the evidence establishes

### 1. The write can change weights and behavior

- Antecedent cells fit their training text by about 4.4 nats while failing the
  out-of-context question read. This proves assimilation of the supplied text,
  not usable event memory.
- Legacy write cell A was positive on both compiler panels in the terminal
  three-life replication (mean `+0.0386 / +0.0161`) but transported the
  bootstrap-supplied routine. It establishes behavioral carriage, not
  discovery or experiential intelligence.
- QA-shaped writers C and C-TMEM damaged or destroyed the native action
  interface. The representation and dialect of the write materially determine
  what is learned.

### 2. Read protocol is part of the mechanism

- Re-reading the old rank-8 occurrence-preserving cell B with the matching
  completion prefix exposed a dose-related owner component
  (`I_d_frame=0.438 [0.044,0.857]`) that paraphrased questions did not expose.
- Completion-frame writes replicated an owner-minus-look-alike component on
  fresh owners and bindings. On the fresh seed-1 banks, rank-8 cells with one,
  four, and sixteen available forms had positive intervals
  (`2.602 [1.971,3.249]`, `1.182 [0.813,1.582]`, and
  `2.013 [1.365,2.748]` nats).
- This licenses “canonical-cue associative recall with an owner-conditioned
  component.” It does not license “selective memory” or “knowledge.” Spill was
  about `0.38--0.40` on those fresh banks and remained far above the frozen
  `0.03` gate.

### 3. More exposure helps the trained completion; more surface forms do not
have a stable ordering

- Keeping occurrences and repeating the completion moves the trained-owner
  completion strongly. In the tested seed-0 material, increasing from the
  low-exposure frame to sixteen renderings per occurrence raised completion;
  increasing to 64 renderings did not improve the 16-rendering result.
- One, four, and sixteen surface forms change ordering across banks/material
  seeds. The evidence does not establish that paraphrase variety itself buys
  specificity. Repeated supported endpoints are the reliable ingredient in
  this test; the best number of forms is unidentified.

### 4. Rank 32 is not an available rescue under the tested recipe

Across six banks from two material seeds, rank 32 never improved the
completion-frame program and often hurt it when few forms were available. On
fresh seed-1 banks, rank-32 versus rank-8 owner contrast was
`0.52 vs 2.60` nats for one form, `0.55 vs 1.18` for four forms, and
`2.36 vs 2.01` for sixteen forms. Paraphrased-question color mass also
collapsed under rank 32. The permitted conclusion is recipe-local: additional
rank at the same optimizer settings does not solve access or specificity.
Rank 8 remains the development default; this is not a general capacity law.

### 5. Declarative negatives did not produce calibrated abstention

- Synthetic “not observed” rows failed at both low dose and exposure parity.
  At roughly 14% of the corpus, `P(" not")` stayed about `0.001` and positive
  recall/specificity weakened.
- Child-negative variant c was not a clean ablation because its positive prose
  differed from b. One bank showed selective movement; two showed weak or
  unselective movement and large positive-memory damage. No calibrated
  abstention finding is established.
- This rules out the tested negative-frame recipe, not parametric negative
  knowledge in general and not a thinker-side applicability judgment.

### 6. Constrained child-generated prose can be write-compatible

On the exploratory seed-0 banks:

| corpus | completion | owner contrast (nats) | spill |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| synthetic F | 0.913 | 2.836 | 0.427 |
| child a, endpoint appended | 0.628 | 1.797 | 0.292 |
| child b, asked to end with endpoint; misses repaired | 0.822 | 2.279 | 0.308 |
| child c, b plus separately generated negatives | 0.506 | 1.506 | 0.226 |

This is feasibility under a constrained, repaired generation protocol. It is
not a self-authorship effect: a and b train on bare `child prose + canonical
endpoint`, with full-token loss, and identical prose would yield identical
training bytes. The b prompt changed the prose: the planted color appeared in
about 78% of b prose versus 26--28% of a prose. The correct owner-color
relation appeared in only 2--4%, so semantic restatement is a live mediator,
not an identified one. Bank variation is large (`a=0.307/0.631/0.945`).

### 7. The current organism does not author post-outcome records

The shadow articulation audit found final-window articulation `0.000` in all
28 lives and at most 2.5% raw-note admission under a lenient episode-grounding
gate. The notes become either recipe rituals or slogans about learning. This
is partly a harness limitation: the current `PREDICT/NOTE` slot precedes
`ACT`, so the child has no native post-outcome slot in which to record what it
did and observed. The result establishes that the present pipeline does not
produce grounded child-authored records; it does not establish that the child
cannot learn to produce them.

## What remains unidentified

1. Can one conservative rank-8 writer carry several simultaneous
   tool-by-mode native-action mappings rather than a global completion/action
   habit?
2. Can it do so while leaving unsupported keys and the unrelated native
   interface unchanged?
3. Does a passing mapping survive a later identity-disjoint cumulative write?
4. Can unrepaired, episode-grounded child action-outcome records produce the
   same effect?
5. Does a lesson change later authoring after the lesson is removed? The
   current t/u prompts cannot answer this.
6. Is the b advantage mediated by correct semantic restatement, full-row loss,
   endpoint placement, or optimizer/material variance?
7. What part of the large bank variation is material, generation, train-seed,
   node, or adapter-load variance?
8. Can a candidate-blind active-text memory using the same admitted records
   match or beat the adapter? A static waking brief is not that comparator.
9. No present result establishes retention, connected memory, traversal,
   expansion, compression, increasing-lifetime improvement, or superiority to
   a strong active-memory baseline.

## Decision on the queued t/u cells

Finish them unchanged. `t` is a prompt-conditioned corpus intervention;
`u` is secondary and is not a clean negative ablation unless it reuses t's
exact positive bytes.

The currently queued seed-0 t/u run is exploratory because the lesson was
designed from seed-0 results. Use it only as a screen:

1. Validate stripping, then audit a reproducible blinded sample for correct
   owner-color assertions and unsupported detail.
2. If t does not meet the registered behavior manipulation
   (`names-both >=0.50` and `t-b >=0.40` in every bank, with the directional
   interval excluding zero), stop this lesson; do not spend six confirmation
   fits.
3. If the behavior manipulation lands but mean storage does not move in the
   predicted direction, do not claim mechanism matching. The only justified
   follow-up is the already-specified semantic-restatement x loss-placement
   2x2, not another forms/rank/dose sweep.
4. If behavior and storage both move without material spill or false-abstention
   worsening, run **t only** on the already-generated fresh seed-1 and seed-2
   banks (six fits). Apply q11's B/S/N and guardrail rules there. Do not promote
   seed 0 or u to confirmation.

Allowed after a confirmatory pass: immediate guidance changed frozen-child
authored training text and improved bounded storage on the tested materials.
Forbidden: the child learned to remember; parenting persisted; authorship
caused storage; selective memory; continual learning.

## V10R1 remains the next highest-value gate

Yes. t/u and V10R1 answer different questions. t/u can improve one completion
corpus while leaving open whether the writer merely learns a global answer
habit. V10R1 forces 16 seen tool-by-mode keys per adapter, complementary maps,
held templates, chance-bounded enumerated shortcuts, exact native actions,
and explicit interface/spill gates. Four fits under the three-A40-hour cap are
the cheapest current falsifier of the missing conditional-writer capability.

One caveat must be preserved: V10R1 supplies eight training examples per key.
The current evidence says exposure matters, so `OPTIMIZATION_INCONCLUSIVE`
would not identify a capacity limit. It would mean only that the exact dose and
optimizer did not move every key by 0.50 nat. Do not rewrite that label as a
writer failure.

### V10R1 stopping rules

- Any byte, tokenizer, mask, identity, receipt, or oracle failure:
  `NONREPORTABLE_ABORT` or `ASSAY_INVALID`; repair the assay, no science claim.
- `OPTIMIZATION_INCONCLUSIVE`: stop downstream qualification. If deadline
  permits, approve one separately bound dose diagnostic; do not alter V10R1
  post hoc.
- `INTERFACE_INVALID`: stop. Fix dialect/interface preservation before any
  parenting scale-up.
- `BINDING_WITH_SPILL`: conditional mapping exists on the tested surface but
  is not localized. Do not call it usable memory and do not advance to a
  paper-grade childhood.
- `GATEWAY_NEGATIVE` with valid assay/optimization/interface: the exact writer
  failed the conditional-binding gate. Redesign representation or read policy;
  do not answer with more rank.
- Only `MULTIKEY_BINDING_PASS` advances to retention. The maximum permitted
  statement remains supervised seen-key conditional endpoint-policy carriage,
  not lived learning or memory.

V10R1 is currently proposal-only and still requires exact human ratification,
the scoped three-file implementation/tests, independent review and advocate,
then a separate execution grant.

## Exact minimal experiment ladder after V10R1

### E1. V10R1 (4 fits, <=3 A40-hours)

Run exactly the reviewed gateway. No extra ranks, forms, negatives, or t/u
crossing. Stop unless the total label is `MULTIKEY_BINDING_PASS`.

### E2. One cumulative-retention write, only after E1 passes

For every passing root-map adapter, build one successor from the clean base on
the exact old corpus plus one equal-sized identity-disjoint new corpus, using
the frozen V10R1 representation and recipe. Re-read old and new keys. Require:

- old per-key/aggregate mapping and interface/spill gates still pass;
- at least 80% of each adapter's original mean NLL gain remains; and
- the new conditional mapping is itself acquired.

If old mapping is erased, stop the childhood program and solve cumulative
retention. A new-only overwrite control is scientifically useful but not
required for this first gate.

### E3. Unrepaired lived-source relay, only after E2 passes

First run a slot-only authoring pilot: give the frozen child a post-outcome
record slot and no automatic endpoint repair. Before fitting, require at least
`A_s^raw >=0.20` and at least 64 distinct, mechanically grounded admitted
items in each of two blocks. Otherwise stop and improve the interaction or
parent instruction; do not pad the corpus.

If the source gate passes, use mirrored two-action worlds and fit the exact
qualified writer to grounded child continuations. Compare correct bindings
against OFF and within-world shuffled action-outcome bindings on fresh held
renderings. This can establish an authentic
THINK -> outcome -> child record -> SLEEP -> later action relay. It still does
not establish parenting or an open-ended organism.

### Conditional diagnostic: restatement x loss placement

Run this only if t clearly changes the registered prose endpoint but fails to
improve storage. Use the four cells S-FULL, S-END, R-FULL, R-END, identical
emitted bytes within prompt type, rank 8, and three optimizer seeds on one
predeclared fresh bank. The compiler may validate/mask but never repair. Stop
before fitting if valid endpoints are below 75%, the relation manipulation is
weak, or unsupported claims exceed the predeclared limit. This localizes
semantic content, loss coverage, and their interaction; it is not a parenting
test.
