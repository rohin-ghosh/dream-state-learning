# Preschool / causal-parenting reconciliation audit

Date: 2026-09-11

Status: **fresh independent design audit; not an authorization to implement,
launch, parent, train, or make a scientific claim.** This reads commit
`6e2f7dee` (Astra q14 / THESIS v2 section 7), `abf06bef` (Rohin's subsequent
thesis additions), and `e17f15c6` (minimal causal parenting / H2 consensus)
against the simple-hygiene-now / C11-later ruling and the child-frame
authorship boundary.

## Verdict

The designs are **architecturally compatible but not evidentially
interchangeable**.

Q14 repairs a real missing affordance: the child currently has no generation
turn after observing an action's result. Its `NOTE_AFTER` slot, execution ID,
factual validator, shadow reporting, and parent-absent probe are useful pieces
of a causal parenting experiment.

Q14's proposed six-life `slot / slot+lesson / slot+lesson+10` grid is instead a
developmental ablation. It cannot establish causal parenting or H2 and its
adults cannot be used for a CompilerGym deployment claim, because their
childhood is already conducted inside CompilerGym. The one-root
`targeted-parent versus active-sham` by `running versus shadow-promotion`
design is the causal canary. Q14 should be nested before it as a disposable
affordance scout, not merged with it or promoted into it.

## What q14 can establish

With the proposed fixed opportunities and denominators, q14 can establish:

1. whether a frozen child writes faithful post-outcome records when the
   interface finally gives it a place to do so;
2. whether an explicit artifact lesson changes record production under that
   teaching configuration;
3. whether a numbered practice suggestion changes production further;
4. whether an accepted write immediately damages or preserves that prompted
   behaviour.

Even a clean positive establishes record production under scaffolding. It
does not establish discretionary memory, reflection, use of a record in a
later decision, adapter-carried parenting, or faster later learning.

## Exact scientific blockers in q14 as a parenting claim

1. **No active sham.** Cell A has no parent while B/C receive parent text,
   examples, context occupancy, and refreshers. `B-A` therefore contrasts a
   whole interaction package, not the content of parenting. `C-B` isolates a
   numbered suggestion more closely, but still estimates that suggestion's
   package effect rather than learned discretion.
2. **Deployment contamination.** The proposed preschool uses CompilerGym,
   actual pass vocabulary, and repeated compiler episodes. Those adults are
   forever task-exposed and cannot later seed a clean CompilerGym deployment
   comparison. Use a task-disjoint nursery or deploy to a different sealed
   domain.
3. **Prompted articulation is not H1.** A neutral field after a measured
   outcome still affords restatement. A post-sleep articulation percentage
   does not show that a learned disposition changed a later choice.
4. **No adapter mediation contrast.** The q14 H1 probe does not bind the
   same `P/S adult x adapter ON/OFF` contrast. Comparing a final adapter to a
   base replay is not enough when parent package, accumulated sleeps, and
   child histories differ.
5. **Two lives are feasibility, not inference.** Requiring both lives to pass
   an arbitrary threshold is a reproducibility screen. It gives no defensible
   sampling uncertainty or general parenting claim.
6. **The scaffold is strong.** A post-outcome field at every tick and “aim for
   10” can install a prompted recording routine. That is a useful floor, not
   evidence that the child learned when a memory is important.
7. **Corpus policy is ambiguous at the causal boundary.** “Gate shadowed,”
   “admitted record replaces the harness line,” and “legacy items stay” must
   be resolved into exact rendered bytes. If harness-authored outcomes remain
   loss-bearing, a positive cannot be attributed to child-authored records.
8. **The parent is fixed-content, not adaptive.** Q14 can test a fixed lesson;
   it cannot support claims about the strongest parent learning how to teach
   from context. An adaptive parent is compatible only if its model, policy,
   access, timing, and dose are fixed before the comparison and matched in the
   sham arm.
9. **Manual precision is underspecified.** The 98% factual-precision condition
   needs an exact denominator, audit sample or complete census, blinding, and
   treatment of invalid/unmatched numbers.
10. **Token and opportunity effects are not separated.** All arms should get
    the same 16 post-result opportunities and maximum budgets. Extra records
    and extra loss-bearing tokens may remain part of the treatment pathway,
    but then the claim is about the teaching package, not semantics at matched
    training dose.
11. **Loss masking alone is insufficient.** Parent speech may be visible to
    the child during wake, but it should be absent from the rendered sleep
    sequence, not merely present as a zero-loss conditioning prefix. Otherwise
    the writer can learn “produce this continuation when the lesson is in
    context,” which is the opposite of the parent-removal claim. Sleep receives
    the grounded child continuation and its world evidence, never the parent's
    wording.

## Executor blockers inherited by the causal canary

The current runner still cannot execute the one-root canary validly. Before a
GPU launch, CPU/transactional fixtures must prove:

- the clean sealed childhood adapter is loaded by every descendant;
- later sleep extends or cumulatively reconstructs it rather than replacing
  it with a clean-base LoRA;
- shadow sleep compiles, trains, validates, and preserves artifacts exactly as
  running sleep does, differing only in candidate promotion;
- parent, lesson, classroom history, waking briefs, retrieval state, and
  caches are absent at H1 and deployment;
- no primary arm receives persistent cross-episode text;
- occurrence/visit index enters task and decode RNG;
- pre-first-sleep running/shadow outputs match on the common tape;
- invalid and missing actions remain zero in the fixed-K denominator;
- failed candidates are retained and the previously mounted adapter survives;
- fresh roots, ancestry hashes, same-node blocks, and randomized positions are
  enforced.

These are not C11 machinery. They are the minimum needed for the proposed
factorial's treatment to mean what its label says.

## Smallest reconciled experiment

### Stage 0 — disposable affordance scout

Run one 32-episode **task-disjoint** slot-only life, with no parent and no use
as a future ancestor. Give all 16 actions the same `NOTE_AFTER` opportunity.
Its only purposes are to verify plumbing/cost and estimate raw faithful-record
production. No sleep or parenting claim is needed. The three-cell q14 grid is
not on the critical path.

If the slot alone saturates record production, redesign the targeted lesson
around **conditional use** rather than record syntax. If it produces too few
records for one qualified sleep, test the artifact lesson and the numbered
baseline in another disposable development root; do not adapt the main causal
root after seeing its treatment response.

### Stage 1 — one-root H1 canary

Fork one byte-identical, deployment-naive birth checkpoint into:

- `P`: the strongest pinned parent model operating under a predeclared
  adaptive teaching policy;
- `S`: the same parent model, tools, schedule, turns, tokenizer-counted parent
  dose, warmth, and context occupancy, delivering an active sham lesson.

Both children receive the same post-outcome slot and a matched factual
recording affordance. The bounded treatment distinction is not “write text.”
It is:

> after informative feedback, form a source-linked first-person
> condition--action--outcome record, use it when the condition recurs, and do
> not invoke it on matched non-trigger cases.

The sham teaches equally long factual post-outcome transcription without the
condition/recurrence/use distinction. This keeps action/outcome exposure and
the existence of a record slot common while testing the relational packaging
the paper actually needs. Omit the `10` suggestion initially; if used after a
separate dev result, give the same number in P and S so it is not the target
factor.

Use the fixed 32-episode balanced childhood and one qualified sleep from the
minimal consensus. Parent text is visible during wake but absent from the
rendered sleep bytes, not merely loss-masked. The compiler may validate and
mask child bytes, but may not repair, append, paraphrase, or invent the target.
Parent state may adapt within its child's transcript; it must be isolated
between experimental roots and never learn in weights.

After complete parent/context removal, create disposable adapter ON/OFF clones
and test fresh trigger, non-trigger, and later-choice cases under a neutral
post-outcome interface. Score faithful recording, correct later use, and
correct non-use separately. Continue only if all three point favorably.

The one-root H1 estimand is

\[
\theta_{H1}=(\bar Y_{P,on}-\bar Y_{P,off})-
             (\bar Y_{S,on}-\bar Y_{S,off}),
\]

where the primary `Y` is balanced correct use/non-use on fresh cases, not
lexical similarity or record count. With one root this is directional
feasibility only.

### Stage 2 — four-cell H2 deployment canary

Only after the H1 canary passes, seal P/S and fork:

`P-running, P-shadow, S-running, S-shadow`.

Deploy on CompilerGym only if the nursery was task-disjoint. Use the exact
128-episode common-tape design in the minimal consensus: eight fresh tasks,
16 visits each, sleeps at 32/64/96/128, fixed-K scoring, and an untouched
terminal transfer family. Running and shadow branches execute identical sleep
pipelines; only technically accepted candidate promotion differs.

For branch `a,m`, let

\[
G_{a,m}=\frac{1}{8}\sum_j
 (\overline{Y}_{j,visits\ 13:16}-
  \overline{Y}_{j,visits\ 1:4}).
\]

The H2 interaction is

\[
\theta_{H2}=(G_{P,running}-G_{P,shadow})-
             (G_{S,running}-G_{S,shadow}).
\]

Predeclare and report additionally `G_P,running` and
`G_P,running-G_P,shadow`. All three must be positive to justify scaling. One
root remains an excluded directional executor canary; independent roots are
the unit for confirmation.

## Claim boundary

From Stage 0:

> A post-outcome turn repaired the opportunity to author grounded records;
> the reported rate is prompt-conditioned production.

From a positive one-root Stage 1:

> In one excluded randomized root, a targeted teaching package produced the
> reported adapter-dependent change in correct use and non-use after the
> parent and childhood context were removed.

From a positive one-root Stage 2:

> The four-cell executor isolated candidate-adapter promotion, and one
> excluded root showed the predeclared directional learning contrasts over
> 128 episodes.

Not supported: general parenting, discretionary memory, superiority to text
memory, connected/compressed experiential knowledge, indefinite continual
improvement, or the whole Dream--LoRA--Think flywheel. Those require fresh
multi-root confirmation and the later claim-ladder experiments.

## Authority boundary now

Under current authority, the following may proceed:

- already-running scouts, unchanged and labelled exploratory;
- retrospective analysis of q13 / child-frame / current-life artifacts;
- proposal refinement, independent critique, exact-byte fixtures designed on
  paper, and C11 kept parked;
- preservation and inspection of existing outputs.

The following may **not** proceed yet:

- q14 tick-format implementation or a new q14 GPU launch;
- the one-root P/S childhood or four-cell H2 canary;
- using a CompilerGym-exposed adult as a clean final ancestor;
- enforcing the q14 articulation gate mid-run;
- claiming H1/H2 from the existing q14, CF, RP, or historical adults.

Both q14 and the minimal causal consensus materially change the loop,
visibility, learning corpus, and estimands. Astra q14 is one interpretation,
and `e17f15c6` labels itself unratified. The simple-hygiene ruling authorizes
ongoing scouts; it does not waive the repository's deliberation and exact
human-ratification path for a new material experiment.
