## 1. What we are trying to do (plain words)

A small language model (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, frozen) plus one small adapter (a LoRA). The adapter is the only trainable parameter component; the text memory (ledger, waking brief) also changes across the life. The adapter is rewritten from the text record of the agent's own life. Three mechanisms plus parenting, as **proposed** for the v7 design (the numbers in §2 come from the earlier v6 mechanism; see the note after the list):

- **THINK.** The child writes free text in turns. The harness reads exactly one thing, a line starting `ACT:`, and sends it to the gym. Everything else (plans, predictions, notes, questions, words to its parent) is the child's own thinking, appended to a ledger nothing is ever deleted from.
- **DREAM** (context management). At wake the child sees its birth brief, its own summary from the last sleep and its parents' latest brief; the rest of the ledger fades from context and is reachable only by retrieval.
- **SLEEP** (the write). After every round of problems, child-authored spans from the ledger are trained into a fresh adapter from the frozen base. The compiler selects, orders and packs *existing* child-authored spans; it never generates thoughts, paraphrases lessons into child rows or synthesizes missing rows (§4.1). Proposed commit rule for the v7 run: a brake refuses a write only for broken format or stopped work; a patience rule rolls back to the best committed adapter if the harness's operational gate-panel score (§4.4; never a report or final-test score) falls for three adapters in a row. Neither the brake nor the patience rule has run; nothing in §2 was produced by them.
- **PARENTING.** Stronger agentic models read the child's redacted record and write short briefs about *how it thinks*. Policy for the v7 run: no answers, recipes or scores in a brief. That is the proposed policy, not a description of historical prompt content and not proof of leak-free delivery: the historical leak scan was a pass-name regex with a quote-back exemption since 09-10 and was porous, and delivered briefs were not audited [E:10; main.tex §3 "Gym and split"]. Parent text never enters a training sequence (§4.2). A child's restatement of a lesson, literal or reworded, may be trained because it is child-authored; authorship, not paraphrase, is the criterion (the historical design allowed literal restatement), and such restatements are not counted as uptake or as proof of independent reasoning.

The question all of it serves: **can an agent be taught to learn** — does a child raised under parents learn faster, in a gym it was not raised in, than the identical mechanism untaught? Mechanism sentence (Codex, 2026-09-11): bootstrap teaches the habit of making and revising rules; parenting teaches when to use that habit; lived outcomes supply the evidence; sleep makes the child's own successful use persistent. **The whole sentence is a hypothesis**, subject to the pretests of §3; no clause of it is established by the numbers in §2, and it is not the mechanism that produced them.

This is the v7 design (`CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md`). Every number in section 2 comes from the earlier v6 mechanism: four markers (PREDICT / ACT / NOTE / RECALL) plus DONE, a format canary on every commit, and in gated arms a score-plus-brevity gate on the 8 report programs whose floor is max(latest OFF, latest ON) − 0.02; nothing below was produced by the v7 brake or patience rule.

## 2. What we have measured (five numbers)

1. **The write changes behaviour, then harms late.** 9 ungated lives of 1,024 episodes in the compiler gym, probed adapter-on vs adapter-off on 8 programs held out by identifier (siblings of 4–6 are in training): mean life-level gain +0.019 (SE 0.011; 6/9 lives ≥ +0.02, 3/9 below zero; a 0.02 gain = programs shrink two more percentage points). 4/9 lives produced a harmful pair (< −0.03), first at episode 384, every one passing the format check. [E:47, E:58–59]
2. **In the gated lives, where tabulated, what it learned is one recipe.** Adapter-on plateaus repeat to four decimals across those lives (0.4878 = the birth prompt's four-pass list run with no model; 0.5291 = one six-pass routine). Five ungated endpoint adapters were scored once, at episode 1,024 (seeds 0, 1, 2, 5, 6): two sit exactly on those values (seed 1 0.5291; seed 6 0.4878, above its adapter-off 0.4823 on the 8 programs), one is 0.003 below the routine (seed 0, 0.5262) and two collapsed to 0.4329, below adapter-off (seeds 2 and 5). Compiler `-Oz` scores 0.522 on the same programs. [E:50, E:54; SEQ-017, SEQ-018, SEQ-019, SEQ-022 / E:73]
3. **On programs no life trained on, final-adapter means are near base or negative.** Disjoint panel v1 (12 programs), complete across both machines: 48 adapter cells, two adapter replicates per cell against that machine's three-replicate base (0.2574 / 0.2541; replicate SD 0.006–0.007, so ±0.015 is a descriptive band of about two replicate SD, not a significance test). Final adapters: −0.017 ungated (n = 9), +0.003 gated (n = 6), −0.010 gate+parent (n = 7), +0.001 parented (n = 3); mid-life adapters +0.005 (n = 9), +0.004 (n = 6), 0.000 (n = 5), −0.005 (n = 3). Tally: 9/48 above +0.015 (7 exactly the six-pass routine 0.2731; two near it, R2 seed 7 mid 0.2712 and RP 400 final 0.2713), 33/48 within, 6/48 below −0.015 (all final adapters, each with a collapsed replicate). Gated arms R3+R4 (24 cells): mean −0.001, 3 above, 1 below (R4 604, −0.064, one replicate at 0.138). No R3 cell fell below −0.015; one R4 cell did; this does not establish that the gate prevents collapse. Ungated lives lost their mid-life gain by the end (final − mid negative in 6/9, zero in 3/9). We have not demonstrated program-dependent choice in any cell. For the R3/R4 adapters this evaluation removes selection on this panel, not every design or selection confound (they were chosen by the 8-program gate). "Final" = the last adapter committed by the time of the re-probe (episode 1,024 for R2/RP; the latest committed adapter for gated lives still running, e.g. R4 601's sleep-416 adapter). The earlier one-machine figures (21 cells; −0.025 / +0.004 / −0.024; 6 / 11 / 4) are a labelled historical subset [SEQ-019], not current evidence. [SEQ-022 / E:72; SEQ-012, SEQ-016, SEQ-019]
4. **Text memory against weights, 8 lives.** The frozen model reading the child's own final brief, no adapter, matched or beat the adapter on the 8 programs in 5/8 (R2 seeds 0, 2, 5, 6; R3 500 a tie at 0.5291) and lost in 3/8 (R2 seed 1 by 0.013, RP 402 by 0.007, R3 501 by 0.016; each a fixed-recipe adapter). On the disjoint panel, against the *final* adapter, the brief won 4/8 (seeds 0, 2, 5, 6), lost 3/8 (seed 1 by ~0.006, RP 402 by ~0.010, R3 500 by ~0.009) and tied 1/8 (R3 501, both at the birth recipe's 0.2495). Two life-level crossed conditions, each on both panels: with seed 0's final adapter (whose collapsed replicate is on the disjoint panel), adapter + brief scored 0.4987 vs 0.5291 brief-only / 0.5262 adapter-only, and 0.1619 vs 0.2731 / 0.2145; with seed 1's routine adapter, adapter + brief = adapter alone on both panels (0.5291; 0.2731). Routine-only text (the one recipe line), tested for two lives: seed 0 scores 0.5291 = its full brief on the 8 programs and 0.2730 ≈ its full brief's 0.2731 on the disjoint panel; seed 1 scores 0.5291 > its full brief 0.5163 on the 8 programs but 0.2443 < its full brief 0.2667 and its adapter 0.2731 on the disjoint panel — a contrary cell that limits any "the recipe line is the whole benefit" account. Reading: the adapter exceeded the full brief in three of eight eight-program comparisons, while the brief matched or exceeded it in five. The inspected behaviour and recipe controls suggest fixed-routine execution explains much of these scores. These controls do not establish a general advantage of weight memory, equivalence of the memories, or absence of other stored information. [SEQ-022 / E:73–74; SEQ-017..SEQ-021]
5. **Storage is not extraction (as a resemblance); thinking ritualizes; the gate is porous.** The adapter lowers loss on its own rows by ~3 nats/token while behaviour moves ~+0.02 (2 lives); this resembles a storage–extraction gap, but lower loss does not identify usable stored knowledge. Thinking ritualizes — by the operational definition, ≥ 2 of 4 repetition flags (same first action, flat predictions, templated notes, same recall) in a 32-episode window, not simply repeating a recipe — at episodes 160–224 in 8/9 scored unparented lives (6 of the 7 scorable R2 lives plus 2 old-writer lives; not the nine complete R2 lives). The behavioural gate's brevity rule refused 17 writes that had passed its score rule at the recount (five gated lives; the paper's Table 2) plus two documented later refusals, at least 19 in all; five candidates across three lives were at or above the unrelaxed floor. These are counts, not rejection rates (eligible writes are not tallied). Its floor decayed once to admit a write in R4 602 0.037 below the replaced adapter's committed score 0.5307 (about 0.017 below that adapter's 0.5104 re-probe; not a paired causal loss estimate), and a gate-approved adapter (R4 604) had one replicate at 0.138 on the disjoint panel. [E:35, E:32, E:85, SEQ-001 table, SEQ-012, SEQ-013, SEQ-016]

**What these do not show:** that parenting works (n = 3 lives; nothing separates from controls); that the adapter stores knowledge the agent can use; that anything transfers beyond the gym; that gated writes are safe (their 0/60 harmful pairs is post-selection, on six R3 lives incomplete at the recount, and does not extend to later gated observations); that the gate prevents collapse; that "sleep amplifies whatever is frequent" (a hypothesis — no frequency intervention was run); that anything here is a first.

## 3. The next experiment (spec: `CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md`; order of work: `PRETESTS_2026-09-11.md`) — proposed; nothing here is measured

We propose to test whether a taught child learns faster than an untaught one. Every setting below is a proposed design constant from `CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md` and the COORDINATION entries "[Fable + Astra] 2026-09-11 — the child spec landed" (memo 3 problem 1: two estimands; memo 4 R1–R7) and "[Astra pipeline] 2026-09-11 01:16 UTC" (pretests P0–P12), subject to those pretests; none is a result, and none is authorized by SEQ-022.

- **The child.** One adapter lineage. Free text with one interface (`ACT:`); a problem lasts ≤ 16 turns and the next simply arrives (no end token; a nudge every 4 silent turns). After every 32 problems, 4 turns of private reflection. The write keeps every thought beside its situation (no dedup), trains only child-authored spans, and removes parent text and briefs from the training sequences (verified in receipts). Commit rule for this run: brake = broken format or stopped work only; floor = best committed adapter ever; roll back with patience. This is a different design from the score-gated disjoint panel with its own tolerance and best-committed floor that a claim about the write's *safety* would need (paper, "Future work"); the two are alternatives, not joint requirements on one experiment.
- **The gyms.** Clones, one per GPU, each in its own trait-teaching gym (reasoning-gym families held out by name for this run; persistence is the first trait), all merging into the one child at sleep. Eligible clone provenance (which sessions, gyms, parents and mechanism version a clone's rows come from) is defined and recorded before any merge; "same child" does not by itself admit foreign-lineage data. The compiler gym is withheld from the lineage as the final test.
- **The parents.** Two-parent rooms per gym (Astra + Astra): propose, cross-critique for leaks and answers, merge one brief of ≤ 10 lines ending in the rehearsal request. A central parent (Fable, with Codex as reviewer) reads across gyms and advises the rooms; it never blocks a sleep. Access strictly per §4.4.
- **The test.** Taught child vs untaught child with the identical mechanism, each from an empty ledger in the compiler gym. Two separate prospective endpoints, kept distinct: a frozen-checkpoint comparison, and a matched continual-learning comparison over a proposed 512 problems (the proposed headline). The result, if any, could support only a case study of this lineage under its internal controls, not a general parenting or cross-gym learning claim.
- **Pretests first**, before the mechanism freezes: P1 write A/B (organization, rank 8 vs 32, epochs, budget); P2 free-form interface and v7 pace; P3 preparation of the first child; P4 gym headroom band; P6 asynchronous two-clone merge; P7 the untaught comparator life; P8 roll-back numbers from existing gate records. Hard deadline: everything experimental lands by **2026-09-18**.

## 4. Rules every system must obey

1. **Thinker/compiler line (operational invariant).** The compiler (sleep) is organizational: it may select, order and pack *existing child-authored spans*. It may not generate thoughts, paraphrase lessons into child rows, or synthesize missing rows; a compiler model that restates parent text as the child's violates this line, whoever runs it. Parents stay on the thinker side: their words are context, never training rows.
2. **No foreign thoughts in weights.** Only child-authored spans are trained on. Parent text and briefs are removed (not masked) from training sequences, and the removal is verified in the training receipt (zero parent-origin target spans). A child's own restatement of a lesson may be eligible for training, but it is not counted as uptake or as proof of independent reasoning. Eligible clone provenance is defined before any merge (§3); rows from another lineage are not "the same child".
3. **Split hygiene.** The 8 report programs are held out *by identifier only* — never write "sealed" or "never seen" about them. Disjoint panel v1 is the operational/gate panel, researcher-used and disclosed as such. Label its history correctly: the R3/R4 re-probes on v1 were evaluations conducted without selection on v1 (those adapters were selected on the 8 programs), and R5 (life 700) used v1 for gating [SEQ-012 / E:86]; v1 is not an untouched panel and must not be called one. A fresh report panel v2 is sealed only if it does not delay the writer; otherwise final compiler results are labelled exploratory. The exploratory label is a disclosure about evidential status; it does not authorize leaking final-test content or scores to any parent, brief or bootstrap. The final gym is withheld from the lineage's training; no bootstrap, brief or parent may carry test-gym content; every child carries a provenance record (sessions, gyms, parents, mechanism version).
4. **Parents never see scores (explicit access rule).** Parents receive only redacted training records and allow-listed gate decision/reason codes. They never receive report-panel or final-test scores, including through ledgers, tools, briefs or other parents. Numerical gate-score access requires a separate explicit approved policy; verified panel disjointness alone is not permission. The harness raises on any forbidden read. The harness (not parents) may use the operational gate-panel score stream (disjoint panel v1) for patience and "best committed" roll-back; the roll-back checkpoint is never selected using report-panel or final-test scores.
5. **No waiting.** The forever loop: clones never block at a sleep boundary or on a parent; the writer trains about one round behind on its own GPU; a clone that reaches a boundary before a new adapter is approved continues with the last approved adapter. "No waiting" never means skipping validation or loading a partial or unapproved merge; a new adapter is picked up only after it passes its checks, at the next problem boundary. Briefs are read when they arrive; central parents advise and never hold up a sleep.
6. **Never quote private reflection.** Parents may read the child's private time; they never mention, quote or grade it, in a brief or anywhere else.
7. **Numbers.** Every empirical statement is a cell count or a measured mean with its noise band from `EVIDENCE_TABLES.md` or a dated `COORDINATION.md` entry, with the entry cited. Label post-selection numbers as such. Write "not measured" rather than guess. Mechanisms are "readings, held loosely" or explicit hypotheses, each with a falsifier. Proposed design constants are labelled proposed, with their design document cited.
8. **Access and secrets.** Never call WebFetch (use `python3 tools/webtext.py URL`). API keys are environment-only, never on disk, and never inherited by a trainer. No internal hostnames, node names or lease systems in any paper text ("one machine", "two machines").
