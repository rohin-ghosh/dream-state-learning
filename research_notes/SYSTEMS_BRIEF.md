## 1. What we are trying to do (plain words)

A small language model (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, frozen) plus one small adapter (a LoRA) that is the only thing that ever learns. The adapter is rewritten from the text record of the agent's own life. Three mechanisms plus parenting:

- **THINK.** The child writes free text in turns. The harness reads exactly one thing, a line starting `ACT:`, and sends it to the gym. Everything else (plans, predictions, notes, questions, words to its parent) is the child's own thinking, appended to a ledger nothing is ever deleted from.
- **DREAM** (context management). At wake the child sees its birth brief, its own summary from the last sleep and its parents' latest brief; the rest of the ledger fades from context and is reachable only by retrieval.
- **SLEEP** (the write). After every round of problems the child's own text is trained into a fresh adapter from the frozen base. A brake refuses a write only for broken format or stopped work; a patience rule rolls back to the best committed adapter if scores fall for three adapters in a row.
- **PARENTING.** Stronger agentic models read the child's record and write short briefs about *how it thinks* — never answers, recipes or scores. The parents' words reach the weights only if the child repeats them in its own words, because only the child's text is ever trained on.

The question all of it serves: **can an agent be taught to learn** — does a child raised under parents learn faster, in a gym it was not raised in, than the identical mechanism untaught? Mechanism sentence (Codex, 2026-09-11): bootstrap teaches the habit of making and revising rules; parenting teaches when to use that habit; lived outcomes supply the evidence; sleep makes the child's own successful use persistent. The first two clauses are untested.

This is the v7 design (`CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md`). Every number in section 2 comes from the earlier v6 mechanism: four markers (PREDICT / ACT / NOTE / RECALL) plus DONE, a format canary on every commit, and in gated arms a score-plus-brevity gate on the 8 report programs; nothing below was produced by the v7 brake or patience rule.

## 2. What we have measured (five numbers)

1. **The write changes behaviour, then harms late.** 9 ungated lives of 1,024 episodes in the compiler gym, probed adapter-on vs adapter-off on 8 programs held out by identifier (siblings of 4–6 are in training): mean life-level gain +0.019 (SE 0.011; 6/9 lives ≥ +0.02, 3/9 below zero; a 0.02 gain = programs shrink two more percentage points). 4/9 lives produced a harmful pair (< −0.03), first at episode 384, every one passing the format check. [E:47, E:58–59]
2. **In the gated lives, where tabulated, what it learned is one recipe.** Adapter-on plateaus repeat to four decimals across those lives (0.4878 = the birth prompt's four-pass list run with no model; 0.5291 = one six-pass routine). Of four ungated adapters tabulated once, at episode 1,024, two sit on those values (0.5291, 0.4878), one is 0.003 below the routine (0.5262) and one collapsed (0.4329, below adapter-off). Compiler `-Oz` scores 0.522 on the same programs. [E:50, E:54; SEQ-017, SEQ-018, SEQ-019]
3. **On programs no life trained on, final adapters average at or below base.** Disjoint panel v1 (12 programs; first machine complete, 21 cells from 4 ungated, 3 gated, 3 gate+parent and 1 parented life; base 0.2574, replicate SD 0.006–0.007): final adapters −0.025 ungated, +0.004 gated, −0.024 gate+parent; the only cells above two replicate SD (6 of 21) are the six-pass routine (0.2731); no cell shows a program-dependent choice; late-life adapters lost what mid-life adapters had in 6/9 ungated lives and improved in none; one gate-approved adapter had a replicate at 0.138 here. These cells are not post-selection on this panel, though the gated adapters were chosen by the 8-program gate. [SEQ-012, SEQ-016, SEQ-019]
4. **Text memory matched the weights on these panels.** In 6 lives the frozen model reading the child's own final brief, no adapter, matched or beat the adapter on the 8 programs in 4 (one a tie, in gated life 500) and lost in 2 (by 0.013 and 0.007; both adapters a fixed recipe). Crossed cells: with a collapsing adapter (seed 0), adapter + brief scored 0.4987 vs 0.5291 brief-only / 0.5262 adapter-only (0.1619 vs 0.2731 on the disjoint panel); with a routine-locked adapter (seed 1), adapter + brief = adapter alone (0.5291). The one routine line alone = the full brief on both panels (0.5291; 0.2730 vs 0.2731). This is no evidence that the weights add capability beyond the written brief; it is not evidence that they hold nothing else (equal scores are not equal stored information, and neither panel rewards a situation-dependent choice). [SEQ-017..SEQ-021; SEQ-021's tally line says "7 lives" but enumerates these six]
5. **Storage is not extraction; thinking ritualizes; the gate is porous.** The adapter lowers loss on its own rows by ~3 nats/token while behaviour moves ~+0.02 (2 lives). Thinking ritualizes (one action and note template) at episodes 160–224 in 8/9 scored unparented lives. The behavioural gate's brevity rule refused 17 writes that had passed its score rule at the recount (five gated lives; the paper's Table 2) and at least two more afterwards; five in all scored at or above the floor itself (3 lives). Its floor decayed once to admit a write 0.037 below the adapter it replaced (0.5307 → 0.5104 → 0.4933), and a gate-approved adapter had one replicate at 0.138 on the disjoint panel. [E:35, E:32, E:80, SEQ-001 table, SEQ-012, SEQ-013, SEQ-016]

**What these do not show:** that parenting works (n = 3 lives; nothing separates from controls), that the adapter stores knowledge the agent can use, that anything transfers beyond the gym, that gated writes are safe, that anything here is a first.

## 3. The next experiment (spec: `CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md`; order of work: `PRETESTS_2026-09-11.md`)

- **The child.** One adapter lineage. Free text with one interface (`ACT:`); a problem lasts ≤ 16 turns and the next simply arrives (no end token; a nudge every 4 silent turns). After every 32 problems, 4 turns of private reflection. The write keeps every thought beside its situation (no dedup), trains only the child's own words, and strips parent text and briefs from the training sequences. Brake = broken format or stopped work only; floor = best committed adapter ever; roll back with patience.
- **The gyms.** Clones, one per GPU, each in its own trait-teaching gym (reasoning-gym families held out by name for this run; persistence is the first trait), all merging into the one child at sleep. The compiler gym is withheld from the lineage as the final test.
- **The parents.** Two-parent rooms per gym (Astra + Astra): propose, cross-critique for leaks and answers, merge one brief of ≤ 10 lines ending in the rehearsal request. A central parent (Fable, with Codex as reviewer) reads across gyms and advises the rooms; it never blocks a sleep.
- **The test.** Taught child vs untaught child with the identical mechanism, each from an empty ledger in the compiler gym. Two co-primary endpoints: frozen-checkpoint transfer, and the matched 512-problem continual learning curve (the headline).
- **Pretests first**, before the mechanism freezes: P1 write A/B (organization, rank 8 vs 32, epochs, budget); P2 free-form interface and v7 pace; P3 preparation of the first child; P4 gym headroom band; P6 asynchronous two-clone merge; P7 the untaught comparator life; P8 roll-back numbers from existing gate records. Hard deadline: everything experimental lands by **2026-09-18**.

## 4. Rules every system must obey

1. **Thinker/compiler line.** The compiler (sleep) is organizational: it selects, orders and packs text; it never writes thoughts. Parents stay on the thinker side: their words are context, never training rows.
2. **No foreign thoughts in weights.** Only the child's own text is trained on. Parent text and briefs are removed (not masked) from training sequences. The child's own restatement of a lesson may be trained; that is the point.
3. **Split hygiene.** The 8 report programs are held out *by identifier only* — never write "sealed" or "never seen" about them. Disjoint panel v1 is the operational/gate panel, researcher-used and disclosed as such; a fresh report panel v2 is sealed only if it does not delay the writer, else final compiler results are labelled exploratory. The final gym is withheld from the lineage's training; no bootstrap, brief or parent may carry test-gym content; every child carries a provenance record (sessions, gyms, parents, mechanism version).
4. **Parents never see report scores.** Parents may read the ledger, ritual/efficiency metrics, prior briefs and coarse gate decisions (decision + reason code) from the gate panel. Report-panel and final-test scores are forbidden reads; the harness raises on them. Gate scores are shown only when the gate panel is verifiably disjoint from the report panel.
5. **No waiting.** The forever loop: clones never block at a sleep boundary or on a parent; the writer trains about one round behind on its own GPU; a new adapter is picked up at the next problem boundary; briefs are read when they arrive; central parents advise and never hold up a sleep.
6. **Never quote private reflection.** Parents may read the child's private time; they never mention, quote or grade it, in a brief or anywhere else.
7. **Numbers.** Every empirical statement is a cell count or a measured mean with its noise band from `EVIDENCE_TABLES.md` or a dated `COORDINATION.md` entry, with the entry cited. Label post-selection numbers as such. Write "not measured" rather than guess. Mechanisms are "readings, held loosely", each with a falsifier.
8. **Access and secrets.** Never call WebFetch (use `python3 tools/webtext.py URL`). API keys are environment-only, never on disk, and never inherited by a trainer. No internal hostnames, node names or lease systems in any paper text ("one machine", "two machines").
