# Paper claim audit v1 — 2026-09-11

Status: read-only scientific audit. This does not change `main.tex`, authorize
an experiment, or make a claim. It records what the current evidence can and
cannot support before the next result lands.

## The strongest defensible paper today

> Across the tested periodic writer recipes for Qwen2.5-7B, measured updates
> lowered NLL on evaluated own-record or rendered synthetic rows and altered
> downstream behavior.
> In compiler lives, beneficial-looking changes were frequently dominated by
> a supplied fixed routine; in the synthetic association assays, completion
> changes remained broadly spilling rather than qualifying as selective
> retrieval. This characterizes the named recipes and assays, not LoRA or
> sleep writers generally.

The compiler-life evidence characterizes one frozen production writer/base-
model/gym regime. The owner-binding and completion evidence comes from
separate synthetic diagnostics, some using different writer recipes. Neither
supports a prevalence claim about LoRA, sleep, or learning agents generally.

## Evidence supporting that narrow statement

1. **Behavior changes, but does not improve cleanly across the full life.** In
   nine ungated 1,024-episode lives, the life-mean paired ON-minus-OFF value on
   the historically visible eight-program panel was +0.019 (SE 0.011), with
   four lives containing a harmful pair below -0.03. This is exploratory
   because the panel is not source-disjoint and several histories have resume
   defects.

2. **The source-disjoint endpoint is near-zero or negative.** In the 48-cell
   disjoint re-probe, final-adapter arm means were -0.017 (ungated), +0.003
   (gated), -0.010 (gate plus parent), and +0.001 (parented). Across all 48
   final-plus-mid cells, nine were above the descriptive +0.015 band; seven
   exactly reproduced one fixed six-pass routine and two were near it. These
   are descriptive cell summaries, not causal arm estimates or independent
   samples, and they do not show program-dependent choice.

3. **Likelihood fit is real; selective access is not yet shown.** Historical
   probes show large likelihood gains on the agent's own rows under a
   mismatched chat-template rescore; synthetic diagnostics show strong fit to
   their rendered training text. Exploratory completion screens contain
   owner-specific components but no cell qualifies as selective binding. The
   disposable cross-node bank-0 Cell-F screen spills 0.4389, 0.3446, and
   0.3326 for K1/K4/K16 against the frozen 0.03 qualification rule; the two
   diversity cells spill 0.345 and 0.333. The appropriate phrase is
   `training-text fit without cue-general selective retrieval`, not `usable
   knowledge stored in weights`.

4. **Bundled writer recipes yield different behavioral outcomes.** In the
   tested cells, adapters trained with antecedent-form recipes were better at
   using a fact present in context, while short-piece recipes produced
   recitation or completion-habit patterns. In the write pretest, A reproduced
   the routine, A-v3 showed no clear disjoint transfer, C destroyed the ACT
   interface, and C-TMEM continued acting but was harmful. Existing A/B/C
   comparisons change several trainer factors at once, so they are recipe
   comparisons, not causal attribution to masking, context, rank, or packing.

5. **Text memory is competitive but not yet a strong RAG baseline.** In the
   latest descriptive tally, the frozen model reading its own waking brief
   matched or beat the adapter in 12 of 20 lives; interactions and node effects
   are heterogeneous. This is an unaudited live descriptive tally with mixed
   horizons, resume integrity, parent conditions, and node baselines—not a
   frozen eligible cohort. The brief is a carrier diagnostic, not a candidate-
   blind online retrieval baseline, and no inferential weights-over-text claim
   is available.

6. **Parenting uptake is unproved.** The one audit-eligible parented life with
   a valid v3 brief shows near-perfect lexical restatement, a transient
   realized-action change, and no durable task gain four sleeps later. Existing
   parented lives mix parent policies, model sizes, resumes, and nonmatched
   controls. The only safe summary is `persistent verbal echo, transient
   action change in one qualifying life`.

## Main-paper sentences that need correction before submission

These edits should be made only in a separately approved paper revision; this
note does not alter the manuscript.

1. Replace `the early gain is clear` with `all nine early-window life means
   were positive on the historically visible panel; this is exploratory and
   not an unbiased lifetime effect`.
2. Replace section/title uses of `storage is not extraction` with `likelihood
   gains on own rows did not establish selective access under changed cues`.
3. Remove any implication that rank/capacity has been ruled out. No tested
   rank-32 diagnostic passed the clean-binding conjunction; some owner or
   marginal components nevertheless increased, so these cells do not rule out
   capacity, rank, strength, data volume, or their interactions with
   representation.
4. Keep ritual as a secondary behavioral pathology, not a contribution or a
   developmental finding.
5. State that the production writer uses legacy whole-text training; the
   standalone v3 target-masked writer has not yet entered a live lineage.
6. Keep parenting to one bounded audit sentence unless a future matched
   parent-versus-sham childhood produces parent-absent executed uptake.
7. Do not update the paper's eight-life brief table by simply substituting the
   live 20-life tally. First freeze eligibility, horizon, node-matched base,
   resume integrity, and per-life records.

## Evidence sequence that can change the paper

1. **Conditional writer canary:** true coherent mapping versus a coherent
   marginal-preserving permutation, both required to learn their own mapping,
   with OFF, GOLD, TEXT, fixed-routine, spill, interface, and cumulative-
   interference controls across six banks. A selective pass qualifies only the
   exact synthetic ACT-target writer representation for a later parenting
   canary. When GOLD succeeds, both T and PI optimize, preserve the interface
   and non-harm behavior, all six roots and paired receipts are valid, and all
   non-classification assay controls pass: failed `tau_bind` while both T and
   PI show the preregistered marginal shift supports `broad habit`; passed
   `tau_bind` with failed specificity/spill supports `association component
   mixed with broad habit`. No marginal shift, or failure of an assay-validity
   condition, is inconclusive or operational.
2. **Clean parenting canary, only after writer qualification:** matched sham
   versus substantive parent during childhood, then parent removal and
   deployment-sleep OFF/ON forks. One root is a developmental case study, not
   population evidence.
3. **Strong text baseline:** frozen candidate-blind retrieval over eligible raw
   records under the same task/context budget. A waking brief alone is
   insufficient.
4. **Paper-grade confirmation:** newly sealed, source-disjoint report data,
   independent roots, fixed thresholds, node blocking, common randomness, and
   the full C11 guard only when the final C11 run is actually next.

## Submission decision rule

- If the conditional writer passes selectively and survives interference,
  retain that exact ACT-target writer representation as a qualified component
  for a later clean-child test. The canary alone does not validate the
  Dream--LoRA--Think lifetime mechanism.
- When GOLD succeeds, both T and PI optimize, preserve the interface and non-
  harm behavior, all six roots and paired receipts are valid, and all non-
  classification assay controls pass: classify failed `tau_bind` while both
  arms show the preregistered marginal shift as `broad habit`; classify passed
  `tau_bind` with failed specificity/spill as `association component mixed
  with broad habit`. Either may support the appropriately bounded writer-
  characterization headline. No marginal shift, or failure of an assay-
  validity condition, is inconclusive or operational.
- If GOLD fails, the assay is invalid and supports no memory conclusion.
- If TEXT succeeds while weight binding fails, the strongest localization is
  failure of the tested weight-writing/access route, not failure of memory or
  task accessibility generally.
- A positive whole-agent headline requires a newly sealed evaluation,
  condition-dependent choices beyond the best fixed routine, matched untreated
  and sham controls, a strong text-memory baseline, parent removal, and more
  than one independent lineage. Multiple lineages permit only a repeated
  demonstration; a population or parenting-efficacy claim requires a
  prospectively fixed number of independent childhood roots.
