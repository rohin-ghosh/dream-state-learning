# V10R1 congruence with the replicated seed-1/2 writer failure

Date: 2026-09-11 PDT (review completed 2026-09-12 UTC)

Status: fresh independent read-only scientific review. No implementation,
model, training, adapter, benchmark, GPU, or claim authority follows.

## Verdict

V10R1 is the right next writer kill-gate. It directly tests the failure that
replicated across the seed-1 and seed-2 child-frame roots: strong movement of
the trained association accompanied by broad off-key activation. No smaller
experiment or proposal redesign is required before implementation.

## Why the gate is congruent

- The observed failure is not weak storage: all six child root-by-variant
  cells changed the intended completion, but spill was `0.230`--`0.423`
  against the registered `0.03` limit.
- V10R1 requires each adapter to express opposite actions across sixteen
  tool-by-mode keys. The balanced XOR maps make constant, tool-only,
  mode-only, stratum-only, and stratum-by-mode habits insufficient.
- Candidate-normalized likelihood removes the shared `ACT:` prefix from the
  measured action-choice gain, so learning the output frame alone cannot pass
  the optimization gate.
- A pass also requires low probability and generation-rate change on missing
  modes, unsupported modes, one-character-neighbour tools, and unrelated
  native-interface prompts.
- It obtains this information with four fits, two disjoint roots, and a
  separately ratified cap of three A40 GPU-hours.

The broad action-choice pathology seen in the frame assays cannot pass on the
declared V10R1 panels: every adapter must keep each spill-family mean binary
TV and legal-action-rate change at or below `0.05`, while preserving the exact
native interface.

## Boundaries

V10R1 can still miss spill on untested prompt families, a shared-prefix shift
that changes neither binary action choice nor strict generation, or a large
change on a small number of items hidden by a family mean. Those limitations
are acceptable only because its maximum claim is explicitly restricted to
the frozen surface and declared spill families.

A pass establishes supervised seen-key conditional-policy carriage. It does
not establish general writer safety, independent memories, authentic child
authorship, retention, DREAM, parenting, generalization, connected memory,
continual learning, or the whole organism.

One stale reference in the decisive-evidence path was corrected from V7 to
V10R1. V7 remains accurately described elsewhere as retired.
