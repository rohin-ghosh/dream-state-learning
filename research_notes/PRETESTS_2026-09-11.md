# PRETESTS for the 2026-09-11 mechanism freeze — what runs on each GPU as it frees, in what order, and what each decides

Written 2026-09-10 (night) by Fable. Companion to `CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md` (the mechanism these tests finalize). **Revised 2026-09-10 23:50 UTC** after two critiques: the day plan is now scheduled against the build's three tiers (none of the v7 code existed at 23:08 UTC; section 0.5), every time estimate is recomputed at 16 turns per problem and in token-passes (a token read once by the trainer), and the untaught comparator runs under the lineage's own asynchronous loop (one clone plus its own writer GPU) instead of waiting for its writes. Hard rules that shape everything below: the internship ends 2026-09-18 (nothing experimental after); node 1 (8×A40) dies 2026-09-14, its exact hour unconfirmed; node 2 (8×A40) is assumed to last to 09-18; the two nodes cannot talk to each other (files move through the laptop as single tarballs); the child's mechanism is frozen on 09-11 and never changes afterwards; parents, gyms, tests and briefs may change at any time.

**Plain-language glossary** (terms also defined in `CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md`): *adapter* — the small trainable add-on (a LoRA) that is the only thing that learns; *rank* — its size; *frozen model* — the base model with no adapter (adapter OFF); *ledger* — the append-only record of everything a child wrote and did; *problem* — one task from a gym, 16 turns of up to 400 tokens; *round* — 32 problems; *sleep / write* — training a fresh adapter from the ledger; *writer* — the process on its own GPU that does the writes and examines each new adapter; *token-pass* — one token read once by the trainer (a million tokens read three times is three million token-passes; it sets a write's wall clock); *epoch* — one full reading of a training set; *probe / exam* — playing a fixed held-out panel with fixed random seeds, so scores compare across time and children; *8-panel* — the 8 report programs of the compiler gym; *disjoint panel* — 12 compiler programs from datasets the curriculum never used (the `panel` list of `disjoint_panel_v1.json`); *rep* — one seeded run of a panel; *SD* — standard deviation, the size of the noise; *replicate SD* — how much the same adapter's panel score moves between two identical runs (0.0065 here); *GPU-hour* — one GPU busy for one hour; *tier* — a runnable cut line of the build (section 0.5).

**Yardsticks used for every time estimate** (measured unless marked assumed or derived). Wake: the measured ≈ 22 problems per GPU-hour of a v6 life was at ≈ 11.6 turns per problem (design section 8; SEQ-011); at 16 turns that is ≈ 16 problems per hour, so **a batch of 8 problems ≈ 30 minutes** (derived; the reasoning gym has no compiler subprocess and may be faster — P2 measures both and every later estimate is corrected). Probes: the 8-program probe was ≈ 6 minutes per rep at ≈ 11 turns (COORDINATION 2026-09-10 ~00:30); at 16 turns **≈ 9 minutes per 8-program rep, ≈ 13 minutes per 12-program rep, so a 2-rep exam ≈ 26 minutes and an ON+OFF report pair ≈ 18 minutes** (derived). Engine start and adapter load ≈ 4 minutes. The Astra two-parent room ≈ 7 minutes at high reasoning (COORDINATION 2026-09-10, first live room). Training throughput **assumed** 1,200 tokens per second on one A40 (≈ 4.3 M token-passes per hour) — optimistic, because the v7 trainer needs gradient checkpointing (a memory-saving trick, about a third slower) for 7,168-token sequences; P1 measures it under the shipped settings. Corpus size: **≈ 10,000 tokens per problem at the two-scale write** (≈ 5,000 for whole problems plus ≈ 5,000 for the action windows, which re-cover the same text), ≈ 5,000 at scale 1 alone. So one life's first 512 problems ≈ 5 M tokens: **≈ 1.2 h at 1 epoch, ≈ 2.3 h at 2, ≈ 3.5 h at 3.** The write budget T_max = 5 M token-passes ≈ 1.2 h per write.

## 0.5 Build tiers and cut lines (why the plan is scheduled against tiers)

At review time (`date -u` 2026-09-10 23:30 UTC) none of the v7 code existed: the parser still reads five markers with an optional colon, the head still renders notes and recall, every problem starts a fresh tail, the schedule is a fixed list, `harness_done` is never read, the trainer truncates at 512 tokens with no mask and no seed, the sleep compile is the success-filtered v6 write, the clone coordinator is barrier-based, the inference engine cannot swap an adapter without a restart, and the collapse medians are computed nowhere. This is not a prompt-and-parser change; it is roughly three engineer-days of code plus GPU smokes. Appendix C of the mechanism document therefore lists the build in three tiers, each a runnable cut line:

| tier | what it delivers | target (UTC) | which pretests it unblocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the single-life v7 runner: ACT-only parser (colon required; decoration stripped), head without notes/recall/surprises, neutral intro line, compiler goal-text format sentence, nudge, no end token, solve ends a puzzle (no lane refill), 3-turn carry-over, brief refresh per batch, `[PARENT]` line, reflection without note rows, forever schedule, `--seed` on the trainer, collapse medians | 09-11 midday | P2-F and P2-F-c, P4's free-form band, P3's lives, the interim lineage |
| 2 | the v7 writer: two-scale compile with gym spans masked and parent text stripped, `--fixed-rows`, T_max in token-passes with the newest-first draw, the pre-registered test-life composition, span-masked trainer at 7,168 tokens with gradient checkpointing, corpus leak check, hygiene checks on the writer path; exams on the writer, floor = best committed, lineage-level patience, `ACTIVE` pointer; parent-side changes (room out of the clone's path, marker-free rehearsal tail, `rehearsal_rate` on the first thought, no score scalars) | 09-11 mid-afternoon | P1's W1 cells, P3's sleeps and rooms, P5, the freeze entry |
| 3 | the asynchronous writer process, clone polling, hot-swap (LoRA always on, new adapter id per publication), rollback pointer, lag log | 09-12 midday | P6, the full-width lineage, the asynchronous test lives |

If a tier misses its target, the rows of section 2 that need it slide; nothing that needs a tier is started on a stub. The lineage can launch before tier 3 on the existing coordinator's non-blocking mode (round and barrier timeouts near zero: no clone ever waits, the coordinator clone writes, the new adapter lands at each clone's next sleep boundary — `clone_coordinator.wait_for_verdict`, `attach_missed_rounds`, both CPU-tested), labelled `interim merge` in the manifest; the child's mechanism is the same under both, only the swap timing differs.

## 1. The battery, ranked by decision value per GPU-hour

### P8 — Patience roll-back simulation on the existing gated records (0 GPU-h; CPU, after a pull)
- **Question.** Under the v7 rule (every write commits unless format/collapse; floor = best committed adapter ever; roll back at the third consecutive adapter below floor − tol), how long would the bad writes of the record have governed play, would any good adapter have been rolled back by noise, and does a fast path for large drops pay?
- **Step 0 — the pull (≈ 20 minutes; the records are not on the laptop).** The local analysis files (`research_notes/analysis/analysis_tables_2026-09-10_{a40,ovx}.json`) hold per-life probe series and rejection counts only, and the node-1 file was captured before R3 500's late writes; they cannot replay the gate. Pull `sleep_*/gate.json`, `probe_gate*.json`, `probe_ep*.json` and `probe_ep*_adapterOFF.json` (a few MB) for R4 601 and R4 602 (node 2) and R3 500 and R3 505 (node 1) through the existing scp scripts (`gpu/ovx_scp.sh`, `gpu/a40_scp.sh`; no GPU is touched).
- **Setup.** R4 602 (the floor-decay case: committed 0.5307, re-probed 0.5104, then 0.4933 accepted at sleeps 800 and 832 — SEQ-016/017), R4 601 (four refusals in a row, a 0.114 write), R3 500 and R3 505 (15 and 17 refusals; late writes 0.337/0.347/0.210 and 0.5235 above floor with 5.6 turns per problem). Replay each life sleep by sleep under five rules: **V** (as run: veto at tol 0.02 with the decaying floor); **P-0.02** and **P-0.01** (v7: brake on the brevity rule only, floor = best committed adapter ever, three-adapter patience, tol 0.02 or 0.01); **P-0.01+fast** (as P-0.01 plus "committed but not activated" when one exam is ≥ 0.05 below the floor); **P-0.01+frozen** (as P-0.01 with the frozen model as an initial floor — for decision D5's information only). Exam proxy: the recorded gate candidate score in `gate.json` (one rep on the gate panel as run) with the ON report probe every 64 problems as a check; the record has no 2-rep exam of the held adapter, and the table says so.
- **Decision rule.** For each life and rule: the adapter active at every sleep; the number of rounds a below-floor adapter governed play; the worst active score; roll-backs; false roll-backs (an adapter within 0.005 of the floor rolled back). Recommend the fast path (D6) if, under P-0.01 without it, an adapter ≥ 0.05 below the floor would have governed ≥ 2 rounds in ≥ 2 of the 4 lives. Raise tol to 0.013 if P-0.01 shows any false roll-back; otherwise 0.01 stands (the arithmetic in `CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md` 3.4 predicts none). Report how many rounds P-0.01+frozen would have left each life adapter-less.
- **Cost.** 0 GPU-h; ≈ 20 min pull + ≈ 2 h analysis. **Unblocks.** The roll-back numbers in the freeze entry; Rohin's D5/D6 decisions before P7 launches.

### P5 — Astra room smoke per gym on the free-form child's ledger (≈ 0 GPU-h; API + CPU)
- **Question.** Does the two-parent room produce a usable brief for a child whose ledger has no `PREDICT:`/`NOTE:`/`RECALL:` lines, without teaching syntax the harness no longer honours and without any parent — room or central — seeing a score?
- **Prerequisites (tier 2's parent-side changes; without them P5 fails by construction).** (1) `parent_brief.REHEARSAL_TAIL` rewritten without marker words — today it is appended to every delivered brief and says "as your first NOTE before you act", so every room would fail the no-marker condition. (2) `agentic_parent.gate_decisions` returns decision and reason code only — today it returns candidate, floor and base scalars whenever the gate panel is disjoint from the report panel, which is exactly v7's configuration. (3) `parent_brief.rehearsal_rate` scores the first `thought` row of each problem instance, not the first `NOTE:` line (today it returns 0 for every problem without one). (4) A central-parent note path that writes to the parental ledger from a fresh context with the allow-listed tools only. All four files join `RUNTIME_FILES` so the manifest hash covers them.
- **Setup.** `bash gpu/run_parent_agent.sh <P2 free-form life dir>` on node 2 with `PARENT_A_*` and `PARENT_B_*` set to the inference-hub Astra model in the ssh environment (key never on disk), `PARENT_REASONING=high`, smoke directory (no `--commit`); two windows (`--sleep-dir` at the 32- and 64-problem boundaries of the P2 reasoning-gym free-form ledger). Then one real `--commit` call at P3 variant (a)'s first sleep, through the daemon path (the room writes `parent_brief.txt`; no clone process is involved). If a Minesweeper ledger exists (P4), one call on it. One central-parent note written from a fresh Fable context with the allow-listed tools, scanned the same way. Prompt version must be the v7-aware one (marker-free rehearsal sentence; no gate score tool).
- **Decision rule.** Pass if all of: the delivered brief is not the fixed FALLBACK in ≥ 3 of 4 invocations; leak scan and identifier scan 0 hits; no `NOTE:`, `PREDICT:`, `RECALL:`, `DONE` or `ACT:` example move in the delivered text; no score scalar anywhere in the tool trace or in the central note (the `read_gate_log` tool shows decision and reason only); wall clock ≤ 15 min per room; the frontier estimate and curriculum move are logged; the ritual metrics and `rehearsal_rate` compute without error on a marker-free ledger (the frozen ritual flags for notes and recall must read 0, not crash; `rehearsal_rate` must read a non-degenerate number on the first-turn text). Fail on fallback rate → the lineage starts with the parents' fixed opening brief while the prompt is fixed (parents may change mid-life; nothing in the child waits). Fail on a score leak → the tool or the note path is removed before any parent runs beside a live child.
- **Cost.** ≈ 0 GPU-h (the room is API calls; the ledger read is CPU); ≈ 1 h wall. **Unblocks.** P3 (which needs the room at two sleeps) and the lineage's parents.

### P2 — Free-form vs marker format smoke, and the v7 pace (≈ 12 GPU-h)
- **Question.** Can the frozen model turn its work in through the single `ACT:` interface with nothing else parsed, what does removing the markers cost in score and behaviour, and how long does a batch take at 16 turns?
- **Setup.** Frozen model (arm A), temperature 0.7, 16 turns × 400 tokens, generation seed 1000, wake batch 8. Reasoning gym, 64 problems from the training schedule (seed 0; families cycle, ≈ 9 problems per train family): cell **M** = today's marker prompt (`bootstrap_reasoning_gym.txt`, no end token, as the existing code runs — needs nothing new); cell **F** = the v7 birth brief (`CHILD_MECHANISM_v7.md` Appendix A) with only `ACT:` parsed (colon required; Markdown, backticks and fences stripped), the nudge after every 4 silent turns, the 3-turn tail carry-over, a solved puzzle ending its problem (tier 1). Compiler gym, 32 problems each from the train split (seed 0): cell **M-c** = today's `bootstrap.txt` (with its recipe, as the record ran) and cell **F-c** = the v7 brief with no recipe and no example pass, **with the one format sentence added to the compiler goal text** (pass names with a leading dash, comma-separated) — without it F-c would measure a missing sentence, since today the syntax lives only in `bootstrap.txt`. All four cells log every instrument of section 5 of the mechanism document, including parse-miss candidates, and the wall clock per batch.
- **Decision rule.** F is viable if all of: ≥ 90% of problems contain at least one scoreable action; actions per problem ≥ 0.5 × M's; mean score ≥ M − 0.05 (64 problems scored mostly 0/1 give a standard error ≈ 0.06 — the smoke detects only a large loss, and says so); nudges per problem ≤ 2. Parse-miss candidates > 10% of turns that contain "ACT" → one edit to the interface sentence of the brief, re-run 32 problems (≈ 2 GPU-h); still > 10% → Stage-0 variant (c) becomes mandatory. Actions per problem < 0.5 × M → nudge after 3 turns, re-run 32 problems. Also recorded, not gating: the unprompted prediction, note and self-question rates in F (how much of the v6 syntax the frozen model does on its own), idle-repetition rate, cross-problem carry-over (first turn names or repeats the previous problem), the share of lane-time idle on solved puzzles, and **minutes per batch in each gym — the yardstick every later estimate is corrected from**. F-c viable if ≥ 90% of problems have a scoreable action and ≤ 50% of actions are `INVALID` (unknown pass names) — the frozen model must be able to name passes without an example.
- **Cost.** Reasoning gym 2 × 64 problems = 2 × 8 batches ≈ 2 × 4 h; compiler 2 × 32 = 2 × 4 batches ≈ 2 × 2 h; ≈ 12 GPU-h (less if the reasoning gym runs faster than the compiler gym). **Unblocks.** The interface freeze (section 1 of the mechanism); P3, P5, P7.

### P1 — Write A/B on one finished ungated ledger, rank 8 vs 32, epochs under the budget, probed ON vs OFF (≈ 22 GPU-h)
- **Question.** Does organizing the write around every thought in its situation (two scales, neighbourhood packing, gym spans masked, no filter, no principles) store more usable experience than today's success-filtered short pieces; does scale 1 alone (half the tokens) do as well; does rank 32 help or hurt; and how much do extra epochs buy, given that every epoch costs width (mechanism 2.3)?
- **Setup.** Ledger: **R2_B_seed0** (node 1; finished; the one life whose text-memory cells exist — frozen model + its own brief scored 0.5291 on the 8-panel and 0.2731 on the disjoint panel, SEQ-017 — so the write is measured against text memory for free). Corpus horizon: the first 512 problems (rows up to the `sleep_0512` boundary; the design's cycle-16 read). **Cells that need only today's code (start 00:30):** **W0** = today's write, taken as the recorded `sleep_0512/corpus.json` (success-filtered pieces + principles) trained with today's `train_adapter.py` — W0-r8 and W0-r32 at (1e-4, 3 epochs), W0-r32 at (5e-5, 2), and an unseeded second run of W0-r32 (today's trainer has no seed; the replicate is labelled unseeded); the frozen model re-measured at seed 4242 on both panels (2 reps) rather than borrowed — the recorded OFF 0.4872 (SEQ-017) is the life's own seed-777 probe; and the mid-life text-memory cell: the frozen model + `sleep_0512/waking_brief.txt` on both panels, 2 reps. **Cells that need tier 2 (start when it lands):** **W1** = the v7 write built from the same rows (scale-1 whole problems with gym spans masked, scale-2 action windows, neighbourhood packing by program and time, every thought, no dedup, no principles; max length 7,168; gradient checkpointing): W1-r32 at (1e-4, **1 epoch** — 5 M token-passes, the budget's default), W1-r8 at (1e-4, 1), W1-r32 at (1e-4, 3 — 15 M token-passes), W1-r32 at (5e-5, 2), **W1s-r32** = scale 1 alone at (1e-4, 1 — 2.5 M token-passes), and a seed-1 replicate of W1-r32 at (1e-4, 1); training seed 0 otherwise. Probes per adapter with `probe_adapter.py`: the 8-panel and the disjoint panel, 2 reps each, `--gen-seed 4242`, budget 16, the v6 birth prompt (the ledger is in the v6 dialect; the organizational result transfers to v7 as a hypothesis, stated). Recorded per cell: panel means, both reps, collapsed reps (a rep below the frozen reference − 0.04), ritual metrics on the probe ledgers (modal first-action share, first-note Jaccard), **training tokens per second under the shipped settings and write wall clock** (these set T_max's token-pass number at the freeze), peak memory.
- **Decision rule.** Noise: the SD of a 2-rep mean ≈ 0.0046; of a difference of two such means ≈ 0.0065; two noise widths ≈ 0.013. (i) *Write:* adopt W1 if, at rank 32 and (1e-4, 1), disjoint W1 − W0 ≥ −0.0065 (not worse beyond one noise width) and at least one of: modal first-action share on the disjoint probe ≤ W0's − 0.10 (less ritual), disjoint W1 − W0 ≥ +0.013, 8-panel W1 − W0 ≥ +0.013. Ship W0 minus principles if W1 is worse by > 0.013 on both panels, or if W1 has a collapsed rep and W0 does not. (ii) *Scale:* ship scale 1 alone if W1s is within 0.0065 of W1 on the disjoint panel and has no collapsed rep — it halves every write and doubles what fits in the budget. (iii) *Rank:* 32 unless rank 32 is worse than rank 8 by > 0.013 on both panels at the chosen write, then rank 8. (iv) *Epochs / learning rate (decision D1's evidence):* E = 1 ships unless (1e-4, 3) or (5e-5, 2) beats (1e-4, 1) by ≥ 0.013 on the disjoint panel at the chosen write and rank, or (1e-4, 1) shows a collapsed rep and the other does not — in which case the result goes to Rohin with the width it costs (E = 2 → three clones per writer; E = 3 → two). (v) *Replicates:* the two training seeds of W1-r32 must agree in sign on the disjoint panel; if the seed-to-seed gap exceeds 0.02, the write decision is labelled "trainer variance dominates" and the default ships (W1, rank 32, 1e-4 × 1) with that label — the freeze cannot wait. (vi) *Against text memory:* whichever write ships is reported beside the brief-only cells; if no adapter beats the mid-life brief on the disjoint panel, the paper says so and the lineage still runs (the parenting question is about learning to learn, not about beating one brief).
- **Cost.** Today's-code cells: 4 × (0.5 h train + 0.8 h probe) + 0.8 + 0.8 ≈ 6.8 GPU-h. Tier-2 cells: training 1.2 + 1.2 + 3.5 + 2.3 + 0.6 + 1.2 ≈ 10 h (at the assumed speed; slower if P1's own measurement says so) + 6 × 0.8 h probing ≈ 15 GPU-h; on three node-1 lanes ≈ 5 h wall (the 3-epoch cell alone is ≈ 4.3 h). **≈ 22 GPU-h in all.** **Unblocks.** Sections 2.2–2.3 of the mechanism (write, scale, rank, lr, epochs), the token-pass number for T_max (from the measured speed), the freeze entry, P7's launch.

### P4 — Frozen-model headroom band for the trait gym, collapse medians, install smokes (6–10 GPU-h)
- **Question.** Does the frozen model have room to improve in reasoning-gym (and Minesweeper, if its adapter is built), how noisy is a 12-item exam, and what are the frozen medians the collapse brake compares against?
- **Setup.** Reasoning gym: the 12 gate items (`n_queens` ×6, `tower_of_hanoi` ×6) and the 12 exam items (`zebra_puzzles`, `acre`, `rush_hour` ×4), frozen model, budget 16, 4 seeded reps (777, 1777, 2777, 3777), the v7 free-form brief (if tier 1 is not ready when the GPU frees, band with the marker prompt first — headroom is a property of the gym and the model — and re-band 2 reps under the free-form brief later, because the tolerance and the medians must come from the free-form band). Per set: mean, SD across reps (the band SD), per-family accuracy; per problem: child tokens, actions and distinct actions — **the median tokens per problem over the problems the frozen model did not solve is the collapse brake's reference** (mechanism 3.1); actions and distinct actions are recorded as instruments. Train families' headroom comes free from P2's 64 problems (per-family accuracy). Minesweeper: CPU install smoke on node 2 (`pip install textarena==0.7.4`; 20 random episodes; read the hidden grid hook) ≈ 10 min; a 4-rep band on 12 boards of one untrained size only if the Gym adapter (≈ 150 lines, GYM_SURVEY section 6) is built by 09-12 morning; otherwise Minesweeper is out of this run and reasoning-gym is the only trait gym. Minesweeper's gate score is the board outcome; its Brier reading (how well the child's stated mine probabilities match the hidden grid) uses the detector-flagged probabilities of instrument 5.1 with the precision audit — no marker is required. **TextWorld** (C1 of the ruled shortlist) is out of this run: its adapter is ≈ 0.5 engineer-day and the build day is spoken for; it is first on the list for the next run.
- **Decision rule.** A0 (the design's saturation guard, section 7): the gym is a parenting gym if ceiling 1.0 − frozen mean ≥ 0.10 and ≥ 3 × band SD on its gate set. A train family the frozen model solves ≥ 0.9 of the time (no room) or ≤ 0.02 (floor) at this budget is dropped from the train list (a gym change, allowed at any time). The gate set's band SD becomes that gym's tolerance tol_g (mechanism 3.3). Expected magnitude, stated: a 12-item 0/1 exam at 30% accuracy has SD ≈ 0.13 for one rep and ≈ 0.07 for a 4-rep mean — the reasoning gym's exams can only see large changes; that is why the disjoint compiler panel (replicate SD 0.0065) is where the fine comparison lives.
- **Cost.** 4 reps × 24 items = 96 problems = 12 batches ≈ 6 GPU-h; the 2-rep free-form re-band, if needed, +3; Minesweeper band +4. **Unblocks.** The gym list for the lineage; tol_g; the collapse reference; the reading of P3's exams.

### P6 — Two-clone asynchronous merge smoke with the hot-swap (≈ 12 GPU-h; tier 3; 09-12)
- **Question.** Do two clones and one writer run with no barrier, swap adapters at batch boundaries without waiting or restarting, keep the ledger whole, and survive a kill — and what is the writer's real cycle time?
- **Setup.** Node 1, three GPUs (09-12, when tier 3 lands; node 1 has the idle GPUs and the lineage is on node 2): clone 0 and clone 1 in the reasoning gym (seeds 10 and 11, v7 mechanism at the frozen settings, 64 problems each = 2 rounds), one writer process (trains whenever ≥ 1 new slice exists; the frozen T_max and epochs; brake; one gate exam per adapter; publish with a round tag and the `ACTIVE` file); clones poll the group directory at every batch boundary and hot-swap. **Clone 0 runs with the Astra room on** through the parent daemon, so the idle-gap check below covers a live parent. At ~problem 40, kill clone 1 mid-batch and restart it.
- **Decision rule — pass/fail on lag, integrity and latency only; P6 changes no frozen number.** Pass if all of: pooled rows = the sum of slice rows with every ledger index pooled exactly once; every pooled row carries clone id, gym, round and adapter round; the writer completes two writes and the second includes rows no older than one round (lag ≤ 1); each clone swaps within one batch of a publication and the swap takes < 60 s with no engine restart (a child born without an adapter attaches one); no clone ever waits (zero calls to a verdict wait; the life log shows no idle gap > 2 min at a round boundary, including the round where the room ran); the brake ran (canary 3-of-4 on train-split items, collapse median on unsolved problems) and each published adapter has exactly one 2-rep exam in the lineage record; the restarted clone resumes from its last completed batch with no duplicate rows; `assert_split_hygiene` and `target_blind_check` ran on the writer path; the corpus leak check for `[PARENT]` found nothing; the writer's cycle wall clock (train + canary + exam + engine start) is recorded. Fail on integrity → fix and re-run one round (≈ 4 GPU-h). Lag > 2 rounds → reported to Rohin under D1 (the budget is his to lower); the lineage continues on the interim merge meanwhile. The earlier rule "if a write exceeds 1.5 h, T_max drops or epochs fall" is withdrawn: T_max and epochs are fixed at the freeze entry and P6 may not change them after P7 has launched.
- **Cost.** 2 clones × ≈ 4 h + writer ≈ 4 h ≈ 12 GPU-h; ≈ 4 h wall. **Unblocks.** The lineage at full width on the asynchronous writer; P7 and the taught test life as asynchronous lives (D3).

### P3 — Preparation variants a / b / c for the first child (≈ 15 GPU-h; tiers 1–2)
- **Question.** Which Stage-0 preparation makes the child fluent in the interface and receptive to its parents fastest, without putting anyone else's thoughts into its weights?
- **Prerequisites.** Tier 1 and tier 2; P5 passed (or the fixed opening brief); the parent-side changes of P5's prerequisites (`rehearsal_rate` on the first thought — otherwise P3's deciding metric is identically zero for all three variants and P3 cannot choose); for (c), the interface rows written, leak-scanned and hashed.
- **Setup.** Three lives on three GPUs (node 2), reasoning gym, the v7 mechanism at the labelled default write (two-scale, rank 32, 1e-4 × 1) — P1's W1 cells finish about when P3 does, so P3's two small sleeps cannot wait for P1's verdict; P3's readings are about the first two sleeps' behaviour (fluency, receptiveness, persistence), not the write's quality, and stand if P1 changes the write (a stated limitation). The same training schedule (seed 0), generation seed 1000, 64 problems each (2 rounds, 2 sleeps), the Astra room at both sleeps through the daemon (the brief lands at the next batch), reflection at both rounds. **(a)** birth brief + the parents' opening brief (written before birth from the gym description only). **(b)** as (a) plus, for both rounds, one harness line at the start of every problem — `[PARENT] Before you start, say in your own words what your parents asked of you.` — stripped from training sequences, never trained; the child's restatements are its own text and are written at both sleeps. **(c)** as (a) plus 60–100 interface-only rows — a real gym head followed by a minimal turn containing only a well-formed `ACT:` line whose move is **content-free** (a syntactically valid move the verifier scores 0, or the gym's `INVALID` placeholder; never an answer), every row leak-scanned against its item's reference answer, the set hashed into the manifest — given to the writer as `--fixed-rows` so every retrain-from-base includes them, and a `sleep_0000` adapter trained from them before the first problem (rank 32, the default setting). Measured after each sleep: rehearsal rate (first turn of a problem shares ≥ 4 content words with the parents' brief) minus the null (the frozen first turns of P2-F scored against the same brief); interface fluency (fraction of problems with ≥ 1 scoreable action, nudges per problem, parse-miss candidates); actions after a non-improving outcome per problem (persistence); thoughts per action; first-turn similarity across problems (the ritual seed); the gate exam after sleep 2 (12 items, 2 reps, on the writer); reflection length and connectedness; both writes' brake outcomes.
- **Decision rule.** Choose the variant with the highest (rehearsal − null) after sleep 2 among variants with fluency ≥ 0.9 and thoughts per action ≥ 0.5 × variant (a)'s; ties go to (a) (least imposed). (b) is rejected outright if its first-turn similarity across problems is ≥ 0.5 (a templated opening — the v6 echo trap). If no variant reaches fluency 0.9, (c) ships with its rows kept for life (D8). Exam differences smaller than 0.09 (one 2-rep noise width on 12 items) are ignored; only a difference ≥ 0.2 counts, and then only as a flag.
- **Cost.** 3 × (64 problems ≈ 4 h + 2 small writes ≈ 0.3 h + exam ≈ 0.5 h) ≈ 3 × 5 h ≈ 15 GPU-h; ≈ 5 h wall on three GPUs. **Unblocks.** How the lineage is born (mechanism section 4).
