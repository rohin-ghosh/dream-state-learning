# Experience Models — one-page abstract for onboarding (2026-09-10)

**Claim in one sentence.** A frozen instruction-tuned model plus a small per-life adapter, rewritten from the frozen model at every sleep from the agent's own successful thinking (per-life success-filtered self-training into a rank-8 LoRA), changes the agent's behaviour on a compiler-optimization gym; we characterize what that write learns, how it fails late in life, and what a behavioural gate catches. Parenting is an intervention we studied, not an established mechanism. (Revised 2026-09-10 after the harsh review; see research_notes/REVIEW_HARSH_2026-09-10.md.)

## The system, in three mechanisms
- **THINK** — the loop. The agent lives in a stream of thought with a few markers (PREDICT a score, ACT, NOTE to itself, RECALL its past). Every step is recorded in an append-only ledger. It plays real tasks: CompilerGym (choose LLVM passes to shrink real programs; scored on 8 programs held out by identifier from a curriculum that contains same-source siblings of 4–6 of them — not a sealed split) and a rule-induction game used for teaching (10 textbook rules; instance-held-out only).
- **DREAM** — context management. Notes persist at the head of context; the rest fades; a brief written at sleep becomes the next wake's opening. Kept deliberately simple.
- **SLEEP** — the write, as actually run in every reported life: `compile_sleep` selects the agent's improving actions and notes, a THINK-call writes principle lines and a waking brief, the cumulative corpus is deduplicated, and a rank-8 LoRA is retrained from the frozen model with the v1 bare-text trainer (lr 1e-4, 3 epochs). Paraphrase/replay-mix enrichment exists only in an offline n=2 screen. Ungated (R2, RP) lives committed on a format canary alone; gated (R3, R4) lives add a score gate and a behaviour gate — both evaluated on the SAME 8-program panel and seed used to report their scores, so gated arms' probe numbers are post-selection statistics (see review F1).
- **PARENTING** — a stronger model (14B or 32B) watches the child, never gives answers (leak-scanned), and prescribes better thinking patterns when the child's thinking becomes repetitive. The child is asked to restate the lesson in its own words every episode so the lesson enters its own thoughts, which is what sleep writes. The parent's weights never learn; the child's do.

## What has been measured (cell counts, not stories)
- The write changes behaviour every time. Ungated children collapsed late in life in 3 of 9 lives with a passing format canary. Gated children show no harmful pair so far, but their probe numbers are measured on the gate's own selection panel and cannot be read as a safety result until re-probed on a disjoint panel (in progress); the gate's rejection log is the honest evidence: four collapse-shaped candidates with passing scores refused before commit.
- The measured gain is recipe lock-in: adapter-ON probe scores repeat exactly across lives (48.78%, 52.91%) because the child applies one pass sequence every time; the exploring frozen model varies 46–49%. A fixed-recipe and -Oz/-O3 reference is being computed; until then "gain" means "a supplied procedure was transported and locked". Single paired-difference noise is ~1.5–2 points, not 1.
- 12,031-row corpora written once with a different trainer scored below the base panel in most cells; frequency was not isolated (every sleep retrains from the frozen model, so a life's final adapter is itself a one-shot write over its corpus). Rank is unresolved at n ≤ 5 (r16 above r8 in 4/5 seeded pairs, mean +0.042, 90% CI [+0.001, +0.083]).
- Children's first notes lexically overlap the parent's brief in 50–100% of episodes (an echo metric with no control null yet); none of the 3 gym-parented children collapsed (vs 3/9 unparented, Fisher p ≈ 0.38); two classroom-parented lineages did collapse. The habit returns within ~2 sleeps; "the write amplifies whatever the child does most" is a reading, not a measurement (brief withdrawal is a confound).
- Thinking ritualizes at 160–224 episodes in 8 of 9 unparented children and stays; gym-parented children first flagged at 192, 256 and 288 (the parent only speaks after a flag, so it cannot delay first onset by design).
- Storage ≠ extraction (reading): the adapter lowers per-token NLL on its own training rows by ~3 nats (0.6–1.7 nats above another life's rows), while behaviour moves ~2 points; probe format differs from the bare-text training format (disclosed).

## What we are testing next
- **Bootstrap (pretrain the adapter):** 32B-authored metacognitive text rendered in the child's dialect from rule-game classroom ledgers, trained before birth. Status: DEV_UNVERIFIED_PROVENANCE (Codex STOP, 2026-09-09): not leak-scanned, upstream ledgers produced under known scanner defects; may not be parented or called clean until an ancestry guard admits it. Not measured.
- **Scale thinking:** more thought per situation, parallel branches of thought merged by the child, no end token (idle thought becomes experience).
- **Finals:** {finalized adult, regular agent} × {long unsupervised gym, parented gym}, judged on the 7-day horizon.

## Positioning
Honest frame (per the harsh review): per-agent online continual learning implemented as full-rehearsal LoRA retraining from a frozen instruction-tuned model, with a keyword-retrieval RECALL over the life ledger (so this system also uses text memory). Closest neighbours: success-filtered self-training (STaR/ReST/expert iteration) and its diversity-collapse failure mode, SEAL, fast-weights/TTT, agent memory (Generative Agents, MemGPT, Reflexion, Voyager), and Cummins et al. 2023 on this compiler objective. The in-context / external-text / in-weights trichotomy is the positioning axis; no text-memory baseline has been run yet.
