## What is established now (terminal refresh)

1. The tested writers can lower likelihood loss on their training material
   and can strongly alter downstream behavior.
2. The dominant behavior in the compiler lives is usually a supplied fixed
   routine, not program-conditioned judgment. In the terminal three-life
   writer closure, only legacy cell A was positive on both panels in 3/3
   lives (mean deltas +0.0386 / +0.0161), and it carried that routine. No
   non-routine writer qualified.
3. Cell F contains an owner-specific component, but every tested condition
   spills far beyond the owner. None passes binding, specificity, interface,
   and non-harm together.
4. Historical parenting shows durable verbal repetition and, in one eligible
   life, only transient executed change. It is not a causal parenting result.
5. In the matched post-hoc nine-life disjoint-panel 2x2, the adapter contrast
   was -0.0146 and the static waking-brief contrast was +0.0041. The brief is
   therefore a useful carrier diagnostic, but it is not yet a prospective,
   candidate-blind active-text baseline.
6. The frozen child already produces varied observations (87.3--99.2%
   distinct, 1.3--2.0% factual drift), but it omits the requested canonical
   memory form in 13.3--14.6% of cases and mishandles negative knowledge in
   45.7--67.2%. The current bridge compiler repairs those failures, so its
   fitted rows cannot identify child-authored perception.

Therefore the immediate bottleneck is not more lifetime scale. It is whether
the writer can encode several conditional action policies without turning
them into one global habit.

## Ordered decision path

### 1. Close work already in flight without changing it

- The seed-0/7/8 writer replications are now terminal. Preserve the negative
  decision: no legacy non-routine writer advances.
- Finish the current R4 parenting lives and preserve their terminal receipts.
- Continue R5 unchanged as a mechanism-development life; do not promote its
  historically used panels to confirmatory evidence.

### 2. Run the smallest conditional writer falsifier

The Multi-key Writer Gateway **V10R1 simple scout** is the next development
experiment. Under the current standing builder authorization, Astra may
implement, test, and execute it once its own CPU tests and provenance checks
are logged; no additional per-experiment ratification is required. V7 was
retired during review because its apparently balanced mapping still admitted
tool-only and mode-only solutions. V8--V10 progressively closed that defect and
the remaining label/interface ambiguities; V10R1 is the stable ratification
candidate. The larger V6 custody shell remains deferred with C11.

V10R1 asks whether complementary rank-8 adapters can carry multiple seen
tool-by-mode endpoint policies under held renderings while preserving the ACT
interface and avoiding explicit spill families. It uses two disjoint roots,
sixteen evaluated keys per root and four clean-base fits under a three A40-hour
cap. Its XOR-oriented maps make every enumerated constant, tool-only,
mode-only, stratum-only, stratum-by-mode, and declared categorical nuisance
policy achieve at most 1/2 balanced accuracy on the frozen surface.

Passing V10R1 means only supervised conditional endpoint-policy carriage. It
does not establish lived-outcome causality, discovery, generalization,
retention, parenting, connected memory, or a whole-agent learning effect.

### 3. Qualify cumulative-replay coexistence only after a V10R1 pass

Do not pay for a cumulative campaign if V10R1 cannot first carry conditional
policies. If V10R1 passes both roots, freeze its exact writer representation
and add one separately logged qualification stage: four clean-base NEW-only
fits on an equal-sized identity-disjoint bank, then four clean-base OLD+NEW
fits on the deterministically interleaved replay union. OLD remains the four
immutable V10R1 adapters.

Do not use an 80% ratio of the 64 item-level mean NLL gains. That mean can be
zero or negative even when every gate-bearing per-key median passes. For each
bank and root-map adapter, define `KMG` as the mean of its 16 per-key median
NLL gains and require:

- every cumulative key still meets the absolute V10R1 median-NLL threshold;
- `KMG_CUM >= 0.8 * KMG_SINGLE` for OLD and independently for NEW;
- at least 12/16 keys and 6/8 per stratum individually retain at least 80% of
  their corresponding single-bank per-key median gain; and
- cumulative generated BA gain over the same immutable OFF records is at
  least 80% of the matching OLD-only and NEW-only BA gain, alongside a full
  absolute V10R1 pass on each bank.

Compute these as cross-multiplied inequalities; ratios are display-only. Make
bank orthogonality a precondition: OLD-only stays neutral on NEW, NEW-only on
OLD, and every adapter stays neutral on both banks of the other root. Test
candidate-TV per key (not only a bank-wide mean) and the **absolute** legal-ACT
rate difference from OFF, each within 0.05. NEW identifiers and orientation
are presealed and source-disjoint; pooled OLD+NEW geometry must exclude
bank-only and bank-crossed shortcut policies, and cumulative rows are
interleaved rather than concatenated.

Put every fit on one node, use the same optimizer seed within each root across
OLD/NEW/CUM and both maps, and a different predeclared seed across roots.
State `training_run_replication=false`, `root_seed_confounded=true`, and that
four maps are not four independent units. Stage the NEW-only fits and
orthogonality reads before paying for cumulative fits.

The terminal labels must preserve cause:
`NEW_OPTIMIZATION_INCONCLUSIVE` (or its exact interface/spill/binding subtype),
`BANK_ORTHOGONALITY_FAIL`, `CUMULATIVE_OPTIMIZATION_INCONCLUSIVE`,
`CUMULATIVE_WRITER_UNUSABLE`, `OLD_AND_NEW_CARRIAGE_FAIL`,
`OLD_CARRIAGE_FAIL`, `NEW_CARRIAGE_FAIL`, or
`TWO_BANK_CUMULATIVE_REPLAY_PASS`, after integrity and oracle precedence.
Report every gate boolean and never retry until pass.

This stage tests two-bank cumulative-replay coexistence/reconstruction, not
sequential parameter retention or unrehearsed retention: the old rows are
deliberately replayed and every adapter is rebuilt from the clean base. A pass
shows only that the mechanism used by this SLEEP design can reconstruct old
and new supervised banks together without the detected interference.

### 4. Test authentic child experience after the writer and cumulative-replay gates

The already-deliberated lived-mirror gateway is a useful engineering blueprint
for the shortest local THINK--experience--SLEEP--later-THINK relay, but do not
execute its original one-root endpoint-only scope unchanged after V10R1 and
cumulative-replay qualification. In that design the child-authored final `ACT` is the direct correct
supervised target and situation alone predicts it, so the write may ignore the
public outcome completely. An unablated pass would establish only
success-filtered child-grounded endpoint-policy carriage.

Rebind the scientific source assay before any output so TRUE and
OUTCOME_SHUFFLED are paired within every mirrored world. Use the same admitted
row IDs and all the same non-outcome fields: situation, initial action, final
target, target multiset, surfaces, order, masks, dose, optimizer seed, and
node. Permute only the public outcome token within each
`(world, situation/final-target)` stratum under a predeclared map that preserves
that stratum's outcome counts while breaking the within-row
action--outcome--endpoint binding.
Strengthen source validity so the admitted rows themselves cover both initial
actions and both outcomes in each situation, rather than relying on coverage
in discarded attempts. Evaluate TRUE and SHUFFLED through identical primary,
likelihood, interface, spill, and non-harm panels under common inference
seeds.

One TRUE/SHUFFLED block is a development bridge. Any paper sentence about
outcome binding needs the directional TRUE-minus-SHUFFLED effect to pass in
three disjoint predeclared scientific roots, with a
different source/optimizer seed across roots and a blockwise conjunction
rather than a universal reliability claim. A pass supports only that breaking
the within-row outcome binding reduced later mapping-specific action in these
micro-worlds. It still does not establish autonomous credit assignment,
reasoning-path learning, discovery, parenting, connected knowledge, lifetime
improvement, or the whole organism.

### 5. Certify the text comparator

Run a frozen target-blind retriever over the same admitted endpoint rows under
a fixed token budget. Declare whether it is candidate-visible (the actor
supplies candidates) or truly candidate-blind (it ranks the entire eligible
store); never use those labels interchangeably. Validate retrieval recall,
citation identity, oracle accessibility, store-swap sensitivity, necessary-row
use, and strict action use before comparing carriers. A failed retriever
integration cannot be evidence for parametric-memory superiority.

Keep three comparisons separate:

1. `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS` versus LoRA asks which carrier exposes the same exact
   evidence better at a fixed age.
2. `TEXT_COMMON_RAW` with shadow-versus-promoted LoRA asks whether a weight
   update adds value when both forks already receive the same basic text
   memory.
3. `ACTIVE_TEXT_NATIVE` versus `DLT_NATIVE` compares complete independently
   evolving systems and is a total lifetime effect after their histories
   diverge, not a same-data carrier effect.

Do not give a graph-expanding retriever to a LoRA mechanism arm and then credit
LoRA for connectedness or traversal performed by that retriever. Match the
generated-token and action envelopes; report retrieval compute, input tokens,
persistent bytes, training work, and latency rather than calling unlike
offline resources matched. The historical static brief remains a cheap
carrier diagnostic, not this baseline.

Before calling any text arm a **strong** baseline:

- freeze a simple comparator roster including recency, raw BM25/RAG, and
  static/evolving briefs; if development cannot distinguish RAW from AGG,
  confirm both or narrow the label instead of defaulting to the easier arm;
- make `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS` executable by binding one canonical read-key
  schema, four adaptive actor-selected candidate-visible lookups,
  prompt/response grammar, exposure dose, response-only mask, adapter mount
  scope, returned-row wrapper, and failure law, then score read fidelity
  separately from actor use;
- protect identical system/task/current-state/history partitions. Equal
  generated tokens do not make visible filler, position shifts, or state
  displacement neutral; certify padding or report the prompt surfaces as
  unmatched;
- constrain the native updater to pre-frontier public events with complete
  source identities and pass future-target, hidden-answer, store-swap,
  necessary-row/link-cut, and stale-supersession tests; and
- use independent adult lineages as the top-level unit for an architecture
  claim, or explicitly report `n_child=1`. Environment roots, checkpoints,
  candidates, and decode repeats are nested observations.

Permitted future plateau wording is configuration- and resource-conditional:
"This exact active-text updater/retriever at its frozen read/token budget met
the late-life equivalence rule while oracle headroom remained." A broader
external-memory saturation claim requires a predeclared higher-retrieval-budget
sensitivity.

### 6. Parenting becomes causal only after writer qualification

Use one clean pre-parent checkpoint and randomly assign matched childhood
roots to substantive versus sham process feedback. Before the parent-absent
exam, remove the parent, all childhood context, and every nursery external-
memory store, index, embedding, cache, and query state. The primary parenting
estimand remains the root-level interaction

`[AUC(parented, write-on) - AUC(parented, write-off)]
 - [AUC(sham, write-on) - AUC(sham, write-off)]`.

Lexical restatement is an echo diagnostic, not success. Executed conditional
action, world outcome, and persistence after later writes are the meaningful
measurements. Existing R4 histories remain developmental evidence only.

### 7. C11 closes only the supplied-memory mechanism ceiling

Only when the supplied-memory ceiling is actually ready to run do we finish
and enforce the full C11 guard. C11 tests a fixed-policy, fixed-topology
connected-route benchmark using supplied memory. It does not test learning,
LoRA transport, retention, recurrence, lifetime improvement, parenting, or the
complete organism, and it cannot stand in for those results.

### 8. The learned-organism relay is a separate experiment

The broader paper requires a separately specified and bound learned-organism
PCFL relay:
the child acquires supported atoms and authentic co-use links through its own
actions; a new goal tests goal-conditioned traversal; the resulting memory
causes a discriminating information-seeking action; the public outcome is
compiled and written; and a later disjoint goal needs that new relation.

That relay requires fresh source-disjoint material, independent roots, fixed
root-level statistics, common randomness, strong active-text memory,
parent/nursery removal, wrong-life and link/binding derangements, bridge and
write cuts, and immutable provenance/receipt handling. C11 can validate that
the benchmark and finite read topology are solvable before this more expensive
learned-carrier test; it cannot validate the learned-carrier claim itself.

The broader Dream--LoRA--Think objective additionally requires increasing-
lifetime evidence and compression or a clearly justified information-
efficiency measure. None is inferred from a writer-gateway or C11 pass.

## Stop rules

- If V10R1 returns `INTERFACE_INVALID`, `BINDING_WITH_SPILL`, or
  `GATEWAY_NEGATIVE`, stop scaling parenting through that writer
  representation and redesign the failed interface, locality, or conditional
  representation component. If it returns `OPTIMIZATION_INCONCLUSIVE`, infer
  no writer-capacity result: stop downstream qualification and permit at most
  the separately bound one-dose diagnostic; do not treat an unlucky or
  under-dosed fit as evidence that the representation cannot work.
- If V10R1 passes but the cumulative rebuild fails to reconstruct OLD, do not
  start a paper-grade childhood; solve two-bank cumulative-replay coexistence
  first.
- If the matched text carrier succeeds while the adapter fails, localize the
  bottleneck to parametric writing/access rather than the task or memory
  problem.
- If the writer and text carrier both fail while the explicit oracle passes,
  repair the carriers. If the oracle fails, the assay is invalid.
- Do not relabel any development panel, root, or post-hoc threshold as C11.
