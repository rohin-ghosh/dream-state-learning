## Scope that does not move

This paper has no classroom, cohort, peer exchange, teacher ensemble,
population learning, shared child state, or cross-child communication.

1. One reset, frozen, target-blind parent teaches one child process-level
   thinking through target-blind practice tasks and correction.
2. Practice forces the child to turn thought into action. The parent proposes
   credit assignment; a separate public task outcome decides whether the
   lesson is admissible.
3. Parenting then ends. Parent prose, correction text, nursery context,
   nursery ledger, and parent state are absent at deployment.
4. The parented checkpoint enters a fresh deployment gym as `P1`, with the
   Think--Dream--Sleep per-life LoRA loop. `R0` is the paper-facing strong
   frozen-parameter agent whose isolated active-text memory may evolve.
5. The public result is the `P1` versus `R0` learning-curve comparison. The
   matched `U0/U1/P0/P1` experiment is required causal support, not a
   classroom: it separates inherited starting competence, ordinary per-life
   writes, and the parenting-by-write interaction.
6. Independent roots are isolated reruns of the same fixed parent/child policy
   pair for uncertainty only. They never observe or affect one another.

## The result required for the intended claim

The central claim is not that parenting makes the entry checkpoint better.
It is that parenting improves the child's ability to learn from later,
parent-absent experience.

The sole primary is the registered gain-AUC interaction

```text
D = [gain_AUC(P1) - gain_AUC(P0)]
  - [gain_AUC(U1) - gain_AUC(U0)].
```

The registered joint success rule requires the two-sided 95% Student-t lower
endpoint for root-level `D` to exceed zero and observed mean `D >= 0.05` at
fixed `N=32`. The planning simulation estimates about 0.87 joint-pass
probability for several named mean-.07/SD-.10 families, but only 0.64 under
the retained rare-adverse-root family. This is a sensitivity calculation,
not a universal power guarantee.

The fixed downstream claim order is `D`, then the parented write gain
`W_P`, terminal learning `L_terminal`, the public system contrast `C_public`,
and the validated active-text control `T_R0`. A failed earlier rung forbids a
later superiority claim. The phrase *parenting improved the ability to learn*
requires the joint event `D AND W_P AND L_terminal`; `D` alone can be positive
when writes harm both parented and unparented children and therefore supports
only a relative write-benefit interaction. Entry advantage alone is inherited
competence.

## Scientific gates, in order

### Gate 0 — exact v2 deliberation authorization

Current state: blocked on Rohin's exact approval of the frozen workflow,
source manifest, and initialized state bytes. The recent conceptual agreement
on one-parent scope is not substituted for byte-level authorization.

Passing this gate authorizes external architecture deliberation only. It does
not authorize implementation, model/tokenizer calls, benchmark generation,
adapter work, CompilerGym, external scientific execution, or GPU use.

### Gate 1 — external five-role consensus and exact human ratification

The systems, benchmark, critique, consensus, and advocate roles must dispose
of every source-bound concern. Rohin then ratifies exact consensus bytes and
scope. Only then may scoped implementation and CPU/static validation begin.

### Gate 2 — model-free implementation proof

Required evidence:

- one immutable root packet implements `U0/U1/P0/P1/R0`, common-random
  coupling, generated-token equality, transactional failures, and resume
  invariants;
- typed thought, dispatched action, public outcome, dream successor state,
  accepted training row, and adapter receipt preserve exact provenance;
- parent and all childhood-only artifacts are provably absent at deployment;
- unsupported advice, raw ledgers, scalar outcomes, parent prose, and prose
  imitations of actions cannot become positive targets;
- the active-text baseline passes model-free lifecycle/resource fixtures;
- reducers reproduce the sole primary and fixed claim sequence; and
- all deterministic tests and the separately frozen resource manifest pass.

### Gate 3 — fresh implementation/science review and separate GPU gate

A fresh independent reviewer and a separate author-side advocate inspect the
implemented bytes and receipts. A later human authorization must separately
permit the exact canary targets, model/tokenizer operations, adapter fits,
CompilerGym execution, resource envelope, and GPU use.

### Gate 4 — writer and baseline canaries

Before parenting claims are tested:

- native response-only writes must preserve both free typed routing and
  typed-forced proposal quality;
- adapter mount/unmount and clean-base cumulative training must reproduce;
- `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` must pass disjoint once-only semantic faithfulness,
  prospective updater, retrieval/use, end-to-end, and task-headroom gates;
  otherwise it loses the word *strong*; and
- task/probe identifiers, actions, outcomes, and target content must remain
  absent from parenting material and hidden from prohibited roles.

### Gate 5 — one-parent writer/near-transfer canary

The first parenting result is deliberately small: at least one preregistered
process correction must be performed on a fresh homologous practice task,
supported by its public outcome, compiled in the child's native deployment
dialect, and expressed again with the parent absent. Reading or reciting a
lesson is not success. Parent-present compliance is not success. Better task
answers caused by leaked task content are not success.

The canary must also show that the write did not erase proposal quality,
typed action compliance, or the active-text path. Failure here stops the
deployment pilot and yields a writer/near-transfer diagnosis. Passing licenses
only same-family task-specific teachability and scientific spending. It does
not establish a durable cross-family thinking disposition or improved later
learning efficiency.

### Gate 6 — exact four-root spending pilot

Run four excluded roots through the exact confirmation packet. Advance only
if provenance/resource parity is exact and the directional conjunction
`D > 0`, `W_P > 0`, and `L_terminal > 0` holds within at least three of four
roots and for all three pilot means. This is a spending rule, not inferential
evidence and not a replacement for the fixed confirmation margins. A
`D`-only pilot can advance writer engineering, but it cannot justify spending
32 roots under an "improved learning" objective because `D` may be positive
while parented writes are harmful or the parented child never improves.

The pilot must print the full writer mediator by arm and cut: eligible and
accepted scientific rows, rehearsal fills, target types, support/admission
rates, supervised tokens, optimizer slots, fit/mount failures, and subsequent
typed behavior. This reveals whether parenting merely taught the child to
produce rows favored by the deterministic writer.

### Gate 7 — fixed-N confirmation

Run 32 isolated roots of the same fixed one-parent/one-child policy pair.
Each root receives twelve matched nursery opportunities, then the frozen
48-program wake deck and eight-program sealed probe deck under the registered
finite-universe distribution. No root, parent, child, store, adapter, context,
or artifact crosses root boundaries.

Produce entry-adjusted learning curves, root-level `D`, forward transfer,
retention, valid/distinct/informative actions per token, proposal/routing
decomposition, administrative-missingness accounting, and exact resource
receipts. Interpret only the highest surviving rung.

## Honest fallback ladder

1. `C4`: parenting improves deployment-time learning (`D`, beneficial `W_P`,
   and literal `L_terminal` all pass in the fixed sequence) — intended paper.
2. `C3`: parent-absent `P1` beats validated `R0`, but `D` fails — system win,
   not evidence that parenting taught learning ability.
3. `C2`: unparented personal writes beat the frozen twin — per-life
   experiential consolidation paper, not parenting/meta-intelligence.
4. `C1`: one process correction becomes parent-absent behavior — narrow
   teachability/writer result.
5. `C0`: prospective writer pathology/instrument result — submit only if the
   negative evidence is broad, clean, and important enough; never rebrand the
   contaminated exploratory v6.1 run as confirmation.

If no rung above C0 is clean by September 16, continue the science for a later
venue instead of submitting an inflated claim.

## AI-use and authorship posture

ICLR 2027 requires disclosure in both the manuscript and submission form.
The policy explicitly requires disclosure of AI assistance with conceptual
frameworks, hypotheses, methodology/experiment design, implementation, data
processing, and interpretation, and recommends disclosure of code editing,
artifacts, paper drafting/editing, literature work, and brainstorming.

The appropriate posture is factual rather than defensive: Rohin originated
and repeatedly corrected the thesis, architecture, claim priorities, and
protocol decisions; he controls the submission and is responsible for its
claims. Generative AI systems assisted with research engineering, design
critique, implementation, artifact audit, literature synthesis, analysis,
and drafting. Failed, null, adverse, and overclaimed AI-assisted work is kept
in the provenance record rather than silently removed. Human authors review
the final evidence, manuscript, code, and disclosure and take responsibility
for the submission.

Writing more of the final manuscript by hand may improve voice and ownership,
but it does not erase mandatory disclosure of earlier AI use. The defensible
answer to “AI slop” is a strong result, transparent provenance, independent
audits, and restrained claims—not concealment.
