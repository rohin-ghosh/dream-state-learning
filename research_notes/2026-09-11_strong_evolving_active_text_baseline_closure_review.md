## Verdict

**PASS for architecture-intake/reconciliation; not PASS for source authoring
or execution.** The repair accepts and correctly disposes every fatal finding
in the adversarial critique. In particular, it no longer makes one text system
serve incompatible causal roles:

- `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS` is only a fixed carrier diagnostic;
- `TEXT_COMMON_RAW` is only a narrow common auxiliary-memory control for an
  incremental promotion estimand;
- `ACTIVE_TEXT_NATIVE` is a standalone strong external-memory opponent; and
- PCFL E2--E5 mechanism cells retain the finite one-hop reader and receive no
  PPR or automatic graph expansion.

That separation closes the central attribution failure. A graph retriever can
no longer donate the connectedness/traversal mechanism to cells intended to
test the child or LoRA, while `ATE+promoted` versus `ATE+shadow` is no longer
misreported as direct superiority over ATE.

The repair also closes the previously identified design blockers:

- matched payload bytes are distinguished from common raw provenance;
- childhood external memory is deleted before weight-persistence exams;
- CompilerGym saturation language is removed;
- historical ledgers are demoted to reconstructed diagnostics;
- repeated visits receive occurrence-indexed, arm-independent tapes;
- batch barriers and query-per-continuation semantics are explicit;
- legacy `Ledger.recall` is disabled in text cells;
- tokenizer-measured prompt packing replaces character-budget assertions;
- the invalid LCB and noisy latency tie-break are withdrawn;
- opaque IDs are normalized before dense retrieval;
- actor, retrieval, and true-shadow training costs are separated;
- the infeasible 16/32/64 sweep, fixed `n=100`, `0.01` certificate margin,
  unsupported Pareto claim, and CG plateau are withdrawn; and
- root-first AUC, independent confirmation, powered N, novelty-certified
  PCFL cuts, equivalence, retention, and adverse filling are required before
  any corresponding claim.

## Exact boundary on this PASS

The repair deliberately leaves implementation arithmetic and confirmation
statistics for separate exact contracts in its Stages 0--2. That is acceptable
because the note grants no source or run authority. It would **not** be
acceptable to treat this closure PASS as satisfying those later contracts.

Before source-authoring ratification, the successor packet must make four
currently verbal choices exact:

1. **Paired scientific root.** For the direct-system comparison in Sections 1
   and 6.1, define one independent root as one sealed, parent-deleted starting
   child forked into byte-identical `DLT_NATIVE` and `ACTIVE_TEXT_NATIVE`
   potential-outcome branches. Different roots are independent; the two
   systems within a root do not begin from separately selected children.
   Otherwise "root-paired AUC" is undefined or child selection is confounded.
2. **SESOI chronology.** In Section 6.1, replace any reading of “derived from
   DEV-stage SESOI” that permits outcome-dependent practical margins. The
   SESOI/equivalence band must be sealed before the DEV outputs used to choose
   `CG-RAW` versus `CG-AGG` are opened. DEV may estimate variance, not decide
   what difference counts as meaningful after observing it.
3. **One identifier-normalization law.** Section 4.3 currently says
   first-public-appearance **or** registered-public-slot order. The exact
   contract must choose one per named system and bind collision, restart,
   twin, and store-swap behavior. An implementer may not select whichever
   produces better retrieval.
4. **Namespace and authority.** The successor must distinguish writer-ladder
   E2/E3 from learned-PCFL E2--E5 (for example `W-E2` and `M-E2..M-E5`) and
   bind the exact finite-reader source digest. “Existing finite reader” is not
   authorization to import a rejected/unratified proposal or the parked C11
   implementation.

These are mandatory closure fields for the next packet, not reasons to reject
the repaired role design.

## Acceptance conclusion

The staged acceptance path in Sections 8.0--8.7 is sufficient as a design
boundary:

1. role/claim and reset freeze;
2. provenance/resource feasibility audit;
3. separate exact contracts;
4. model-free identity, chronology, randomness, blindness, ranking, packing,
   isolation, and accounting tests;
5. a powered opposing-store actor certificate;
6. a promotion-versus-true-shadow dry run before long lives; and
7. separate PCFL mechanism and native-baseline paths.

No model or GPU experiment should be inferred from this PASS. The next valid
move is adjudicated consensus over the repaired role matrix plus the four
exact fields above, followed by exact human ratification of a bounded source
