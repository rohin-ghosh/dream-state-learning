## 1. Area chair verdict

As drafted in ONBOARDING and mandated by PROMPT, this paper would be rejected, and not narrowly. Both headline cell counts of the abstract ("0 collapses in gated lives", "2–5 points above the plain model on held-out programs") are measured on the same eight programs, with the same seed, on which the gate selected every reported adapter minutes earlier (`run_life_v2.py:166-189, 229-303`), so the safety number is largely a restatement of the gate rule and the gain is a winner's-curse statistic; the "held-out" panel also has same-source siblings inside the training curriculum under mibench-v1 names (`run_life.py:43-59`), which any CompilerGym user can check against the public manifests. The positioning frame ("recurs in weights, not continual learning, not RAG, the model edits its weights by thinking") is contradicted by the code (the adapter is recomputed from the clean base over a cumulative text corpus every sleep; RECALL is keyword retrieval), has zero supporting cells (no text-memory arm was ever run), and omits the closest literature: success-filtered self-training (STaR/ReST/ReST-EM/expert iteration) and its known diversity-collapse failure mode, SEAL, TTT/fast weights, the agent-memory canon, continual learning for LLM agents, and Cummins et al. 2023 on this exact compiler objective. The repository currently holds two incompatible paper plans and three manuscripts with no ruling between them; the plan with the most design (64_: PCFL M/L/C, E0, clean lineage) has no executable code and cannot report by Sep 25. Beneath this there is a real, honest, publishable characterization paper: a 1,024-episode per-life self-SFT system with paired probes at every checkpoint, an ungated arm that shows late-life behavioural collapse invisible to a format canary (3/9 lives), a behavioural gate that has now caught three such collapses before commit with passing scores, a pre-registered ritual detector with a consistent onset window, a recipe lock-in explanation of the measured gain that the authors themselves reached on Sep 10, and a storage-vs-extraction diagnostic. That paper is writable by Sep 18 if, and only if, the five fatal items below are cured (four are decisions and text; one needs roughly 11 GPU-hours and must start by Sep 11), the sixteen major items are either fixed or disclosed, and the abstract avoids every sentence in section 6. Recommendation to the authors: withdraw the ONBOARDING framing today, rule which paper this is, and submit the characterization paper with the parenting and bootstrap material as observational and future work.

The gate was designed this way (COORD:537 "using the existing 8-eid seeded probes"; COORD:544 "awaiting your ratification" — no ratification appears).
