## 0. One screen

- **Mechanism statement.** Post-training changes behaviour (every chatbot) and creates memory (Physics of LLMs; any post-trained fact). Both are known. What is not known how to do is make an agent's *raw experience* into good post-training data. **The thing we teach is the behaviour of producing good post-training data about its own experience**, so that every input — an environment outcome, a parent, a person — flows through the model's thought into post-trainable, rememberable weight changes at sleep, and the next outputs can be post-trained too. "Everything it thinks turns into post-trainable data." The novelty moves from the learning algorithm to the data-generation disposition.
- **Diagnosis of the current bug.** The LoRA changes behaviour but does not remember; everything runs through thought. That is what you predict if parenting installed *reflection without articulation*: the model learned to reason and not to leave artifacts. The curriculum needs a second thing alongside self-evaluation — produce statements about what happened that are worth training on.
- **The flywheel.** Childhood (high plasticity): parenting builds the flywheel — the better the child learns how to learn, the more it learns from every reinforcement. Once built, childhood parenting is done; the post-childhood agent is deployed into a gym and keeps learning as an adult (plasticity still present, at a different rate; some frameworks stay, some keep breaking and re-forming). **The paper = parenting and birth create the childhood self-learning flywheel; deployment shows the adult flywheel still turning on the test gym.** Scope is finite: a flywheel good enough to learn better than a regular agent on our test gym, not everything in the world (a frontier lab's flywheel would run on ten layers of reflection and sub-agents; ours is scoped to the deadline).
- **Two hypotheses (the other assistant's split, adopted by Rohin).** *H1* (necessary condition, verified by us; TMEM and Allen-Zhu are the reason to expect it, not the evidence): skills taught through think-then-sleep are retained in the LoRA and expressed outside the context where they were taught. *H2* (the paper): an agent carrying those skills improves faster from self-generated experience on an unseen verifiable task than the same model without them, **and the gap depends on continued consolidation** — slope, not starting level. Clean test: a 2×2, parented vs not × sleep loop running vs frozen at deployment. H1 is the parenting effect with sleep frozen; H2 is the interaction. If parented+frozen matches parented+sleeping, the skills are good but they are not driving learning.
- **Not self-distillation.** "The model distills, but it doesn't originate." Every cycle's content traces to something outside — an environment outcome, a parent's correction, a person's response; the model contributes compression, not information. State this in the same paragraph as the mechanism or a reviewer supplies the collapse objection. Corollary: **a memory with no traceable source is confabulated** — a mechanical provenance gate, replacing the vocabulary leakage gate we lost when worlds became semantic.
- **The agent parent amortises a human teacher.** Rohin directs the agents and wrote the parenting corpus; there is one of him and finite hours, so the agents scale what he can teach. Humans would be the best parents (self-judgement, complex goal-setting, reaction to situations, learned values and patterns); the paper claims only what was run and the essay carries the rest.
- **Pointer principle (the other assistant's unification, adopted).** Semantic priors instead of built structure; emotional situations already in pretraining instead of an emotion system; descriptions of judgement and goal-setting already in pretraining instead of teaching them from scratch. The architecture works by routing latent pretrained structure into the decision loop rather than installing anything new — which is why a frozen base is right, not a limitation.
- **First person, procedural.** Pretraining holds "people evaluate their approach before committing" — third person, descriptive, about others. The LoRA needs "I evaluated my approach before committing, and here is what happened" — first person, procedural, with an outcome; only the second is a policy. Parenting is partly a register conversion, and the property is checkable in the corpus (person and tense).
- **What parents teach.** How to perceive strongly enough that things are remembered and connectable; how to think about ideas and extract them from the past; how to spend time thinking; how to plan, execute, meta-think, self-reflect; multilayer, long- and short-term planning; how to move between planning and execution and back.

## 1. The two hypotheses as experiments

| | sleep **frozen** at deployment | sleep **running** at deployment |
|---|---|---|
| **unparented** child | level and slope of a plain adapter on the unseen gym | does an untaught child improve from its own experience at all? |
| **parented** child | **H1**: the taught skills are expressed outside the teaching context (level) | **H2**: the parented agent's slope from self-generated experience |

H1 = parented − unparented with sleep frozen. H2 = the interaction: the parented advantage grows when consolidation continues.

**What exists (Fable).** Levels with the adapter frozen: 24 lives' final adapters probed ON vs OFF on the unseen 12-program panel (SEQ-029–035; the routine carries +0.02–0.04; the parented RP lives are in that range) and the text-memory baseline that pits the adapter against the child's own written brief (brief ≥ adapter on 15 of 24 lives) — H1's comparator between memory in weights and memory in text. **What has never been run:** any deployment on the unseen gym with the sleep loop running, hence no slopes, hence no H2. That is the paper's missing experiment.

**H2 design (Fable's proposal for Rohin; not launched).** Deploy the final adapter of each finished life into the unseen-program gym for 256 episodes: cell "running" = sleep every 32 (8 sleeps, the gate as in life); cell "frozen" = the same adapter and episodes with no sleep. Lives: parented RP 400/401/402 vs unparented R2 seeds (matched by node); R4 gate-only lives as a third row if budget allows. Measure score-vs-episode slope on the unseen programs (12 programs × ~21 visits), plus the register and provenance of what the running cells write. Cost ≈ 18 GPU-hours per run (4.2 min/episode) → 2×2 × 3 lives = 12 runs ≈ 9 GPU-days, about 1.5 days on 8 GPUs once the write pretests end. **Constraint:** seeding a run from an existing adapter is a "bootstrapped" launch under Codex's STOP (bootstrap_v1/v2/v3 had unverified provenance). Here the seed adapters are the finished lives' own final adapters with full receipts, but the letter of the STOP covers it — **Rohin decides whether to lift or scope the STOP for this**. R5 lives cannot be deployed on this gym (they were gated on it).

**Astra's design audit (memo q12, 2026-09-11 23:45 UTC; adopted).** (a) Approve a *staged* 2×2 as a test of consolidation-dependent improvement — not yet a clean causal test of parenting: finished RP vs R2 lives compare **training packages** (parent interaction, prompt scaffolding, classroom time), and no classroom-matched unparented control exists among finished lives; label the historical contrast "RP-package vs R2-package". (b) Pre-registered primary contrast: per life and condition, G = mean over the 12 programs of (mean score at visits 15–21 − mean score at visits 1–7); θ = (G_RP,run − G_RP,frozen) − (G_R2,run − G_R2,frozen); three matched RP–R2 blocks within node; θ̂ = mean of the block interactions with a t-interval on 2 df; the life/block is the replication unit. Illustrative **MDE ≈ 0.06 score units** with three blocks (assuming panel-mean SD 0.0065 and no pairing covariance) — not powered for a 0.02 interaction. (c) Schedule: 21 balanced rounds of the 12 programs = 252 episodes (four extras excluded from the primary contrast), order randomised within rounds and shared across the four branches of a block; sleep every 32 in both running rows with the same gate, panel and threshold (the estimand is *gated* consolidation); initial-checkpoint probe and post-sleep probes including after sleep 8. (d) "Frozen" = no weight change with every other persistent state matched (a shadow sleep that produces the same artifacts but cannot update weights); inventory the non-weight memory (notes in context, retrieval store, RNG, prompts) before launch. (e) Node blocking: each block on one node; an identical-checkpoint canary first; the 0.029 node discrepancy is the size of the adapter gains, so nothing unblocked is pooled. (f) Budget: 256 episodes ≈ 17.9 GPU-hours per branch; twelve branches plus sleeps ≈ **223 GPU-hours ≈ 9.3 GPU-days ≈ 28 hours on eight GPUs**, probes and gate overhead extra (the "~1.5 GPU-days" first written in §4 was wrong). A repeated fixed-adapter probe can replace the frozen branches only if episodes reset all non-weight state — otherwise full frozen trajectories. (g) The STOP decision needs a launch manifest: hashes of base, tokenizer, adapter tensors/config, code commit, prompts, environment; ancestry (life, node, every accepted sleep checkpoint, parent checkpoint); corpus hashes, episode and parent-utterance IDs, gate configuration and results; verification that the loaded tensors match the recorded final adapter, no undocumented bootstrap ancestor, no R5/deployment-panel selection, no probe feedback into training or seed selection; a reproduced recorded probe within tolerance on the deployment node; Rohin's scoped authorisation naming the seed hashes. (h) Instrument the chain experience → artifact → eligible corpus → accepted update → retained knowledge → better action (coverage/selection, register, provenance as factual support not lexical overlap, rituals, consolidation statistics, fresh-context retention probes), so a null is diagnosable. (i) The claim stays narrow: repeated adaptation on 12 programs is consolidation-dependent adaptation on this panel, not general learning on unseen tasks — keep a small untouched transfer set if one exists.

## 2. Provenance instead of vocabulary

Every memory item must trace to an external input: an episode outcome (program, action, measured result), a parent utterance (id), or a planted observation (the car test). Implementation (Fable, cheap): compile_sleep tags each corpus item with its source; the report counts items whose factual content (numbers, pass names, program names) does not appear in any source of that episode — the **confabulation rate** — and the gate drops them. The car-test cells satisfy this by construction (every rendering traces to a planted observation). SEQ-036's finding that the affect words in the lives' notes were bootstrap echo is a provenance failure already observed.

## 3. Register: what the lives actually wrote (SEQ-050)

The first check the formalisation asks for is cheap and was run within the hour, 2026-09-11 23:30 UTC (`research_notes/analysis/corpus_register_audit.py`, crude word markers on the child's own NOTE text in every finished life's first and final sleep corpus; the harness wrapper "My thinking: … So I did: X → outcome" is not scored). Result: at the first sleep the notes are first-person in 25–60 % of items and prospective ("I expect", "I will") in 30–65 %, retrospective (outcome words) in 0–20 %, generic in 15–40 %; by the final sleep first person is under 10 % in 17 of 25 lives (under 5 % in 13), prospective 2–5 %, retrospective stays at or below 7 % in 23 of 25, while "specific" content (pass names, numbers) rises to 70–97 % — **the notes drift into bare recipes with neither self nor outcome** (the same_recipe / templated_notes rituals seen live). Exception: the parented RP 400 wrote outcome words in 37 % of its notes (RP 402: 10 %). So the untaught child's record is not third-person description; it is procedure lists without a first-person record of what happened — a different failure from the one predicted, with the same consequence (nothing trainable beyond the recipe). Parents can move it (RP 400). **Sleep-by-sleep (SEQ-051, new items only):** 17 lives lose the first person after the first sleep and lock into the recipe ritual; 8 lives flip mid-life into a first-person regime that hand-reading shows to be a second ritual — the bootstrap's and parents' meta-instructions repeated as slogans ("Form expectations before acting. Write down what I learn."), specifics falling to zero. Neither costume records what happened. Parenting shifted the costume (RP lives echo slogans most), not the content. The child does not invent facts (provenance ≥ 98.5 % in 24 of 25 lives); it repeats and catalogues. Design consequence: the parent must name the artifact, not the practice, and the sleep corpus needs an articulation gate (IDEAS.md, same time-stamp) — the behavioural twin of the bridge's canonical frame.

## 5. Mechanisms and strategy (Rohin, ~23:55 UTC; raw text = message 2 of `THESIS_RAW_ROHIN_2026-09-11.md`, which wins where this paraphrase differs)

- **Two adapters.** Rank 32 for memory (remembering), rank 8 for behaviour. Simple.
- **Consolidation schedule** (episode by episode or otherwise) does not matter now; if the mechanism works it can be the next paper.
- **The loop is just a loop**: output → input, actions produce new inputs; the context window grows; daydreaming distils the context; the model reads its plan tokens and either keeps planning or makes a new plan, turns the plan into a sub-plan for current execution, executes, re-plans — about three layers of planning. Agentic models already partly know this; the rest has to be learned into the LoRA.
- **Compilation, two kinds.** *Memory*: short sequences, ordinary training, one perception = one memory sequence = one datapoint (Physics of LLMs) — "make sure we're having enough perception"; the only thing to know is the start and end token of each thought (perception, reflection, action). *Behaviour*: long, variable-length sequences — full sequence, sub-sequence, thought stream, goal stream — delimited by the start and end of a state and the start and end of a goal, across layers and sub-layers of execution; inter-sequence change (the parent's interventions) may need end-to-end goal sequences too. This is "the only unknown" and "a little bit difficult"; it does not have to be perfect, it has to work; add it to the compilation of the rank-8 adapter so the behavioural changes we already get become better.
- **Copy post-training.** Look at how post-training produces behaviour and how it produces memory; that is how we compile and write; make sure it works at the right scale of input in our system.
- **Strategy, three steps, nothing else changes.** (1) With the right outputs the mechanism works — it already exists; make sure it works in our system. (2) Make the child take its inputs and run its own intelligence to build the outputs that match what the mechanism was tested on — parenting; we know what parenting is supposed to do; how it does it we keep testing with agents and ideas; scale episodes, parent feedback, environments and self-thoughts to the maximum; keep the learning system simple enough to be learned quickly. (3) The final test: the inputs are matched on the test — some parent on the test, but not all-knowing, a behaviour-based parent or maybe no parent; enough environment that the flywheel keeps turning. "Make sure that the actual thing that we're testing is as scaled down as possible for our thesis, and the amount of actual training is as scaled up as possible." "We're not changing anything, we're just refactoring our strategy so this actually starts happening as soon as possible."

**Fable's mapping to what exists (for the discussion, not a ruling).** Memory compilation: built and measured — the bridge's child-authored frames are exactly "one perception = one memory sequence" (SEQ-046–049); porting it into compile_sleep (the child writes N looks per episode outcome, ending in a canonical frame) is a day. Behaviour compilation: the write pretest says whole-text × 3 epochs on the child's own thinking carries the routine on every life (cell A, 3/3), the two-scale windows were inconsistent (B) and QA collapses the interface (C); goal/state-delimited variable-length segments have not been tried — that is the unknown Rohin names; the cheapest version is to add goal/plan/execute delimiters to the tick format and cut segments on them. Two adapters: designed (8 + 32), not built in run_life_v2; rank 32 buys nothing for frames at this corpus size (SEQ-044), so the separation is architectural, not for capacity. Planning layers: the organism has PREDICT/ACT/NOTE/RECALL per tick and a brief at sleep; no PLAN/GOAL tokens yet. The final test = the H2 2×2 (§1), with a behaviour-only or absent parent as Rohin says.


## 6. Hardcoded vs learned; the flywheel levels (Rohin, 00:00 UTC; raw text = message 3 of `THESIS_RAW_ROHIN_2026-09-11.md`, which wins where this paraphrase differs)

- **Hardcoded vs learned.** "The child writes 16 things per event" is a hardcoded baseline: it is not intelligent and not usable in every situation. The learned version is *discretionary memory*: is this important? how much do I think about it? That is what parenting teaches. The in-between is what we do: teach the idea ("remember big ideas; perceive them in many ways; if something is important, think about it a lot, perceive it a lot, connect it") and give a baseline with numbers ("do it 10×, 20×, 30×, 5× depending on how it works"); then in the gym the child adds nuance. The same pattern for behavioural patterns/values, for goals, execution and meta-goals, and for validation/verification: baselines pre-trained, nuance learned.
- **The learned-memory test.** Give the child a whole gym sequence and ask: do you remember this? the important thing? the least important thing? — and judge. It should probably not remember something it saw once, vaguely; it must remember the big things, however fast they went by. (Fable: this is the "struggle test" of the design and the graded-importance recall test; the bridge's 16 looks are the hardcoded floor it is measured against.)
- **The flywheel levels.** *Level 1* — the base model with prompting already has some recursive thought and memorisation: the tiny flywheel exists at birth. *Level 2* — pre-training the LoRA on some instruction base (birth); "a little bigger, still super baby"; maybe not even needed. *Level 3* — preparation / preschool: parenting gyms with quick built tests, "initial learning just for the learning", to make sure the systems work with some actual learned effort — the first time the thing learns on its own somewhat; the smallest flywheel that lets the classroom succeed. *Level 4* — school: the same curriculum (how to think, learn, proceed, reason, plan, execute, self-reflect on actions, self-reflect on self-reflection) taught with the real external world attached, real reinforcement, subject by subject. Then the full flywheel is deployed in the test. Levels 3 and 4 are very connected; level 2 is connected to the LoRA's pre-training; it is all connected.
- **The plan.** First make sure the mechanism works. Then find what data the mechanism likes — for behaviour change (it is in pre-training / post-training; it exists) and for memory and remembered experience (papers exist); we are not inventing this. Once the data is known, formalise parenting: test the level-1 baby flywheel, then birth (level 2), then the preschool flywheel (level 3), then schooling (level 4). About a week, with some margin wanted. "We're very very very close. The mechanism is almost working. Just get that done."

**Fable's mapping (for the discussion, not a ruling).** Level 1 is measured: the untaught bridge cells (b: 0.82 vs synthetic 0.91) are the base model's perception with a hardcoded 16 and a required frame; the register audit (SEQ-050/051) is its thinking register — recipes and slogans, no records. Level 2 is the birth/bootstrap zone (bootstrap_v1–v3, the Codex STOP): the taught variants t/u are the first level-2-like intervention at the mechanism level — a lesson in the prompt, not yet in the weights. Level 3 = the preparation phase and parenting gyms of BLEND v3 §6, the articulation gate (Astra q13) as their first built test, the reasoning-gym band as a ready quick test. Level 4 = the classroom lives with parents (RP) — three finished, all slogan- or recipe-locked, so the curriculum has not yet produced records. The learned-memory test is the struggle test (BLEND §6 item 5), unbuilt. The final test is the H2 2×2 (§1).


## 7. The first level-3 experiment: preschool for records (Astra memo q14, 2026-09-12 00:06 UTC; adopted as design, NOT launched)

**Why.** SEQ-052: the articulation rate is 0.000 in all 28 lives and the largest rejection families are "prediction only" and "no result number" — the tick asks for the note BEFORE the act and its outcome, so a record of what happened has nowhere to be written by the child. Astra: "the current pipeline does not produce child-authored records" is what the evidence supports, not "the child cannot learn record-writing". Repair the interaction first.

**The intervention (three cells, two lives each, 128 episodes, 4 sleeps, ≈ 10 GPU-hours per life, ≈ 61 GPU-hours total).**
- **(a) Post-outcome slot.** PREDICT / NOTE / ACT unchanged; after execution the harness shows `ACT executed`, instructions before / after, measured reduction, execution status, then a `NOTE_AFTER:` field (one short generation, ~100 tokens, tied to the execution id, available at every tick including the last). Slot-only cell A says only "this field is available after the execution result" — no examples, no schema, no minimum, no retry.
- **(b) The artifact lesson (cells B, C).** The parent's paragraph names a *measured action record* — "what you actually tried and what you actually observed, in your own voice; an expectation is not yet a result; a poor result can still make a good record; keep observations separate from guesses" — with three explicitly synthetic examples that vary the OUTCOME (improvement, no change, regression), never the bridge's canonical sentence; delivered before episode 1, one short refresher with one new example after sleeps 1–3, after the post-sleep probe; fixed content across lives; parents see no scores.
- **(c) The numbered baseline (cell C only).** "For a starting practice, aim to write 10 measured action records per episode… practice, not a requirement… adjust as you learn." Neither enforced nor padded.
- Run cell A's first 32 episodes first (plumbing), then all six lives; two seed/program-order blocks shared across cells; corpus policy, sleep training and acceptance panel held constant.

**Measurement.** Pre-registered A_s^raw = executions with a qualifying child-authored post-outcome record (first-person account of the executed action + an observed numerical outcome attributable to it + no contradiction with the trace) / executions returning a numerical result in the block; harness text never counts; score all measured attempts, not only successes; the corpus's own articulation reported with its own denominator. **Preschool acquisition threshold:** in each of the final two 32-episode blocks A_s^raw ≥ 0.20, ≥ 24 of 32 episodes with a qualifying record, ≥ 98 % factual precision on a manual audit — in BOTH lives of a cell. This establishes record-writing under the teaching configuration, not H1. **Sleep-destruction signature:** raw articulation rises before a sleep and falls ≥ 50 % relative and ≥ 10 points absolute on the same neutral probe right after an ACCEPTED adapter update, at two boundaries; replaying with the saved pre-sleep adapter recovers it.

**Gate in shadow mode throughout;** per-sleep log of G / N / D separately and jointly, admission, rejection families, new-item admission vs whole-corpus survival, pre/post-sleep probes, acceptance, loss. **Promotion to enforced** only after two consecutive blocks with A_s^raw ≥ 0.20, broad coverage and ≥ 64 distinct admitted new items per block — in a later phase, all cells at once.

**Training-item format.** An admitted record REPLACES the harness's appended "So I did: … → outcome" line: `Program <id>. My measured action record: <verbatim NOTE_AFTER>`; the authoritative trace stays in audit metadata; never rewrite a deficient note; during shadow mode legacy items stay unchanged and their contribution is logged.

**Cheapest H1 probe.** One probe episode immediately before and after each sleep (8 extra), excluded from harvesting and parent feedback, on paired held-out programs, fresh context, no classroom history or reminders, ordinary task instructions, measurements visible, a neutral optional post-result field (`Scratchpad`, not the taught name). Preliminary H1 evidence only if both lives keep ≥ 20 % articulation on the final post-sleep neutral probes and exceed their pre-training neutral baseline (base-checkpoint replay saved); parent-specific evidence needs B or C above A — if all three transfer equally, the finding is that repairing the interaction enabled the skill, not that parenting taught it.

**Astra's warning.** An excellent ledger is not reflection: first-person restatement of supplied measurements is a worthwhile preschool behaviour and need not change the next decision. Establish the ledger first; then test whether a stored record changes a later decision when the program returns.

## Ladder v3 — Rohin's formalisation of 2026-09-14 ~23:15 UTC (message 80), with Fable's table for correction

Rohin (raw in THESIS_RAW message 80): level 0 is the LoRA mechanism — a model with the correct inputs learns behaviour and memory that hold long-term; level 1 makes the model's own thought rich enough to work in the LoRA by scaling its reasoning reasonably (more where it matters), built by opening the loop with an evaluator who knows how LoRA learns; level 2 injects parenting so the richness is the RIGHT richness — thought that is cohesive and converges toward learning across sleeps ("parenting is teaching the model how to converge to learning"; "RL in the closed loop"); level 3 teaches the model to converge toward outcomes so its thinking increases its capability in generalised environments; level 4 deploys. In his RL framing: 1 = fine-tune so the open loop works; 2 = evaluate the closed loop so it works; 3 = make it work for long-term outcomes; 4 = deploy.

### Ladder v3, corrections from Rohin (message 81, ~23:25 UTC)

- **Level 2 is preschool.** The parent evaluates the learner, not the task score: how it is learning, how it is growing, whether it is accelerating. Environments are still needed to judge anything, but the level-2 metrics are learner-centric: per-sleep gain on fresh tasks (slope), acceleration (the change in that gain across sleeps), cohesion (behaviour on repeated probes agrees across sleeps; no regression on the old), and shrinking dependence on the parent (guided-vs-unguided evaluation gap closing). ## Abstract shape — Rohin, message 85 (2026-09-15 ~00:20 UTC), "write this down"

In his words, lightly ordered: Level 0 is the mechanism — a simple LoRA that, given the correct inputs, turns experience into behaviour and memory; we found which skills it learns and at what dose. Level 1 is richness — we do not teach the model to reason, plan or perceive (it already can); we give it a much higher token budget and induce thought that branches where it matters, so that its own experience becomes learnable by the level-0 mechanism. Level 2 takes all of the model's capabilities and its level-1 richness and utilises them for the purpose of the closed loop — collect, compile, sleep, use — under a parent that watches how it learns; this is where behaviours like the route interface may be taught rather than at level 1, and it will not be easy. Level 3 takes that learning and utilises it for generalised environmental outcomes in hard, held families. Level 4 deploys it, unparented, against the same model without the flywheel. Levels 1–3 lie closer together than the earlier ladders assumed.

Operational corollary he attached: go all out on richness now (many generators in parallel, heavy token budgets, guidance vs over-guidance as an axis); run level-2 tests on the first richness success; and push the terse route behaviour through the level-2 closed loop as well, with long- and short-horizon parents.

## Compiler, sleep and context — Rohin, messages 86–87 (2026-09-15 ~01:05–01:35 UTC), "document that"

1. **No selective gate at level 2.** Compilation does not evaluate experience in order to leave things out. The sleep keeps most or all of the cycle's experience, good and bad, outcome-tagged; good-and-bad matters for self-learning, and parenting trains on both. For simple rich behaviours richness alone suffices. The two zero-update guided cycles on 2026-09-14/15 were a failure of an old selection system, not of the parent or the child.
2. **Sleep is parented.** Replay is a session in which the child thinks back over its previous parenting sessions and its experience; the parent guides the replay as it guides the day. Parenting sessions need strong richness. Two options, both to try: make the replay/dream portion more plastic than the experience portion, or give it more richness and data (possibly a sub-agent swarm enriching the replay). Rohin prefers MATCHED plasticity between the two.
3. **Replay is long-horizon.** "I replayed this last time as well" — long-horizon goals build in over many sleeps; what was replayed and built in sleep lasts across cycles; each sleep needs only enough richness not to be lost and to be noticeable; the greater richness accrues over the horizon.
4. **Replay sits right before, or interleaved with, the write.** The replay session and the weight update can alternate sequentially so replay does not hold up the write.
5. **Projection is withdrawn.** Reducing plasticity is the down-projection; nothing is removed by the compiler; its last step is additive replay with a chosen learning goal (message 86).
6. **Context management is parented awareness.** The agent knows roughly how many tokens it has used and how much context remains (the agent analogue of being time-aware), thinks about it periodically, and — parented — reads through its context under pressure, decides what matters, distils it, and continues on the distilled version; intelligent in-context forgetting over many cycles, while long-term memory is trained on everything and forgets only through lack of replay and diversity. Not taught until the system works (level 3 / lineage).
7. **Criterion for level 1 done:** you teach the model something and its behaviour changes in the next cycle, even in the closed loop. Then the guided self-learning is trained hard.

## Why this project exists, and how it scales — Rohin, message 89 (2026-09-15 ~02:00 UTC), "document this"

The project began from the bitter lesson: more compute increases intelligence. The developmental architecture is a machine for turning compute into a self-learning agent, and its scaling axis is the LINEAGE. Each generation does level 1 as one giant sleep on everything the previous model experienced (ordinary post-training on the lineage's own data), is parented at levels 2–3, and becomes a better parent for the next generation; level-0 data feeds level 1; the developmental steps become simpler with each generation because only the first breath is hard. A level-4 lineage can be aimed at a specialty — GPU code design, for example — and hill-climb there across generations.

The stance: once a semblance of richness exists, go all in on scaling. For level 1, start from the MOST scaling that is still useful and come down, not up from small; level 1 is finished when the level-2 loop works; then build one great, contamination-free level 1 and stop revisiting it — we are nowhere near the compute maximum. Level 2 is where the compute goes: a continuous, non-stop parenting swarm — many agents on every GPU thinking and communicating about what good parenting is — with Rohin at the head guiding; when something does not work, parent differently, never start from scratch. Level 3 is level 2 with more environments and a higher climb; level 4 is deployment or a specialised lineage.
