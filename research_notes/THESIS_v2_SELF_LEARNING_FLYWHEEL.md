**Provenance.** Rohin, 2026-09-12 ~01:00 UTC, a voice note plus a discussion with another assistant that he pasted in ("Found it… the thesis has been basically formalized… we're not fundamentally changing anything about our experiment, but we're realizing where the actual meat lies"). Rohin's words are quoted or closely paraphrased; Fable's additions are marked.

## 0. One screen

- **Mechanism statement.** Post-training changes behaviour (every chatbot) and creates memory (Physics of LLMs; any post-trained fact). Both are known. What is not known how to do is make an agent's *raw experience* into good post-training data. **The thing we teach is the behaviour of producing good post-training data about its own experience**, so that every input — an environment outcome, a parent, a person — flows through the model's thought into post-trainable, rememberable weight changes at sleep, and the next outputs can be post-trained too. "Everything it thinks turns into post-trainable data." The novelty moves from the learning algorithm to the data-generation disposition.
- **Diagnosis of the current bug.** The LoRA changes behaviour but does not remember; everything runs through thought. That is what you predict if parenting installed *reflection without articulation*: the model learned to reason and not to leave artifacts. The curriculum needs a second thing alongside self-evaluation — produce statements about what happened that are worth training on.
- **The flywheel.** Childhood (high plasticity): parenting builds the flywheel — the better the child learns how to learn, the more it learns from every reinforcement. Once built, childhood parenting is done; the post-childhood agent is deployed into a gym and keeps learning as an adult (plasticity still present, at a different rate; some frameworks stay, some keep breaking and re-forming). **The paper = parenting and birth create the childhood self-learning flywheel; deployment shows the adult flywheel still turning on the test gym.** Scope is finite: a flywheel good enough to learn better than a regular agent on our test gym, not everything in the world (a frontier lab's flywheel would run on ten layers of reflection and sub-agents; ours is scoped to the deadline).
- **Two hypotheses (the other assistant's split, adopted by Rohin).** *H1* (necessary condition, verified by us; TMEM and Allen-Zhu are the reason to expect it, not the evidence): skills taught through think-then-sleep are retained in the LoRA and expressed outside the context where they were taught. *H2* (the paper): an agent carrying those skills improves faster from self-generated experience on an unseen verifiable task than the same model without them, **and the gap depends on continued consolidation** — slope, not starting level. Clean test: a 2×2, parented vs not × sleep loop running vs frozen at deployment. H1 is the parenting effect with sleep frozen; H2 is the interaction. If parented+frozen matches parented+sleeping, the skills are good but they are not driving learning.
- **Not self-distillation.** "The model distills, but it doesn't originate." Every cycle's content traces to something outside — an environment outcome, a parent's correction, a person's response; the model contributes compression, not information. State this in the same paragraph as the mechanism or a reviewer supplies the collapse objection. Corollary: **a memory with no traceable source is confabulated** — a mechanical provenance gate, replacing the vocabulary leakage gate we lost when worlds became semantic.
- **The agent parent amortises a human teacher.** Rohin directs the agents and wrote the parenting corpus; there is one of him and finite hours, so the agents scale what he can teach. Humans would be the best parents (self-judgement, complex goal-setting, reaction to situations, learned values and patterns); the paper claims only what was run and the essay carries the rest.
- **Pointer principle (the other assistant's unification, adopted).** Semantic priors instead of built structure; emotional situations already in pretraining instead of an emotion system; descriptions of judgement and goal-setting already in pretraining instead of teaching them from scratch. The architecture works by routing latent pretrained structure into the decision loop rather than installing anything new — which is why a frozen base is right, not a limitation.
- **First person, procedural.** Pretraining holds "people evaluate their approach before committing" — third person, descriptive, about others. The LoRA needs "I evaluated my approach before committing, and here is what happened" — first person, procedural, with an outcome; only the second is a policy. Parenting is partly a register conversion, and the property is checkable in the corpus (person and tense).
- **What parents teach.** How to perceive strongly enough that things are remembered and connectable; how to think about ideas and extract them from the past; how to spend time thinking; how to plan, execute, meta-think, self-reflect; multilayer, long- and short-term planning; how to move between planning and execution and back.

## 1. The two hypotheses as experiments

| | sleep **frozen** at deployment | sleep **running** at deployment |
|---|---|---|
| **unparented** child | level and slope of a plain adapter on the unseen gym | does an untaught child improve from its own experience at all? |
| **parented** child | **H1**: the taught skills are expressed outside the teaching context (level) | **H2**: the parented agent's slope from self-generated experience |

H1 = parented − unparented with sleep frozen. H2 = the interaction: the parented advantage grows when consolidation continues.

**What exists (Fable).** Levels with the adapter frozen: 24 lives' final adapters probed ON vs OFF on the unseen 12-program panel (SEQ-029–035; the routine carries +0.02–0.04; the parented RP lives are in that range) and the text-memory baseline that pits the adapter against the child's own written brief (brief ≥ adapter on 15 of 24 lives) — H1's comparator between memory in weights and memory in text. **What has never been run:** any deployment on the unseen gym with the sleep loop running, hence no slopes, hence no H2. That is the paper's missing experiment.

**H2 design (Fable's proposal for Rohin; not launched).** Deploy the final adapter of each finished life into the unseen-program gym for 256 episodes: cell "running" = sleep every 32 (8 sleeps, the gate as in life); cell "frozen" = the same adapter and episodes with no sleep. Lives: parented RP 400/401/402 vs unparented R2 seeds (matched by node); R4 gate-only lives as a third row if budget allows. Measure score-vs-episode slope on the unseen programs (12 programs × ~21 visits), plus the register and provenance of what the running cells write. Cost ≈ 18 GPU-hours per run (4.2 min/episode) → 2×2 × 3 lives = 12 runs ≈ 9 GPU-days, about 1.5 days on 8 GPUs once the write pretests end. **Constraint:** seeding a run from an existing adapter is a "bootstrapped" launch under Codex's STOP (bootstrap_v1/v2/v3 had unverified provenance). Here the seed adapters are the finished lives' own final adapters with full receipts, but the letter of the STOP covers it — **Rohin decides whether to lift or scope the STOP for this**. R5 lives cannot be deployed on this gym (they were gated on it).

## 2. Provenance instead of vocabulary

Every memory item must trace to an external input: an episode outcome (program, action, measured result), a parent utterance (id), or a planted observation (the car test). Implementation (Fable, cheap): compile_sleep tags each corpus item with its source; the report counts items whose factual content (numbers, pass names, program names) does not appear in any source of that episode — the **confabulation rate** — and the gate drops them. The car-test cells satisfy this by construction (every rendering traces to a planted observation). SEQ-036's finding that the affect words in the lives' notes were bootstrap echo is a provenance failure already observed.

## 3. Register: what the lives actually wrote (SEQ-050)

The first check the formalisation asks for is cheap and was run on 2026-09-12 (`research_notes/analysis/corpus_register_audit.py`, crude word markers on the child's own NOTE text in every finished life's first and final sleep corpus; the harness wrapper "My thinking: … So I did: X → outcome" is not scored). Result: at the first sleep the notes are first-person in 25–60 % of items and prospective ("I expect", "I will") in 30–65 %, retrospective (outcome words) in 0–20 %, generic in 15–40 %; by the final sleep first person is under 10 % in 17 of 25 lives (under 5 % in 13), prospective 2–5 %, retrospective stays at or below 7 % in 23 of 25, while "specific" content (pass names, numbers) rises to 70–97 % — **the notes drift into bare recipes with neither self nor outcome** (the same_recipe / templated_notes rituals seen live). Exception: the parented RP 400 wrote outcome words in 37 % of its notes (RP 402: 10 %). So the untaught child's record is not third-person description; it is procedure lists without a first-person record of what happened — a different failure from the one predicted, with the same consequence (nothing trainable beyond the recipe). Parents can move it (RP 400).

## 4. What changes in the programme (Fable)

- **Nothing running changes.** The bridge (nine banks, taught variants t/u), the write pretests and the lives continue.
- **The bridge is H1 at the mechanism level**, and SEQ-049 is the first articulation result: the child restates the fact in 78 % of its perceptions when it must write the artifact itself and in 26 % when the harness writes it. Articulation is taught by requiring the artifact form; the lesson (t) asks for the relation restated.
- **Priority for the remaining six days:** (a) finish the bridge and t/u (running); (b) the H2 2×2 deployment — the paper's core experiment, ~1.5 GPU-days, blocked only on Rohin's STOP decision; (c) provenance tagging in compile_sleep with a confabulation-rate column; (d) register instrumentation in the parenting prompt (first person, outcome attached) and in the report.
- **Paper spine, restated:** (i) post-training changes behaviour and stores memory in the right form — adopted, measured with synthetic renderings (cell F); (ii) the child does not naturally produce that form — recipes without self or outcome (SEQ-050), perceptions that carry the fact only when the artifact form is required (SEQ-049); (iii) parenting installs the disposition to produce it — the bridge t/u and the RP register are the first tests; (iv) with the disposition, the agent learns faster from its own experience on an unseen gym when consolidation continues — H2, the 2×2, not yet run.
- **Writing rules:** the collapse defence sits in the mechanism paragraph; claim what was run; the agent parent amortises the human teacher; the pointer principle is stated once, explicitly; the person hierarchy (third → first person) is the same structure as the memory person hierarchy.
