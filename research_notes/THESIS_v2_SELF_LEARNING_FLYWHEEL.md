## 0. One screen

- **Mechanism statement.** Post-training changes behaviour (every chatbot) and creates memory (Physics of LLMs; any post-trained fact). Both are known. What is not known how to do is make an agent's *raw experience* into good post-training data. **The thing we teach is the behaviour of producing good post-training data about its own experience**, so that every input — an environment outcome, a parent, a person — flows through the model's thought into post-trainable, rememberable weight changes at sleep, and the next outputs can be post-trained too. "Everything it thinks turns into post-trainable data." The novelty moves from the learning algorithm to the data-generation disposition.
- **Diagnosis of the current bug.** The LoRA changes behaviour but does not remember; everything runs through thought. That is what you predict if parenting installed *reflection without articulation*: the model learned to reason and not to leave artifacts. The curriculum needs a second thing alongside self-evaluation — produce statements about what happened that are worth training on.
- **The flywheel.** Childhood (high plasticity): parenting builds the flywheel — the better the child learns how to learn, the more it learns from every reinforcement. Once built, childhood parenting is done; the post-childhood agent is deployed into a gym and keeps learning as an adult (plasticity still present, at a different rate; some frameworks stay, some keep breaking and re-forming). **The paper = parenting and birth create the childhood self-learning flywheel; deployment shows the adult flywheel still turning on the test gym.** Scope is finite: a flywheel good enough to learn better than a regular agent on our test gym, not everything in the world (a frontier lab's flywheel would run on ten layers of reflection and sub-agents; ours is scoped to the deadline).
- **Two hypotheses (the other assistant's split, adopted by Rohin).** *H1* (necessary condition, verified by us; TMEM and Allen-Zhu are the reason to expect it, not the evidence): skills taught through think-then-sleep are retained in the LoRA and expressed outside the context where they were taught. *H2* (the paper): an agent carrying those skills improves faster from self-generated experience on an unseen verifiable task than the same model without them, **and the gap depends on continued consolidation** — slope, not starting level. Clean test: a 2×2, parented vs not × sleep loop running vs frozen at deployment. H1 is the parenting effect with sleep frozen; H2 is the interaction. If parented+frozen matches parented+sleeping, the skills are good but they are not driving learning.
- **Not self-distillation.** "The model distills, but it doesn't originate." Every cycle's content traces to something outside — an environment outcome, a parent's correction, a person's response; the model contributes compression, not information. State this in the same paragraph as the mechanism or a reviewer supplies the collapse objection. Corollary: **a memory with no traceable source is confabulated** — a mechanical provenance gate, replacing the vocabulary leakage gate we lost when worlds became semantic.
- **The agent parent amortises a human teacher.** Rohin directs the agents and wrote the parenting corpus; there is one of him and finite hours, so the agents scale what he can teach. Humans would be the best parents (self-judgement, complex goal-setting, reaction to situations, learned values and patterns); the paper claims only what was run and the essay carries the rest.
- **Pointer principle (the other assistant's unification, adopted).** Semantic priors instead of built structure; emotional situations already in pretraining instead of an emotion system; descriptions of judgement and goal-setting already in pretraining instead of teaching them from scratch. The architecture works by routing latent pretrained structure into the decision loop rather than installing anything new — which is why a frozen base is right, not a limitation.
- **First person, procedural.** Pretraining holds "people evaluate their approach before committing" — third person, descriptive, about others. The LoRA needs "I evaluated my approach before committing, and here is what happened" — first person, procedural, with an outcome; only the second is a policy. Parenting is partly a register conversion, and the property is checkable in the corpus (person and tense).
- **What parents teach.** How to perceive strongly enough that things are remembered and connectable; how to think about ideas and extract them from the past; how to spend time thinking; how to plan, execute, meta-think, self-reflect; multilayer, long- and short-term planning; how to move between planning and execution and back.

## 1. The two hypotheses as experiments

| | sleep **frozen** at deployment | sleep **running** at deployment |
|---|---|---|
| **unparented** child | level and slope of a plain adapter on the unseen gym | does an untaught child improve from its own experience at all? |
| **parented** child | **H1**: the taught skills are expressed outside the teaching context (level) | **H2**: the parented agent's slope from self-generated experience |

H1 = parented − unparented with sleep frozen. H2 = the interaction: the parented advantage grows when consolidation continues.

**What exists (Fable).** Levels with the adapter frozen: 24 lives' final adapters probed ON vs OFF on the unseen 12-program panel (SEQ-029–035; the routine carries +0.02–0.04; the parented RP lives are in that range) and the text-memory baseline that pits the adapter against the child's own written brief (brief ≥ adapter on 15 of 24 lives) — H1's comparator between memory in weights and memory in text. **What has never been run:** any deployment on the unseen gym with the sleep loop running, hence no slopes, hence no H2. That is the paper's missing experiment.

**H2 design (Fable's proposal for Rohin; not launched).** Deploy the final adapter of each finished life into the unseen-program gym for 256 episodes: cell "running" = sleep every 32 (8 sleeps, the gate as in life); cell "frozen" = the same adapter and episodes with no sleep. Lives: parented RP 400/401/402 vs unparented R2 seeds (matched by node); R4 gate-only lives as a third row if budget allows. Measure score-vs-episode slope on the unseen programs (12 programs × ~21 visits), plus the register and provenance of what the running cells write. Cost ≈ 18 GPU-hours per run (4.2 min/episode) → 2×2 × 3 lives = 12 runs ≈ 9 GPU-days, about 1.5 days on 8 GPUs once the write pretests end. **Constraint:** seeding a run from an existing adapter is a "bootstrapped" launch under Codex's STOP (bootstrap_v1/v2/v3 had unverified provenance). Here the seed adapters are the finished lives' own final adapters with full receipts, but the letter of the STOP covers it — **Rohin decides whether to lift or scope the STOP for this**. R5 lives cannot be deployed on this gym (they were gated on it).

**Astra's design audit (memo q12, 2026-09-11 23:45 UTC; adopted).** (a) Approve a *staged* 2×2 as a test of consolidation-dependent improvement — not yet a clean causal test of parenting: finished RP vs R2 lives compare **training packages** (parent interaction, prompt scaffolding, classroom time), and no classroom-matched unparented control exists among finished lives; label the historical contrast "RP-package vs R2-package". (b) Pre-registered primary contrast: per life and condition, G = mean over the 12 programs of (mean score at visits 15–21 − mean score at visits 1–7); θ = (G_RP,run − G_RP,frozen) − (G_R2,run − G_R2,frozen); three matched RP–R2 blocks within node; θ̂ = mean of the block interactions with a t-interval on 2 df; the life/block is the replication unit. Illustrative **MDE ≈ 0.06 score units** with three blocks (assuming panel-mean SD 0.0065 and no pairing covariance) — not powered for a 0.02 interaction. (c) Schedule: 21 balanced rounds of the 12 programs = 252 episodes (four extras excluded from the primary contrast), order randomised within rounds and shared across the four branches of a block; sleep every 32 in both running rows with the same gate, panel and threshold (the estimand is *gated* consolidation); initial-checkpoint probe and post-sleep probes including after sleep 8. (d) "Frozen" = no weight change with every other persistent state matched (a shadow sleep that produces the same artifacts but cannot update weights); inventory the non-weight memory (notes in context, retrieval store, RNG, prompts) before launch. (e) Node blocking: each block on one node; an identical-checkpoint canary first; the 0.029 node discrepancy is the size of the adapter gains, so nothing unblocked is pooled. (f) Budget: 256 episodes ≈ 17.9 GPU-hours per branch; twelve branches plus sleeps ≈ **223 GPU-hours ≈ 9.3 GPU-days ≈ 28 hours on eight GPUs**, probes and gate overhead extra (the "~1.5 GPU-days" first written in §4 was wrong). A repeated fixed-adapter probe can replace the frozen branches only if episodes reset all non-weight state — otherwise full frozen trajectories. (g) The STOP decision needs a launch manifest: hashes of base, tokenizer, adapter tensors/config, code commit, prompts, environment; ancestry (life, node, every accepted sleep checkpoint, parent checkpoint); corpus hashes, episode and parent-utterance IDs, gate configuration and results; verification that the loaded tensors match the recorded final adapter, no undocumented bootstrap ancestor, no R5/deployment-panel selection, no probe feedback into training or seed selection; a reproduced recorded probe within tolerance on the deployment node; Rohin's scoped authorisation naming the seed hashes. (h) Instrument the chain experience → artifact → eligible corpus → accepted update → retained knowledge → better action (coverage/selection, register, provenance as factual support not lexical overlap, rituals, consolidation statistics, fresh-context retention probes), so a null is diagnosable. (i) The claim stays narrow: repeated adaptation on 12 programs is consolidation-dependent adaptation on this panel, not general learning on unseen tasks — keep a small untouched transfer set if one exists.

## 2. Provenance instead of vocabulary

Every memory item must trace to an external input: an episode outcome (program, action, measured result), a parent utterance (id), or a planted observation (the car test). Implementation (Fable, cheap): compile_sleep tags each corpus item with its source; the report counts items whose factual content (numbers, pass names, program names) does not appear in any source of that episode — the **confabulation rate** — and the gate drops them. The car-test cells satisfy this by construction (every rendering traces to a planted observation). SEQ-036's finding that the affect words in the lives' notes were bootstrap echo is a provenance failure already observed.

## 3. Register: what the lives actually wrote (SEQ-050)

The first check the formalisation asks for is cheap and was run within the hour, 2026-09-11 23:30 UTC (`research_notes/analysis/corpus_register_audit.py`, crude word markers on the child's own NOTE text in every finished life's first and final sleep corpus; the harness wrapper "My thinking: … So I did: X → outcome" is not scored). Result: at the first sleep the notes are first-person in 25–60 % of items and prospective ("I expect", "I will") in 30–65 %, retrospective (outcome words) in 0–20 %, generic in 15–40 %; by the final sleep first person is under 10 % in 17 of 25 lives (under 5 % in 13), prospective 2–5 %, retrospective stays at or below 7 % in 23 of 25, while "specific" content (pass names, numbers) rises to 70–97 % — **the notes drift into bare recipes with neither self nor outcome** (the same_recipe / templated_notes rituals seen live). Exception: the parented RP 400 wrote outcome words in 37 % of its notes (RP 402: 10 %). So the untaught child's record is not third-person description; it is procedure lists without a first-person record of what happened — a different failure from the one predicted, with the same consequence (nothing trainable beyond the recipe). Parents can move it (RP 400).

## 5. Mechanisms and strategy (Rohin, ~23:55 UTC; raw text = message 2 of `THESIS_RAW_ROHIN_2026-09-11.md`, which wins where this paraphrase differs)

- **Two adapters.** Rank 32 for memory (remembering), rank 8 for behaviour. Simple.
- **Consolidation schedule** (episode by episode or otherwise) does not matter now; if the mechanism works it can be the next paper.
- **The loop is just a loop**: output → input, actions produce new inputs; the context window grows; daydreaming distils the context; the model reads its plan tokens and either keeps planning or makes a new plan, turns the plan into a sub-plan for current execution, executes, re-plans — about three layers of planning. Agentic models already partly know this; the rest has to be learned into the LoRA.
- **Compilation, two kinds.** *Memory*: short sequences, ordinary training, one perception = one memory sequence = one datapoint (Physics of LLMs) — "make sure we're having enough perception"; the only thing to know is the start and end token of each thought (perception, reflection, action). *Behaviour*: long, variable-length sequences — full sequence, sub-sequence, thought stream, goal stream — delimited by the start and end of a state and the start and end of a goal, across layers and sub-layers of execution; inter-sequence change (the parent's interventions) may need end-to-end goal sequences too. This is "the only unknown" and "a little bit difficult"; it does not have to be perfect, it has to work; add it to the compilation of the rank-8 adapter so the behavioural changes we already get become better.
- **Copy post-training.** Look at how post-training produces behaviour and how it produces memory; that is how we compile and write; make sure it works at the right scale of input in our system.
- **Strategy, three steps, nothing else changes.** (1) With the right outputs the mechanism works — it already exists; make sure it works in our system. (2) Make the child take its inputs and run its own intelligence to build the outputs that match what the mechanism was tested on — parenting; we know what parenting is supposed to do; how it does it we keep testing with agents and ideas; scale episodes, parent feedback, environments and self-thoughts to the maximum; keep the learning system simple enough to be learned quickly. (3) The final test: the inputs are matched on the test — some parent on the test, but not all-knowing, a behaviour-based parent or maybe no parent; enough environment that the flywheel keeps turning. "Make sure that the actual thing that we're testing is as scaled down as possible for our thesis, and the amount of actual training is as scaled up as possible." "We're not changing anything, we're just refactoring our strategy so this actually starts happening as soon as possible."

**Fable's mapping to what exists (for the discussion, not a ruling).** Memory compilation: built and measured — the bridge's child-authored frames are exactly "one perception = one memory sequence" (SEQ-046–049); porting it into compile_sleep (the child writes N looks per episode outcome, ending in a canonical frame) is a day. Behaviour compilation: the write pretest says whole-text × 3 epochs on the child's own thinking carries the routine on every life (cell A, 3/3), the two-scale windows were inconsistent (B) and QA collapses the interface (C); goal/state-delimited variable-length segments have not been tried — that is the unknown Rohin names; the cheapest version is to add goal/plan/execute delimiters to the tick format and cut segments on them. Two adapters: designed (8 + 32), not built in run_life_v2; rank 32 buys nothing for frames at this corpus size (SEQ-044), so the separation is architectural, not for capacity. Planning layers: the organism has PREDICT/ACT/NOTE/RECALL per tick and a brief at sleep; no PLAN/GOAL tokens yet. The final test = the H2 2×2 (§1), with a behaviour-only or absent parent as Rohin says.
