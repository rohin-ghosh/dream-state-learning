## 1. The system is still only three things

1. **THINK:** the child's continuing stream of thought, judgment, planning,
   action, outcome, and reflection.
2. **DREAM:** ordinary management of the finite active context.
3. **SLEEP:** compile grounded parts of the child's experience, then write
   them into its LoRA.

Parenting supplies experiences and process guidance. It is not a fourth
cognitive mechanism. Canaries, provenance records, panels, and stopping rules
are laboratory hygiene, not parts of the mind.

## 2. What the evidence says now

- The writer can strongly change behaviour, but often writes a fixed routine
  or damages the action interface.
- Text memory is already a strong comparator. Across the first 19 completed
  lives, the own brief matched or beat the adapter in 12 and the adapter beat
  the brief in 7. At one matched mid-life cut the adapter beat the brief on the
  familiar panel but tied it on the unseen panel.
- Larger rank and greater adapter strength did not repair the planted-fact
  retrieval failure. The open variable is the representation and its exposure,
  not raw capacity alone.
- R3's persistent adapter advantage is real exploratory evidence but is
  dominated by one supplied six-pass routine and was selected on the same
  report programs. It is not a headline learning result.
- The current parented and unparented long lives differ in too many ways to
  isolate parenting.

Therefore the next clean test must separate three quantities:

1. what childhood leaves in the child at deployment;
2. what ordinary sleep adds during deployment; and
3. whether parenting changes how useful later sleep becomes.

## 3. Work that continues now under simple hygiene

### 3.1 Writer/mechanism scouts

Finish the already-running completion-frame exposure test and the fresh R5
life. Read all pass/fail labels as exploratory diagnostics, not claims.
`R5_B_seed701` is specifically an R5-loose pilot: its launch used the current
default gate tolerance of 0.02, and its format canary still draws four programs
from the old report panel. A later clean replication must state the tolerance
explicitly and use a train-split canary.

The mechanism is good enough to enter a childhood pilot only if it shows:

- **absorption/extractability:** the intended distinction moves at its matching
  cue, not merely in training loss;
- **specificity:** the same change is materially smaller at the wrong owner,
  wrong situation, or wrong life;
- **retention:** the change survives later unrelated writes;
- **interface preservation:** the child still emits valid actions and keeps
  working problems; and
- **no material harm:** a development-selection panel does not fall by more
  than one prospectively chosen noise width.

No single threshold from the current Cell-F implementation is treated as
ratified. Inspect the raw binding, spill, mass, retention, and canary numbers.
Before selecting a writer, register numerical owner, wrong-owner, wrong-life,
absolute answer-mass, spill, retention, interface, and development non-harm
criteria. Missing evidence is inconclusive, never a pass. The later sealed
report panel cannot participate in this selection.

The visible Cell-F and R5 scouts may choose a candidate and numerical rule;
they cannot validate it prospectively. That frozen candidate must then pass
once on an untouched development bank/life. If no untouched confirmation can
finish before the cutoff, the parenting canary is `NOT_RUN`.

### 3.2 Parenting scouts

Continue short lessons to learn which teaching modes produce child-authored
reasoning that survives the parent's absence. The parent may question,
explain, demonstrate, suggest, repeat, offer a recipe as an opinion, or stay
silent. The parent is strong and may improve its teaching through its own
context, notes, and tools; its weights do not learn.

That adaptive parent is appropriate during scouts. Before the clean canary,
freeze the parenting policy and initial context. Each independent childhood
root gets a fresh parent state: no notes, caches, examples, report information,
or tool state cross between roots. The parent may still adapt *within* one
childhood from that child's history.

Measure both immediate and delayed effects. Agreement or restatement alone is
not uptake. Useful uptake means the child later makes a better supported
decision, rejects bad advice when appropriate, or carries a bounded process
distinction into a different case.

## 4. The small canary guard

These rules are enough for the next causal canary; the full C11
mechanism-benchmark guard is not required. Before that canary launches, these
rules and the exact run bytes still pass the repository's ordinary
deliberation, ratification, independent-review, and GPU gate. Existing scouts
remain exploratory and continue under the simple rules now.

1. Start every childhood from a named, hashed clean checkpoint.
2. Never parent a checkpoint that has entered the deployment/compiler gym.
3. Fork deployment copies; never merge deployment experience back into the
   childhood trunk.
4. Give compared children byte-identical task schedules, token budgets, and
   write opportunities. A neutral/sham interaction matches the substantive
   parent's turn timing, tool opportunity, and tokenizer-counted output length
   with task-uninformative text.
5. Keep parent words out of positive training targets. The child may absorb,
   restate, reject, or transform them in its own subsequent thought.
6. Use three named namespaces: train/canary data; a development panel that may
   select the writer; and a sealed source-disjoint report panel.
7. Keep sealed-report identities, answers, outputs, and scores hidden from
   parents, writers, gates, curriculum decisions, researchers, and every
   operator who can affect execution until all four cells are terminal, their
   failure states are sealed, and all restart/repair decisions are closed. Use
   train-split canaries. A sealed-report item never selects an adapter.
8. Record the source childhood, source episode, writer recipe, adapter, and
   every fork/merge. Refuse unknown ancestry.
9. Preserve failed runs and negative outcomes; do not relabel or silently
   restart them as clean.
10. State the LoRA rank, learning rate, token dose, sleep cadence, gate
    tolerance, decoding seeds, and panel identities in the run receipt.

The guard has three canonical JSON layers. An immutable **prelaunch manifest**
binds exact code/container/dependencies, model/tokenizer, expanded
flags/defaults, prompts and policies, ordered task schedules, all three data
namespaces and source-group map, trainer/optimizer, seed derivation, root and
fork identities, input ledgers/corpora, and planned node/GPU blocks. Its hash is
the run hash. Each event then appends a hash-chained **transaction receipt**
with times, resolved seeds, inputs, outputs, and artifacts. A terminal
**closure manifest** binds the final chain, adapters, failures, and unblinding.
Phase-specific validators fail closed on missing required fields; future
outputs are never required at prelaunch and never change the run hash.

Every stochastic component—child, substantive parent, sham, retriever, sleep
synthesis, adapter initialization/dropout/data order, canary, gate, and report—
gets a stored seed derived from
`H(run_hash, root, stage, component, situation_id, turn, replicate)`. Treatment
labels, GPU number, batching, and launch order are excluded wherever common
randomness is intended.

Wake problem, parent turn, compile, train, gate, activation, and report are
transaction boundaries. Each commits one artifact hash, ledger offset, and RNG
receipt atomically. A crash discards temporary artifacts and replays from the
last committed boundary only if the receipt reproduces exact inputs and RNG;
otherwise the whole root is invalid. Forks have disjoint directories and an
adapter activates only after its commit marker.

## 5. The minimum clean parenting canary

Raise two children on the same reasoning-classroom schedule:

- **S:** matched neutral/sham interaction only.
- **P:** adaptive strong parent teaching process and meta-cognition.

P and S fork from one byte-identical hashed birth state. Their side labels,
GPUs, and launch order are randomized before childhood; pre-treatment RNG is
paired and all assignments are stored in the sealed prelaunch manifest.

Both children receive the same task exposure, child-token budget, sleep
opportunities, parent-input allowance, interaction timing, context occupancy,
tool opportunity, and amount of childhood replay. This estimates substantive
guidance over a matched neutral interaction, not parenting versus literal
absence. A no-parent agent may be added as a descriptive practical baseline.
Before the compiler gym, save and seal both childhood checkpoints and delete
the parent and nursery context from deployment inputs.

Fork each sealed child at compiler entry:

| childhood | deployment sleep OFF | deployment sleep ON |
|---|---|---|
| matched sham | **S0** | **S1** |
| parented | **P0** | **P1** |

At compiler entry each row has one immutable sealed adapter, `A_entry`. In cell
`0`, that adapter remains mounted and no deployment write is performed. In
cell `1`, each scheduled sleep reconstructs the adapter from the frozen base
using one frozen writer policy over a fixed-total-token mixture of that row's
childhood corpus and its new deployment experience. The manifest fixes the
childhood replay allowance, new-data allowance, optimizer steps, lag,
activation point, and failure treatment; P1 and S1 receive identical
allowances. This cumulative reconstruction is how the childhood adapter
survives the first deployment write. A failed write leaves `A_entry` or the
last committed adapter mounted. No writer-only summary, gate decision, or
artifact enters the child's wake context.

Before launch, reconstruct each row once with zero new deployment data. The
result must reproduce `A_entry` behaviour within a frozen tolerance and pass
the interface canary. If it does not, cumulative reconstruction is not a
qualified ON transition: either an incremental-from-`A_entry` writer was
separately qualified before launch, or the canary is `NOT_RUN`. Do not switch
write policies after seeing a causal-cell result.

All four causal cells use the same context policy and the same seeded
128-problem compiler schedule. No external active-text memory or childhood
brief is present during wake or the primary report; the primary `D` therefore
tests the writer without a text-memory interaction.

At entry and 128, a secondary read-only carrier diagnostic crosses the current
adapter OFF/ON × a text snapshot absent/present. Within each diagnostic the
snapshot is produced once, by a frozen candidate-blind retriever from that
cell's own eligible history, and reused byte-identically across adapter OFF/ON.
This measures text/weight interaction inside a realized life; it is not part of
`D`. A separate online text-memory arm with the same task and retrieval budget
is required before any claim that weights beat a strong memory agent. A waking
brief alone is not that baseline.

Evaluate at entry, 64, and 128 on a fresh source-disjoint panel that is never
used by a gate.

All four deployment forks run on one qualified node. GPU assignment and launch
order are randomized and recorded; frozen references are measured on that
node. Random seeds derive only from root, age, item, and replicate—not from P,
S, ON, OFF, GPU number, or launch order. A later multi-root confirmation
randomizes P/S side assignment within each root pair.

The report panel contains at least 24 programs from at least three source
groups and two fixed decoding replicates. Its source identity and near-duplicate
equivalence function are frozen; a machine report proves zero overlap with
train/canary/development sources. Escrowed panel bytes are hash-bound before
launch and become readable only after the terminal receipt lock.
The manifest also binds the source universe/version, eligibility and exclusion
rules, deterministic stratification/sampling seed, and access history. Panel
selection uses no model outputs, candidate-writer scores, or earlier
experimental outcomes.

The three readings are:

- **inherited childhood effect:** `P0 - S0`;
- **ordinary deployment learning under sham:** `S1 - S0`;
- **parenting changes later learning:**

  `D = (P1 - P0) - (S1 - S0)` over entry-adjusted lifetime AUC.

For condition `c` at age `a`, first average fixed decoding replicates per
program, then programs within each predeclared source group, then source groups
equally; an empty required group invalidates the root. Let
`gain(c,a)=score(c,a)-score(c,0)` and
`AUC(c)=[gain(c,0)+2*gain(c,64)+gain(c,128)]/4`, the normalized trapezoidal AUC
over 0..128. Unscoreable actions and terminal execution failures score 0;
infrastructure failures follow the transactional rule above. `D` is the sole
primary. For this noninferential canary the rule is hierarchical: first require
`AUC(P1)-AUC(P0) >= +0.015` and raw terminal
`score(P1,128)-score(P0,128) >= -0.015`; only then require `D >= +0.020`. A
relative D advantage can never advance a writer that harms P.
Source-bootstrap intervals are reported only as conditional descriptions of
this one observed childhood pair.

One matched childhood pair is a go/no-go canary and a developmental case
study, not a population claim. Programs, checkpoints, decoding repeats, forks,
and both children are nested measurements. One independently initialized
matched P/S childhood pair producing one `D` is the independent root unit that
ultimately supports a claim.

## 6. Decision after the canary

- If **P1-P0 fails either absolute condition**, stop: improve/freeze the writer
  before spending on long parenting, regardless of `D`.
- If **S1-S0 is null or harmful** while P1 passes its absolute conditions,
  report the asymmetry and apply the hierarchical `D` rule; never describe
  relative harm as ordinary learning. Here harmful means
  `AUC(S1)-AUC(S0) < 0`.
- If **S1-S0 is positive but D is null**, the system learns during deployment,
  but this parenting curriculum has not made it a better learner. Retain any
  P0 inheritance result as a separate finding.
- If **D passes the canary rule**, design a later fixed-N independent-root
  confirmation with the same frozen protocol. One root means one independently
  initialized paired P/S childhood producing four deployment forks. Exclude
  the canary root. Choose one N before unblinding, from prior variance or a
  blinded variance release; otherwise label the follow-up descriptive.
- If only the full P1 package wins, call it a developed-system demonstration.
  Do not call the difference causal parenting.

No efficacy stopping occurs inside the confirmatory fixed-N run.

## 7. Final practical test and C11

After the causal canary, a separate practical 2x2 may compare:

| deployed agent | no deployment parent | answer-aware parent present |
|---|---|---|
| regular/untaught | ordinary agent | temporary assisted baseline |
| taught learning adult | autonomous experiential learner | experiential learner receiving withheld-answer guidance |

An answer-aware parent can leak solutions through apparently process-only
advice. If this diagnostic is retained, it uses separate children and panels,
all influenced rows are marked oracle-assisted, and it supports no
target-blind-parenting claim. Parent-absent performance after assistance can
still distinguish durable uptake from temporary hint following.
