# 50 — RML paper design adjudication

**Date:** 2026-09-03

**Status:** durable scientific direction, refined by
`51_rml_paper_contract_after_primary_prior.md`. This is not architecture
ratification, implementation authority, GPU authority, or a scientific result.
Exact runtime changes still follow `AGENTS.md`.

## Bottom line

The project has a credible Paper-1 path, but the paper is a staged causal
memory study rather than a single organism demo.

The core paper question is:

> Can a frozen language agent turn its own public action--outcome history into
> target-blind, prospectively outcome-admitted connected semantic memory,
> traverse that memory under new goals, and thereby improve held-out
> multi-step action after the original history no longer fits in context?

LoRA is a conditional transport result over the identical admitted semantics.
One-cycle improvement of future evidence acquisition is a separate randomized
extension. Learned controller/agency adapters, rank cascades, base-model
promotion, and repeated open-ended improvement remain later papers.

## Adjudicated architecture

```text
public action -> ordinary public outcome -> append-only episodic ledger
                                            |
                                            v
                                  DREAM: broad proposal search
                           local relation/schema + roots + prediction
                                            |
                                  later ordinary outcome only
                                            |
                                            v
                              SLEEP: deterministic compiler
                   status -> admit -> dedup -> multi-view -> replay mix
                           |                              |
                           v                              v
                  explicit semantic snapshot       per-life LoRA
                           +--------------+---------------+
                                          |
                                one-hop bounded reads
                                          |
                                          v
                             THINK: goal-driven traversal
                        query -> follow/backtrack -> public action
                                          |
                                  new ordinary outcome
```

THINK and DREAM use one resolver operation family but different regimes.
THINK spends depth on a current goal and may act. DREAM spends breadth on
replay and may propose prospective local connections. Neither certifies its
own claims. SLEEP makes no model call and invents no semantics; it compiles
only chronologically supported public claims.

The explicit ledger/snapshot is epistemic authority. A per-life MEMORY LoRA
is a lossy one-hop transport, not the truth store, verifier, global graph, or
multi-hop reasoner. Multi-hop composition remains externalized in recurrent
tokens. Co-traversal, cited paths, and repeated useful chains provide future
training data for shortcut materialization and a distinct cross-life LOOP
adapter, but that agency-policy training is not Paper 1.

Working vocabulary remains:

- base checkpoint: general pretrained intelligence;
- context/workspace: current situation and focus;
- explicit ledger/snapshot: auditable episodic and semantic experience;
- per-life MEMORY adapter: compressed life-specific access surface;
- prompted THINK/DREAM loop: current agency teacher;
- later cross-life LOOP adapter: learned search/control policy.

## Benchmark decision

`rml_d0` remains an immutable deterministic regression micro-fixture. It is
useful for exact nine-action mechanics, J cuts, P atoms ceilings, target-byte
twins, canonical serialization, and cheap controller falsification. Its
targets are nested in one fixture; it supplies no independent paper sample.

The paper-bearing population is a new **Randomized Typed Causal Workshop
(RTCW)** generator. One private `pair_root` generates one H/twin world-life
pair and is the sole independent unit. A root independently samples causal
topology, shallow local programs, schema law, scoped exceptions, source
opportunities, target skeletons, public renderer, handles, and assignment
tapes. H and twin have identical target-visible bytes but different decisive
bindings and learnably different public life histories.

RTCW is finite and exact rather than a noisy continuous simulator. It retains
replayable public transitions, exhaustive adaptive no-life bounds, exact
legal-history headroom, J necessity, prospective P chronology, counterfactual
binding interventions, and later on-policy collection. Its programs are
shallow typed causal motifs rather than D0's small affine table. A
generator-aware program inducer remains a strong reference and may win.

The three confirmation packs must be non-isomorphic in transition mechanics,
not merely renamed skins:

1. fluid/thermal;
2. power/control;
3. material/biological.

Public nouns aid parsing, but every decisive binding is freshly randomized.
Nonce handles, target permutations, renderer variants, and shortcut probes
audit pretrained-prior and serialization leakage.

## Evidence ladder

### G1 — supplied knowledge can change action

The current exact proposal is
`chg_20260903_rml_g1_gold_action_fast_v1`. It uses one D0 J twin pair and one
D0 P twin pair as nested diagnostics. The repaired roster is:

- `GOLD_REC`: 4 trajectories;
- `NONE_REC`: 4;
- P `ATOMS_REC`: 2;
- `AUTH_REPLAY`: 2;
- `SHAM_REC`: 2;
- `CUT_REC`: 2;
- `TWIN_REC`: 2.

There are exactly 234 registered opportunities and at most 234 scientific
dispatches. Four reads close further reading but do not terminate the nine
action slots. The supplied store/index/query universe seals before target
selection. CUT must change behavior, not merely invalidate a citation. A new
offline trace reducer complements, rather than silently extends, the D0 world
oracle. Runtime, decoding, tokens, context, scratch, rows, wall time, GPU time,
and cost must be bounded before dispatch.

G1 is a controller/construct gate only. Its consensus is structurally valid
and awaits exact human ratification. A pass licenses RTCW/G2; it is not a
learning or paper result.

### G2 — experience produces useful connected text

Build `RTCW-MR1` only after G1 passes:

- one fluid/thermal pack;
- two independently generated DEV pair roots;
- one measured `2 L_native` cut;
- two J and two P targets per side;
- common public action/outcome lives;
- complete CPU validity/certification and non-author usability checks;
- no LoRA, scaling, or powered on-policy claim.

At fixed writer opportunities and a cached shared proposal pool, compare:

- witnessed public atoms;
- raw DREAM proposals;
- blind self-check selection;
- later-public-outcome admission;
- matched hierarchical reflection;
- full recurrent admitted `E-TEXT`;
- native raw RAG;
- native linked/A-MEM-like memory;
- exact witnessed graph;
- generator-aware program induction;
- target-independent gold text;
- binding/twin/cut/sham interventions.

G2 is credited only if public-outcome admission improves the
precision--coverage--action frontier; `E-TEXT` recovers at least 70% of the
gold-over-none gain; neither DEV root is rescued by averaging; strong native
RAG/reflection/linked controls are not handicapped; and authentic content
matters under interventions. The program reference is allowed to win.

### G3 — identical semantics survive in weights

Freeze one admitted semantic set `M` before targets. Derive both substrates
only from `M`:

- indexed `E-TEXT`;
- recognition-assisted `E-LORA-R`;
- generative `E-LORA-G` sentinel;
- candidate-only clean base;
- raw-event LoRA;
- outcome-trained direct-QA/TMEM-like LoRA;
- wrong-life, binding-shuffle, and whole-twin text/adapter swaps.

The text and LoRA channels return identical canonical row semantics to the
same clean-base resolver. Candidate rosters are target-independent and their
construction cost is charged. The adapter is unmounted for composition. G3
earns only recognition-assisted per-life parametric transport, never LoRA
discovery, reasoning, verification, retrieval-free recall, or superiority to
text.

### G4 — confirmatory finite post-context development

After a disjoint six-pair locked calibration, confirmation uses at least 48
fresh pair roots, 16 per fixed pack. A predeclared nuisance-only blinded rule
may increase to 72 or 96; no effect, arm, threshold, or claim may change. If
96 cannot provide adequate power, narrow or stop.

Each root contains descriptive `0.5 L_native` and confirmatory `2x`, `4x`,
and `8x` cuts. N/O/J/P/X targets, sides, cuts, writer samples, and adapter
seeds are nested within the pair root. Technical seeds are averaged before
inference.

The primary efficacy comparison is frozen now:

```text
E-TEXT minus native linked/A-MEM-like memory
on pair-level equal-weight N/O/J/P/X post-native action AUC.
```

The full native panel still includes NONE, honest context/truncation, strong
iterative raw RAG, hierarchical reflection, witnessed graph, program
induction, Voyager-like skill library, direct-QA LoRA, raw LoRA, and the
proposed text/LoRA systems. Native interfaces are primary for system
comparisons; the common one-row interface is a mechanism diagnostic and the
identical-corpus transport channel.

Paper-level finite development requires a positive `4x -> 8x` lower bound,
not merely a positive `2x -> 8x` total; old retention, new acquisition, J/P
action, causal coverage, and resource guardrails must also pass.

### G5 — separate randomized one-cycle flywheel

G5 uses new pair roots and one frozen `8 L_native` boundary. Within each
world-side block, randomize authentic, null, binding-shuffled, and twin memory
before a real costly information-gathering phase. Preseal the later target;
allow one subsequent DREAM/SLEEP cycle; retain every branch under
intention-to-treat.

Start at 72 independent pair roots and allow a nuisance-only increase to 96.
Authentic memory must beat each control, not a pooled easy comparator, on
target-relevant information gain and later sealed action. Changes in action
choice and supported memory quality are additional ordered links. This shows
one causal feedback cycle, not repeated open-ended self-improvement or natural
mediation.

## Claims and sample-size rulings

The previous 24-pair confirmation and `.02` plateau criterion are retired.
Twenty-four roots may reveal a large effect but cannot establish a precise
plateau. The lean core paper needs 48--96 independent roots; calls, targets,
sides, cuts, packs, skins, and adapter seeds cannot inflate `n`.

The core paper omits `saturation`. Report response surfaces and resource
frontiers. If a named crossover claim becomes important after G4 passes, run a
separate equivalence extension: name one fixed-capacity baseline, justify a
practical margin such as `.05`, retain oracle headroom, begin only with a
blinded power audit supporting at least 96 roots, and permit 144/192 if
predeclared. A `.02` margin likely needs roughly 192--256 or more roots.

Likewise, use `compiled` or `consolidated`, not `compressed`, until a separate
rate--distortion test shows that retained representation grows more slowly
than the raw ledger while action, causal coverage, and false-memory guardrails
remain within registered bounds. File size or LoRA rank alone is not semantic
compression.

## Conditional paper claims

If G2 plus confirmatory A1/B1 pass:

> In the prespecified RTCW population, a frozen language-model controller
> converted its executed public action--outcome history into target-blind,
> prospectively outcome-admitted connected semantic memory whose recurrent
> use improved held-out post-native multi-step action.

If same-corpus T1/T2 also pass:

> The identical admitted semantics retained action value when transported
> into isolated per-life LoRA weights through a recognition-assisted read
> channel.

If D1 passes:

> Across the tested `2x--8x` native-context range, the system continued to
> acquire new structure, retain old structure, and combine old and new
> structure for action.

If the separate randomized G5 trial passes:

> Authentic lifetime memory caused better evidence acquisition, subsequent
> supported memory, and later sealed action over one reconsolidation cycle.

No result here licenses general intelligence, open-ended continual learning,
first dreaming, first parametric agent memory, retrieval-free weight memory,
or learned agency.

## Primary-prior boundary (2026-09-03 audit)

A same-day primary-source audit materially narrows the novelty boundary:

- TMEM already performs within-rollout LoRA memory updates from extracted
  supervision and outcome-trains the extraction policy.
- Auto-Dreamer already learns offline, provenance-linked, multi-source memory
  consolidation from downstream agent reward across ScienceWorld, ALFWorld,
  and WebArena.
- PEAM already consolidates embodied success and failure--correction
  trajectories into isolated MoE-LoRA skill modules.
- A-Mem already builds and evolves linked atomic-note memory for multi-hop
  retrieval.
- EVAF already isolates goal-conditioned parametric "memory depth" after
  context unload and separates selection from LoRA actuation strength.
- Parametric Memory Law already measures LoRA rank--length capacity, exact
  recall dynamics, and a small seen-versus-unseen rule-learning tradeoff.

Therefore the paper's contribution cannot be any of those ingredients alone.
Its novelty hypothesis is the **identified causal pathway** from public
action--outcome experience through prospective support and connected causal
semantics to post-context held-out action, plus the decomposition of semantic
induction, access/traversal, and same-corpus weight transport. Auto-Dreamer-
style consolidation, native linked/A-Mem-like retrieval, outcome-trained
direct-QA/TMEM-like LoRA, and (for any agency claim) PEAM-like procedural
adaptation are serious comparator obligations. A selective EVAF-like LoRA arm
is additionally required for post-unload behavioral claims; rank curves alone
cannot carry novelty against Parametric Memory Law.

The exact source audit and claim deletions are recorded in
`research_loop/advisory/20260903_primary_prior_delta.md`.

## Scientific decision tree

1. G1 fails: reject the exact recurrent action interface; do not spend on
   DREAM or LoRA.
2. G1 passes, RTCW validity fails: repair or abandon the benchmark before
   model calls.
3. RTCW gold works, G2 fails: the proposed writer/admission/compiler fails;
   retain the benchmark and negative attribution.
4. Native linked/graph/program wins: no learned-memory moat; publish only if
   the instrument/phase diagram is independently valuable.
5. G2 works, G3 fails: external consolidation paper; remove the weights clause.
6. G3 works, G4 lacks last-interval growth: selected-cut transport, not
   development.
7. G4 passes without G5: credible fixed-deck causal experiential-memory paper.
8. G5 passes: add one-cycle on-policy feedback evidence.
9. Separate equivalence or rate--distortion extensions fail: omit saturation
   or compression language without weakening the core result.
