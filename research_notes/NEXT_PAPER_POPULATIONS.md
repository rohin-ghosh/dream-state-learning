## Surrounding philosophy (Rohin)
Emotions and low sentience are compressed intelligence that is favourable, to a degree, for agency. At collaborative societal scale, sheer intelligence-maxxing is not always best; collaboration-maxxing may require more non-divergent, lower-sentience actors (mimicking, collaborative intelligence) alongside self-defining actors who lead the search — diversity is a requirement.

## Formal backing (citable; verify entries before use)
- Lazer, D. & Friedman, A. (2007). The Network Structure of Exploration and Exploitation. Administrative Science Quarterly 52(4). Less efficient communication networks outperform highly connected ones on complex problems: fast diffusion of the current best answer causes premature convergence; slower diffusion preserves diversity. Design against this from the start.
- Hong, L. & Page, S. E. (2004). Groups of diverse problem solvers can outperform groups of high-ability problem solvers. PNAS 101(46).
- Lehman, J. & Stanley, K. O. (2011). Abandoning objectives: evolution through the search for novelty alone. Evolutionary Computation 19(2). Quality-diversity: Pugh, Soros & Stanley (2016); MAP-Elites: Mouret & Clune (2015), arXiv 1504.04909.

## Sharpening (from the thread; adopt)
Sentience and divergence come apart. What matters for collective search is behavioural diversity; self-definition is one source, but different experience histories, priors and temperatures produce it too — a population of our agents gets diversity for free because each lives a different life. Practical reduction: "how sentient is this agent" is a point in (temperature, plasticity) space — low/low = convergent exploiter that consolidates; high/high = divergent explorer that keeps changing. The population is a distribution over those two knobs, and the classroom question becomes empirical: which distribution maximizes collective progress.

## Seeds to design around
1. Dreaming over the union of logs: text corpora merge in a way weights do not; a population can sleep on the pooled ledger (with per-source provenance kept for hygiene even if attribution is not the point).
2. Vocabulary divergence: do agents with different histories develop dialects divergent enough that memory transfer between them degrades? Measure with the dialect/ritual instruments already built.
3. Slow diffusion: Lazer–Friedman says sharing the best current answer fast is harmful; the merge schedule and what is merged (thoughts vs answers) are design variables.
4. Learning from people: human parents alongside agentic parents (relay path already exists in the parent ledger).
5. Roles: a few self-defining explorers to lead search, many collaborative exploiters to consolidate; the parental society (frozen-weight agentic parents with shared ledgers) as the coordination layer; recursion (children becoming parents) further out.
