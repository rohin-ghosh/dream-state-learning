\begin{abstract}
Can developmental teaching improve an agent's production of useful training
material and its later learning after the teacher is removed? We study this
proposed causal chain using a frozen instruction-tuned model, experience-derived
text, and LoRA consolidation. Across three optimizer seeds, oracle useful-board
material yields 2, 3, and 5 first-action solves on a reused 16-board development
panel, versus 0, 1, and 0 for wrong-board material and zero for every adapter-OFF
condition. This small directional effect does not establish generalization or
developmental learning. Child-material formation encounters both an eligibility
shortage and, under a distinct raw-event recipe, degraded parent-free performance.
Memory prefix masking and fixed-prefix OFF-distribution preservation reduce
spill while suppressing acquisition; neither yields a passing selective-memory
result. The completed preservation pair fails both fixed selectivity and
abstention gates. A separate native semantic carrier passes its declared
action-surface check, not a writing or retention test. Together these observations
separate interface validity, useful learning material, and selective consolidation.
They motivate the proposed H1/H2 developmental questions without demonstrating
parental internalization, an achieved self-learning flywheel, joint retention,
or a completed campaign.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
An agent may act repeatedly without improving how it learns from acting. The
project asks whether developmental teaching can change that: can a child learn
to notice useful evidence, record it in a trainable form, and subsequently benefit
more from its own externally grounded experience? The intended loop is
experience, interpretation, learning material, consolidation, and renewed
experience. Every cycle must trace new information to an environment outcome,
parental intervention, or other external observation. The model contributes
selection and interpretation, not evidence merely by generating a sentence.
Grounding is necessary; it does not by itself establish correctness or prevent
feedback amplification. \ev{C01}

This is a synthesis and developmental research question, not a claim to have
invented post-training, parametric memory, or sleep. The agent parent is intended
to amortize human-designed teaching, not to stand in for an experiment with human
teachers. A frozen base supplies prior capabilities; the proposed treatment
changes how those capabilities produce and use experience-derived learning
material. Perceptions and inferences should remain distinguishable in the record.
The raw rulings explicitly leave alternative adapter partitions as testable ideas,
not prerequisites or demonstrated mechanisms. \ev{C01}

\paragraph{Proposed H1.} Does developmental teaching yield adapter-mediated
learning-process competence expressed on task-disjoint situations after reload,
with teacher access and temporary teaching context removed? A useful starting
level under frozen deployment is an H1-type effect, not evidence of an improving
learning loop. Narrow routine carriage is insufficient for the full question.

\paragraph{Proposed H2.} Does that development increase improvement from new,
self-generated experience on an unseen verifiable task, and does the advantage
depend on continued consolidation? This is a learning-change interaction, not a
comparison of terminal scores alone. H1 and H2 remain unestablished here, even
where earlier project prose described a necessary condition as verified. \ev{C01,C02}

The canonical manuscript characterizes a per-life writer, its failures, storage
versus use, and observational parenting. We retain that empirical caution while
organizing this companion around three links: a qualified writer given useful
material; parent-induced, parent-absent production of useful material; and an
integrated adult learning loop. H1/H2 remain the core research questions; writer
characterization supports their causal prerequisites rather than replacing the
developmental research program. The present evidence blocks qualification at
oracle usability and selectivity. This justifies reporting the diagnostic record,
not stopping feasible parenting diagnosis or declaring the campaign complete.
\ev{C00,C03,C04,C08}

\section{System and methods}
\subsection{Parameter, text, and visibility contracts}
The historical online system uses frozen Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and a rank-8 LoRA.
Its ordinary sleep path compiles successful action/outcome records, child
generations, and harness-rendered records, merges/deduplicates earlier text, and
refits a new adapter from the frozen base. In the historical compiler lives this
occurs every 32 episodes, with paired probes every 64 episodes. Replaying a
cumulative corpus is not sequential retention without rehearsal. The usual
whole-text writer uses hard-token causal-language-model training, not
teacher-distribution distillation. Actual rank, objective, masks, seed, and source
must be read from each run rather than inferred from a default or filename.
The legacy offline A recipe, for example, used rank 32 and unseeded fits; it is
not the rank-8 online recipe. \ev{C02,C09,C12}

``Child-authored'' does not imply that every training token is verbatim child
text: older compilers add templates and canonical endpoints. For the active P0
diagnostic, the intended downstream corpus instead preserves admitted child
bodies with source joins. Teacher copying, orphan records, and unsourced claims
must be identified rather than reclassified as observations. A statement of
thought is an observable behavioral artifact, not access to an internal causal
reasoning process. Parent-visible development must be separated from evaluation;
parents remain blind to sealed scores. \ev{C08,C09}

\subsection{Evidence units and qualification}
We separate historical life-level summaries, within-fit owner contrasts, and
developmental prompt diagnostics. No pooled success rate combines these units.
G1 requires persistent relevant memory; G2 nontrivial behavioral transfer; G3
repeated acquisition, retention, interference, and reload/resume evidence; G4
usefulness of extraction against suitable controls; P1 parent-absent learning
competence; G5 an integrated developmental pilot; and G6 a bounded campaign with
justified follow-ups and replications. G9 is the separate memory-dose selectivity
criterion below, not shorthand for completing G1--G6. \ev{C03,C11}

\subsection{A1/A2 selectivity diagnostic}
A1 is the \texttt{F\_r16k16} family, source-bank seed 1, optimizer seeds 2 and 3.
A2 is \texttt{CF\_r16\_b}, source-bank seed 0, optimizer seeds 0, 1, and 2.
Each configuration supplies three evaluated banks. Each bank has 64 owners,
16 each at doses 0, 1, 4, and 16, and 1,313 evaluation cues. The fitted recipe
is rank 8, three epochs, learning rate $10^{-4}$, and lambda 1. \ev{C03}

Let $\Delta\ell$ denote an adapter-on minus adapter-off target/alternative
log-odds change under the frozen scorer. The binding contrast is
\[
 I_{d,\mathrm{frame}}=\frac{1}{16}\sum_{i=1}^{16}
 (\Delta\ell_{i,\mathrm{frame}}-\Delta\ell_{i,\mathrm{similar}}).
\]
Alternatives follow the saved scorer rule (stored alternative or strongest under
OFF). A paired-owner percentile bootstrap uses 2,000 resamples and seed 0 within
each bank/fit. Spill is the unweighted mean of three subgroup means of absolute
candidate-normalized probability changes: 48 exposed similar-frame, 16 unexposed
frame, and 48 exposed bicycle-frame owners. G9 requires an interval lower bound
strictly above zero \emph{and} spill at most 0.03. Similar-frame is an unseen
look-alike control, not an exposed-partner swapped-ID test. Candidate-normalized
quantities must not be mistaken for unconditional probability mass. \ev{C03}

Banks share family/source structure; bootstrap intervals do not represent
optimizer-seed uncertainty. A1 and A2 differ in source data and cannot identify
a material-matched causal effect. Missing reference fits are not imputed. These
are synthetic diagnostics, not eligible clean developmental ancestry.

\section{Related work: a bounded verified subset}
SEAL learns self-edits evaluated through adaptation utility, rather than merely
replaying an agent's unselected output \cite{zweiger2025seal}. Our inspected path
shares generated-text adaptation, not SEAL's learned outer self-edit policy.
TMEM uses experience-derived instruction/answer supervision for within-episode
parametric memory and learns extraction through an outer RL objective
\cite{ren2026tmem}. Its rank-6, fixed-factor memory recipe and temporal scope differ
from the per-life writer here; our local recipe adaptation is not a replication.
MemSkill separates remembered experiential content from reusable operations that
construct and revise memory \cite{zhang2026memskill}. It motivates questions
about what to record and revise, but does not establish internalization into this
child's LoRA. \ev{C10}

These connections support testing the utility of learning material and the
behavior that creates it. They establish neither efficacy in this deployment
nor priority for the proposed developmental claim. We cite only the intersection
of existing bibliography entries and the supplied independently verified
primary-source register. OEL and SDFT identities were also verified there, but
are not added to the canonical bibliography by this sidecar. \textbf{Citation

\section{Results and negative findings}
\subsection{Historical context is not a developmental success}
Nine ungated 1,024-episode compiler lives have a mean life-level paired gain of
$+0.019$ (SE 0.011). Across the full life, 4/9 have at least one harmful pair
below $-0.03$ (seeds 2, 3, 5, 6); at the final 1,024-episode probe, 3/9 have a
harmful pair (seeds 2, 3, 5). These are distinct ever-harm and final-probe
estimands, not conflicting counts. Seed 2's final difference rounds to $-0.0300$
but is harmful under the source's unrounded rule. Scores are fractional
instruction-count reductions on identifier-held-out programs, not a general
learning score. A later disjoint re-probe reports final-adapter gains of $-0.017$
for ungated lives ($n=9$), $+0.003$ for gated lives ($n=6$), and $-0.010$ for gate
plus parent ($n=7$). The broader re-probe includes 48 adapter cells on 12 programs;
these cells are not 48 independent learners. Different panels and packages cannot
be pooled into an H1 estimate. \ev{C02}

A distinct matched disjoint adapter/text factorial has an R2 subset of nine
lives: frozen base 0.2528, adapter 0.2382, text 0.2570, and adapter plus text 0.2424;
the reported interaction is $+0.00008$ from unrounded inputs. This is a static
carrier comparison, not the parenting-by-adult-consolidation interaction of H2.
Age-matched brief-minus-legacy-A differences average $+0.00464$ on the report
panel and $-0.00405$ on the disjoint panel; neither carrier is uniformly superior.
All 31 available historical life-tail articulation summaries are zero, but some
tails are partial and earlier nonzero rows exist. Retrospective grounding can
use later revisits, and parent text persists in supposed withdrawal windows:
these observations do not provide a clean negative test of P1. \ev{C02,C09}

\section{Causal protocol for integration, not a launch instruction}
\paragraph{Separate the three links.} First establish oracle material correctness,
interface usability, selective fresh-process write effects, and OLD/NEW retention
under repeated learning. Then compare developmental teaching with a genuinely
matched active sham: same base, tasks, schedules, opportunity, substrate and
budgets, with realized token differences exposed. Evaluate fresh material
generation after teacher removal, not merely lesson-time compliance. Compare
the utility of sourced child material with no-update and appropriate
raw/replayed/shuffled controls under declared budgets. Finally test adult
adaptation. P0's one schedule pair and unequal teacher dose cannot complete these
links or establish a general causal parenting effect. \ev{C08,C11}

\paragraph{Parent-free and task-disjoint means more than a fresh prompt.}
Remove teacher access, lesson text, persistent teaching prefixes, retrieval
residue, and live feedback channels before evaluation; preserve the intended
learned adapter. Inventory every remaining text store, state, generator, and RNG.
Development task families must not reveal adult test solutions; distinguish
task-disjoint transfer from identifier-only splits. Use identical adult instances
and decoding schedules within matched comparisons. Historical RP versus R2
contrasts compare training packages, not isolated parenting. P0's named probe
panel is development evidence until a disjointness audit and untouched successor
panel exist. Unknown clean status stays unknown. \ev{C02,C08,C11}

\paragraph{H2 interaction.} For matched learner block $b$, developmental treatment
$p\in\{P,U\}$, and adult consolidation $s\in\{R,F\}$, let $G_{b,p,s}$ be a
predeclared change from early to late adult performance (or a registered slope).
The contrast of interest is
\[
 \theta=\mathbb{E}_b[(G_{b,P,R}-G_{b,P,F})-(G_{b,U,R}-G_{b,U,F})].
\]
Running permits consolidation; frozen/shadow performs matched non-weight work
without changing parameters. Initial performance is reported separately. The
same starting checkpoint branches into R/F, with other persistent state matched.
The life/block is the replication unit, not tasks, checkpoints, tokens, or sleep
cycles. Earlier notes propose balanced early/late windows, but count, metric,
power, intervals, and stop rules must be bound to the actual future campaign
before outcome inspection. No effect estimate or power guarantee is supplied
here. \ev{C11}

The integrated loop must show that adult updates reach the policy that generates
or selects later learning material, and that subsequent experience actually uses
that policy. A fixed external extractor tests a smaller subsystem. Connectivity,
nonzero gradients, and a higher terminal score alone do not show improving
learning; retention, interference, learning change, and cost must be measured.
