## Auditor's brief (v3)

The five claims most at risk after the fix pass, and how a second model should attack each. Items 1 and 4 are unchanged in substance from v2; 2, 3 and 5 are updated to what v3 now says.

1. **Carrier attribution remains post-hoc.** A matched disjoint-panel 2x2 now exists for nine coherent R2 lives: `A-F=-.0146`, `T-F=+.0041`, `AT-F=-.0104`, with nearly zero mean factorial interaction. It is evidence about these historical carriers, not proof that text generally beats weights: the panel is researcher-used, the waking brief is static and handoff-sized, and the comparison was not prospectively registered. Attack: run a prospective, candidate-blind evolving textual-memory arm and a fixed-routine-in-context control under the final task's exact action budget, seeds, and information access.
2. **"In gated lives the common adapter-on score 0.4878 equals the birth recipe; ungated adapter-on scores are untabulated" (abstract, §4.3).** v3 no longer claims the ungated gain is the birth recipe, but the indirect evidence it gives (best ungated life means near 0.52; R2 fully locked in 39–61% of checkpoints vs 61–73% gated) points the other way for the best lives.
