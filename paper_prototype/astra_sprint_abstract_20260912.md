## Abstract

Can developmental teaching improve an agent's production of useful training material and its later learning after the teacher is removed? We study this proposed causal chain, not an achieved self-learning flywheel. The system combines a frozen instruction-tuned model, experience-derived text, and LoRA consolidation. Across three optimizer seeds, oracle useful-board material yields 2, 3, and 5 first-action solves on a reused 16-board development panel, versus 0, 1, and 0 for wrong-board material and zero for every adapter-OFF condition. This is a small directional material effect, not established generalization or developmental learning. A same-input memory prefix mask reduces spill but erases acquisition; both original and masked fits fail the fixed selectivity and abstention criteria. The original P0 lesson/sham formation supplies no eligible faithful records and skips writing. A separate source-linked raw-wake fork trains 32 examples per arm without teacher context at sleep or probe, but both adapters solve zero boards and lower native partial scores relative to OFF. Unequal teacher and target-token doses prevent an isolated semantic-parenting interpretation. A subsequent preservation attempt stops at a numerical cache check before training; this is infrastructure evidence, not a scientific negative. A teacher-present, no-write process/sham probe ties at one solve per 16 training questions; format improves from four to seven without additional valid solutions. Without a no-teacher anchor, this is only a relative format difference, not internalization. Preservation outcomes remain pending. These diagnostics localize distinct links in proposed H1/H2 without demonstrating an achieved flywheel, joint retention, or a completed developmental campaign.
