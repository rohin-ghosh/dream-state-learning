## Abstract

Can developmental teaching improve an agent's production of useful training material and its later learning after the teacher is removed? We study this proposed causal chain using a frozen instruction-tuned model, experience-derived text, and LoRA consolidation. Across three optimizer seeds, oracle useful-board material yields 2, 3, and 5 first-action solves on a reused 16-board development panel, versus 0, 1, and 0 for wrong-board material and zero for every adapter-OFF condition. This small directional effect does not establish generalization or developmental learning. Child-material formation encounters both an eligibility shortage and, under a distinct raw-event recipe, degraded parent-free performance. Memory prefix masking and fixed-prefix OFF-distribution preservation reduce spill while suppressing acquisition; neither yields a passing selective-memory result. The completed preservation pair fails both fixed selectivity and abstention gates. A separate native semantic carrier passes its declared action-surface check, not a writing or retention test. Together these observations separate interface validity, useful learning material, and selective consolidation. They motivate the proposed H1/H2 developmental questions without demonstrating parental internalization, an achieved self-learning flywheel, joint retention, or a completed campaign.
