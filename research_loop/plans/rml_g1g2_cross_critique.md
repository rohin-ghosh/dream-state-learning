# Adversarial cross-critique: RML G1/G2 interpretations A and B

**Reviewed artifacts:** the minimalist and faithful G1/G2 interpretations,
the end-to-end traceability audit, the RML pilot, and the repository operating
contract.

**Status:** adjudication input only. This artifact selects a recommended
design but does not ratify it, edit any existing plan/code, authorize a model or
GPU call, open locked data, or make a scientific claim. Exact bytes still
require consensus, human ratification, scoped implementation, CPU/pre-GPU
evidence, and fresh independent review.

## Executive decision

Neither interpretation should be adopted verbatim.

- A has the better staged economy, explicit X-sidecar boundary, experiential
  binding falsifiers, and locked-confirmation idea. Its “authentic wake trace”
  is scientifically misnamed: the model's proposed action is ignored and a
  scripted action produces the outcome. That tests a traversal annotation on a
  fixed deck, not wake action -> outcome experience. Its eight-pair,
  many-contrast confirmation is materially underpowered.
- B preserves the intended wake action -> public outcome semantics and adds
  the important atoms-versus-connected G1 factor and wake/cross trace 2x2. It
  overbuilds the first DEV run, leaves X authority implicit, omits several
  whole-pipeline binding controls, and stops at descriptive calibration rather
  than specifying a publishable confirmation.

Select a **hybrid with B's executed frozen collector and content factorial,
A's staged execution and binding attacks, and a larger locked confirmation**.

For fast DEV, use four pairs, J/P only unless a separately ratified X sidecar
already exists, no post-native requirement, a four-cell G1 screen, and a
fail-fast incomplete G2 sequence. DEV is an engineering/mechanism gate and has
no inferential claim.

For a narrow publishable G1/G2 result, use one strictly post-native cut of one
fluid/thermal pack, 16 new locked twin pairs, eight targets per retained
stratum per twin, the full trace x offline-formation matrix plus compiler,
replay, no-write, specialized, and binding controls, and simultaneous exact
world-pair inference. The maximum claim is a one-pack, one-checkpoint,
fixed-deck text-memory mechanism result. It is not a lifetime curve, LoRA
result, multi-pack benchmark, or flywheel.

## 1. Agreements worth preserving

Both interpretations correctly agree that:

1. D0 is a CPU instrument prerequisite and supplies no model or paper evidence.
2. G1 must establish generic recurrent use of local gold memory before learned
   G2 content is interpretable.
3. One reader return is one local atom or `NOT_FOUND`; the model receives no
   graph neighborhood, answer-shaped query, proof path, rank, alternative,
   hidden program, scorer output, or correction.
4. Direct-record closure must be zero, nonadaptive/open reads must remain low,
   and correct action alone does not earn constructive credit.
5. Complete-cut masking and counterfactual-twin substitution are required for
   causal memory-use credit.
6. Wake traces are selection-only. Co-retrieval, frequency, critic approval,
   wording repetition, and compiler views never become factual evidence.
7. DREAM proposals commit before distinct later ordinary outcomes; deterministic
   admission may reject but may not invent, repair, rank, or broaden them.
8. Text precedes LoRA. Failure of learned text blocks parametric transport.
9. Compiler-only, replay-only, shuffled trace, no durable write, and specialized
   resolver conditions are necessary to allocate organ credit.
10. Native context, raw RAG, A-MEM, exact graph, and generator-aware program
    induction are real competitors, not teachers or hidden ceilings.
11. The independent unit is the counterfactual world-life pair. Targets,
    twins, calls, DREAM samples, traces, and reads are nested.
12. Fixed experience cannot support on-policy acquisition or flywheel language.

These agreements are the causal spine. The dispute is how to instantiate it
without spending a paper-sized budget before the mechanism is known to exist.

## 2. Contradictions and hidden failure modes

### 2.1 A's wake trace is not the trace it claims

A invokes a model before each scripted source action, seals its traversal, then
ignores its proposed action and executes the script. The subsequent outcome is
not the consequence of the model's released action. A may call this a
chronological traversal selector, but not a wake action/outcome trace.

B's solution is scientifically faithful: one frozen target-blind collector
chooses actions, those actions execute, and the resulting public life is frozen
and replayed to every G2 arm. This does **not** become G5 because memory is not
randomized during collection and no G2 arm changes the experience deck.

The B collector nevertheless introduces a risk: weak or malformed collection
can starve all memory arms. Retain that failure; never replace a world or let a
candidate-dependent action repair coverage. Use a predeclared, outcome-blind
confirmation block after candidate commitment so chronology is testable without
letting a proposal select its own support.

**Disposition:** select B's executed collector, with one common frozen life per
world and an outcome-blind confirmation schedule. Reject A's ignored-action
semantics for any claim containing “wake action/outcome trace.”

### 2.2 Candidate B is not automatically a post-native model world

D0 has 996 short events, 24 modules, 285 witnessed mappings, no model, and
model-independent K0--K3 cuts. Neither interpretation proves that this exceeds
the selected model's usable native context; with a long-context 32B model it
may not. Repetition would not fix the problem because unique causal information
must grow.

**Disposition:** fast DEV does not require post-native placement. Clear all
context/cache/workspace at evaluation and call it a fixed-snapshot mechanism
test. A publishable one-checkpoint post-native clause is legal only after a new
CPU-certified model-facing stream has unique causal growth and its complete
events exceed the mechanically computed `L_native`. If that construction
fails, retain the mechanism study and delete “post-native.”

### 2.3 X is a material benchmark extension

D0 deliberately excludes X. A correctly requires a separately reviewed X
sidecar; B treats CPU-certified X decks as a precondition without a distinct
authority disposition. X adds exceptions, information gathering, backtracking,
sealed analogous targets, and revision semantics. It is not a fixture tweak.

**Disposition:** omit X from fast DEV. Include X in calibration/confirmation
only if a separately ratified CPU sidecar passes gold `>=.85`, no-life/shortcut
`<=.35`, disambiguating-evidence necessity, pre-outcome analogous-target seal,
and leakage/isolation gates. Otherwise use J/P only and delete every revision
word.

### 2.4 A's G1 is too lean; B's is too broad for first contact

A compares no memory, connected gold open, and connected gold recurrent. It
does not show that witnessed atoms alone fail on P under the same model reader.
B's full 2x2 content x policy factorial answers that, but spending all model
cells on every stratum before a basic controller pass is unnecessary.

**Disposition:** fast G1 uses:

- `NONE_REC` on all retained strata;
- `C_OPEN` on all retained strata;
- `C_REC` on all retained strata;
- `A_REC` on P, and on J/X only if already generated at negligible marginal
  cost.

Use complete-cut/twin audit forks instead of a full model-facing twin arm in
DEV. Locked confirmation runs B's complete 2x2 plus no memory and twin memory.

### 2.5 B's 2x2 G2 is identifying; A's single shuffled-trace edge is not enough

A has authentic and shuffled trace only inside cross-episode DREAM. It can show
that trace binding matters to the full cell, but it cannot distinguish a trace
main effect from a trace x cross-episode interaction. B crosses authentic versus
shuffled trace with wake-only versus cross-episode content formation.

**Disposition:** keep B's 2x2 in confirmation. In fast DEV, stage it: run the
full cross-episode authentic cell first, then its cross-shuffled control; run
wake-auth/wake-shuffled only after the full cell clears the basic value gate.
The interaction is reported, not required for wake-trace credit. Residual DREAM
credit still requires full to beat compiler-only and replay-only.

### 2.6 B is undercontrolled for experiential binding

Trace shuffle is not an action/outcome shuffle, corpus-binding shuffle, or twin
memory assignment. These falsify different causal links. A correctly separates
life shuffle (`SL`), postcompile binding shuffle (`SB`), and twin text swap
(`TT`).

**Disposition:** retain all three for confirmation. Fast DEV requires `SB` and
one pre-DREAM life shuffle after the full cell passes; per-success twin atom
forks remain mandatory. `TT` may wait for calibration because it reuses a
frozen text snapshot and adds little generation cost.

### 2.7 The proposed 256-atom cap confounds formation with capacity

B fixes 256 retained atoms without showing that this accommodates the eligible
G2 semantic set. A similarly fixes semantic capacity without a construction
proof. A truncation failure can look like poor DREAM, and a target-blind cap can
evict exactly the rare old edges G2 is meant to test.

**Disposition:** G2 is not a compression experiment. Set the text cap to the
exact maximum eligible supported atoms under the frozen source generator so no
positive eligible atom is capacity-evicted. Report bytes. Capacity/rank pressure
belongs to G3/G4. If finite execution requires truncation, add a matched
no-truncation ceiling and treat capacity as an explicit factor under new
ratification.

### 2.8 Same-model CHECK is an unnecessary first-stage factorial

The one-resolver proposal lists no check, correlated check, fresh critic, and
mechanical check. Neither A nor B makes this central, correctly. Same-model
approval cannot support facts, and adding a critic sweep to G1/G2 would multiply
calls without identifying the requested trace/sleep arrows.

**Disposition:** primary G2 uses schema validation plus deterministic public
chronology/admission only. Preserve model CHECK outputs as non-evidentiary
diagnostics if emitted. Defer critic factorials unless false proposals or
deferral become the observed bottleneck.

### 2.9 The comparator tournament is efficient but cannot prove “strongest”

A selects `B*` from six calibration pairs. Fresh confirmation protects the
chosen comparison's type-I error, but six pairs can mis-rank seven heterogeneous
baselines. If an unselected graph/program/A-MEM arm would win on confirmation,
the phrase “strongest baseline” becomes selection rhetoric.

**Disposition:** use the tournament only to decide which optional expensive
reflection/procedural arm advances. Locked confirmation always includes no
memory, native context, hybrid RAG, native A-MEM, exact graph, and explicit
program induction. Require simultaneous noninferiority to each declared
primary baseline; do not compare only to calibration `B*`.

### 2.10 Eight locked pairs cannot carry A's multiplicity

A asks eight pairs to clear seven superiority bounds, two noninferiority bounds,
per-stratum positivity, causal-use rates, and exact validity. With only
`2^8=256` sign assignments, exact lower bounds are extremely discrete; after
max-T adjustment, a scientifically real moderate effect will usually be
inconclusive. Four targets per stratum/twin reduce item noise but do not create
independent replication.

B correctly calls four DEV and six calibration pairs descriptive, but supplies
no claim-eligible confirmation.

**Disposition:** four DEV pairs for fail-fast debugging; six new calibration
pairs for frozen practical-margin and variance checks; **16 new locked pairs**
for narrow confirmation. Use eight targets per retained stratum per twin.
Adapter/model samples remain nested. If pre-confirmation blinded power analysis
shows 16 insufficient, stop; a larger study requires new authority and may not
inspect confirmation outcomes first.

## 4. Selected fast DEV mechanism gate

### 4.1 Preconditions and scale

- independently green, reviewed D0 predecessor;
- separately ratified model-facing J/P pack, prompts, models, reader, compiler,
  target decks, call budgets, replay schedule, and visibility contract;
- four predeclared DEV twin pairs, four J and four P targets per twin;
- X absent unless its separate sidecar already passed;
- no requirement to exceed `L_native`; context/cache/workspace is still cleared
  for every evaluation item;
- one target-blind frozen collector per life, maximum **64 wake branches**, one
  executed public action per released branch, using only the current public
  state and witnessed `C0` snapshot;
- a predeclared candidate-independent confirmation block after proposal seal;
- at most eight replay bundles per checkpoint and four preallocated DREAM
  samples per bundle, as in B; rejected/malformed slots consume budget.

The 64-branch ceiling is a DEV resource bound, not a scientific constant. If
CPU constructibility shows J/P cannot receive distinct confirmation under it,
the design must return to deliberation; the runner may not increase it.

### 4.2 G1 fail-fast cells and gate

Run `NONE_REC`, `C_OPEN`, `C_REC`, and `A_REC` on P. Use 12 resolver
operations, six reads, one atom/read, and B's bounded workspace. Exact DEV go:

- direct-record closure `=0`;
- no-memory `<=.35` on J and P;
- `C_REC >=.85` separately on J and P;
- constructive cited-path rate `>=.80` separately;
- `C_OPEN <=.35` overall and `<=.45` in either stratum;
- `A_REC <=.35` on P;
- complete-cut mask and twin substitution pass on `>=.80` of C_REC successes;
- missing-edge unsupported decisive release `=0`;
- no visibility, parser, reset, candidate-channel, or evaluation-isolation
  failure.

DEV margins are descriptive spending gates, not estimates of a population.
Failure stops G2.

### 4.3 Staged G2 cells

Run in this fixed order:

1. `C0 compiler_only` and `F cross_auth`;
2. only if F clears the value screen: `TS cross_shuf`, `R1 replay_only`,
   `NW no_write`, `SB corpus_bind_shuffle`, and `SL life_shuffle`;
3. only if the corresponding mechanism margins pass: `WA wake_auth`,
   `WS wake_shuf`, and `SP specialized`;
4. run forged-statistic and shuffled-grouping attacks on DEV inputs; the former
   must fail schema/replay validation unless it is explicitly a model-visible
   attack cell.

Fast DEV go to calibration requires:

- zero taint, future-evidence, target, hidden-truth, critic-only, trace-as-
  evidence, compiler-invention, or provenance violations;
- accepted semantic-edge precision `=1.0`, false-memory use `=0`, and local
  text-read fidelity `>=.90`;
- `g_full/g_gold >=.70`, with positive gold gain, separately on J and P;
- F is no more than `.10` below the strongest DEV native context/RAG/graph/
  program comparator in either stratum;
- `F-TS >= max(.15,.5*g_full)`;
- `F-C0 >=.10` and `F-R1 >=.10`;
- `F-NW >=.10`;
- `F-SB >= max(.15,.5*g_full)` and F-SL is positive;
- `F-SP >=-.05` if shared-resolver adequacy will be claimed;
- causal cut/twin rate `>=.80` in each stratum;
- every arm-specific compute, corpus atom/view/byte count, candidate scan, and
  failure is reportable.

No confidence interval or p-value from four pairs is claim evidence. A failure
deletes the corresponding clause or stops before calibration according to the
predeclared decision table; it never triggers prompt/threshold/world repair on
the same pairs.

## 5. Selected narrow publishable confirmation

### 5.1 Frozen design

After DEV and six new descriptive calibration pairs, freeze all bytes and run
16 new locked counterfactual pairs. Use one fluid/thermal pack and one cut. The
cut may be called post-native only if its complete public events exceed exact
`L_native` and unique causal information grew to that point. Use eight targets
per retained stratum per twin. Include X only if its separate CPU sidecar and
model-facing calibration gates passed before confirmation was generated.

Required mechanism cells:

- compiler-only;
- wake-auth and wake-shuffled;
- replay-only;
- cross-auth full and cross-shuffled;
- cross-auth no durable write;
- shared and specialized cross-episode resolvers if shared adequacy is claimed;
- life shuffle, postcompile corpus-binding shuffle, and twin text swap;
- shuffled grouping if DEV showed grouping was model-visible or materially
  affected the result.

Required comparison systems:

- no memory and gold connected memory;
- actual native context;
- hybrid raw episodic RAG;
- native A-MEM;
- exact witnessed graph with native planner;
- explicit generator-aware program induction;
- any reflection/procedural comparator selected as strongest on calibration.

All model systems see their declared native interfaces; common one-atom
versions are protocol diagnostics. Exact graph/program outputs never enter
headline prompts or corpora.

### 5.2 Estimands and inference

Average targets and all model/DREAM samples inside twin, twins inside pair, and
test pair scores. Use all `2^16` paired sign flips, a studentized statistic,
and Romano--Wolf max-T simultaneous one-sided 95% bounds over the registered
primary family:

```text
trace binding:       F - CROSS_SHUF
compiler residual:   F - COMPILER_ONLY
dream residual:      F - REPLAY_ONLY
durable write:       F - NO_WRITE
corpus binding:      F - CORPUS_BIND_SHUFFLE
shared adequacy:     F - SPECIALIZED          # NI margin -.05
external adequacy:   F - each primary native baseline  # NI margin -.10
```

Life shuffle and twin text swap form a Holm-corrected secondary causal family.
Wake-auth versus wake-shuffled and the trace x cross-episode interaction are
mechanism decompositions; they do not replace the primary full-cell trace
contrast. J/P/X stratum-specific full-minus-no-memory tests form a separate
Holm family. Targets never receive confidence intervals as if independent.

Claim-eligible confirmation requires all DEV practical margins again and:

- superiority bounds above `0` for trace, compiler, DREAM, durable-write, and
  corpus-binding contrasts;
- shared lower bound above `-.05` if the shared-resolver clause is retained;
- simultaneous lower bounds above `-.10` against **every** declared primary
  native baseline;
- full-minus-no-memory lower bound above `0` in every retained stratum;
- F recovers `>=70%` of gold gain in every retained stratum;
- accepted-edge precision `1.0`, false-memory use `0`, read fidelity `>=.90`,
  causal cut/twin rate `>=.80`, and unsupported missing-edge release `0`;
- `F-SB >= max(.15,.5*g_full)` in observed mean, with life/twin effects in the
  harmful direction;
- no visibility, chronology, trace-matching, compiler, reset, candidate-scan,
  evaluation-write, failure-accounting, or resource-ledger violation.

If blinded calibration predicts that 16 pairs cannot resolve this family, do
not open confirmation. A larger study needs a new ratification; it may not be
chosen after seeing locked outcomes.

### 5.3 Maximum claim

If every applicable gate passes, the narrow claim is:

> In one controlled rendered fluid/thermal pack at one frozen checkpoint,
> authentic executed wake traces and target-blind cross-episode proposals,
> followed by chronological deterministic admission and text compilation,
> produced local memory that a frozen recurrent resolver used causally for
> later connected/prospective (and, if separately certified, revision) action;
> the result exceeded compiler, replay, trace-shuffle, no-write, and binding
> controls and was noninferior to every declared primary native memory
> baseline within the registered margin.

If the checkpoint is not strictly post-native, delete that adjective. One pack
and one cut never support a lifetime curve, continued improvement, saturation,
cross-pack generality, LoRA/weights, compression, on-policy acquisition, or a
flywheel.

## 6. Fail-closed leakage and feasibility checklist

Before any execution proposal can be ratified, it must dispose these risks:

- **collector target leakage:** collector missions, replay triggers, and
  confirmation actions are frozen before held-out targets and never selected
  from downstream score;
- **self-confirming proposals:** confirmation actions are outcome- and
  candidate-content-blind; proposals cannot choose the test that supports them;
- **trace answer laundering:** traces contain no held-out goal, answer, future
  action/outcome, scorer result, or free rationale; remapped traces cite valid
  recipient-life public handles but add no evidence;
- **invalid shuffle:** trace shuffles preserve operation/read/found/miss/path/
  age/type/root/token distributions and break only the intended binding;
- **target-conditioned reader:** catalog and reader receive no target/state,
  desired relation/value, hidden family, rank, topology, or timing channel;
- **hidden exhaustive search:** reader candidate enumeration and latency scale
  are logged; an `O(lifetime)` scan is called external search;
- **program leakage:** affine grammar/program and exact graph remain offline
  competitors, never demonstrations, query plans, checker responses, or
  compiler inputs beyond publicly allowed rules;
- **hidden-truth admission:** offline truth may score accepted-edge precision
  only after sealing; it may not decide proposals, support, truncation, or
  replay selection;
- **no-write mismatch:** reusing compiler-only evaluation bytes is legal only
  if corpus, reader index, prompt, RNG/cache/process state, and every
  model-visible byte hash-identically; search compute remains separately
  charged;
- **context fiction:** no post-native label until exact serialization proves
  it; padding/repeated views/touches do not count as growth;
- **resource infeasibility:** the exact collector-call count, replay bundles,
  DREAM samples, baseline calls, target cells, maximum tokens, wall time, GPU
  hours, storage, and failure policy are bound before approval;
- **confirmation underpower:** 16-pair blinded feasibility is checked before
  locked outcomes, never repaired afterward;
- **scope creep:** G1/G2 produces text mechanism evidence only. No LoRA, G3,
  extra pack, on-policy collection, paper submission, or scientific claim is
  automatically released by a pass.

## 7. Final disposition

Select the hybrid design above. It is faster than B for first contact, more
faithful than A about wake action/outcome traces, better controlled than B for
experiential binding, and statistically defensible where A is not.

The fast DEV gate should be expected to fail cheaply if the resolver cannot use
gold memory, cross-episode DREAM adds nothing, trace binding is irrelevant, or
the compiler alone explains the gain. The locked study should run only if those
arrows have already cleared practical margins without prompt or benchmark
repair. That separation—not an indiscriminate union of cells—is the smallest
credible path from D0 to a narrow G1/G2 paper result.
