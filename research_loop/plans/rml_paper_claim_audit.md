# Paper-claim audit: Rendered Maintenance Lifetimes

**Scope:** bounded audit of RML-D0 and the proposed experimental ladder. This
is not implementation authority and does not authorize model, GPU, training,
locked, on-policy, or publication work.

## Bottom line

RML has the right *shape* for a controlled mechanistic paper, but D0 is not a
scientific experiment. It is a reference-instrument construction test. A green
D0 could show that one exact synthetic action world has the intended oracle,
necessity, leakage, twin, chronology, and isolation geometry. It could not show
that any model learned, that memory improved action, that weights retained
experience, or that an agent learned from its actions.

The current Candidate A is additionally a hard **no-go**. Its target/twin
involution and its fixed-BYPASS bridge each make the accepted set empty. The
Bayes measure, P ablation, source-marginal projection, schemas, proposal kernel,
and resource proof are also not implementation-exact. D0 cannot start until a
repaired candidate passes fresh deliberation and exact human ratification.

For a credible ICLR mechanistic/systems paper, the smallest honest post-D0
program is: gold construct validation, learned fixed-deck text attribution,
same-corpus transport, three-pack post-native confirmation, and randomized
on-policy memory assignment. The last rung may be omitted only if the paper
explicitly claims **experience-conditioned representation and use**, not
learning to gather experience or a self-improving flywheel.

## 1. What D0 can and cannot support

### What a repaired, green D0 can support

D0 may establish only the following model-independent facts about one
fluid/thermal reference instrument:

- deterministic generation, replay, exact scoring, and sealed evaluation
  noninterference under a finite CPU envelope;
- nonempty counterfactual twins with byte-identical target-visible inputs,
  different valid plans, and authentic-history identifiability;
- exact legal-history headroom and bounded no-lifetime Bayes performance under
  a fully specified accepted-item measure;
- per-target connected-J necessity: the registered old transform, recent
  mechanism, and state-flow join are each necessary, and early-only or
  recent-only information cannot solve the target;
- per-target prospective-P geometry: a prospectively committed, later-supported
  schema determines a withheld edge, while a precisely defined atoms-only
  completion or witnessed-edge controller does not;
- growth of unique public roots, identifiable local relations, old/new
  interactions, and prospective commitments across complete causal-era cuts;
- absence, within registered ceilings, of target-, state-, identifier-,
  renderer-, passive-signature-, frequency-, source-string-, and selection
  shortcuts.

These are construct-validity and benchmark-engineering results. They show that
the world *can in principle* separate supplied legal history from no-life
inference. They do not show that a learned system occupies that gap.

### What D0 cannot support

D0 cannot support any claim of:

- a validated paper benchmark, population generality, or external validity;
- model learning, experiential learning, developmental improvement, memory
  utility, or better action;
- learned DREAM/THINK behavior, autonomous structure discovery, compression,
  recurrent-sleep benefit, or revision;
- post-native retention, context crossover, scaling, saturation, or continuing
  improvement with lifetime;
- LoRA fidelity, advantage, efficiency, or causal contribution;
- policy-selected information gathering, improved future experience, or an
  action--experience--memory flywheel.

Even P is only **grammar-conditioned prospective edge reconstruction** at D0:
the affine hypothesis class and exact program search are supplied. D0 does not
show that a model discovers a schema. Likewise, a nine-action target is not
nine decisions if logistics dominate and the consequential choice is a small
cartridge/valve lookup.

## 2. Smallest post-D0 matrix for a paper

The matrix below is the minimum defensible ladder. Stages are sequential;
passing one does not authorize the next.

| Stage | Minimum arms and factors | What it identifies | Maximum allowed claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1: gold construct | no-life Bayes; gold atoms with nonadaptive reads; the same atoms with generic recurrent reads; witnessed graph without schema; supplied schema/program search; legal-history oracle | whether J/P/X require adaptive reconstruction and whether the frozen controller can use sufficient information | RML is model-usable as a controlled construct |
| G2: learned text, fixed deck | no memory; honest native context; raw episodic RAG; hierarchical reflection; native A-MEM; exact witnessed graph; explicit generator-aware program induction; learned DREAM/SLEEP text | whether learned, chronologically admitted structure adds action value under identical experience | bundled experience-conditioned compilation/reader/controller improves later action |
| G3: transport factorial | frozen admitted corpus in text; recognition-assisted LoRA; strict unaided generative LoRA; direct-QA LoRA; raw-event LoRA; clean base | corpus value versus transport loss, reader assistance, and direct parametric writing | per-life weights retain the admitted corpus's value, if noninferiority passes |
| G4: common-deck confirmation | all primary native baselines above; three non-isomorphic packs; `0.5x, 2x, 4x, 8x L_native`; at least eight fresh twin pairs per pack; two adapter seeds averaged within world | post-native acquisition, retention, old/new composition, prospective transfer, revision, and baseline response curves | fixed-deck post-native developmental representation/use, with no flywheel clause |
| G5: randomized on-policy | authentic, null, binding-shuffled, and twin memory assignment before an information-gathering block; one reconsolidation; sealed later target; collector traces crossed through compiled, RAG, native-graph, and direct-QA representations | causal effect of assigned memory on evidence collection, later memory, and later action; experience quality versus representation/use | bounded action--experience--memory flywheel |

Three points are non-negotiable.

First, the explicit program learner and native exact graph are primary
baselines, not ceilings hidden in an appendix. P is designer-supplied program
structure; omitting program induction would make a table-to-schema prompt look
novel.

Second, common-interface and native-interface results must both be reported.
Forcing A-MEM or a graph through a one-atom reader tests protocol compatibility,
not its strongest system. Conversely, recognition-assisted LoRA cannot be
credited for candidate enumeration or multi-hop reasoning performed outside
the adapter.

Third, three non-isomorphic packs and three strictly post-native cuts are the
minimum for growth language. One pack can establish a mechanism interaction,
not a lifetime phase diagram. A standard external benchmark is optional for a
narrow controlled-mechanism paper, but without it the paper must present RML as
a synthetic causal instrument and make no external-validity claim.

## 3. Strongest baselines and decisive confounds

### Baselines that can erase the paper claim

The strongest adversaries are, in order of scientific importance:

1. **Explicit generator-aware program induction.** This is the fair opponent
   for P and higher-order laws. If it wins, RML may still be a useful benchmark,
   but learned compression has no demonstrated moat.
2. **Exact witnessed-event graph with a native planner.** If it is lossless,
   smaller, cheaper, and at least as good, the result is explicit causal memory,
   not a reason to use weights.
3. **Native A-MEM/linked memory and procedural-skill memory.** These are the
   strongest learned external-memory comparisons; common-reader-only versions
   are insufficient.
4. **Actual native long context with honest truncation and a hierarchical
   summary/reflection agent.** `L_native` must be computed from the frozen model,
   template, state, workspace, and output reserve—not a marketing limit.
5. **Raw episodic RAG.** Use a hybrid lexical/embedding retriever and report its
   native resource use; a deliberately weak one-atom RAG is not persuasive.
6. **Direct-QA LoRA and batch SFT on the same life.** These test whether DREAM
   compilation or online cadence adds anything beyond ordinary post-training.
7. **No-memory prompted controller and exact accepted-deck Bayes-N.** These
   measure active reacquisition and prior/selection leakage, not merely a random
   floor.
8. **Hidden legal-history/gold oracle.** This establishes remaining headroom and
   prevents a flat baseline from being mislabeled saturation.

### Confounds that must be broken, not discussed away

- **Empty or selected target space:** Candidate A currently fails here. Exact
  first-accept conditioning and all rejection history belong in Bayes-N.
- **Renderer/handle/seed leakage:** public handles derived from an enumerable
  root can identify latent slots. Nuisance namespaces require a declared
  ex-ante measure and scorer-only random keys until sealing.
- **Marginal rather than counterfactual twins:** equal counts and byte lengths
  are inadequate. Require cutwise equality of a binding-erased projection that
  retains public values, state deltas, outcomes, and rendered substitutions.
- **Scripted panels as labels:** near-exhaustive reset bench trials may be a
  rendered truth table. Outcome-binding shuffles, program induction, and
  nonexhaustive schedules determine whether action semantics matter.
- **Stored-solution leakage:** no source `(state, goal)`, complete plan,
  terminal sequence, direct record, or reader return may close J/P/X.
- **Controller-supplied intelligence:** adaptive query selection, recognition,
  candidate scans, and explicit composition must be credited separately from
  the corpus and LoRA.
- **Supplied grammar:** P does not show schema discovery unless the learned
  system commits the schema prospectively and beats atoms-only and explicit
  program controls on held-out productions or grammar families.
- **Within-item reacquisition:** a strong agent may relearn the target inside
  the action budget. Exact Bayes-N and matched action budgets are essential.
- **Capacity/compute mismatch:** report retained bytes, active bytes, hidden
  candidate work, retrieval/model calls, train tokens/FLOPs, and wall time.
  Linear adapter growth or an `O(lifetime)` hidden scan defeats a compression
  or scalable-parametric-read claim.
- **False replication:** targets, calls, dream samples, and adapter seeds are
  nested. The counterfactual world-life pair is the independent unit.
- **Cross-life or evaluation contamination:** adapters, caches, indexes, RNG,
  workspaces, files, and evaluation outcomes must fail canary and run/skip hash
  tests.
- **One-pack semantic overfit:** every pack must have a positive point estimate;
  pooled significance driven by fluid/thermal alone is a no-go for the phase
  diagram.

## 4. Does RML test learning from actions rather than lookup?

**D0: no.** It contains no learner.

**Common-deck RML: only partially.** The input records are consequences of
actions, the targets require later actions, and binding/twin interventions can
show that authentic causal content matters. But the experience-producing
actions are supplied, the controller and grammar are frozen, and the memory
may reduce the task to retrieving two or three local bindings followed by
prompted composition. The defensible claim is that *action-outcome-conditioned
memory changed later action under a fixed experience distribution*. It is not
that the agent learned which actions to take to learn.

RML tests learning **through actions** only at G5. Randomized memory assignment
must occur before any new observation; it must change information-seeking
actions; those actions must improve target-relevant information; the resulting
ordinary outcomes must improve supported memory after a fixed reconsolidation;
and that memory must improve a pre-generated sealed later action. Crossing the
collected traces through alternative memory pipelines is required to separate
better experience from better representation.

Even a G5 pass would not establish a learned resolver, autonomous scientific
taste, or open-ended continual learning: `phi`, the action API, and the world
family remain designer-supplied and frozen.

## 5. Exact go/no-go gates

All gates are conjunctive. A failure narrows or kills the associated clause; it
does not license threshold tuning, seed replacement, baseline removal, or a
larger run.

### Gate 0: D0 constructibility and authority

**Current decision: NO-GO.** Before any implementation, Candidate A must:

- repair both empty-set contradictions and generate nonempty target/bridge
  sets on both twins;
- bind exact public/hidden/selection/proof/score/audit schemas, proposal and RNG
  draw ledgers, accepted-item Bayes measure, atoms-only P completion measure,
  cutwise source projection, compact state-quotient certificates, and resource
  accounting;
- pass fresh interpretations, adversarial critique, adjudicated consensus, and
  exact human ratification of the repaired bytes and CPU-only scope.

A ratified D0 then passes only if, on all 64 preselected uncurated twin pairs:

- legal-history/gold-memory value is `>= .85` in every primary stratum;
- accepted-deck Bayes-N and every registered leakage probe are `<= .35` (and
  the predeclared 90th-percentile Bayes pair value is `<= .45`);
- every J target passes the registered old-transform cut, early-only failure,
  recent-only failure, uniqueness, and minimality certificates;
- every P target has pretarget schema commitment, later public support, schema
  or program value `>= .85`, and atoms-only/witnessed-graph value `<= .35`;
- target-visible twin bytes collide exactly, correct plans differ, authentic
  history identifies the side, and the binding-erased source projection is
  exactly equal at every cut;
- each era adds the frozen minimum unique roots, relations, old/new interaction,
  and prospective prediction; no padding counts as growth;
- replay is byte-identical and evaluation run versus skip leaves source state,
  log, RNG, suffix, and later-evidence hashes identical;
- the frozen wall/RSS/disk/worker ceilings pass without weakened filters. The
  candidate's proposed two-hour, 8-GiB, four-worker, 2-GiB-artifact envelope
  must be demonstrated by a conservative preflight, not asserted.

Any single uncertified target, leakage/firewall failure, exhausted selector, or
resource overrun is D0 failure. Green D0 stops at an instrument-conformance
report.

### Gate 1: gold model usability

Proceed to learned memory only if:

- legitimate single-record closure is exactly zero;
- generic recurrent gold scores `>= .85` on each of J/P/X and has certified
  constructive-proof rate `>= .80`;
- the identical gold atoms with all queries committed before reads score
  `<= .35`;
- memory-cut masking changes the decisive operation and matched twin-atom
  substitution produces the twin-valid operation on `>= .80` of credited
  successes;
- all native-cut projections are recertified after the exact model, tokenizer,
  template, and reserves are frozen.

Failure invalidates the model-facing construct; do not compensate with LoRA or
more training.

### Gate 2: learned text and binding

LoRA remains forbidden unless learned compiled text:

- admits zero chronology, provenance, visibility, or target-conditioning
  violations;
- recovers at least `70%` of the gold-memory gain over no memory;
- is no more than `.10` below the strongest of raw RAG, native A-MEM, exact
  graph, reflection, and explicit program induction on every critical DEV
  stratum;
- loses at least `max(.15, .5 * G_text)` under the matched corpus-binding
  intervention, where `G_text = E_text - N`, and has same-direction losses for
  life shuffle and twin assignment.

If `G_text <= 0`, the binding ratio is undefined and the gate fails. A text
failure ends the learned-memory paper path; it cannot be rescued by weights.

### Gate 3: same-corpus transport

A weights clause is allowed only if:

- fixed-query read fidelity is `>= .90` at every registered cut;
- `E-LoRA - E-text >= -.05` on the frozen corpus under simultaneous paired
  bounds;
- `E-LoRA` beats direct-QA LoRA and binding controls in the registered
  direction, with intact-minus-binding loss `>= max(.15, .5 * G_LoRA)`;
- strict unaided generative reads, clean-base reads, candidate enumeration,
  rank/bytes, and all train/query resources are reported;
- the result does not rely on linear hidden candidate scans or adapter capacity
  growth for any efficiency/compression language.

Noninferiority supports retained value in weights, not LoRA superiority.

### Gate 4: common-deck paper confirmation

Use three non-isomorphic packs, at least eight new twin pairs per pack, two
adapter seeds averaged within world, and `0.5x, 2x, 4x, 8x L_native` with the
last three strictly post-native. Proceed to a fixed-deck paper claim only if:

- every interval adds unique causal information and every pack has a positive
  point estimate;
- the full system's post-native AUC exceeds each declared primary native
  baseline by mean `>= .10` with simultaneous one-sided world-pair lower bounds
  `> 0`;
- at `8x`, each of N/O/J/P/X recovers `>= 70%` of its gold-memory gain and its
  corrected contrast to its strongest baseline has lower bound `> 0`;
- old-relation value at `8x` is noninferior to `2x` with margin `-.05`, while
  new acquisition and J/P remain positive at all three post-native cuts;
- authentic-versus-binding/twin/cross-life controls pass the same half-gain
  rule as Gate 2;
- failed scientific cells remain zero-valued and no pack, world, or seed is
  replaced.

Use “baseline saturated” only when both later-interval improvement upper bounds
are `<= .02`, oracle headroom remains `>= .10`, and unique causal information
continues to grow. Without all three post-native cuts, delete growth, crossover,
and saturation language.

### Gate 5: on-policy action-learning claim

Randomize authentic/null/binding-shuffled/twin memory before the information
block. A flywheel clause requires, versus the strongest randomized control:

- a registered difference in pre-outcome information-seeking actions at
  family-wise `alpha=.05`;
- target-relevant information gain/coverage improvement of mean `>= .10` with
  simultaneous lower bound `> 0`;
- later supported-memory quality improvement of mean `>= .10` with lower bound
  `> 0`;
- later sealed action-value improvement of mean `>= .10` with lower bound
  `> 0`;
- the final action gain to lose at least `max(.15, .5 * G_onpolicy)` under
  binding/twin assignment;
- the collector-trace cross to show separately whether the gain came from
  experience quality, representation/use, or both.

If memory changes action but not information, information but not later memory,
or memory but not sealed later action, the causal chain is broken. Report the
passing links and delete the flywheel claim.

## Paper decision

RML is worth pursuing only as a falsifiable measurement system in which graph
or program induction is allowed to win. A repaired D0 is necessary but carries
near-zero positive paper evidence. The first publishable result begins at the
three-pack fixed-deck confirmation; the thesis-level result begins only at the
randomized on-policy gate. If the explicit graph/program baseline dominates or
same-corpus LoRA transport fails, the honest paper is a benchmark/compiler or
negative parametric-memory result—not a weakened claim that weights learned
from actions.
