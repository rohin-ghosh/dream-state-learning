# One-parent/one-child headline v3 spending repair

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: proposal-only delta to v1 plus the v2 addendum. It authorizes no
implementation, task generation, model/tokenizer call, adapter operation,
CompilerGym execution, external scientific execution, GPU use, or claim.

## Reason for this delta

The v2 four-root spending pilot advances when at least three root-level `D`
values and mean `D` are positive. Fresh independent attacks found a valid
counterexample: `D` can be positive when deployment writes harm both the
parented and unparented checkpoints, provided they harm the unparented branch
more. It can also be positive without the parented child improving over its
own entry value. Such a pilot cannot justify spending 32 confirmation roots
under the claim that parenting improved later learning.

This delta changes only the pre-confirmation spending decision and required
receipts. It adds no task, family, service, parent, child, fit, probe, endpoint,
scientific root, or confirmation hypothesis. All v1/v2 topology, estimands,
confirmation margins, fixed sequence, failure values, and authority boundaries
remain unchanged.

## Replacement for the v2 Gate-6 advancement rule

For each of the four excluded spending-pilot roots, compute the already
registered quantities:

```text
D_r = [gAUC_r(P1)-gAUC_r(P0)] - [gAUC_r(U1)-gAUC_r(U0)]
W_P,r = gAUC_r(P1)-gAUC_r(P0)
L_terminal,r = V_P1,r(48)-V_P1,r(0)
```

Advancement to the fixed `N=32` confirmation requires all of:

1. zero provenance, firewall, parent-deletion, resource-parity, probe-return,
   transactional, native-routing, DREAM-restoration, fit/mount, and generic-
   anchor violations;
2. `D_r > 0`, `W_P,r > 0`, and `L_terminal,r > 0` jointly in at least three of
   the four roots;
3. `mean(D) > 0`, `mean(W_P) > 0`, and `mean(L_terminal) > 0`; and
4. no root crosses any separately ratified adverse safety/performance bound.

These are directional pathology/spending guards. The `.05` practical margins
and confidence rules remain exclusive to confirmation and are not applied to
the four pilot roots. The pilot remains excluded from confirmation inference.
All four roots and all raw values are retained and reported.

A pilot that passes only `D` may justify writer diagnosis or a new source-
bound engineering proposal. It cannot authorize the fixed-N confirmation
under an "improved ability to learn" objective.

## Required writer-path receipts

Before pilot identities exist, freeze an arm-by-cut receipt table containing:

- prospective eligible opportunities and accepted scientific rows;
- `THINK_TO_ACT` versus `DREAM_STATE` target mix;
- improvement versus surprise/revision admission route;
- scientific rows versus deterministic rehearsal fills;
- labeled and attended tokens plus fixed optimizer positions;
- rejected rows and exact rejection reasons;
- fit, quarantine, mount, rollback, and adapter-off outcomes; and
- subsequent strict typed behavior and probe value.

These variables are post-treatment mediators. They are reported as mechanism
decomposition and may not be used for post-assignment matching, adjustment,
stratification, root exclusion, or an alternative yield-adjusted primary.

## Claim consequence

The phrase "parenting improved the ability to learn" still requires the
registered confirmation sequence `D -> W_P -> L_terminal`. This repair merely
prevents a directionally misleading pilot from funding that test. It supplies
no evidence itself and does not expand the eligible claim.
