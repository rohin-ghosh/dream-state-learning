# Paper-ROI adversarial appendix to RML-D0 Stage-A REWORK v1

**Status:** decision advice only. No implementation, model, network, or GPU
action is authorized.

## Bottom line

Do **not** implement the full 797-line repair now. It is a defensible formal
verification program, but a poor next investment for the paper: nearly all of
its information is about whether the benchmark implementation conforms, not
whether explicit recurrent memory, wake traces, or offline formation work.

Choose **B: a newly ratified minimal D0 instrument**, then run the cheapest G1
gold-memory screen. Archive Candidate B unchanged rather than trying to
incrementally simplify its frozen bytes. Option C is acceptable only as an
explicitly non-publishable scout; it is not a substitute for a causal
instrument if the result is positive.

The estimates are deliberately asymmetric. A successful A result carries high

contract is not yet independently established.

## Why A is scientifically overbuilt

The full repair spends most effort on 17 record schemas, a source-closure
manifest, a 3,072-role exact posterior, disposable mutations, complete graph
certificates, cross-platform sandboxing, and exact accounting. Those are
appropriate for a durable benchmark release. They are not required to decide
the current highest-value uncertainty:

> Does connected local text memory causally help a frozen recurrent resolver,
> and does atoms-only memory fail on the prospective item?

If G1 connected gold fails, essentially all Candidate-B-specific Bayes,
chronology, certificates, balance construction, and production-ledger work is
sunk. If G1 succeeds, the paper still needs the much larger G2 collector,
DREAM/admission/compiler, controls, baselines, and locked inference. A delays
both outcomes.

The full repair is worth reviving only if the project first obtains a positive
G1/G2 signal and then needs Candidate B itself as a reusable public benchmark
or audit artifact.

## Recommended B: minimum valid causal instrument

Create a new protocol, not an edit falsely presented as Candidate-B
conformance. Preserve only the causal spine needed by G1/G2:

1. **J and P only.** Remove N, O, R, production reserves, 64-pair roster,
   balance marginals not needed for target-only equality, and paper-irrelevant
   general benchmark machinery.
2. **One tiny finite world family.** Keep the two-bit coolant, four setter
   families, four valve modes, three observed affine descriptors, and one D11
   prospective descriptor. Use a direct constructive sampler with no world
   rejection.
3. **Short actual chronology.** E0 identifies one of 24 laws from three public
   points. E1 contains one old/current bridge and one precommitted held-out
   valve edge. Commit before the confirming source event and P target.
4. **Rendered J provenance.** Two source-old and two current cartridge
   families are visible through module/family handles; the successful pair
   requires one of each. All registered removals make success impossible.
5. **Counterfactual twins.** Target-visible bytes collide while useful pairs
   and correct modes differ. Keep fresh handle tapes independent of role.
6. **Exact small no-life oracle.** Exhaustively enumerate the complete tiny
   hidden space and first-accept kernel; do not implement the 3,072-role
   production factorization. Require target-only/Bayes value `<=.35` and legal
   history/gold/program value `=1`.
7. **One independent literal oracle.** It must not import subject code and must
   independently establish transition bytes, minimum plans, twin collision,
   chronology, J/P necessity, and no-life value. A small frozen manifest is
   enough; a full mutation platform is not.
8. **Actual disposable evaluation clone.** Implement one OS-contained path
   because the same boundary is reusable by G1/G2. Scope the claim to this
   world/runtime; do not build a cross-platform benchmark framework.
9. **Minimal closed artifacts.** Define only records that cross process or
   evidence boundaries. Do not recreate all Candidate-B record families unless
   the smaller instrument actually emits them.

Target a subject plus independent oracle small enough to audit linearly:
roughly `<=2,500` non-test logical lines, `<10 s` Stage-A wall, `<256 MiB` RSS,
and `<100,000` enumerated transition edges. These are design timeboxes, not
post-hoc scientific thresholds.

The minimal D0 pass means only that the G1 screen is interpretable. It does not
need to be a general benchmark or carry the eventual paper's full locked
inference.

## Option C: when a scout is rational

C can be rational if the team believes even generic gold-memory use is highly
uncertain and wants the fastest possible negative result. Run only a tiny
four-pair G1 scaffold on a simple world:

```text
no memory
connected gold with open queries
connected gold with recurrent generic queries
atoms-only recurrent on P
```

Include the typed/generator-aware ceiling, no-memory controller, target-byte
collision, cut masking, and twin substitution even in the scout. Label every
outcome exploratory and non-publishable. Do not run the broader G2 factorial,
locked confirmation, or claim allocation on an unhardened world.

If the scout is negative even with typed connected gold, stop the RML text
mechanism. If recurrent connected gold is positive and open/atoms/no-memory
remain low, return to B and freeze the minimal instrument before any
claim-bearing G2 run. A positive scout never licenses retrospective benchmark
hardening around the observed winners.

## Kill criteria

### Kill A (full repair) if any holds

- A five-day feasibility spike cannot demonstrate an independent literal
  planner, actual evaluator containment, and exact posterior on one integrated
  fixture within the Candidate-B Stage-A limits.
- Forecast implementation exceeds 15 engineer-days before fresh review, or
  more than half the new code is Candidate-B-specific verification machinery
  not reused by G1/G2.
- The 3,072-role Bayes or cross-platform sandbox becomes the critical path to
  the first model-facing result.
- The project cannot state a paper claim that uniquely requires Candidate B
  rather than any valid J/P world.

### Kill or redesign B if any holds

- The complete hidden space is not exhaustively enumerable in `<100,000`
  action edges or the CPU gate exceeds 10 seconds/256 MiB on the target host.
- Target-only exact Bayes, identifier-only, or open-read value exceeds `.35`
  on J or P.
- Legal-history/gold/program value is below 1, any twin target bytes differ,
  or a J/P necessity cut remains solvable.
- A J success uses two same-era facts, P has a witnessed decisive edge, or a
  direct record contains the answer/complete plan.
- The instrument requires more than two weeks before the four-pair G1 DEV
  screen can begin.

### Kill the model mechanism after the G1 screen if any holds

- The typed connected-gold ceiling is below `.80`; the task/model interface is
  not viable.
- Generic recurrent connected gold is below `.85` after the predeclared DEV
  prompt/parser budget.
- No-memory, target-only, open-read, or P atoms-only exceeds `.35`.
- Fewer than `.80` of connected-gold successes pass complete-cut masking and
  twin substitution.
- Any visibility, source mutation, evaluator isolation, or unsupported-release
  failure occurs.

### Kill C as a paper path if any holds

- The team wants to interpret a positive scout without first freezing B.
- The simple world cannot support independent target-only and legal-history
  ceilings, twin controls, and necessity.
- More than six engineering days are spent without reaching the four-pair
  scaffold; at that point B is the more honest use of effort.

## Recommendation

Archive Candidate B and the full rework as a high-assurance fallback. Ratify a
new minimal J/P D0 and timebox it to two weeks. Its first release should be the
four-pair G1 gold-memory screen, not a larger D0 benchmark campaign.

If the minimal D0 cannot be made shortcut-resistant within the timebox, use C
only for a negative scout and make no paper claim. If G1 passes its causal
gates, proceed to the staged G2 DEV matrix and revisit which additional
benchmark hardening is genuinely required by the surviving claim.
