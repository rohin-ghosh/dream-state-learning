## 0. Plain-language decision

Keep the small PCFL ruler exactly as designed in V4, but remove every remaining
place where an implementer or scorer could make an outcome-changing choice.

The ruler still asks only whether one frozen text model can use **supplied**
connected memory to construct two routes, use separately supplied public
uncertainty evidence, and later use a supplied old-plus-new carrier after a
sterile reset. It is not DREAM, SLEEP, LoRA, parenting, retention, online
learning, or a lifetime result.

V4's five-role consensus returned `rework` because eleven contracts were still
underspecified. Three fresh source-only repairs now close those eleven items
without adding a root, condition, call, token, topology, or scientific claim.
V5 asks whether the exact inert preparation-source bytes may be **authored**
next. It does not ask to run them.

## 3. Authority is now two non-overlapping operations

V5 adopts the exact `SourceAuthoringGrantV1`, `SourceAuthoringPlanV1`,
`NormativeSourceManifestV1`, `SourceProvenanceRowV1`, and
`PreparationExecutionGrantV1` schemas in the authority repair.

The distinction is strict:

1. `SOURCE_CANDIDATE_AUTHORING_AUTHORITY` may create/edit only the exact paths
   in a preapproved `SourceAuthoringPlanV1`, compute their byte lengths and
   hashes, and write a nonauthoritative source manifest. It may not import,

2. `PREPARATION_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY` can exist only after all real source bytes
   are individually hash-bound, freshly reviewed, freshly deliberated, and
   explicitly approved. It may execute one exact deterministic CPU preparation
   under a closed read/write/environment/resource manifest. It cannot edit
   source and cannot retry.

`source_authoring_plan.json` in this change. It contains 24 literal paths,
roles, media types, and size maxima. The output manifest is the only plan row
with `manifest_member=false`. No directory, glob, symlink, variable, omitted
transitive source, or unlisted write is authorized.

The authority graph is acyclic:

```text
V5 design deliberation -> human source-authoring grant
-> candidate source bytes + nonauthoritative source manifest
-> exact-byte review/deliberation -> human preparation-execution grant
-> one nonconsumable candidate preparation -> separate human fixture freeze
```

Passing a checker, producing a manifest, or finishing a stage never creates

## 7. Exact behavioral and causal endpoints

trace law from the endpoint repair.

diagnostics. U has four separate public-evidence endpoints. D has its own

A path trace records every charged relation attempt and exactly one of:


the effective-move skeleton. Upper and lower routes normalize to the same

Connected trace dependence requires, for both A and B and both h twins:

1. AUTH succeeds constructively;
2. BRIDGE_CUT leaves the successful normalized class;
3. TWIN_REDIRECT reaches decisive state C and switches its first terminal
   relation from `R05` to `R06` for A or from `R06` to `R05` for B; and
4. model-free deletion of all registered supports invalidates the cited trace.

Text differences, path-choice differences, extra harmless `NO_EFFECT`, or a
changed answer without the registered terminal switch do not count.
