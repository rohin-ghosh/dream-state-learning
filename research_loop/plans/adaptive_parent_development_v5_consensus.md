# Adaptive Parent Development v5 — adjudicated consensus

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: exact proposal for human ratification. It is not implementation or
authorization for model/tokenizer execution, benchmark generation, adapter
work, GPU use, resource acquisition, or external scientific execution.

## Plain-language system

The cognitive architecture remains small:

```text
THINK   continuous thought, judgment, feeling, plan, action, and public outcome
DREAM   rebuild the finite active context; never write weights
SLEEP   compile grounded experience and transactionally write the child LoRA
PARENT  teach the child and improve teaching through nonparametric state;
        parent weights remain fixed
```

The child is the only parametric learner. Laboratory ledgers, hashes, canaries,
leakage scans, disposable exams, and rollback receipts are audit apparatus,
not extra cognitive organs.

## What replaces the old v4

The old `one_parent_child_headline_v4` exact bytes remain immutable audit
history. This v5 explicitly supersedes its scientific topology, parent/child
identities, stateless/reset-parent semantics, target-blind-only teaching,
nursery/deployment arm names, fixed pilot arithmetic, resource counts, and
claim object.

Retained from v4 only after restatement here:

- one immutable evidence identity per valid public action/outcome;
- compiler derivatives may change training dose but never evidence count;
- response-only loss: parent text may condition the child but is loss-masked;
- exact interface/provenance/leakage checks and transactional rollback;
- isolated roots, independent review, and separate execution/resource gates.

## Parent

There is one child-facing parent policy per lineage. At lineage creation we
bind the strongest teacher inside the ratified cost, latency, privacy, and
reproducibility envelope: exact model/revision, prompt, decoding, permissions,
tools, files, and skills. Its weights never update. Changing any bound field
forks a new lineage.

The parent improves through a bounded model-visible notebook, initially capped
at 32 KiB:

- reusable `pedagogy_core`: teaching rules, curriculum coverage/difficulty,
  and the independent-struggle schedule;
- private `child_record`: evidenced misconceptions, masteries, and responses;
- at most eight active teaching hypotheses with predicted effects, confidence,
  status, and evidence references;
- at most sixteen recent intervention summaries and eight open questions;
- exact teacher, child, mechanism, lineage, and developmental-age identities.

The parent also receives the current round's bounded observations and a fixed,
manifest-bound set of teaching files/skills. Initial v5 has no general
retriever and no automatic replay of an unbounded transcript.

All events remain in a separate append-only audit log that the parent does not
automatically see. The parent proposes a notebook patch; deterministic schema,
cap, hash, and evidence-reference checks precede atomic promotion. Valid failed
lessons and child rollbacks can improve pedagogy. Infrastructure-invalid events
cannot.

Rohin, Codex, and Fable jointly develop the school: they propose curriculum,
skills, and teaching-policy revisions through the governed repository. The
strongest bound parent is the single online child-facing policy. Direct Rohin
interventions, if used during exploratory development, are source-tagged and
make the treatment explicitly team-authored. Any promoted external policy or
skill revision forks the scientific lineage; it is not silently spliced into a
growth curve.

## Development phase

Fork two sibling children from the same birth checkpoint:

- **taught child:** adaptive parent, curriculum, practice, reflection, and
  SLEEP;
- **matched non-parented child:** the same base, THINK/DREAM/SLEEP mechanism,
  tools, writer, task pool, budgets, saved ages, and final opportunities, but
  no parent during development.

The primary estimand is the **total adaptive-parenting package**, not correction
alone. A small yoked/sham-correction canary may diagnose one correction, but it
is not the headline.

One teaching unit is:

1. child independently attempts a novel practice task;
2. a public outcome exposes what happened;
3. the answer-aware parent uses the answer to diagnose but withholds a direct
   solution and gives a process intervention;
4. the child reasons, restates if useful, and acts on a fresh/isomorphic case;
5. every valid public action/outcome—success or failure—enters canonical
   evidence with a usefulness label;
6. SLEEP produces one candidate child write and compares it with the previous
   committed child before atomic commit or rollback;
7. the parent's bounded notebook advances once from all valid pedagogical
   outcomes.

Evidence is never filtered merely because teaching failed. Paraphrases,
replays, contrasts, scope statements, and connections retain their source IDs.

Parallel classrooms may gather short isolated experiences from one shared
child/parent snapshot. They make no local weight or canonical-parent updates.
The reducer pools the exact set-union of valid action/outcome evidence and makes
one cumulative child write. It never averages independently trained adapters.

## Developmental exams

At saved ages, run disposable child clones. Their task experiences never enter
the continuing child or SLEEP corpus. Only preregistered aggregate skill/error
codes may update the parent's notebook.

- immediate parent-absent absorption on a fresh homologous task;
- delayed retention after unrelated work and a context reset;
- acquisition speed on a genuinely fresh family;
- matched parent-present versus parent-absent responsiveness.

Repeated ages from one lineage are correlated observations, not independent
replicates. Every development panel becomes consumed selection data.

Writer certification uses identical seeded representative real contexts. A
candidate is compared with the previous child, not base. It may not reduce
required-action parseability or introduce a new illegal form; absorption,
retention, and no-material-harm checks also apply. The smallest rank passing
representative-scale certification wins, with ties going to the smaller rank.

## Graduation and freeze

Development ends under a preregistered budget/stopping rule. Then seal exact
hashes for both adults, THINK/DREAM/SLEEP, mature reusable pedagogy core,
teacher spec and parent-state transition, final task split/keys, budgets,
randomness, stopping, metrics, and analysis.

"Frozen" means immutable source states and update rules. Final root-local
parent and child updates may occur under those frozen rules, are discarded at
root completion, and never inform a later root or the mature seed.

## Final evaluation

Each independent root is one randomized block:

| adult | parent absent | parent present |
|---|---|---|
| matched non-parented adult | autonomous lifecycle | audited tutoring |
| adaptively taught adult | autonomous lifecycle | experiential learning plus audited tutoring |

Both parent-present cells receive the identical mature `pedagogy_core`, exact
parent spec, permissions, and hint/turn/token budgets, plus a fresh empty
root-local `child_record`. The treated child's private developmental history
never initializes the control's parent.

Measure separately:

- retained developmental effect on a solo entry deck;
- immediate tutoring before writes;
- entry-adjusted fresh-task acquisition AUC/time-to-threshold;
- autonomous lifetime improvement;
- optional retention after final tutoring and parent removal.

The parent is answer-aware but direct-answer-withholding. This does not prove
zero answer information: wording, timing, strategy, and curriculum can leak.
Require complete message records, identical permissions in parent-present
cells, fixed hint budgets, literal/compiler scans, counterfactual answer-swap
tests, and decoder/red-team audits. The defensible phrase is **audited
answer-aware tutoring**, not guaranteed answer-free process learning.

## Claim boundary

If the tests pass, v5 can support claims about:

- tutoring from the exact mature parent;
- retention from the total adaptive-development package;
- faster later acquisition and autonomous lifetime improvement for the exact
  saved adult(s).

One selected sibling pair supports conditional claims. General developmental
effectiveness requires multiple independently grown sibling pairs. The final
2x2 alone does not identify correction-only causality, SLEEP/LoRA/DREAM
necessity, connected knowledge, general intelligence, sentience, or asymptotic
self-improvement. A writer-specific claim requires an auxiliary matched
commit-on/commit-off fork with identical experience.

## Current evidence labels

- Old-writer behavioral changes and dialect bifurcation are engineering
  evidence motivating a paired real-context interface canary.
- Old R_B, two-panel enrichment, and incomplete rank comparisons are
  exploratory/noncausal.
- The 12-lesson parenting scout is directional only: unseeded, coarse, and
  parent=child confounded.
- Procedural discovery, universal exploration collapse, reassuring synthetic
  canaries, and "SLEEP reliably writes" remain retracted/unsupported.
- R2 writer lives are certification candidates; current classroom merge runs
  are engineering smoke until the governed implementation is re-audited.

## Minimum implementation delta after separate authorization

1. Separate exact parent and child backends.
2. Add the bounded validated notebook and audit-only event log.
3. Replace favorable episode admission with per-dispatch valid evidence and an
   exact cumulative canonical index.
4. Compile provenance-preserving dose; loss-mask parent text.
5. Compare candidate with the previous child using paired real-context
   canaries and atomic rollback.
6. Add sibling developmental lineages, disposable exams, pedagogy/private-state
   split, and a sealed final runner.
7. Complete model/tokenizer/mechanism/state/evidence/compiler/exam hashes.
