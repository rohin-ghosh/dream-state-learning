# PCFL-lite: paper-aligned constructive-memory calibration

**Status: NONAUTHORITATIVE SCIENTIFIC ADVISORY ONLY.** This document is not
an architecture decision, ratification, preregistration, implementation order,
model/provider-call approval, CPU/GPU authorization, LoRA-training approval, or
scientific result. It does not repair, ratify, or supersede the PCFL Active
Stream V1/V2 materials. Any implementation or model execution still requires
the repository's durable deliberation, exact human-approved scope, and the
applicable independent-review and GPU gates.

## Decision in one paragraph

Build PCFL-lite only as a short, falsifiable calibration of the missing
constructive chain: **lived public action/outcome experience -> target-blind
compiled local memory -> bounded recurrent THINK -> executed legal action**.
Use a meaningful field-lab/rescue world, whole-world held-out roots, a compact
information boundary, strong native baselines, and root-level reporting. Run
**text first, then LoRA conditionally**. The first stage establishes on
development roots that the world, compiler, reader, and recurrent thinker have
a non-shortcut action signal. Only if that gate passes should the exact frozen
corpus be realized both as bounded text and as a freshly reset per-life LoRA on
untouched test roots. This is more compute-efficient than launching every LoRA
arm immediately while preserving the eventual paired same-corpus comparison.

PCFL-lite is sufficient to decide whether Dream-LoRA-Think has a viable
experience-to-action mechanism worth scaling. It is not the headline lifetime
proof because its source experience is scripted, its controller is frozen, its
world is finite and synthetic, it has only an early/late bounded-context
calibration, and it does not show that better actions create better future
experience or that improvement continues after strong baselines saturate.

## 1. North star preserved; tested arrow narrowed

The full Dream-LoRA-Think lifetime goal is unchanged. The exact project
flywheel remains:

```text
action
  -> outcome-bearing experience
  -> recurrent dream: compress, connect, revise, preserve provenance
  -> persistent per-life experiential world approximation
  -> goal-conditioned retrieval and multi-hop planning
  -> better action
  -> better and more informative future experience
  -> repeat
```

PCFL-lite tests only the central constructive pass under common experience:

```text
scripted ordinary public actions and outcomes
  -> target-blind local semantic/causal claims
  -> chronological support or contradiction from later public outcomes
  -> deterministic multi-view compilation
  -> bounded text memory or freshly reset per-life MEMORY LoRA
  -> recurrent goal-conditioned local reads and workspace updates
  -> executed legal multi-step action and public outcome
```

It deliberately holds the source action stream fixed. Therefore it can show
that authentic life memory changes later action, but not the final two arrows
of the flywheel: memory-improved exploration, improved subsequent experience,
reconsolidation, and repeated developmental gain.

The narrow calibration question is:

> Given the same target-blind public life, frozen base, frozen writer/compiler,
> frozen bounded reader, and frozen recurrent controller, does supported
> compiled memory improve novel A2/A3 legal action over no memory, honest
> context, iterative raw RAG, organized linked memory, and direct-QA LoRA; does
> the identical compiled corpus preserve its action value when carried by a
> freshly reset per-life LoRA; and do binding shuffle and wrong-life assignment
> remove the gain?

The eligible positive conclusion is correspondingly narrow:

> In the registered PCFL-lite world family, memory compiled solely from one
> scripted public action/outcome life changed held-out multi-step legal action
> under a bounded recurrent reader/controller, survived a matched text-to-LoRA
> substrate comparison, and failed under registered misbinding controls.

No result from this calibration licenses “keeps improving with lifetime,”
“online self-improvement,” “baseline saturation,” “learned dreaming,” “learned
agency,” “LoRA superiority,” “formal mediation,” or external-generalization
language.

## 2. Minimal meaningful semantic action world

Use a compact field-lab/rescue life whose ontology is familiar enough for the
base model to reason about actions, but whose local causal assignments must be
learned from this life's outcomes.

Each world root samples a coherent local ecology with:

- specimens/materials with visible traits such as porous, brittle, conductive,
  reactive, insulated, wet, or oxygen-sensitive;
- sites with hazards such as acid mist, heat, flood, electrical exposure, low
  oxygen, and unstable terrain;
- preparation families such as seal, dry, insulate, reinforce, neutralize, and
  ventilate, each with a stable world-local effect;
- site acceptance rules and route preconditions requiring combinations of
  visible or prepared traits; and
- paired counterfactual variants in which a plausible batch/formulation or
  local site law differs while names, descriptions, action counts, and outcome
  marginals remain matched.

The semantics constrain which questions and actions make sense; they do not
reveal the correct local mapping. For example, “seal” may plausibly protect one
material from acid while trapping heat in another. The operative relationship
must be inferred from witnessed preparation and intervention outcomes, not from
an arbitrary cipher or from pretrained common sense alone.

The legal action interface is small and consequential:

```text
SURVEY(site)
ASSAY(specimen, trait_or_condition)
PREPARE(specimen, preparation)
TRAVERSE(route, specimen)
INTERVENE(specimen, site)
COMMIT(mission)
```

Actions have costs and observable outcomes. Unsafe traversal or intervention
may irreversibly fail an item. `COMMIT` succeeds only after the required world
state has actually been reached; it is not a QA answer field.

Source episodes use fresh entities and missions. Held-out targets use new
entities, state/goal combinations, and action sequences. No source episode may
contain a target state-goal pair or complete target plan.

The two scored capability families are:

- **A2 composition:** combine a preparation effect and a site rule learned in
  different source clusters to choose and execute a novel intervention.
- **A3 planning:** combine two preparation effects with a route precondition
  and site rule in a four-to-six-action execution, with opportunity to
  backtrack after an ordinary public outcome.

A0 witnessed-transition recall and A1 near transfer are retained only as
installation diagnostics. They are never pooled into the primary action
endpoint.

### Construct validity gates

Before model science, an independently implemented exact world oracle and an
exact finite-horizon no-lifetime controller must establish the following on a
fixed CPU qualification suite, not by curating future test roots:

1. hidden-graph/exact-memory recurrent action value is at least `0.85`;
2. an exact no-lifetime adaptive controller is at most `0.35` on A2/A3;
3. oracle local atoms plus recurrent reads are at least `0.85`, with valid
   constructive traces at least `0.80`;
4. the same oracle atoms with every query committed before any read are at most
   `0.35`;
5. no legitimate record or single reader return directly closes a target;
6. authentic exact memory gains at least `0.30` over no lifetime memory, and
   binding shuffle and counterfactual/wrong-life assignment each remove at
   least half that gain and at least `0.15` absolute;
7. every target needs roots from at least two source clusters, and each A3
   target needs a third source block; and
8. the simulator is deterministic under replay and evaluation cannot mutate
   the source life.

Failure invalidates this generator version. It is not repaired by a larger
model, more ranks, more roots, or a weaker threshold.

## 3. Whole-world split and calibration lifetime

The split unit is the **whole world root**, not an episode, target, rendered
skin, or row. A root binds its hidden local laws, counterfactual twin relation,
entity pools, renderer family, source schedule, target generator, and RNG
namespace.

Freeze before any model call:

- `CPU-QUAL`: 32 counterfactual twin pairs used only for deterministic
  generator/oracle/shortcut qualification;
- `DEV`: 4 new twin pairs / 8 world roots, used for prompt, compiler, reader,
  baseline, and LoRA-installation choices; and
- `TEST`: 8 new twin pairs / 16 untouched world roots, used once for the
  complete matched calibration.

No target, entity pool, renderer template, hidden mapping, or source episode
crosses DEV/TEST. Renderer families and surface lexicons are disjoint as well
as RNG seeds. A structurally invalid TEST root invalidates the frozen generator
release; it is not replaced after any model output is observed.

Use two source-life checkpoints:

```text
L_early = 0.5C
L_late  = 2C
```

`C` is the exact disclosed per-decision working/input-and-memory budget after
fixed prompt, state, workspace, output reserve, and operation history. Every
late target needs one decisive root whose last support is more than `C` source
tokens old and one recent decisive root; A3 also needs a third independently
located source block. Both intervals add new causal coverage rather than
filler.

Checkpoint age is computed from realized canonical public-event payload bytes,
not requested generation length, wrapper text, idle calls, padding, or compiler
views. The source cadence and maximum envelope are frozen, but no arm receives
synthetic filler to reach a cut. `OLD`/`RECENT` target membership is assigned
from the sealed source schedule before memory quality is known; a target stays
in its denominator even when the writer fails to acquire its required atom.

This is explicitly a **calibration-only bounded-context axis**. `2C` is not
called post-native-context unless it actually exceeds the pinned model's
effective native input capacity after all reserves. The honest context arm
receives the full chronological transcript up to that real native limit and
receives the entire life if it fits. Therefore the two points diagnose early
versus delayed constructive use but cannot establish a lifetime curve,
crossover, saturation, or continuing acquisition. Those claims require a
fresh PCFL-Stream-style study with increasing unique causal cohorts and at
least three strictly post-native checkpoints.

At each checkpoint, evaluation starts from a fresh item process, mounts a
read-only snapshot, clears context/KV/retrieval state, and seals evaluation
outcomes away from the source life. Only `L_late` enters the primary calibration
decision; `L_early` diagnoses whether an apparent gain is merely early context
conditioning.

## 4. Compact information boundary

The design needs a small explicit boundary around conclusion-changing
channels, not an exhaustive component ontology.

| Principal | May read | May write | Must never receive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source policy/environment | current public state and legal actions | ordinary public action/observation/outcome events | held-out targets, arm identity, memory, scores |
| DREAM writer | eligible source-prefix public events and previously supported local claims | target-independent one-edge proposals with cited public roots and a future-testable prediction | evaluation goals/outcomes, hidden truth, scorer fields, target allocation, full plans |
| Chronology checker | committed proposal and later ordinary public outcome | support/contradiction status only | ability to propose, rewrite, rank, or repair claims |
| SLEEP compiler | supported canonical local claims | deterministic views and corpus manifest | hidden truth, evaluation data, target-conditioned examples, new semantic claims |
| Memory backend | assigned frozen corpus/snapshot and local query | one local atom or `NOT_FOUND` plus audit receipt | goal, hidden target, scorer, other-life state, global answer candidates |
| Recurrent THINK | public goal/state, bounded workspace, last local read, ordinary action outcome, remaining budgets | query/update/backtrack/action/defer/stop operation | source transcript except through its assigned arm, truth/proof graph, evaluator, target answer |
| Environment executor | one typed legal action | ordinary next public state/outcome | memory content, hidden rationale, scorer decision |
| Offline scorer/auditor | sealed trace, hidden world, frozen assignments | action scores and audit reports | any channel back into cognition or later source memory |

Only ordinary public source events and chronologically supported local claims
may enter positive memory. Provisional and contradicted claims remain visible
to audit but outside positive text/LoRA data. Paraphrases and multi-view rows
inherit their roots and never count as extra evidence.

The common reader returns at most one local relation per read. A decision is
bounded to six reads, twelve resolver operations, and six environment actions.
Later queries may use values returned by earlier reads; this adaptive
dependency is the intended constructive mechanism. A one-shot full-chain or
plan return is forbidden in every primary arm.

The scorer may inspect hidden truth only after the action trace is sealed.
Evaluation outcomes cannot update the writer, compiler, text store, adapter,
retrieval index, controller, or later item.

Reset is stated modestly and testably: create a new per-life adapter, corpus
view, optimizer, retrieval index, context/KV cache, RNG namespace, temp
directory, and worker process; revoke old handles; tear down the old worker;
and verify prior-life canaries are unreachable through declared interfaces.
This is logical isolation and non-reachability within the declared runtime,
not a claim of universal physical destruction or remote-provider erasure.

## 5. Conditions and fair budgets

Every primary condition uses the same frozen base/controller, source life,
public target, legal action cap, resolver-operation cap, and per-decision
input/output budget. Native systems keep their legitimate interfaces; resource
differences are reported rather than hidden by artificial padding.

| ID | Condition | Scientific role |
|---|---|---|
| `NOMEM` | no persistent life memory; within-item scratch only | frozen-agent floor |
| `CTX` | honest chronological context up to the pinned native limit | strongest context control |
| `RAW-RAG` | iterative retrieval over raw public source episodes | strong episodic control |
| `LINKED` | frozen organized linked memory built independently from raw public episodes | A-MEM/graph-style external-memory control |
| `DIRECT-LORA` | direct trajectory-to-atomic-QA per-life LoRA from public source events | matched direct-write parametric control |
| `E-TEXT` | supported compiled local claims through the common bounded reader | compiled-text mechanism |
| `E-LORA` | the identical supported compiled corpus/views in a fresh per-life LoRA | same-corpus substrate transport |
| `BIND-TEXT`, `BIND-LORA` | fixed-point-free within-type binding permutation of the frozen E corpus | content-binding falsifier |
| `WRONG-TEXT`, `WRONG-LORA` | authentic memory from the counterfactual twin or another root | life-assignment falsifier |
| `ORACLE-OPEN`, `ORACLE-REC` | same exact target-independent atoms with nonadaptive versus recurrent reads | construct negative/ceiling |

`E-TEXT` and `E-LORA` use exactly the same canonical supported atom set and
the same deterministic forward/reverse/paraphrase/incident-edge realizations.
The text backend uses those atoms directly; the LoRA backend trains only on
those views and reads through the same one-atom result schema. Equality is at
the admitted semantic corpus and visible reader contract, not at bytes,
capacity, or compute. Every LoRA is rebuilt from the same clean base for one
world-life and checkpoint; adapters are never merged across lives or promoted
into the base.

`DIRECT-LORA` is not weakened to raw next-token replay. It receives a faithful
direct action/outcome-to-atomic-QA compilation from the same public life under
the same rank, target modules, optimizer family, training tokens, steps, and
seed manifest as `E-LORA`. Any unavoidable corpus-length or compute difference
is disclosed.

`RAW-RAG` is iterative: each new public/read value may drive the next retrieval
within the same six-read budget. `LINKED` retains its legitimate linked-memory
construction and native retrieval behavior in its primary score; an optional
common-reader projection is diagnostic and cannot replace the native baseline.

Report, per root/arm/checkpoint: environment actions, source tokens, active and
retained memory bytes, reader calls/returned tokens, resolver input/output
tokens, writer/compiler calls and tokens, adapter parameters/rank/training
examples/steps/FLOPs, inference latency, failures, and wall time. Do not claim
fixed-memory or fixed-compute equality across different substrates.

## 6. Shortcut and path audits

Freeze the following finite audits on DEV before opening TEST. They bound the
registered routes; they do not prove absence of every pretrained shortcut.

### Target/current/render shortcuts

1. **Target-only:** goal text and goal fields, with state and life removed.
2. **Current-state-only:** current visible state and legal menu, with target
   wording, history, and memory removed except the minimum objective type.
3. **Renderer-signature-only:** template ID, token/character lengths, field
   order, punctuation, and position features, with semantic values masked.
4. **Target + current state:** no experience, memory, or prior action outcomes.
5. **Nonadaptive atom/action prior:** action-frequency and fixed-query policies
   that cannot condition later choices on returned values.

Use a small frozen family of exact tables, regularized linear/n-gram models,
and shallow trees trained only on DEV roots. Score them without refitting on
TEST. Also rerender every TEST target through two frozen, semantically
equivalent renderer families and one handle/label renaming. Correct actions are
invariant across renders.

A generator is CPU-eligible only if qualification-suite shortcut policies stay
at or below `0.35` normalized A2/A3 action value. A TEST shortcut above the
registered threshold is reported and invalidates the unqualified constructive
claim; the root is not replaced. Material render sensitivity earns an explicit
`RENDERER-BOUNDED` limitation even if average action is positive.

### Memory-dependent path audit

For every decisive action, record which returned local atoms were copied into
the bounded workspace and which public/action arguments depended on them. In a
deterministic replay diagnostic, mask the cited decisive atoms and separately
replace them with type-matched twin atoms. A constructive trace requires:

- at least two provenance-distinct returned atoms before the decisive A2
  action and at least three for A3;
- at least one later query whose anchor came from an earlier read or ordinary
  public outcome;
- masking to alter the decisive operation/action; and
- twin substitution to shift toward the twin-valid operation/action.

This is a finite counterfactual path-use audit. It is **not** a formal mediation
analysis and does not identify an indirect causal estimand. A correct action
that fails the audit remains behavioral success but receives no
constructive-memory-path credit.

## 7. Action endpoints

The primary endpoint is behavioral, never QA accuracy, reader fidelity, a
claim status, citation presence, or compiler acceptance.

For each TEST root at `L_late`, calculate separately for A2 and A3:

- legal executed mission success by the six-action cap;
- normalized terminal return, with illegal/malformed/defer/irreversible
  failure/cap exhaustion scored zero;
- actions-to-success and regret relative to the root's minimal legal plan,
  censored at six for failures; and
- recovery after a failed intermediate action or backtrack opportunity.

The co-primary calibration summaries are equal-weight mean A2/A3 normalized
action value and success by the cap. Secondary mechanism measures are fixed
read accuracy, adaptive-query rate, valid dependency depth, constructive trace
rate, false-memory use, and text-to-LoRA transport loss. Mechanism measures can
explain a failure mode but cannot substitute for action gain.

## 8. Root-level statistics and decision thresholds

The independent observation is a whole world root; the counterfactual twin
pair is the clustering/randomization unit. Targets, actions, checkpoints,
decoding seeds, reader calls, and adapter seeds are repeated measurements, not
replicates.

Within each root:

1. average all preassigned targets inside A2 and inside A3;
2. average A2 and A3 equally for the root score;
3. average paired adapter seeds inside the root if two are used; and
4. average the two twins only when forming the pair-level contrast.

Report every root and target denominator, including zero-valued scientific
failures. Show root-wise trajectories, the eight pair differences, their mean
and median, and a descriptive 95% paired cluster-bootstrap interval. An exact
sign-flip/randomization interval over the eight frozen pairs may be added, but
no target-level p-value or population claim is eligible. Infrastructure loss
before any scientific dispatch may rerun the identical sealed cell; a partial
scientific cell remains in the denominator.

Define on DEV, before TEST, the strongest explicit/context baseline identity
`B_EXT` from `CTX`, `RAW-RAG`, and `LINKED`; freeze that identity rather than
selecting a favorable comparator on TEST.

A **favorable calibration**, not a paper confirmation, requires all of:

1. `E-TEXT - B_EXT >= 0.10` mean action value at `L_late` on TEST;
2. `E-LORA - B_EXT >= 0.10` and `E-LORA - DIRECT-LORA >= 0.10` mean action
   value;
3. at least six of eight twin-pair differences have the favorable sign for
   both comparisons in item 2;
4. `E-LORA >= E-TEXT - 0.10`, fixed-read fidelity is at least `0.90`, and
   adapter-seed root-mean-square score spread is at most `0.10`;
5. for each E substrate, binding shuffle and wrong-life assignment reduce its
   gain over `NOMEM` by at least half and at least `0.15` absolute;
6. oracle recurrence, direct-record closure, shortcut, reset, target embargo,
   and evaluation-sterility gates all pass; and
7. the mean constructive-path rate is at least `0.70`, with the oracle
   recurrent arm at least `0.80`.

These are practical go/no-go thresholds for a small calibration. They are not
confidence-bound claims and must not be back-translated into powered evidence.

## 9. Compute-aware two-stage scientific execution

### Stage T — text construct gate on DEV only

Run `NOMEM`, `CTX`, `RAW-RAG`, `LINKED`, `E-TEXT`, `BIND-TEXT`, `WRONG-TEXT`,
`ORACLE-OPEN`, and `ORACLE-REC` on the four DEV twin pairs at `L_early` and
`L_late`. This stage may tune only predeclared DEV-eligible prompt/parser,
retrieval, compiler, and renderer choices. Freeze exact bytes afterward.

Advance only if:

- the CPU construct gates and all information/reset tests pass;
- `ORACLE-REC` works while `ORACLE-OPEN` stays near floor;
- `E-TEXT` exceeds both `RAW-RAG` and `LINKED` by at least `0.10` mean DEV
  action value at `L_late` and is no worse than `CTX` by more than `0.10`;
- binding and wrong-life controls remove at least half the E-text gain over
  `NOMEM`; and
- target/current/render audits remain below threshold.

If Stage T fails, stop. Do not train LoRA to rescue the world, reader, writer,
or thinker. Preserve the negative result and revise the construct only under a
new design/split.

### Stage L — matched text/LoRA calibration on untouched TEST

After Stage T freezes, use DEV only for a small installation sweep, preferably
ranks `{8, 32}` with one training seed, selecting the smallest rank that reaches
`0.90` fixed-read fidelity. Rank is treated as capacity, not a timescale. Do
not introduce a rank pyramid, adapter merge, weight promotion, or base update.

Then run the complete mandatory roster on the eight untouched TEST twin pairs.
Evaluate `E-TEXT` and `E-LORA` in the same frozen release with identical
canonical claims, compiler views, item assignments, and visible reader
contract. Use two paired adapter seeds only if the budget allows; average them
inside roots. Run `DIRECT-LORA`, `BIND-LORA`, and `WRONG-LORA` under the frozen
training envelope. Open the full TEST package only after all assigned cells are
sealed.

Thus the answer to “text-only first or matched text/LoRA immediately?” is:
**text-only first, then a predeclared matched text/LoRA test**. Same-run
execution from the outset wastes the expensive part when the cheap construct
may be invalid. Sequential gating does not weaken the substrate comparison
because TEST roots, corpora, hyperparameter rule, arms, thresholds, and
statistics are frozen before Stage L, and Stage T never sees TEST.

## 10. Negative-result decision table

| Observation | Decision | Maximum honest interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| CPU oracle, no-lifetime headroom, closure, or deterministic replay fails | Stop before model calls; reject this world version | Instrument invalid |
| Target-only/current-state/renderer/nonadaptive policy clears its threshold | Stop or label the registered construct invalid; do not replace TEST roots | Shortcut route remains |
| `ORACLE-OPEN` approaches `ORACLE-REC`, or recurrent oracle cannot produce valid paths | Stop before LoRA | Task does not identify adaptive reconstruction |
| `E-TEXT` does not beat iterative `RAW-RAG` and native `LINKED` on DEV | Stop before LoRA | Compiler/structured-memory advantage unsupported |
| `E-TEXT` works but render variants are unstable | Continue only as explicitly renderer-bounded calibration | Semantic generality unsupported |
| Authentic text gain survives binding shuffle or wrong-life assignment | Invalidate experiential-content attribution | Prompt, prior, or leakage may explain action |
| `E-LORA` fixed-read fidelity is below `0.90` | Report transport failure; retain text result only | Per-life LoRA did not faithfully carry the corpus |
| `E-LORA` is within the transport margin but does not beat `DIRECT-LORA` | Delete compilation advantage in weights | Direct parametric write suffices at this scale |
| `E-LORA` does not beat `E-TEXT` | Do not claim LoRA superiority; a tie within margin is transport only | Explicit text is at least as good here |
| `E-TEXT`/`E-LORA` beat memoryless arms but not honest context | Retain bounded mechanism result only | No context-overflow or scaling advantage |
| Action improves but memory masking/twin substitution does not change the decisive path | Report behavioral association only | Constructive memory-path credit unsupported |
| Reads improve but legal action/return does not | Installation result only | Memory is not behaviorally useful |
| Any target/scorer/evaluation descendant enters cognition or training, or reset canary survives | Invalidate affected release; performance cannot rehabilitate it | Contaminated result |
| Favorable TEST means arise from fewer than six of eight pairs or one renderer/ordering | Report heterogeneous exploratory result | No stable calibration pass |
| All gates pass | Request a new, larger lifetime/on-policy design on untouched roots | Viable mechanism, not headline proof |

## 11. Traces preserved for future LOOP training

Preserve successful, failed, malformed, redundant, backtracked, and deferred
trajectories. The reusable training projection is:

```text
PUBLIC_STATE
  -> ASSISTANT_OPERATION
  -> MEMORY_RETURN or ENVIRONMENT_RESULT
  -> NEXT_PUBLIC_STATE
```

For each transition retain immutable byte lineage for world-root split,
checkpoint, arm, public goal/state, legal menu, bounded workspace projection,
last reader return, operation/query/action, public result, budgets, parser
status, and terminal return. Separately retain audit-only provenance, hidden
world score, failure classification, and resource receipts.

A future LOOP corpus may train only the legal operation and public-argument
tokens. Hidden mappings, proof graphs, target allocations, evaluator/scorer
fields, future outcomes, chain-of-thought rationales, post-hoc explanations,
backend identity, and audit-only provenance must be removed from model-visible
inputs and targets. Failure traces remain available for target-blind selection
or negative examples; successful traces are not automatically authoritative.
No LOOP adapter is trained in PCFL-lite.

## 12. Candidate bounded CPU implementation scope

The following is a **requested scope candidate**, not permission to act. After
fresh deliberation and exact human ratification, a CPU-only implementation
scope could be limited to:

1. deterministic PCFL-lite generator, counterfactual twins, source policy,
   public renderer variants, target allocator, and legal-action simulator;
2. whole-world `CPU-QUAL`/`DEV`/`TEST` manifest generation with disjointness and
   no-replacement checks;
3. exact hidden-graph and finite-horizon no-lifetime controllers, target
   minimality/uniqueness, direct-closure, chronology, and coverage checks;
4. compact public-event, claim/status, corpus, local-query/read, operation,
   action, trace, score, reset, and resource schemas;
5. deterministic support checker and multi-view compiler fixtures using only
   synthetic/mock proposals—no model-generated claims;
6. mechanical oracle, no-memory, honest-context serialization, raw-retrieval,
   linked-memory, bind-shuffle, and wrong-life backend fixtures;
7. target/current/render probe harnesses and paired rerender/rename audits;
8. logical per-life reset, canary, source/evaluation write-denial, and
   pre/post-state-hash tests;
9. root/pair-level scoring, failure inclusion, bootstrap/sign-flip utilities,
   resource ledgers, and the negative-decision report; and
10. property tests over deterministic replay, twin collisions, hidden/public
    separation, target embargo, query/action bounds, evaluator sterility,
    malformed input, and trace lineage.

That CPU scope must explicitly forbid model/provider calls, network model
downloads, GPU/CUDA execution, LoRA training, adapter loading, scientific DEV
or TEST dispatch, rank selection, publication claims, on-policy source lives,
LOOP training, Stage C/D successors, weight merging, and base updates. Its
completion would establish only that the instrument and schemas satisfy their
mechanical contracts. A separate reviewed and human-bound GPU/model scope would
be required first for Stage T and again for Stage L.

## 13. Machinery intentionally omitted

PCFL-lite does not require the V3 cryptographic/governance superstructure. In
particular it omits:

- an exhaustive field-by-component ontology or proof of trace-total closure;
- a brokered nonreifiable-capability fork and universal destruction claim;
- token-position common-random-number proofs or equality of provider draws;
- formal mediation/indirect-effect terminology;
- an exhaustive terminal state machine, post-result selector, or dual
  alternative-system roster;
- a full Pareto-dominance calculus over partially observed resource vectors;
- a rank pyramid, weight merge, base promotion, or automatic successor route.

Deterministic decoding should be used where feasible. For stochastic writer or
training operations, pair frozen seeds and record exact requests/configuration,
but claim only paired seeds/configuration—not token-level common randomness.
Compact manifests, hashes, canaries, target embargo tests, and full traces are
enough to audit the few information paths that can change the scientific
conclusion.

## 14. Why this is sufficient—and why it is not the headline proof

This design is sufficient as a calibration because it puts the scientific
risk in the right order. First, a meaningful legal-action world must demand
cross-episode structure and adaptive recurrence. Second, a target-blind
compiled-text system must beat honest raw and organized external alternatives.
Third, authentic bindings must matter. Only then is it worth asking whether the
same corpus survives lossy parametric transport. The controls distinguish a
real experience-conditioned action signal from stored target answers,
current-state decoding, renderer artifacts, generic policy priors, ordinary
long context, raw retrieval, linked organization, and direct-QA adaptation.

It is not the Dream-LoRA-Think headline lifetime proof because it lacks:

- policy-selected information gathering and the better-action ->
  better-experience feedback arrow;
- recurrent reconsolidation of outcomes caused by the agent's own memory;
- a learned cross-life THINK/DREAM/LOOP policy;
- increasing unique causal coverage across at least three post-native-context
  lifetime points;
- empirical evidence that strong baselines actually flatten or degrade;
- revision/retention curves over meaningful rule change; and
