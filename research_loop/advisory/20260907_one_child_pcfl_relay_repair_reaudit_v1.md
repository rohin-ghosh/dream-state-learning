# One-child PCFL relay causal-repair re-audit v1

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: fresh-context, independent, proposal-only re-audit. This advisory
authorizes no source change, implementation, target or root generation,
model/tokenizer call, benchmark or adapter operation, external access, GPU use,
or scientific claim.

## Verdict

**REVISE.** Note 54 repairs the high-level causal graph of the attacked relay,
but it does not yet provide an executable identification contract. It correctly
keeps exactly one fixed parent-deleted child, treats roots as environment trials
of clones of that child, restores the end-of-A state after the Phase-B fork,
branches the controlled D write comparison after a common authentic Phase-C
action/outcome, separates authentic links from matched link derangement, and
uses ordered root-level gates rather than the old selected maximum. Those are
material repairs.

The proposal nevertheless leaves unresolved how authentic links are acquired,
how arm-specific text or LoRA carriers relate to the supposedly single `M_A`,
how the prospective Phase-C event compiles to exactly one truthful row, how all
hidden reader and post-C environmental carriers are closed, how the
`REACHOUT_OFF` path enters D, and what exact scores and powered tests release a
claim. The present prose can therefore be implemented in causally inequivalent
ways, some of which would pass its named gates without establishing connected
experiential memory, goal-conditioned traversal, or new-write causality.

This is a repairable design defect, not a rejection of the one-child relay.
Do not ratify or execute note 54 as the relay specification in its present
bytes.

## Binding scope disposition

The topology is **PASS** as a scope statement:

- one fixed terminal child is selected by a prospectively fixed rule;
- the one parent and all nursery state are deleted before relay entry;
- no parent, second child, classroom, cohort, peer, ensemble, shared memory, or
  cross-root learning edge exists;
- pair roots are independent environment trials of byte-identical clones of
  that same child; and
- inference is conditional on that one checkpoint and the registered
  environment distribution.

`WRONG_LIFE`, if retained, must mean an isolated carrier from another sealed
environment root of the same fixed child, mounted only as a negative control.
It is not another learner and creates no cross-root communication or population
claim. Relay execution occurs only after the parenting headline and the
parenting result are immutable. If the protocol, generator, root identities,
analysis, or child-selection rule was chosen after that headline was unblinded,
the relay is exploratory and conditional; note 54 freezes only the
child-selection rule explicitly at that boundary, which is insufficient for a
confirmatory label.

## Remaining blockers and exact repairs

### R1 — freeze the whole post-parenting assay, not only child selection

“Before any scientific model output” is ambiguous in a relay designed after a
parenting headline. Introduce explicit times: parenting protocol lock,
parenting unblind, relay protocol lock, `A0`, end of A, B fork, C action/outcome
seal, D write seal, and D goal reveal. For a confirmatory relay, before
parenting unblinding freeze the child-selection function, relay/root generator,
source-opportunity distribution, target and intervention algorithms, candidate
universe, score functions, analysis, and RNG commitment. The eventual selected
child bytes can be hashed only after they exist, but their selection rule may
not change. A design or root rule created after the headline must be labeled
exploratory; later pre-A sealing does not make it confirmatory.

For each relay root, seal all root bytes before `A0` without reading the child
checkpoint's behavior, logits, stored rows, adapter reads, parenting arm
metrics, or relay feasibility. Record a no-replacement intention-to-treat
receipt. This completes the repair of post-treatment target selection.

### R2 — distinguish eligible relations from realized Phase-A state

Note 54 simultaneously seals an “authentic link set” before output and admits
links only between atoms actually supported at the end of A. Those are not the
same object. Freeze an eligibility graph `E_A` from hidden truth plus the public
source-opportunity tape. After A, apply one deterministic, target-blind
admission/projection function to the realized public event ledger to obtain the
atom deck `S_A` and link deck `L_A`; the function may not inspect any B/C/D
goal, target proof, score, or adapter behavior.

Predeclare what happens when required atoms are absent or the realized induced
graph has no fixed-point-free matched derangement. The root must receive its
registered failure value in every affected endpoint; it may not be regenerated,
restratified, relaxed, or replaced. A presealed permutation over eligible slots
is acceptable only if its deterministic projection preserves every declared
match on the realized deck; otherwise that root is a failure. Report atomic
proposal precision, admission precision, retention/read fidelity, and a
prospectively fixed atomic floor before connectedness can release.

### R3 — make links experiential rather than hidden-world gifts

Atoms have public-event provenance, but `USE_LINK` does not. A correct link
inserted by a hidden-world generator can make `LINK_AUTH` beat derangement even
though the child never learned a connection. Freeze an exact link schema,
proposal source, public-event citations, chronology rule, and deterministic
admission predicate. Each authentic link must be either:

1. proposed by the child from its own pretarget Phase-A public experience and
   supported by a prospectively declared co-use event; or
2. mechanically compiled from such public events by a target-blind rule whose
   output is fixed before B/C/D targets are visible to every writer and reader.

If links instead come from hidden truth or benchmark authors, the maximum claim
is that the fixed child used **supplied relational adjacency over experiential
atoms**, not that it learned or carried connected experience. Ban links whose
single row or joint incidence pattern determines a held-out goal, terminal
action, target family, path order, or success. Apply direct-record closure to
joint row sets and interface behavior, not only individual text fields.

### R4 — define the common state and arm-specific carrier construction

One literal `M_A` cannot both contain the authentic LoRA and serve as the common
state for `LINK_AUTH`, `LINK_DERANGED`, `ATOMS_ONLY`, and `SHAM_SIGNAL` carrier
assignments. Split it into:

```text
A_CORE = fixed child base parameters + allowed target-blind deployment state
A_EVENTS = immutable public Phase-A provenance ledger (audit-only after build)
S_A, L_A = deterministically admitted semantic decks
CARRIER(a) = clean-base build from the deck assigned to arm a
```

Freeze and hash `A_CORE` before building any arm. Rebuild every text or LoRA
carrier from that common base using coupled, presealed build randomness and the
registered corpus/envelope. Never mutate an authentic `M_A` in place to create
a deranged control. Define separately which AUTH carrier is restored for Phase
C. For LoRA, adapter mounting must affect only READ; the resolver and action
policy remain the same clean frozen components. For explicit text, use the same
one-hop READ contract. This makes carrier contrasts interpretable and makes B
rollback testable.

### R5 — make connectedness and traversal observable, separate endpoints

Define `connected_value` and `traversal_value` mechanically. Connectedness must
require a model-selected, correctly bound multi-relation return from the
assigned carrier, an authentic absolute-performance floor, preserved atomic
fidelity, and failure of all single-row/direct-answer closures. It must not be a
renamed final task score. `LINK_AUTH - LINK_DERANGED` then tests authentic link
binding; `LINK_AUTH - ATOMS_ONLY` remains only a lower-resource diagnostic
unless an exposure/resource-matched null-link carrier is added.

Traversal must require, before the decisive action, a fully public typed trace
whose selected query/candidate IDs, returned rows, dependency edges, and
timestamps validate without semantic repair. Register two goal-specific
relevant path sets over the identical visible start and carrier. Define
root-level goal contrasts for first query and complete selected path, with a
nontrivial divergence margin. Both goals must clear action and path floors;
merely changing a first query before executing the same exhaustive suffix is a
failure. Cut masking must change the decisive operation and a type-matched twin
must redirect it to the twin-valid action, not just reduce success.

The current `T_r = LINK_AUTH - BRIDGE_SUB` does not itself test goal
conditioning. Add the registered goal-path contrast and exhaustive-controller
ceiling as conjunctive root-level components of `T`, or give them separate
ordered hypotheses. No prose-only diagnostic may license the traversal claim.

### R6 — close exhaustive and hidden reader routes over the whole interface

A single target-blind “exhaustive controller” is not a falsification class.
For the finite PCFL interface, either enumerate all legal target-blind
nonadaptive query schedules within the resource cap or prove a coverage bound
and run the strongest schedule selected without confirmation outcomes. Count
reader-internal candidate scans, semantic searches, retries, batched reads, and
resolver calls against the same cap; an apparently one-hop call may not hide an
unmetered exhaustive scan.

Candidate membership, catalog, index, order, padding, ranks, lengths, IDs,
latency classes, miss patterns, and provenance affordances must be identical
across paired goals, target/twin worlds, and authentic/control arms except for
the declared semantic substitution. “Presealed” and “target-blind at runtime”
are insufficient because a generator that knows the jointly presealed target
can construct an answer-bearing candidate universe. Exhaustively test single
features and combinations against the oracle action, and require goal-only,
state-only, identifier-only, passive-signature, candidate-only clean-base,
source-action-string, unaided generative-read, adapter-off, and wrong-life
controllers to remain below fixed ceilings.

The reader/shortlister must expose the exact candidate list to the model and
copy only an explicitly selected ID. It may perform syntactic lookup but no
semantic ranking, path completion, relevance validation, fallback generation,
or malformed-trace repair. Hash code and configuration, not just returned
bytes, and audit timing/retry behavior under both hits and misses.

### R7 — make the Phase-C sham a valid controlled intervention

Specify `SHAM_SIGNAL` as a read-time or carrier-build substitution activated
only at the C boundary. It must preserve truthful marginal rows, record and
link counts, endpoint types, degrees, ages, tokens, candidates, queries, public
state, legal action menu, posterior support, and resources. It must not inject
a false fact, reveal the arm, make the registered experiment illegal, or change
the hidden-world answer. A prospectively chosen true irrelevant relation or a
counterfactual-twin construction with an explicit branch-specific oracle is
acceptable; an unspecified “irrelevant/twin signal” is not.

Define separate scores for (a) emitting a live supported gap and two hypotheses,
(b) selecting a legal registered separating experiment, and (c) target-relevant
expected information gain under the exact actor-public posterior. The offline
posterior may score only after the action seal and may condition on no hidden
provenance, writer status, target-selection success, or unobserved source
events. AUTH must clear absolute floors and AUTH-minus-SHAM must clear the
registered effect margins for experiment selection and relevant information
gain separately.

### R8 — compile the new row prospectively and truthfully

Naming a “claim family” before the outcome does not uniquely determine a
training row. Freeze a canonical typed proposal containing the relation schema,
endpoints, permissible value domain, cited old-memory premises, chosen
experiment, and one candidate output row for every possible public outcome.
After the outcome, a deterministic compiler may select exactly the
precommitted row and attach the public event citation; it may not paraphrase,
infer a new family, choose endpoints, use DREAM prose generated after the
outcome, or inspect D. Specify whether PCFL is deterministic or what posterior
support threshold makes one observation sufficient. Unsupported, ambiguous,
or out-of-domain outcomes receive the registered failure/no-admission value.

`EDGE_WRITE` must contain exactly this one truthful reusable relation. The
writer receives only the canonical row and allowed pre-C rehearsal rows, never
the C goal, hypotheses, full action trace, terminal policy, raw outcome prose,
or D bytes. Byte-audit the supervised target and response-only mask.

The proposed sham and binding controls also need truthful mechanics.
`SHAM_WRITE` must be a predeclared, genuinely supported, type/token/candidate-
matched irrelevant row, or else be explicitly classified as a synthetic
corruption outside normal admission. `NEW_BIND_SWAP` should preferably permute
endpoints at read time on an otherwise byte-identical authentic carrier. If it
is trained as a false positive row, the normal writer/admission path must not be
said to have passed writer truth, and its interpretation is limited to a
corruption diagnostic. “Optimizer-matched” may mean the same schedule,
hyperparameters, examples, tokens, steps, slots, and coupled randomness; it
cannot mean identical realized gradients for semantically different rows.

### R9 — close every alternate Phase-C-to-D carrier

Reset and hash both actor state and environment state before D. The Phase-C
action may leave a physical world mutation, public inventory change, action
counter, clock, reward, observation cache, or passive signature even after its
text is deleted. The D start-state generator must remove or counterfactually
match all such effects, or the exhaustive proof must show they are unreachable
and noninformative. Audit capability reachability to raw/audit ledgers; saying
they are “actor-inaccessible” is not a receipt.

Apply D direct-record closure to adapter/text-carrier bytes and metadata,
checkpoint names and sizes, candidate membership/order/count, optimizer or
mount status, file descriptors, process environment, caches, retries, errors,
latency, RNG advancement, and failure paths. The candidate universe and reader
must not already encode the new binding. The common pre-C core plus assigned
carrier must be the only arm difference visible before the decisive D action.

The unique-dependence audit must enumerate all proper subsets and combinations,
not only each source alone. In particular, old memory plus any residual C
outcome/state, old memory plus the C action, hypotheses plus a passive signature,
or a candidate/menu cue may reconstruct the new relation even though each item
alone fails. Enumerate all legal active and passive histories within the cap and
show that only authentic old plus authentic new carrier reaches the ceiling;
masking either cut changes value and twin substitution redirects the action.

### R10 — complete the intervention graph

Keep the correct timing already present: B forks from the end-of-A snapshot,
writes nothing, is destroyed, and cannot affect C; C AUTH/SHAM branches from the
same pre-C state; D write arms clone one byte-identical AUTH declaration,
action, public outcome, and event-seal receipt; new-binding substitution occurs
only after that common seal. Preassignment must not leak branch identity into
the common prefix: prove byte-identical prefix hashes and coupled RNG state up
to each activation boundary.

Define the missing `REACHOUT_OFF -> D` continuation. It must specify passage of
time, public/environment state, failure value, writer/build calls, carrier
mounted at D, D goal reveal, and resource handling. `Freacq` is a total-path
effect because reachout denial changes C experience; it is not a controlled
write or binding effect and must not be pooled with them. `Q` uses AUTH versus
SHAM only. `W0`, `W1`, and `Fbind` use only clones of the common AUTH outcome.

### R11 — turn named controls into registered acceptance tests

For every control, freeze its exact bytes, intervention point, potential
outcome, absolute ceiling/floor, paired contrast, practical margin, and failure
value. In particular:

- require the atomic acquisition/retention floor before `C`;
- require authentic and deranged carrier corpora to have exact admitted-row,
  exposure, token, candidate, build-call, and resource receipts;
- retain `ATOMS_ONLY` as a diagnostic unless a resource-matched null-link arm
  is constructed;
- define bridge and binding substitutions as fixed-point-free within immutable
  strata, with no post-output relaxation;
- define wrong-life as an isolated other root of the same fixed child and prove
  no carrier or state was shared;
- run the complete explicit-text relay through the common interface as a
  feasibility oracle and a registered primary carrier result, not decoration;
- require a complete LoRA relay plus adapter-off, wrong-life, candidate-only,
  unaided-read, cut, and twin/binding failures before adding the LoRA transport
  clause; and
- include the strong active-text same-semantics comparator required by the
  evidence stack. Without a registered between-carrier estimand, make no LoRA
  advantage or baseline-superiority claim; unequal physical resource vectors
  must be reported rather than called matched.

An explicit-text success cannot rescue a failed LoRA carrier clause, and a
LoRA failure cannot erase a valid semantic relay established in text.

### R12 — replace the provisional statistics with an exact powered lock

The statistical unit is correctly the independent PCFL pair root, conditional
on the one child. Targets, H/twin sides, calls, phases, and technical or adapter
seeds are repeated measures and must be averaged by a frozen rule inside a root.
Retain every randomized root and score scientific failures at their registered
values; resume only predeclared infrastructure failures from the identical
sealed cell.

Before any confirmation root exists, define bounded root-level components at
least for:

```text
Aret
Cbind
Tgoal, Tcut, Ttwin, Texhaustive
Qselect, Qrelevant_IG
W0, W1
Fbind, Freacq
TEXT_chain, LORA_chain
COMPLETE_chain
```

Freeze exact aggregation, absolute AUTH floors and control ceilings, SESOIs,
test direction, confidence construction or randomization test, multiplicity
and fixed-sequence logic, missing-cell handling, denominator, and a blinded
nuisance-only extension rule. A point mean of `.05` plus a 95% lower endpoint
above zero does not establish a `.05` smallest effect; for a `.05` SESOI the
relevant lower confidence endpoint must exceed `.05` (or another prospectively
justified decision rule must be used).

Power the registered `N` for the least favorable conjunctive endpoint after
familywise/fixed-sequence handling and the observed root-level dependence. The
current `N=32` is explicitly provisional and cannot authorize dispatch. Within
`Q`, test both selection and relevant-IG contrasts; within `W`, test both `W0`
and `W1`; within `F`, test `Fbind` and the separately interpreted `Freacq` by
intersection-union rules. If component claims are also released, specify their
error control rather than relying on the global intersection alone.

Define `COMPLETE_chain` per root using every registered mediator in temporal
order and all assigned roots as denominator. Freeze a nontrivial `pi_min`, a
one-sided lower confidence rule, and power before confirmation. Average
positive arm contrasts across different roots do not by themselves show that
any root completed the experience-to-action-to-write-to-later-action relay.
The top flywheel/complete-relay wording requires both the ordered contrast
gates and the powered complete-chain gate.

## What v1 genuinely repairs

The following parts of note 54 should be preserved in a revision:

1. eligibility and targets are intended to be preconstructed from hidden truth
   and the source-opportunity tape, not selected from realized accepted memory;
2. acquisition failures remain failures under intention-to-treat;
3. B is a write-denied diagnostic fork and the exact A state is restored for C;
4. link authenticity uses a matched derangement and path necessity uses a
   matched substitution rather than literal deletion alone;
5. traversal is public and typed, and the runtime is forbidden to repair or
   semantically choose a path;
6. C requires pre-action gap, hypotheses, predictions, and experiment choice;
7. D write arms branch after one common AUTH action/outcome;
8. no-write, sham-write, and new-binding controls are separated;
9. C/D transcript, ledger, workspace, cache, and candidate-state purging is
   required in principle;
10. root-level rather than call-level inference, no success conditioning, and
    fixed ordered release are required; and
11. the claim remains conditional on one parent-deleted child and expressly
    excludes classroom, population, compression, autonomous-theory, general-
    intelligence, and unrestricted baseline-superiority interpretations.

These repairs justify **REVISE** rather than **REJECT**. They do not justify
**PASS** until R1--R12 are converted into exact source-bound manifests,
fixtures, scores, and powered acceptance rules and then independently
re-reviewed under the architecture contract.

## Maximum claim before further repair

No causal relay claim is presently authorized. At most, note 54 is a coherent
proposal for a post-parenting, one-fixed-child mechanism assay whose major
branches are now in the right chronological order. If the assay is specified
only after the parenting headline, it remains exploratory. No outcome from it
can rescue a failed parenting headline, and the parenting headline supplies no
evidence for the relay.
