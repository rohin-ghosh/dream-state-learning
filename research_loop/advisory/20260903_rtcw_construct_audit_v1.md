# RTCW construct-validity and anti-overfitting audit

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** hostile methods advisory; no implementation, ratification, or GPU authority

## Verdict

**As presently specified, RTCW is at substantial risk of being a bespoke
ontology-matched unit test.**  That is a correct criticism even if every
transition is randomized and every target is sealed.  The proposed learner
stores typed local relations, schemas, exceptions, provenance, and connected
rows, then a generator samples typed causal motifs, schema laws, exceptions,
roots, and target skeletons which reward their recurrent composition.  This
is not invalid—matching an intended construct is often useful—but it makes a
method win ambiguous: it may demonstrate learning from experience, or merely
that the benchmark's latent language is congenial to the method's prescribed
intermediate representation.

`rml_d0` makes the risk concrete rather than hypothetical.  It is a closed
23-action fluid/thermal state machine over four-valued conditioner and valve
tables.  Its 996-event source life is deliberately enumerated from those
tables; targets are fixed nine-action composition templates, and H/twin
bindings are registered involutions.  Exact target-byte twins, J deletion
witnesses, P chronology, exhaustive planners, and canonical rendering make it
an excellent regression fixture and a controller falsifier.  They do not make
its relations, units, source curriculum, or target grammar independent of the
planned DREAM/SLEEP/THINK representation.  D0 must remain outside all
benchmark-development and paper inference.

The smallest credible repair is **not** to abandon the Dream/Think/Sleep
separation.  Keep DREAM target-blind and prospective, SLEEP deterministic and
outcome-admitting, and THINK a bounded recurrent action policy.  Instead,
make the *environmental causal program and its public expression* independent
of that representation, and score only unseen environment action.  The
benchmark claim can then be: within predeclared RTCW program families, can an
agent turn its own public action--outcome experience into durable information
that improves later sealed interventional action?  It cannot support a claim
about general causal-world-model learning or natural environments.

## Necessary changes, distinguished from useful extras

| Safeguard | Ruling | Smallest acceptable form | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Separately authored locked pack | **Necessary** | One of the three confirmation packs is specified and implemented by an author who did not tune the system or open-pack generator; it is locked, hashed, and inaccessible to all development decisions. | A seed holdout from the same author-written grammar is not an independence test. |
| Program-generator holdout | **Necessary** | The locked pack uses a transition-program family absent from development, not merely unseen parameter seeds: different state factorization, action preconditions/effects, and exception mechanism, while retaining executable public action/outcome lives. | Otherwise random roots can only test interpolation within an ontology chosen around the method. |
| Surface and ontology shifts | **Necessary** | For every confirmation root, randomize handles, target order, renderer, irrelevant observations, and action serialization; across packs change both domain vocabulary and at least the representation that is convenient (e.g., relational composition versus threshold/control or delayed resource transformation). | Nonce names alone test leakage, not representation dependence.  A score that collapses under a changed renderer or latent factorization is not learning from experience. |
| External-environment validation | **Not necessary for the narrow RTCW benchmark paper; necessary for any claim beyond RTCW.** | Do not delay the text-only locked RTCW confirmation for an underpowered imitation of ScienceWorld/ALFWorld.  Before claiming general action-outcome learning, validate the frozen protocol on at least one independently maintained action environment, with native baselines. | An external task is a scope expansion, not a substitute for a locked generator.  RTCW alone can establish a bounded synthetic instrument, never external validity. |

The existing three non-isomorphic packs (fluid/thermal, power/control,
material/biological) are a good starting partition but are insufficient by
their labels.  A pack passes the independence test only if a compact program
translator from the open packs cannot express its state/action semantics by
renaming predicates and choosing new table entries.  This should be assessed
before model evaluation through a written transition specification, exhaustive
or sampled conformance tests, and a recorded rationale from the independent
pack author—not by a post-hoc claim of semantic difference.

## Locked anti-overfitting protocol

1. **D0 isolation.**  Permit D0 only for action-interface, canonicalization,
   trace, and memory-reader tests.  Do not use D0 target categories, source
   counts, wording, row schema, success thresholds, or model traces to choose
   RTCW prompts, writer budgets, proposal formats, compiler rules, readers, or
   endpoint weights.  Report D0 separately as an engineering gate.

2. **Open development versus confirmation.**  Develop only on two explicitly
   named open program families and their public root seeds.  Before any locked
   root is generated, freeze the resolver and backbone, THINK operation
   grammar and action/read budgets, DREAM call/token/row/hypothesis caps,
   SLEEP admission/compiler rules, context boundary, target sampler, baseline
   implementations, decoding, and analysis code.  A change after a locked
   result is viewed creates a new version and requires a newly authored lock.

3. **Independent locked generation.**  A separate author writes the locked
   pack's transition specification and generator implementation against only a
   narrow environment API (reset, legal public actions, public transition,
   termination, and verifier).  They may not inspect system outputs, prompts,
   memory-row schemas, thresholds, or development-root results.  Commit the
   specification, generator hash, root-seed commitment, target-selection
   algorithm, renderer variants, and evaluator before releasing any locked
   public life.  Keep root seeds, latent programs, target answers, and
   intervention assignments private until all predictions/trajectories are
   written.

4. **Experience is necessary and target access is excluded.**  The target is
   sampled after the public lifetime, remains unavailable to DREAM/SLEEP and
   baseline writers, and is sealed before memory construction.  The source
   life contains randomized probe opportunities or explicit `do` interventions
   sufficient to distinguish competing programs; temporal correlation alone
   is not credited as causal learning.  At the fixed post-context boundary,
   remove every decisive source event from controller context for every arm.
   Include honest long-context/full-history and the same adaptive truncation
   policy without durable memory as resource controls.

5. **Pre-admission prospection audit.**  Cache one target-blind proposal pool
   before supporting outcomes are visible and feed that identical pool to raw
   proposal, blind-self-check, and later-outcome-admission arms.  Precommit
   caps on calls, tokens, rows, unique hypotheses per antecedent, and mutually
   contradictory hypotheses.  Publish pre-outcome precision, recall/coverage,
   contradiction rate, and cost.  A broad incompatible enumeration followed
   by outcome filtering is a failure of the prospective-induction claim even
   if final action is high.

6. **Native comparator and intervention fairness.**  Run native iterative
   raw-event RAG, native linked/evolving A-Mem-like memory, and faithful
   provenance-aware Auto-Dreamer-style region rewriting on the same public
   lives, target seal, backbone class, action budget, and quality--cost
   accounting.  Exact graph, generator-aware program induction, and
   target-independent gold are ceilings, not straw baselines.  At the read
   boundary counterbalance authentic, binding-shuffled, twin, cut, and sham
   memory within root; preserve the target and action environment.  Native
   interfaces are used for efficacy; a common row interface is diagnostic
   only.

7. **One statistical unit and one release.**  Use 48 fresh pair roots, 16 per
   pack.  H/twin sides, targets, intervention assignments, writer samples,
   cuts, and technical decoding seeds are nested measurements; average them
   within root before inference.  Freeze a stratified-by-pack paired analysis
   and release root-level scores, failures, costs, false-memory rates, and
   all invalid trajectories.  Do not alter the system after inspecting a pack
   and then average the repaired result with earlier packs.

## Primary endpoint and inference

The primary endpoint should be a single **pair-root sealed interventional
action score**, not graph recovery, citation validity, N/O/J/P/X labels,
proposal coverage, or number of connected rows.  For each root, sample and
freeze a balanced set of target-visible-byte-matched H/twin tasks *after* the
life and post-context boundary.  Score the mean successful completion of the
environment's unseen interventional multi-step task over the two sides; a
malformed, illegal, over-budget, or invalid trajectory scores zero.  The
target sampler may use mechanism strata for balance, but the reported primary
number is root-level environment success, with each root equal weight.

The confirmatory estimand is the stratified-by-pack paired difference in this
score for `E-TEXT` versus each of (a) native A-Mem-like memory and (b) faithful
Auto-Dreamer-like consolidation at the registered fixed budget.  These are
co-primary superiority tests under an intersection-union rule: report a
one-sided 95% stratified paired-bootstrap lower bound for each; no multiplicity
discount is claimed from averaging many diagnostic targets.  The predeclared
practical effect is **+0.10 absolute root-level success**.  Full-history,
truncation, atoms, raw RAG, graph, program, and gold scores explain the result
but are secondary unless separately registered.

## Go/no-go criteria

**Go to a narrow RTCW paper claim only if all conditions hold:**

- all three program packs satisfy the non-isomorphism audit, and the locked
  pack's specification, code hash, roots, targets, assignments, and analysis
  were fixed before system-output access;
- across all 48 roots, the lower bound for `E-TEXT - A-Mem` and for
  `E-TEXT - Auto-Dreamer` is greater than zero and each point estimate is at
  least +0.10; no pack has a negative paired mean against either comparator
  or an unrepaired integrity deviation;
- authentic memory's within-root score exceeds both binding-shuffled and twin
  memory by at least +0.10 with a positive lower bound, while sham/cut controls
  behave in their preregistered direction; otherwise the action result is not
  attributable to learned content;
- the proposal cap is obeyed, pre-outcome precision/coverage is above a
  precommitted non-enumerative floor established on open roots, and the locked
  pack does not require a new schema, prompt, or exception rule;
- no target leakage, hidden program field, cross-root residue, uncharged retry,
  invalid score replacement, or baseline interface handicap is found; and
- E-TEXT is above the matched no-durable-memory/truncation control.  A program
  inducer or exact graph may win without invalidating the benchmark; it limits
  the system claim rather than licensing a weaker baseline.

**No-go / reframe:** a failure of the first two bullets means no efficacy claim
over native linked or offline-consolidating memory.  A failed authentic-content
intervention means no causal-memory claim, even with a positive headline
score.  A locked-pack collapse or ontology-shift collapse means RTCW remains a
method-proximal diagnostic and must not be called a benchmark of experiential
learning.  A clean, negative, locked result can still support an instrument or
phase-diagram paper if the generator, native comparators, and root-level data
are released; it does not justify redesigning the locked pack around the
observed failure.

This protocol preserves the intended division of labor: DREAM may hypothesize
within a bounded target-blind budget, SLEEP only compiles later public support,
and THINK alone turns the resulting information into ordinary action.  What it
removes is the ability to count success on a representation-friendly fixture
as evidence that those stages learned a generally useful world ontology.
