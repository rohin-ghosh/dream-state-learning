# ICLR headline science attack v1

Date: 2026-09-06

Status: **design-only independent advisory**. This file authorizes no change,
implementation, target enumeration, model call, training or GPU run, external
export, or scientific claim. Adoption requires the complete `AGENTS.md`
deliberation and exact-byte human ratification path.

## Verdict

**REWORK.** Counterfactual Confluence v0.3-R is a useful paired causal fixture,
but the proposed headline protocol does not yet identify C2--C6 as worded. In
particular, the current fixture is a generated observation sequence ending in
a one-token counterfactual question, not an interactive agent life; its
pre-goal operational probe selects the exact source--target join that later
matters; the C5 carrier cells confound connected semantics with decision/action
supervision; the proposed agent is deprived of the active external resources
available to the regular agent instead of testing an additive per-life write;
and 20 roots have no prospective power justification for the registered family
of simultaneous superiority, growth, retention, non-inferiority, cut, and rate
claims.

Do not dispatch the v0 headline protocol. Preserve v0.3-R as a CPU instrument,
repair the estimands and visibility boundary, and narrow every conclusion to
the finite generated population and the actual common-deck intervention.

The C6 scope is binding: each replication contains exactly one fixed parent
teaching one child one-to-one; the parent and all parenting artifacts disappear
before deployment; the visible comparison is the parented Think--Dream--Sleep
child versus the regular agent; and the only causal diagnostics are the four
cells of parenting by deployment-time online writing. Replications do not form
a classroom, cohort, teacher ensemble, peer system, or population-learning
mechanism.

## What the present fixture does and does not establish by construction

`lands/v03r.py` and `lands/test_v03r.py` provide valuable prerequisites: both
twins require different answers; the public oracle solves both; exactly one
primary intervention-effect record differs across twins; no configured local
window contains the full oracle proof; removal of each registered edge family
breaks that oracle; and the literal final-goal question is absent from the
precheckpoint export.

Those tests do not certify the proposed experiment. They do not instantiate a
source actor, causal action-to-outcome transitions, the super-life relay,
target panels, native writes, any baseline, or the C6 loop. The shortcut audit
reports 31 feature combinations but makes only the passive predictor a validity
gate; it deliberately omits valve-route joins and does not test sequence,
phase, identifier, operational-probe, rendering, frequency, adaptive-query, or
super-life shortcuts. The final-question string being absent is not equivalent
to the later task being semantically unrevealed.

## Claim-by-claim attack

| Claim | Design verdict | Blocking issue |
|---|---|---|
| C2 | REWORK | The common deck can identify a periodic compiler/write package under a frozen-source experience stream. It does not identify an on-policy flywheel, the current fixture has no real agent-chosen source actions, and the headline delta is not additive to the regular agent's external-memory/resources layer. |
| C3 | REWORK | Active text and native weights are produced by different compilers and receive different access paths; this cannot localize a substrate advantage. Removing text from native also answers the wrong system question. The required final Early-Experience-style batch control is absent. |
| C4 | REWORK | `0.75C` is not post-context, four cuts are sparse, “strongest comparator” is not a fixed estimand, and the present retention/acquisition gates can pass at low absolute value. |
| C5 | REJECT AS SPECIFIED | Connected records, shortcuts, multi-parent `DECIDE` rows, and action targets move together. Bridge removal is not an identifiable intervention on a semantic element stored in an undifferentiated LoRA, and the compression denominator omits representation machinery. |
| C6 | REWORK | The 2x2 is the right shape, but “byte-identical public outcomes” is incompatible with genuinely on-policy branches, entry-level/headroom differences can imitate slope changes, and 12 dyads are unpowered as proposed. |

### C2: action and experience construct

The fixture presents `AtomicEpisode` records and asks for exactly one
color/state-token. Phrases such as “when you closed the valve” do not make an
agent responsible for the intervention. The existing adapter confirms the
problem: `public_life_from_v03r` in
`research_loop/recurrent_text_organism.py` assigns each generator-authored
episode's `phase` string to `PublicExperience.action`, its rendered record to
`observation`, and its `kind` string to `outcome`. No actor selected that
“action,” and no environment dispatched it. There is no existing v0.3-R
interactive source-action API.

Therefore “own action--outcome life” requires a new, ratified interactive
wrapper that offers legal actions, records the selected action before dispatch,
and returns its ordinary world consequence. Without it, the result is
post-context consolidation of presented observations, not learning from the
agent's own actions. A consequential typed answer at the end would still not
repair passive source collection, and renaming `phase` as `action` cannot carry
the construct.

Cloning one frozen actor's sealed chronology across arms is scientifically
useful but deliberately off-policy. `NATIVE_PERIODIC` may change later DREAM
proposals, yet it cannot change the wake evidence it receives. C2--C5 must
therefore be described as **fixed common-experience consolidation**. A positive
C6 interaction in CompilerGym would be a separate bounded on-policy result; it
cannot retroactively convert C2--C5 into an on-policy flywheel.

Source eligibility is also selection-sensitive. A root whose actor misses a
later-needed edge cannot be dropped and replaced merely because eligibility
was computed before the goal string appeared. The hidden target path was still
used to condition inclusion. Freeze master roots using generator-validity
criteria only, keep every assigned actor realization, and score missing support
at the registered failure value. A separate coverage funnel may be descriptive.

### Leakage and target construction

The operational probe is a target selector. Before the final question, it
reveals the decisive source endpoint and target endpoint and explicitly asks
for their causal join. In `precheckpoint_export`, model-visible phase labels
also distinguish `passive_target` from `passive_control`. If the actor, DREAM,
text updater, compiler, candidate builder, or trainer sees either signal before
the carrier and adapters are sealed, “target-blind model-authored connection”
is false even though the answer token is absent.

Required firewall:

1. Give all source records target-neutral, control-neutral model-visible phases;
   permute and namespace handles independently of role, chronology, and answer.
2. Seal every source event, proposal, admission decision, semantic snapshot,
   training row, and adapter before revealing any operational probe or final
   goal coordinate. Probe responses are evaluation only and never return to
   sleep.
3. Alternatively query a prebound exhaustive/matched endpoint roster, with no
   indication of which join will later be scored; charge all queries and seal
   them before selecting targets.
4. Isolate twin sides in processes, ledgers, caches, retrieval indices, writers,
   and adapters. The counterpart's one differing effect must never be visible
   before all responses on both sides commit.
5. Build split groups by the transitive closure of every reused latent tuple,
   passive signature, source-role table, collision class, renderer, handle
   map, target skeleton, and twin root. A passive-hash split alone is not a
   leakage certificate.
6. Extend the no-model shortcut ceiling to sequence/position, phase, IDs,
   lengths, frequency, operational-probe fields, candidate ordering, adaptive
   queries, and action priors. Gate the maximum predictor, not only passive
   accuracy.

### C3: baseline fairness

The binding system question is additive: the proposed agent may use the same
Markdown/files, skills, external artifacts/tools, and active external memory as
a regular agent. The v0 headline instead makes `TEXT_ACTIVE` carry text while
`NATIVE_PERIODIC` is evaluated with semantic text and source ledger removed.
That is a weights-versus-text replacement test, not “does personal
Think--Dream--Sleep add value to the same capable regular agent?” It can make
the proposed system win or lose for the wrong reason.

Freeze one **regular-resource layer** and expose it identically to every
headline arm: the same generic files/skills/tools, the same life-specific active
external-memory interface, the same read/action/context budgets, and the same
artifact visibility. The primary C2 comparison is then
`NATIVE_PERIODIC_ADDITIVE` versus `REGULAR_ACTIVE`, where the personal adapter
write is the intended delta. On the C2--C5 common deck, build the active-memory
snapshot through one treatment-blind regular update path and clone its exact
bytes into every cell at each evaluation cut; otherwise differing external
memory remains a second treatment. Raw-periodic, final Early Experience,
LEAFE-style, and deranged cells must also be additive to that same layer. No
headline arm may be made “weights only.”

This does not by itself isolate LoRA transport if the adapter changes later
DREAM outputs or external-memory contents. For that narrower mechanism, replay
the exact same external-memory snapshot and public state adapter-on versus
adapter-off, and add a same-corpus text carrier: freeze one admitted semantic
set, serve it through the identical one-record read contract and downstream
resolver, and compare it with recognition-only LoRA transport of those exact
rows. Weights-only, text-only, and same-corpus substrate cells are mechanism
diagnostics, not the headline comparison. A win of the additive system may be
claimed only as a whole-system increment; it is not “weights beat text.”

Add a final Early-Experience-style state/action/outcome batch write. Raw
periodic action distillation is not that comparator, and LEAFE-style rollback
and recovery is a different one. The LEAFE cell may be described only as its
registered data-generation/objective adapted to a common rank-8 per-life
budget, not as a reproduction of published full-SFT performance.

Generated-response-token equality is not compute equality. Predeclare separate
caps and report input tokens, retrieval/index work, unique accepted rows,
weighted supervised exposure, optimizer work, FLOPs or occupied accelerator
time, and wall time. Do not duplicate weak evidence or add neutral padding and
then imply equal epistemic support. Since all primary arms possess the same
external interface, any native access on every actor turn must still be charged
and disclosed; exact same-corpus substrate diagnostics must use identical read
opportunities.

### C4: lifetime interpretation

Treat `0.75C` as a context-removal diagnostic, not a post-context point. Use at
least three complete post-context cuts; fix the comparator before confirmation
or require simultaneous contrasts against every named comparator. Do not pick
the “strongest” observed arm after seeing confirmation data.

The growth gate must require, with root-level intervals: a positive change in
native advantage across fixed post-context endpoints; terminal old-task value
non-inferior to a fixed earlier checkpoint, not a selected peak; new-era value
above frozen and the fixed comparator by a practical margin; and mixed-age
joint value above that comparator, not merely above a no-memory floor. This is
finite common-deck accumulation across independent eras. It is not evidence of
asymptotic saturation or repeated on-policy self-improvement.

### C5: connected and compressed without expected-thought scoring

C5 can avoid expected-thought scoring, but not with the current cells.
`COMPACT_LINKED` contains links, shortcuts, successful decision paths, and
multi-parent action targets, whereas `ATOMS_ONLY` removes all of them. Any
difference could be action imitation, better policy supervision, abstraction,
or connectivity. A target-specific candidate cut at inference tests the
prescribed read interface; it does not prove that a named bridge was removed
from LoRA weights.

Use a separate carrier assay:

- Before any target selector, freeze model-authored typed records and their
  public provenance. Score only executable typed relation precision/coverage,
  prospective prediction agreement, target-blind fixed-query fidelity, and
  later action on fresh compositions. Never score prose resemblance or private
  reasoning.
- Make `EXPANDED_LINKED` the deterministic exact expansion of
  `COMPACT_LINKED`; both must have the same denotation and supported facts.
  Keep downstream `DECIDE` supervision byte-identical across carrier cells, or
  omit it and use one common frozen resolver. Train recognition-only `READ`
  adapters if the claim concerns parametric transport.
- Compare compact, expanded, atoms-only, graph/degree/marginal-preserving
  binding derangement, same-corpus text, and a presealed leave-one-necessary-
  bridge-out retrain. The independent intervention builder may use scorer-hidden
  structure to select a cut only after source sealing; those bytes must never
  reach a model or candidate builder.
- Credit connectedness only if authentic links precede the target, improve
  fresh-composition action over atoms under the common resolver, and lose the
  corresponding gain under binding/twin substitution and bridge removal.
  Adapter-off alone establishes only that parameters matter.
- Credit semantic rate reduction only if compact and expanded denotations are
  equal, action is non-inferior with simultaneous root-level intervals, and
  compact total code is smaller after charging schemas, dictionaries,
  candidate rosters, indices, provenance, and shortcut-expansion machinery.
  Tight-read superiority is secondary because it is partly built into a
  shortcut representation. Raw-ledger fraction and LoRA byte size alone are
  not compression evidence.

The maximum C5 wording from this design is that a target-blind, explicit linked
semantic code was model-proposed, publicly admitted, and transported through a
parametric recognition interface. Behavior cannot reveal a human-interpretable
graph inside the LoRA.

### C6: one parent, one child, parent absent

Keep exactly the following deployment 2x2 and no additional causal arms:

| | Online write off | Online write on |
|---|---|---|
| Unparented regular child | `UNPARENTED_FROZEN` | `UNPARENTED_CONTINUAL` |
| Parented child | `PARENTED_FROZEN` | `PARENTED_CONTINUAL` |

The headline pair may remain `PARENTED_CONTINUAL` versus
`UNPARENTED_FROZEN`, but only the parenting-by-online-write interaction
identifies a change in subsequent improvement. All four cells retain the exact
same regular-resource layer: generic files/skills/tools and active external
memory. “Online write” changes only the personal parametric-write permission,
not access to ordinary agent resources. Match assigned practice tasks,
environment randomness, opportunities, tokens, updates, and rehearsal; do not
require byte-identical realized outcomes after actions diverge. The public
world must return each branch's actual consequence.

The parent may see only the child's committed public trace and ordinary public
outcomes, choose from a frozen process-only vocabulary, and emit no entity,
domain, action argument, reference answer, hidden score, compiler term, or
deployment content. Selection among correction types is itself an information
channel and must be included in the leakage audit. Nursery and CompilerGym must
be disjoint in ontology, actions, programs, and artifacts.

At deployment, verify a fresh process plus deletion/absence of parent context,
corrections, restatements, nursery ledger/text, notes, retrieval state, and
KV/prefix caches. This removal applies to parenting-specific state, not to the
generic regular-resource layer, which remains byte-identical across cells.
Retain the frozen base, the permitted child adapter, and only those shared
regular resources. All four cells receive the same Think--Dream--Sleep call
opportunities; off cells seal and discard personal parametric writes while
retaining ordinary external-memory access.

Use baseline-adjusted AUC and early slope above each cell's gym-entry value.
Prebind task strata with comparable entry value and headroom; otherwise a
positive interaction may be a floor/ceiling artifact. The claim, if its gate
passes, is limited to the tested isolated one-to-one procedure and the fresh
CompilerGym deployment. It is not general parenting or meta-intelligence.

## Can 20 roots carry the headline?

Not as currently justified. Twenty whole roots can produce a valid interval
for a large, unusually stable simple paired contrast; checkpoints, twins,
targets, writer samples, and decoding seeds do not increase `n`. As a rough
planning check, even a single ordinary paired contrast with 20 roots needs a
standardized effect around `0.65` for conventional 80% power at a two-sided
5% level; simultaneous C3--C5 max-contrast and non-inferiority gates require
more. Four-root spending results cannot estimate the needed variance, and a
20-root bootstrap is fragile for tail and max statistics.

Use a blinded nuisance-only power design before confirmation, with at least 48
fresh paired roots and a predeclared increase to 72 or 96 based only on outcome-
blind variance/missingness criteria. If resources fix the maximum at 20, call
it a locked pilot and allow only narrow contrasts whose simultaneous intervals
actually clear the registered margins; do not pre-authorize the C2--C5 bundle,
growth, or compression headline. Apply the same power discipline to C6; 12
isolated dyads are not a justified basis for its interaction threshold.

## Minimum decisive protocol

1. **Instrument gate.** Ratify an interactive, target-neutral v0.3-R wrapper
   with real typed action-to-outcome transitions, uniform public metadata,
   carrier-before-probe sealing, twin isolation, exhaustive shortcut controls,
   and no actor-coverage selection. If this cannot be built, rename the study
   as post-context observation consolidation.
2. **Common-deck additive confirmation.** On at least 48 fresh paired
   super-life roots, clone one prospective frozen-source chronology per side.
   Give every main cell the exact same regular-resource layer. Compare
   `REGULAR_ACTIVE` with additive raw-periodic, final Early Experience, final
   LEAFE-style, native-periodic, and binding-deranged writes; retain a
   no-learning floor as a diagnostic and run exact native adapter-off replay
   over the same external-memory snapshot. Treat `0.75C` as descriptive and
   confirm at three post-context cuts. Keep all assigned roots at predeclared
   failure values.
3. **C2--C4 gates.** Use whole-root paired estimates, fixed practical margins,
   simultaneous intervals, free-native/typed-forced guards, a fixed lifetime
   comparator, and separately reported resource frontiers. Word the estimand as
   fixed common-experience consolidation.
4. **C5 carrier gate.** Freeze the model-authored semantic set before targets;
   run the recognition-only compact/expanded/atoms/same-corpus-text/deranged/
   bridge-cut panel through one common resolver. Count the complete code and
   require denotational equality, prospective fidelity, fresh-composition
   action, and causal loss under the registered cuts.
5. **C6 bridge.** In a separately powered set of isolated one-parent/one-child
   replications, complete process-only parenting, destroy all parent/nursery
   state, and deploy only the exact 2x2 above on unique fresh CompilerGym
   programs. Gate C6 on the baseline-adjusted root-level interaction, its
   practical margin, compliance/proposal non-inferiority, and comparable entry
   headroom.
6. **Claim gate.** Failure of an earlier rung prevents a later diagnostic from
   rescuing it. C6 cannot rescue C2--C5. Connectedness without the clean carrier
   assay is omitted; rate without full accounting is called shorter code, not
   compressed knowledge; fixed-deck evidence is never called an on-policy
   flywheel.

## Deadline-aligned reordering under the additive-agent ruling

**Recommendation: prefer this ordering to the four-cut seven-arm v0 if the
deadline is binding.** Make the parent-absent CompilerGym 2x2 the only headline
lifetime experiment and demote v0.3-R to one terminal controlled carrier panel.
This is conditionally paper-worthy, but it is a different and narrower paper.

The paper-worthy headline would be:

> In isolated one-to-one replications, a fixed process-only parent taught one
> child and disappeared; with ordinary agent files, skills, tools, and active
> external memory matched in every deployment cell, parenting increased the
> child's subsequent improvement from online personal writes on fresh
> CompilerGym programs.

That sentence is available only if the baseline-adjusted 2x2 interaction, not
merely the visible extreme-cell difference, clears its interval and practical
margin with comparable entry headroom. The four deployment cells remain
exactly `UNPARENTED_FROZEN`, `UNPARENTED_CONTINUAL`, `PARENTED_FROZEN`, and
`PARENTED_CONTINUAL`. “Frozen” disables only personal parametric writes; it
does not remove the regular-resource layer. No additional parenting arm,
wrong-child arm, classroom, cohort, multiple-parent, peer, or shared-learning
mechanism is permitted.

This reordering changes the claim ladder:

- **C2 narrows** to on-policy online-write efficacy within the fresh
  CompilerGym deployment. A general C2 claim requires a positive write-on
  versus write-off contrast; a positive parenting interaction with no positive
  unparented write effect supports “parenting enabled useful online writing,”
  not general periodic-write efficacy.
- **C3 is deleted.** The 2x2 contains no active-text, Early Experience, or
  LEAFE superiority comparison, and all cells deliberately retain ordinary
  external resources. Terminal v0.3-R substrate diagnostics cannot restore C3.
- **C4 becomes a finite deployment learning-curve claim** over the registered
  unique-program cuts, with backward/fixed probes and baseline-adjusted slope.
  Delete `0.75--6C` lifetime scaling, saturation, and common-deck growth
  language.
- **C5 becomes a separate terminal controlled-world mechanism result.** It may
  show that target-blind linked semantic code improves fresh compositions and
  has a measured rate/read advantage. It does not mediate the CompilerGym C6
  interaction and cannot explain the parenting effect without a separate
  randomized mediator design.
- **C6 is the sole headline.** Its maximum scope is the tested one-parent,
  one-child procedure, parent absent, under the tested CompilerGym deployment.
  Delete general parenting, general meta-intelligence, and population-learning
  language.

### Minimum terminal v0.3-R panel

Do not run four lifetime cuts or the full seven-arm system race. Build one
target-neutral terminal super-life reaching at least `2C`, with every scored
path requiring relevant old and recent edges, then seal its carrier before any
operational probe or final goal. Use at least **48 fresh paired roots** under the existing
nuisance-only blinded power rule; if the deadline permits only 20, call this a
locked mechanism pilot and omit confirmatory connected/compressed wording.

The minimum causal cells are:

1. `COMPACT_LINKED_READ`: recognition-only compact admitted links;
2. `EXPANDED_LINKED_READ`: deterministic denotationally identical expansion;
3. `ATOMS_ONLY_READ`: witnessed atoms with no cross-event carrier;
4. `BINDING_DERANGED_READ`: topology/degree/marginal/dose-matched false
   bindings; and
5. `BRIDGE_CUT_READ`: a presealed leave-one-necessary-bridge-out retrain.

Add two no-fit diagnostics: the exact same corpus served as text through the
same one-record interface, and exact compact adapter-off replay. Every cell
uses one common frozen resolver and byte-identical downstream decision
supervision (preferably none). This is the smallest panel that separately asks
whether links help, compact and expanded denote the same information, authentic
bindings matter, a necessary bridge matters, and any parametric effect is in
the adapter. Full schema/dictionary/index/provenance/candidate costs remain in
the rate denominator.

### Deadline decision rule

Run the writer/interface floor first, then freeze and execute the C6 2x2. The
terminal v0.3-R panel can proceed in parallel only after its target firewall is
ratified. If C6 misses, v0.3-R cannot rescue the parenting headline; at most it
supports a narrower carrier paper. If C6 passes but the v0.3-R panel misses,
submit only the parent-absent learning-rate result and delete connected or
compressed mechanism language. If both pass, present two bounded contributions,
not one claimed mediation chain.
