# PCFL-Active-Stream C03 paper-strategy audit

Date: 2026-09-02

Status: **read-only advisory only**. This memo assumes the C03 ruling that the
current protocol terminates after eight-root B3 DEV and records Stage C as
`DEFERRED_NEW_PREREGISTRATION`. It does not edit the proposal, authorize work,
ratify a design, or release a claim.

## September 25 claim boundary

The ICLR submission can honestly claim only:

- a released, deterministic PCFL-Active-Stream benchmark/protocol whose
  generator, twins, target allocation, depth/headroom/shortcut certificates,
  context cut, isolation, failure inclusion, and estimand implementation passed
  the separately reviewed Stage-A conformance suite;
- successful execution or falsification of the frozen B0--B3 procedure on the
  exact eight predeclared DEV roots, stated as a finite observed result—for
  example, “on six of eight DEV roots” or “the DEV mean was X”—without a
  population or confirmatory gloss; and
- measured feasibility, resource accounting, failure modes, and observed
  component behavior sufficient to motivate a later preregistration.

It cannot claim P1--P4 confirmation, population superiority,
noninferiority/retention, baseline plateau, 80% joint power, finite-range
continual learning, generic model-owned-memory efficacy, or the C1--C3 claim
ceiling. It also cannot turn B3 gate passage into evidence that the gate's
scientific hypothesis is true. `DEFERRED_NEW_PREREGISTRATION` is neither a
positive result nor a null result.

Appropriate main-paper figures are:

1. the causal/protocol diagram and construct-certificate table;
2. all eight root-level trajectories across checkpoints and eras, with every
   failure and assigned denominator visible;
3. raw paired AUTH/TWIN/NULL cascade components, same-event delta lanes,
   semantic/outcome cuts and shams, binding-only interventions, and
   common-history conditions at 3L;
4. unaggregated P1--P4 root summaries plus explicitly descriptive means, SDs,
   and intervals; and
5. the complete per-root resource/failure Pareto and stage-stop accounting.

Do not show confirmatory stars, multiplicity-adjusted p-values, “significant,”
“retained,” “outperformed,” “plateaued,” or an 80%-powered label. If intervals
are shown, label them pilot/estimation intervals and place all eight points
beside them. Report both AS-EXT candidates because B3 also selects the winner;
the selected-winner contrast on the same data is selection-biased.

## Can eight roots be main-paper evidence?

Yes, if the paper is framed as a benchmark/causal-protocol paper with a
failure-inclusive pilot, not as a confirmed continual-learning paper. The
factorial is unusually informative about implementation and attribution: it
can show that the intended treatment unmount, public-outcome path, compiled
delta, cuts, shams, and common-history controls behaved in the eight realized
roots. That is legitimate main-paper evidence for assay feasibility and
falsification pressure.

It is not population evidence. B0--B2 are ordered expansion gates, B3 is DEV,
AS-EXT is selected on these roots, and cognition/resources/stopping are judged
using the same dataset. Those facts require all stopped cells, unsuccessful
roots, selection inputs, and ordering to remain visible. The strongest honest
empirical sentence is of the form:

> In eight predeclared development roots, the frozen system exhibited the
> following failure-inclusive acquisition, old-snapshot, cross-era, and causal-
> factorial patterns; these estimates are design evidence for a new independent
> confirmation and are not confirmatory population tests.

A design-only paper remains the correct fallback if B3 does not seal.

## Minimal B3 handoff for a later preregistration

B3 must emit one immutable, checksummed design-input package containing:

1. **Raw statistical table:** all eight signed root IDs; P1--P4; every P1
   cumulative-link/compiler/binding component; every P2 checkpoint component;
   all P3 `A`, `L`, `tau`, and NI components; P4 SELF and each comparator AUC;
   assigned denominators; and exact failure codes. Nested observations remain
   labeled nested.
2. **Distribution facts:** per-summary mean, unbiased SD, covariance/correlation,
   support extrema, unique-value/atom histogram, skew, outlier and Shapiro
   diagnostics, plus leave-one-root-out summaries. These are pilot inputs, not
   proof of a distribution or variance cap.
3. **Failure law inputs:** root-, arm-, checkpoint-, lane-, compiler-, reader-,
   and target-level failure counts and co-occurrence masks, including scientific
   abort propagation. No complete-case rate is permitted.
4. **Selection and construct receipts:** both AS-EXT candidates and the exact
   selector receipt; Stage-A/B0--B3 conformance, independence, RNG/CRN,
   taint/reset/unmount, shortcut, support, and attainable-support receipts;
   exact hashes for generator, cognition, readers, scorer, estimands, and
   analysis.
5. **Resource vector:** measured per-root and per-cell calls, tokens, latency,
   cost, occupied-device time, artifacts, transfer, retry/failure overhead, and
   the complete-roster projection without pruning.
6. **Decision manifest inputs:** the candidate later test family, null and
   planning effect for every final composite, variance/support assumptions,
   alpha allocation, proposed fixed root count, exact thresholds, any fully
   enumerated simulation distributions/couplings/failures/nulls and simultaneous
   Monte Carlo interval rule, plus an explicit list of conditions that force
   STOP.

The later design may use B3 as disclosed design data only because it will be a
new preregistration over fresh roots. It must freeze its estimands, scenario or
analytical power rule, sample size, resources, and claim sentence before any
new-root outcome. B3 must not itself choose among later inferential rules based
on which one yields the smallest favorable sample size.

## Strategy recommendation

Lead the September paper with the construct and the controlled causal assay;
present B3 as a transparent eight-root stress test and estimation package.
State Stage C as `DEFERRED_NEW_PREREGISTRATION` in the abstract, limitations,
figure captions, and claim table. This preserves a credible main-paper result
without converting DEV into confirmation.
