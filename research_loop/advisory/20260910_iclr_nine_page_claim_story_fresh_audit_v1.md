## Executive verdict

The strongest honest paper is not “we built a lifelong experience model.” It
is a **causally decomposed test of whether one clean, parent-deleted agent can
turn its own grounded action--outcome history into useful parametric personal
knowledge**. The full Dream--LoRA--Think objective remains intact as the
question:

1. can a transactional native writer preserve the acting interface while
   transporting eligible personal experience into a life-local LoRA;
2. does target-blind parenting improve later learning rather than merely birth
   competence;
3. does the resulting experience form connected knowledge that can be
   traversed under different goals and expanded by an informative action; and
4. does that system keep improving across an increasing lifetime after a
   strong evolving textual-memory agent reaches its own prospectively defined
   local plateau?

Those are four separate causal burdens, not four descriptions of one curve.
PCFL may give them a shared environment vocabulary, but mechanism (`M`),
longitudinal development (`L`), and rate--distortion (`C`) have different
units, controls, and claims. Parenting is another separately identified
factorial. None may borrow units, thresholds, sample sizes, or success from
another.

Today, no positive full-story result is available from the audited sources:

- the old writer is contradicted as a safe scientific writer and its
  replacement is unproved;
- parenting (`E1`), connected experience (`E2`), traversal (`E4`), expansion
  (`E5`), and lifetime superiority (`E6`) are unproved;
- whole-acting-state physical compression (`E3`) is presently foreclosed at
  the measured rank-8 scale;
- there is no executable PCFL benchmark, `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` service, `M`
  reducer, `L` runner, or `C` representation experiment; and
- the current clean-lineage successor is a proposal, while the extant
  parenting/CompilerGym entry points fail open on ancestry and return-channel
  contamination.

Therefore no empirical abstract for the full thesis is honest yet. The paper
version, title, abstract verbs, and conclusion must be selected only after
sealed evidence, from the highest noncompensatorily passing rung. “Preserving
the full objective” means testing and reporting every intended hypothesis; it
does not mean preserving positive wording after a falsification.

## The strongest honest one-paper narrative

### One-sentence question

Can a separately raised agent, after its target-blind parent disappears,
compile only its own grounded action--outcome continuations into a
transactional personal LoRA that supports connected cross-goal use,
information-seeking expansion, and retained lifetime improvement beyond a
certified evolving text-memory agent?

### Paper-ready narrative spine, conditional on evidence

Fleet post-training supplies shared inherited competence; it does not include
the future biography of one deployed agent. We study one developmental stack:
optional target-blind schooling, one-to-one adaptive parenting, parent
deletion, and a deployment agent whose only cognitive organs are THINK,
DREAM, and SLEEP. THINK selects and revises actions, DREAM manages finite
active context, and SLEEP compiles eligible public action--outcome
continuations into a transactional cumulative life-local LoRA. A clean
parenting factorial separates entry competence from later learnability; a
relational mechanism assay tests whether personal experience is connected,
goal-traversable, and expandable; and an independent-lineage longitudinal
study compares the full learner with one certified evolving typed-text-memory
agent under matched opportunities and resource envelopes. A separate
rate--distortion assay asks the narrower question of whether the textual
semantic interface is compressed. The paper reports the highest rung that
passes, including failed upstream or downstream rungs, rather than inferring
the complete organism from one favorable score.

This framing preserves the vision while making the novelty compositional and
causal. It must not claim to be the first system that trains an acting model
on its own future-state/action data, distills reflection into weights, or
updates online textual memory; the superseded abstract itself identifies
direct neighbors. The defensible contribution, if supported, is the clean
developmental separation plus the connected-relay and strong-baseline lifetime
tests.

### What “one parent, one child” means scientifically

The biological metaphor describes one life, not the replication unit. Each
scientific lineage begins independently from the same admitted birth policy.
Within a lineage, one target-blind parent may adapt through root-local
developmental context/files, then disappears. Across lineages, no parent or
child state may carry evaluation outcomes. Population language is unsupported
unless the registered independent child lineages justify it. `M` and `C`
remain conditional-on-one-fixed-child environment-root experiments; only `L`
and the parenting factorial can support across-lineage statements.

## Claim-to-evidence contract

### S0 — the tested system and its provenance actually exist

**Permitted claim after passage:** “We implemented and evaluated a clean,
parent-deleted THINK--DREAM--SLEEP agent whose admitted SLEEP rows derive from
its public action--outcome history and whose accepted state advances
transactionally.”

**Required evidence:** exact source/launch/artifact receipts; immutable birth,
model/tokenizer, mechanism, writer, corpus, parent, exposure, and ancestry
identities; a fail-closed `CleanSourceReceipt`/`ExperimentPlan` boundary;
read-only clean source; isolated descendant stores/processes/RNG; atomic
adapter/corpus/writer/manifest promotion; pre/post source hashes; and all
negative contamination tests. Missing, unknown, unhashed, aliased, or
unratified ancestry must reject before any model/provider/GPU boundary.

**Current disposition:** fail. The current sources report no executable PCFL
path, and the clean-lineage design remains unratified and unimplemented.
Without S0, every downstream scientific row is invalid rather than merely
negative.

### E0 — native personal-write truth, utility, and preservation

**Permitted claim after passage:** “A transactional cumulative LoRA writer
transported eligible native child continuations while preserving the acting
interface.”

**Design/evidence slot:** native prompt to child-response pairs; response-only
loss; immutable-birth rebuild over committed cumulative rows; exact
action/outcome/source provenance; candidate adapter and corpus staged as one
transaction; comparison with both raw base and previous accepted child on
disjoint panels; atomic accept/reject and rejected-row quarantine.

**Must be measured:** absorption/recognition, free extraction, typed forced
proposal, native action validity/routing, task behavior, old-competence
retention, imprecision/false-memory and harm, adapter-off, wrong-life/binding,
rollback, and preservation relative to the previous child—not merely raw
base. Thresholds and the replication unit must be fixed prospectively; rows,
prompts, and checkpoints are not independent lives.

**Current disposition:** the legacy result proves only that writes can strongly
alter behavior and can corrupt the action dialect. It is failure/safety
characterization. The old writer cannot fill a positive E0 slot, and no `M`,
`L`, or parenting claim can bypass E0.

### E1 — parenting changes later learnability

**Permitted claim after passage:** “Target-blind parenting increased the
incremental benefit of later personal writes after deployment-entry
competence was removed.”

**Design/evidence slot:** independently raised roots in the exact factorial
`P0` (parented/write off), `P1` (parented/write on), `U0`
(unparented/write off), and `U1` (unparented/write on). Every cell receives the
same certified active textual memory and registered opportunity stream. The
parent is absent during the evaluated deployment and cannot learn from its
descendants.

**Must be measured:** entry score; entry-adjusted acquisition curve; the
root-level interaction
`[(P1-P0) - (U1-U0)]`; absolute parented learning; delayed parent-absent
retention and transfer; harm/imprecision; task value and information gained
per generated token; uncertainty and a practical margin. A bootstrap earns
“learnability-policy” language only through later acquisition or a
bootstrap-by-parent interaction, not through a better entry score.

**Falsifier:** a parenting main effect at entry with no interaction is installed
competence, not learning-to-learn. A null/harmful interaction removes the
parenting-benefit claim and “raising” from the title, even if `M` or `L`
passes.

### E2 — personal experience is connected rather than an atom bag

**Permitted claim after passage:** “The child used linked personal experience
in a way not explained by possession of the same atoms.”

**Design/evidence slot:** `M`, with authentic links matched against atoms-only,
truthful-null links, and deranged links; matched atomic semantics/exposure;
atomic-retention floor; the same finite reader/read plan for text and LoRA;
adapter-off, wrong-life/root, and binding-swap controls.

**Must be measured:** root-level authentic-minus-each-control value with
uncertainty and a practical margin; atomic fact retention; path validity and
terminal answer separated from guessing/shortcut scores; false relation use;
and absolute headroom. The unit is an environment root conditional on one
fixed child. Goals, paths, branches, calls, and checkpoints are repeated
measures.

**Falsifier:** authentic links that do not beat atoms/null/deranged support at
most atomic personal transport. They do not support “connected experiential
knowledge.”

### E4 — goal-conditioned traversal is causally used

**Permitted claim after passage:** “The same personal evidence supported
different valid traversals for different goals, and cutting the necessary
path removed the benefit.”

**Design/evidence slot:** two same-start goals with distinct required paths;
authentic path emitted before action; necessary bridge cut;
twin/redirection/binding control; derangement; matched token and information
budgets; no fixed-controller or lexical/identifier shortcut.

**Must be measured:** correct goal-conditioned path switch, legal-path score,
answer score, authentic-minus-cut/deranged loss, revision and action value,
and root-level uncertainty. Trace length, eloquence, and a post-hoc path
reconstruction are not endpoints.

**Falsifier:** correct answers without a pre-action path effect, or no loss
under the required bridge cut, support task solving but not causal traversal.

### E5 — experience-directed expansion completes the full relay

**Permitted claim after passage:** “The child detected a missing relation,
selected an informative action, wrote its public outcome, and later used both
old and new personal knowledge.”

**Design/evidence slot:** a predeclared chain
`t0 old knowledge -> t1 uncertainty -> separating action -> public outcome ->`
`eligible SLEEP write -> reset -> t2 delayed old+new goal`, with authentic
uncertainty versus truthful sham, `REACHOUT_OFF`, write-off, old-row cut,
new-row not-written, binding swap, wrong life/root, and certified active-text
controls.

**Must be measured:** calibration of the uncertainty signal; probability and
cost of selecting a registered separating experiment; information gain and
public outcome accuracy; write acceptance/provenance; delayed absolute value;
old-only and new-only retention; the old-by-new necessity interaction; token,
call, retrieval, and optimizer cost. All links in the chain must pass; a later
answer cannot compensate for a failed action or invalid write.

**Falsifier:** benefit with `REACHOUT_OFF`, sham uncertainty, write-off, old
cut, or wrong binding points to a shortcut or text/controller effect. A good
experiment that does not survive reset is information seeking without
parametric expansion.

### E6 — continuing lifetime improvement beyond strong active text

**Permitted claim after passage:** “Across independently raised paired
lineages, the full learner continued to improve after a certified evolving
text-memory comparator reached its registered local plateau, retained old
competence, and finished ahead by a practical margin.”

**Design/evidence slot:** within each clean birth, isolated `F` and `B`
descendants receive a byte-identical exogenous cumulative information stream,
ordinary tools, public outcomes, update opportunities, and matched generated-
token envelope. `F` uses personal writes; `B` has a frozen acting model and
one versioned, typed, evolving `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` memory. Evaluation actions
do not alter later learning input. The independent unit is the separately
raised paired lineage; cuts/tasks/samples are repeated measures.

**Must be measured before seeing confirmation results:** baseline write/read/
use faithfulness, headroom, terminal-load behavior, exact resources, and a
prospective local-plateau certificate; registered entry and lifetime cuts;
at least three post-anchor late cuts; baseline late-slope equivalence margin;
positive learner late slope; positive paired slope advantage; old-competence
retention floor; terminal practical superiority; absolute values, paired
uncertainty, failures, missingness, multiplicity, and stopping. Root count
must come from E6-specific development covariance/effect/cost evidence, not
parenting or fixed-child `M` numbers.

**Falsifiers:**

- no certified baseline plateau -> no “after saturation/plateau” claim;
- no positive learner late slope -> no continuing-improvement claim;
- slope gain without retention -> adaptation with forgetting, not continual
  learning;
- retained improvement without terminal margin -> no superiority claim; and
- fixed-child environment clones -> no across-life conclusion.

### E3/C — honest rate--distortion, not rhetorical compression

**Permitted near-term claim after passage:** “A normalized/schema-residual
textual semantic code reduced complete live text-interface cost at registered
distortion and utility.”

**Design/evidence slot:** disjoint PCFL roots and increasing semantic loads;
expanded observation text, normalized connected text, and schema-plus-
residual connected text; deterministic decoder/denotational round trip;
complete bytes for representation, codec, decoder, index, prompt, and retained
auxiliary state; retrieval/compute work; utility and false memory. Loads are
repeated measures within roots.

**Must be measured:** exact lossless and live bytes; fidelity/distortion;
false memories; path/task utility; rate--distortion curve before and after a
real crossover; root-level uncertainty. The observed rank-8 adapter is
80,792,096 bytes, so strict rate below 0.50 requires expanded semantic text
above 161,584,192 bytes before other retained state is charged.

**Falsifier/scope rule:** below a complete measured physical crossover, LoRA
may be described only as transport of semantics, never physical compression.
`C` is ancillary to the nine-page causal spine and cannot rescue a failed
writer, relay, or lifetime result.

### Optional mechanism claims — scaled THINK and learned DREAM

Scaled THINK is a mechanism sibling, not a silent upgrade. A benefit claim
requires a matched-total-token comparison of visible sequential depth with
context-isolated sibling routes plus a generous-budget package test. Endpoints
must be public decision/action value, information acquired, revision quality,
learned stopping, and harm—not branch count or private-trace length. If it is
not frozen and positive before the scientific child begins, use the safe
reference THINK and move branching to future work.

DREAM may be named operationally as finite-context management. “Learned DREAM
improves behavior” requires an equal-generous-budget context-pressure assay
showing better preservation of state/intentions and later action without
hiding information loss. Without it, do not promote DREAM from architecture
to result.

## Adversarial contamination and inference audit

1. **Plans are not observations.** All requested paper-core, benchmark, and
   clean-lineage sources disclaim implementation/execution authority. Their
   precision cannot be cited as empirical evidence.
2. **Adjacent executable software is not PCFL.** Symbolic-kernel, RML, and
   byte-accounting checks recorded in the implementation audit are reusable
   patterns only. They do not establish a PCFL world, readable carrier,
   causal relay, or compression result.
3. **Legacy LoRA lives are development/failure evidence.** Heterogeneous,
   unseeded 1,024-episode paths, repeated programs, absent active-text
   comparator, and writer dialect collapse exclude them from E0/E6
   confirmation. Reanalysis cannot make them prospective independent lives.
4. **Current entry points fail open.** Arbitrary lineage directories, mutable
   `DONE` markers, directory scans for prior corpora, self-declared exposure,
   shared parent/evaluation trees, and bootstrap ledgers without authenticated
   ancestry permit contamination. No claim-bearing run through those paths is
   admissible merely because its output hashes.
5. **Rejected evidence must be rejected with the adapter.** Quarantining a
   candidate weight file while later loading its corpus, summary, playbook,
   selection signal, or derivative leaves a return channel.
6. **Never parent an evaluated child.** A final/deployment visit begins a
   disposable descendant. Its weights, rows, memory, DREAM/SLEEP products,
   parent notes, scores, rankings, schedules, thresholds, and favorable-child
   selection cannot flow back to the canonical child or parent.
7. **Adaptive parenting is a contamination surface.** Parent adaptation must
   be root-local and development-only. A parent that sees delayed evaluation
   outcomes turns the purported parent-deleted deployment into continued
   training and invalidates the parenting estimand.
8. **No pseudoreplication.** `M/C` roots are conditional on one fixed child;
   `L` and parenting units are independently raised lineages. Tasks, goals,
   probes, routes, calls, branches, loads, and checkpoints cannot inflate
   degrees of freedom.
9. **No result-selected plateau.** Baseline identity, updater/retriever,
   plateau window, equivalence margin, late cuts, and failure values must be
   frozen before the relevant results. Choosing the anchor after viewing the
   learner and baseline curves makes E6 descriptive only.
10. **Resource matching is semantic, not token-only.** Match outcome access,
    update opportunities, ordinary tools, reachout, generated-token envelope,
    memory/storage/retrieval work, and failure handling. Report optimizer work
    separately. A deliberately weak or unreadable text baseline cannot support
    the headline.
11. **Development feedback consumes a family.** Text-model smoke tests and DEV
    results may change a later plan only on a registered disjoint reserve.
    Confirmation roots, order, stopping, reducers, child, parent, writer, and
    hyperparameters cannot change after partial confirmation is visible.
12. **CompilerGym is quarantined secondary evidence.** Its supplied strategy,
    weak graph identifiability, and known development artifacts prevent it
    from carrying the PCFL connected-memory claim or selecting a supposedly
    clean CompilerGym child. It may characterize transfer and failure only
    under a separate clean packet.
13. **Narrative selection is itself an analysis decision.** The version ladder
    below must be frozen before confirmation. Otherwise choosing “the most
    interesting” surviving outcome is undisclosed multiplicity.

## Final recommendation

Use **`M` plus `L` as the main causal spine**, with E0 as a visible
noncompensatory prerequisite and the parenting factorial as the developmental
claim. Keep `C` as a narrow ancillary assay and treat CompilerGym as secondary
failure/transfer characterization. This gives one coherent paper rather than
an architecture catalogue:

```text
clean development and E0
        -> M: what personal experience can causally support
        -> L: whether that benefit persists and grows across a lifetime
        -> parenting: whether development improves the rate, not just entry
        -> C: what is and is not compressed
```

The present paper state is **not submission-ready for the full thesis**. The
page budget is adequate; the evidence and contamination boundaries are not.
No prose revision can substitute for an executable PCFL path, a certified
writer and active-text comparator, independent clean lineages, and sealed
claim-specific results. Once those exist, select the prebound fallback above,
fill the five abstract sentences, and remove every result word belonging to a
higher failed rung.
