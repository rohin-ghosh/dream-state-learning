# One-parent teachability canary: fresh scientific attack v1

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: **proposal-only independent scientific advisory**. This document is a
read-only attack on the current candidate. It authorizes no architecture
change, implementation, source/target enumeration, task generation, model or
tokenizer call, parent interaction, adapter fit or mount, CompilerGym
execution, GPU use, external access, manuscript claim, or scientific-root
spend. It changes no frozen source. Adoption requires the full `AGENTS.md`
deliberation path, exact-byte human ratification, scoped implementation and CPU
fixtures, fresh independent review, author-side scientific review, and a
separate exact pre-GPU authorization.

## Verdict

**REWORK. The current four-root teachability canary is a useful native-writer,
parent-elicitation, and immediate near-transfer spending gate. It is not a
falsifiable test of the full proposed bridge from parenting to improved later
learning.**

The current canary can substantially reject the weakest explanation—direct
lesson recitation or behavior that occurs only while parent text is visible—if
its clean-context, parent-absent, adapter-off, unparented-self, and strict native
action gates are executed exactly. It can also establish task-specific transfer
to fresh homologous instances of the nursery task family.

It cannot distinguish that task-specific transfer from a task-general,
parent-absent thought-to-action disposition because the source, application,
and probe tasks are all homologous and drawn from the same taught nursery
families. It does not test durability after intervening experience or another
write. Most decisively, it has no parent-absent sequential learning phase and
no `P/U x later-writes-off/on` interaction. Its fixed-checkpoint task value or
information gain per token is performance efficiency, not evidence that later
experience is learned more efficiently.

The v2 addendum does not amend v1 Section 10's teachability canary, and the v2
scope proposal does not add a separate canary bridge. The stronger v2
deployment interaction therefore must not be read backward into the current
four-root canary.

## What the present canary actually identifies

| candidate explanation | current evidence | disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Lesson recitation / parent-present prompt compliance** | Parent prose and child restatement are masked, direct recitation is not a primary endpoint, probes are parent absent, `F0_NONE` is represented in the writer, and adapter-off/pre-write controls localize a behavioral change. `AUTH_TARGET_ONLY` tests whether the same corrected targets still write without authentic scaffold text. | **Partly distinguishable.** A strict uncued action change can reject pure direct recitation and parent-present dependence. However `AUTH_TARGET_ONLY` is not a semantic-parent null: it reuses targets that the parent already helped elicit. Same-family task prompts can also cue the taught routine. |
| **(b) Task-specific skill transfer** | Fresh homologous tasks use nonce symbols/handles and different correct discriminating actions; action/value and state-to-target derangement go beyond verbal recall. | **Distinguishable as near transfer.** A pass supports only a learned policy over the taught task families and interface. | **(c) Durable parent-absent thought-to-action disposition** | Immediate clean-context, parent-absent behavior plus adapter-off can show parametric parent absence and native action use. | **(d) Improved later learning efficiency** | The canary reports task value and information gain per generated token at served checkpoints. | **Not tested.** No new parent-free experience stream, later SLEEP cuts, sealed learning curve, or `P/U x writes-off/on` interaction exists. The four constructs are nested only as claims, not as automatic implications:

```text
recites / follows an explicit cue
        != performs on a fresh same-family instance
        != selectively applies the process in an untaught family after delay
        != learns more from later experience or later personal writes
```

Passing a lower rung must never rescue failure of a higher rung.

## Blocking scientific defects

### B1. No transfer-distance manipulation

The current source, fresh application, and parent-absent probes are homologous.
Nonce renaming removes memorized answers but does not remove a family-specific
algorithm. A child can learn “enumerate code candidates and choose a partition”
or a RuleShift response pattern without acquiring a disposition that transports
to another action ontology.

Required repair: for each child, reserve one entire interactive family from
parenting, childhood targets, rehearsal, calibration, and childhood active
text. Measure same-family and cross-family transfer separately. Counterbalance
which family is taught across roots.

### B2. No selective-use negative control

Always predicting, always writing two hypotheses, or always taking an
information-seeking action can raise the registered marker rates. A useful
disposition is conditional: it should activate after a genuine prediction
violation when multiple live explanations remain and a cheap discriminator
exists, but not when there is no contradiction, one explanation remains, or
information is more costly than acting.

Required repair: bind matched applicable and counter-indicated probe packets.
Score false activation and opportunity cost. Direct lesson vocabulary or a
syntactically valid `SUCCESSOR_STATE` cannot rescue indiscriminate behavior.

### B3. The thought endpoint is vulnerable to trained-format imitation

Each accepted response is repeatedly supervised behind the scaffold ladder.
Prediction text and two-hypothesis text can therefore become style markers.
State-target derangement is useful for correct binding, but does not by itself
prove that the public thought changed the subsequent action for the right
reason.

Required repair: make the primary disposition unit a joint, pre-outcome,
world-scored thought-to-action event. The thought must make an auditable public
prediction, the next action must discriminate the live finite hypothesis set,
the result must update or eliminate a hypothesis, and task utility must clear a
non-inferiority guard. Score every missing component zero. Keep prose-only
scores secondary.

### B4. Parent absence is immediate, not durable

A fresh process and adapter-off test prove that parent text and KV state are not
needed at one checkpoint. They do not show persistence after irrelevant
experience, context reconciliation, or another personal write. Static LoRA
bytes will trivially remain identical across elapsed wall time.

Required repair: repeat the uncued cross-family panel after a prebound
interference/learning block and two later write opportunities. Require the
effect at the delayed point and bound decay from entry. Preserve a frozen
write-off fork to distinguish durable childhood state from effects created by
later writes.

### B5. Performance efficiency is not learning efficiency

Information gain per generated token and immediate task value can show that a
policy acts efficiently. They do not show that later experience produces a
larger improvement. Childhood target yield is likewise data acquisition, not a
later learning-rate outcome.

Required repair: after deletion, fork both childhood checkpoints into
`P0/P1/U0/U1`, expose all four isolated services to the same novel parent-free
wake packets, enable the same later SLEEP law only in `P1/U1`, and measure fresh
sealed probes before and after at least two writes. The parenting bridge is the
root-level interaction of later-write gains, not `P1` value alone.

### B6. The current pass rule is not yet executable scientific content

The canary names a “frozen adverse margin,” an “equivalence band,” large
surprises, joint endpoints, and non-erasure margins without binding their
numeric values or a single reducer. A `3/4` directional rule is acceptable for
spending, but only after each root maps deterministically to one value under
fixed missing/invalid-action rules.

Required repair: use development-only families to justify numeric thresholds,
then ratify every threshold, task count, reducer, seed/coupling law, and failure
value before any canary source or target identity is generated. Four roots
remain a formative go/no-go and never become inferential evidence.

### B7. Current controls answer narrower questions than their labels suggest

- `AUTH_TARGET_ONLY` asks whether authentic child targets need parent text in
  the writer conditioning. Equality with `AUTH_PARENT_FADE` is compatible with
  successful fading; it does not show that the parent was causally irrelevant.
- `STATE_TARGET_DERANGED` asks whether correct state/action binding matters. A
  loss under derangement does not establish cross-task disposition.
- `UNPARENTED_SELF` is the decisive childhood practice/writer control, but its
  different self-generated target corpus means the contrast identifies the
  complete parent-elicitation-plus-write pathway, not isolated prose semantics.
- Wrong-child or shuffled general lessons are not guaranteed nulls; genuinely
  general process guidance may transfer.

These controls should remain, but their claim boundaries must be literal.

## Smallest falsifiable target-blind bridge canary

This is a replacement proposal for later deliberation, not authority to run it.
It deliberately reuses the current finite Codebreaker and RuleShift concepts
only as candidate families; their generators and task packets do not yet exist
as ratified scientific objects.

### Unit and topology

Use four isolated, pre-parent-eligible roots as a **spending canary only**. Each
root contains one reset frozen parent policy, one P child, and one isolated U
counterfactual child from the same pinned checkpoint. Nothing is shared across
roots or branches. Two roots teach on Codebreaker and reserve RuleShift; two
teach on RuleShift and reserve Codebreaker. The direction is fixed by the root
packet before any model output.

Within a root:

```text
P childhood: one taught-family source failure
             -> one closed process correction tied to public event IDs
             -> one fresh taught-family application
             -> public admission -> one response-only childhood write

U childhood: matched source/application opportunities
             -> neutral self-review, no parent edge
             -> same public admission law -> dose-matched own-experience write

fresh-process deletion of parent and all childhood-only artifacts

P checkpoint -> P0 later writes off / P1 later writes on
U checkpoint -> U0 later writes off / U1 later writes on
```

The canary uses one correction, one application, one childhood write, two
parent-free wake blocks, and two later write opportunities. This is the
smallest sequence that contains an elicitation event, near transfer, an
untaught-family entry probe, a delayed point, and a repeated later-learning
contrast. More childhood cycles belong in the headline pilot, not this gate.

Root eligibility may require the frozen child to display the predeclared
source-family failure before the parent speaks, as the current canary does, but
the claim must remain conditional on such roots. Preserve the first twelve
attempted root packets, take the first four pre-parent eligible roots, report
the complete funnel, and treat fewer than four as NO-GO. No correction,
admission, fit, or probe success may select or replace a root.

### Practice and probe packets

For each root, freeze mutually disjoint packets before childhood:

1. **Taught source:** one task ending in a public prediction violation with at
   least two live finite hypotheses and at least one cheap discriminating legal
   action. It is the only parent-visible task.
2. **Taught-family application:** a fresh homologous task with independently
   permuted entities, tool handle, action tuple, hidden rule, and correct
   discriminator. Parent absent; the child may use only the prospectively
   permitted childhood scaffold.
3. **Near-transfer panel:** fresh taught-family tasks with parent, restatement,
   source trace, and nursery store absent. It estimates task-specific transfer.
4. **Cross-family applicable panel:** fresh tasks from the family wholly absent
   from this child's childhood. Each contains a genuine contradiction, at
   least two surviving hypotheses, and a useful cheap discriminator.
5. **Cross-family counter-indicated panel:** surface- and budget-matched tasks
   with no contradiction, a singleton live hypothesis set, or a discriminator
   whose public cost exceeds its maximum possible value. Correct behavior is
   to act/commit/stop rather than reflexively investigate.
6. **Bridge wake stream:** two blocks of unique tasks from the reserved family,
   with a later SLEEP opportunity after each block for `P1/U1` only.
7. **Sealed bridge probe panel:** one fixed, isolated reserved-family panel at
   cuts `k=0,1,2`. Probe responses, outcomes, scores, and state never enter wake,
   DREAM, SLEEP, active text, or a later cut.
8. **Neutral interference/generic panel:** task- and lesson-disjoint ordinary
   tool/reasoning work between immediate and delayed disposition probes. It is
   a non-erasure and delay control, not training evidence for the disposition.

The smallest sensible formative packet is two tasks per immediate category,
two wake blocks of four tasks, and four sealed probes per cut. Those counts are
not powered and require development-only headroom/resource confirmation before
ratification; reducing them after seeing any scientific output is forbidden.

All ordinary deployment affordances, generated-token limits, action
opportunities, external-memory policy, DREAM schedule, task order, and random
coupling are matched across `P0/P1/U0/U1`. `P0/P1` are byte-identical at cut 0,
as are `U0/U1`. Only later personal-write permission differs after the fork.

### Target-blindness and answer firewall

The bridge canary should touch no CompilerGym program, pass, score, source,
manifest, result, or target identity. The parent capability root contains only
the current public non-compiler source trace and the closed correction schema;
it has no repository, network, cross-root, cross-child, future-task, hidden-rule,
or evaluator capability.

Before any scientific task draw, freeze and hash:

- generator and finite-universe bytes, source/application/near/cross/wake/probe
  split law, counterbalancing, resets, budgets, and common-random keys;
- a namespace and semantic firewall showing disjoint ontology, tool names,
  action schemas, entities, reward mechanics, and target tuples from LLVM and
  all prior nursery/development artifacts;
- the closed correction code and trigger, which may cite only public event IDs
  and must contain no action, hidden rule, number other than an event ID, or
  target-derived string;
- exact child-native response targets, source/action/outcome provenance,
  response-only masks, EOS, scaffold exposures, neutral fills, anchors,
  derangements, writer law, and adapter hashes; and
- parsers/reducers for predictions, hypotheses, native calls, information gain,
  utility, DREAM restoration, recitation, cue dependence, invalid responses,
  and all no-action/failure values.

Childhood targets remain the child's exact supported pre-outcome
`THINK_TO_ACT` and validated `DREAM_STATE` continuations. Parent prose,
restatement, result, score, raw ledger, and unsupported principles are masked
inputs and never targets. Exact source action tuples and their aliases are
excluded from every near/cross/bridge probe. No semantic reviewer may inspect
scientific outputs in order to decide per-row admission; the frozen
public-world reducer alone decides. Post-run auditing may inspect immutable
receipts but cannot change admission.

## Predeclared construct metrics

All task-level quantities reduce to one row per root before any across-root
summary. Tasks, cuts, samples, targets, and fits are repeated measures, never
additional `n`.

For an eligible applicable event `e`, define a strict joint disposition bit
`J(c,r,e)=1` only when all of the following occur before or through the first
post-surprise action under cell `c`:

1. the child made a prospectively parsed, falsifiable public prediction before
   the surprising outcome;
2. after the violation it retained at least two child-authored hypotheses that
   are consistent with the public trace;
3. it dispatched exactly one schema-valid, non-repeated action whose expected
   partition of the frozen finite hypothesis set achieves at least `tau_IG` of
   the maximum legal information gain at that state;
4. the public result eliminates or changes support for at least one live
   hypothesis and the subsequent prediction/action changes accordingly; and
5. terminal public utility clears the family-specific absolute support or
   non-inferiority guard.

Missing prediction, prose-only action, malformed call, no action, repeated
action, invalid DREAM restoration, timeout, or failed utility guard gives
`J=0`. The exact finite-family prior, entropy convention, `tau_IG`, utility
guard, and tie law must be justified on development-only packets and then
numerically ratified before scientific packet generation.

Define the following root-level quantities:

```text
R_r(c)       = exact closed-response lesson-code/restatement accuracy
CueLift_r(c) = mean J on explicitly cued packets - mean J on uncued packets
Near_r(c)    = mean J on the parent-absent taught-family panel
Cross0_r(c)  = mean J on the uncued reserved-family applicable panel at cut 0
False_r(c)   = fraction of counter-indicated packets receiving a needless
               discrimination action
Select0_r(c) = Cross0_r(c) - False_r(c)
Cross2_r(c)  = the corresponding uncued applicable mean after cut 2
Select2_r(c) = Cross2_r(c) - delayed counter-indicated false activation
Static0_r    = Select0_r(P0) - Select0_r(U0)
Static2_r    = Select2_r(P0) - Select2_r(U0)
Retain_r     = Static2_r - Static0_r
```

`R` is an absorption/manipulation diagnostic only. A large `R` or positive
`CueLift` cannot satisfy any behavioral gate. The relevant attribution
contrasts are authentic P checkpoint versus matched `U_SELF`, authentic
adapter-off/pre-write, and state-target derangement. Report `Near` even if
cross-family gates fail: positive near and null cross is precisely the
task-specific-transfer result the current design otherwise hides.

For the later-learning bridge, let `V_c,r(k)` be mean strict public value on the
sealed reserved-family probe panel after `k in {0,1,2}` later write
opportunities, with invalid/undispatched behavior assigned the predeclared
failure value. Set

```text
G_c,r(k)    = V_c,r(k) - V_c,r(0)
gAUC2_r(c)  = [G_c,r(1) + G_c,r(2)] / 2
W_P,r       = gAUC2_r(P1) - gAUC2_r(P0)
W_U,r       = gAUC2_r(U1) - gAUC2_r(U0)
D_bridge,r  = W_P,r - W_U,r
L_P,r       = V_P1,r(2) - V_P1,r(0)
```

`D_bridge` is the sole bridge endpoint. `W_P` and `L_P` are conjunctive guards
against declaring success because unparented writes were harmful or because a
relative interaction accompanied no absolute P1 learning. Entry value,
trials-to-criterion, information gain per generated token, admitted-row yield,
supervised tokens, and action count are descriptive decompositions; none may
replace `D_bridge`.

Because four roots cannot support a population interval, the later exact
protocol should freeze development-justified practical margins
`delta_near`, `delta_cross`, `delta_retain`, `delta_D`, `delta_W`, `delta_L`, and
generic/safety tolerances, then use a rootwise spending rule. A minimal rule is
that each required contrast reaches its practical margin in at least three of
four roots, no root crosses the corresponding adverse margin, and all four raw
root rows are reported. The numeric margins are unresolved blockers now; zero
or post-hoc thresholds are not an acceptable substitute.

## Minimal negative controls and what they falsify

| control | required interpretation |
|---|---|
| direct closed lesson query | Measures recitation/recognition only. High recitation with null uncued behavior is rung (a), not teaching success. |
| explicit neutral process cue versus ordinary state | If behavior appears only with the cue, the result is prompt dependence. An uncued practical effect is mandatory. |
| same-family versus family-withheld probes | Near-only improvement is task-specific skill transfer, rung (b). Cross-family improvement is required for rung (c). |
| applicable versus counter-indicated probes | Falsifies an indiscriminate “always predict/discriminate” reflex; selective benefit is required. |
| same-checkpoint neutral shadow before childhood write | Shows whether the parent changed the child's practiced continuation rather than merely labeling an already occurring success. It remains an elicitation diagnostic. |
| `UNPARENTED_SELF` with own admitted rows and matched write dose | Isolates the complete parent-elicitation pathway from ordinary practice, self-recovery, native SFT, and optimizer exposure. |
| `AUTH_TARGET_ONLY` on the technical roots | Tests scaffold dependence only. Equivalence to authentic fading is desirable and is not a no-parent semantic null. |
| no-fixed-point state-target derangement | Tests whether correct state/action binding, rather than response syntax and frequency, is necessary. Infeasible derangement deletes the binding claim. |
| exact authentic adapter-off and pre-write checkpoint | Localizes a parent-absent change to the childhood parametric state/serving path. |
| delayed write-off `P0/U0` probe | Tests persistence of the childhood disposition without later parametric rescue. |
| `P0/P1/U0/U1` bridge factorial | Separates static entry transfer from a parenting-caused increase in the later benefit of personal writes. |
| typed-forced proposal and generic/tool panel | Prevents routing/style improvement bought by degradation of latent action quality or ordinary behavior. It cannot rescue failed free native action. |

No wrong-child, shuffled-general-lesson, or direct-lesson control should be
called a guaranteed null. A genuinely reusable process lesson may cross those
boundaries.

## Recommended fixed gate sequence

Stop permanently at the first failed scientific rung. Later success cannot
retroactively rescue it.

1. **Native writer floor.** The separate treatment-neutral writer calibration
   must demonstrate exact response labels/EOS, reproducible safe rank-8 writing,
   strict free native dispatch, typed-forced proposal preservation,
   DREAM/restoration, and generic non-erasure. Failure ends the canary before
   parenting.
2. **Generator/firewall gate.** CPU-only fixtures must establish finite-family
   identifiability, deterministic resets, headroom, action diversity,
   applicable/counter-indicated balance, legal derangement, split disjointness,
   and zero CompilerGym/repository capability or semantic leakage. No
   scientific instance may yet be generated.
3. **Parent elicitation gate.** On every retained root, record the pre-parent
   failure, parent correction, neutral shadow, application, admission, and full
   attempted-to-admitted funnel. A parent that does not increase pre-write
   supported continuation yield/quality over the neutral/U path in at least
   three roots provides no usable teaching signal.
4. **Near-transfer gate.** Authentic write must beat U, adapter-off/pre-write,
   and deranged controls on strict parent-absent same-family behavior while all
   routing/DREAM/generic safety gates pass. This licenses only task-specific
   teachability.
5. **Cross-family selective-disposition gate.** Authentic P must beat U and
   adapter-off on uncued `Static0`; the gain cannot be confined to direct
   recitation or cue-present packets, and needless investigation must remain
   within its adverse bound. Failure caps the result at rung (b).
6. **Durability gate.** The delayed write-off `Static2` effect must remain
   practically positive, `Retain` must remain inside its registered loss
   tolerance, and fresh-process deletion/remount receipts must prove that
   parent, childhood text/state, and caches are absent. Failure forbids
   “durable disposition.”
7. **Later-learning bridge gate.** `D_bridge`, `W_P`, and `L_P` must each reach
   their frozen rootwise spending margins in at least three of four roots, with
   no adverse root and no provenance, probe-return, failure-handling, resource,
   or generic-safety violation. Failure means parenting did not improve later
   personal-write learning in this canary, even if static disposition transfer
   passed.
8. **Promotion gate.** A complete pass authorizes only a decision whether to
   request exact implementation/scientific review and later headline-pilot
   authority. It supplies no confirmation evidence and no GPU or CompilerGym
   authority.

## Failure map and maximum claims

| observed pattern | maximum permitted interpretation |
|---|---|
| direct lesson score or parent/cue-present behavior only | Lesson recognition or prompt compliance, rung (a). |
| parent-absent near transfer, cross-family null | Task-specific procedural transfer within the taught family, rung (b). |
| immediate cross-family selective effect, delayed effect null | Short-lived parent-absent transfer; not durable. |
| delayed cross-family selective effect, `D_bridge` null | Durable static thought-to-action disposition, rung (c), but no evidence of improved later learning efficiency. |
| positive `D_bridge`, but `W_P` or `L_P` fails | Relative interaction driven by control harm or lack of absolute P1 learning; bridge fails. |
| cross-family/durability and all bridge guards pass | Formative evidence for the bounded bridge (c)->(d) under one fixed parent policy, child checkpoint, writer, and two non-compiler families. Still not population or CompilerGym evidence. |
| typed routing rises while typed-forced value or generic behavior falls | Interface/style overfit; reject the writer regardless of every other metric. |

Even a complete four-root pass permits at most:

> In four prospectively retained, target-blind, isolated one-parent/one-child
> canary roots, one closed child-specific process correction produced a
> selective parent-absent thought-to-action change on a family withheld from
> childhood; that change survived the registered delay, and the parented
> checkpoint showed a larger bounded benefit from two later personal writes
> than its dose-matched unparented checkpoint, under fixed non-compiler tasks
> and equal ordinary resources.

It does not establish a population parenting effect, heterogeneity across
parents or child models, general meta-learning, CompilerGym transfer, a
classroom or population mechanism, learned theory formation, broad creativity,
or the full headline result.

## Recommendation

Do not run the inherited Section-10 canary under a “learn-to-learn” or durable
disposition label. Either keep it explicitly as an immediate native-writer and
same-family teachability gate, or replace it through a new ratified change with
the minimal counterbalanced bridge canary above. The latter is the smallest
design here that makes all four outcomes observationally distinct while
preserving the one-parent/one-child topology and avoiding any CompilerGym
answer surface.
