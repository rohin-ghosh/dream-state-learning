## Immutable topology finding

**PASS without qualification.** Each P life instantiates one reset frozen
target-blind parent policy teaching one child through target-blind practice and
closed thought-to-action correction. The child must apply the correction on a
fresh homologous task, and public-world predicates—not the parent—control row
admission. The parent and prohibited childhood artifacts disappear before the
child's deployment (`v2-addendum:13-25`).

The U child is an isolated causal counterfactual with no edge to the treatment
dyad. There is no classroom, cohort, peer exchange, teacher ensemble, shared
child state/store, stateful parent across children, or population-learning
mechanism. Independent roots are iid task/life realizations under one fixed
parent policy and child checkpoint, not interacting children or samples of
different parents/models (`v2-addendum:21-25,33-71`). `P1-R0` is the public
full-package illustration under equal ordinary deployment affordances only;
parenting causality comes solely from `U0/U1/P0/P1`
(`v2-addendum:27-31`).
