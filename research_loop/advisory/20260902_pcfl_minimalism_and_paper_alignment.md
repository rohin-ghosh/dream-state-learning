# PCFL minimalism and Paper-1 alignment — fresh area-chair advisory

**Status: advisory and non-authorizing.** This is an independent scientific
assessment, not a proposal, ratification, implementation instruction, test
result, scientific-call authorization, or permission to run CPU/GPU/model,
training, LoRA, Stage C/D, or successor work. It does not adjudicate V2 or
override the repository authority process.

## Bottom line

Do **not** choose path A, a full V3 repair and implementation, by default.
The 27-class-by-24-component field/visibility regime, brokered fork runtime,
trace-total ontology, exact-CRN requirements, and total terminal machine would
be proportionate only if the paper's primary contribution were a high-assurance
protocol for a narrow synthetic causal-cascade benchmark. That is not the
declared Dream-LoRA-Think thesis.

The declared thesis is a growth claim: an agent's later-life action quality
continues to improve after strong alternatives flatten or degrade, because its
own action/outcome history is consolidated and used in later decisions. The
V2/V3 PCFL object explicitly delivers neither a LoRA result nor a lifetime
scaling curve, and its eight B3 roots are expressly descriptive rather than a
population or efficacy result. A complete V3 success would therefore be a
carefully controlled *precursor/instrument* paper result, not evidence for the
headline paper claim. Its engineering cost is large enough to crowd out the
actual empirical question.

My default recommendation is **path B: build a substantially simpler,
scientifically honest PCFL-lite benchmark as a time-bounded calibration gate,
then either carry its working world/instruments into a fresh lifetime/adapter
study or stop it cleanly.** Do not make the full V3 protocol a prerequisite for
the loop-adapter/whole-lifetime successor. If the team would not publish the
minimal text-memory result on its own, or if the simplified construct cannot
show a non-shortcut, memory-dependent action signal quickly, choose **path C**
instead and design the lifetime successor directly on untouched worlds.

## What PCFL does and does not buy the paper

PCFL has a valuable causal idea: an ordinary action yields public evidence;
target-blind memory construction uses only lawful evidence; a later decision
is scored by a legal world action; and a wrong-life/shuffled/raw-evidence
comparison can expose false memory credit. This can establish that a memory
path is not merely a stored target answer or a task-rendering shortcut. That is
useful calibration for Dream/Think and for a later same-corpus text-versus-LoRA
transport comparison.

But a target-blind, unmounted text-memory cascade is not the full recurrent
organism. It does not show policy-selected information gathering, durable
parametric transport, learned dreaming or LOOP policy, improvement over a
life, or baseline saturation. The project thesis and experiment map already
treat those as separate arrows. Calling the B3 result a “stress test” is
honest; treating its completion machinery as the main scientific bottleneck
for the lifetime paper is not.

The eight roots and 908 nested rows/root further invert the value ratio. The
rows make failure accounting rich, but they do not create 908 independent
lives. The V3 repair's 24 components, 648 matrix cells, and closure machinery
would mostly strengthen conformance evidence for that finite stress test. They
would not add independent lifetime units, new duration checkpoints, a LoRA
comparison, or a measured saturation curve.

## Essential controls versus protocol ceremony

The following are essential scientific controls in any simplified B or direct
C study. They prevent an apparent memory/lifetime effect from being target
leakage, post-selection, ordinary long context, or a starved baseline.

| Keep as a hard requirement | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| World-/life-level development versus untouched evaluation split, frozen before results | Prevents renderer, prompt, rank, or baseline selection on evaluation outcomes. |
| Hidden target/truth/scorer never enters the memory writer, controller input, or training target | Blocks direct answer and score leakage. |
| Fresh reset of every per-life state (adapter, text store, context/KV/cache, optimizer, RNG namespace) | Makes a life rather than process residue the unit of attribution. |
| One predeclared legal-action endpoint plus predeclared lifetime checkpoints and failure denominator | Measures later behavior, not QA/read receipts or selectively completed rows. |
| Strong native controls: frozen/no-memory, honest long context, iterative raw RAG, and wrong-life or shuffled memory | Separates any structured-memory result from context, raw evidence, and generic policy priors. |
| Same admitted experience corpus for text and per-life LoRA, with read/budget and resource reporting | Necessary for a substrate comparison; otherwise corpus, controller, and representation are confounded. |
| Small finite shortcut audit: target-only/current-state-only/renderer-signature predictors plus held-out relabel/render variants | Bounds the obvious synthetic-world decoder without claiming to eliminate all pretrained priors. |
| Complete root-level traces, frozen configuration hashes, and an independent reproduction pass | Lets reviewers inspect lineage and discover a mistaken information path. |

The following are not generally essential to the lifetime claim and should be
removed unless the narrow PCFL protocol itself is the intended paper object:

- An exhaustive 27-by-24 visibility matrix for every serialized metadata,
  cache, receipt, failure alternative, and post-B3 reporter. A compact
  dataflow/taint table for the few model-visible and target-bearing channels is
  sufficient; ordinary software isolation and trace audit can cover the rest.
- A bespoke content-blind randomizer, nonreifiable capability broker, exact
  physical destruction claim, and token-level common-random-number proof. If
  the diagnostic fork is retained, use local process isolation and logical
  reset; if it remains the dominant implementation burden, omit the fork from
  PCFL-lite rather than make unmount mediation the paper's central result.
- Eight-root, 908-row arithmetic, dual AS-EXT candidates, full Pareto
  dominance over an enormous resource vector, and a terminal taxonomy for
  every abort/review state. These are useful release governance, but neither
  establishes the expected lifetime curve nor fixes the core causal contrast.
- A post-B3 selector and associative-LoRA eligibility state machine. This
  handles authorization and reporting, not the empirical mechanism. A later
  LoRA study needs its own preregistered design regardless of PCFL's label.

This is not an argument to relax leakage discipline. It is an argument to put
the discipline around the *few causal paths whose violation changes the paper
conclusion*, rather than attempting to formalize the entire runtime as a
research contribution.

## Three paths

| Path | Scientific value | Main cost/risk | Area-chair view |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Full V3 repair and implementation** | Best possible evidence for a very narrow finite text-memory causal-cascade protocol. | Months of protocol/runtime closure; residual semantic-shortcut uncertainty remains; no LoRA or lifetime-scaling evidence at completion. | Choose only if the paper is deliberately retitled as a benchmark/protocol paper and that result alone is worth publishing. Otherwise reject as overengineered. |
| **B. PCFL-lite, then fresh lifetime study** | Preserves target-blindness, held-out action, leakage tests, and fair text/LoRA transport while producing a useful feasibility/falsification result. | Cannot support V2/V3's strongest causal-cascade or exhaustive-conformance language. ## Minimum publishable empirical result

The smallest result that could support a credible paper is not a 7,264-row
text protocol release. It is a complete, held-out **world-life** result with a
single behavioral endpoint:

> On predeclared unseen world roots and later-life goals beyond the honest
> context window, target-blind memory built solely from that life’s earlier
> public action/outcome experience improves executed legal-action success (or
> reduces tries-to-goal) relative to frozen/no-memory, honest long-context,
> iterative raw-RAG, and wrong-life/shuffled-memory controls; the same admitted
> corpus is compared in structured text and freshly reset per-life LoRA.

Report every independent world-life, all failures, the action traces, the
lifetime curve, and renderer/relabel shortcut checks. The claim should remain
narrow: a bounded synthetic environment exhibits memory-dependent held-out
action under the registered information and budget contract. If text wins but
LoRA does not transport faithfully, that is a useful negative substrate result
and the lifetime paper must not claim Dream-LoRA-Think. If LoRA merely matches
text at short lifetime, it is not evidence that weights matter; extend the
lifetime until the prompt-budget/capacity distinction is real or report the
tie.

For the stated headline, this minimum must be extended—not replaced—by
multiple presealed lifetime lengths and a strong-baseline curve. The publishable
headline requires observed later-life improvement on structural/analogy goals
after controls are measured, not assumed, to plateau or degrade. The PCFL-lite
result is therefore a go/no-go instrument, not the headline itself.

## Decision rule

Before authoring another formal repair, answer these four binary questions
using only a lightweight implementation/design review:

1. Can a single synthetic world support a legal held-out action endpoint that
   target-only/current-state/renderer-only predictors and nonadaptive atom
   policies do not solve?
2. Can one frozen, target-blind admitted-experience corpus be mounted as both
   structured text and a fresh per-life LoRA, with strong raw-RAG and
   wrong-life controls, without inventing a new privileged runtime?
3. Can the planned study produce several genuine independent world-life
   observations across presealed lifetime lengths, rather than merely many
   nested evaluations of eight roots?
4. Would the team still submit the resulting narrow calibration result if the
   LoRA arm tied or lost to text/RAG?

Choose **B** only if answers 1–3 are yes and the answer to 4 is yes. It should
then be explicitly scoped as PCFL-lite calibration; no full V3 closure, fork
mediation claim, or rank pyramid is required. Choose **C** if 1 or 2 is no,
or if 4 is no: the protocol is not a paper-relevant instrument, and the new
study should target the loop adapter’s operation-transfer and whole-lifetime
endpoint directly with fresh worlds. Choose **A** only if the decision owner
affirmatively values a finite protocol/benchmark paper over the lifetime
scaling paper and accepts that its conclusion is descriptive text-memory
conformance, not Dream-LoRA-Think scaling evidence.

The rank/LoRA advisories reinforce this ordering. Rank is capacity, not a
timescale; a rank pyramid and weight merge should not rescue a weak PCFL
signal. If path C is chosen, begin with the separate frozen-LOOP, whole-world
operation-transfer assay and the small development-only rank response surface;
advance to the content-memory/action factorial only after that transfer gate
passes. This is closer to the declared paper, while preserving the same
nonnegotiable life-reset, target-embargo, and fair-baseline discipline.
