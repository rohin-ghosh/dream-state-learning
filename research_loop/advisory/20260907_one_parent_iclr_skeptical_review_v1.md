# Skeptical ICLR review: one-parent Experience Models evidence

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: read-only advisory. This review authorizes no architecture change,
implementation, target generation, model or tokenizer call, adapter operation,
CompilerGym execution, external access, or GPU use. It makes no change to the
frozen/source-bound proposal.

## Review boundary

I reviewed `AGENTS.md`, `REVIEW_PACK.md`,
`paper/iclr2027_experience_models/main.tex`, the two ICLR readiness/critical-path
notes, and the source-bound one-parent v2 proposal surface: the human directive,
scope proposal, source-binding manifest, v1 headline plan, v2 addendum,
`ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` contract and prompt schema, and v2 deliberation workflow.
The inspected manifest SHA-256 is
`d91e2a017a26f71264291aa5be899e086d3cd6666d9e35f4f735bd9e167ded9c`;
the inspected v1, v2-addendum, active-text, and manuscript bytes match the hashes
listed in that manifest.

## Rating and verdict

**Current rating: 2/10, strong reject; confidence 5/5.** There is no confirmatory
one-parent result, no live `U0/U1/P0/P1/R0` root, and no passed writer or
active-text certificate. The present submission would therefore be a detailed
protocol plus a carefully delimited exploratory failure, not an empirical ICLR
paper supporting its intended contribution.

**If `D` alone passes: 4/10, reject.** A passing interaction would be a useful
and unusually clean fixed-protocol result, but it would not by itself show that
the parented child improves during life, forms connected or compressed
experiential knowledge, traverses or expands knowledge toward goals, or beats a
strong memory system.

**If the entire registered sequence through `T_R0` passes, the active-text
certificate passes, and the claims are narrowed exactly: about 5/10,
borderline reject.** That package could support a solid bounded systems claim:
for one fixed parent policy, child checkpoint, writer, and CompilerGym root
distribution, parenting increased the benefit of enabling personal writes;
the parented write-on system improved over its entry state and outperformed the
specified active-memory systems under the stated, unequal-total-resource
comparison. The paper would still lack the construct evidence needed for its
connected/compressed, goal-directed, or broad meta-intelligence story.

The engineering discipline is a strength. In particular, the source binding,
one-row-per-root analysis, prospective failure accounting, strict action
channel, parent deletion, fixed sequence, and explicit limits on the `P1-R0`
comparison are much stronger than typical agent evaluations. My rejection is
not about protocol care. It is about the gap between the behavioral endpoint
and the cognitive/representational thesis.

## Most likely reject reasons

### 1. Construct validity: the endpoint rewards useful compiler routines, not the claimed experiential cognition

The primary task score is best instruction-count reduction among up to twelve
dispatched action sequences. It does not measure hypothesis quality,
information-seeking choice, path traversal, causal credit assignment, or
knowledge expansion. A child can win by consolidating and repeatedly emitting a
broadly useful pass routine. The project's own v6.1 evidence makes this the
default skeptical explanation: the surviving signal concentrated on a supplied
routine and reached a local plateau. New programs prevent literal program
memorization but do not prevent reuse of a universal action template.

Free-routing and typed-forced proposal assays rule out some interface failures;
they do not distinguish an improving investigation policy from stronger habit
imitation. More roots make this estimate precise but do not repair the construct.

### 2. `D` is not a sufficient success criterion for “learns better”

For example, `W_P=-0.01`, `W_U=-0.07`, and `D=+0.06` can satisfy the `D` margin
while deployment writes harm both branches. The v2 fixed sequence then stops at
failed `W_P`, but it still permits the rung-1 sentence that parenting “increased
the later benefit” of writes. That mathematical statement is defensible; the
paper's stronger language that parenting improved the child's **ability to
learn** is not.

For the intended learning claim, the minimum joint evidentiary event is:

```text
D passes AND W_P passes AND L_terminal passes,
with the deterministic routing/provenance gates intact.
```

`C_public` and `T_R0` are additionally necessary for the full public system
comparison. The manuscript should not call a `D`-only result lifetime
improvement or meta-intelligence.

### 3. The parenting treatment is aligned to the writer's admission grammar

The nursery lessons teach `PREDICT_UPDATE`, `DISCRIMINATE`, and `SCOPE`.
Deployment `THINK_TO_ACT` admission requires a numeric prediction and either an
improvement or a typed `REVISION` after surprise with a changed action family.
Thus parenting can raise the number and composition of eligible scientific
training rows simply by teaching the exact behavior the deterministic writer
admits. Rehearsal fills equalize optimizer positions, not scientific content.

That is a legitimate causal pathway for this designed system, but it weakens
the interpretation that parenting taught a domain-general way of learning. A
reviewer will ask whether `D` is mostly “parenting taught the child how to pass
the write filter.” The plan should expose, by arm and cut, eligible-row rate,
scientific-versus-rehearsal fraction, target-type mix, write/mount success,
and outcome-supported yield. This mediation accounting cannot by itself prove
general learning, but it can prevent a misleading interpretation.

### 4. “Strong memory baseline” is currently a self-certified bespoke comparator

`ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` is carefully specified and materially better than a dummy
transcript. Its disjoint semantic, retrieval, use, and headroom gates are good.
Nevertheless, passing author-defined thresholds on synthetic fixtures and 24
development programs does not make it a competitive reproduction of ACE,
ReMem, ReasoningBank, MemoPilot, or another named method. The contract itself
properly calls it an ACE/ReMem-style functional comparator.

The strongest scientifically clean statement is therefore singular and local:
“LoRA writes added value beyond this prospectively validated active-text
mechanism,” based on `P1-P0`. “Superior to strong memory baselines” in the plural,
or superiority to named methods, is unsupported. `P1-R0` is especially easy to
overread because total training compute, childhood history, and learned state
are intentionally unequal.

### 5. Generalization is over roots from `Q`, not over parents, children, models, or environments

Thirty-two roots are genuine analysis units for the registered finite-universe
task distribution. They are not 32 parent policies, 32 child checkpoints, or 32
deployment domains. A precise interval around `mu_D` does not license “parents
teach agents to learn,” “models acquire meta-intelligence,” or a general
Experience Models law. The defensible subject is singular: this fixed parenting
policy applied to this fixed child system over `Q`.

### 6. The proposed horizon is too short for broad “lifetime” and forgetting claims

Three deployment writes and 48 wake programs can establish a finite within-life
learning curve. They do not show indefinite stability. The repeated sealed
probe panel is a forward-transfer panel; the v2 packet does not define a clean
backward-retention or forgetting endpoint. The manuscript currently promises
forward transfer, backward retention, and forgetting even though the v2
addendum explicitly removes the latter undefined claims.

### 7. The current manuscript is materially ahead of the source-bound claim surface

The manuscript presently:

- calls the textual memory “validated” before its once-only certificates pass;

- can be read as giving agents the same evolving memory, when only the policy is
  common and isolated on-policy contents differ;

- promises early slope, backward retention, and forgetting despite their
  removal in the v2 addendum;

- retains a LEAFE-style diagnostic that v2 removes;

- advertises a terminal parametric-carrier result, while the permitted terminal
  diagnostics identify explicit-text link contribution and fixed-history
  action/outcome binding, not a parametric carrier; and

- calls the factorial a diagnostic even though `D` is the sole primary.

These are repairable after ratification, but fatal if left in a submitted paper
because they make the central claim boundary internally inconsistent.

## Claims the CompilerGym headline cannot support even if `D` passes

Even a clean, well-powered `D` cannot establish any of the following:

1. A graph, linked structure, compressed code, or human-readable experiential
   representation exists inside the LoRA.

2. The child deliberately traverses connected knowledge in response to a goal.
   Automatic textual one-hop expansion is not a chosen reasoning path.

3. The child expands knowledge toward a goal. No endpoint traces the required
   memory-to-query-to-new-outcome-to-write-to-later-benefit relay.

4. Personal writes improve the parented child's absolute performance. That
   requires at least `W_P` and `L_terminal`, not `D` alone.

5. The full system beats a strong memory reference. That requires the entire
   fixed sequence through `C_public` and `T_R0` plus every active-text strength
   gate.

6. Parametric memory is intrinsically superior to external memory. Both `P1`
   and `P0/R0` use active text, their stores diverge on policy, and `P1-R0` is
   unequal in childhood and fitting compute.

7. Superiority to named or plural memory baselines, a batch-learning method,
   or an oracle memory system.

8. General parenting, general meta-intelligence, general learning-to-learn,
   creativity, novel strategy discovery, or transfer across deployment
   ontologies.

9. DREAM caused the gain, correct links exist in weights, or correct
   action/outcome binding has an on-policy causal effect. The planned terminal
   diagnostics support narrower, explicitly stated conclusions.

10. Backward retention, absence of forgetting, open-ended improvement, or
    improvement after generic memory saturation. The optional local-plateau
    composite supports only its exact 16--48-program, R0, budget-bound sentence.

## Fatal versus repairable issues

### Fatal for the broad Experience Models claim under the current headline

- No amount of additional CompilerGym roots, tighter confidence intervals, or
  routing diagnostics can turn task-score improvement into evidence of
  connected/compressed parametric knowledge or goal-directed expansion.

- A `D`-only pass cannot support positive lifetime learning. Releasing broad
  learning language after `W_P` or `L_terminal` fails would be a causal-claim
  error, not a presentation issue.

- `P1-R0` cannot support equal-compute, equal-history, equal-memory, or
  parametric-carrier superiority by design.

- One bespoke comparator cannot support plural or named-method superiority,
  even if it earns the protocol's internal “strong” label.

### Repairable without classroom or population work

- Narrow the main claim to one fixed dyad policy and finite CompilerGym `Q`.

- Require `D + W_P + L_terminal` jointly before using “improved ability to
  learn”; use `D` alone only for a relative write-benefit interaction.

- Make `P1-P0`, not `P1-R0`, the leading evidence that LoRA adds value beyond
  the common active-text mechanism. Keep `P1-R0` as the explicitly qualified
  full-package illustration.

- Report writer-yield mediation and the exact resource frontier. This does not
  remove the treatment/writer alignment, but it shows whether the effect is
  mostly increased admission, altered target content, or post-write behavior.

- Conform the manuscript to v2: remove premature “validated/strong,” LEAFE,
  carrier, early-slope, backward-retention, and forgetting language unless a
  later ratified protocol supplies those exact measurements.

- Add one small controlled, single-agent compositional-relay benchmark if the
  connected/traversal/expansion thesis is to remain a contribution.

## Minimum additional benchmark component

The minimum useful addition is **one controlled single-agent compositional
experience relay**, not another parenting cohort and not more CompilerGym
programs. It can reuse the fixed parented/unparented entry checkpoints and
remains within one-parent/one-child scope. It needs a separate source-bound
workflow and authorization, as the current v2 scope correctly requires.

One root should contain four sealed phases over nonce symbols and a randomized
relation/action ontology:

1. **Acquire.** The child dispatches actions whose public outcomes reveal
   atomic relations. Every candidate memory item therefore has own-action,
   public-outcome provenance.

2. **Traverse.** A fresh goal is solvable only by composing at least two edges;
   no direct answer or complete path appeared in training. Counterfactual goal
   variants over the same acquired graph require different paths, separating
   goal-conditioned traversal from habitual action reuse.

3. **Expand.** One necessary edge is missing. The child chooses among legal
   information-gathering actions; only some reduce the registered hypothesis
   set. The selected action yields a new public outcome and a later write may
   admit it.

4. **Use later.** A still-later fresh goal requires both an old relation and the
   newly acquired relation. Score success only after the relevant write
   boundary, with no direct stored answer.

The minimum interventions are authentic binding plus writes, adapter-off,
degree/marginal-preserving link or binding derangement, necessary-bridge cut,
and same-corpus active text. Read the parametric condition with parent text,
raw ledger, and actor-visible memory removed; read the active-text condition
with the same experience corpus and retrieval budget. This localizes behavioral
use without pretending to decode a graph from weights.

The minimum endpoints are:

- fresh two-or-more-edge composition success;

- goal-appropriate path/action choice under counterfactual goals;

- information gain of the chosen query, or exact reduction in the finite
  hypothesis set, plus surprise-to-query latency and generated tokens;

- later value attributable to the newly acquired edge; and

- authentic-minus-deranged and authentic-minus-bridge-cut effects for both the
  LoRA and same-corpus text carrier.

If the word **compressed** is retained, this same benchmark also needs a
compact-carrier versus denotationally equivalent expanded-carrier condition,
with a canonical grammar, all shared costs, serialized description length or
supervised target bits, a preregistered rate threshold, and behavioral
non-inferiority on the fresh compositions. Even then the defensible phrase is
“behavioral use of compact connected experiential code,” not “a compressed
graph exists inside the weights.” If this rate-distortion component is not
affordable, remove compression from the paper rather than substitute byte
counts.

This single relay is the smallest component I can see that discriminates the
paper's thesis from routine reinforcement. A second ordinary optimization
benchmark would broaden task coverage but still fail to measure traversal or
expansion.

## Information-gain-ranked action list

1. **Run the already specified writer/teachability and four-root spending gates
   before any fixed-32 confirmation spend, after all required authorization.**
   The highest-value question is whether a healthy native writer produces any
   parent-absent practiced disposition and nonpathological scientific rows at
   all. A failure makes every higher claim moot.

2. **Freeze the interpretation gate `D AND W_P AND L_terminal` now.** This costs
   no extra scientific run and prevents the most likely positive-result
   overclaim. If only `D` passes, report relative write-benefit moderation; do
   not report improved learning ability.

3. **Pilot the controlled compositional relay under a separate deliberation.**
   Its first information-gain question is simply whether authentic versus
   deranged/bridge-cut experience changes unseen multi-edge behavior with the
   actor-visible memory removed. A null result should delete the connected and
   goal-directed thesis from this submission.

4. **Make the validated `P1-P0` contrast the main memory result and demote
   `P1-R0` to the qualified full-package figure.** Report certificate results,
   actual memory yield, token/call/fit costs, and performance-resource curves.
   If the certificate fails, use “active-memory reference,” never “strong.”

5. **Publish arm-by-cut writer mediation before interpreting `D`.** At minimum:
   eligible and accepted native rows, rehearsal fills, target types, supported
   observation count, transactional failures, labeled tokens, and subsequent
   behavior. This is essential for distinguishing protocol-alignment throughput
   from improvement after comparable experiential evidence.

6. **Add a live named memory implementation only if the paper insists on plural
   or named-method superiority.** Otherwise keep the claim singular and local
   to `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED`. A literature comparison table is not an experimental
   baseline.

7. **Add a backward-retention panel only if the manuscript keeps retention or
   forgetting language.** It should use predeclared earlier-task homologues or
   another leakage-safe retrospective assay; otherwise delete those endpoints.

8. **Do not spend scarce roots on compression, DREAM attribution, or local
   plateau after the headline fails.** Those are conditional mechanism claims,
   not rescues for absent lifetime improvement.

## Bottom line

The source-bound v2 plan can, if every sequential gate passes, produce a
credible narrow result about a fixed parenting protocol increasing the benefit
of per-life LoRA writes in a finite compiler-optimization life, with unusually
good provenance and failure handling. It cannot make the CompilerGym curve do
the work of a knowledge-structure experiment. Connected/compressed experience,
goal-directed traversal, and knowledge expansion require the controlled relay
above or must remain motivation. Superiority should be stated only against the
single validated in-house active-text mechanism, with `P1-P0` carrying the
cleanest claim and `P1-R0` explicitly remaining a full-package, unequal-history
comparison.
