# One-parent closest-work audit v2: attack cross-critique and deadline adjudication

**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** concise read-only advisory; no frozen source, implementation, task,
model, adapter, benchmark, or GPU state was changed  
**Binding scope:** one reset frozen parent teaches one child; no classroom,
cohort, peers, parent ensemble, cross-child state, shared memory, or population
learning  
**Decision:** **protect the parenting factorial and cut the representational
thesis. Do not replace the spending canary, add named-method runs, or build the
compositional relay on the September 7--16 critical path.**

The official deadlines are September 18, 2026, 11:59 PM AoE for the abstract
and September 25, 2026, 11:59 PM AoE for the paper
([ICLR dates](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/Dates),
[call for papers](https://www.iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers)). On
September 7 that leaves 11 and 18 days. The unimplemented, unratified v2
headline is already the critical path.

## 1. Cross-critique of the two attacks

### Skeptical review

**ACCEPT** its scientific claim boundaries:

- `D` alone is not “improved ability to learn”; that phrase requires the joint
  survival of `D`, `W_P`, and `L_terminal`.
- `P1-P0` is a total closed-loop effect of enabling writes on top of this common
  active-text policy, because later actions and stores diverge.
- `P1-R0` is a full-package comparison with deployment-only parity, not a
  parenting, equal-history, equal-compute, or carrier contrast.
- `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` is one prospectively validated in-house comparator, not
  plural or named-method superiority.
- arm-by-cut writer yield, target mix, rehearsal fills, fit failures, and later
  behavior must be reported because parenting is aligned with the writer's
  prediction/revision admission grammar.
- CompilerGym task value cannot prove connected/compressed structure in LoRA,
  child-chosen traversal, or goal-directed knowledge expansion.

**REJECT** its implication that the compositional relay is a minimum addition
for the narrow parenting paper. That is true only if the paper retains the
older connected/traversal/expansion thesis. The v2 factorial already
distinguishes the remaining claim from the closest prior art: none of TMEM,
PEAM, EVAF, LEAFE, or Learning on the Job estimates whether prior target-blind
process teaching changes the later marginal benefit of personal writes after
teacher deletion. A relay would strengthen construct validity, but is not part
of the minimal identification set for that interaction.

**DEFER judgmental pre-scores** such as 2/10, 4/10, or 5/10. “No evidence yet”
is correct; an acceptance rating before seeing a result, implementation
quality, or final paper is not an actionable scientific gate.

### Teachability-canary attack

**ACCEPT** its diagnosis: the inherited Section-10 canary identifies native
writer health, parent elicitation, scaffold fading, state/target binding, and
immediate parent-absent same-family near transfer. It does not establish a
durable cross-family disposition or improved later learning.

**REJECT replacing it now with the proposed four-cell bridge canary.** The
replacement adds a new counterbalanced family-withholding generator,
applicable/counter-indicated packets, delayed interference, two later fits,
sealed learning curves, a second `P0/P1/U0/U1` interaction, new numeric margins,
and new review/ratification. That is a miniature duplicate of the actual
spending pilot and headline. It would delay, not de-risk, the only confirmatory
experiment.

Keep the current canary and label it literally: **writer + parent-absent
same-family near-transfer spending gate, not headline evidence**. The fresh
CompilerGym `D` experiment is the test of later learning after cross-environment
transfer. If the canary passes but `D` fails, the result stops at near-transfer;
the canary cannot rescue it.

## 2. Minimum baseline set under the September deadlines

| Item | Decision | Exact reason / permitted claim |
|---|---|---|
| `P0/P1/U0/U1` with dose-matched U childhood | **ACCEPT — mandatory** | This is the only baseline structure that identifies the novel parenting-by-write interaction rather than entry competence or ordinary practice. |
| Common isolated `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` in all four cells | **ACCEPT — mandatory** | Makes `P1-P0` an incremental total-effect comparison above a non-static text path. It must pass its once-only certificate or lose “strong.” |
| `R0` raw actor + isolated active text | **ACCEPT — retain** | Supplies the public full-package illustration and local resource frontier. Never call it equal total history/compute or the parenting control. |
| Current writer/teachability controls: U-self, adapter-off/pre-write, target-only, state-target derangement, routing/generic non-erasure | **ACCEPT — mandatory spending gate** | These falsify broken writing, prompt dependence, syntax-only fitting, wrong binding, and erasure. They do not establish cross-family meta-learning. |
| Arm-by-cut writer mediation | **ACCEPT — mandatory reporting** | Necessary to reveal whether `D` is carried mainly by admission throughput or by benefits after comparable scientific rows. Treat it as decomposition, not formal mediation. |
| [TMEM](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04536v1) live reimplementation | **DEFER for this deadline; citation mandatory** | It is mandatory only for “our writer advances online parametric memory.” The narrow paper instead claims a parent-induced interaction. A rushed partial TMEM arm would be neither faithful nor powered. |
| Naive/raw/native continual LoRA | **DEFER; required for a later compiler-method claim** | Needed to show Dream/Sleep compilation beats ordinary continual SFT, but not needed to estimate `D` when every factorial cell uses the same frozen writer law. Delete compiler-superiority language. |
| [LEAFE](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.16843v1) matched terminal batch fit | **DEFER; citation mandatory** | Required only for periodic-versus-batch or reflection-internalization superiority. V2 correctly removed the descriptive LEAFE allocation; do not restore it now. |
| [PEAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27762v2) procedural/failure-correction arm | **DEFER; citation mandatory** | Required only for claims of better embodied procedural internalization, failure-aware consolidation, or forgetting control. Remove those claims. |
| [EVAF](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26806v1) selective-LoRA arm | **DEFER; citation mandatory** | Required only if selective admission, sparse writing, memory depth, or selection--actuation is claimed as the method contribution. Do not make those claims. |
| [Learning on the Job](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.22157v1) / Spark live baseline | **DEFER; citation and explicit distinction mandatory** | It is the closest active-text precedent. `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` may be called a functional comparator, never a Spark reproduction or evidence of named-method superiority. |
| ACE, ReasoningBank, ReMem, MemoPilot, FLEX, CL-Bench, Macaron-V1, When CL Moves to Memory | **CITE; no new run** | These define external-memory, continual-learning, and terminology boundaries. They do not answer the target-blind parenting interaction. |

Thus the **minimum experimental baseline set is the already frozen five-service
roster plus its canary controls and certificates**. The minimum literature set
is broader, but citation does not imply reproduction. If the paper insists on
being a parametric-memory *method* paper rather than a developmental causal
paper, this minimum is no longer sufficient; TMEM-style and naive/native LoRA
become required, and the responsible decision is a later venue rather than a
rushed September add-on.

## 3. Does the compositional relay materially distinguish the claim?

### What it would distinguish

A properly controlled relay—own action reveals atomic relations, a fresh goal
requires an unseen two-edge composition, a necessary edge is missing, memory
changes an information-seeking action, the new public result is written, and a
later goal needs both old and new edges—would add an important construct none
of the five closest works isolates with the same interventions:

| Prior | Increment supplied by a valid relay |
|---|---|
| TMEM | Goes beyond online QA-to-LoRA performance to prospective multi-edge composition, chosen information gain, and authentic-versus-bridge-cut/binding interventions. |
| PEAM | Separates relational acquisition/traversal from procedural skill imitation and failure-correction rehearsal. |
| EVAF | Goes beyond durable goal tendency after unload to action-grounded acquisition of a missing relation and later compositional use. |
| LEAFE | Goes beyond batch recovery imitation to a new public observation caused by a memory-dependent query, followed by a later write and later benefit. |
| Learning on the Job | Same-corpus text/LoRA and adapter-off interventions could compare external rule access with parametric transport on fresh compositions. |

That is **material for connected use, goal-conditioned traversal, and
knowledge expansion**. It is not evidence that a graph literally exists in
LoRA, and it does not make online LoRA, reflection, or external memory novel.

### What the current relay proposal does not automatically distinguish

The relay does not strengthen the paper's *parenting* novelty unless it also
uses the parented and dose-matched unparented entry checkpoints and retains a
write-off/on interaction with positive parented-write and absolute-learning
guards. Authentic-versus-deranged, adapter-off, and text-versus-LoRA controls
alone identify experience binding/carrier use, not a parent-induced change in
later learnability.

The minimum parenting-compatible relay would therefore require all of:

```text
P0/P1/U0/U1 on one sealed relay packet
D_relay plus W_P_relay and L_P_relay guards
parent/nursery deletion and an ontology withheld from childhood
counterfactual goals requiring different paths
authentic vs binding-deranged vs necessary-bridge-cut experience
same-corpus active text and a fixed-store adapter-off read
```

That is a separately powered experiment, not a cheap diagnostic. The
teachability attack's proposed bridge canary supplies the parenting factorial
and withheld-family/delay logic, while the skeptical review's relay supplies
multi-edge composition and missing-edge information seeking. Combining them
would be scientifically coherent—and far too large for the current critical
path.

### Relay decision

**DEFER for the ICLR September submission.** Remove connected, compressed,
child-directed traversal, and goal-directed knowledge-expansion claims from
the abstract/title/contributions. Preserve the relay as the first post-headline
experiment for a later revision or venue. Do not run an unpowered relay after
seeing the headline merely as a favorable appendix rescue.

Only reconsider this defer decision if the full headline is sealed by
September 16 **and** a separate relay already has ratified bytes, model-free
fixtures, independent review, and a predeclared resource envelope before any
relay output exists. Those conditions are not currently met.

## 4. Deadline-bound action decisions

1. **ACCEPT:** freeze the manuscript claim now as the fixed-dyad `D` question;
   require `D + W_P + L_terminal` for “improved later learning.”
2. **ACCEPT:** keep the inherited near-transfer canary as Gate 5 and move
   directly to the exact four-root spending pilot if it passes.
3. **ACCEPT:** keep writer-yield mediation and active-text certificate results
   in the main evidence table.
4. **REJECT:** no new named baseline implementation, relay generator, expanded
   canary, compression assay, backward-retention panel, or DREAM attribution
   before the headline run.
5. **REJECT:** no plural “strong baselines,” first online parametric learning,
   writer superiority, connected/compressed LoRA, general parenting, or broad
   meta-intelligence language.
6. **DEFER:** TMEM-style, naive/native LoRA, LEAFE batch, PEAM/EVAF functional
   controls, Spark reproduction, and the combined parenting-compatible relay
   to a later source-bound experiment.
7. **GO/NO-GO:** if v2 is not ratified and implementable by September 9, or no
   healthy writer plus parent-absent near transfer exists by September 12, stop
   promising the C4 headline. By September 16 the abstract may state only a
   sealed surviving rung. If no meaningful rung survives, do not inflate the
   protocol into an ICLR result.

## 5. ICLR-facing bottom line

The minimum credible September paper is:

> For one fixed parent policy and child checkpoint, under a target-blind
> nursery and a fresh CompilerGym root distribution, does process parenting
> increase the total closed-loop benefit of enabling the child's own later
> LoRA writes, beyond dose-matched own practice and with the same evolving
> active-text policy in every arm?

This is already distinct from the audited nearest works. Its value comes from
the factorial and deletion boundary, not from inventing per-life LoRA or
experience memory. The compositional relay would materially deepen the
representational/goal-directed story, but it is neither necessary for this
narrow causal claim nor feasible without displacing the evidence required for
the September submission.
