# PCFL-Compose opposite-direction cross-critique and Paper-1 adjudication

**Date:** 2026-09-02

**Status:** read-only scientific advisory. This artifact is not architecture
consensus, human ratification, implementation scope, model/provider/network or
GPU authorization, experiment approval, or evidence for a scientific claim.

## Verdict

Choose:

```text
Paper-1 candidate core: PCFL-Compose factorized action law
Mandatory negative regime: matched independent compound-action table
Small mechanism diagnostic: directly observed old-factor/operator retention
Do not retain: a second full PCFL-Stream headline or parity-door headline
```

This is **Compose core plus an independent-table control**, not a two-world
hybrid paper. The independent condition supplies the unstructured-memory
falsifier inside one renderer, action interface, lifetime schedule, target
constructor, and analysis. A separate Stream core would double the scientific
surface while preserving the very lookup degeneracy that motivated the
red-team.

The recommendation is conditional. Two weeks is enough for a CPU-certified
generator and a text-level falsification pilot, not a claim-grade paper. If the
primary generic compiler cannot prospectively infer the factor law, or if
compiled text offers no action or fully accounted target-time resource
advantage over strong raw RAG/A-MEM, stop before LoRA and reconsider **neither**
as the honest Paper-1 answer.

## Attack on the “safer Stream core, schema sentinel” recommendation

The safer recommendation confuses engineering risk with paper risk.
Independent PCFL-Stream is easier to generate and certify, but after the
transition-tree audit its central computation is an exact indexed lookup of
independent bits followed by a supplied planner. That has four consequences.

First, an exact graph or sufficient-statistic program is not merely a ceiling;
it is the obviously appropriate algorithm. It stores the minimal independent
information losslessly, retrieves the required edge by public key, and executes
the path. DREAM has nothing to abstract, A-MEM has no organization problem to
solve, and LoRA has no principled accuracy or storage advantage. A learned
system can at best approximate a tiny exact data structure with more training
cost and less reliability.

Second, increasing independent bits gives a valid retention curve but not a
capability-abstraction curve. Longer D4 paths do not repair this. They multiply
independent lookup success probabilities while the action grammar remains
supplied. Cross-era paths are joins over old and new keys, not prospective
generalization beyond witnessed atoms.

Third, the expected negative result is scientifically weak. “An exact graph
beats LoRA at storing random edges” is foreseeable from the construct, not a
surprising diagnosis of current memory systems. The area-chair's benchmark
accept path relied on the conjunction of causal entropy, twins, age/depth,
substrate comparison, and action scoring. The later lookup audit materially
weakens the claimed action and constructive-memory components of that
conjunction. Causal measurement cleanliness alone may support an assay or
benchmark note, but it does not make the paper thesis compelling.

Fourth, a small Compose sentinel cannot rescue a Stream headline. If Compose
succeeds, the most interesting result is relegated to a diagnostic and the
paper's core estimand is wrong. If Compose fails, Stream remains a storage
benchmark whose strongest baseline is dictated by construction. A dual-world
paper also creates two target generators, two depth semantics, two source
policies, and two baseline interpretations under a resource envelope already
judged near-infeasible.

Thus Stream's genuine advantages—linear exact entropy, easy twins, and clean
retention—should be preserved as controls inside Compose, not as a second
headline. A small directly observed-operator retention panel can verify atomic
installation and forgetting. The matched independent-table regime can test
whether the compiler hallucinates structure. Neither requires carrying the
full transition-tree world into Paper 1.

## Attack on PCFL-Compose

Compose fixes the lookup construct, but it introduces serious and potentially
fatal problems of its own.

### It may be a supplied algebra exercise

The visible two-part tool labels, anchor row and column, `S_6` state space, two
composition orders, and chord protocol nearly announce the solution. If the
compiler prompt or output schema names this family, DREAM only canonicalizes
observed permutations and selects one order bit. That is known-grammar matrix
completion, not autonomous abstraction.

The primary compiler therefore cannot be told the factor equation, the anchor
formula, the two-order hypothesis set, or whether the life is factorized. It
may receive only a frozen generic instruction such as “compress supported
causal regularities that predict later action,” plus a general-purpose action-
model representation language. That identical prompt must be used on
factorized and independent-table lives. A class-informed compiler belongs only
as a ceiling. If only the class-informed condition works, the allowable claim
is known-family completion, not DREAM discovery.

Even a generic operator DSL supplies some representational bias. Its primitives
and parser must be frozen before development outcomes and disclosed. The
mechanical verifier may test committed predictions against eligible public
chords; it may not propose factors, expose hidden target pairs, enumerate
candidate laws, or repair the compiler after failure.

### Exact-program dominance is likely

The exact public program will recover each observed permutation, identify the
order, store `r+c-1` group elements plus one bit, and solve every valid target
with negligible query work. It will probably dominate every neural condition
in correctness, canonical bytes, and compute.

This does not invalidate Compose's construct, because the exact program proves
that source experience contains the needed information. It does invalidate any
claim that DREAM or LoRA is the rational memory substrate for this synthetic
law. The program must be labeled an exact structured ceiling; the paper's
learned-system question is whether generic compilation, linked target-time
reasoning, direct weight learning, or compiled LoRA approaches it and where
their failures occur.

If only the exact program succeeds, the one-world result is too unsurprising
for a strong negative paper. It becomes publishable only with a released,
well-audited benchmark and a broader controlled model/scaling study showing a
stable prospective-abstraction failure—not as a two-week single-backbone null.

### Target-time reasoning may erase the compiler contribution

Raw RAG or A-MEM can retrieve `T[i,j0]`, `T[i0,j0]`, and `T[i0,j]` and perform
the same three-operator completion at evaluation. A-MEM may also evolve an
external schema. If either matches compiled text under its legitimate native
interface and full resource accounting, the result is not a DREAM failure in
general; it shows that target-time external abstraction is sufficient at this
scale.

The paper must compare:

- exact observed-compound lookup with no completion;
- exact factor program;
- generic target-time raw RAG;
- native linked/A-MEM memory;
- target-blind oracle-schema text;
- generic prompted compiled text;
- identical-corpus compiled LoRA;
- direct raw-transition-to-QA LoRA.

Compiled memory earns a mechanism claim only through higher action value or a
fully counted amortized target-time resource advantage, plus directional factor
and order interventions. An arbitrary common read cap that disables A-MEM is
not evidence.

### D4 can still be decorative

Sixteen candidate action sequences can be exhaustively searched once the
operators are known. D4 is valid physical/action depth only if all shorter
routes are excluded, every sequence endpoint is certified, each decisive tool
pair is unseen, and four distinct factor cuts are independently necessary. It
does not prove learned graph depth, recursive retrieval, or sophisticated
planning. The paper should call it minimum order-sensitive action depth or
schema-use depth.

### The world remains synthetic and narrow

Fresh tray handles block state replay, but the public morphology and exact
permutation structure still look puzzle-like. One renderer and one finite law
family support only a one-generator claim. Naturalistic, embodied, broad
continual-learning, and autonomous-discovery rhetoric remains forbidden.

## FactWorld and ActionShift overlap

The environment mathematics is not the novelty.

According to the novelty audit, FactWorld already exposes a non-abelian `S_5`
word/state family, state-by-binding composition, deferred readout, and
parametric-versus-context variants. This forecloses “first noncommutative action
world,” “first order-sensitive composition benchmark,” and “first parametric
versus context composition” claims. Compose survives only if its implemented
delta is the complete conjunction:

```text
hidden per-life operator meanings inferred from public action outcomes
  -> target-blind cross-episode compiler
  -> text / native graph / direct-QA LoRA / compiled-LoRA comparison
  -> post-context factor acquisition and retention
  -> never-executed old/new compounds
  -> exact target-byte twins and factor/law interventions
```

At minimum, the paper must cite and explicitly tabulate FactWorld's state-only,
binding-only, state-by-binding, deferred, context, and parametric conditions
against Compose. A cheap empirical bridge should be included if their public
interfaces can be run faithfully. If PCFL omits action-outcome inference,
target-blind consolidation, post-context factor entropy, or causal memory
interventions, FactWorld overlap makes its benchmark novelty weak.

ActionShift already treats a hidden compositional action interface—permutation,
sign, scale, frame, lag, gripper, and related factors—as an adaptation problem
with bounded probes, belief/oracle controls, and unseen compositions. This
forecloses “first hidden compositional action contract” claims and creates a
more dangerous conceptual objection: Compose may merely be a hidden action ABI.

The required distinction is operational. Compose's transformations must be
persistent world semantics learned across outcome-bearing episodes and reused
after the source phase; evaluation must forbid target-time probing or retries;
and the paper must study durable text/graph/LoRA memory across age and context.
Include a no-persistent-memory bounded-probe/action-interface control at a
sentinel if it can be matched. If a short bounded calibration within each
target solves the task, ActionShift is the more accurate framing and the
long-lifetime memory claim is undermined.

FactWorld and ActionShift need not make Compose impossible, but they move the
contribution from “new compositional world” to “causally identified lifetime
memory study over hidden compositional dynamics.” If that conjunction is not
fully implemented, select neither rather than making a priority claim.

## DREAM prompt and hypothesis-class leakage disposition

Use a preregistered two-level compiler design:

1. **Primary generic compiler.** Identical prompt, tools, output DSL, budgets,
   and stopping rule on factorized and independent lives. It is not told the
   family, group, anchors, completion equations, orientation alternatives, or
   target pair partition. It commits one supported predictive memory before
   prospective outcomes and target rendering.
2. **Class-informed ceiling.** Receives the finite factor hypothesis class and
   may fit it from public source evidence. This localizes whether failure is
   schema search or downstream transport/use. It cannot support autonomous
   DREAM language.

Also retain oracle-schema text and the exact program. These are different
ceilings: oracle text tests whether the clean model can use the representation;
the exact program tests public identifiability and exact planning.

The primary compiler, trainer, reader, candidates, caches, errors, filenames,
RNG, and conversation state must be blind to targets and to the factorized
versus independent regime label. Every schema prediction is hashed before any
held-out outcome. Unsupported and uncompiled lives remain in the denominator.

Interpretation is mechanical:

- generic succeeds, independent control remains chance: prospective
  abstraction is supported;
- only class-informed succeeds: known-grammar completion only;
- only oracle text succeeds: compiler induction failed;
- factorized and independent both appear successful: leakage or invalid
  matching;
- raw RAG/A-MEM matches generic compilation: abstraction occurred or was
  supplied at target time, not uniquely in DREAM.

## LoRA value condition

LoRA is secondary and conditional. It earns a Paper-1 result only if all of the
following hold:

1. generic compiled text has already passed prospective action and causal-law
   gates on disjoint lives;
2. the identical canonical corpus in LoRA is noninferior in new, old, and
   cross-era D1/D4 action;
3. it beats clean-base, candidate-only, wrong/twin-adapter, and direct-QA LoRA
   on the registered estimand, or occupies a distinct nondominated resource
   point;
4. whole-life authentic/twin adapters redirect trajectories correctly and a
   paired decisive factor/order cut hurts more than a sham cut;
5. adapter precision, optimizer/checkpoint state, candidate/index bytes,
   training views, compiler/training work, query work, latency, and
   amortization denominator are all counted.

Compiled LoRA need not beat the exact program; expecting that would be
irrational. It must either improve over the strongest learned/direct-write
competitor or achieve noninferior action with a material, fully accounted
target-time external-state/query/latency advantage. If exact text, A-MEM, or
the program dominates the frontier, LoRA remains a transport diagnostic and
must leave the title and main contribution sentence.

Fixed rank is not compression. The adapter stores linearly growing per-factor
entropy and may outsource information to candidates. Use “parametric transport
of a factorized per-life model” unless exact total information accounting earns
a narrower rate--distortion statement.

## Post-context entropy disposition

Compose does contain real increasing causal information, not merely a
quadratic-combination illusion. Every new stem or suffix contributes one new
life-specific `S_6` factor, approximately `log2(720)` bits, modulo the one
global gauge. At checkpoint `k`, report the exact observable entropy:

```text
H_k = (r_k + c_k - 1) * log2(720) + 1
```

alongside introduced, publicly supported, compiled, read, and action-usable
factors. The `r_k*c_k` compound closure is generative coverage, not independent
entropy and not semantic compression.

Cross context only by adding new factor-bearing anchor experiences. Chords,
paraphrases, repeated trays, replay views, delays, or distractors do not count
as lifetime growth. The independent-table regime should expose the same number
of independently sampled observed compound permutations, the same source-token
envelope, and the same chronology and marginals, while leaving registered
unseen pairs unidentifiable.

Use one pre-native and at least three strictly post-native checkpoints derived
from the exact tokenizer and full visible envelope. Acquisition uses lagged
snapshots; earliest-factor retention uses a predeclared noninferiority margin;
cross-era action is compared with old, new, and both-factor cuts. If factor
counts or training cost prevent three post-native points, omit growth,
saturation, crossover, and post-context-curve claims.

The lifetime still mostly tests accumulation of independent factor bindings
after the one-bit law is learned. Its advantage over Stream is not that entropy
becomes sublinear; it is that each retained factor supports prospective actions
that no local episode answered.

## Two-week feasibility disposition

Neither candidate can produce the area-chair's claim-grade evidence package in
two weeks. Stream is easier to implement, but its full native A-MEM, direct-QA
LoRA, same-corpus LoRA, twin-adapter, resource-frontier, three-post-context,
and powered world-life confirmation surface is also not a two-week study. Its
apparent feasibility comes from ignoring required baselines or accepting an
uninformative exact-graph result.

A credible two-week **Compose falsification pilot** is narrower:

### Days 1--5: CPU construct gate

- deterministic total generator and exact public solver;
- factorized and entropy-matched independent regimes;
- never-executed-pair, target-byte twin, goal-twin, unique-path, shorter-route,
  noncommutation, factor/order-cut, and target-only Bayes certificates;
- exact tokenizer accounting and feasible factor counts for four lifetime
  snapshots;
- frozen manifests, failure semantics, and descendant-taint audit.

### Days 6--10: text and external-memory gate

- generic compiler versus class-informed and oracle-schema text;
- exact observed table, exact program, raw episodic RAG, and strongest
  available faithful linked/A-MEM condition;
- D1 and D4 new/old/cross-era DEV outcomes on a small independent pair panel;
- compiled-text factor/order cuts and prospective independent-law control.

### Days 11--14: decision, not automatic LoRA promotion

- complete failure-inclusive and resource-aware DEV analysis;
- decide whether generic compilation contributes beyond target-time memory;
- prepare a separately ratified LoRA/confirmation request only if the text
  gate passes.

Do not promise full LoRA, tens of independent twin pairs, multiple backbones,
rank grids, native reimplementations, or a paper-ready confirmation in this
window. If the external baseline is not faithful, record it as NOT_RUN rather
than relabeling a weak surrogate A-MEM. Targets and seeds within a life cannot
inflate the independent sample size.

If “Paper 1 within two weeks” means a submission-ready empirical result rather
than a construct decision, the answer is **neither**.

## Negative-result publishability disposition

Negative results are not equally informative.

- **Stream exact graph dominates.** Expected from independent bits; weak as a
  standalone paper unless the benchmark release and broad substrate response
  surface are themselves the contribution.
- **Compose exact program succeeds; every learned method fails.** Construct is
  valid, but a one-backbone algebraic failure is insufficient. It becomes a
  credible negative benchmark paper only with multiple capable systems,
  controlled generic-versus-class-informed prompts, scaling, and a public
  generator demonstrating a stable abstraction gap.
- **Generic compiled text succeeds; LoRA fails.** Potentially publishable as a
  prospective experiential-abstraction and substrate-boundary result.
- **A-MEM/RAG matches or beats compiled text.** Potentially publishable as a
  benchmark/causal comparison, but not as a DREAM mechanism paper; the result
  says target-time structured external memory suffices.
- **Direct-QA LoRA matches compiled LoRA.** No explicit DREAM advantage, but a
  useful result about implicit factor learning if causal twins and context
  curves pass.
- **All learned arms and exact program perform similarly.** The world is too
  easy or the prompt supplied the solution; reject the construct.
- **Factorized and independent controls both gain.** Leakage or invalid
  target/marginal construction; no paper result.

The project should precommit to publishing or at least preserving the full
failure-inclusive benchmark evidence, but it should not assume every null is
ICLR-worthy. A null is publishable only when it discriminates plausible
mechanisms rather than restating the generator's obvious optimal algorithm.

## Option-by-option adjudication

| Option | Scientific value | Feasibility | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stream core + Compose sentinel | Clean storage curve, but core remains exact lookup; sentinel either contains the real result or cannot rescue it | Two worlds and two analyses exceed the apparent safety benefit | Reject |
| Compose core + independent-table control | Prospective action-law completion, real per-factor entropy, one interface, and a matched no-structure falsifier | CPU/text pilot feasible; confirmation later | **Select conditionally** |
| Full hybrid with powered Stream and Compose cores | Separates unstructured storage from structured reuse comprehensively | Too many generators, cells, depth meanings, and claims for Paper 1 | Reject |
| Neither | Correct if novelty distinctions, generic compilation, faithful baselines, or confirmation resources cannot be satisfied | Safest terminal decision under failed gates or a hard two-week submission deadline | Retain as stop outcome |

Parity-door should not replace Compose. Its affine XOR law is cheaper to
certify but easier to dismiss as a cipher/linear-regression puzzle; order is
commutative, the persistent schema is tiny, and post-context tokens can become
redundant rather than adding causal entropy. It remains at most a CPU smoke test
outside the paper.

## Final Paper-1 boundary

The candidate paper is not “DREAM/LoRA learns throughout life” and not “a new
noncommutative action benchmark.” Its strongest defensible thesis is:

> PCFL-Compose is a controlled fixed-source assay of whether a generic
> target-blind compiler can turn public action--outcome histories with growing
> per-life factor entropy into a reusable predictive action model, and whether
> text, linked external memory, direct weight learning, or compiled parametric
> memory preserves and uses that model for never-executed old/new compounds
> after context overflow. Matched independent-law lives, exact target-byte
> twins, and factor/order interventions separate prospective abstraction from
> local lookup and target decoding.

Benchmark contribution is primary only if the deterministic generator,
certifiers, manifests, exact program, target/twin tests, and evaluator are
released. Mechanism contribution requires generic compiled text to pass
prospectively and survive the factor/order causal panel. LoRA contribution is
conditional on the explicit Pareto and causal conditions above. On-policy
evidence acquisition, autonomous open-ended discovery, generic SOTA,
naturalistic validity, learned scheduling, unbounded continual learning, and
semantic/asymptotic compression remain forbidden.

## Authority boundary

This adjudication recommends a new material benchmark direction. It does not
modify or ratify the existing PCFL-Stream change, authorize implementation,
approve the proposed two-week pilot, or permit model/provider/network use,
training, GPU work, confirmation, promotion, or claim release. Under
`AGENTS.md`, PCFL-Compose requires a fresh bound intake, independent
interpretations and adversarial critique, adjudicated consensus, exact human
ratification, scoped implementation and tests, fresh independent review, and a
separate GPU/scientific-claim gate.
