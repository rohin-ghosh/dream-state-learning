# One-child PCFL relay v2 final attack

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: fresh-context, independent, source-only attack. This advisory authorizes
no architecture ratification, source change, target or root generation,
implementation, model/tokenizer call, benchmark or adapter operation, external
access, GPU use, or scientific claim.

## Verdict

**PASS, narrowly, for entry into a formal architecture deliberation.** This is
not a pass for ratification, implementation, root generation, pilot execution,
or confirmation. If `PASS` is read as permission to execute note 58 as written,
the verdict is **REJECT**.

The proposal is now coherent enough to serve as a serious candidate
interpretation in the durable deliberation path. It keeps the binding topology
to exactly one fixed parent-deleted child and isolated environment-root clones,
gives authentic links experiential public provenance, separates a frozen core
from arm carriers, makes the reader finite, restores the correct B/C/D branch
order, prospectively compiles one C outcome into one row, supplies a real
`REACHOUT_OFF` continuation, and makes the missing statistics/power lock an
explicit kill gate rather than guessing an `N`.

It is not yet an executable causal contract despite its title. The remaining
issues below admit causally or statistically inequivalent implementations.
They must be explicit concerns in the architecture record, disposed by
adjudicated consensus, incorporated into exact replacement bytes, and
separately ratified by Rohin. None may be silently delegated to implementation.

## Binding scope

**PASS.** The unit remains one selected terminal child after deletion of its
one parent and all nursery artifacts. Roots and arm forks are isolated trials
of byte-identical clones of that same child, not additional children. There is
no classroom, peer, cohort, ensemble, shared-memory, cross-root learning, or
population estimand. `WRONG_LIFE` is correctly limited to a carrier from an
isolated environment root of the same fixed child. All inference is conditional
on that checkpoint and the registered PCFL distribution.

The formal record must preserve those exact exclusions. In particular, it may
not turn the isolated root that supplies `WRONG_LIFE` into another learner or
allow it to communicate with the assayed root.

## Exact remaining blockers

The following are nonwaivable before exact-byte ratification or any execution.

### B1 — resolve the `R0`/pilot lock collision

Choose and encode one chronology. For a confirmatory label, the estimands,
component formulas, failure values, decision family, pilot algorithm, allowed
pilot fields, and rule mapping blinded nuisance information to final `N` must
be hash-bound at `R0` before parenting unblind. A later pilot may estimate only
the prospectively permitted nuisance quantities and apply that fixed rule. It
may not choose endpoints, score definitions, floors, ceilings, SESOIs,
multiplicity, or favorable roots after seeing the selected child's relay
performance. Otherwise declare the whole relay exploratory and require a new
source-bound deliberation for any later confirmation protocol. Bind the actual
`P0`, `R0`, and `PU` receipts; prose cannot establish their order retroactively.

### B2 — freeze one complete event/proposal/compiler grammar

Define the immutable Phase-A ledger as typed, ordered bytes containing public
events plus the child's atom and co-use proposals. Give `G_atom` and `G_link`
their exact inputs, chronology comparisons, citation resolution, canonical
serialization, public success/co-use predicate, offline truth-check boundary,
duplicate handling, and total failure outputs. State exactly which link fields
enter the carrier. `intended_operation`, proposal prose, citations, event IDs,
and joint row layout must not become an undeclared answer channel. Carry every
failed required atom/link into every affected root score at its registered
failure value.

### B3 — define every carrier as a state transition from the common cut

For text and LoRA separately, identify the exact authentic Phase-C carrier and
choose one Phase-D construction, for example either:

```text
D_ARM(z) = WRITE(CLONE(CARRIER_A_AUTH), row_or_padding(z), coupled_rng_z)
```

or a complete clean-base rebuild from the identical authentic old deck plus
the assigned D row. Freeze which parameters, adapter bytes, optimizer slots,
mount state, text store, rehearsal rows, and RNG state are cloned, reset, or
rebuilt. Define `REACHOUT_OFF` by the same grammar. Without this, `W0`, `W1`,
and `Fbind` do not isolate one new semantic write and old-memory retention is
not comparable.

Also give `LINK_NULL` a deterministic source and projection rule. It must be
truthful, useless for the registered goals under the exhaustive policy class,
matched on the declared semantic/resource strata, and a registered root
failure when the matched deck does not exist. Do not describe hidden-authored
null links as experiential links.

### B4 — repair the Phase-B arm names and score algebra

Replace the inconsistent `BRIDGE_SUB`/`BRIDGE_CUT`/`BRIDGE_TWIN` names with
three explicit potential outcomes or one explicit multi-level intervention.
Define `CONNECTED_DERANGED`, `CONNECTED_NULL`, `action_value`, `Q0`, `Q1`,
path equality, malformed/missing trace values, and within-root aggregation.
Make the twin endpoint demonstrate redirection, not merely success in the twin
world; it must require the twin-valid action/path and failure of the original
authentic action/path under the same substitution. Require every necessary
proper semantic row subset and direct-answer route to fail, not only a single
named bridge. Couple all non-goal exogenous bytes and RNG for the paired goals,
or define the valid paired randomization distribution explicitly.

### B5 — close the exhaustive-controller class exactly

List the finite histories available to a target-blind controller, including
returned rows, miss/error/timing classes, adaptive next-query choice, stopping,
batches, retries, resolver calls, and RNG. Enumerate all legal policies under
the cap, or provide a machine-checkable reduction showing why the registered
nonadaptive schedules dominate that class. Freeze whether the “best” schedule
is selected per root, on DEV, or once for confirmation; its selection cannot
silently inspect confirmation outcomes. The leaked-oracle floor and all subset
ceilings need exact score mappings.

For D, say that the matched candidate catalog and its metadata do not encode
the new binding. Reserve a fixed slot whose returned semantic row differs by
the declared D intervention; do not simultaneously require that the authentic
carrier contain the new row and that its entire candidate universe contain no
answer-bearing binding.

### B6 — make `AUTH_SIGNAL`, `SHAM_SIGNAL`, and `REACHOUT_OFF` single-valued

Identify the exact authentic old row/link/read whose semantic substitution
defines `AUTH_SIGNAL`, the paired sham row and slot, and the public information
set before and after that read. The sham matcher must include a frozen
actor-public posterior/equipoise test, support/citation status, legality,
resources, and all passive signatures; missing matches are failures, not a
search for easier shams.

Pick one reachout intervention boundary. If the selected information action is
dispatched and returns `UNAVAILABLE`, keep the prefix through declaration and
action selection byte-identical and activate denial at outcome dispatch. If
reachout is unavailable earlier, define the different estimand explicitly.
In either case bind identical time/resource charges, the counterfactually
matched D world, same D target/twin/catalog and coupled RNG, no new supported
row, the exact old carrier, the no-semantic build receipt, and all failure
values.

### B7 — bind the D writer and closure fixtures

For each D arm, freeze the canonical input bytes, response-only mask, rehearsal
and padding targets, update/rebuild transaction, failure/rollback behavior, and
post-build carrier hash schema. Construct `SHAM_WRITE` prospectively as a
genuinely supported, previously uncarried irrelevant row with a matched public
citation, or state and justify a different nonredundancy rule. Absence of such
a row is a root failure.

Turn “every proper subset and combination” into the literal generated family
of active and passive histories, including compound old-memory rows, C action,
outcome, hypotheses, caches, carrier/catalog metadata, mount/build status, and
all reacquisition routes. Define the oracle and failure scores for each member
and prove the declared old row plus the selected new row is the unique minimal
semantic support for D.

### B8 — compile every named control into an acceptance test

For `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS`, explicit text, LoRA, adapter-off, wrong-life,
candidate-only, unaided-generative read, source-action-string, leaked oracle,
`LINK_NULL`, derangement, cut, twin, no-write, sham-write, and binding-swap,
freeze exact carrier bytes/construction, intervention time, visible resource
vector, potential outcome, root score, absolute floor or ceiling, paired
contrast, practical margin, and failure value. Define the active-text
same-semantics between-carrier estimand. Without it, no LoRA-versus-text or
baseline-superiority language is available. Unequal physical resources remain
reported, not “matched.”

### B9 — add a per-root temporal complete-chain endpoint

Define `TEXT_chain_r` and `LORA_chain_r` as explicit indicators (or another
bounded predeclared score) requiring the same randomized root, in temporal
order, to clear every required mediator: A acquisition/retention, authentic
connected use, goal-specific traversal, C gap/hypotheses and experiment
selection/relevant information gain, truthful `G_new` admission and write, and
D use requiring authentic old plus authentic new memory, with all required
cuts/twins/controls. Every randomized root stays in the denominator.

Then define `COMPLETE_chain`, its relation to the text feasibility gate and
LoRA claim, nontrivial `pi_min`, confidence rule, and power. Ordered positive
population-average contrasts are necessary but do not replace this within-root
endpoint.

### B10 — complete the separate source-bound statistics/power lock

Before confirmation-root generation, freeze every bounded component and
within-root aggregation; AUTH floors; control ceilings; SESOIs; test directions;
confidence/randomization construction; intersection-union, fixed-sequence, and
component-claim multiplicity handling; missing-cell and infrastructure-resume
rules; carrier-specific and complete-chain estimands; and a simulation-based
least-favorable conjunctive power calculation using only allowed pilot
quantities. Ratify the resulting fixed `N` and blinded extension rule as exact
bytes in a new bound deliberation. Until then the pre-execution statistics gate
is closed.

## R1--R12 bottom line

The prior twelve objections have been repaired enough that no fatal theorem or
scope contradiction prevents formal deliberation. R2, R3, R8, and most of R6
and R9 are substantively repaired. R1, R4, R5, R7, R10, R11, and R12 still
contain choices that change identification or claim release. The architecture
process must adjudicate B1--B10 rather than treating them as clerical details.

After those dispositions, the exact replacement must undergo the remaining
AGENTS.md path: human ratification, scoped implementation and tests, fresh
independent review plus a separate author-side scientific advocate, and the
explicit pre-GPU/scientific-claim gate. This advisory supplies neither
ratification nor a GPU gate.

## Maximum present claim

No relay result or causal claim is authorized. At most, note 58 is a
well-formed one-child architecture candidate with a plausible identification
strategy and explicit pre-execution gates. If its actual `R0` occurred after
parenting unblind, every eventual relay result under these bytes is exploratory
and conditional regardless of later sealing.
