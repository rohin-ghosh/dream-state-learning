# PCFL-Compose cross-critique: parity-door alternative and PCFL-Stream

Date: 2026-09-02

Status: fast independent advisory. Read-only scientific critique; no
implementation, model/provider/network/GPU run, ratification, or claim
approval.

## Decision

PCFL-Compose is the stronger of the two schema proposals, but it is not the
safer Paper-1 core. I recommend:

```text
Paper-1 core:     PCFL-Stream, with its narrow fixed-source
                  acquisition/retention/composition claim.
Schema sentinel: PCFL-Compose, tightly scoped to prospective text-schema
                  action completion and exact causal diagnostics.
Cut from Paper 1: parity-door as a headline construct (retain only as a
                  backup CPU/interface smoke test if useful).
```

Compose has a real advantage over parity-door: noncommutative `S_6` operators
make composition order behaviorally consequential, and fresh tray handles
make endpoint memorization less plausible. Parity-door is simpler and easier
to certify, but its affine XOR law is more readily characterized as a small
cipher or linear-regression puzzle. Neither design, as currently specified,
identifies autonomous DREAM discovery: both expose a finite schema family and
ask a prompted compiler to fit hidden parameters.

## Construct validity

Compose is a credible test of factorized action completion. A source
intervention reveals only a before/after permutation; a target compound tool
has never been executed; and the agent must compose old and new factor
operators and execute a depth-four trajectory. The noncommutativity and fresh
handles are substantially better than an indexed transition table. It also
has a clean negative-law sentinel: if compound pairs are independent, unseen
pairs should remain chance.

The adversarial concern is that the factorization, group operation, anchor
protocol, and two-order hypothesis class are supplied by the world contract.
The compiler need only canonicalize observed permutations, recover anchor
operators, and select one bit of order from chords. That is useful
factorized completion under a known grammar, not evidence that DREAM invented
the reusable abstraction. It is also possible for a target-time linked reader
to retrieve anchors and perform the same algebra, leaving no compiler
contribution.

Depth four is executable action depth, not automatically learned graph or
memory depth. The target tray exposes the desired terminal permutation, so a
strong reader/planner may search candidate compositions at action time. Fresh
states and no retry prevent trivial state-edge replay, but do not by
themselves prove that the stored schema, rather than target-time algebra, was
used. The honest construct is therefore “target-blind factorized operator
completion used for action,” unless decisive order/factor interventions move
behavior.

Compared with parity-door, Compose is the better schema sentinel because its
order bit cannot be reduced to an endpoint-invariant XOR translation and its
operator closure is directly executable. Compared with safer PCFL-Stream,
Compose gains unseen compound generalization but gives up Stream's cleaner
interpretation as finite causal-memory retention. Fixed common source lives in
both cases remain off-policy; neither supports a self-learning or
memory-directed evidence-acquisition claim.

## Leakage and twin risks

The Compose visibility contract is directionally right: targets are presealed,
source episodes contain no rules or QA, the schema is frozen before probes,
and stem-swap twins preserve target bytes. The contract must be treated as an
experiment artifact, not merely prose. In particular, taint must transitively
cover target pair IDs, reserved-factor roles, target templates, filenames,
world IDs, caches, retries, error paths, index construction, candidate lists,
training batches, and timing/length channels.

The source outcome for an executed pair reveals its entire `S_6` permutation.
One accidental execution of a registered target pair, or a target factor
binding included in an anchor/chord, converts a schema item into lookup.
“Never executed” must be certified mechanically from the immutable event log.
The source schedule, target allocations, schema commitment hash, and delayed
confirmation order should be frozen before source realization and checked by
an independent audit.

The stem involution twin is promising but fragile. Exact byte equality must
include start/goal trays, menu ordering, action labels, caps, serialization
lengths, and all model-visible metadata. World IDs, RNG state, handle order,
cache keys, failures, conversation state, or elapsed-time differences must not
identify the side. The twin should be tested on every primary target, not just
on a few examples. The independent-law sentinel must have matched renderer,
source counts, chronology, and target bytes; above-chance unseen-pair value is
a leakage failure.

Targets and compiled memories cannot be selected after compiler success.
Unsupported, uncompiled, malformed, training-failed, timeout, and runtime-
failed items must stay in their preassigned denominators. A schema prediction
must be hashed before any held-out outcome or evaluation goal is visible.

## Exact-program dominance and DREAM attribution

The exact public sufficient-statistic program will almost certainly solve every
valid item. It should be a ceiling and a construct certificate, not a claim
that a neural system must beat it. The more serious threat is that the exact
program, raw RAG, or A-MEM matches the proposed compiler because the supplied
factor grammar makes target-time composition trivial. In that result the assay
is still valid, but it measures a known algebra plus retrieval, not DREAM.

Required discriminations are:

- observed-compound table with no factor completion;
- exact factor-law program;
- target-blind oracle schema (compiler bypass);
- prompted compiled text;
- identical compiled corpus in LoRA;
- full semantic facts with composition/order links deleted;
- target-time raw episodic RAG and native A-MEM.

An order-law cut must hold all observed `R[i]`, `C[j]`, and `M` operators fixed
while flipping only the order statement; noncommuting targets must change
directionally. A complete decisive factor/order cut must remove equivalent
records, derived indexes, candidates, and caches. Equal-sized sham cuts should
not hurt. If edge-deleted full facts match, no learned links are identified.

A prompted compiler plus mechanical verifier can establish corpus utility, but
not autonomous or learned DREAM. For a DREAM sentence, the compiler prompt,
hypothesis family, admission rule, and stopping policy must be frozen and
evaluated on whole held-out lives. Compiled text must beat raw history and
target-time external abstraction at matched action/read/compute budgets. If
oracle-schema text succeeds and prompted compilation fails, the bottleneck is
compiler induction; if raw RAG succeeds equally, compilation adds no
demonstrated value; if only the exact program succeeds, neural abstraction is
unsupported.

## LoRA value condition

Compose's canonical statistic is `O(r+c)` factor operators plus one order bit,
while it exposes `O(r*c)` unseen compound actions. That is a useful
factorization response surface, but it is not compression of independent
information: factor entropy and likely adapter capacity grow linearly. A
fixed-rank LoRA result must not be called compression, and if rank/optimizer/
index state grows with factors it is storage/transport only.

LoRA earns a paper-level role only if the identical compiled corpus in LoRA
beats clean-base, candidate-only clean-base, wrong/twin adapter, direct
raw-transition-to-QA LoRA, and matched compiled text under a disclosed
resource frontier. Candidate/index bytes and work must be counted; otherwise
recognition-assisted LoRA is an external enumerator plus reranker. Whole-life
authentic-versus-twin adapters and binding-deleted versus sham panels are
needed; deleting a displayed citation is not a weight intervention. If exact
text or the explicit program dominates, omit LoRA from the title and retain it
as a transport diagnostic.

## Lifetime scaling

Compose has a legitimate finite scaling idea: introduce fresh stems and
suffixes over time, observe only anchor rows/columns and chords, and test
never-executed cross-era products. Raw experience grows with factors while
the reusable factor law covers a quadratic set of compounds. This can cross
exact `L_native` at `2x`, `4x`, and `8x` checkpoints.

But the apparent growth is partly recombination. The order law is one bit and
the per-factor bindings are the only new causal information. Report unique
factor entropy, compiled bytes, and action value separately; do not call the
quadratic closure semantic compression. Require lagged snapshots, positive
new-factor acquisition, earliest-factor retention within a preregistered
noninferiority margin, and cross-era action above old/new/both cuts. If fewer
than three strictly post-native checkpoints are affordable, omit scaling,
saturation, and crossover language. PCFL-Stream remains the cleaner test of
finite continued acquisition because its independent causal mappings are
explicit rather than mostly generated recombinations.

## Feasibility in two weeks

An exhaustive `S_6` generator, target/twin certifier, exact solver, and a
small prompted-compiler pilot are feasible. A claim-grade study is not: the
full package requires disjoint calibration/confirmation lives, at least three
post-native checkpoints, native A-MEM and RAG controls, direct-QA and text/
LoRA transport, whole-life adapter interventions, exact resource accounting,
and enough independent pairs for inference. The permutation/action serializer,
cross-era allocation, and twin side-channel audit add substantial debugging
surface. Parity-door is cheaper to implement, but that economy buys a weaker,
more cipher-like construct. Stream's narrower core is the only realistic
two-week paper path.

## Statistical acceptance conditions

The independent unit is a world-life/twin pair, not a target, stage, menu,
depth, or action call. Use pair-blocked or hierarchical inference with
failure-inclusive denominators, multiplicity hierarchy, and explicit
noninferiority margins. Plan for tens of independent world-life/twin pairs
unless blinded variance supports a smaller number; four development pairs are
not evidence. Preselect a complete full-lifetime core rather than pruning
cells after observed compiler or arm outcomes. Keep D1 as an installation
diagnostic and power D4/new-old-cross-era endpoints separately; a single
aggregate score can hide forgetting or no schema use.

## Required repairs before any claim-grade Compose run

1. Downgrade the headline to fixed-source factorized operator completion;
   remove self-learning, on-policy, autonomous-discovery, and compression
   language.
2. Separate public ontology from learned content. If DREAM discovery is kept,
   add a matched family-choice/independent-law sentinel and state exactly what
   is supplied to the compiler; otherwise call it known-grammar completion.
3. Add an independent target/descendant-taint audit and mechanical
   never-executed certification.
4. Hash compiler/schema commitment before held-out outcomes and freeze every
   target, twin, allocation, and failure denominator prospectively.
5. Run the observed-table, exact-program, oracle-schema, edge-deleted-facts,
   raw-RAG, and native A-MEM controls.
6. Implement complete order/factor binding cuts, sham cuts, wrong/twin memory,
   and candidate-only accounting.
7. Require exact `L_native` crossings and separate new/old/cross-era
   lagged-snapshot estimands; no scaling claims below three post-native points.
8. Predeclare world-life/twin sample size, hierarchical analysis, margins, and
   missing/failure treatment; release generator, certifier, manifests, and
   scoring code.
9. For a two-week sentinel, restrict to D1/D4, compiled text plus exact
   controls, and one small independent pair panel. Defer LoRA and broad
   rank/reader sweeps until the text construct passes.

## Final recommendation

Use PCFL-Stream as Paper-1 core. Run a repaired PCFL-Compose text/exact-program
sentinel only to decide whether a prospective-schema follow-up is warranted.
Cut parity-door from the paper because its affine law is easier to dismiss as
a nonce cipher, while Compose offers the more defensible action-level
factorization test. Do not promote Compose to Paper-1 core unless it first
passes the leakage, exact-program dominance, negative-law, order-cut, and
whole-life paired statistical gates; the area-chair's accept path still favors
the narrower PCFL-Stream benchmark and rejects a broad Dream--LoRA--Think
self-learning headline.
