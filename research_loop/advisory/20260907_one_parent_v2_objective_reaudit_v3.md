## 1. Experiential-object onset is now total

The repaired hierarchy still admits only own, same-service public experience:

```text
OWN_OBSERVATION
  -> live nonsuperseded EVIDENCE_UNIT
  -> status==SUPPORTED SUPPORTED_UNIT
  -> supported-endpoint AUTHENTIC_LINK
  -> one-hop-only returned TRAVERSED_LINK(q).
```

The new definitions close the two lifecycle cases that were previously
ambiguous:

- `support_onset(key)` is the earliest immutable commit ordinal after which
  the sole live record first qualifies as a `SUPPORTED_UNIT`; and
- `authentic_link_onset(source,target)` is the earliest immutable ordinal
  after which the directed link exists and both current endpoints qualify as
  supported.

Thus a provisional record committed in era 1 and first supported in era 2 is
new supported knowledge in era 2. A link committed before an endpoint becomes
supported begins only when both endpoints qualify. `ERA_EXPANSION(t)` counts
objects by those onsets, while current live status at cut `t` still reflects
later contradiction, supersession, or endpoint-status loss. Such later loss
does not backdate onset or manufacture a second new object. The diagnostic is
therefore failure-inclusive and single-valued without being mislabeled as
child-directed exploration.

## 2. The link intervention is coherent and bounded

The repaired diagnostic now has the needed separation between the ordinary
retrieval law and the intervention:

1. At every interactive continuation, intact and overlay branches build the
   registered query from their own current objective, metric, last two actions,
   and latest outcome. The overlay therefore does not borrow an intact-arm
   post-divergence query.
2. Each query is ranked against the immutable authentic index, after which
   that continuation's pre-expansion seed IDs and ranks are frozen.
3. The actor sees diagnostic copies of those seed records. Their sole changed
   field is `linked_memory_ids`; targets remain otherwise byte-identical
   authentic records. Copies are neither stored nor indexed and cannot become
   valid commits.
4. The overlay acts on every eligible edge instance, ordered by
   `(source_id,target_id,edge_ordinal)`. Endpoint type, status, scope, and exact
   pinned-tokenizer target-record length define the bucket. Exact length makes
   an interval-boundary convention unnecessary.
5. Corresponding target stubs are ordered lexicographically and tested in
   ascending cyclic offsets. A candidate fails for an original target,
   self-link, duplicate target at a source, already-linked replacement,
   missing target, or superseded target. The accepted rotation must preserve
   source out-degree, target in-degree, endpoint fields, exact target lengths,
   packed-document cardinality, total returned tokens, and the frozen pack
   order class under the 1,024-token cap.

The preservation clauses are hard invariants, not favorable-post-hoc
selection: if no rotation satisfies the pack law, that probe is infeasible
and contributes zero. The root reducer still averages all eight probes; no
root or probe is removed for absent eligible traversal, failed derangement, or
zero engagement. Selection uses only pre-action receipts, never score or a
hidden solution. These bytes make `K_link` an intervention on actor-visible
one-hop adjacency while preserving the authentic retrieval index and seed
ranking.

A later implementation fixture should prove the asserted source-copy token
length, complete packed ID/order, cardinality, and total-token equalities for
every accepted rotation and fail closed otherwise. That is an implementation
of the present invariant, not a missing scientific choice in the addendum.

The allowed conclusion remains narrow: a passing result supports a functional
contribution from explicit, goal-conditioned one-hop active-text links. It
does not prove a graph inside weights, adaptive child-chosen traversal, or
compression.

## 3. The outcome-binding shadow is now reproducible

The shadow-admission object is no longer an unspecified “semantic bundle.” It
is the exact primitive tuple

```text
(dispatch_valid, compiler_valid, outcome_class, normalized_public_score),
```

with frozen enums and a rational score from the public scorer. The donor tuple
is combined only with recipient-authentic action/program identity, chronology,
prediction, prior incumbent, raw public text, event IDs, ordinals, hashes, and
`REVISION` references. The reducer then mechanically recomputes positive-
improvement, prediction-residual, surprise, non-inferiority, and recovery bits;
it cannot reparse or reinterpret the raw text.

Within `(root,era,normalized_action_family)`, recipient block IDs are sorted
lexicographically and the correspondingly ordered donor tuples move forward
by one position around the same cycle. This is a no-fixed-point mapping for
strata of size at least two. Size-zero/one strata remain authentic and are
reported. The permutation never conditions on outcome validity or score-effect
bin.

All authentic ledger bytes and the terminal P1 active-text store remain fixed.
The shadow bits alter only which terminal deployment slots the fixed writer
law selects. “Unchanged target bytes” now unambiguously means every selected
suffix is an unmodified authentic child response; it does not require the
authentic and shadow arms to select the same suffix set. Childhood rows,
rehearsal fills, anchors, four-exposure schedule, optimizer, and clean base are
also fixed. If no slot identity changes, the root remains in `K_binding` with
contribution zero.

This can support only the effect of correct public action--outcome binding on
a fixed-history terminal compilation. It does not establish an on-policy
mediated lifetime effect or an outcome representation inside LoRA.

## 4. `D` support and receipt are repaired

Because P1/P0 share one cached P entry value and U1/U0 share one cached U
entry value, the entry terms cancel in each write contrast:

```text
W_P = [2(V_P1,16-V_P0,16) + 2(V_P1,32-V_P0,32)
       + (V_P1,48-V_P0,48)] / 6 in [-5/6,5/6],
W_U in [-5/6,5/6],
D = W_P-W_U in [-5/3,5/3].
```

The repaired source uses exactly those bounds and asserts their relationship
to the cached-entry gain-AUC comparison bound. Receipt v2 names the new source
hash, correct support, seed, repetitions, environment, invocation, and stdout
hash. The exact rerun reproduced its stdout hash. It also retains the adverse

the invalid “at least 80% power” language. The power material is therefore an
honest named-shape planning sensitivity, not a mean/SD envelope or evidence
for the later claim rungs and diagnostics.

## Objective and scope disposition

The repairs do not enlarge what the present experiment can establish:

- **Headline:** parenting changed the benefit of the same child's later
  personal writes; those writes helped; P1 improved across the finite life;
  and, only after the registered gates, the full P1 package gained more and
  ended higher than the certified R0 comparator. R0 can show only the named
  local plateau, not intrinsic or universal saturation.
- **Terminal active-text diagnostics:** supported own-experience objects and
  links were formed/returned, and explicit link or binding corruption changed
  terminal behavior under the bounded interventions. They do not establish
  compression or an online expansion relay.
- **Future carrier:** only a separately ratified controlled carrier study may
  test parametric transport and behavioral use of authentic connected
  experiential code. It cannot claim a human-readable graph in weights.
- **Future expansion relay:** only a separately ratified sequential study may
  test memory changing an information-seeking action, the resulting new public
  outcome being admitted, and a still-later fresh goal benefiting under
  sham/binding/write-off controls.

Every surface remains one parent and one child per life, with isolated
counterfactual/service/root state. Replication over `Q` is repeated sampling
of one fixed policy pair, not classroom, peer, parent-population, or shared
learning.
