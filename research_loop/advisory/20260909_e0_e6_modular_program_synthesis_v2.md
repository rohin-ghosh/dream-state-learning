# E0--E6 modular program synthesis v2

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: **unbound source-only synthesis**. This document changes no organism,
writer, parent, child, benchmark, endpoint, control, model, data, adapter,
resource allocation, scientific claim, or release decision. It authorizes no
implementation or execution. Every material successor still requires the
deliberation, exact-ratification, independent-review, implementation-review,
and separate execution gates in `AGENTS.md`.

## Plain-language result

The organism remains simple:

```text
THINK -- live thought, action, outcome, and revision
DREAM -- manage the active context
SLEEP -- compile grounded experience and write the eligible result into LoRA
```

The laboratory proof is modular because different claims require different
independent units. One run cannot turn repeated task roots into independent
childhoods, and a useful LoRA cannot be called compressed merely because it
contains useful information.

The current program is therefore:

1. **E0 -- certify the writer.** Show that a candidate SLEEP write is absorbed,
   readable, behaviorally usable, interface-preserving, transactional, and
   recoverable without recycling evidence from a rejected sleep.
2. **E1 -- certify parenting separately.** Independently raise comparable
   children and test whether parenting changes later parent-absent learning,
   retention, and transfer. Classroom branches inside one child are not extra
   children.
3. **M -- mechanism relay, E2/E4/E5.** In a fixed parent-deleted child, test
   connected experience, goal-specific traversal, experience-directed action,
   a grounded new write, and later joint use of old and new knowledge.
4. **L -- longitudinal development, E6.** Across independent child lineages,
   test whether the full learner keeps improving after one certified active-
   text comparator reaches and remains at a registered local plateau.
5. **C -- compression, E3.** First test exact prospective semantic coding on
   CPU and through explicit text. Treat optional LoRA access as transport, not
   compression. Whole-carrier physical compression remains closed at the
   present scale.

This organization is a bounded resource recommendation, not a proof of a
mathematical minimum. The modules may share one generator family, task
interface, compilers, and sealed identity machinery, and they may be nested in
one physical execution if their units, denominators, reducers, gates, and
claims remain separate.

## Current evidence status

No central positive rung is presently established prospectively. Legacy work
does establish that LoRA writes can materially change behavior, can preserve
atomic information under a suitable read protocol, can damage the action
interface, and can concentrate or collapse behavior. It does not establish a
passing E0 writer, causal parenting, connected parametric experience,
prospective traversal/expansion, physical compression, or lifetime superiority
over a certified strong memory comparator.

The present long-lifetime runner is specifically not evidence that the newer
writer is deployed. `organism_v6/run_life_v2.py` still calls the legacy
bare-text compiler/trainer. Its adapter canary protects the active head but
does not quarantine the rejected sleep's corpus from a later cumulative build.
That is an E0 blocker.

## E0 -- writer floor

E0 is first because every later result depends on the write meaning what we
say it means. A ratified writer must bind:

- exact compiler and trainer source, chat rendering, response-only mask, base
  model and tokenizer revisions, rank, dose, seed, and cumulative-corpus ID;
- immutable birth plus candidate-only staging;
- separate promotion decisions for the adapter head and for evidence/corpus;
- rejection quarantine, so a failed candidate cannot re-enter through the
  next cumulative rebuild unless a row remains independently grounded and
  eligible;
- comparison against the previous child, not merely the raw base;
- absorption and exact semantic-read checks, native thought/action routing,
  generic capability and DREAM-restoration checks, behavior/no-harm checks,
  and atomic rollback; and
- an end-to-end receipt binding evaluated outputs to the exact source, launch,
  corpus, and adapter bytes.

E0 does not require parent-absent far transfer from a tiny corpus. It requires
evidence that the writer stores eligible material, exposes it through the
registered read/use path, preserves the organism's interface, and refuses or
reverses bad writes without contaminating later sleeps.

The existing V9 writer draft is not adopted by this synthesis. Its 154 MiB
expanded visibility surface should be replaced, if separately deliberated, by
a compact normative source plus deterministic exhaustive projection and an
independently reproduced equality receipt. That changes representation
efficiency, not any acceptance obligation.

## E1 -- parenting and developmental continuity

E1 answers whether teaching changes a child's later ability to learn; it is
not identified by the longitudinal E6 comparison. The ratified adaptive-parent
v5 proposal supplies the high-level shape, but current classroom code does not
fully implement it.

The minimum scientific distinctions are:

- one composite child may fan out from one shared snapshot into parallel
  classroom experiences, pool the child-authored grounded evidence, and make
  one write; those branches are data parallelism, not population replication;
- short teaching outcomes and delayed outcomes must both update the parent's
  bounded notebook during development: comprehension, next-attempt change,
  world outcome, later spontaneous use, retention, transfer, calibration, and
  side effects such as ritualization or reduced action;
- parent text may influence the waking child but is not itself a child target;
  eligible child thought/action/outcome continuations cross into SLEEP;
- same mechanism plus new lessons continues the canonical developmental
  child; a change to THINK, DREAM, SLEEP, writer, rank/capacity semantics,
  eligibility, masking, or promotion law forks an immutable sibling from the
  last common snapshot; and
- every candidate child is compared against the previous child on preservation
  and relevant competence before promotion.

Repetition is a teaching tool, but lexical restatement is not mastery.
Unprompted, situation-sensitive later use is the durable endpoint.

The still-pending Fable--Codex exchange owns the successor design for scaled
THINK, child continuity, adaptive teaching, and durable personal perspective.
No choice among serial depth, parallel routes, merge policy, or stopping law is
made here.

## M -- E2/E4/E5 mechanism relay

M uses one fixed parent-deleted child cloned across independent environment
roots. The root is the inferential unit. Goals, routes, calls, samples, and
forks inside a root are repeated measures and never increase `N`.

The E2 carrier panel must retain matched exposure and an atomic-retention
floor:

- atoms-only / truthful-null text;
- authentic linked text;
- atoms-only / truthful-null LoRA;
- authentic linked LoRA; and
- matched deranged linked carriers.

Authentic-minus-deranged alone is insufficient: derangement could merely be
harmful while authentic links add nothing over isolated facts. E2 requires
linked-minus-atoms/null benefit plus preserved atoms under the same read plan.

E4 then requires two same-start, different-path goals, authentic goal-specific
path choice, necessary bridge cuts, truthful twin redirection, and matched
derangement under fixed ordinary affordances. This supports an operational
goal-conditioned traversal result, not identification of an internal graph.

E5 requires the full public chronology:

```text
t0 old experience -> choose a needed experiment -> dispatch it
-> receive the public outcome -> admit/write an eligible new row
-> t2 task whose success needs both old and new information
```

Keep `REACHOUT_OFF`, truthful sham-signal, no-write, old-row cut, new-binding
swap, adapter-off, wrong-life/root, and the certified `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED`
comparator wherever the released E5 claim excludes their corresponding
alternative explanations. The active-text comparator must have the same tools,
public outcomes, ordinary query/update opportunities, and generated-token
budget; relay-local text is not automatically a strong comparator.

For the delayed old/new factorial, report absolute `Y11` and make the two
necessity contrasts claim-gating:

```text
d_write = Y11 - Y10
d_old   = Y11 - Y01
```

The additive interaction

```text
d_joint = Y11 - Y10 - Y01 + Y00
```

is diagnostic, not a general definition of joint necessity; ceiling effects
can erase it even when both old and new information are necessary.

Development thresholds are explicitly heuristic, no-claim engineering
triage. Any conditional attribution run after selecting core-positive roots
describes only that selected subpopulation. Adverse zero filling is valid only
for a prospectively defined gated binary product; it is not an observed
component effect. `N=96, X>=47` applies at most once to the one frozen joint
root-probability estimand for which it was derived; it cannot power separate
E2/E4/E5 effects.

## L -- E6 longitudinal development

Use the fresh E6 design's two-arm paired topology:

- independently raise one parented entry child per E6 root;
- delete parent and nursery state;
- fork byte-identical, isolated descendants;
- `F`: the frozen full learner, with the certified writer enabled;
- `B`: the same child with certified `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` and personal
  parametric writes disabled.

Each root pair is one independent unit. Checkpoints and episodes inside it are
repeated measures. Use seven nested lifetime cuts `T0..T6`; `T3` is the sole
prospectively fixed plateau anchor and `T4..T6` are the three post-anchor cuts.
Lifetime advances only with genuine new factor-bearing experience, not
paraphrases, replay, padding, or repeated programs.

At each cut, evaluate the same sterile held-out value panel under common
randomness. Evaluate an old-competence panel at `T3` and `T6`. The released
claim requires this strict noncompensatory order:

1. writer/instrument/active-text certification;
2. equivalence-based baseline plateau before `T3` plus search headroom;
3. equivalence-based baseline plateau from `T3` through `T6`;
4. positive absolute late slope and `T6-T3` gain for `F`;
5. positive late-slope advantage over `B`;
6. old-competence retention; and
7. absolute terminal superiority by a frozen practical margin.

The exact margins, experience quantum, and confirmation `N` are not ready.
Run one plumbing pair, then a disjoint E6-only DEV set (about eight pairs is an
aggressive planning recommendation, not a power claim), estimate adverse
paired covariance/tails/cost, simulate the whole conjunction across candidate
`N={24,32,40,48,64}`, and choose the smallest `N` whose worst registered
family has a one-sided 95% Monte Carlo lower bound of at least `.80` joint
power and fits the separately ratified resource bound. Neither E1's `N=32` nor
M's `N=96` transfers to E6.

Current execution disposition: **NO-GO** until the exact experience generator
and increment, extended active-text certificate, E6 DEV covariance/power
receipt, and all-in resource bound exist.

## C -- E3 compression

At current scale, positive physical compression of the complete rank-8 carrier
is mathematically closed. The observed adapter file is `80,792,096` bytes.
Before auxiliaries, a ratio below `.50` requires expanded equivalent semantics
of at least `161,584,193` bytes; a raw ratio below `.35` requires at least
`230,834,561` raw bytes. Smaller data cannot cross those boundaries.

The useful near-term assay is CPU-first prospective semantic coding with three
actor-facing carriers under one read/task protocol:

- `EXPANDED_OBSERVATION`;
- `NORMALIZED_CONNECTED`, the strong ordinary normalization baseline; and
- `SCHEMA_RESIDUAL_CONNECTED`, the only candidate that can support a
  nontrivial semantic-code result.

The compact carrier must round-trip to the same registered public denotation,
count complete store/resident/simultaneous bytes and all decoders, schemas,
indices, prompts, and scratch, beat ordinary normalization/general codecs by a
prospectively useful margin, preserve utility, and pass authentic/deranged,
bridge/twin, schema-binding, atoms-only, structured/independent-regime,
post-outcome leakage, false-memory, and raw-ledger-denial controls.

Choose four load cuts using a CPU fixture: the point before the first semantic
crossover, then the first crossover and `2x` and `4x` that load. Load points
are repeated measures, not independent replications; functional uncertainty
is across independent roots/streams.

Only if explicit text passes may one optional largest-load assay test LoRA
transport with authentic and deranged builds plus read-time cuts, adapter-off,
and wrong-life. Passing says compact semantics were behaviorally accessible
through LoRA. It does not say LoRA compressed the experience. Physical E3
reopens only when complete-state accounting shows at least three useful loads
strictly beyond the actual crossover.

## Maximum claims if modules pass

- **E0:** the frozen writer stored eligible grounded experience, preserved the
  registered organism interfaces and competencies, and transactionally
  rejected/rolled back ineligible candidates under the executed tests.
- **E1:** on the registered childhood distribution, parenting changed later
  parent-absent acquisition/retention/transfer relative to the specified
  comparison children.
- **M:** conditional on one fixed parent-deleted child and the registered root
  distribution, authentic connected experience supported the executed
  connection/traversal/experiment/write/delayed-use relay under the named
  interventions and strong comparator. This is not causal mediation, internal
  graph identification, or a lifetime effect.
- **L:** on the registered lifetime distribution and budget, the full learner
  continued improving across three post-anchor epochs while the certified
  active-text comparator remained at its registered local plateau; it retained
  old competence and ended higher by the frozen practical margin.
- **C:** at most a prospective schema-plus-residual semantic-code result and a
  separate optional LoRA-transport result until complete-state physical
  crossover is actually observed.
