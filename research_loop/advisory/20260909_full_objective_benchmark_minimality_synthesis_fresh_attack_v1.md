## Verdict

**REVISE; do not adopt this as a proof of full-objective minimality or as a
scientific gate.** The core separation of estimands is correct: reset
fixed-child environment roots cannot identify lifetime development, and a
relay success cannot identify compression. Separate denominators and claims
for mechanism, longitudinal development, and rate--distortion are essential.

What is not established is that three physically separate experiments are
mandatory, minimal, or information-optimal. More importantly, the proposed M
core omits controls and comparators required by the bound definitions of E2
and E5, the proposed L experiment lacks an inferential specification, and C
does not state its replication unit. The safe surviving result is a modular
**recommendation for E2--E6**, not a full-objective adjudication and not an
adoptable DEV contract.

## 1. The decomposition is valid, but “mandatory” and “minimal” are too strong

The scientific requirement is separation of estimands, units, accounting,
endpoints, and claims. It does not logically require three executions. The
bound minimality review itself describes a valid, though costlier, lineage-root
design with PCFL episodes nested inside independently generated lineages. A
rate/load panel could likewise be nested into a larger protocol while retaining
separate accounting and inference. Thus M/L/C is a sensible modular and likely
resource-preserving organization, not a mathematical minimum.

The title and “full-objective” framing are also incomplete on their face. The
full evidence stack defines E0 through E6. The proposed M/L/C set covers only
E2--E6. L is not E1: it has no parented/unparented factorial and cannot identify
whether parenting changes the benefit of later personal writes. A writer gate
inside M is not the complete E0 behavioral-preservation result either. The
closing statement that the parenting arc is complementary correctly preserves
the distinction, but it does not put E0/E1 into the stated three-experiment
result. The document must either be labeled an E2--E6 decomposition or add the
separately gated E0/E1 arc to its objective map.

## 2. The reduced M core does not earn the named E2--E5 rung

The synthesis's narrow operational relay can be useful, but its claim and
control list no longer match the bound rung definitions.

### E2 has lost a decisive baseline

The full evidence stack requires `ATOMS_TEXT`, `LINKED_TEXT`, `ATOMS_LORA`, and
`LINKED_LORA`, atomic retention above a floor, and linked-minus-atoms benefit at
matched exposure. Authentic linked versus matched deranged linked carriers
shows sensitivity to correct binding; by itself it does not show that linked
experience improves over isolated facts. A deranged carrier may merely be
harmful while authentic links add nothing over atoms. The fresh minimality
review also calls both derangement **and truthful-null** essential for
connection.

The successor core nevertheless demotes `LINK_NULL` and does not state an
atoms-only LoRA arm or an atomic-retention gate. That is incompatible with
calling the result E2 as presently defined. Either restore a matched
atoms/null baseline and atomic-retention endpoint as claim-gating, or narrow
the sentence to “correct versus deranged binding affected this fixed child's
root-level relay.” Conditional `LINK_NULL` is acceptable only as diagnosis for
that narrower claim, not as a substitute for the missing E2 comparison.

### E5 has lost a required path control and its strong comparator

The full evidence stack's E5 requires `REACHOUT_OFF` and a strong active-text
memory with equal tools, token budget, and public outcomes. The synthesis
demotes `REACHOUT_OFF` unless the final claim names it, and the relay-local TEXT
core is not the certified `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` comparator. The latter is stated
explicitly in the fresh minimality review. Consequently, M can test controlled
selection, write necessity, and delayed use, but it cannot be called the full
E5 flywheel or strong-baseline result without restoring those objects.

The same rule applies to `BRIDGE_TWIN`, `SHAM_WRITE`, binding controls, and
specific leakage controls: a control may be omitted or made diagnostic only
after the released claim is narrowed so that the alternative explanation it
closes is no longer excluded. The synthesis states this caveat abstractly but
then retains the names “E2/E4/E5” and “connection,” creating an internal claim-
control mismatch.

### Conditional controls have a limited valid use

Short-circuiting adapter-off, wrong-life, binding-swap, or other controls after
an earlier failure is logically valid for one preregistered binary product:
once one factor is zero, the joint root endpoint is zero. It is not valid to
turn only the selected core-positive roots into an unconditional estimate of a
standalone attribution effect. Such results estimate a selected, core-positive
subpopulation and are vulnerable to winner's-curse conditioning. They must be
reported as conditional diagnostics or rerun on the fixed denominator for any
population attribution claim.

A “wrong-life” adapter drawn from another environment root of the same fixed
child establishes root/experience specificity, not between-person or
between-child specificity. “Personal” should therefore mean this fixed
child's own admitted root experience, not general person identification.

## 3. The stagewise DEV ladder is a heuristic, not a statistical design result

The ladder is permissible as disjoint no-claim engineering triage, but none of
the bound sources derives its cutoffs from a loss function, cost model,
operating-characteristic target, variance estimate, or decision-theoretic
optimization. Therefore “smallest,” “information-efficient,” and “require” are
unsupported beyond a proposal label.

Specific statistical limitations are:

1. Continuing after `2/4`, then requiring `6/8`, selects favorable early
   realizations. Means and contrasts reported after continuation are
   selection-biased and cannot be treated as estimates with ordinary
   uncertainty.
2. The amber rule is a data-dependent sample-size extension. The acceptance
   boundary (`4/8` normally, but `6/12` after exactly `3/8`) is a defensible
   triage convention, not a calibrated test. It makes the final pass fraction
   depend on the path taken and supplies no error guarantee.
3. “Every block mean positive” and thresholds such as `.20` do not establish
   precision. A mean also permits adverse roots within a component; it is
   non-compensatory across named components, not across roots.
4. Setting unrun LoRA outcomes on TEXT-failed roots to adverse zeros is exact
   for a prespecified joint binary endpoint such as `TEXT_OK * LORA_OK`. It is
   not an observed LoRA potential outcome and cannot identify a LoRA effect or
   a paired LoRA contrast. Zero-filling both unobserved arms fabricates a
   contrast of zero unless the estimand itself is explicitly redefined as a
   gated composite.
5. Repeated protocol edits with fresh DEV identities can still optimize to the
   generator family. A disjoint confirmation protects identity-level reuse,
   but repeated confirmatory attempts after redesign would inflate error unless
   there is one frozen confirmation per claim or a prospective multiplicity/
   alpha-spending rule.

The `OLD x NEW` factorial is a useful minimum for delayed dependence. However,

\[
d^{joint}=Y_{11}-Y_{10}-Y_{01}+Y_{00}
\]

is additive interaction, not a general definition of joint necessity. Positive
`d_write` and `d_old`, together with adequate absolute `Y_11`, test the two
necessity contrasts. Requiring positive additive interaction can reject real
old-and-new dependence under ceiling effects; observing it cannot alone prove
necessity. The successor must say which of these quantities is claim-gating
and why.

The three-build construction can realize the four read cells only if old-row
cuts are faithful read-time interventions and old-plus-pad is exposure-matched
to old-plus-new. That equivalence, deterministic build/randomness handling,
and absence of untargeted read disruption must be proven. Otherwise semantic
treatment remains confounded with build/exposure or out-of-distribution mask
effects.

## 4. Hidden pseudoreplication risks remain

The synthesis correctly names the M root and L lineage units, but the reducers
must enforce them.

- In M, goals, paths, calls, forks, and repeated samples are paired observations
  nested within an environment root. They may improve a root-level summary but
  may not increase `N`. Eight roots means at most eight experimental units,
  conditional on one selected child and the registered root/RNG distribution.
  Root uniqueness is not a proof of stochastic independence or identical
  sampling.
- In L, checkpoints and experience blocks are repeated measures inside a
  lineage. They do not increase the lineage sample size. For efficiency and
  balance, the clean minimum is normally independent starting lineages with
  randomized, isolated full-learner and `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` branches cloned at
  a common pretreatment cut, with trajectory/opportunity matching where the
  estimand permits. Merely assigning different child lineages to the two arms
  is weaker and less efficient unless randomization and baseline adjustment
  are specified.
- In C, load points are not independent replications. Byte rates can be
  deterministic, but functional distortion and path utility need a declared
  independent stream/lineage/root unit and clustered or repeated-measures
  analysis. “At least three points beyond crossover” supplies a shape check,
  not `N=3` independent evidence.

For the exact-binomial confirmation, `N=96, X_R>=47` is coherent for the one
frozen Bernoulli estimand `p_R > .40` only if root outcomes are independent and
identically distributed under a prospectively fixed generator/RNG law,
technical failures are adverse, and the rule is used once. It neither powers
separate E2/E4/E5 effects nor cures within-root pseudoreplication.

## 5. L has the right unit but no adoptable inferential contract

The proposed L conditions are scientifically pointed but insufficiently
specified. Point-estimate statements such as positive late slope, positive
slope advantage, and terminal superiority are not claim tests. L still needs:

- the number of independent lineages and a prospective power/precision receipt;
- exact checkpoint times, root-level slope reducer, failure values, practical
  margins, uncertainty method, and multiplicity/hierarchy;
- a certified, singular `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` baseline with matched ordinary
  affordances and a prospectively defined resource policy;
- an equivalence-based local-plateau rule rather than failure to reject growth;
  and
- handling for the data-dependent time at which the comparator “first” reaches
  plateau. Selecting a hitting time and then measuring from it can bias both
  baseline and learner slopes unless a fixed landmark, independent selection
  data, or valid sequential procedure is registered.

Even after passage, the claim must remain “continued improvement beyond this
single comparator's registered local plateau on this task distribution,
horizon, and resource policy.” It is not intrinsic saturation and does not
support plural strong-memory-baseline wording.

## 6. C separation and arithmetic are sound, but C is not yet a design

The strict crossover arithmetic is correct: an observed 80,792,096-byte
rank-8 adapter requires an expanded equivalent strictly larger than
161,584,192 bytes for a rate below `.50`, before other charged life-specific
state. The synthesis also correctly separates semantic-code compression,
LoRA transport, and physical full-carrier compression.

C nevertheless needs the missing unit and endpoint contract noted above,
denotationally valid compact/expanded comparators, complete decoder/compiler/
index/prompt/candidate-scan accounting, functional distortion margins, and
uncertainty across independent units. Semantic-code compression through the
text interface may be a near-term result without a LoRA byte crossover, but it
must be named exactly that. Combining it with a separate transport result does
not imply that the adapter or full retained agent is compressed.

## 7. Claim-language ruling

The proposed L and C claim boundaries are directionally correct once the
missing inferential contracts are added. M's current wording is too broad for
the reduced controls.

The maximum statement supported by the proposed reduced M core, before
restoring the missing E2/E5 objects, is approximately:

> Conditional on one fixed parent-deleted child and the registered environment-
> root distribution, authentic rather than deranged old bindings affected
> goal-specific selection/traversal, and a controlled new write contributed to
> delayed old-plus-new task success under the executed interventions.

It remains an operational, intervention-tested relay, not causal mediation,
not an internal-graph identification, not a lifetime effect, and not a strong-
baseline superiority result. Calling it the full E2--E5 relay requires the
atoms/null and atomic-retention objects for E2 and the acquisition-path and
strong-comparator objects for E5, plus whatever E4 controls remain named.
