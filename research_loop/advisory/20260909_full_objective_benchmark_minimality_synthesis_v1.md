## Adjudicated result

Use **one benchmark family but three experiments**:

1. **M -- mechanism relay (E2/E4/E5).** One fixed, parent-deleted child is
   cloned across controlled PCFL environment roots. This asks whether its
   authentic personal carrier supports connection, goal-dependent traversal,
   experience-directed experiment selection, a grounded new write, and delayed
   old-plus-new use. The inferential unit is an environment root conditional on
   that fixed child.
2. **L -- longitudinal development (E6).** Independent child lineages receive
   increasing, cumulative experience under the full learner or the strong
   `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` comparator. This asks whether the learner keeps
   improving after the comparator reaches its own preregistered local plateau.
   The inferential unit is a child lineage, not an environment root.
3. **C -- compression (E3).** A separate rate--distortion experiment accounts
   for the complete carrier bytes and semantic distortion across increasing
   loads. At present scale the feasible near-term target is semantic-code
   compression through the text interface plus a separate LoRA-transport
   assay, not physical compression of the entire rank-8 adapter or retained
   system state.

These experiments may reuse the generator, task interface, exact compilers,
and sealed identities. Their denominators, endpoints, gates, and claims may not
be pooled.

## Why the separation is mandatory

The current PCFL relay resets or rebuilds between phases and evaluates
byte-identical clones of one selected child. More PCFL roots improve precision
over tasks **conditional on that child**; they do not create more ages, more
independent children, or evidence of lifetime improvement. Relabeling those
roots as lives would be pseudoreplication.

E6 therefore needs cumulative checkpoints from independently replicated child
lineages, a strong active textual-memory baseline with the same affordances,
the baseline's own registered plateau, at least three later checkpoints, a
positive learner late slope, a positive slope advantage, and absolute terminal
superiority by a frozen margin.

Likewise, a successful joint E2--E5 relay does not establish physical
compression. Current rank-8 serialization is about 80.79 MB, so a positive
expanded-to-adapter byte ratio below 0.5 requires more than about 161.58 MB of
denotationally equivalent expanded semantics before all other retained state is
counted. That crossover is outside the intended near-term relay scale.

## Mechanism experiment: what must remain separately visible

Discovery must not average the mechanism into one compensatory scalar. Keep a
vector whose elements cannot rescue one another:

- authentic versus matched deranged connection;
- authentic versus necessary bridge-cut traversal on two same-start,
  different-path goals;
- authentic versus truthful-sham experiment selection and information gain;
- written versus no-write new-row use; and
- delayed dependence on both the old and the new row.

For delayed old-plus-new use, the smallest clean intervention is the factorial

\[
OLD\in\{available,cut\},\qquad NEW\in\{written,no\text{-}write\},
\]

retaining absolute authentic success and

\[
d^{write}=Y_{11}-Y_{10},\quad
d^{old}=Y_{11}-Y_{01},\quad
d^{joint}=Y_{11}-Y_{10}-Y_{01}+Y_{00}.
\]

These are developmental diagnostics, not released claims. The eventual
confirmation may still use a strict all-links binary conjunction if its only
claim is the narrow joint relay probability.

## Resolution of the two reviewers' apparent disagreement

The minimality reviewer correctly identifies the current eight-root, five-build
V4 screen as the **smallest defensible design already specified in executable
source**. The causal-cost reviewer correctly shows that it is a brittle and
uninformative discovery gate: at the illustrative planning marginals it opens
only about 59% of otherwise planning-compatible programs and provides little
localization when the product endpoint is zero.

Therefore:

- **Current-byte ruling:** V4 remains the only presently specified executable
  source. Nothing in this note silently changes it.
- **Successor recommendation:** deliberate a new no-claim DEV candidate using
  stagewise, non-compensatory paired endpoints and a smaller three-build LoRA
  core. Do not inherit V4's confirmation claim or thresholds by implication.
- **Confirmation ruling:** retain a disjoint binary confirmation only after the
  DEV mechanism is frozen. If its endpoint remains the V4 joint relay
  probability, `N=96` and `X_R>=47` remain mathematically coherent. Any
  separate E2/E4/E5 population claims need a new endpoint hierarchy and power
  receipt.

## Recommended successor DEV candidate (not adopted)

Preseal eight DEV roots and four disjoint reserve roots. DEV is explicitly
no-claim and is never pooled with confirmation.

1. **DEV-0, CPU only.** Prove root/twin uniqueness, chronology, exact compiler
   behavior, hidden-state separation, target-blind closure, reset laws,
   canonical bytes, opportunity tapes, and reducer behavior. A systematic
   counterexample is a version-level `NO_GO`.
2. **DEV-1, TEXT roots 1--4.** Run the minimal connection/traversal/selection/
   old-plus-new core. Stop below `2/4` or if any required paired block has no
   favorable root.
3. **DEV-2, TEXT roots 5--8.** Require at least `6/8` complete TEXT cores,
   authentic B and D means at least `.75`, and every required paired contrast
   mean at least `.20`. No component may compensate for another.
4. **DEV-3, LoRA core.** On presealed TEXT-core roots, run only three distinct
   builds: authentic old-plus-pad, deranged old-plus-pad, and authentic
   old-plus-new. Carry TEXT failures as adverse zeros. Require at least `4/8`
   dual-core roots and no required contrast mean at or below zero.
5. **DEV-4, conditional attribution.** On dual-core candidates only, require
   adapter-off, wrong-life, new-binding-swap, and omnibus no-semantic controls;
   require at least `4/8` survivors.
6. **One amber extension.** Only if exactly `3/8` survive, every block mean is
   positive, and integrity is clean, run the four presealed reserve roots.
   Require at least `6/12`; allow no further extension.
7. **Freeze or restart.** Any prompt, carrier, threshold, control, interface,
   or eligibility change consumes fresh DEV identities.

Bridge cuts, sham signals, old-row cuts, adapter-off, and binding swaps are
read-time interventions. `LINK_NULL`, `BRIDGE_TWIN`, `SHAM_WRITE`, and detailed
leakage localization may run conditionally after their parent contrast is
positive, but they cannot be demoted if the final scientific claim still names
them. This proposed reduction is material and requires full deliberation before
implementation.

## Information-per-GPU-hour order

1. Prove every static theorem, compiler, leakage, reset, and reducer property
   on CPU before model work.
2. Establish the full relay through explicit TEXT before any LoRA build.
3. Use the stagewise endpoint vector to localize failures; never tune against a
   confirmation identity.
4. Build LoRA only on prospectively eligible roots and short-circuit controls
   whose earlier failure already fixes the final product at zero.
5. Run conditional attribution only after core survival.
6. Do not spend mechanism-relay GPUs on E6 or E3 and then relabel the result.

## Claim language preserved by this synthesis

- M may establish an **observed and intervention-tested E2--E5 relay for one
  fixed child across environment roots**. It is not causal mediation and not a
  lifetime claim.
- L may establish **lifetime improvement beyond a matched active textual-memory
  plateau** only with independent child lineages and the registered time-axis
  conditions.
- C may establish **rate--distortion compression** only after complete-state
  accounting and a real crossover; semantic-code compression and LoRA
  transport remain distinct nearer-term assays.

The parenting/developmental experiment and the experiential-knowledge relay
are complementary evidence arcs. Neither substitutes for the other.
