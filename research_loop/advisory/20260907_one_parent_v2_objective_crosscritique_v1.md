## Verdict

**REVISE BEFORE V2 DELIBERATION.**

The synthesis is directionally sound. It preserves exactly one reset frozen
parent and one child per parented life, treats roots as isolated stochastic
replications rather than a classroom, fixes the central `D`/beneficial-write/
absolute-learning/public-value hierarchy, demotes “saturation” to a local
plateau, and explicitly refuses to infer a connected graph inside the LoRA
from CompilerGym performance. Its stated replacement arithmetic of 128 calls,
16,384 generated tokens, and one fit per root is arithmetically correct.

It is not yet an exact repair. C leaves “supported,” “traversed,” “used,” the
expanded equivalent, and compression success underdefined. D does not specify
an identifiable link intervention or an executable immutable-ledger outcome
corruption, gives neither diagnostic a statistical release rule, and omits
several resource classes. The separate carrier panel is the correct place for
a parametric-carrier question, but even that panel can never establish a
human-interpretable graph *inside* LoRA weights. As written it also does not
test goal-directed knowledge expansion, and it does not yet require its source
to be the child's own dispatched actions and public outcomes.

The synthesis can honestly support the narrower headline after repair. It
does not yet close the full standing Dream--LoRA--Think objective.

## What passes

### Topology and causal scope — PASS

- The treatment remains one stateless/reset frozen parent policy teaching one
  child in each parented life.
- U and the deployment services are causal counterfactuals, not peers or
  children that communicate.
- Independent roots estimate life/task realization variation conditional on
  one parent policy and one child checkpoint; they are not a population-
  learning mechanism and do not estimate parent/model heterogeneity.
- The carrier panel is correctly staged as a separate experiment rather than
  another childhood or a rescue for the parenting result.

Any “wrong/twin-life” carrier control remains topology-safe only if it is a
sterile post-seal evaluator assignment with no write, update, communication,
or return edge into either source life. It must not mount another child's state
into deployment life, DREAM, SLEEP, or a shared store. A within-root twin or
binding derangement is cleaner than an actual cross-life memory mount under
the user's ruling.

### Headline causal hierarchy — PASS WITH ONE STATISTICAL REPAIR

The synthesis correctly requires, in order:

1. a positive practical `D`;
2. a positive practical parented write effect `W_P`;
3. positive absolute finite-life P1 improvement;
4. greater P1 deployment gain than R0; and
5. greater absolute terminal P1 value than R0.

This prevents a favorable interaction caused only by U1 being more harmful,
and it distinguishes gain superiority from terminal value superiority. The
root distribution, one-row-per-root object, counter-key RNG, restart law,
failure values, and conditional generalization are material improvements over
v1.

The remaining statistical repair is that the local-plateau sentence and the
C/D diagnostic sentences are new claim families outside the five-rung fixed
sequence. The synthesis neither adds them to a hierarchy nor allocates alpha
or a named Holm/max-t family. The statistics attack explicitly prohibited an
unadjusted second confirmatory family. Each must be declared descriptive or
given an exact multiplicity and joint-power disposition before confirmation.

### Manuscript direction — PASS IN PRINCIPLE

The proposed manuscript edits correctly make active-text strength conditional,
print gain-AUC and `D`, foreground the factorial, close the parent surface,
expand the deletion audit, describe the fixed-slot writer, and use singular
“strong active-memory comparator.” The final edit should also delete the
current manuscript's suggestion that repeated lives under one fixed parent
establish a “general parenting effect.” They establish repeatability over `Q`
for one fixed parent/child policy pair.

## Blocking C issues: the experiential objects are not yet total

### 1. `SUPPORTED_UNIT` does not require supported status

The synthesis defines it as any nonsuperseded live record with earlier own
citations. Under `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED`, a live nonsuperseded record may be
`PROVISIONAL`, `SUPPORTED`, or `CONTRADICTED`. The present definition can
therefore count provisional or contradicted content as supported knowledge.

Use two exact objects:

```text
EVIDENCE_UNIT := any nonsuperseded live record derived exclusively from
                 eligible same-service OWN_OBSERVATION blocks preceding q

SUPPORTED_UNIT := EVIDENCE_UNIT with record.status == SUPPORTED
```

Only `SUPPORTED_UNIT` may enter connected/compressed knowledge counts. Report
provisional and contradicted units separately. A live contract `LINK` whose
source or target is not `SUPPORTED` remains audit-visible but is ineligible for
the claim-facing authentic-link count.

### 2. “Both records returned” does not establish traversal

A linked target can already be independently retrieved by BM25/dense ranking.
If both endpoints appear, the link may have done no retrieval work. Define a
claim-facing traversal as:

```text
TRAVERSED_LINK(q) := source is an intact fused seed;
                     target is absent from the pre-expansion packed seed set;
                     target is added only by source->target one-hop expansion;
                     both complete supported records are rendered before act;
                     all evidence/link commits precede q.
```

This is goal-conditioned one-hop retrieval by the explicit memory mechanism,
not proof that the child adaptively chose a graph path or privately reasoned
over it.

### 3. `EXPANSION(t)` mixes counts with an undefined use judgment

“Subsequently used units” has no byte-level reducer. Retrieval is availability,
not use; prose mention or private reasoning cannot be judged. Split it into:

- exact counts of newly supported semantic keys, cross-program units, and
  eligible authentic links whose first valid commit lies in each era;
- `LATER_RETURNED`, defined by a complete pre-action retrieval receipt; and
- `LINK_VALUE`, defined only by the registered intact-versus-corrupted action
  contrast.

This can show that the automatic textual store grew from own public experience
and that authentic accumulated links later mattered. It still cannot be called
child-directed knowledge expansion. Neither C nor terminal D contains a goal
that causes the child to seek new evidence, write a new unit, and use it on a
still later goal.

### 4. Compression remains a placeholder

`EXPANDED_EQUIVALENT` is described but no canonical construction, byte grammar,
equality oracle, pass margin, root reducer, or functional non-loss comparison
is fixed. “Thresholds must be frozen later” is not an exact v2 repair. Multiple
expanded encodings can reproduce the same typed record, so code length is not
identified until one construction and all shared-cost amortization rules are
bound.

At minimum, v2 must bind:

1. one deterministic expansion algorithm and canonical bytes;
2. exact denotation equality over proposition, scope, status, guidance,
   evidence, link direction, and supersession;
3. whether schema/index/renderer costs are charged once per life or per item;
4. compact/expanded/raw denominators and aggregation order;
5. a frozen practical rate threshold and confidence rule; and
6. a functional non-loss test if the word “compression” rather than “shorter
   code” is desired.

The C reducer alone may establish a shorter **actor-facing textual code**. It
cannot establish complete-system storage compression while the lossless ledger
is retained, and it says nothing about LoRA compression.

## Blocking D issues: neither diagnostic is independently executable yet

### 1. The link corruption cannot both mutate indexed records and preserve seed ranks

Dense documents are complete canonical live records, including
`linked_memory_ids`; BM25 also consumes their full bytes. Changing link targets
changes record bytes, hashes, dense embeddings, and potentially lexical/dense
seed ranks. The synthesis simultaneously requires link derangement and
preserved seed ranks without specifying an intervention boundary.

The smallest identifiable repair is a post-ranking adjacency intervention:

1. run the exact intact query and freeze its pre-expansion fused seed list;
2. keep the intact store, indices, embeddings, seed documents, and ranks
   immutable;
3. apply a separate diagnostic adjacency overlay only to one-hop target
   selection and the diagnostic renderer;
4. preserve link count, directed degrees, endpoint status/type/scope, returned
   document count, and prebound token-length buckets; and
5. never commit the corrupt overlay into the valid store or feed it to life.

Bind the no-fixed-point matching algorithm, bucket boundaries, deterministic
tie/failure law, and exact renderer bytes. If an exact derangement does not
exist, retain the root at the predeclared diagnostic failure value; do not pick
another link, probe, or root. The target/link eligibility rule must be fixed
from pre-action receipts without inspecting probe success or hidden solutions.

Then define one root scalar over all eight assigned probes and its interval,
SESOI, non-erasure condition, and multiplicity family. “Intact P1 must beat” is
not yet a statistical contract.

### 2. The action--outcome corruption conflicts with immutable provenance unless its shadow semantics are specified

The live ledger cannot literally move complete outcome/score events to another
action block without breaking event IDs, ordinals, source hashes, action/result
provenance, and possibly `REVISION` references. The synthesis does not say
whether permuted values affect only admission or also enter training
conditioning. Those are different interventions.

For the intended narrow fixed-history diagnostic, preserve every authentic
ledger and row-conditioning byte. Create a separate sealed diagnostic mapping
that permutes only the outcome/score semantic bundle used by a shadow copy of
the deterministic terminal admission reducer. It must never masquerade as a
valid public event or mutate active text. Rebuild the terminal adapter from:

- the exact authentic childhood rows;
- deployment targets selected by the shadow admission bits;
- the same fixed slots, deterministic rehearsal mapping, anchors, exposure
  law, optimizer, and clean base; and
- the exact authentic terminal P1 active-text store during probe evaluation.

Bind the stratum roster, permutation algorithm, no-fixed-point feasibility,
event-to-shadow provenance receipt, and failure value. Do not condition on the
original outcome/validity bin so tightly that corruption leaves the admission
decision unchanged. Report how many slot identities actually change; do not
exclude zero-change roots.

This contrast can show that correct outcome binding affected terminal
admission/compilation on the already generated P1 history. Because that
history was generated under intact online P1 adapters, it is not another
on-policy lifetime, not mediation, and not proof that outcomes are represented
inside the LoRA. The synthesis's fixed-history qualifier is correct and must
remain inseparable from the result.

As with the link diagnostic, bind the root statistic, interval, SESOI,
non-erasure gate, and multiplicity status.

## The separate carrier assay is necessary but does not yet cover the full objective

The proposed compact/expanded/atoms/deranged/bridge-cut panel, same-corpus
text, adapter-off, and common frozen resolver are the right minimum families
for a **parametric carrier** assay. The following boundaries are mandatory.

1. **Never claim a graph inside weights.** Even a perfect intervention panel
   can show that a LoRA trained on authentic compact linked code preserves and
   exposes information that changes fresh composed actions under a common
   resolver. Behavior cannot reveal a human-interpretable graph topology inside
   an undifferentiated LoRA. Replace “connected/compressed knowledge inside the
   LoRA” with “parametric transport and behavioral use of authentic connected
   experiential code.” The synthesis correctly forbids the internal-graph
   claim without the panel, but the claim remains invalid even *with* it.

2. **Require own interactive provenance.** Every claim-facing source unit must
   originate in that source child's own prospectively available typed action,
   actual dispatch, and public outcome. A generator-authored observation deck
   whose `action` is merely a fixture phase cannot close the standing
   own-experience objective. Carrier/source sealing must precede target
   selection and final goals.

3. **Hold policy supervision fixed.** Compact and expanded carriers must be
   denotationally equal; decision/action targets must be identical across
   carrier arms or absent; a common frozen resolver must act. Otherwise action
   imitation, policy supervision, semantic content, and connection move
   together. Atoms, derangements, and cuts must preserve registered marginal
   dose, candidate domain, and interfaces.

4. **Bind genuine composition and compression gates.** Fresh target actions
   must require at least two prior supported edges, lack a direct stored
   answer/path, and lose the registered gain under both binding corruption and
   a pre-goal necessary-bridge cut. Compression additionally needs exact full
   code accounting, compact-versus-expanded non-inferiority at an unrestricted
   read budget, and a predeclared rate/read advantage. Equal fixed LoRA file
   size is not compression.

5. **Do not call terminal carrier reading expansion.** A terminal fixed-corpus
   panel tests transport, connection, retrieval, and composition. It does not
   test goal-directed knowledge expansion. The latter minimally requires a
   sealed relay in which prior memory changes an information-seeking action,
   that action creates a new public outcome, a later write admits it, and a
   still later goal benefits under sham/binding/write-off interventions. That
   is a separately budgeted experiment, not a zero-call diagnostic.

The carrier panel and relay do not require a classroom or population learner.
Each source remains one isolated child life; carrier arms are read-only causal
clones and exchange no information. Multiple independent roots provide
inference, not social learning.

## Resource-neutrality audit

### Narrow arithmetic — PASS

For two extra terminal probe suites:

```text
2 diagnostics * 8 probe programs * 8 calls = 128 calls/root
2 * 8 * 1,024 generated tokens = 16,384 output tokens/root
1 terminal deranged clean-base fit/root
```

This is below the optional LEAFE-style allocation of 160 calls, 45,056 output
tokens, and one fit/root. The synthesis is exact on these three quantities.

If the replacement runs on every development, pilot, and confirmation root,
the revised ceilings are:

```text
per root: 5,419 calls; 988,928 output tokens; 13 fits
N=20 route / 26 total roots: 140,894 calls; 25,712,128 output; 356 fits
N=32 route / 38 total roots: 205,922 calls; 37,579,264 output; 512 fits
```

If diagnostics run only on confirmation roots, bind that smaller roster and
recompute totals; do not leave the roster implicit.

### Whole-resource neutrality — REVISE

The synthesis does not account for:

- 128 additional actor retrieval/query embeddings per root;
- up to 131,072 returned active-text tokens/root;
- terminal-fit labeled/attended-token ceilings (the same 81,920/655,360
  geometry as the replaced fit only if the fixed slots truly remain exact);
- diagnostic actor input tokens outside the returned-memory block;
- persistent clone/overlay bytes, canonicalization, index/packing work, CPU
  time, wall time, and GPU inference time;
- any record re-embedding or BM25 mutation if the link corruption is
  implemented by changing indexed records rather than by the post-rank
  overlay above; and
- up to 128 diagnostic native action/environment dispatches. The replaced
  LEAFE path has at most 48 rollback plus 64 terminal-probe dispatches, so the
  replacement may increase the action/dispatch ceiling by 16 even while calls
  and generated tokens decrease.

Thus “within the optional call/output/fit envelope” is correct.
“Resource-neutral” without qualification is not. Bind the overlay so it uses
zero updater calls, zero new query types, zero live-store mutation, and zero
record re-embedding; require the outcome-written diagnostic to reuse the exact
terminal P1 store. Publish revised input, embedding, dispatch, persistent-byte,
wall-time, and energy ceilings. If the lease constraint is defined by fit/GPU
time rather than logical calls, demonstrate neutrality with measured p95s.

The separately powered carrier panel and goal-directed expansion relay are not
inside this replacement allowance. They need independent manifests and cannot
be described as resource-neutral additions to the headline.

## Disposition

**REVISE, without changing the headline topology.** C and D are valuable
repairs, and their narrow claims can fit inside the optional fit/token budget.
They do not yet make the standing objective complete. After the exact repairs
above, the headline can validly test one-to-one parenting, beneficial personal
writes, finite-life action improvement, and absolute superiority over one
certified active-memory reference at a registered local plateau. C/D can add a
bounded explicit-text connection and fixed-history outcome-binding result.

Parametric connected-carrier evidence remains a separate controlled assay;
goal-directed experiential expansion remains a later causal relay. Neither
requires nor permits a classroom, peer exchange, shared child memory,
population-learning mechanism, or an internal-LoRA graph claim.
