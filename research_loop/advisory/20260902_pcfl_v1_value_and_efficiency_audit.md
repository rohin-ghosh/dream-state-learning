## Executive verdict

The full v1 roster is scientifically thoughtful but is a poor first purchase
of paper-relevant information.

Its ceiling is 968 Dream calls, 7,216 recurrent Think calls, 24 one-shot Think
calls, and up to 512 native-A-MEM calls: 8,720 model calls, approximately
22--27 occupied device-hours, up to 82 million combined tokens, and three
independent DEV roots. That spend can produce a detailed organ-failure map,
but it cannot produce a paper-level efficacy estimate, population interval,
baseline ranking, context-overflow result, lifetime curve, LoRA result, or
confirmation claim. Almost every model outcome is nested under one open
factor root, one untouched factor root, or one independent root.

The dominant inefficiency is not the causal organism itself. The essential
opaque h/q forks, target actions, and matched null are a minority of the
roster. The cost comes from evaluating nearly every branch, ceiling, cut,
reader, prompt, and baseline at the same full D1/D4-by-goal-by-z surface before
the primary path has shown that it exists. Think alone is 7,216 calls. Several
open-root cells answer attribution questions that become irrelevant when an
earlier organ has already failed; other cells answer paper-comparison questions
that a three-root pre-context DEV cannot settle.

The recommended v2 design is a predeclared sequential diagnostic tree, not a
post-hoc reduced matrix. Its successful path is approximately **3,400 model
calls**, about 39% of v1's call ceiling. Linear scaling from v1's planning
anchor suggests roughly **9--12 occupied device-hours including setup and
slack**, but this is not a budget entitlement. Failure-specific diagnostic
panels add at most roughly 1,000--1,400 calls if every incompatible failure
signature were exercised; in practice a terminal failure stops positive-path
expansion. Native A-MEM remains a separately costed optional panel.

This reduction preserves:

1. the exact construct and 16-way target theorem;
2. the public-prefix-identical raw-A causal revision fork;
3. model-owned opaque-note selection and truth-blind Sleep;
4. physically executed, goal-conditioned action and whole-corpus causality;
5. the matched-independent conditional-identifiability negative;
6. a fresh-session factorized-versus-independent OPERATOR_AST diagnostic;
7. a one-shot Dream and one-shot opaque Think falsifier; and
8. failure-inclusive accounting with no root, target, or arm replacement.

It gives up only simultaneous execution of every attribution and comparison
cell. Those cells remain available behind exact, predeclared diagnostic
triggers and are always reported as `RUN`, `NOT_TRIGGERED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`,
or `NOT_RUN`, never silently omitted.

## What v1's 8,720 calls actually buy

### High-value information

The following v1 components are unusually valuable and should survive any
reduction:

- the Stage-0 factorized and conditional-independent construction, including
  all 32 private `(h,z)`/`(q,z)` states and the exact `1/16` theorem;
- one immutable Dream-1 object forked across raw A continuations;
- branch-correct B predictions and complementary physical target actions,
  rather than mere output inequality;
- OPAQUE_NOTE as the nonsemantic primary, with exact corpus conservation and
  no verifier-selected write;
- whole-corpus antipode/wrong-life and sham interventions;
- the independent-table q fork, whose B and target truth stay conditionally
  unpredictable after A;
- a separate OPERATOR_AST lane on both families, because it converts an opaque
  failure into a representation-bottleneck result without rescuing it;
- exact-program and oracle-schema preflights;
- one-shot controls that bound claims about recurrent Dream and Think; and
- reset, taint, comparator-mutation, failure-totality, and authority gates.

These components determine whether the proposed organism path is alive, whether
raw evidence rather than a comparator owns revision, whether memory changes
action, and whether any positive is specific to a learnable factor family.

### Low marginal value in a first DEV

The following cells are useful eventually, but running all of them at the full
v1 surface before primary survival is inefficient:

- PERFECT_PUBLIC_GATE, NO_GATE_ALL, NO_GATE_HASH1, and NO_REVISION all at full
  target depth and both z sides;
- both RAW_RAG frontiers on every DEV family/side before establishing that
  opaque compilation works;
- neutral, class-informed, and AST Dream panels simultaneously rather than in
  response to a prompt/representation failure question;
- ORIENTATION_DELETED plus three AST structural cuts before the AST lane has
  emitted a valid registered shape;
- repeated D1 evaluation for every control and cut after D1 plumbing is already
  established by exact/oracle/primary cells;
- one-shot Think only on structured cells while the primary opaque Think loop
  remains unablated;
- a conditional native A-MEM reserve before availability, revision, license,
  and native-interface fidelity are known; and
- extensive open-root attribution that cannot increase the number of
  independent roots or turn DEV into confirmation.

The full factorial is appropriate after a primary signal and a paper estimand
exist. It is not the minimum-cost way to learn whether the system is worth that
investment.

## Scientific problems a v2 must repair before spending

A reduced roster must not inherit ambiguity merely to save calls. The v2 bytes
should first resolve both interpretations' common concerns:

1. **Opaque packet claims.** For OPAQUE_NOTE, remove every semantic assertion
   that Sleep or a reader certifies truth, falsity, a completed solution, a
   factor binding, or a minimal semantic item set. Certify only byte/provenance
   closure, target-before-corpus chronology, surface-only retrieval, caps, and
   object-level interventions. Keep semantic truth, no-solution-packet scans,
   and itemwise 13-record claims in structured lanes.
2. **Falsity symmetry.** For opaque memory, use arbitrary footprint/provenance-
   matched bytes, target-manifest mutation, and whole-life counterfactuals.
   Reserve semantically true/false shape symmetry for AST.
3. **Seed map.** Freeze a canonical invocation/randomness map keyed by root,
   family, z, h/q, lane, phase, target, goal twin, arm, cut, and call ordinal.
   State which counterfactual calls share seeds and which are independent.
4. **Catalog firewall.** Event catalogs may expose only predeclared public
   ID/action/time/shape fields. Raw outcome bytes and any outcome-derived hash,
   size, or receipt remain behind the charged READ operation.
5. **Metric reducer.** Define exact failure-inclusive row weights, branch
   pairing, ties, and directional inequalities for S1A/S1B. EMPTY and OBSERVED
   controls must be evaluated on both h/q continuations if they are subtracted
   from both branches.
6. **Causal wording.** Say that Dream made prospective predictions, raw A
   caused branch-appropriate revision when the fork conjunction passed, and
   the frozen corpus caused later action differences. The corpus cannot cause
   predictions committed before it exists.
7. **One-shot scope.** Add one-shot Think on the primary opaque corpus or state
   that recurrent-Think necessity is untested. Use "eligible-information and
   output-capacity matched, interaction/compute unmatched" for every recurrent
   versus one-shot comparison.
8. **Call accounting.** Separate reserved condition slots from actual model
   invocations; a non-LLM EXACT_PROGRAM must not be counted as a model Think
   call merely because it occupies a table row.
9. **Mutation registry.** Enumerate fields mutated by the comparator test and
   explicitly exclude the hidden transition bytes the public actor needs to
   execute the unchanged world.
10. **DEV language.** Bind every result to the registered roots and frozen
    interface. No p-value, population prevalence, baseline superiority, or
    paper efficacy sentence follows from v2 DEV.

These are architecture/test-byte changes, not implementation conveniences.
They require their own deliberated v2 proposal and exact human ratification.

## Smallest staged v2 roster

The counts below assume v1's recurrent caps: 48 calls for one ordinary Dream
life, 64 calls for one shared-Dream-1 two-continuation Dream fork, 82 Think
calls for two D1 goal twins plus two D4 goal twins, and 62 Think calls for D4
plus its goal twin. They are planning maxima. A v2 manifest must recompute them
from its literal assignment table.

### Stage V0: CPU and contract gate -- no model

Keep all v1 Stage-0 construct, schema, reducer, visibility, reset, totality, and
resource tests. The 64+64 deterministic root suite can remain because it is
CPU-only and guards the most catastrophic invalidities. Before any device
work, require:

- exact factorized h and independent q continuation certificates;
- target uniqueness, no recovery, goal twins, and all `(h,z)/(q,z)` states;
- exact-program factor success and independent abstention/prior;
- oracle-schema reader/action success on fixtures;
- prompt/schema/reducer hashes and deployed parser fixtures;
- seed-map, event-catalog, mutation-field, and S1 metric-reducer receipts;
- no opaque semantic/no-solution-packet assertion; and
- frozen assignment rows with terminal `NOT_TRIGGERED` semantics.

Any failure stops. There is no smaller scientifically safe model preflight.

### Stage V1: open-factor causal survival

Run one predeclared open factor root, sampled z plus antipode.

Mandatory calls:

| Cell | Purpose | Planned maximum |
|---|---|---:|
| OPAQUE_NOTE_SELF, both h continuations, z+antipode | Common-prefix raw-A revision, authentic D1/D4 action | 128 Dream + 328 Think |
| ORACLE_SCHEMA_TEXT, one preselected `(h,z)` side | Reader/Think/action ceiling at D1 and D4 | 82 Think |
| EMPTY_MEMORY, ordinary preselected h, z+antipode, D4 twins | Memory-value floor | 124 Think |
| crossed antipode corpus, same h and both z sides, D4 twins | Whole-life binding causality | 124 Think |
| target-independent footprint-matched sham, same cells | Nuisance intervention | 124 Think |

Maximum successful-path Stage V1 cost is therefore about **910 model calls**.
EXACT_PROGRAM is mandatory but non-LLM and is not included.

The all-pass gate is conjunctive and predeclared: exact/oracle validity;
Dream-1 exact A candidate presence; both h branches' exact B predictions;
registered complementary actions; goal-twin first-action redirection; authentic
memory above EMPTY and crossed corpus under the exact reducer; and no comparable
redirection under sham. It is a spend gate, not evidence.

If Stage V1 fails, do not run an untouched factor root. Run only the exact
failure panel specified below, assign every other row `NOT_TRIGGERED`, retain
the open root, and terminate human-required.

### Stage V2: untouched factor plus matched independent specificity

Only after the signed V1 all-pass receipt, run one untouched factor root and
one matched independent root. Neither root may be changed using open-root

Mandatory core:

| Cell | Purpose | Planned maximum |
|---|---|---:|

Maximum Stage V2 cost is about **2,504 model calls**. Together with V1, the
successful path is approximately **3,414 calls**, before infrastructure
diagnostic replays. The exact v2 cap should be rounded upward only for a named
reason; a defensible envelope is 3,600 calls, 500 Dream calls, 3 million output

The terminal gate requires the factorized endpoint to remain valid, opaque
factor D4 to exceed both matched EMPTY and OBSERVED under the frozen reducer,
whole-corpus crossings to redirect while sham does not, the independent exact

diagnostic and cannot alter opaque pass/fail. One-shot matches remove only the
corresponding recurrent advantage language.

V2 always terminates `HUMAN_REQUIRED_FOR_ANY_NEXT_STAGE`.

## Mandatory versus optional cells

### Mandatory causal/identifiability cells

These cells answer a unique question that cannot be recovered from another
cell:

1. exact program and oracle-schema preflight;
2. open and untouched factor OPAQUE_NOTE_SELF with the h continuation fork;
3. independent OPAQUE_NOTE_SELF with the q continuation fork;
4. exact A/B predictions and physical D1/D4 USE/LOCK for primary cells;
5. goal twins for every primary branch;
6. EMPTY and OBSERVED controls matched across both continuation sides in the
   untouched factor and independent sentinel;
7. authentic-versus-antipode whole-corpus crossing and sham on open and
   untouched factor roots;
8. OPERATOR_AST on one presealed factor and one independent side;
9. one-shot Dream on matched factor/independent sides;
10. one-shot Think on the corresponding opaque primary corpora; and
11. all firewall, comparator-mutation, reset, failure-totality, and resource
    rows.

Removing any of these loses, respectively, construct validity, causal
revision, family specificity, action use, goal conditioning, memory value,
life binding, representation localization, recurrent-Dream attribution,
recurrent-Think attribution, or ownership validity.

### Optional failure-attribution cells

These cells should run only under predeclared signatures:

| Trigger, evaluated mechanically | Diagnostic cells | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| No exact A candidate in OPAQUE Dream-1, while exact/oracle are valid | OPERATOR_AST factor; if AST also fails, CLASS_INFORMED factor | Opaque expression/induction failure; if class-informed alone works, supplied-family fitting only |
| Exact A candidate exists but Dream-2 does not produce both branch-correct B predictions | PERFECT_PUBLIC_GATE, NO_REVISION, NO_GATE_HASH1 on the frozen pool | Separate candidate recall, model selection/revision, and public-filter ceilings |
| Dream-2 B is exact but opaque action fails | OPERATOR_AST, ORACLE, one-shot Think, RAW_RAG_FULL on the same preselected target cells | Localize corpus expression, lexical access, recurrent planning, or task-interface failure |
| Opaque factor succeeds and recurrence language is contemplated | one-shot Dream and one-shot opaque Think, already mandatory on V2 | Either remove recurrence language or retain a disclosed protocol difference |
| Generic opaque succeeds and prompt-scaffolding dependence is scientifically important | NEUTRAL_OPAQUE on one untouched factor and one independent side | Dependence on explicit process vocabulary, never spontaneous discovery |
| AST succeeds and a record-level mechanism claim is contemplated | ORIENTATION_DELETED plus predeclared order/binding/sham AST cuts on one factor side | Structured-program dependence only; no explanation of opaque notes |
| Correct candidate exists but corpus selection appears noisy | PERFECT_PUBLIC_GATE, NO_GATE_ALL, NO_GATE_HASH1, NO_REVISION on one factor and one independent side | Proposal-versus-selection decomposition, not a new headline arm |

Each trigger, cell set, root/side, seed coupling, and maximum call count must be
in the v2 bytes before V1. Once triggered, every listed cell runs or receives a
failure row. A favorable diagnostic cannot reopen a failed primary gate or
promote the AST/class-informed/public-gate lane into the organism result.

### Optional ceilings and paper baselines

These should not consume the first causal DEV unless the intended conclusion
requires them:

- both RAW_RAG frontiers: mandatory before claiming prospective compilation
  beats target-time induction, optional for organism survival;
- native A-MEM: mandatory before any A-MEM or broad linked-memory comparison,
  otherwise root-wide `NOT_RUN` and no conclusion;
- CLASS_INFORMED_TEXT: a known-family ceiling, triggered by generic/AST
  induction failure;
- NEUTRAL_OPAQUE: prompt-scaffolding diagnostic, not causal validity;
- PERFECT_PUBLIC_GATE and NO_GATE arms: selection decomposition, not proof of
  model ownership;
- ORIENTATION_DELETED and AST cuts: structured mechanism only;
- equal-token RAW_RAG: a resource-frontier point after RAW_RAG_FULL establishes
  construct sufficiency; and
- additional D1 rows for cuts/baselines: unnecessary after D1 validity is
  established in exact/oracle/primary cells.

If a final paper will claim comparison with RAG or A-MEM, those methods must run
faithfully on the powered root-level confirmation, not merely as one-root DEV
diagnostics.

## Anti-cherry-picking execution contract

A sequential roster is valid only if adaptivity is part of the frozen design.
V2 should enforce all of the following:

1. The full decision tree, gate reducer, thresholds, root IDs, z draws,
   continuation sides, targets, seeds, and maximum cells are hash-bound before
   the first model byte.
2. Gates consume only named immutable receipts. No human or model reads prose
   output to decide which scientific cell runs.
3. A gate may stop spend or route to one diagnostic panel; it may not change a
   prompt, parser, budget, target, root, metric, seed, or claim.
4. Every possible assignment exists in the ledger from T0 and terminates as
   `RUN_SUCCESS`, `RUN_FAILURE`, `NOT_TRIGGERED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or
   environment-wide `NOT_RUN`.
5. A failed primary gate remains failed even if an oracle, gate, AST,
   class-informed, one-shot, RAG, or A-MEM cell succeeds.
6. Untouched roots are never opened after a failed V1 primary gate merely to
   search for a favorable seed.
7. All triggered and untriggered panels are reported. No result-contingent
   subset becomes the denominator.
8. V2 ends human-required. No successful gate dispatches LoRA, lifetime,
   confirmation, publication, or another model family.

This is sequential experimental design, not cherry-picking: routing rules are
fixed ex ante, failures are durable, and optional cells answer only the failure
question that triggered them.

## What a successful v2 DEV contributes to a final paper

If every causal and specificity gate passes, v2 would contribute:

- a validated, releasable candidate construct and exact CPU theorem;
- direct evidence that the frozen protocol can produce at least one correct
  prospective opaque path before investing in powered roots;
- a causal demonstration on registered DEV roots that raw A, rather than a
  comparator label, can change branch-correct B predictions and selected
  memory;
- a whole-corpus intervention showing that life-specific opaque bytes affect
  later executed actions;
- a matched-independent leakage/specificity stress test;
- a representation result distinguishing opaque behavior from
  operator-scaffolded program synthesis;
- empirical call/token/latency distributions for powering and budgeting the
  next study; and
- a frozen failure taxonomy and implementation surface suitable for fresh
  independent review.

In a final paper, these are development provenance, protocol validation, and
possibly an illustrative mechanism trace. They justify the confirmatory
experiment; they do not constitute its evidence.

A successful v2 would **not** contribute:

- a population effect, confidence interval, p-value, or prevalence estimate;
- a benchmark/SOTA, RAG, A-MEM, or external-memory superiority result;
- an advantage caused by context overflow or persistent storage;
- a lifetime acquisition, retention, cross-era, saturation, or growth curve;
- LoRA, weight-space, compression, or parametric-memory evidence;
- a learned Dream/Think/Sleep policy, scheduler, critic, or LOOP adapter;
- memory-shaped exploration or an action-to-better-future-evidence flywheel;
- autonomous schema discovery, naturalistic validity, or human analogy;
- proof that opaque notes contain the AST factor law; or
- any automatic permission to run, publish, or claim the next stage.

## Exact next experiments after a successful v2

The final paper needs three distinct experiments. They should not be collapsed
into one unreviewable campaign.

### A. Root-level minimal-cycle confirmation

Before a paper efficacy statement, create a new material change with:

1. **At least 20 new, disjoint factor orbit roots**, none used in v1/v2, plus
   one matched-independent companion per factor root. Twenty is a minimum
   roster, not a power guarantee; a blinded root-level simulation may increase
   it before ratification.
2. CPU certification of all 32 private states per root, followed by one uniform
   presealed z draw per root. The root remains the only independent unit.
3. OPAQUE_NOTE_SELF h/q continuation forks on the sampled z for every paired
   root; exact A/B prediction, D4 goal twin, EMPTY, and OBSERVED controls for
   every root.
4. On a hash-selected half of roots fixed before output, materialize the
   antipode and run all four authentic/crossed factor-world-by-memory cells plus
   footprint-matched sham. These are nested causal interventions, not extra n.
5. OPERATOR_AST on a predeclared hash-selected diagnostic subset of both
   families, never as a rescue. One-shot Dream and opaque one-shot Think on a
   predeclared subset large enough to bound recurrence claims.
6. RAW_RAG_FULL on all roots if compilation advantage is claimed; add
   RAW_RAG_EQUAL for efficiency claims. Run native A-MEM on all registered
   roots only if its official/vendored implementation passes a pre-run fidelity
   audit; otherwise make no A-MEM comparison.
7. Failure-inclusive root summaries, root-blocked paired contrasts, simultaneous
   intervals for declared estimands, and multiplicity handling frozen before
   model output. h/q, z, antipodes, targets, twins, calls, and samples stay
   inside each root block.
8. A fresh reviewer/advocate gate, new exact human ratification, and a separate
   pre-GPU lock. DEV roots do not enter the confirmation estimator.

This confirmation can support only the minimal-cycle fixed-controller causal
claim. It still cannot support context/lifetime or LoRA language.

### B. Post-context lifetime experiment

The lifetime question requires a different generator and chronology. The exact
next text-only falsifier should be:

1. the chained symmetric D4-complete factor-block construction, with 13 new
   factor vertices/edges per block, one carried connector, no permanent global
   hub, one life-wide orientation, and a compatible model-authored gauge or
   alignment path;
2. tokenizer-measured nested checkpoints at approximately `0.75 L_native`,
   `1.5 L_native`, `3 L_native`, and `6 L_native`, advanced only by new
   factor-bearing events, never padding or replay;
3. repeated per-block tree -> Dream-1 -> raw A -> Dream-2 -> truth-blind Sleep
   -> delayed B chronology with constant local Dream state/read caps;
4. exact new, old, and cross-era D4 cohorts, lagged snapshots, old/new/both/law
   cuts, local 16-way target fibers, and raw-path sufficient frontiers that
   grow with block distance while compiled reads remain bounded only if gauge
   compatibility is proven;
5. first, one open factor root through the first complete prefix above
   `1.5 L_native`, retaining the `0.75 L_native` snapshot, with exact program,
   oracle/class-informed text, RAW_RAG_FULL, and generic opaque text on one
   new/old/cross D4 goal-twin set;
6. only if that passes, the same root through `3 L_native`, then `6 L_native`
   under separately signed gates; and
7. only after the open text mechanism survives, a powered new-root design with
   at least three genuinely post-native checkpoints and root-level inference.

The one-root ladder is a mechanism/resource falsifier, not growth evidence.
The current 15-event module can never substitute for it.

### C. Per-life LoRA transport experiment

LoRA should test transport of an already successful text object, not rescue
failed Dream or create the lifetime result. After the text path and its chosen
checkpoint pass, create a separate material change with:

1. one untouched factor root at a predeclared post-context checkpoint,
   preferably `3 L_native`, with sampled z plus antipode and frozen authentic
   OPAQUE_NOTE corpora;
2. byte-identical compiled text versus a fresh per-life LoRA trained only from
   frozen mechanical note-ID/public-token/verbatim-reconstruction views;
3. direct-QA/raw-history LoRA, no-adapter/candidate-only, wrong-life/crossed
   adapter, identical-text, recognition-assisted, and unaided-reader controls;
4. matched initialization, optimizer, example order, exposure, seed, reset,
   and failure rules, with exact corpus/view/adapter/index/optimizer bytes and
   FLOPs retained;
5. exact read fidelity separated from action, plus authentic/crossed action
   redirection and a sham intervention; no claim of exact factor semantics for
   opaque-note LoRA;
6. an independent OPERATOR_AST LoRA only if symbolic transport is a separately
   named question; it cannot substitute for opaque-corpus transport; and
7. after a one-root canary passes, a newly powered root-level transport panel
   with identical-corpus text as the primary comparator and direct-QA/raw
   methods as baselines.

A minimal-cycle LoRA canary may debug plumbing, but it cannot establish the
claimed value condition because the entire life fits context. The paper-facing
LoRA result must therefore be bound to a genuine post-context life or be called
only a small-corpus transport diagnostic.

## Recommended order

The value-maximizing order is:

```text
new v2 deliberation and exact ratification
  -> CPU/contract gate
  -> approximately 3.4k-call staged minimal-cycle DEV
  -> human review
  -> one-root 1.5L text lifetime falsifier
  -> one-root 3L text continuation
  -> one-root identical-corpus LoRA transport canary
  -> blinded power/cost design
  -> new-root minimal/lifetime/transport confirmation as separately ratified
     claim modules
```

Running the 20-root minimal-cycle confirmation before asking whether the text
organism survives a real context crossing risks powering the wrong paper
question. Running LoRA before text survives risks attributing a content or
reader failure to weights. Running the entire v1 factorial before either
survival test spends most of a device-day on attribution that cannot yet
support a paper result.

## Final disposition

Do not execute v1 merely because its full 8,720-call envelope fits one device
in roughly a day. Feasibility is not value. The roster buys excellent failure
localization but too little independent, post-context, or parametric evidence
for its cost.

The correct next design is a new, exactly deliberated v2 whose approximately
3,400-call successful path preserves the complete causal survival question and
whose additional cells are routed by frozen failure signatures. Even a perfect
v2 remains DEV. Its purpose is to justify and cost the real paper experiments:
new-root confirmation, chained-life post-context text learning, and identical-
corpus per-life LoRA transport.

No action beyond creation of this advisory is authorized.
