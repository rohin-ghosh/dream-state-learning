# One-parent canary and compositional-relay cross-critique v2

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: **proposal-only independent advisory**. This cross-critique authorizes
no frozen-source edit, architecture change, implementation, task/target
generation, model or tokenizer call, parent interaction, adapter fit/mount,
CompilerGym execution, GPU use, external access, scientific-root spend, or
claim. Any adopted change requires the complete `AGENTS.md` deliberation,
exact-byte human ratification, scoped implementation/CPU tests, fresh review,
and separate pre-GPU authority.

## Bottom-line decision

**The existing experimental content is sufficient before `N=32`; the existing
spending rule is not.** Keep the narrow native-writer/near-transfer canary and
then run the already specified exact four-root `P0/P1/U0/U1/R0` deployment
spending pilot. Do **not** add the larger non-compiler bridge mini-lifetime from
v1 of this attack. The deployment pilot already supplies what that addition
was meant to provide: a new ontology after complete parent deletion, 48 unique
wake tasks, three later writes, four sealed cuts, frozen/write-on forks, and the
root-level parenting-by-write interaction.

One source-level repair is non-negotiable before confirmation: the four-root
pilot may not advance on `D_r>0` in `3/4` roots and `mean(D)>0` alone. That rule
can pass while parented personal writes are harmful. Advancement toward
`N=32` must require the **directional conjunction `D`, `W_P`, and
`L_terminal`**, plus the existing zero-validity-failure gates. This is a repair
to the decision rule, not a new task family, arm, or model run.

Thus:

- current canary + current pilot **as written**: insufficient;
- current canary + same exact pilot with a predeclared `D + W_P + L_terminal`
  spending gate and writer-yield receipts: sufficient for the narrow
  fixed-system parenting confirmation;
- larger bridge canary: not required and scientifically duplicative;
- controlled compositional relay: a separate later mechanism experiment, not
  a prerequisite or rescue for parenting confirmation.

## Cross-critique of the two fresh reviews

### What the skeptical review gets right

The skeptical review correctly separates the central causal result from the
larger cognitive thesis. `D` identifies whether this fixed target-blind
parenting protocol changes the marginal closed-loop benefit of later writes.
It does not show positive learning unless `W_P` and `L_terminal` also pass, and
it cannot establish connected/compressed knowledge, chosen traversal,
goal-directed expansion, DREAM causation, or a graph inside LoRA.

It also identifies the main interpretation risk: the parent teaches
prediction/revision behavior that the deployment writer preferentially admits.
That is a legitimate designed pathway, but it may be “better at supplying this
writer” rather than a general improvement in investigation. The exact
accepted-row, target-type, rehearsal-fill, and write-success trajectory must be
reported by arm and cut. It must not be adjusted away, used for matching, or
turned into a post-treatment covariate.

The review is too pessimistic only if its rating is read as a requirement to
solve every representational ambition before testing the developmental causal
question. A clean `D + W_P + L_terminal` result remains a distinct bounded
contribution even without a compositional relay, provided the paper abandons
broad meta-intelligence and connected/compressed claims.

### What the closest-work audit gets right

The closest-work audit correctly finds the broad online-LoRA and evolving
memory lane occupied by TMEM, PEAM, EVAF, LEAFE, Learning on the Job, ACE,
ReasoningBank, ReMem, and related systems. The defensible novelty is not the
writer. It is the target-blind developmental intervention crossed with later
write enablement, with starting competence and later responsiveness separated
by `P0/P1/U0/U1`.

Its proposed TMEM-style, naive/native, matched-batch, and released-memory
comparators are non-negotiable only if the paper claims writer/schedule or
named-method superiority. They are **not required to identify the parenting
interaction**, because all four parenting cells use the same writer and the U
branch is the dose-matched causal counterfactual. Adding writer baselines before
`N=32` would enlarge the systems paper while leaving the developmental
estimand unchanged. Cite the closest work now; add those arms only under their
explicit conditional claims.

A compositional relay likewise does not repair writer-baseline novelty. It
tests a different construct. It should not be sold as the missing TMEM/PEAM/
EVAF comparison or used to inflate the parenting result.

## What is genuinely non-negotiable before `N=32`

### 1. Preserve the narrow canary's actual role

The treatment-neutral writer floor must pass first. The four-root teachability
canary must then show strict, parent-absent, uncued native thought/action change
over pre-write/adapter-off, matched `UNPARENTED_SELF`, and feasible state-target
derangement while preserving typed-forced proposal quality, DREAM/restoration,
and generic behavior. Direct lesson recognition remains diagnostic only.

This licenses “parent-absent practiced recovery behavior on fresh homologous
tasks,” not general learning-to-learn. `AUTH_TARGET_ONLY` remains a scaffold-
dependence test, not a semantic-parent null. A failure ends the path before the
deployment pilot.

### 2. Use the deployment pilot as the bridge test

The four excluded pilot roots must execute the complete future confirmation
surface without local simplification: matched P/U childhood, complete deletion,
`P0/P1/U0/U1/R0`, identical ordinary deployment resources, 48 unique wake
programs, cuts `0/16/32/48`, sealed nonreturning probes, all three transactional
writes, exact failure values, and the v2 reducers.

The pilot is already the smallest honest test of later learning efficiency.
Replacing it with a second non-compiler mini-life would reduce relevance and
still leave the real CompilerGym pathway untested.

### 3. Repair the spending decision

For pilot root `r`, compute the already registered:

```text
D_r          = [gAUC_r(P1)-gAUC_r(P0)]
               - [gAUC_r(U1)-gAUC_r(U0)]
W_P,r        = gAUC_r(P1)-gAUC_r(P0)
L_terminal,r = V_P1,r(48)-V_P1,r(0)
```

Before pilot identities exist, ratify a directional spending rule requiring:

1. zero provenance, firewall, parent-deletion, resource-parity, probe-return,
   transactional, native-routing, DREAM-restoration, mount, and generic-anchor
   violations;
2. `D_r>0`, `W_P,r>0`, and `L_terminal,r>0` jointly in at least `3/4` roots;
3. `mean(D)>0`, `mean(W_P)>0`, and `mean(L_terminal)>0`; and
4. no root crossing predeclared adverse safety/performance bounds.

Do not require the confirmation `.05` SESOIs in four pilot roots; that would
effect-select a tiny pilot. These are direction-and-pathology spending guards,
not efficacy evidence. All roots and raw values remain reported and excluded
from confirmation.

If changing the frozen spending rule is not ratified, do not spend `N=32` under
an “improved learning” objective. A `D`-only pilot may justify only further
engineering investigation.

### 4. Freeze writer-alignment mediation receipts

For every pilot arm and cut, report without conditioning the analysis:

- prospective eligible opportunities and accepted scientific rows;
- `THINK_TO_ACT` versus `DREAM_STATE` mix;
- improvement versus surprise/revision admission route;
- scientific rows versus deterministic rehearsal fills;
- labeled/attended tokens and fixed optimizer positions;
- rejected rows and exact rejection reasons;
- fit, quarantine, mount, and adapter-off outcomes; and
- subsequent strict action and probe value.

These receipts cannot prove general learning, but they reveal whether a
positive interaction is mostly increased writer throughput, different grounded
target content, or benefit after comparable write yield. No post-assignment
matching, stratification, exclusion, or “yield-adjusted D” is permitted.

### 5. Bind the claim before unblinding

Only `D + W_P + L_terminal`, with validity gates intact, permits “this fixed
parenting protocol improved this child system's bounded deployment learning.”
`D` alone permits only “changed/increased the relative benefit of enabling this
write loop.” `C_public` and `T_R0` remain necessary for the separately qualified
full-package comparison. No result licenses parent/model populations,
cross-domain learning-to-learn, connected/compressed LoRA knowledge, or
writer-method superiority.

## Which pieces of the larger bridge canary survive

No extra non-compiler wake stream, extra `P0/P1/U0/U1` fork, or second learning
curve is non-negotiable. Those duplicate the exact deployment pilot.

Two small ideas remain conditional:

- If the manuscript insists on **selective disposition**, add at least one
  parent-absent counter-indicated/no-surprise probe to the existing canary and
  predeclare false activation. Otherwise use “practiced surprise-recovery
  behavior” and rely on strict task value/generic safety to price overuse.
- If it insists on **durable childhood disposition** as a distinct mechanism,
  define a delayed `P0-U0` behavior panel after the deployment horizon.
  Otherwise the registered finite-horizon `D/W_P/L_terminal` claims are enough;
  do not imply indefinite retention.

These are wording-conditional diagnostics, not blockers for the narrow causal
confirmation. The only unconditional bridge content is already in the full
deployment pilot.

## Sequencing the compositional relay without repeating parenting

The relay is necessary only if connected experiential code, goal-conditioned
traversal, or goal-directed expansion remains an empirical contribution. It
must have its own scope, finite nonce-world distribution, estimand, power,
resource manifest, multiplicity rule, ratification, and pre-GPU review.

Recommended sequence:

1. **Freeze, do not execute, the relay protocol before headline unblinding.**
   CPU-only generator/denotation/derangement feasibility may be separately
   proposed. Relay results cannot alter the parenting root roster, endpoints,
   or analysis.
2. **Run the writer/near-transfer canary and exact four-root deployment pilot.**
   Failure stops parenting confirmation. A relay cannot rescue failed native
   writing or a nonpositive `D/W_P/L_terminal` path.
3. **Run `N=32` parenting confirmation under the narrow claim.** Lock all root
   data, fixed-sequence decisions, and manuscript claim eligibility before any
   relay result is opened.
4. **Start the relay from immutable within-root clones of the terminal P1 child
   state, using all eligible locked roots with no outcome filtering.** The parent
   remains absent; no new child, parent call, lesson, P/U branch, or parenting
   interaction is created. Parenting is held fixed as provenance, not
   re-estimated. If terminal P1 does not exist or its required headline gates
   fail, the relay may be pursued only as a separately framed standalone study.
5. **Reset relay-visible context/store to one identical, task-disjoint state in
   every within-root branch.** CompilerGym/nursery text, probes, and target
   answers are unavailable. The common starting adapter may contain its prior
   life, but every relay intervention clones the exact same bytes.
6. **Run the four causal phases:** own-action/public-outcome atomic acquisition;
   fresh counterfactual-goal two-edge traversal; missing-edge information
   seeking and later write; then a still-later goal requiring old plus newly
   acquired evidence.
7. **Randomize only relay mechanisms within the same cloned child:** authentic
   binding/write, adapter-off with fixed history/store, degree/marginal-matched
   binding derangement, necessary-bridge cut, and same-corpus active text.
   Actor-visible memory is removed in the parametric-read condition. No direct
   stored answer or complete path is allowed.
8. **Analyze relay roots separately.** Do not pool relay tasks with CompilerGym
   probes, reinterpret them as more parenting roots, or fold relay success into
   `D`. Use its own fixed sequence: unseen multi-edge use, counterfactual-goal
   path selection, information-seeking action, new public outcome, admitted
   later write, and later value requiring the new edge.

This sequencing tests the missing composition/expansion chain while spending
zero additional parenting realizations. It also avoids the reverse error:
using a positive parenting interaction as evidence that connected structure
exists in weights.

## Final disposition

Advance the narrow canary and full four-root deployment pilot, after exact
authority, instead of building a duplicate bridge canary. Before `N=32`, repair
the pilot's spending gate to require directional `D + W_P + L_terminal`, freeze
writer-yield mediation receipts, and bind the narrow claim. Keep the
compositional relay separate and downstream of immutable parenting results.
This is the shortest scientifically coherent path under one parent, one child,
no classroom, and no population-learning mechanism.
