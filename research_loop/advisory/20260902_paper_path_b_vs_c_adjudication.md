# Paper path B versus C — senior area-chair adjudication

**Date:** 2026-09-02

**Status:** advisory only. This is not an architecture decision, ratification,
implementation instruction, preregistration, model-call or GPU authorization,
or scientific result. It does not alter any frozen plan or authority artifact.
The seven-day sequence below is a scientific priority order; every executable
stage remains subject to the repository's deliberation, exact human
ratification, independent review, and GPU gates.

## Decision

Choose **C: stop PCFL-lite as a standalone program and directly build the
LOOP/whole-lifetime study**, with one deliberately tiny precursor embedded in
that same study.

This is not a recommendation to launch the full LOOP-by-memory factorial or a
rank pyramid. It is a recommendation to spend the next seven days testing one
causal microcycle that is already on the path to the paper:

```text
earlier action/outcome memory
-> frozen cross-life operation policy chooses an information-seeking action
-> public outcome becomes new life-local memory
-> later held-out action improves
```

As of September 2, only sixteen calendar days remain before September 18. The
proposed PCFL-lite sequence would consume a material fraction of that window
and, even if completely positive, would still leave the headline experiment
unrun. Under this deadline, calibration is justified only when its artifacts
are the actual lifetime harness, traces, controller interface, and causal
intervention used in the eventual paper. The proposed standalone PCFL-lite
does not meet that standard.

## Why C dominates B under the stated objective

The project has already established several prerequisites: atomic experiential
content can be installed in LoRA; recognition reads can be faithful; a clean
base can compose supplied leaves; and recurrent atomic traversal can work when
the leaves and interface are good. It has also established the important
negative that a direct-proposal dreamer can create a poisoned corpus. The
highest-value unresolved uncertainty is therefore no longer whether another
finite synthetic protocol can certify a target-blind local memory cascade. It
is whether a learned, reusable operation prior changes evidence acquisition
and produces a better later life.

That uncertainty is exactly where the user's LoRA-as-agency proposal becomes
testable in its defensible form: not “agency,” but **across-life transfer of a
policy over legal retrieval, search, backtrack, write, act, defer, and stop
operations**. A positive result can be connected directly to later action. A
negative result kills the rank-pyramid motivation before expensive hierarchy,
merge, or base-promotion work.

The alternatives rank as follows for information gained per model/GPU dollar:

| Path | First expensive uncertainty answered | Directness to the flywheel | September 18 value |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Full proposed PCFL-lite | Whether an elaborate new finite world, writer, compiler, reader, text memory, and LoRA pipeline jointly work | Stops at memory -> action under scripted experience | Too indirect and too large |
| Direct full LOOP/lifetime factorial | Many controller, memory, world, and substrate questions simultaneously | High | Too confounded and too large |
| **C with the microcycle gate** | Whether a frozen learned operation prior causes useful evidence acquisition and later action | **Highest per call** | **Best option** |

Path B would be preferable without the deadline if the team valued a
standalone controlled-memory benchmark result even when text/RAG beat LoRA.
That is not the decision posed here: the objective is a paper by September 18
and direct progress on action -> experience -> memory -> better action.

## Is the proposed PCFL-lite still too large?

**Yes. It is substantially too large for a calibration gate.** “Lite” is
relative to the V3 governance/runtime design, not relative to a seven-day
scientific decision.

The proposed design includes 32 CPU-qualification twin pairs, four DEV twin
pairs, eight TEST twin pairs, two lifetime cuts, an exact no-lifetime
controller, counterfactual worlds and renderer variants, a model-owned writer,
chronological claim admission, a deterministic compiler, bounded recurrent
THINK, a native linked-memory baseline, iterative raw RAG, context, two oracle
conditions, direct-QA LoRA, same-corpus text/LoRA, binding perturbations,
wrong-life assignments, installation rank selection, action/path audits, and
root-level inference. Stage T alone is nine conditions over eight DEV roots at
two cuts before any LoRA test. Stage L then opens the complete roster over
sixteen untouched roots and multiple newly trained adapters.

Every listed control is individually defensible. Together they constitute a
small benchmark paper, not a minimal precursor. More importantly, the design
still fixes the source action stream. It cannot show that memory changes what
the agent learns next, cannot show reconsolidation of policy-caused outcomes,
cannot show a learned LOOP policy, and cannot show continuing improvement after
strong baselines flatten. Its engineering surface is therefore much larger
than its contribution to the deadline-critical claim.

The finite PCFL-13 backbone is also only thirteen mappings, about 16.7 bits.
The PCFL-Stream repair correctly adds genuinely new causal cohorts, but doing
so turns B into the lifetime study itself. Once that move is made, there is no
scientific reason to pay first for the complete standalone PCFL-lite roster.

## Smallest decisive precursor

The smallest decisive precursor is a **one-cycle flywheel relay**, not the
whole PCFL-lite assay and not a rank sweep.

Each sealed evaluation life contains a prior public action/outcome prefix and
a fresh local causal uncertainty. The current life memory contains one old
supported relation but lacks one discriminating recent relation. At an
identical cloned public state, the agent must choose one information-seeking
operation/action. Only one legal probe distinguishes the live alternatives.
Its ordinary public outcome is appended to the life and deterministically
compiled into a supported one-edge fact. After context/KV reset, a later fresh
goal requires joining the old relation with the newly acquired relation to
execute a legal action.

Use a minimal randomized factorial at the cloned decision:

```text
operation policy:  P-BASE versus P-LOOP
life memory:        authentic versus wrong-life/binding-shuffled
```

Add `P-SKILL` or bounded operation demonstrations and matched ordinary
trajectory SFT as the two strongest alternative explanations for P-LOOP. Use
bounded text facts for the first causal relay. The source facts should be
mechanically compiled public action/outcome records so a learned dream writer
does not confound the controller question. Exact facts remain evidence; the
LOOP adapter is only a policy bias.

The relay is decisive only if all four links move in the expected direction:

1. At byte-identical states, P-LOOP changes the legal operation distribution
   toward the discriminating query/action on unseen, renamed worlds.
2. The chosen action produces greater information gain or a more diagnostic
   public outcome than the best prompt/skill/trajectory-SFT control.
3. That outcome is admitted through the same target-blind compiler and is
   actually used in the later decision.
4. Later held-out action value improves, while wrong-life or binding-shuffled
   memory removes at least half the gain.

An operation-classification improvement alone is only an installation result.
A later-action improvement without the operation shift and authentic-memory
dependence is not evidence for the proposed LOOP mechanism. This relay is the
smallest experiment that reaches the last two arrows of the flywheel rather
than merely testing memory playback.

## Seven-day gated sequence

The sequence is intentionally serial. A failed gate cancels all later GPU work;
no larger rank, model, world roster, prompt search, or PCFL repair may rescue it
inside this seven-day window.

### Day 1 — freeze the causal relay and qualify it on CPU

Bind the whole-world train/DEV/TEST split, legal operation grammar, public
state, deterministic one-edge compiler, cloned-state intervention, three
checkpoints, scorer embargo, reset boundary, endpoint, and resource cap. Reuse
an existing action-world/runtime only if it can express the relay without a
new benchmark rewrite.

CPU gates:

- exact-memory/oracle later-action value `>= 0.85`;
- exact no-lifetime adaptive value `<= 0.35`;
- authentic memory gains `>= 0.30` over no life memory;
- wrong-life/binding assignment removes at least half the gain and `>= 0.15`
  absolute;
- target/current-state/renderer-only routes remain `<= 0.35`;
- deterministic replay, sealed evaluation, and per-life reset pass.

**Kill:** any gate fails, or the relay requires inventing a new PCFL-scale
runtime. Spend no model calls; either simplify the relay once on TRAIN/DEV
under a new frozen release or stop this paper direction.

### Day 2 — build and audit the operation corpus

Generate verified successful and failed traces on development worlds with the
frozen teacher/exhaustive controller. Project only:

```text
PUBLIC_STATE + legal menu + bounded memory return -> NEXT_LEGAL_OPERATION
```

Remove hidden proofs, future outcomes, target allocations, rationales, scorer
fields, and life identifiers. Preserve failed/backtracked traces and balance
world, operation, length, and outcome strata. Seal untouched renamed and
counterfactual worlds before training.

**Kill:** a target/answer/future descendant survives mutation tests; fewer than
two genuinely different search motifs exercise query and backtrack; operation
labels can be predicted from handle, renderer, position, or source-world ID at
the registered shortcut ceiling; or test-world bytes influenced selection.

### Day 3 — cheap cloned-state operation-transfer gate

Train one small P-LOOP LoRA on the masked operation target. Start at `r=8` on a
frozen 7B-class base and fixed layer set. Train matched ordinary trajectory SFT
on the same verified examples and resource envelope. Compare on DEV cloned
states against P-BASE, bounded P-TEXT/P-SKILL, and trajectory SFT. Record legal
operation logits, selected action, query redundancy, backtrack timing, and
resources.

**Advance only if:** P-LOOP improves the predeclared operation-alignment or
diagnostic-action rate by at least `0.10` over the strongest frozen
prompt/skill control on every DEV world, survives handle/skin and
counterfactual renaming, and does not decode world identity/content above the
registered null.

**Kill or narrow:** if trajectory SFT matches within `0.03`, delete the
LOOP-specific claim and use the simpler winner only if the paper can be
reframed honestly. If `r=8` underfits the training operation alphabet, permit
one predeclared `r=16` installation check; do not sweep ranks on evaluation.

### Day 4 — run the one-cycle flywheel relay on DEV

Execute the `P-BASE/P-LOOP x authentic/wrong` randomized memory assignment at
byte-identical cloned states. Let the selected information action produce one
ordinary outcome, compile it, reset short context, and score the later action.
Include the best prompt/skill/trajectory-SFT controller identified on Day 3.

**Advance only if:** P-LOOP improves both diagnostic evidence acquisition and
later action value by `>= 0.10` over the strongest controller baseline under
authentic memory; the new public fact is cited/used in the later bounded path;
and wrong/binding memory removes at least half of the authentic gain and
`>= 0.15` absolute. The sign must agree on every DEV world.

**Kill:** operation shifts without later action gain, later gain without an
authentic-memory-dependent shift, any evaluation feedback entering later life
state outside the declared public outcome, or any reset/taint failure.

### Day 5 — extend the same harness to a short whole life

Do not introduce a new environment. Run early, middle, and late checkpoints
with genuinely new causal cohorts and presealed new/old/cross-era goals. Use
bounded text memory and compare P-LOOP against the strongest Day-3 controller,
no persistent memory, and one strong iterative raw/linked-memory baseline.
Measure action value, regret, information gain, old retention, new acquisition,
cross-era composition, operation counts, and memory/read compute.

**Advance only if:** the P-LOOP advantage in action value is `>= 0.10` at the
late checkpoint, its early-to-late improvement exceeds the strongest baseline's
by `>= 0.10`, old-goal value does not fall by more than `0.10`, and both new and
cross-era cohorts have favorable signs. New unique supported structure must
increase at every checkpoint; transcript growth alone does not count.

**Kill:** improvement is only early, only recall, only extra actions/tokens, or
only one renderer/order; a strong explicit memory wins under its native
interface; or the per-query hidden work scales as an unreported exhaustive
scan.

### Day 6 — conditionally test per-life MEMORY-LoRA transport

Only after the text flywheel passes, realize the identical admitted life corpus
in one freshly reset per-life MEMORY LoRA using the existing proven write/read
recipe. Keep MEMORY and LOOP adapters separately mountable. Compare M-TEXT and
M-LORA with the frozen P-LOOP and run the same wrong-life assignment. Add the
faithful direct-QA/content-LoRA comparator only here.

**Transport gate:** fixed-read fidelity `>= 0.90`, M-LORA action value no worse
than M-TEXT by more than `0.10`, wrong-life assignment removes the registered
gain, and the direct-QA comparator does not erase the claimed compilation
advantage.

**Disposition:** transport failure does not erase the LOOP/text result, but it
removes Dream-LoRA-Think and parametric-life-memory language from the paper.
Do not increase rank, merge adapters, or change the compiler to rescue it.

### Day 7 — one untouched whole-world decision run

Open four presealed untouched evaluation world-lives only after all prior
bytes and thresholds are frozen. Run the smallest complete roster:

- best nonlearned/trajectory controller with M-TEXT;
- P-LOOP with M-TEXT;
- P-LOOP with wrong/binding memory;
- no-persistent-memory plus the strongest raw/linked baseline; and
- P-LOOP with M-LORA and matched direct-QA LoRA only if Day 6 passed.

Use the three lifetime checkpoints and new/old/cross-era endpoints. World-life
is the unit; targets, checkpoints, and adapter seeds are not replicates. Report
every failure and all resource vectors.

**Go to paper-scale confirmation only if:** mean late-life P-LOOP action gain
over the strongest baseline is `>= 0.10`, at least three of four untouched
world-lives favor P-LOOP, the developmental difference is `>= 0.10`, all three
goal cohorts retain favorable signs, and wrong/binding memory removes at least
half the authentic gain and `>= 0.15` absolute. Otherwise stop expansion and
write the bounded negative or diagnostic result.

The Day-7 sample is a go/no-go pilot, not confirmatory evidence. A September 18
paper claim would still require approximately Days 8--11 for a newly untouched,
prepowered world-life confirmation and Days 12--16 for analysis, ablations,
artifact audit, and writing. If that confirmation window cannot be reserved,
do not spend Days 1--7 pretending the pilot is the paper.

## Rank-pyramid and LoRA-as-agency ruling

Do **not** test the rank pyramid, weight-mediated SVD promotion, merge/reset,
singular-value decay, or base promotion in this paper cycle. Rank changes
capacity and optimization, not learning time. The geometric hierarchy would
confound rank, data volume, optimizer state, update frequency, teacher quality,
and chronology. Its information value is near zero until a single small LOOP
adapter first transfers operations and improves held-out action.

For this adjudication:

- `P-LOOP` means a frozen across-development-life operation-policy LoRA;
- `M-LORA` means a fresh, life-local content adapter;
- exact supported facts remain in the evidence system and never become true
  merely because either adapter emits them;
- P-LOOP and M-LORA never merge, share optimizer state, or cross a life; and
- the eligible phrase is “experience-conditioned operation-policy transfer,”
  not learned agency.

If the seven-day sequence passes, a later paper may study the rank/data
response surface on development worlds. The first fair question is whether a
fixed small rank matches larger ranks at equal verified examples and compute,
not whether a presupposed pyramid can be made to work.

## Claim boundary and final paper advice

A positive seven-day result would permit only this provisional statement:

> On untouched synthetic world-lives, a frozen cross-life operation adapter
> changed information-seeking behavior; the resulting public experience was
> reconsolidated into isolated life memory and improved later held-out action
> relative to strong prompted/trajectory and external-memory controls.

Add “the same life corpus transported to a per-life LoRA” only if Day 6 and the
untouched evaluation transport cells pass. Add continuing-development or
post-context language only after a powered confirmation has multiple genuinely
post-native checkpoints with increasing unique causal coverage and strong
baselines are observed—not assumed—to flatten.

This path intentionally leaves model-owned dreaming/selection as a later
factor. That is scientifically cleaner than making a failed writer obscure the
new controller question. Once the operation-policy and closed-loop relay work,
the model-owned DREAM writer can replace the deterministic compiler in a
separate causal intervention. Until then, PCFL-lite's broader writer/compiler/
substrate roster spends the deadline testing the wrong bottleneck.

**Final adjudication: C.** Retain PCFL's essential lessons—whole-world splits,
target embargo, legal action endpoints, exact headroom, wrong-life/binding
controls, fresh life resets, and full trace/resource reporting—but do not build
PCFL-lite as a standalone prerequisite. Build the smallest closed-loop relay
inside the lifetime harness, kill it quickly if any causal link fails, and
reserve the remaining September window for untouched confirmation and the
paper.
