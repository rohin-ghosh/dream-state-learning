# One-parent v2 manuscript cross-critique v1

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: independent proposal-only cross-critique of
`20260907_one_parent_v2_repair_synthesis.md` against the manuscript consistency,
statistics, and objective-coverage audits. This memo changes no synthesis,
manuscript, bound source, workflow, implementation, benchmark, model,
tokenizer, adapter, or GPU state and authorizes none of those operations.

## Verdict

**REVISE.** The synthesis faithfully preserves Rohin's topology and repairs the
three central manuscript blockers in the correct direction. It should not yet
be called exact enough for v2 ratification: the local-plateau claim family is
not allocated statistically, `R0` is omitted from the cut-0 checkpoint law,
the maximum parenting sentence is not explicitly conditional on the one fixed
parent/child policy pair, and several new diagnostic reducers still defer
decisive failure and threshold bytes.

These are bounded repairs. There is no reason to reopen the one-parent/one-child
decision or replace the `P/U` factorial.

## What passes

### Topology fidelity — PASS

The synthesis keeps one reset frozen target-blind parent, one taught child,
fresh-task application, public-world admission, complete pre-deployment parent
removal, and no classroom/cohort/peer/teacher-ensemble/shared-memory/population
mechanism (`synthesis:10-27`). It correctly treats roots as isolated stochastic
replications under a stateless reset parent policy rather than parents learning
across children (`synthesis:53-69`). This disposes the topology concern in the
manuscript audit (`manuscript-audit:104-123`) and matches the two peer audits
(`statistics:97-126`; `objective:79-95`).

For byte-level clarity, the opening topology should still say explicitly that
the U child is an isolated counterfactual branch with no edge to the treatment
dyad. That relation is implicit in the five-service root later, but the v1
headline made it explicit.

### Causal and claim hierarchy — PASS in structure

The synthesis restores the exact entry-adjusted gain-AUC and makes `D` the sole
primary rather than treating pointwise curves or generic `nAUC` as alternatives
(`synthesis:93-116`). It then requires positive `D`, positive parented write
benefit `W_P`, absolute P1 improvement, greater P1 deployment gain than R0, and
absolute terminal P1-over-R0 value in that order (`synthesis:118-137`). This
closes the peer-audit counterexample in which `D>0` while writes still harm P1
(`statistics:393-409`) and the objective audit's distinction between gain and
absolute outperformance (`objective:137-162`). `P1-R0` remains explicitly a
full-package public contrast, never the parenting estimand.

### Proposed manuscript corrections — PASS in direction

The seven corrections at `synthesis:263-277` resolve the manuscript audit's
three freeze blockers:

- active text stays a candidate until its once-only strength evidence passes,
  and stores are isolated despite sharing one frozen policy;
- exact gain-AUC and `D` replace undefined `nAUC`, with the four-cell
  interaction leading causal results;
- the parent surface, deletion audit, fixed-slot clean-base writer, and
  topology-versus-root distinction become explicit; and
- undefined secondary endpoints are deleted rather than defined after data.

The synthesis also uses singular comparator language and correctly prevents
CompilerGym from being relabeled as proof of a connected graph inside the LoRA
(`synthesis:246-261`). Its conditional maximum active-text statement is narrow:
connection/traversal belongs to the explicit textual layer, and compression of
the full retained life is not claimed (`synthesis:190-218,334-374`).

## Required revisions before PASS

### 1. Close the local-plateau statistical family

The five-rung fixed sequence claims family control (`synthesis:118-146`), but
the later R0 local-plateau release rule adds two equivalence intervals, a search
headroom comparison, two one-sided increment tests, and the terminal R0 rung
without placing that composite claim in the fixed sequence or assigning it a
separate error budget (`synthesis:165-188`). The statistics audit explicitly
required the plateau claim to be the next gated composite, receive separate
alpha, or remain descriptive (`statistics:263-294`).

V2 must choose one of those dispositions and bind the simultaneous equivalence
rule, sidedness, familywise level, exact equivalence margin, and whether the
`.10` headroom condition compares the search reference with mean R0 or another
quantity. Also state whether operating-characteristic simulation powers only
the primary `D` joint rule—as the statistics audit recommends—or the whole
five-rung sequence. Calling routing/proposal checks "safety gates" does not by
itself resolve their joint aggregation, uncertainty, or multiplicity; v2 must
bind those bytes (`synthesis:150-163`; `statistics:283-289`).

### 2. Repair cut 0 and resource-parity wording

The outcome law applies to every service `c`, but says cut 0 comes from "the
exact common P or U checkpoint" (`synthesis:93-101`). It omits R0's raw pinned
checkpoint. State all three entry cases exactly: one shared P checkpoint for
P0/P1, one shared U checkpoint for U0/U1, and raw pinned actor weights for R0.

Likewise, "equally provisioned" in the public story (`synthesis:20-23`) must
mean equal ordinary **deployment** affordances. R0 receives neither the P/U
practice-written childhood state nor fitting compute. Therefore P1-R0 is not an
equal-total-compute, equal-history, equal-memory-content, carrier-only, or
component comparison (`manuscript-audit:132-139`; `statistics:324-347`). Add
that qualifier to the synthesis's manuscript corrections, not only to the
methods discussion.

### 3. Make the released parenting sentence distribution-bound

The root section correctly limits inference to one fixed parent policy and one
fixed child checkpoint (`synthesis:53-69`), but the proposed maximum sentence
begins "In independent one-parent/one-child lives" (`synthesis:334-343`). That
can still be read as replication over parents or child models. Use the peer
audit's exact scope: under this fixed reset parent policy, fixed child
checkpoint, curriculum/writer/resource layer, and registered root distribution,
parenting **increased** the benefit of later writes (`statistics:97-126,411-418`).
Do not promote this to a general parenting effect or broad model
meta-intelligence. Also change rung 1's "changed" to "increased" because its
release rule is directionally positive (`synthesis:123-124`).

### 4. Finish the new diagnostic reducers or label them unresolved v2 fields

The experiential ontology and replacement diagnostics are scientifically
well-scoped, but not yet exact enough to claim closure:

- `SUPPORTED_UNIT` does not say which live statuses qualify, and
  "subsequently used unit" has no total reducer (`synthesis:190-206`);
- compression rate and functional-nonloss thresholds are deferred, so the
  shorter-code release condition is not yet reproducible
  (`synthesis:208-218,345-356`);
- the read derangement lacks an explicit disposition for roots with no usable
  link or no feasible matched derangement; and
- the action--outcome derangement lacks the peer audit's explicit prohibition
  on stratifying by original validity/score bin and an impossible-derangement
  failure value (`synthesis:220-244`; `objective:263-332`).

Bind those definitions, retain every preassigned root, and state exact
failure-inclusive values before calling these "exact repairs." They may remain
clearly named fields for the v2 deliberation, but cannot be silently completed
during implementation or manuscript writing.

### 5. Complete the manuscript repair list

The synthesis resolves the blockers but omits several lower-level corrections
from the manuscript audit. Add explicit instructions to:

1. name the two nursery families and frozen dose/schedule, not only the three
   lesson codes;
2. describe `REFLECT` as an attempt with conditional/no-call `CURATE`, and
   retrieval as complete records plus lexical-only public event blocks;
3. state the R0 full-package/resource qualification above;
4. prevent wrong-child/shuffled advice from being captioned as a guaranteed
   null; and
5. keep the frozen parent identity, exact root/statistics bytes, figures,
   citations, reproducibility receipts, and AI-system versions pending until
   their actual immutable evidence exists.

These are manuscript exactness tasks, not new scientific arms
(`manuscript-audit:96-152`).

## Overclaim boundary after revision

The conditional active-text and terminal claim language is otherwise sound.
If the full hierarchy and a multiplicity-valid R0 plateau composite pass, the
paper may report absolute terminal outperformance at a registered local plateau.
If the rate audit and matched link derangement pass, it may separately report a
shorter connected actor-facing textual code and functional one-hop bindings.
Neither result establishes a connected/compressed graph inside the LoRA,
adaptive child graph traversal, compression of the retained full system,
multiple strong memory baselines, or generalization across parent policies,
child checkpoints, or deployment distributions.
