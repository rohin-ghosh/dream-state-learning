## Verdict

**REVISE. V3 is a substantially corrected and scientifically honest
directional map, but it does not yet pass exact source/authority review.**

V3 fixes the material scientific defects identified in the supplied v2 audit.
It keeps E0 and E1 open, restores fixed-denominator control logic for M,
preserves independent-root units, incorporates the exact E3/E6 sources rather
than treating summaries as replacements, narrows E6's released words to its
actual tests, and repeatedly denies protocol or execution status. It should
not be rejected and does not need a scientific-topology rewrite.

Two narrow defects remain. First, v3 describes two explicitly unbound advisory
designs as “complete normative” contracts whose requirements “apply,” which
is avoidable authority inflation even though v3's opening disclaimer denies
adoption. Second, its exact `N=96, X>=47` reservation and associated derivation
conditions are not independently supported by the supplied source boundary.
Those defects require a source-only successor repair before v3 can receive a
clean PASS.

## 1. E0 is correctly open and its evidence classes are not pseudoreplicated

V3 accurately carries the writer-wiring audit: the long-life runner uses the
legacy bare-text path and adapter-head rejection does not quarantine the
associated corpus from later cumulative rebuilding. It also correctly
separates deterministic transaction/provenance fixtures from stochastic
behavioral evidence.

The deterministic side binds source/model/tokenizer/compiler/trainer/mask/
rank/dose/seed/corpus identity, candidate-only staging, distinct adapter-head
and evidence-corpus promotion, rejection quarantine, atomic rollback, prior-
child preservation, and end-to-end receipts. The stochastic side retains
absorption/retention, native routing and typed proposal/provenance, previous-
child noninferiority, adapter-off, wrong-life/root, binding shuffle, and
failure-inclusive root reducers. Calls, prompts, rows, samples, and repeated
fits do not inflate `n`.

Most importantly, v3 supplies no invented panel size, margin, failure value,
uncertainty method, or power claim. It says E0 is an unresolved blocker. That
is the correct directional disposition on these bytes.

The V8/V9 size attribution is also repaired exactly: the `161,345,175`-byte
object is the V8 architecture-change JSON/derived exhaustive projection, while
the V9 repair draft is about 9 KiB.

## 2. E1 is correctly open, and the proposal-only authority is accurately bounded

The old v1--v4 `P0/P1/U0/U1` surface and root-level interaction are represented
faithfully enough for a map. The old candidate does distinguish later
write-enabled learning from an entry-only shift, and v3 correctly counts one
independently raised root as one row while treating lessons, tasks, calls,
fits, probes, and classroom branches as repeated measures.

V3 also correctly refuses to adopt that older candidate. The supplied v5
proposal says it supersedes v4's scientific topology and claim object; the
closure receipt binds the exact v5 hash, names Rohin as ratifier, says it
supersedes v4's scientific intent, and limits authority to proposal closure
and preparation of a later proposal. It explicitly forbids implementation,
model/tokenizer execution, benchmark generation, adapter work, GPU use,
resource acquisition, scientific execution, promotion, and publication. Thus
“user-ratified ... proposal” is supported only in that proposal-closure sense,
which v3 preserves by calling the receipt proposal-only and leaving E1 open.

The contamination rules are consistent with the supplied v5 and continuity
bytes: bounded nonparametric parent development may use short- and delayed-
horizon evidence; confirmation must freeze the parent or the complete lawful
adaptive update rule; outcomes do not cross independent roots; unchanged
mechanisms may continue a canonical child; mechanism changes fork an immutable
sibling; developmental/final descendants do not flow back; and final
identities restart prospectively from clean frozen stages.

V3 does not pretend that this settles the mismatch between the older
parenting-by-write interaction and v5's paired adaptive-development/final-2x2
target. It explicitly requires reconciliation and releases no final factors,
claim, margin, reducer, power rule, or sample size. E1 openness therefore
passes.

## 3. M fixes the control and denominator defects, subject to one provenance repair

At the level appropriate to a directional map, v3 repairs the v2 defects:

- atoms, truthful-null, authentic-linked, and deranged-linked text and LoRA
  carriers are distinct matched-exposure interventions rather than
  slash-combined cells;
- the common actor read plan and adapter-off, wrong-life/root, binding,
  authentic/deranged, atomic-retention, linked-minus-atoms, and linked-minus-
  null gates close the named E2 carrier alternatives;
- E4 requires same-start goal-dependent paths, pre-action authentic returns,
  necessary-bridge cuts/twins, derangement, leakage controls, and root-level
  rather than path-level replication;
- E5 preserves the public old-gap-action-outcome-write-later-goal chronology
  and makes reachout-off, truthful sham, no-write/sham-write, old necessity,
  new-binding redirection, adapter-off, wrong-life/root, derangement, the
  certified active-text comparator, absolute `Y11`, and both old/write
  necessity contrasts claim-gating on the complete denominator; and
- additive interaction is diagnostic, development thresholds are no-claim,
  positive-root analyses are selected-subpopulation diagnostics, and each
  separate effect needs its own root-level uncertainty and power receipt.

This is a fixed-child effect over independently isolated environment roots.
Rows, calls, paths, goals, and potential-outcome contrasts within a root do not
become independent replications. V3 therefore passes the M denominator and
pseudoreplication attack as a map, and it honestly says M still needs design
repair before DEV.

The unsupported part is lines 217--220. Within the supplied source boundary,
the E6 design establishes only that `N=96` and `47/96` belong to a fixed-child
E2--E5 relay claim and cannot transfer to E6. The supplied v2 audit supports
the general rule that one frozen Bernoulli joint-root count cannot power
individual E2/E4/E5 effects. No supplied direct source establishes all of
v3's stronger exact assertions: the `X>=47` notation, that the number is
reserved “at most once,” the exact frozen joint estimand, iid-root condition,
or the adverse-technical-failure convention. V3 lists an active-goal evidence
matrix hash, but that matrix was not among this audit's supplied source bytes;
a hash citation is not an independent reading of the derivation.

This is a provenance defect, not evidence that the rule is scientifically
wrong. A successor must either bind and subject the exact derivation/receipt
to the review boundary, or demote the sentence to the supported warning that
the relay number cannot be transferred or used for individual effects.

## 4. E6 is incorporated without a claim--test mismatch

## 6. Cross-module quarantine and directional-map status pass

V3 permits only shared frozen source code and forbids shared scientific
identities, child/parent state, ledgers, rows, outcomes, adapters, stores,
caches, splits, streams, panels, nuisance estimates, sample sizes, power
receipts, and data-dependent selection. The explicit E6-versus-M quarantine
matches the E6 source. The same general prohibition also prevents C, E0, and
E1 artifacts or roots from silently supplying M/L replication.

V3 honestly functions as a directional map rather than a protocol. It calls
E0 a blocker with an open confirmation design, E1 pending, M in need of design
repair before DEV, E6 execution `NO-GO`, and E3 text-first optional with
physical compression closed. It leaves effect-specific power unresolved,
labels resource ordering heuristic rather than optimized, says no central
positive claim exists, and closes by denying complete-protocol and execution-
gate status. Those limitations are substantive, not boilerplate.

## 7. Authority wording still needs exact repair

V3's opening and closing no-authority language is strong. Nevertheless, lines
222--230 and 264--272 call the E6 and E3 advisories “contracts” and “complete
normative” designs whose requirements “apply without ... relaxation.” The E6
source labels itself an unbound scientific design attack and says adoption of
even its target requires a new material deliberation. The E3 source likewise
labels itself unbound and non-executable. Neither source is a ratified
normative contract on the supplied bytes.

The whole-document disclaimer prevents this wording from authorizing work,
but exact source attribution should not rely on readers resolving that local
tension. Replace the headers and first sentences with proposal-level language,
for example “referenced unbound E6 design, incorporated as a candidate without
summary relaxation,” and make the application conditional: a later ratified
successor may adopt the module only by binding the complete source through the
`AGENTS.md` path. Make the parallel E3 change. Do not weaken any scientific
obligation while removing the unsupported normative status.
