## Verdict

**REVISE.** The v0 addendum is not ready for amendment deliberation. Its local
multiplications are arithmetically correct given its new 256-call/root premise,
but that premise doubles the currently proposed v2 terminal-diagnostic call,
output, embedding, return, and dispatch ceilings; gives each counterfactual
probe twice the cached intact probe's call roster; and contradicts the source-
bound independent re-audit. The resource canaries, lease gate, and shadow-fit
failure law also leave material execution authority and failure dispositions
undefined.

## Exact blockers

### B1. The branch/call roster conflicts with the current v2 bytes

Inherited v1 gives every terminal probe eight calls and 1,024 aggregate
generated tokens. V2 Section 10 then gives the **two replacement diagnostics
collectively** 128 calls, 16,384 output tokens, 128 query embeddings, 131,072
returned tokens, and 128 possible dispatches/root. The source-bound objective
re-audit confirms the collective reading: 4,096 calls, 524,288 output tokens,
4,096 embeddings, 4,194,304 returned tokens, and 4,096 dispatches over 32
roots.

V0 instead assigns each added branch `8 programs * 16 calls = 128 calls`, for
256 calls/root and 8,192 calls at `N=32`. This is not the same common-RNG
terminal call roster as the cached intact P1 probe, which has only
`8 programs * 8 calls = 64 calls`. Call slots 9--16 have no cached intact
counterpart. It also makes the still-operative v2 statement that the
diagnostics fit inside the former 160-call/root optional-comparator envelope
false (`256 > 160`).

The amendment must make one exact choice:

- preserve current v2: cached intact P1 uses its existing 64 calls; link
  overlay adds 64; shadow binding adds 64; collective added ceilings/root are
  128 calls, 16,384 output tokens, 128 embeddings, 131,072 returned tokens,
  128 dispatches, one fit, and—if 6,144 is ratified as the input cap—786,432
  fully rendered input tokens. At `N=32`, the corresponding figures are
  4,096; 524,288; 4,096; 4,194,304; 4,096; 32; and 25,165,824; or
- explicitly amend v2's call roster, counter-key manifest, cached-control law,
  scorer, ceilings, former-comparator sentence, and prior audit. A 16-call
  counterfactual cannot be paired to the current cached eight-call intact
  value; an exact valid control construction and its added resources would
  also be required.

The branch table must show cached-versus-added status explicitly. “Receipted
separately” must mean that intact query/rank/pack receipts are immutable cached
receipts, not permission to reconstruct or rerun an intact branch after lock.

### B2. The campaign accounting omits the resource-canary campaign

Sections 1 and 3 account only the 32 post-lock roots, but Section 5 newly
requires eight development canary roots, each exercising both inference
branches and a maximum-token fit. Under v0's own ceilings those canaries add
2,048 calls, 262,144 output tokens, 12,582,912 rendered input tokens, 2,048
query embeddings, 2,097,152 returned tokens, 2,048 possible dispatches, eight
fits, 655,360 labeled tokens, and 5,242,880 attended tokens. None appears in a
campaign total or lease-spend total. With B1's current-v2 roster, the inference
quantities halve, but the eight fits remain.

Add a separate hard canary table and an all-in diagnostic campaign table,
including model, embedding, compiler/native-action, fit, storage, and artifact
verification work. Prospectively freeze whether these are new roots or named
existing development roots and prohibit post-measurement root substitution.

### B3. The purported pre-GPU gate is circular and could imply hidden authority

The “pre-GPU diagnostic lease gate” can be evaluated only after eight roots
have already made model/embedding calls and maximum-token LoRA fits on A40
GPUs. V0's disclaimers correctly authorize none of that now, but its stage
sequence does not define the separate gate that may authorize those canaries.
Plan ratification or implementation fixtures must not silently become model,
CompilerGym, fit, mount, or GPU execution authority.

Freeze two distinct transitions: (1) source binding, CPU/static fixtures,
independent implementation review, and exact human pre-GPU authorization for
the eight excluded canaries only; then (2) immutable canary receipts and a
fresh lease/implementation review that may recommend an exact confirmation-
diagnostic authorization. Neither transition may authorize headline roots or
infer permission from a PASS.

### B4. The empirical-p95 and lease design is gameable as written

The nearest-rank arithmetic is correct—at `n=8`, empirical p95 is the observed
maximum—but the observations are not yet scale-fixed:

- canary root selection has no prospective sampling/key law;
- “maximum-size synthetic active-text store” has no exact bytes, generator,
  live/audit-record cardinalities, record-length/link-density distribution,
  raw-block/index population, or hash;
- “same hard ceilings” does not require calls, output, retrieval, dispatches,
  compiler work, and fit geometry actually to exercise their maxima;
- canaries may run anywhere from one to eight concurrently, while the lease
  formula assumes four eight-root waves;
- “A40-class,” engine/batch/cache policy, storage topology, and the wall-clock
  start boundary are not exact; expensive staging, corpus/store/index
  construction, validation, or durable writes can therefore fall outside the
  measured interval.

Freeze those bytes and conditions before canary identities are opened. Either
run the witnessed confirmation concurrency and saturated deterministic stress
path or cap confirmation concurrency/resources by what was actually
witnessed. Measure end-to-end time from availability of the sealed input
bundle before any branch-specific staging through verified durable artifacts.

The two lease inequalities are also insufficient by themselves. Aggregate
A40-hours plus a duration does not prove eight contemporaneous GPUs are
available for four waves; per-root scratch/RSS does not prove aggregate host,
disk, or I/O capacity. Bind an enforceable nonfragmented GPU reservation,
aggregate scratch/RAM/I/O and durable-storage headroom, scheduler overhead,
and a fresh capacity check immediately before post-lock launch. Do not call
the 1.25 multiplier a tail or availability guarantee.

### B5. The promised exact resource manifest still has unbounded surfaces

V0 hard-bounds logical/rendered tokens and fit tokens, but merely reports
engine dispatches, query batches, compiler invocations, CPU/index work,
bytes read/written, padding, cache behavior, latency, and energy. Clone/overlay
bytes, BM25/dense work, padded/model-processed tokens, persistent artifacts,
and compiler calls have no prospective maxima. This does not yet discharge
v2 Section 10's requirement for an exact resource-manifest addendum, and the
claim that accounting prevents accidental extra search/rendering capacity is
too strong.

Add hard definitions and ceilings (or explicit fail-closed capacity laws) for
every named surface, including padding/batch shape, clone/overlay material,
index operations, compiler invocations, CPU/RSS, scratch and persistent bytes,
and durable receipts. Keep the honest “not whole-resource-neutral” statement:
the cached factual computation is sunk, link adds inference, and binding adds
corpus construction, fit/mount, and inference. No equality between those total
resource histories should be claimed.

### B6. Shadow-fit/mount failure has no single-valued reducer disposition

V0 calls a failed/nonfinite shadow fit or mount canary a committed behavioral
failure handled by “quarantine/restore,” then says v2 already supplies its
exact zero/failure disposition. It does not. V2 Section 7 restores the prior
committed adapter and continues; for a clean-base shadow branch, “prior” could
mean clean base, authentic P1, or no mounted diagnostic adapter. Those choices
produce different `V_shadow_write` and `K_binding`. Treating a missing/failed
shadow value as zero would usually make `K_binding` favorably positive, while
a mount-canary/equality failure elsewhere blocks validity.

Specify, for every pre-request failure, committed malformed request,
fit/nonfinite/quarantine event, mount-canary failure, post-commit artifact
loss, and restore failure: whether probing occurs, exactly which checkpoint is
mounted, the numeric branch value if observed, whether `K=0`, or whether the
diagnostic claim is blocked. Preserve no retry after the committed boundary
and administrative missingness after irrecoverable artifact loss. Also replace
the ambiguous fit-start phrase “candidate-output receipts” with a receipt that
can exist before candidate adapter bytes do.

## Non-blocking dispositions

- The arithmetic within v0 Sections 2--3 is internally correct **conditional
  on** its incorrect 256-call/root premise; there is no multiplication error
  independent of B1.
- The proposal honestly denies whole-resource neutrality and does not claim
  that link and binding have equal total compute. That qualification must
  survive repair.
- No classroom, cohort, peer, shared-store, parent re-entry, or population-
  learning edge is introduced. The cached P1 and read-only isolated
  counterfactual branches remain compatible with the one-parent/one-child
  topology, provided every canary/root/branch store, index, checkpoint,
  environment, and stochastic namespace is explicitly isolated and no
  diagnostic artifact returns to life.

## Required disposition

Repair B1--B6 in exact source bytes, bind the revised hashes, and obtain a
fresh independent resource review before amendment deliberation. Even a later
resource PASS would authorize only the next architecture-decision step; it
would not authorize implementation or any model, tokenizer, embedding,
CompilerGym, adapter, or GPU execution.
