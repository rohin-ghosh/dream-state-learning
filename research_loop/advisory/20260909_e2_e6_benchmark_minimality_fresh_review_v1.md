# E2--E6 benchmark minimality: fresh independent review v1

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: **fresh-context, read-only scientific review**. This review changes no
bound plan, contract, architecture, source, workflow, model, tokenizer,
benchmark, data, adapter, root, external state, or resource authority. It
authorizes no implementation, model/tokenizer call, benchmark or data
generation, adapter operation, GPU/resource spend, scientific claim, promotion,
or release.

## Independent verdict

One PCFL benchmark family can host E2, E4, E5, and E6, but the current relay
cannot. E2--E5 may share one controlled world and artifacts; E6 needs a
longitudinal experiment with a different inferential unit. Treating the present
fixed-child environment roots as independent child lifetimes would be a fatal
pseudoreplication/construct-validity error.

## 1. Can one family cover all four?

Yes at the generator/interface level; no as one scalar endpoint or with the
current root definition.

- A single compositional relay is already endorsed for E2/E4/E5, provided their
  endpoints remain distinct
  (`research_notes/DREAM_LORA_THINK_FULL_EVIDENCE_STACK_20260907.md`,
  "Paper-level interpretations," lines 232--245).
- Connection, traversal, expansion, and compression are explicitly different
  assays whose successes cannot release one another
  (`research_loop/advisory/20260907_objective_coverage_audit_v1.md`, "Exact
  scientific gaps" Section 2, lines 120--135).
- E6 additionally requires at least three prospectively fixed post-plateau
  checkpoints, root-level slopes, matched affordances, and a strong active-text
  comparator
  (`research_notes/DREAM_LORA_THINK_FULL_EVIDENCE_STACK_20260907.md`, Section
  E6, lines 198--211). V4 explicitly excludes lifetime improvement and
  strong-baseline superiority
  (`research_notes/60_one_child_pcfl_relay_v4_information_efficient_candidate.md`,
  "Exact retained scientific claim," lines 71--76).

The unit mismatch is decisive. Relay v3/v4 conditions on one selected, fixed,
parent-deleted child and treats roots as environment trials of its clones
(`research_notes/59_one_child_pcfl_relay_v3_execution_complete_architecture_proposal.md`,
"Verdict and invariant scope," lines 28--38). The standing topology instead has
one parent and one child per life, with independent lives as replications
(`research_loop/advisory/20260907_objective_coverage_audit_v1.md`, "Audited
objective and sources," lines 20--24).

Two valid choices exist:

- **Recommended:** share the PCFL benchmark family but run separate experiments:
  fixed-child environment roots for E2/E4/E5, independent child lineages for
  E6. Do not pool their denominators.
- **Valid but much costlier unified design:** redefine a root as one
  independently generated parent-to-child lineage, with presealed PCFL
  environment episodes and checkpoints nested inside it. Environment episodes
  then are repeated measures; inference is across lineages. This would
  materially replace v4's fixed-child claim, power calculation, and `47/96`
  rule.

A fixed-child longitudinal study could support only "this fixed child improved
across randomized PCFL trajectories," not the standing lineage-level objective.

## 2. Minimal topology preserving all four

The minimum is two experimental layers and four scientific endpoints.

1. **G0 instrument gate.** Prospective chronology, compiler truth, generator
   theorem, leakage closure, reset/capability isolation, and reducer validity.
2. **Phase A -- acquisition.** Child-selected public actions produce supported
   atoms and minimal authentic co-use links.
3. **Phase B -- E2/E4.** Use a write-denied common cut; two same-start fresh
   goals require distinct multi-relation paths.
   - E2 endpoint: atomic retention plus authentic linked-carrier success,
     failure under matched derangement and truthful-null links, and LoRA
     specificity under adapter-off/wrong-life.
   - E4 endpoint: goal-specific exact paths, necessary bridge-cut loss,
     binding-twin redirection, and failure of the frozen target-blind
     controller.
4. **Phase C -- relay acquisition.** Authentic versus truthful-sham old-memory
   signal; pre-outcome gap, two hypotheses, complete outcome map, and
   child-selected separating experiment; `REACHOUT_OFF` acts only at dispatch.
5. **Phase D -- E5.** After the common public outcome, clean-base `EDGE_WRITE`,
   `NO_WRITE`, truthful `SHAM_WRITE`, and binding-swap forks; fresh reset; a
   disjoint delayed goal uniquely requires one old plus the new row.
6. **Longitudinal layer -- E6.** Independent lineage roots with isolated
   full-learner and certified `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` arms, repeated across
   increasing lifetime/load. Require the baseline's own registered local
   plateau, at least three later checkpoints, positive full-learner late slope,
   positive slope advantage, and absolute terminal superiority by a frozen
   margin. The relay-local text carrier is explicitly not `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED`
   (`research_notes/59_one_child_pcfl_relay_v3_execution_complete_architecture_proposal.md`,
   Section 4.2, lines 378--380; Section 9, lines 879--882).

Use a fixed sequence E2 -> E4 -> E5 -> E6, with one preregistered root-level
endpoint per rung. E5 may remain a same-root conjunction containing earlier
mechanism bits, but E2 and E4 still need their own population endpoints if
separate claims are desired. V4's sole `COMPLETE_chain` endpoint is valid for
one joint E2--E5 relay claim, not for separately releasing all three. Adding
separate endpoints requires a new prospective power receipt; `N=96` cannot
simply be inherited.

## 3. Essential versus overengineered requirements

### Essential

- Own-public-action provenance, prospective admission, minimal
  non-answer-bearing carrier rows, and hidden-truth capability separation
  (`research_notes/58_one_child_pcfl_relay_v2_executable_contract.md`, Sections
  2--3, lines 66--177).
- Common-cut forks, write denial in B, prospective C declaration, clean D
  rebuild/reset, and adverse fixed-denominator failures.
- Derangement plus truthful-null for connection; paired goals, target-blind
  ceiling, bridge cut, and twin redirection for traversal; authentic/sham
  signal, reachout-off, no-write, sham-write, and binding swap for expansion
  (`research_notes/59_one_child_pcfl_relay_v3_execution_complete_architecture_proposal.md`,
  Section 9, lines 846--880).
- Same-semantics text solvability, adapter-off, wrong-life, exact reset, and no
  side-channel route.
- For E6, independent lineage units, comparator-specific plateau,
  post-plateau slopes, and absolute terminal comparison.

### Overengineered or replaceable realization

- Seventy-five separately powered population decisions, the 32-root dependence
  pilot, 1.6 million copula simulations, and the `[96..512]` adaptive grid. They
  answer a much stronger 75-claim assurance problem, not the relay claim
  (`research_loop/advisory/20260907_one_child_pcfl_relay_v3_information_per_gpu_hour_attack_v1.md`,
  Sections 2.1--2.2, lines 80--111;
  `research_loop/advisory/20260907_one_child_pcfl_relay_v3_power_simplification_audit_v1.md`,
  "Verdict," lines 37--47).
- Eight nominal LoRA rebuilds when only five byte-distinct transactions exist;
  exact immutable artifacts plus fresh process clones preserve the
  interventions
  (`research_notes/60_one_child_pcfl_relay_v4_information_efficient_candidate.md`,
  "Five distinct LoRA transactions," lines 157--176).
- Running LoRA on TEXT-failed roots for a dual-chain primary, and rerunning
  byte-identical carrier-independent negative calls
  (`research_loop/advisory/20260907_one_child_pcfl_relay_v3_information_per_gpu_hour_attack_v1.md`,
  Sections 2.3--2.5, lines 113--167).
- Separately testing every leakage arm. Keep every relevant intervention, but
  reduce them prospectively into one `CTRL_OK` root predicate and report
  constituents diagnostically.
- Exact finite closure is essential; the literal `2^16` feature-mask
  realization and exhaustive administrative receipt surface are not
  scientifically sacred. They may be replaced only by a smaller interface or
  proof that closes the same alternative routes. Until such a replacement is
  proven, moving them to CPU gates is safer than deleting them.

## 4. CPU gates before GPU

Before any model or training spend:

- Complete the `AGENTS.md` deliberation, ratification, independent-review, and
  separate GPU-authorization path (`AGENTS.md`, lines 3--18 and 32--36).
- Prove root/twin uniqueness, distinct B paths, no one-row/direct-action
  shortcut, exact C equipoise/separating action, D unique old+new dependence,
  and truthful/matched derangement/null/sham/twin fixtures.
- Round-trip canonical bytes; prove total `G_atom/G_link/G_new`, chronology,
  collision/conflict behavior, and capability separation.
- Exhaust the finite target-blind reader/controller class and all material
  metadata, cache, action-string, identifier, and reacquisition routes.
- Verify reset laws, RNG addressing, opportunity tapes, adverse-fill reducers,
  fixed endpoint order, and five-build alias equivalence.
- Freeze the E2/E4/E5 endpoint hierarchy and the lineage-level E6
  plateau/slope/terminal reducers; independently reproduce power before
  generating confirmation roots.
- For E3, print the model-free full-state crossover receipt. Current rank-8
  serialization is about 80.79 MB and needs expanded semantics above 161.58 MB

  (`research_notes/56_pcfl_compression_feasibility_adjudication_v1.md`, "Exact
  present adapter byte floor," lines 67--101; "Do not spend GPU on a
  mathematically foreclosed positive cell," lines 114--134).

## 5. Smallest information-efficient DEV experiment

For the fixed-child E2--E5 mechanism assay, the smallest defensible currently
specified DEV panel is v4's eight disjoint roots:

1. pass G0;
2. run full TEXT on all eight;
3. require at least 6/8 TEXT chains;
4. run LoRA only on presealed TEXT-success roots, using five exact builds and
   within-root short-circuiting;
5. require at least 4/8 complete dual chains; and
6. expose component survival/failure and resource vectors only diagnostically,
   with no tuning or pooling.

This is exactly the v4 gate
(`research_notes/60_one_child_pcfl_relay_v4_information_efficient_candidate.md`,
Section G1, lines 113--128). It maximizes mechanism coverage per GPU-hour
because each successful root exercises the complete causal chain and all
controls. It is not statistically "powered": under the inherited `.75/.55`
planning rates its combined pass probability is only `0.59049`, so it is an
aggressive resource screen, not an optimality theorem
(`research_loop/advisory/20260907_one_child_pcfl_relay_v4_revised_candidate_reaudit_v1.md`,
Section 2, lines 71--95).

That DEV does not validate E6. There is no source-justified E6 DEV sample size.
One complete lineage can be a plumbing/resource canary, but cannot support a
stochastic go decision; any E6 feasibility panel must use separate disjoint
lineage roots and a prospectively justified size.

## 6. Compression E3

E3 must remain a separate rate--distortion experiment and claim. It may reuse
the PCFL generator, accepted semantics, and load axis, but it needs
denotationally exact expanded equivalents, total acting-state accounting, a
capacity/load sweep, and at least three points beyond crossover
(`research_notes/DREAM_LORA_THINK_FULL_EVIDENCE_STACK_20260907.md`, Section E3,
lines 105--142). The current all-layer rank-8 carrier makes the proposed scale
physically implausible
(`research_notes/56_pcfl_compression_feasibility_adjudication_v1.md`, "Verdict,"
lines 9--30).

The feasible near-term route is text-interface semantic-code compression plus a
separate LoRA transport assay
(`research_notes/56_pcfl_compression_feasibility_adjudication_v1.md`, Route A,
lines 138--152). Calling the relay, a fixed-rank adapter, or a retained-ledger
system "compressed" without that panel is a fatal validity error.

## Fatal validity issues

1. **Fatal for E6:** counting fixed-child environment clones as independent
   child lineages, or using v4's one-shot relay as lifetime/saturation evidence.
2. **Fatal for saturation:** using the relay-local text carrier as the strong
   `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` baseline.
3. **Fatal for separate E2/E4/E5 claims:** treating v4's one joint `47/96`
   result as if it separately powered each rung.
4. **Fatal for E3:** claiming compression without a feasible full-byte
   crossover and separate rate--distortion panel.

## Disposition

Use one PCFL family only as shared benchmark infrastructure. Preserve separate
mechanism and lineage experiments, separate inferential units, four rung-level
endpoints, and a separately gated E3 panel. Any unified lineage-root rewrite,
new endpoint hierarchy, control reduction, or DEV/E6 design is material and
must traverse `AGENTS.md`; this review supplies no authority to adopt or run it.
