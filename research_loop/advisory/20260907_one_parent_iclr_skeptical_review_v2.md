# Skeptical ICLR review v2: minimum viable submission package

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: concise read-only advisory. It authorizes no frozen-source change,
implementation, task generation, model/tokenizer call, adapter operation,
CompilerGym execution, external access, or GPU use.

## Decision

The closest-work audit is right that the broad parametric-memory lane is
occupied. The teachability attack is right that the inherited same-family
canary does not test the parenting-to-later-learning bridge. Trying to answer
both audits by adding TMEM, native-LoRA, batch, Spark, a full compositional
carrier study, and the 32-root headline before September 18 is not a credible
plan.

The viable fork is a **causal developmental paper**, not a new memory-method
paper:

> For one fixed parent policy and child checkpoint, does target-blind process
> teaching change the later causal benefit of the child's own deployment-time
> writes after the parent and all childhood-only state are removed?

With that thesis, a clean positive package can move the paper from reject to a
credible borderline/weak-accept ICLR submission. The cost is giving up claims
of first per-life parametric learning, writer superiority, strong-memory
superiority in general, connected/compressed knowledge, and broad
meta-intelligence.

## Minimum viable evidence package

### Non-negotiable before the confirmation run

1. **Native writer floor.** Exact response-only targets/EOS, strict free typed
   dispatch, typed-forced proposal preservation, generic non-erasure,
   reproducible clean-base fitting, transactional mount/rollback, and parent
   deletion must all pass.

2. **A four-root cross-family selective bridge canary.** Use the teachability
   attack's smallest topology: teach one non-compiler family, withhold the
   other, then test an uncued parent-absent process on the withheld family.
   Required components are:

   - applicable versus counter-indicated tasks, so “always investigate” fails;

   - the joint public-world event: prediction, at least two live hypotheses, a
     high-information legal action, evidence update, and non-inferior utility;

   - authentic P versus dose-matched U and authentic adapter-off/pre-write;

   - fresh-process deletion; and

   - two parent-free wake/write cuts with `D_bridge`, `W_P`, and `L_P` as
     conjunctive spending guards.

   This remains a go/no-go canary, not inferential paper evidence. A failure
   should stop the 32-root spend. Near-transfer, recitation, cue-lift, and raw
   thought-marker scores are diagnostic only.

3. **An executable active-text validity gate.** The common
   `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` mechanism must pass its semantic, retrieval/use, and
   task-headroom certificates, including raw/full-history and static-retrieval
   checks. If it fails, the submission loses all “validated” or “strong memory”
   language.

### Non-negotiable confirmatory result

4. **The full `U0/U1/P0/P1` factorial over independent roots.** This is the
   novelty. `P1-R0` cannot replace it. Every arm needs the same deployment
   surface, isolated active-text policy, task opportunities, generated-token
   budget, common-random coupling, and failure accounting.

5. **Joint positive evidence, not `D` alone.** “Parenting improved later
   learning” requires all of:

```text
D > 0 by the registered CI and SESOI rule
W_P > 0 by its registered rule
L_terminal > 0 by its registered rule
all routing, provenance, leakage, and transactional gates pass
```

`D` can pass when writes harm both branches but harm U more. Such a result is a
relative write-harm moderation result, not successful learning.

6. **Writer-path mediation.** Report eligible/accepted scientific rows,
   rehearsal fills, target-type mix, supported evidence, labeled tokens,
   fit/mount failures, and later behavior by arm and cut. Because the parent
   lessons overlap the deployment admission grammar, this accounting is needed
   to show whether the effect is primarily write-filter compatibility.

7. **One independently reproduced result manifest.** The two main figures,
   fixed-sequence table, failure table, and resource table must derive from one
   immutable artifact set and survive a fresh audit. Four canary roots never
   enter confirmation.

## Baseline ruling

| Baseline/control | Deadline ruling | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| `U0/U1/P0/P1` | **Non-negotiable** | Identifies inherited competence, ordinary write benefit, parented write benefit, and the parenting-by-write interaction. |
| `P0` with the same parented entry checkpoint and common active-text policy | **Non-negotiable; lead memory comparison** | `P1-P0` is the total closed-loop benefit of enabling LoRA on top of this validated textual-memory mechanism. It is not equal-memory carrier identification. |
| `R0` | **Secondary if the source-bound five-service run proceeds; not the causal headline** | Full developmental package versus raw actor under deployment-only parity. No equal-history, equal-total-compute, or parenting claim. |
| Raw/full-history and static retrieval | **Non-negotiable in the active-text certificate, not necessarily 32-root arms** | Establishes that the homegrown memory is not a dummy comparator. |
| TMEM-style direct-QA LoRA | **Defer unless claiming writer/parametric-method advance** | Without it, cite TMEM prominently and claim no advance over the closest parametric method. |
| Naive/native continual LoRA | **Defer unless claiming the compiler is better than ordinary continual SFT** | Without it, the writer is an experimental substrate, not a contribution proven superior. |
| Matched terminal/batch/LEAFE-style write | **Defer unless claiming periodic Sleep or schedule superiority** | No claim that recurrent writes beat batch internalization. |
| Spark/ACE/ReasoningBank released implementation | **Appendix or defer under the causal-paper framing** | `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` may be called validated under its contract, not state of the art and not representative of all memory systems. |
| EVAF- or PEAM-style arms | **Defer** | Required only for selective-write, procedural-skill, memory-depth, or forgetting superiority claims, all of which should be removed. |

This ruling disagrees with treating all closest-work baselines as mandatory for
the minimum causal paper. They are mandatory only if the manuscript continues
to present the LoRA writer or active-text system as an algorithmic advance. The
deadline-responsible choice is to abandon that claim, not to run shallow
approximations of five neighboring systems.

## Compositional-relay ruling

### Non-negotiable slice

The cross-family bridge canary above is the minimum construct test. Its
withheld-family, uncued, applicable/counter-indicated, information-gaining
action, later-write interaction, and absolute-learning guards distinguish a
taught selective disposition from recitation, same-family skill, reflexive
exploration, and control harm. It stays one parent/one child and adds no
classroom or population mechanism.

### Appendix only if the headline is already safe

- adapter-off or fixed-store terminal replay that localizes a gain to the
  parametric carrier;

- authentic-versus-deranged action/outcome binding;

- explicit-text one-hop link contribution; and

- descriptive near-transfer, cue dependence, durability, and failure funnels.

These can clarify a positive headline but cannot rescue a failed `D`, `W_P`, or
`L_terminal`.

### Defer to a later paper

- fresh two-or-more-edge composition with necessary-bridge cuts;

- counterfactual goals selecting different graph paths;

- the full memory-to-query-to-new-outcome-to-write-to-later-goal expansion
  relay as an inferential mechanism result;

- compact versus denotationally equivalent expanded carriers and any
  rate-distortion/compression claim;

- claims of a connected graph inside LoRA weights; and

- CL-Bench, a second deployment domain, new parent policies, or new child
  checkpoints.

These are scientifically valuable but not minimum viable. If deferred, the
ICLR manuscript must use connected/compressed experience and goal-directed
expansion only as motivation or future work. More CompilerGym roots do not
substitute for them.

## Manuscript package required by September 18

- Retitle and rewrite the opening around the parenting-by-write causal
  question. “Experience Models” may remain program framing, not the novelty.

- Cite and distinguish at least TMEM, PEAM, EVAF, Macaron-V1, LEAFE, Early
  Experience, Learning on the Job, ACE, ReasoningBank, ReMem/Evo-Memory,
  MemoPilot, FLEX, continual-learning-to-memory work, and CL-Bench. Label every
  comparison as exact, functional, or literature-only.

- Lead results with `D` plus the `W_P/L_terminal` guards. Lead the memory
  comparison with `P1-P0`; present `P1-R0` only with its unequal-history and
  unequal-total-resource qualifications.

- Remove claims about first online LoRA learning, writer superiority, DREAM as
  a mechanism, backward retention/forgetting, parametric carrier,
  connected/compressed knowledge, goal-directed expansion, named-method
  superiority, and general parenting/meta-intelligence.

- If clean joint positive evidence and the immutable audit do not exist by
  September 16, submit a genuinely narrow negative/mechanism paper only if the
  prospective evidence is independently valuable; otherwise defer the venue.

## Bottom line

The minimum credible ICLR story is not “we built the best Experience Model.” It
is “a target-blind developmental intervention causally increased successful
later write-enabled learning in one fixed child system.” That story needs the
cross-family selective bridge as a kill canary, the full factorial, joint
`D/W_P/L_terminal` success, a validated common active-text control, mediation
and resource accounting, complete closest-work positioning, and an immutable
audit. TMEM/native/batch/named-memory arms and the full compositional,
expansion, and compression program are deferred together with every claim they
would have been needed to support.
