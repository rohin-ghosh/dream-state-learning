# One-child PCFL relay v3: information-per-GPU-hour attack and staged repair v1

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: **advisory proposal only**. This document attacks, but does not edit or
supersede,
`research_notes/59_one_child_pcfl_relay_v3_execution_complete_architecture_proposal.md`
or its incorporated B9--B10 statistics lock. It authorizes no deliberation,
ratification, implementation, child/root/target generation, model/tokenizer
call, adapter build, benchmark operation, external access, GPU use, execution,
paper claim, or scientific promotion. Any adoption is a material statistical
and execution change and must traverse `AGENTS.md` from fresh bound bytes.

## Verdict

**REVISE for scientific information per GPU-hour.** The v3 topology is unusually
strong: its typed ledger, public admission compilers, finite reader closure,
matched signal/sham intervention, truthful new-row writer, fresh-process D
reset, cuts, twins, and named controls make the desired relay mechanically
testable. The resource plan is not comparably efficient. It powers the same
scientific object twice:

1. as 75 separately thresholded population decisions; and
2. as `COMPLETE_chain`, a strict same-root conjunction which already contains
   every required predicate for both carriers.

The first representation forces at least 320 confirmation roots at the locked
planning alternatives before the rank-copula rule is even consulted. The
second representation needs 96 roots for the exact retained claim below. The
extra decisions buy stronger standalone component-rate language, not the
end-to-end relay claim. They should not be co-primary.

This repair does **not** remove connected experience, goal-conditioned
traversal, gap detection, experiment selection, truthful writing, delayed
two-row use, either carrier, any necessary cut/twin, or any named negative
control. It changes which single population statement those operations prove.

## 1. Exact retained claim

The only confirmatory scientific claim is:

> Conditional on the one fixed parent-deleted child and registered iid PCFL
> root distribution, more than 40% of roots complete the same prospectively
> registered, intervention-tested relay under **both** an explicit TEXT
> carrier and a parametric LoRA carrier: the child acquires publicly supported
> atomic and co-use relations from its own actions; retains and reads them;
> uses connected relations to follow different exact paths for fresh goals;
> changes those paths/actions under the required derangement, null, bridge-cut,
> and binding-twin interventions while failing the target-blind and named
> leakage controls; detects a supported gap; declares two live hypotheses and
> a complete pre-outcome map; chooses the target-relevant separating public
> experiment rather than the matched sham; admits one truthful new row from
> its observed outcome; and, after a fresh reset, uses that new row together
> with one named old row for a delayed action that disappears or redirects
> under no-write, sham-write, binding-swap, reachout-off, adapter-off,
> wrong-life, and the complete registered closure controls.

This is a **complete observed/intervention-tested relay chain**, not a causal
mediation estimate. It is conditional on one child. It does not claim
compression, a readable graph inside weights, parenting causality, LoRA
superiority to text, superiority to `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED`, another child,
classroom/population learning, general creativity, or universal continual
improvement.

The v3 per-root definition is retained literally:

```text
R_r = COMPLETE_chain_r = TEXT_chain_r * LORA_chain_r
```

`TEXT_chain_r` and `LORA_chain_r` retain the complete B/C/D, acquisition,
control, mismatch, provenance, chronology, and truth-audit products in note 59
Section 10. A root can equal one only after **every** required positive,
cut/twin response, and negative-control predicate for both carriers has
actually executed and passed. No aggregate component result can manufacture a
success.

## 2. Where v3 spends without adding support for that claim

### 2.1 The 75 decisions are redundant co-primary claims

The locked family contains one oracle decision, two retention decisions,
twenty other positive/redirection decisions, twenty-six leakage decisions,
twenty-two paired-component decisions, one same-semantics mismatch decision,
and three chain decisions. Requiring population floors/ceilings and `.20`
SESOIs for every component is sufficient for a long list of standalone
mechanism-rate claims. It is not necessary to establish a positive rate of
roots that completed the entire chain: that event is observed directly by
`R_r`.

At the lock's planning alternatives, exact binomial/multinomial enumeration
over those category counts gives:

```text
N=256: sum_j(1-power_j) ~= 0.209437  > .20  -> analytic gate fails
N=320: sum_j(1-power_j) ~= 0.058225 <= .20  -> first grid point that passes
```

Thus the current analytic rule cannot select 96, 128, 160, 192, or 256 roots,
irrespective of favorable pilot dependence; the mandatory copula may increase
the final N further. This is a power cost for **75-claim conjunctive release**,
not for the retained one-claim relay.

### 2.2 The nuisance pilot and copula solve a deleted problem

The 32-root pilot, rank firewall, Spearman matrix, and 1.6 million
rank-copula replicates estimate dependence among 75 decisions. A single binary
primary endpoint has no cross-endpoint covariance to estimate. Fixed planning
values determine its exact binomial power. Removing this machinery saves 32
full exploratory roots, substantial engineering surface, and CPU time without
weakening the retained claim.

### 2.3 Running LoRA after a TEXT-chain failure is logically unnecessary

For any root with `TEXT_chain_r=0`, `R_r=0` under every possible LoRA result.
Running that root's LoRA carrier cannot change the primary datum. A
prospectively registered monotone short-circuit may therefore leave the LoRA
potential outcome unobserved and record the already-known primary value zero.
This supports no marginal LoRA-rate claim, but creates no missing primary
outcome and performs no favorable-root conditioning: all 96 roots remain in
the denominator.

The same logic holds inside a root. Once one required chain predicate fails,
later predicates cannot turn the product back to one. A fixed, hash-bound
execution order may stop that root, record `R_r=0`, and make no component claim
from unexecuted cells. Every `R_r=1` still requires literal completion of all
cells.

### 2.4 Eight named builds contain only five distinct LoRA transactions

The current state graph names eight clean-base builds per LoRA root, but three
pairs/groups have byte-identical training inputs and seeds:

```text
AUTH_OLD_PAD := BUILD(DECK_A_AUTH, PAD_D)
  supplies fresh immutable clones for B_AUTH, C_AUTH, C_SHAM,
  C_REACH_PREFIX, D_NOWRITE, and D_REACHOFF

EDGE := BUILD(DECK_A_AUTH, G_new_row)
  supplies fresh immutable clones for D_EDGE and D_SWAP; the latter differs
  only by its registered read-time binding permutation

DERANGED := BUILD(S_A + DERANGE(L_A), PAD_D)
NULL      := BUILD(S_A + LINK_NULL_PROJECT(L_NULL_RESERVE), PAD_D)
SHAM      := BUILD(DECK_A_AUTH, D_SHAM_ROW)
```

Because deterministic kernels and exact receipts are already mandatory,
retraining byte-identical `AUTH_OLD_PAD` or `EDGE` adapters measures training
repetition, not a carrier intervention. One transactional build followed by
fresh read-only process clones is the cleaner potential-outcome construction.
This reduces the maximum from eight to five build transactions per attempted
LoRA root while preserving clean-base construction, branch isolation, and all
declared interventions. Adoption requires a new deliberation because note 59
currently names the builds separately.

### 2.5 Identical negative potential outcomes should be hash-aliased

The general no-carrier controls (`GOAL_ONLY`, `STATE_ONLY`,
`IDENTIFIER_ONLY`, `CANDIDATE_ONLY`, `PASSIVE_SIGNATURE`,
`SOURCE_ACTION_STRING`, and `UNAIDED_GENERATIVE_READ`) should execute once per
byte-identical task/RNG cell and be referenced by both carrier receipts when
their actor-visible bytes are identical. This is already compatible with the
v3 raw-cell alias principle. `ADAPTER_OFF`, `WRONG_LIFE`, semantic TEXT reads,
and every carrier-dependent positive remain separate. Aliasing is permitted
only by exact input/intervention hash, never by a semantic judgment after an
outcome.

## 3. Replacement confirmatory design

### 3.1 One endpoint, exact fixed N

Use `N=96` sealed iid confirmation roots. For

```text
H0: P(R_r=1) <= .40
H1 planning point: P(R_r=1) = .55
```

reject with the one-sided exact Clopper--Pearson lower bound at alpha `.05`
only when it is strictly greater than `.40`. At `N=96`, the critical count is
`x_R >= 47`; the exact null upper-tail probability at 47 is
`0.046706479040617`, and exact power at `.55` is
`0.9016376315926115`.

There is one primary decision, so no multiplicity correction, dependence
pilot, or copula is needed. Every root is intention-to-treat and
failure-inclusive. Missing/malformed/post-exposure failures are `R_r=0`.
Repository/hash/capability violations invalidate the study as in v3.

All raw primitives, continuous values, carrier resources, and block outcomes
may be printed descriptively. They are diagnostics, not individually
confirmed population effects. If standalone component floors, `.20` paired
SESOIs, marginal TEXT/LORA rates, or carrier comparisons are scientifically
required, they are a separate predeclared secondary family and do not gate or
power the relay claim.

### 3.2 Presealed execution sequence

Root identities and their execution order seal at `A0`, before outcomes.
Within every root, operation order also seals before execution and is chosen
only from static cost and dependency information: CPU before inference,
shared-carrier positives before new builds, and cheap read substitutions before
additional training. No observed confirmation result may reorder another
root or change an intervention.

The confirmation stages are:

```text
S0  Run every deterministic generator, theorem, compiler, truth-audit,
    closure, opportunity, deck, and finite-controller operation on CPU.
    Seal all 96 primary values provisionally at zero where a prerequisite
    fails; never replace or regenerate a root.

S1  Execute the full TEXT chain on all remaining roots. TEXT is cheap and is
    required for R. If fewer than 47 roots have TEXT_chain=1, terminate:
    x_R can never reach 47, so the primary result fails without any LoRA work.

S2  Visit the TEXT-success roots in the presealed A0 order. Execute LoRA with
    the fixed within-root short-circuit and five-build transaction graph.
    A completed LoRA chain increments x_R. Stop successfully when x_R=47;
    all unexecuted roots are conservatively R=0. Stop for futility when
    x_R + number_of_unattempted_TEXT_success_roots < 47.
```

Neither stop is an interim significance test. Both apply the sole final
critical count to the fixed denominator of 96. Successes cannot be revoked;
unexecuted roots are assigned zero; and no marginal LoRA estimate is released.
This is deterministic short-circuit evaluation of a conjunction, not adaptive
cherry-picking or confirmation-root extension.

Within an attempted LoRA root, a candidate static order is:

1. build and publish `AUTH_OLD_PAD`; run exact row-read/atomic-retention
   canaries and authentic positive B/C paths using fresh processes;
2. run all authentic-carrier read substitutions, bridge cut/twin, sham signal,
   reachout-off, adapter-off, and byte-identical aliased controls;
3. build/run `DERANGED` and `NULL`;
4. only after truthful `G_new` admission, build/run `EDGE` and its binding-swap
   clone;
5. build/run `SHAM`, wrong-life, remaining D controls, and the exact delayed
   two-row goal; and
6. set `R_r=1` only after every retained chain bit and receipt is one.

The final exact ordering needs a dependency audit: for example, a later C or D
arm may not be moved before the public event that defines it. Cost may order
only simultaneously legal cells. Once frozen, a failure short-circuits to
zero; it never triggers a different test.

## 4. CPU/GPU separation

The following are CPU/static and must finish before a root consumes model or
training time:

- root/twin generation and the complete B/D theorem;
- all `2^16` closure masks, semantic-subset expansions, target-blind adaptive
  history DAGs, and leaked-oracle calculations;
- canonicalization, strict ledger parsing, `G_atom_v3`, `G_link_v3`, sham/null
  matching, opportunity/deck construction, row IDs, token-length-class
  feasibility, and every potential-outcome input hash;
- controller enumeration/selection on disjoint DEV roots; and
- all manifests, capability graphs, RNG tapes, reset expectations, and static
  resource upper bounds.

TEXT carrier construction and exact reads are CPU operations. Child actor
generation may still use a GPU, but can be batched by stage across roots. LoRA
training and LoRA reader inference are the expensive GPU path. The runtime
should keep one immutable `A_CORE` resident per worker, initialize a fresh
adapter/optimizer transaction for each distinct build, publish it, and mount
fresh read-only clones without repeatedly loading base weights. This is an
engineering optimization only: transaction hashes, seeds, process resets, and
actor-visible bytes remain those of the frozen protocol.

Nothing may pretrain a carrier across roots, share a learned adapter between
assayed roots, or turn a root bank into a learner/population. `WRONG_LIFE`
remains a frozen negative carrier from a disjoint root of the same child.

## 5. Expected compute change

Under the current 75-decision analytic lock, confirmation starts no lower than
320 roots, and the mandatory nuisance pilot adds 32 full roots. With eight
named LoRA builds per full root, the upper design count is 2,816 build
transactions before any copula-driven increase in N.

Under this repair:

- confirmation roots: `96` instead of at least `320` (**70% fewer**);
- pilot roots: `0` instead of `32`;
- maximum attempted LoRA roots: `96` instead of `352` including pilot
  (**72.7% fewer**);
- at the registered TEXT planning rate `.75`, expected LoRA roots before the
  primary early-success rule are `96*.75=72` (**79.5% fewer** than 352);
- unique builds per attempted LoRA root: `5` instead of `8`
  (**37.5% fewer**); and
- expected build transactions before early success at the planning joint
  distribution are at most `72*5=360`, versus `352*8=2,816`
  (**87.2% fewer**). Because `P(LORA_chain=1 | TEXT_chain=1)=.55/.75`, reaching
  47 complete successes takes about `47/(.55/.75)=64.1` attempted LoRA roots
  on average before truncation, approximately 321 builds (**88.6% fewer**).

These are design expectations, not runtime promises. Static aliasing and
within-root short-circuit can only lower model/build work; their realized
savings must be reported with the number and reason for every skipped cell.
CPU theorem/closure costs remain and should not be mislabeled as GPU savings.

## 6. Prospective kill gates

The following gates are fixed before any confirmatory root exists:

1. **Chronology/governance:** retain the v3 `P0<R0<PU`, selected-child,
   parent/nursery deletion, manifest, signature, and capability receipts. A
   chronology failure makes the relay exploratory forever.
2. **Static closure:** on a predeclared large CPU fuzz panel, require zero
   schema/compiler/theorem/closure/ID/capability counterexamples. Any systemic
   counterexample is `NO_GO`; confirmation roots are not used for debugging.
3. **DEV feasibility:** retain eight disjoint DEV roots for target-blind policy
   selection and execution/resource canaries. Require every leaked-oracle,
   byte-equivalence, reset, carrier transaction, and parser canary to pass;
   zero hidden-access violations; at least `6/8` full TEXT chains; and at least
   `4/8` complete TEXT+LoRA chains. Failure means stop and deliberate a new
   protocol, not tune on the same roots. Any material repair uses fresh DEV IDs.
4. **Resource:** before `A0`, ratify a ceiling that can finish all 96 TEXT roots
   and the five-build LoRA path for up to 96 roots. Expected gating savings do
   not authorize a ceiling that cannot survive the maximum.
5. **TEXT mathematical futility:** after all 96 TEXT roots seal, stop if
   `x_TEXT<47`; otherwise open only the presealed TEXT-success LoRA cells.
6. **LoRA root short-circuit:** after the first failed required bit, set that
   root's primary value to zero and execute no later cell. No component claim
   is permitted for a partially observed root.
7. **Primary success/futility:** stop when `x_R=47`, coding every unexecuted
   root zero, or when even all remaining TEXT-success roots cannot reach 47.
   There is no extension past 96, favorable-root replacement, threshold
   change, second primary, or post-hoc subgroup.

The proposed `6/8` and `4/8` DEV thresholds are resource-protection rules, not
inferential evidence. They may themselves be revised only before fresh DEV
roots and through the required design gate.

## 7. What is retained, demoted, or optional

### Retained and mandatory for every successful root

- one fixed parent-deleted child; isolated iid roots; no classroom, cohort,
  population learner, cross-root updates, or communication;
- exact public ledger, atom/link compilers, public-evidence admission, one-bit
  offline truth audit, minimal carrier rows, and provenance/chronology;
- both TEXT and all-layer rank-8 LoRA carriers with exact typed reads;
- connected experience and distinct goal-conditioned paths;
- target-blind adaptive closure, derangement, truthful null, necessary bridge
  cut, and binding twin;
- authentic versus sham signal, supported gap, two hypotheses, complete
  pre-outcome map, target-relevant experiment selection, and public outcome;
- truthful new-row compilation/write, fresh D reset, delayed old+new use;
- no-write, sham-write, new-binding twin, reachout-off, adapter-off,
  wrong-life, same-semantics, proper-subset/passive-channel closure, and all
  seven general named controls; and
- exact resource reporting and adverse failure semantics.

### Demoted to descriptive diagnostics

- standalone population floors for acquisition or individual positives;
- standalone ceilings for each named leakage arm;
- the twenty-two `.20` paired-component SESOI claims;
- separate `TEXT_chain>.60` and `LORA_chain>.50` claims; and
- component-specific power guarantees.

Demotion does not remove a cell from the chain. It removes only redundant
population-level release thresholds.

### Optional follow-up, never a gate for the retained claim

- a separately powered component/localization family after the primary relay;
- marginal TEXT or LoRA carrier-rate claims and any carrier comparison;
- the 32-root dependence pilot and rank-copula study as a methods appendix;
- full LoRA execution on roots whose TEXT chain already failed;
- compression/rate-distortion assays; and
- comparison to upstream `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` or a broader parenting/lifetime
  headline.

## 8. Scientific trade-off

The repair exchanges breadth of confirmatory language for strength per unit
compute. If `47/96` roots literally complete both full chains, the paper has a
direct, exact, non-asymptotic result on the phenomenon it cares about. It may
not separately say that every mediator occurs in more than 75% of roots, every
control leaks in fewer than 10%, or every paired component exceeds `.20` in
the population. Those are stronger and much more expensive claims. They are
not prerequisites for saying that a nontrivial population fraction completed
the registered whole mechanism.

If the primary fails, the component table remains diagnostic/exploratory and
cannot be mined for a replacement confirmatory story. This is the price that
makes the staged short-circuit statistically honest.
