# Paper-1 canary v1a: minimum scientific repair

**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** pre-proposal advisory only. This does not alter the frozen
goalposts, repair or ratify the current change package, authorize code edits,
or authorize model/GPU/LoRA work.

## Disposition

Retain the one aligned seed-5 collision pair and the text-first, conditional
two-LoRA shape, but replace the current adaptive `M0`-or-`M1` transport rule.
The only prospectively eligible transport object in v1a should be each life's
**own-return M1 corpus**, sealed before final-goal reveal. `M0` and distractor
M1 remain diagnostic text conditions; no score, cited path, or offline truth
may choose among corpora. A text pass bit may decide whether the two already
specified fits run, but may not change their input manifests.

This yields a canary, not a Paper-1 result: it can show that one strongly
scaffolded frozen prompted system carried life-specific connected memory
through text and immediate path-local LoRA access on one held-out pair. It
cannot show reliability, beyond-context value, superiority to retrieval,
durability, saturation, autonomous discovery, learned control, continual
improvement, or a flywheel.

## Minimal v1a contract

1. **Visibility and corpus.** Use a per-kind public allowlist rather than
   `dict(episode.payload)`, and expose THINK0 only to its own source/target
   endpoints. Rebuild every model request statelessly. Compile the disclosed
   witnessed atoms plus all and only active model-`SUPPORTED` rows from
   M1-own, including false self-approved rows; never truth-filter, normalize,
   repair, or choose rows by downstream usefulness. Bind one content-addressed
   semantic/provenance/view/touch manifest, with backend-specific text and SFT
   delivery derived from it.

2. **No answer-equivalent memory.** Remove unrestricted
   `TRANSFERRED_VALUE`. Permit only kind-typed local dreamed predicates:
   `ROLE_EQUIV(animal, anchor)`, `CAUSAL_JOIN(source, target,
   CHANGE|STABLE)`, and `ROLE_APPLICATION(animal, target, anchor)`. The last
   contains no color/ratio/state token and must cite a prior `ROLE_EQUIV` plus
   a witnessed target-baseline row. No dreamed row or view may bind an animal
   and target to a color/ratio/state token or encode a complete proof. Public
   labels remain legal only in the separately disclosed witnessed episodic
   kinds. This is a goal-independent structural rule over every target, not an
   answer-aware filter.

3. **Honest self-check.** Creation is always `PROVISIONAL`. A later, separate
   model call sees the claim, its exact cited public provenance, and a bounded
   matched counterevidence view, then emits `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, or
   `UNRESOLVED` with citations, prediction, confidence, and reason. The harness
   checks only schema, local visibility, citation existence, and status
   mechanics; it never checks entailment or correctness. Record
   `support_basis` as `SELF_REVIEW_SAME_EVIDENCE` or
   `INDEPENDENT_CORROBORATION`. Offline truth is consulted only after all
   cognition and corpus bytes seal.

4. **Depth that means something.** Compute observation depth 0 and dreamed
   depth `1 + max(parent depth)`. A credited depth-2 row must be a typed
   `ROLE_APPLICATION` whose required semantic parent is `ROLE_EQUIV`; a bare
   extra citation does not qualify. A fresh `T-PATH-PRUNE` thinker receives
   M1-own with the prospectively registered role parent, its descendants, and
   the registered own causal join removed. A matched `T-SHAM-PRUNE` removes
   predeclared nondecisive rows matched on row count, status, and serialized
   token mass (and kind when available). Depth/path credit requires own
   success, loss of paired success under path-prune, and retained own answers
   under sham-prune.

5. **Matched distractor and life intervention.** Do not use v03r's advertised
   target-1 distractor unchanged: on seed 5 its route/effect separation is 9
   records versus 36 for the own relation. Before any model call, select one
   paired-invariant, distinct-source, same-target control using only public
   geometry: one unique route/effect pair, both records outside one local
   window, closest separation stratum to own, and the same available premise
   kinds and neighborhood cardinalities. For seed 5 this selects the
   paired-invariant `land_00 -> cf_target_00` control (route/effect positions
   2/26, gap 24). OWN and DIST then receive position-wise identical 16-view
   templates, six-row caps, decoding seeds, and call budgets; the focus is
   selection metadata and never citable. `T-TWIN` and `L-TWIN` must mount the
   donor's **same M1-own stage** and produce the exact donor-valid opposite
   token for each recipient, not merely degrade or defer.

6. **Prospective gates.** Freeze prompts with zero seed-5-visible retries.
   Before held-out exposure, require the public-projection mutation suite, the
   full 31-predictor population shortcut rule, and development headroom:
   `GOLD_TEXT` 6/6 correct, `GOLD_TWIN` 6/6 donor-valid, and `NO_MEMORY` 0/3
   paired successes on aligned seeds 0--2. The proposal must bind each
   of the 31 public-feature lookup predictors to individual accuracy no more
   than population majority accuracy plus `.02` and paired accuracy no more
   than `.02`; all 31 must pass. A failure is `INVALID_PRECONDITION`, not
   evidence against an organ.

## Can a unique useful join become `SUPPORTED`?

**Yes under the frozen goalposts, but not under the current package's stronger
“new lineage-disjoint evidence” wording.** Seed 5 contains exactly one public
route and one public effect for the decisive source--target join. No second
independent event exists, so requiring novel transitive provenance makes that
join permanently ineligible for `SUPPORTED`; a descendant or paraphrase must
not be laundered as corroboration.

The goalposts define the headline arm as *model reflection*, explicitly allow
false self-approved memories to remain, and forbid an exact verifier—not
model reinspection of remembered evidence. Therefore a later independent
model call may mark the unique join `SUPPORTED` after re-evaluating its
original route/effect witnesses and a counterevidence view. The epistemic claim
is only “model-self-supported from one evidentiary lineage,” never
“independently corroborated” or “verified.” `ROLE_EQUIV` may separately earn
`INDEPENDENT_CORROBORATION` when disjoint source observations support it. If
v1a insists that all support mean independent corroboration, the honest answer
is **no**, and the unique join must remain provisional and cannot enter a
supported-only corpus without changing the world.

## Exact call and job envelope

One provider generation equals one scheduled DREAM opportunity or THINK
operation. Terminal THINK operations stop early and are not padded; the ledger
must reconcile actual calls against these exact maxima. A malformed dispatched
generation consumes budget, has no retry, and remains in the denominator.

| Category | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Development `NO_MEMORY`, `GOLD_TEXT`, `GOLD_TWIN` | `3 arms * 6 lives * 12 = 216` 32B generations |
| Held-out target-blind DREAM prefix | `2 * (46 + 22 + 16) = 168` 32B generations |
| Held-out OWN/DIST return DREAM | `2 * 2 * 16 = 64` 32B generations |
| Held-out THINK0 | `2 * 12 = 24` 32B generations |
| Core text THINK1: M0/OWN/DIST/TWIN | `2 * 4 * 12 = 96` 32B generations |
| Text PATH/SHAM prune interventions | `2 * 2 * 12 = 48` 32B generations |
| Conditional LoRA OWN/TWIN THINK1 | `2 * 2 * 12 = 48` 32B generations |
| Fixed LoRA fidelity probes | `2 lives * 6 registered slots * 4 cues = 48` 7B generations |
| Conditional online LoRA reads | at most `2 * 2 * 12 = 48` 7B generations |
| Training | exactly 2 fresh rank-64 fits; no fallback, retry, rank, or recipe sweep |

Thus the held-out text gate is at most **400 32B generations**; full held-out
transport is at most **448 32B generations**, **96 7B generations**, and two
fits. Including development qualification, the total ceiling is **664 32B
generations**. The six fidelity slots per life are the three registered path
rows plus three present nonpath rows chosen from the sealed pre-goal manifest
by kind-matched priority and canonical tie-breaks; fewer than three is an
invalid precondition, not permission to substitute after results. The roster,
four cue templates, and exact canonical outputs freeze before fitting;
`NOT_FOUND`, conflict, malformed, or truncated output is wrong, and the `.90`
gate is exactly at least **44/48**.

Every final disposition must be one of `INVALID_PRECONDITION`,
`INVALID_INFRASTRUCTURE`, or `VALID_STAGE_FAILURE/PASS`, with remaining
within-stage alternatives listed. Return-routing credit additionally requires
T-OWN paired success, T-M0 and T-DIST paired failure, a new correct supported
own join absent from both controls, PATH loss, SHAM retention, and exact donor
tokens under T-TWIN. Transport credit requires those fixed M1-own manifests,
at least 44/48 fidelity, L-OWN paired success, and exact donor tokens under
L-TWIN. No result automatically authorizes replication or any Paper-1 claim.
