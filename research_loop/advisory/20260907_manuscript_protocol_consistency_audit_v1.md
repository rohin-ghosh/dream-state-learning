# Manuscript/protocol consistency audit v1

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: independent read-only audit of the manuscript against the four named
bound sources and Rohin's binding topology. This advisory changes no manuscript
or protocol byte and authorizes no implementation, model/tokenizer call,
benchmark generation, adapter operation, or GPU use.

## Verdict

**FAIL for protocol-ready manuscript freeze; PASS on the core topology and
causal-arm story.** The draft correctly centers one target-blind parent
teaching one child process-level thinking and then disappearing; excludes
classroom/cohort/peer/population mechanisms; presents `P1` versus `R0` as the
public comparison; and assigns parenting causality to `U0/U1/P0/P1`. No
topology redesign is indicated. The parent's frozen identity/policy is still an
explicit manuscript and ratification TODO.

The freeze is blocked by a present-tense scientific overclaim, one mismatch to the
registered primary estimand, and several unresolved endpoint definitions. Most
other deficiencies are ordinary manuscript exactness/TODO work, not flaws in
the one-parent/one-child design.

## What is already consistent

- The abstract, parenting section, and causal-arm section preserve parent
  disappearance and prohibit classroom/population mechanisms
  (`main.tex:19-33,122-128,147-164,186-205`). This matches the headline plan's
  one-dyad topology and isolated U counterfactual (`headline:12-25,39-72`) and
  the closure audit (`closure-v9:187-209`).
- `R0` is correctly described as fit-free with raw pinned actor weights but an
  evolving isolated active-text store, not as memory-static
  (`main.tex:186-194`; `headline:59-72,579-583`).
- The manuscript correctly distinguishes the public full-package `P1-R0`
  comparison from the P/U parenting interaction and puts the registered

- The exploratory `0.0511/0.4859/0.5287` result remains explicitly post-hoc,
  unseeded, and confounded rather than being promoted to a learning benefit
  (`main.tex:225-233`; `readiness:81-95`).

## Freeze blockers

### 1. Active-text validation is stated before it exists — manuscript overclaim

The abstract calls the evolving textual memory "validated" (`main.tex:26-28`),
and the contribution list can also be read as saying the gated system already
exists (`main.tex:54-58`). The supporting contract is proposal-only
(`active-text:5-12`), the readiness audit says the matched strong baseline is
still missing (`readiness:19-38,97-111`), and closure v9 explicitly says its
semantic-closure PASS is **not** a strong-baseline result
(`closure-v9:25-29,211-219`). Until the once-only certificates actually pass,
all abstract/contribution language must be conditional: a prospectively fixed
candidate mechanism that *must earn* the word strong. The existing conditional
sentence at `main.tex:118-120` does not cure the abstract's present-tense claim.

The abstract's phrase "the same ... evolving textual memory" also risks
implying shared contents. The contract requires a separate store per service
with no cross-service/root/childhood bytes (`active-text:9-12`). Say "the same
frozen updater/retriever policy with isolated on-policy stores."

### 2. The manuscript does not state the registered primary estimand exactly

`main.tex:166-177` declares a pointwise `I(t)`, while `main.tex:212-221,245-247`
switches to undefined "normalized AUC"/`nAUC`. The bound primary is root-level,
entry-adjusted `gAUC`, with fixed weights
`[2G(16)+2G(32)+G(48)]/6`, followed by
`D=[gAUC(P1)-gAUC(P0)]-[gAUC(U1)-gAUC(U0)]`
(`headline:585-610`). The paper must print that exact definition, identify the
separately raised root as the independent unit, and reserve `I(t)` for a curve
display rather than call it the inferential primary. This is a manuscript
representation error; the bound design itself is exact here.

### 3. Promised secondary endpoints lack bound operational definitions —
protocol-design gap

The manuscript repeatedly promises backward retention/forgetting, early slope,
proposal quality, and routing (`main.tex:28-30,59-61,75-81,210-221,245-247`).
The headline plan names some of these but supplies no backward-retention panel,
forgetting equation, early-slope estimator, typed-forced proposal score, or
quantitative "no erasure" joint-success rule. Its sealed probes and primary
`gAUC` are exact (`headline:400-418,585-610`), but those secondary quantities
are not. `main.tex:218-221` additionally admits that missing-action value and
the joint success rule remain unset.

These items cannot be invented during paper editing. Either ratify their exact
units, cuts, scoring, missingness/failure values, thresholds, and multiplicity
role through the architecture path, or remove them from the abstract,
contribution, endpoint, and result-table promises. Backward retention and
forgetting are the clearest design gap rather than a prose omission.

## Manuscript-only exactness repairs

These do not require a topology change, but the methods are not reproducible
without them:

1. Replace the nursery TBD (`main.tex:132-136`) with the two target-blind
   families, three cycles, four opportunities/cycle, fresh application task,
   24 unique tasks/branch, and closed `PREDICT_UPDATE/DISCRIMINATE/SCOPE` or
   `NO_CORRECTION` surface (`headline:107-163`). "Process-level credit
   assignment" at `main.tex:122-126` is too permissive unless the fixed lesson
   code and no-free-strategy/no-action boundary are stated.
2. State one treatment dyad **per independent root**, the isolated U child, and
   the same frozen parent policy across sealed replications. As written,
   "the paper uses one parent and one child" and "if only one child is
   parented" (`main.tex:127-128,179-182`) blur topology with sample size. The
   exact distinction is in `headline:18-25,598-623`.
3. Strengthen "parent ... absent" (`main.tex:150-152`) to the fresh-process
   deletion audit: parent service, feedback/restatements, nursery state/ledger,
   optimizer, rehearsal corpus, serving cache, and unapproved compiler/repo
   edges are removed (`headline:400-405`). Parent absence applies throughout
   deployment, not only to headline probes.
4. Replace "cumulative accepted corpus" (`main.tex:102-107`) with the actual
   fixed-slot clean-base rebuild: accepted child targets plus deterministic
   rehearsal fills, 20 anchors, four response-normalized exposures, and no
   stacked adapter (`headline:281-370`). Those details are causal dose controls,
   not optional implementation color.
5. Describe the active-text updater as the pinned child base with its adapter
   removed, attempting `REFLECT` and conditionally `CURATE`; describe retrieval
   as complete live records plus lexical-only public event blocks. The current
   "reflector/curator updates" and "returns ... records" shorthand
   (`main.tex:109-120`) hides valid no-update paths and one document class
   (`headline:420-551`; `active-text:534-552,654-718`).
6. Qualify the resource-parity sentence at `main.tex:196-199`: all five systems
   share deployment affordances, but `R0` deliberately lacks a P/U
   practice-written childhood checkpoint and receives no fit. Only the P/U factorial isolates parenting/write causes;
   `P1-R0` is not an equal-compute, equal-memory, carrier-only, or component
   contrast (`headline:598-610`; `closure-v9:169-185`).
7. Do not present shuffled/wrong-child advice as a guaranteed null in Table 2.
   It is staged after immutable headline artifacts, and a general process lesson
   may legitimately transfer; the same-target sham tests scaffold dependence
   (`headline:762-778`).

The remaining explicit TBDs—exact parent identity/policy and ratification,
deployment cuts/root rule/SESOI/intervals, figures and tables, citations,
reproducibility bytes, and AI-system versions—are genuine paper-completion
items (`main.tex:218-250,283-284,295-322`). They must be filled from immutable
receipts, not forecast. Parent identity remains a human-ratification field
(`headline:74-90`), so it is also an execution/governance gate, not something
the manuscript may currently call frozen.

## Claim boundary

A positive `P1-R0` cannot rescue a failed `D`, and a positive `D` establishes
only that this frozen parent protocol increased the later benefit of personal
writes on this bounded transfer. A single CompilerGym deployment distribution
does not establish general creativity, population learning, universal
external-memory replacement, saturation, or broad "model meta-intelligence"
(`headline:780-804`; `readiness:163-199`; `main.tex:286-293`). Keep the term
"model meta-intelligence" out of the result claim unless its bounded,
cross-ontology interpretation and required evidence are stated without implying
general learning-to-learn.
