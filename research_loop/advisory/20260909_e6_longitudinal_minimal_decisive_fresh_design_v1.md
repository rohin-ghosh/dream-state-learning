# E6 longitudinal minimal decisive fresh design v1

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: **fresh source-only, unbound scientific design attack**. This note
changes no bound architecture, benchmark, plan, workflow, prompt, model,
tokenizer, data, adapter, child, root, resource allocation, claim, or release
state. It authorizes no implementation, model/tokenizer/embedding call,

## Verdict

**CONDITIONAL DESIGN PASS; CURRENT EXECUTION NO-GO.**

The smallest decisive E6 experiment is a **two-arm, paired, independently
replicated lineage study**, not an extension of the fixed-child E2--E5 relay:

1. independently raise one parented child state per E6 root, delete the parent
   and nursery, then fork that state into two state-isolated deployment
   descendants;
2. compare the frozen full learner `F` with the same child running certified
   `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` and personal parametric writes disabled, `B`;
3. use seven nested lifetime cuts: entry, three pre/at-plateau cuts, and three
   prospectively fixed cuts after the sole allowable plateau anchor;
4. infer only across independent root pairs, using root-level slopes and paired
   contrasts; and
5. pass a noncompensatory conjunction: strong-baseline certification, the
   baseline's own pre-anchor plateau and headroom, continued post-anchor
   baseline plateau, positive full-learner late slope and endpoint gain,
   positive slope advantage, old-skill retention, and absolute terminal
   superiority by the frozen practical margin.

Two arms are sufficient because the E6 question is the incremental lifetime
value of the full learner above a strong memory-only descendant from the same
entry state. `U0/U1`, raw `R0`, E2--E5 carrier/derangement/relay controls, and
additional named memory methods answer other questions and must not consume
the E6 denominator. Seven cuts are the smallest faithful extension of the
current plateau rule: the existing rule uses the two increments from the first
non-entry cut through the third (`16->32` and `32->48`), and E6 then requires
at least three later checkpoints. Treating entry-to-first-cut adaptation as a
plateau interval could reduce the count to six, but would weaken the current
registered construct and is rejected here.

This design is not ready to run. No audited source provides an E6-specific
effect/covariance estimate, defensible E6 confirmation root count, exact
longitudinal load quantum, terminal-load strong-baseline certificate, or
measured all-in GPU-hour bound. `N=32` belongs to the one-parent E1 interaction;
`N=96` and `47/96` belong to a fixed-child E2--E5 relay claim. Neither may be
inherited. A number asserted without an E6-only DEV covariance/power receipt
would be false precision.

## 1. Exact question and maximum E6 result

Let `F` be the frozen full deployment learner and `B` the chosen strong
agent-memory comparator. The narrow question is:

> Across independent child lineages receiving nested prefixes of one frozen
> lifetime distribution, does `F` continue to improve after `B` reaches and
> remains at its own preregistered local plateau, retain earlier competence,
> and end absolutely higher than `B` under matched ordinary deployment
> affordances?

If every gate below passes, E6 alone may support:

> On this frozen task/lifetime distribution and under the registered
> read/update/action budget, the full learner continued improving over three
> post-anchor epochs while a prospectively certified active-text comparator
> remained at a registered local plateau; the full learner retained its
> earlier competence and ended higher by at least the registered practical
> margin.

The qualifiers **this distribution**, **registered local plateau**, and
**registered budget** are mandatory. E6 alone does not establish an intrinsic,
universal, asymptotic, or representational saturation law.

## 2. Inferential unit and child-lineage topology

One E6 root is one independent draw from a new frozen packet distribution
`Q_E6`. It contains a fresh root master, lifetime opportunity tape, environment
randomness, entry/nursery opportunities if the parented system is used, wake
stream, checkpoint panels, and all stochastic keys.

For the full parented system, instantiate the same reset stateless parent
policy independently in every root, raise one child through the frozen
target-blind parented nursery, then delete all parent and nursery state. Fork
the resulting byte-identical parent-deleted child checkpoint into `F` and `B`.
They become noncommunicating counterfactual deployment descendants with
separate adapters, active-text stores, ledgers, files, caches, RNG namespaces,
and processes. They share only the prospectively specified opportunity packet
and common-random-number groups. Divergent actions may create divergent
on-policy histories; that is part of the closed-loop total effect.

The independent statistical unit is the **root pair**. Each root contributes
one row of paired arm quantities. `N` roots give `N`, not `2N`, independent
units. Episodes, programs, blocks, probes, checkpoints, calls, action samples,
rows, fits, adapters, and environment clones are nested repeated measures and
never increase `n`. Inference remains conditional on the fixed parent policy,
fixed child checkpoint family, frozen software, and `Q_E6`; it does not
estimate heterogeneity over parents, child models, or domains.

Reusing one already raised child across many PCFL environment roots would
estimate a fixed-child trajectory effect and is fatal for the requested
lineage-level result. Independently rerunning the same lifetime prefix at each
checkpoint is also invalid and wasteful: every checkpoint must be a snapshot
of one continuously evolving lineage.

## 3. The only two scientific arms

### `F` -- full learner

- Frozen base/entry child and the common `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` mechanism.
- Same THINK, DREAM, tools, files, action interface, context, wake budget, and
  public outcomes as `B`.
- The already validated, frozen experiential writer/compiler and cumulative
  per-life adapter are enabled at each registered epoch boundary.
- Every candidate adapter is a cumulative clean-base rebuild from that
  lineage's eligible preceding public experience and must pass the native
  routing, DREAM restoration, generic-anchor, mount/off, and transactional
  commit canaries. Failure quarantines the candidate and retains the last
  committed adapter; it never removes the root.

### `B` -- strong active-text comparator

- The byte-identical entry state and exact same ordinary deployment
  affordances.
- Its own isolated, on-policy `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` ledger, updater, typed
  playbook, retriever, and native read/update budget.
- Personal parametric fitting and mounting are disabled. The service still
  computes the same would-be admission slots and receipts, so availability of
  eligible experience is observable without affecting the actor.

`B` may be called strong only after every once-only semantic, retrieval, use,
and headroom certificate in `active_text_fixed_contract_v1.md` passes on
identities disjoint from all E6 development and confirmation roots. The
extended lifetime also needs a separately frozen terminal-load stress
certificate showing that the unchanged updater/retriever remains executable
and non-starved at the `T6` store/index scale. A certificate failure stops E6;
it is not permission to tune on E6 roots or relabel the same system strong.

If a different agent-memory baseline is desired, choose exactly one before
E6 development, give it its strongest frozen native update/read frontier, and
require an analogous independent certificate. Do not switch between `P0`,
`R0`, `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED`, or another method after seeing which plateaus. One
method supports singular baseline language only.

No `U0/U1` factorial is needed because E6 does not estimate parenting. No raw
`R0` is needed because the cleaner incremental comparator shares the parented
entry state. Omitting those arms is the main resource reduction and also
prevents E6 from being misread as an E1 rerun.

## 4. Lifetime axis and minimum checkpoints

Define seven nested cuts

```text
T0, T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6.
```

`T0` is entry. `T3` is the first and only confirmation cut at which a
baseline-plateau claim may be assessed. `T4`, `T5`, and `T6` are the three
prospectively fixed post-plateau checkpoints. The same timing applies to both
arms and every root.

Every interval adds the same preregistered quantum `Delta` of genuine new
experience: unique factor-bearing blocks or unique wake opportunities and
their child-dispatched public outcomes. Repetition, paraphrase, DREAM text,
rehearsal, validation probes, padding, or inflated rendering does not advance
the lifetime clock. Prefixes are cumulative and report exact source-event
count, independently sampled factor/information count, raw public tokens,
generated tokens, and retained state.

For the current CompilerGym cadence, the narrow concrete candidate is

```text
T = {0, 16, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96} unique wake programs.
```

This is the cheapest direct extension of the current `0/16/32/48` design. It
can test finite task-distribution lifetime improvement. Program uniqueness
alone, however, does not establish growth of connected experiential knowledge
or an E5 relay. For the full-objective PCFL family, `Delta` instead must be an
exact number of complete chained, factor-bearing lifetime blocks chosen in
E6-only development; cuts must be reported in exact novel-source tokens and
factor information as required by the lifetime-validity audit. If the chosen
generator cannot supply six balanced, nonrepeated increments, the result is
`NO_GO`; never repeat a small program deck as Fable v6.1 did or call filler a
longer life.

`Delta`, task mixture, checkpoint identities, and maximum life are frozen
before confirmation. Development may choose `Delta` to put the strong baseline
near a plausible plateau at `T3`; confirmation may not move `T3` or search
later cuts for a more favorable plateau.

## 5. Probe panels and leakage-resistant scoring

### Fixed held-out value panel

Each root contains eight presealed held-out programs/goals that are identical
across its arms and evaluated at all seven cuts under common RNG and the same
fixed generated-token and action cap. Let `V_c,r(Tj)` be their mean bounded
public task value, with every invalid, undispatched, or non-improving action
receiving the registered failure value. The same panel across cuts makes a
late slope a behavioral change rather than a changing-difficulty artifact.
The cognition-hidden fixed-seed search reference is evaluated on this exact
panel.

### Old-competence retention panel

Each root also contains eight presealed homologous goals whose necessary
experience occurs no later than `T2`, whose exact probe identities never
occur in wake, and whose evidence is old by `T3`. Evaluate the identical old
panel only at `T3` and `T6`. These 16 probe opportunities per arm are the
smallest clean addition that prevents a rising aggregate from hiding loss of
earlier competence. They are not automatically E4 traversal or E5 relay
assays.

All probe processes are sterile clones. Probe prompts, actions, outcomes,
scores, retrievals, files, KV/cache state, and diagnostics never enter the
living lineage, DREAM, SLEEP, `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED`, a later cut, or another
service. Future wake content, probe goals, target identities, plateau status,
search outputs, offline truth, and evaluator state are absent from actor,
parent, writer, updater, retriever, filenames, indices, caches, exception text,
and model-visible receipts.

The complete `Q_E6` packet and split manifest are sealed before confirmation.
Common randomness is keyed by root, opportunity, arm-coupling group, cut, call
slot, and sample. Keys match only where intended, differ across cuts and
independent groups, and are not advanced by treatment-only calls. Committed
model/system events are never retried or replaced. On-policy stores never
cross arms or roots.

The legacy Fable result makes two extra diagnostics mandatory but not new
scientific arms: strict typed-routing/proposal quality after every write, and
valid/distinct/informative action yield per generated token at every cut.
Policy concentration, rejected rows, target mix, labeled/attended tokens,
optimizer slots, failed fits/mounts, retrieval work, and accepted memory yield
are reported as failure-inclusive mediators. They may not select, match,
exclude, stratify, or adjust roots.

## 6. Exact plateau, slope, retention, and terminal quantities

Let

```text
b1_r = V_B,r(T2) - V_B,r(T1)
b2_r = V_B,r(T3) - V_B,r(T2)
H_r  = V_search,r - V_B,r(T3).
```

The baseline has reached the registered local plateau at `T3` only if the 90%
two-sided root-level Student-t intervals for both mean `b1` and mean `b2` lie
wholly inside `[-.05,.05]`, and the one-sided 95% lower bound for mean `H` is
above `.10`. These are the current two alpha-.05 TOST equivalence tests and
headroom condition applied to the comparator actually named in the claim.
No ceiling-bound or still-improving comparator may be described as saturated.

For `x={0,1,2,3}` corresponding to `T3..T6`, compute within every root and arm

```text
beta_c,r = sum_x[(x-1.5) * V_c,r(T3+x)] / sum_x[(x-1.5)^2]
S_c,r    = 3 * beta_c,r
A_r      = S_F,r - S_B,r
G_F,r    = V_F,r(T6) - V_F,r(T3)
T_r      = V_F,r(T6) - V_B,r(T6).
```

`S_c` is the root's OLS projected change across the complete three-epoch late
window. It uses four values and three increments; checkpoints do not become
independent observations. The post-anchor comparator remains locally
plateaued only if the 90% two-sided interval for mean `S_B` lies wholly inside
`[-.05,.05]`. This prevents a temporary pre-anchor pause or comparator decline
from manufacturing an E6 win.

For the old panel `O`, define

```text
R_self,r = O_F,r(T6) - O_F,r(T3)
R_base,r = O_F,r(T6) - O_B,r(T6).
```

The frozen practical superiority margin is `.05`, inherited only as a
candidate scale from the current bounded task value; it requires E6-specific
DEV justification before ratification. The retention noninferiority margin is
also `.05` on that scale. Changing either after confirmation identities exist
invalidates the version.

## 7. Noncompensatory E6 confirmation rule

Use a fixed sequence, stopping claim release permanently at the first failed
rung. Every comparison is across root rows.

1. **Instrument/strong-comparator validity:** all provenance, deletion,
   isolation, probe-nonreturn, resource-parity, native-routing, transactional,
   restore/mount, and certificate gates pass.
2. **Pre-anchor baseline plateau:** both `b1` and `b2` pass equivalence and `H`
   passes headroom.
3. **Post-anchor baseline plateau:** `S_B` passes equivalence.
4. **Absolute late improvement:** one-sided 95% lower bounds for mean `S_F`
   and mean `G_F` are above zero, and both point estimates are at least `.05`.
5. **Improvement beyond baseline:** the one-sided 95% lower bound for mean
   `A` is above zero and its point estimate is at least `.05`.
6. **Retention:** one-sided 95% lower bounds for mean `R_self` and `R_base`
   are above `-.05`.
7. **Absolute terminal superiority:** the lower endpoint of the two-sided 95%
   interval for mean `T` is above zero and its point estimate is at least
   `.05`.

Each equivalence or multi-part rung is an intersection-union test: every
component must pass and none can compensate for another. Fixed sequence plus
the within-rung intersections controls the released E6 claim family at alpha
`.05`; all estimates and intervals are still reported. Do not demand noisy
pointwise monotonicity. Do not replace the root-level OLS slope with checkpoint
rows, task rows, or second differences.

A positive `A` without positive `S_F` is merely slower decline. Positive
`S_F` without post-anchor `S_B` equivalence does not show improvement where the
baseline plateaued. Either without retention may hide catastrophic forgetting.
Any gain-normalized comparison without `T` may end below the baseline. All
four failure modes are ruled out by the conjunction.

## 8. Root count and power logic

There is currently **no honest fixed numeric E6 `N`**. The exact rule for
obtaining one is:

1. Run one open engineering lineage pair for plumbing and terminal-load timing.
   It supplies no stochastic go/no-go evidence.
2. Run a disjoint, presealed E6-only DEV panel. Eight root pairs is the smallest
   aggressive budget recommendation here, not a source-derived power claim.
   Complete all eight, retain failures, and use them only to estimate the
   paired endpoint covariance, tail behavior, plateau plausibility, and
   all-in cost. Because eight roots estimate variance poorly, use upper
   confidence bounds/inflation rather than plug-in SDs.
3. Before confirmation root IDs exist, simulate the **entire conjunction** on
   a prospective candidate grid such as `N={24,32,40,48,64}`. Include normal,
   standardized t5, skewed, mild two-point, and rare-adverse-root families,
   and the most adverse covariance/SD values allowed by the DEV uncertainty
   set.
4. The minimally relevant planning alternative is zero baseline increments
   and post-slope, headroom `.15`, full-learner projected late change and slope
   advantage `.07`, zero old-panel loss, and terminal superiority `.07`, all
   on the bounded value scale. These are planning points, not expected effects
   or claims.
5. Choose the smallest `N` whose lowest named-family joint-pass estimate has a
   one-sided 95% Monte Carlo lower bound of at least `.80`, and that fits the
   separately ratified resource cap with reserve. Freeze `N`, the simulator,
   seeds, distribution family, covariance bounds, software versions, and
   stdout hash. If no candidate fits, E6 is `NO_GO`, not permission to weaken
   margins or claim an underpowered negative.

`N=32` is therefore a plausible grid point, not the answer. At a `.10` root SD
it may look adequate for one `.05`-scale contrast, but the E6 decision includes
three equivalence demands, headroom, two late-improvement conditions, slope
advantage, two retention bounds, and terminal superiority. Joint power can be
materially lower. Conversely, multiplying endpoints by an arbitrary Bonferroni
factor is unnecessary because the released claim is a fixed-sequence
intersection; the correct calculation is the preregistered joint operating
characteristic.

Confirmation is fixed-`N`. There is no variance-based extension, favorable
root replacement, optional rescue root, or pooling of DEV. If realized
uncertainty is too wide, the conclusion is inconclusive.

## 9. Matched affordances and the compute boundary

Within every paired root, match exactly:

- parented entry bytes; base model; tools/skills/files; context and output
  limits; public action/outcome law; epoch opportunities and order; wake,
  DREAM, and probe schedules; common RNG; action cap; and failure values;
- ordinary actor and updater call opportunities and generated-token ceilings;
- the same frozen `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` updater/retriever policy, per-query
  document/token cap, update cadence, and initially empty store, while keeping
  store contents on-policy and isolated; and
- checkpoint timing, probe identities, evaluator, hardware class, queue
  policy, and artifact durability.

The treatment-defining asymmetry is that `F` performs cumulative LoRA
optimization and owns the resulting adapter; `B` does not. Count and report
every model call, input/output/generated token, dispatched action, accepted and
rejected row, supervised and attended token, optimizer position/step, fit,
mount, embedding, retrieval, store/index byte, wall time, occupied GPU time,
energy if available, and failure by arm and cut.

There is no scientifically useful way to make exact `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` spend
LoRA optimizer work without changing the method; burning an equal GPU budget
on an unmounted neutral adapter equalizes accounting but does not give the
baseline a useful opportunity. The minimal two-arm result is therefore an
**ordinary-deployment-affordance-parity full-system comparison**, not an
equal-total-compute or best-allocation-of-compute comparison. Plot value versus
generated tokens and occupied GPU-hours as resource frontiers and charge all
learner-specific fitting to `F`.

If equal-total-compute superiority is required, add a third, prospectively
certified `B_NATIVE_BUDGET` arm allowed to spend the same total budget through
its strongest native update/read policy. That changes the comparator,
certification, power, and resource problem and is not part of the smallest
design. A shadow/burn fit may diagnose scheduler or thermal effects but cannot
support an equal-useful-compute claim.

## 10. Information per GPU-hour staging and stopping

### Development only

1. **CPU G0:** prove generator closure, genuinely increasing information,
   prefix nesting, split/ID uniqueness, exact probe sterility, future-goal
   invisibility, counter-key coupling, parent deletion, store/adapter isolation,
   failure-inclusive reducers, and the seven-cut/retention arithmetic.
2. **Comparator G1:** finish `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` development on separate
   identities, freeze it, run its once-only strength certificate, then run the
   model-free and terminal-load stress fixtures. Failure ends strong-baseline
   E6 for this version.
3. **Open plumbing G2:** run one root only to the `T3` boundary; continue it to
   `T6` only if chronology, old-panel construction, writer transactions, and
   measured cost are intact. It cannot authorize a stochastic claim.
4. **E6 DEV G3:** run the disjoint DEV root pairs first through `T3`. Extend to
   `T6` only if the baseline has plausible plateau/headroom, the full learner
   has no systematic routing/retention pathology, and the measured all-in
   lease can support the power grid. Tune only on DEV; any prompt, writer,
   baseline, task mix, `Delta`, threshold, or interface change starts a new
   DEV identity namespace.
5. **Power/resource G4:** freeze the full joint simulation and exact `N`; obtain
   independent reproduction and the separate GPU authorization. No E2--E5
   result can substitute for this gate.

### Confirmation

6. **Stage C1:** prebind all `N` root packets and run both arms only through
   `T3`. Do not inspect learner effects. A sealed baseline-only reducer applies
   the strong/reference, two-increment plateau, and headroom gates. If any
   fails, stop before `T4`; report finite curves without saturation language.
7. **Stage C2:** if and only if C1 passes, extend the same living descendants
   through `T4/T5/T6`. Do not rerun prefixes. Once C2 starts, there is no
   efficacy or futility peek; finish every assigned root unless a
   preregistered integrity/safety stop fires.
8. **Stage C3:** freeze all root rows, then apply the fixed sequence in Section
   7. Run no extra arm or root to rescue a failed rung.

A version-level provenance, leakage, parent-deletion, cross-service state,
probe-return, evaluator-capability, or systematic resource-parity defect is an
immediate stop and blocks claims. A committed malformed response, rejected
action, failed active-text update, or quarantined learner fit is a behavioral
event with its registered failure disposition; it does not permit a retry or
replacement. Administrative loss of a required paired cut blocks E6 release;
complete-case or worst-bound tables are descriptive only. Adverse routing,
generic-quality, or safety boundaries may stop continued exposure, but their
exact bytes must be frozen before confirmation.

This order spends no post-plateau learner GPU if the named baseline fails to
plateau, performs every static check before model work, uses one incremental
life rather than seven reruns, and avoids every non-E6 scientific arm.

## 11. Feasibility attack

The apparent arm reduction does not make E6 cheap. Mechanically extending the
current one-parent CompilerGym ceilings to two services, 96 wake programs,
seven eight-program fixed-panel cuts, one parented childhood, and six
deployment write epochs gives a planning ceiling of approximately:

```text
one parented childhood                  324 calls,  46,848 output tokens, 3 fits
two-service 96-program deployment     3,596 calls, 689,152 output tokens, 6 fits
two old-panel evaluations/arm           256 calls,  32,768 output tokens
per E6 root total                     4,176 calls, 768,768 output tokens, 9 fits
```

This is arithmetic extrapolation from the current v1 table, not a runtime
bound. At the merely illustrative grid point `N=32`, confirmation alone is
`133,632` logical calls, `24,600,576` maximum output tokens, and `288` fits;
at `N=64` it is `267,264` calls, `49,201,152` output tokens, and `576` fits.
Neither total includes the open root, E6 DEV panel, writer canaries,
active-text certificates, full input/prefill work, embeddings, search,
storage, audit, retries forbidden by the protocol, reserve, or implementation
review.

The prior resource attack found no finite measured all-in root runtime even
for the 48-program five-service proposal, and the legacy implementation could
not realize the required seeded multi-adapter topology. The PCFL lifetime
advisory separately estimated 120--240 large-model DREAM calls at an
illustrative `6L` life and warned that 16--20 roots plus multiple arms were not
a two-week entitlement. Those numbers do not time this design, but they rule
out assuming that two arms or rank eight makes confirmation feasible.

Before execution, a terminal-size canary must measure complete childhood and
`T0..T6` wall time, input/output tokens, prefill/decode, all fits and
mounts, active-text store/index scaling, occupied GPU time, persistent bytes,
and failure recovery on the exact hardware/runtime. The ratified lease
inequality must include all DEV, confirmation waves, independent review, a
20% capacity reserve, and a 12-hour wall reserve. If the power-selected `N`
does not fit, the honest result is resource `NO_GO`; shortening the late window
below three checkpoints, recycling roots, or reducing `N` after looking is not
a repair.

The exact `ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED` contract was budgeted for the current 48-program,
five-service packet. Extending it to a longer two-service life changes terminal
store/index and query counts even when aggregate counts appear comparable.
Its extended-load behavior and resource bounds must be explicitly deliberated
and certified; silent extrapolation would make a baseline plateau
uninterpretable.

## 12. E2--E5 quarantine

E6 receives a fresh split name, root namespace, root masters, lifetime streams,
checkpoint panels, adapters, stores, and artifacts. It may reuse only frozen
generator/interface/compiler **code** that has passed its own gates. It may
not reuse or initialize from:

- the selected fixed child or environment clones from the E2--E5 relay;
- any E2--E5 DEV, reserve, confirmation, twin, or failed root;
- their ledgers, outcomes, semantic rows, active-text records, adapters,
  checkpoint values, or target identities; or
- their sample size, success threshold, variance, or root count.

E2--E5 outcomes may serve only as an external prerequisite to freeze the full
learner mechanism. They cannot select E6 roots or estimate E6 confirmation
power. Conversely, an E6 pass supplies no authentic-link, traversal,
information-seeking relay, binding, adapter-specific carrier, or compression
intervention. At paper level, separately passing rungs may be stated as a
conjunction of evidence; E6 is not a same-root mediation test showing that the
E5 flywheel caused its slope.

## 13. What cannot be claimed

Even a complete pass cannot establish:

- universal, intrinsic, permanent, or asymptotic saturation of active text or
  agent memory;
- superiority to multiple named memory methods, to an official ACE/ReMem/
  ReasoningBank/MemoPilot implementation, or outside the one prospectively
  chosen comparator and distribution;
- equal-total-compute or compute-optimal superiority under the two-arm design;
- parenting causality, because there is no U factorial in E6;
- that LoRA contains a connected graph, compresses the complete retained life,
  or is the sole carrier of the late effect;
- E2 connection, E3 rate--distortion compression, E4 traversal, E5 expansion,
  or causal mediation of the E6 slope by any of them;
- monotone improvement at every checkpoint, unbounded self-improvement,
  open-world invention, a general learning-to-learn law, or transfer to other
  models, parents, domains, or lifetime scales; or
- a positive result if plateau, headroom, post-plateau equivalence, retention,
  terminal superiority, baseline strength, or integrity is absent, regardless
  of a favorable average slope.

The legacy Fable paths are not positive or negative E6 roots. Their safe use is
to motivate unique lifetime information, strict writer/action-channel canaries,
root-level replication, retention, and a nondeclining-baseline test. They do
not supply E6 variance, power, baseline strength, or scientific evidence.

## Disposition

Adopt the two-arm/seven-cut/root-paired structure as the minimal design target
only through a new material architecture deliberation. Before exact
ratification, close five missing objects: the E6 lifetime generator and
`Delta`, extended-load strong-baseline certificate, DEV identity/count and
adverse bounds, joint power receipt yielding a fixed `N`, and measured all-in
resource/lease proof. Until then the correct verdict is **design-coherent,
execution-NO-GO, no E6 claim**.
