## Verdict

**REVISE. Do not adopt v2 as a complete E0--E6 map, a scientific gate, or an
execution plan.**

V2 is materially better than the attacked v1. It correctly separates E0 and
E1 from the fixed-child relay, restores the central E2 atoms/null concern,
keeps the root rather than within-root calls as M's unit, restores the public
E5 chronology and the strong active-text comparator, makes the old/new
necessity contrasts primary rather than additive interaction, fixes E6's
plateau anchor prospectively, uses independently raised paired lineages, and
keeps semantic code, LoRA transport, and physical compression distinct. Its
evidence-status and no-authority language are also appropriately conservative.

The remaining defects are nevertheless claim-gating. E1 still lacks the
comparison that identifies a parenting effect; M retains broad E2/E5 language
while leaving required attribution controls conditional or ambiguous; L's
terminal and trajectory claims exceed its stated tests; C omits parts of the
rate, utility, replication, and confirmation contract; and two source/
authority assertions are not supported by the bound bytes. The closing claim
that v2 “supplies a complete E0--E6 map” is therefore false on this source
boundary.

## 1. E1 is named but not scientifically identified

Lines 32--35 and 295--297 say that independently raised comparable children
would show that parenting changed later parent-absent learning, retention, and
transfer. Lines 99--130 then describe classroom fan-out, parent feedback, and
lineage continuity, but never bind the comparison arms or estimand that would
separate a parenting effect on later learning from a parenting effect on entry
competence.

The bound evidence-gap matrix requires independently raised child lineages,
parent-deleted deployment, and a `U0/U1/P0/P1` interaction on entry-adjusted
learning curves, retention, transfer, and information per token. V2 supplies
none of the exact factor definitions, randomized/paired assignment rule,
entry adjustment, root reducer, failure values, uncertainty, multiplicity,
confirmation power, or frozen adaptation rule. “Comparable children” is not
an estimand. Classroom branches inside one shared child are correctly denied
population-replication status, but that denial does not create the missing
independent child comparison.

The parent-adaptation rule is also incomplete for confirmation. The wiring
audit permits delayed results to update the bounded parent ledger during
development, but requires confirmation either to freeze the parent or to
prospectively freeze the complete adaptive rule. V2 mentions only development
updates and does not close this adaptivity/leakage boundary.

Therefore the maximum E1 claim is currently unsupported. V2 may retain E1 as
an explicitly open obligation, but it cannot say that the program supplies
the E1 design until the parenting factorial/contrast and inferential contract
are bound.

## 2. M still has claim--control mismatches

### E2 carrier and attribution ambiguity

Lines 141 and 143 write “atoms-only / truthful-null” as if one slash-labeled
cell could stand for two distinct controls. The bound attack says authentic
versus deranged does not establish linked benefit over isolated facts and that
both the atoms baseline and truthful-null are needed for the named connection
claim. V2 must enumerate separate text and LoRA cells/interventions, exact
matched exposure, and the exact linked-minus-atoms and linked-minus-null gates.

The active-goal matrix also requires adapter-off, wrong-life/binding, and the
same read plan for the connected-parametric E2 result. V2 places adapter-off
and wrong-life/root in a conditional E5 list instead of making the relevant
controls claim-gating for parametric E2. Atomic retention and a common read
plan survive, but they do not by themselves attribute the connected effect to
the assigned LoRA carrier.

### E5's required controls remain improperly optional

Lines 164--169 say to keep `REACHOUT_OFF`, sham signal, no-write, old-row cut,
new-binding swap, adapter-off, wrong-life/root, and the certified comparator
“wherever” the release claim excludes the corresponding alternatives. But the
maximum M sentence at lines 298--302 already names connected experience,
experiment selection, writing, delayed joint use, interventions, and a strong
comparator. Those words exclude the very alternatives the listed controls
address. For that maximum claim, the controls are gates, not optional
diagnostics.

In particular, an experience-directed-action statement needs the acquisition-
path control; a grounded-new-write statement needs no-write/sham and binding
controls; an assigned-carrier statement needs adapter-off/wrong-life; a joint
old/new statement needs absolute `Y11` plus both necessity contrasts; and a
strong-baseline statement needs the independently certified comparator with
matched ordinary affordances. V2 correctly states the last two requirements,
but its “wherever” clause reopens the exact caveat rejected in the v1 attack.

The valid repair is binary: make every control needed by the maximum M claim
claim-gating on the fixed denominator, or narrow the released rung and
sentence to the alternatives actually excluded. Conditional attribution after
core-positive selection may remain only a selected-subpopulation diagnostic,
as v2 correctly says.

## 3. L's terminal-margin and trajectory language outrun its tests

The paired root unit, seven fixed cuts, fixed `T3` anchor, disjoint E6 DEV, and
joint-power planning logic are faithful to the bound E6 design. Three claim
problems remain.

First, lines 223 and 303--306 require/claim terminal superiority “by” the
frozen practical margin. The bound E6 test instead requires a two-sided 95%
interval lower endpoint above zero and only a point estimate at least `.05`.
That supports “the mean was above zero, with an observed difference at least
`.05`”; it does not support inference that the population mean exceeds `.05`.
To claim superiority by at least a practical margin `Delta_T`, the lower
confidence bound must exceed `Delta_T`, and the power simulation must use that
gate. Otherwise the claim must be narrowed to match the zero-bound test plus
descriptive point threshold.

Second, the tests use a positive root-level OLS slope and positive `T6-T3`
gain. They do not require improvement in each of the three increments. The
maximum wording “continued improving across three post-anchor epochs” can
therefore overstate a trajectory that declined or stayed flat before a final
jump. Use “improved over the registered three-epoch post-anchor window” unless
a per-increment/shape condition is added.

Third, post-anchor comparator plateau is judged only by equivalence of its
four-cut OLS projected change. A large non-linear excursion with near-zero
linear slope can pass while the comparator did not “remain” at a plateau.
Either narrow the claim to equivalent-to-zero projected mean slope over the
window or add a registered excursion/increment-equivalence condition capable
of supporting “remained at plateau.” This is separate from avoiding noisy
pointwise monotonicity for `F`.

V2 also omits the E6 design's essential resource interpretation. `F` receives
learner-specific LoRA fitting and `B` does not; the two-arm result is ordinary-
deployment-affordance parity, not equal-total-compute or compute-optimal
superiority. That boundary and complete per-arm resource reporting must appear
next to the L claim, not be left implicit in “registered budget.”

## 4. C compresses away claim-defining E3 obligations

V2 preserves the correct physical intercept arithmetic and correctly refuses
to call LoRA transport compression. It also names the three core text carriers
and most semantic controls. The concise version nevertheless loses enough of
the bound E3 contract that its positive C sentence is not yet licensed.

1. “First semantic crossover” at lines 261--264 is ambiguous. In the bound E3
   design, `m*` is the first presealed fixture grid point satisfying all three
   basic semantic byte thresholds in both required coordinates, not merely
   the first point where one compact/expanded curve crosses. Actual admitted
   semantics then determine actual rates, every root/load remains in the
   denominator, and all three positive claim loads must add new atoms and
   authentic links.
2. “Preserve utility” omits the failure-inclusive root-level fidelity,
   distortion/noninferiority, false-memory, schema-value, connection, and
   practical-margin gates. “Beat ordinary normalization/general codecs” also
   omits the exact `C/E`, `C/raw`, `C/N`, and codec conditions that distinguish
   normalization from the stronger schema-plus-residual result.
3. “Functional uncertainty is across independent roots/streams” does not name
   one unit. Loads and streams nested in an environment root cannot increase
   `N`. The bound E3 design requires one fixed child, isolated environment
   roots as the statistical units, root-level failure-inclusive reducers, and
   a new intersection--union power receipt selecting confirmation `N`; v2
   supplies no C confirmation rule or power gate.
4. The optional LoRA paragraph omits claim-gating candidate-only base,
   unaided-generative read, exact atomic retention, `TEXT_SAME_SEMANTICS`, and
   truthful twin/schema redirection requirements. Generic “read-time cuts” is
   not an exact substitute when the maximum sentence says the compact
   semantics were behaviorally accessible through LoRA.

V2 may remain a high-level map only if it explicitly incorporates the exact
bound E3 contract without relaxation. Otherwise these gates and the distinct
normalization, prospective-schema, transport, and future physical claims must
be restored in the synthesis itself.

## 5. E0 is directionally correct but incomplete for its maximum claim

The long-lifetime wiring correction at lines 63--67 is faithful: the current
runner uses the legacy path, adapter-head rollback does not quarantine the
rejected corpus, and this blocks E0. The writer floor also correctly separates
adapter-head and evidence/corpus promotion, requires previous-child
comparison, and binds source/launch/artifact receipts.

However, “stored eligible grounded experience” in the maximum E0 claim is a
carrier-attribution statement. The bound evidence-gap matrix requires
free-routing, typed-forced proposal/provenance, retention, adapter-off,
wrong-life/binding, rollback, and independent development roots. V2 does not
explicitly retain the typed-forced/provenance or carrier-specific controls,
does not define the E0 inferential unit/failure handling, and calls for the
“smallest” disjoint writer panel without a cost, power, or precision receipt.
The safe repair is to restore those obligations, distinguish engineering
transaction tests from stochastic behavioral evidence, and label panel size
as unresolved until justified.

There is also a factual attribution error at lines 93--97. The bound V9 audit
says the **V8 architecture-change JSON/derived exhaustive visibility
projection** is `161,345,175` bytes (about 154 MiB), while the V9 repair draft
is about 9 KiB. Calling the 154 MiB object “the existing V9 writer draft”
conflates the compact draft with the oversized derived surface. Refer to the
V8/expanded projection precisely and preserve the distinction among compact
normative source, derived exhaustive projection, and equality receipt.

## 6. Scope, authority, and cross-module quarantine need repair

Lines 101--104 assert that a “ratified adaptive-parent v5 proposal” supplies
the high-level E1 shape. No such exact source or hash appears in v2's bound
receipts or in this audit's supplied boundary. The bound continuity audit says
the joint Fable--Codex semantics remain pending and explicitly supplies no
protocol or execution authorization. On these bytes, “ratified” is
unsupported. Remove the assertion or bind the exact ratification source,
bytes, scope, and authority while preserving the still-pending decisions at
lines 127--130.

Likewise, line 231's possessive “E1's `N=32`” refers to an E1 number without an
E1 design/power receipt in this boundary. It is safe only as a warning that a
number mentioned elsewhere cannot transfer to E6; it must not be treated as
an adopted E1 sample size.

Finally, lines 47--51 allow modules to share a generator family and be nested
in one physical execution if units, denominators, reducers, gates, and claims
stay separate. The bound E6 design is stricter: E6 needs fresh split and root
namespaces, root masters, lifetime streams, panels, adapters, stores, and
artifacts, and it may reuse only frozen code—not M identities, data, outcomes,
state, sample size, variance, or selection. Add these state/data/power
quarantines. A shared orchestrating process cannot turn cross-module leakage
or dependent roots into valid nesting.

## 7. The efficiency ordering is a heuristic, not an optimization result

The order at lines 273--288 is sensible falsification-first staging, and the
static LoRA intercept genuinely rules out spending resources merely to seek a
positive current-scale physical-rate cell. But the bound sources contain no
common cost model, expected information gain, loss function, or operating
characteristic proving the whole list is an “information-per-GPU-hour”
ordering. E3's DEV size is explicitly a feasibility screen, E6's size is
unresolved, and E0/E1 have no source-bound sample-size optimization here.

Relabel the list a heuristic resource/stopping priority. Remove “smallest” for
the E0 panel until justified. The broader statement that the architecture is a
bounded resource recommendation rather than a mathematical minimum should be
carried into this section explicitly.

## Required fixes before a successor can pass source-only review

1. Bind E1's exact independent-child `U0/U1/P0/P1` factors, parent-deleted
   deployment, entry-adjusted interaction, endpoints, unit/reducer, failure
   values, frozen/adaptive-parent confirmation rule, uncertainty, power, and
   maximum claim; otherwise mark E1 design open and remove the positive claim.
2. Split atoms-only and truthful-null into explicit matched E2 controls; make
   adapter-off/wrong-life/binding and common-read attribution gates explicit
   for parametric E2.
3. Make every alternative-closing E5 control required by the maximum M claim
   claim-gating on the fixed denominator, or narrow the rung/claim; retain
   conditional positive-root analyses only as selected-subpopulation
   diagnostics.
4. Reconcile L's statistical gates with its words: either test the terminal
   practical margin itself or narrow the claim; replace per-epoch-sounding
   improvement and persistent-plateau wording with window/slope language or
   add adequate shape gates; state ordinary-affordance rather than equal-
   compute scope.
5. Restore or normatively incorporate E3's exact crossover definition, byte
   coordinates and thresholds, failure-inclusive utility/noninferiority and
   schema gates, one root unit, repeated-load reducer, confirmation power, and
   full LoRA-transport controls.
6. Restore E0's typed-provenance and carrier-attribution controls, declare its
   independent unit and confirmation logic, and stop calling an unsupported
   panel size “smallest.”
7. Correct the V8/V9 154 MiB attribution; remove or exactly source the claim
   that adaptive-parent v5 is ratified; treat the mentioned E1 `N=32` as
   non-adopted unless its power receipt is bound.
8. Add explicit cross-module split/state/data/artifact/power quarantine,
   especially for E6, and relabel the information-per-GPU-hour list as a
   heuristic resource recommendation.
9. Replace the closing “complete E0--E6 map” statement with “directional
   modular recommendation with unresolved E0/E1/E3/L contracts” until all
   preceding fixes are bound and independently reviewed.

## Disposition

The corrected modular separation should be preserved. This audit does not
reject the program's direction; it rejects adoption of these bytes as complete
or claim-ready. The next legal action is a source-only successor repair through
the `AGENTS.md` architecture-deliberation path. No implementation, experiment,
GPU work, or claim follows from this review.
