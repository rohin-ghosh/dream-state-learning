## Remaining exact rework

### R1 — deterministically freeze the four load points

Sections `6.A` and `6.C` refer to “two consecutive growing loads,” “the
pre-crossover diagnostic,” and “three fixed growing cohort loads,” but never
define the deterministic selection function. This leaves room to choose a
favorable part of the CPU curve after seeing it.

Replace that language with:

```text
On the finite predeclared power-of-two grid, m* is the least m for which
FIXED_SCHEMA_RESIDUAL satisfies, on every one of the eight calibration roots:

  R_packed_plain < .80 and R_packed_codec < .80

at m, 2m, and 4m, and its byte increment is < .80 of the matched packed
increment on both intervals [m,2m] and [2m,4m].

Freeze m_prev=m/2, m1=m, m2=2m, and m3=4m. If no such m exists inside the
ratified finite grid and resource lease, Stage A is NO_GO. No later child
output may change these cuts.
```

For the child-authored confirmation, require the root-level one-sided upper
95% bounds for both `R_packed_plain` and `R_packed_codec` to be below `.80` at
`m2` and `m3`; report `m_prev` and `m1` diagnostically. This makes “at least
20% shorter” an actual registered test rather than prose.

### R2 — define the finite confirmation-size search

Section `6.D` selects the “smallest allowed `N`,” but supplies no allowed set
or maximum. An unbounded or retrospectively chosen search is neither a sample
size rule nor a resource contract.

Freeze a finite set in the successor proposal, for example:

```text
N in {16,24,32,48}
```

Use the smallest member satisfying the stated joint-power and primary-gate
power requirements under the frozen failure-inclusive simulation. If none
passes or the exact lease does not fit, the confirmation is `NO_GO`; narrowing
requires a new deliberation. The particular set may change before binding,
but it must be exact and finite before confirmation roots exist.

### R3 — four LoRA DEV roots cannot license the transport sentence

Section `6.E` correctly labels the one-canary/four-root run `DEV`, but then
gives it the maximum implication that the semantics “were behaviorally
transported.” Section `7` likewise assigns the transport sentence to an `E`
pass. Four development roots can justify feasibility and estimate variance,
not a paper-level scientific transport clause.

Split the rung:

```text
E_DEV:
  one two-build canary plus four fresh DEV roots;
  maximum language = bounded LoRA-transport feasibility in DEV.

E_CONFIRM:
  separately powered, presealed roots with exact lease and the same
  noncompensatory controls;
  only this rung licenses the transport sentence.
```

No sample size, power, or success may be borrowed from semantic-code
confirmation, parenting, C11, targets, technical fits, or repeated probes.
If the paper does not need a LoRA transport clause, stop at `E_DEV` or omit the
rung rather than promoting a four-root result.
