# PCFL v2 paper value and thesis alignment

**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** independent advisory; no implementation, intake, model, GPU, network, or claim authority  
**Audited PCFL v2 proposal:** `chg_20260902_pcfl_compose_self_revision_text_dev_v2/change.json` at SHA-256 `2fc2521d821efc2ff4852ef9decbb4c2bdf834a8f1b0ff438aec7e78d2a0f047`

## Executive judgment

PCFL-Compose v2 is a legitimate *organ survival assay* if its exact-byte
contract defects are repaired before intake. It is not a miniature version of
the paper result. A valid positive would show that one fixed prompted model,
inside one highly structured harness, can turn a supplied synthetic public
event stream into a mutable external text object and later make different
registered actions when that object is intervened on. That is useful causal
plumbing. It does not show learned experiential reasoning, within-life policy
learning, learned compression, parametric memory, persistence beyond context,
lifetime scaling, autonomous discovery, better evidence acquisition, or a
self-improving agent.

A valid negative would reject some part of that exact prompted writer--reader--
actor bundle on this tiny construct. It would not falsify Dream--LoRA--Think,
because v2 contains no LoRA, no weight change, no information-pressure regime,
no growing lifetime, no learned controller, and no on-policy experience loop.

The strongest recommendation is therefore: **repair v2 once and use it only as
a stop/go diagnostic; if it passes, do not enlarge or polish it. Move the next
scientific budget directly to a text-first PCFL-Stream study with genuinely new
causal information at at least three powered post-native checkpoints. Add LoRA
only after the identical compiled text has demonstrated causal action value.**

The paper-worthy object is the lifetime response surface, not the 3,414-call
pre-context trace.

## Thesis used for this judgment

The controlling claim across `IDEAS.md`, notes 42 and 45--48, the empirical
ledger, and the paper-target deliberation is:

> A fixed general resolver should convert its own growing action--outcome life
> into persistent, connected per-life knowledge that continues to improve later
> action after raw history no longer fits, while retaining old knowledge and
> composing across eras; only a later on-policy study can show the complete
> action--experience--memory flywheel.

Several already measured facts constrain the design:

- Small-memory context plus recognition matched LoRA plus recognition. The
  resolution protocol, not the substrate, supplied the observed intelligence.
- The earlier lifetime LoRA study failed honestly: retrieval retained facts,
  adapters did not retain or compose them at scale, and apparent wins were
  answer-style artifacts.
- At small scale, target-shaped atomic memories can be transported through a
  LoRA and used by a clean resolver; this is already an existence result, not
  the remaining paper question.
- Recurrent depth can be made to grow through a citation interface, but depth
  did not converge on correct structure. Autonomous structural hypothesis
  generation and self-scheduled blind verification remain the weak organs.
- Fixed-source experiments can identify memory's effect on later behavior but
  cannot establish the on-policy flywheel, because memory never changes what
  evidence the agent obtains.
- PCFL-13 contains only about 16.66 bits of reusable hidden backbone. Repetition
  after coverage is exposure or delay, not continued learning.

These facts make another tiny transport demonstration low novelty. They make a
post-native acquisition/retention/composition curve high value.

## The strongest case for running v2

The strongest case is not that v2 tests the thesis. It is that it cheaply and
causally stress-tests several interfaces that the thesis will later require:

1. A prospective commitment exists before the decisive public outcome.
2. Raw public outcome A, not a hidden answer, can induce a second Dream state.
3. A model-authored representation freezes through a content-addressed
   commitment boundary.
4. Target-time intervention on the assigned frozen corpus can redirect a
   later legal action.
5. A matched independent factor can serve as a coarse specificity alarm.
6. Exact chronology, actor visibility, failure denominators, reset behavior,
   and resource use can be made mechanically auditable.

This is valuable because a failure can prevent a much more expensive LoRA or
lifetime run from being built on a dead interface. The factorized algebra,
counterfactual fork, exact target theorem, target twins, and finite exhaustive
CPU certification make PCFL unusually strong as a mechanism microscope.

That is the entire strongest case. It supports a preflight, not a headline.

## Exact interpretation of a valid positive

Assume the currently documented pre-intake defects are repaired, Stage 0 is
fully valid, Stage 1 passes its frozen conjunction, conditional Stage 2 runs
without contamination, and every assigned failure remains in its denominator.
Then the result can establish only the following conjunction for the registered
roots and exact model/prompt/harness:

| Observation | What is earned | What is not earned |
|---|---|---|
| Dream-1 commits prospective model-authored bytes | The prompted writer can create a syntactically valid prospective external-memory candidate under the supplied views and caps. | That the candidate is true, semantic, compressed, abstract, autonomous, or learned during the life. |
| Raw A changes Dream-2's retained object in the registered h direction | The prompted writer is behaviorally sensitive to one later public outcome and can revise an external artifact. | A4-style durable belief revision, contradiction handling, calibrated epistemics, or learned self-revision. |
| Authentic frozen corpus improves or redirects the selected later action relative to registered controls | The exact corpus--reader--Think--actor bundle has a causal effect on terminal behavior under this interface. | Semantic mediation, a learned world model, parametric memory, recurrence necessity, or general task efficacy. |
| The independent q alarm remains quiet | No gross factor-family or interface effect appears in this one matched null construction. | Identification of all leakage, a population-level specificity estimate, equivalence, or generalization. |
| Structured AST diagnostic succeeds | The fixed resolver can exploit the explicit structured representation on the sampled diagnostic rows. | That opaque notes contain the same semantics or that AST and opaque lanes test the same construct. |
| All 3,414 scientific calls complete | The exact staged roster executed. | An independent sample size of 3,414; calls are nested measurements, not experimental units. |

The narrow claim sentence should be no stronger than:

> On the exact registered PCFL-Compose DEV roots, the pinned prompted resolver
> made prospective external-text commitments, changed its Dream-2 artifact
> after the registered public A observation, and changed selected later actions
> under the registered frozen-corpus interventions, while the matched
> independent alarm remained descriptive.

Even that sentence is conditional on contract validity. The present authored
bundle is proposal-only and has a separate blocking pre-intake audit; its
current bytes are not ready to run.

## Exact interpretation of a valid negative

A negative must be localized to the first failed arrow. It cannot be promoted
into a broad theoretical falsifier.

| First failure | Honest conclusion | Forbidden conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Stage-0 construct, schema, visibility, maximum-fixture, seed, or ledger failure | The registered instrument or executable contract is invalid. | The model, memory, dreaming, or thesis failed. |
| Dream-1 prospective commitment failure | The exact prompted writer cannot reliably emit the required candidate object under the supplied event/extractor interface and cap. | Agents cannot form prospective memories. |
| Dream-2 raw-A update failure | The exact writer/prompt/state representation does not reliably perform this one-step external revision. | Experiential revision or recurrent dreaming is impossible. |
| Corpus construction or read failure | The exact external representation, mechanical Sleep/index, and reader contract do not provide a usable target-time interface. | Parametric memory, learned compression, or LoRA failed; none was tested. |
| Authentic-versus-control action contrast fails after upstream survival | This bundled opaque-corpus/read/Think/action interface lacks demonstrated causal action value on the selected cell. | Memory cannot improve action. The failure may live in authoring, lexical access, target Think, parsing, or execution. |
| q alarm fires | The specificity construction or an undeclared information channel is suspect; the factor result is not clean. | q learned the factor, or the h result becomes stronger. |
| AST succeeds while opaque behavior is weak | An explicit schema is easier for the fixed resolver than the opaque channel under these unmatched interfaces. | The opaque note is semantically false or that structured memory is generally superior. |
| Stage 1 fails and Stage 2 is not triggered | The registered spending gate stopped. | Untouched-root replication failed; those cells were never run. |

In particular, a v2 null does not update the central lifetime/compression claim
very much. Its likelihood under "Dream--LoRA--Think works only after learned
parametric consolidation and post-context pressure" is entirely compatible
with failure of a pre-context external-text canary.

## Does v2 test learned experiential reasoning or only harness transport?

The most accurate answer is: **it tests a harness-mediated external-memory
behavioral circuit, which is more than byte transport but much less than learned
experiential reasoning.**

What belongs to the model:

- authoring staged Dream nodes and the terminal commitment;
- conditionally changing that artifact after raw A;
- consuming the assigned target-time memory interface;
- emitting registered actions.

What the harness supplies or fixes:

- the tiny world and exact factorization;
- a common factor-blind but semantically strong event extractor that exposes
  each public event's permutation structure;
- chronology, typed handles, state routing, capability grants, and candidate
  serialization;
- content-addressed commitment;
- mechanical Sleep storage/indexing rather than learned consolidation;
- the target menu, action parser, world execution, and offline evaluation;
- the prompts and controller policy; no within-life parameter is learned.

The OPAQUE_NOTE rule prevents the evaluator from validating the construct's
semantic content. Therefore a positive can reflect a useful lexical code,
surface regularity, or writer/reader convention rather than a connected world
model. That opacity is appropriate for avoiding a hidden semantic oracle, but
it also puts a hard ceiling on the construct claim. The structured AST lane is
a different, explicit representation and cannot retroactively validate the
opaque lane.

V2 also does not test compression in any defensible sense:

- there is no LoRA or other parametric write;
- there is no information bottleneck or retained-byte response surface;
- the world does not add unbounded fresh entropy;
- corpus length or selection is not a proof of learned compression;
- Sleep stores/indexes rather than learning a sufficient statistic;
- the whole life remains pre-context.

It does not test learned reasoning policy either. The writer and thinker are a
fixed pretrained model driven by frozen prompts and a hand-designed controller.
At most this is a prompted capability/architecture result. "Learned" becomes
eligible only when a within-life substrate or across-life controller is trained
and its contribution is isolated from the prompt/controller ceiling.

## Is the world scientifically adequate?

### Adequate for the narrow preflight

Yes. For local causal debugging it has excellent properties:

- fully enumerable hidden states and target theorems;
- prospective chronology and a genuine post-A fork;
- goal twins and exact action redirection;
- a matched independent factor;
- D1/D4 action requirements with finite menus;
- exact CPU ceilings and mutation tests;
- no need to trust an LLM scorer.

This makes it a strong unit test for chronology, memory assignment, revision,
and action coupling.

### Inadequate for the paper's scientific construct

The same properties make it inadequate as the paper world:

- **No developmental axis.** The reusable causal content is tiny and fully
  pre-context. More calls do not create more world knowledge.
- **No population claim.** Three selected root-lives/twins, one open stage and
  one conditionally untouched stage do not estimate performance over a useful
  world distribution. The 3,414 calls are deeply nested.
- **No autonomous evidence acquisition.** Source experience is fixed; target
  action cannot improve later evidence or memory.
- **No memory pressure.** Raw history, explicit text, and model-visible state
  are not forced through the post-native regime where LoRA might matter.
- **No discovery stress.** The extractor exposes an exact event-local
  permutation representation. The model is not asked to discover the ontology,
  invent a scalable schema, or decide which experiment would resolve a gap.
- **No semantic-world-model identification.** Opaque success can be a private
  code, while AST success uses an explicit answer-bearing algebra.
- **Weak behavioral ecology.** The terminal task is a deterministic synthetic
  menu/path execution, not sustained action under uncertainty, costs, partial
  observability, or policy-dependent evidence.
- **No benchmark frontier.** There are no strong frozen long-context, raw-log,
  RAG, A-MEM/linked-memory, reflection, explicit sufficient-statistic,
  direct-QA LoRA, batch post-training, or resource-matched competitors.
- **No revision depth.** One raw-A update is not durable reconsolidation across
  multiple confirmations and contradictions.
- **No external validity.** A single algebra/template/renderer family cannot
  support generic agent-memory, SOTA, or natural-world language.

The world is therefore scientifically adequate for "does this exact circuit
have a pulse?" and inadequate for "does the organism improve as it lives?"

## Claims still missing after a perfect v2 pass

### Core Paper-1 claims

1. **Post-native persistence:** useful memory survives after decisive source
   evidence is outside the exact native model-visible envelope.
2. **Continued acquisition:** each later checkpoint adds genuinely new causal
   mappings, and action on new-cohort targets does not plateau immediately.
3. **Old retention:** performance on early cohorts remains noninferior while
   new knowledge is added.
4. **Cross-era composition:** fixed-budget action combines old and recent
   knowledge, with independently certified binding depth and no alternate
   shortcut.
5. **Causal authentic binding:** complete presealed cuts reduce value and
   authentic-versus-twin bindings redirect behavior in the predicted world,
   including a substrate-level intervention for LoRA rather than citation
   masking alone.
6. **Target-blind experiential compilation:** a named compiler class converts
   only the source life into useful memory without outcome-conditioned corpus
   or target admission.
7. **Same-corpus parametric transport:** the identical useful compiled corpus
   is compared as explicit text and LoRA under a substrate-by-reader
   factorial, including candidate-only, no/wrong/twin adapter, and unaided
   read controls.
8. **Strong-baseline response surface:** exact named implementations of native
   context, raw history/RAG, reflection/summary, linked memory/A-MEM, explicit
   graph/program, direct-QA LoRA, and batch post-training are compared under
   legitimate resource policies.
9. **Lifetime statistics:** whole world-life/twin pairs are the independent
   units; at least three powered strictly post-native checkpoints support
   separate acquisition, retention, composition, growth, and endpoint
   estimands with simultaneous uncertainty and all failures retained.
10. **Resource truth:** retained bytes, index/candidate bytes, model-visible
    tokens, training views, precision, optimizer state, query work, calls,
    FLOPs, latency, cache, and amortization denominator are all counted.

### Claims that require later, separate rungs

11. **Compression:** either total incremental information-bearing state grows
    sublinearly at preregistered noninferior action value, or a prospectively
    committed schema improves a fresh sparse cohort beyond all local atoms.
    Fixed rank alone is not evidence.
12. **Autonomous structural discovery:** a learned or genuinely autonomous
    dream policy proposes the useful relations, rather than receiving a
    supplied extractor, exhaustive candidate enumeration, or an exact
    verifier in cognition.
13. **Learned experiential reasoning:** a learned controller or compiler beats
    its frozen prompted ceiling on held-out lives, with prompt/controller and
    memory-substrate contributions separated.
14. **A4 revision:** supported old beliefs are durably reversed or repaired by
    later contradictory evidence without erasing unrelated knowledge.
15. **Complete flywheel:** a memory intervention changes an evidence-acquisition
    action; the resulting different public evidence changes later memory; that
    memory improves a later action or return.
16. **External validity:** the effect survives held-out generator variants,
    skins/ontologies, and eventually at least one standard or independently
    designed interactive environment.
17. **Saturation language:** each named baseline satisfies its own prospective
    equivalence-style plateau rule on its legitimate resource frontier.
    PCFL superiority does not prove baseline saturation.

## Minimal staged experiment ladder after v2

The ladder below is intentionally sequential. A failed arrow stops spending on
later arrows; a pass never automatically grants the next authority.

### Rung 0 — v2 as a repaired DEV kill switch

Run only after the exact schema/prose, actor visibility, content-capability,
seed/common-random-number, maximum-fixture, ledger, and claim-boundary defects
in the pre-intake audit are repaired and freshly reviewed. Preserve its two
stages and terminal `HUMAN_REQUIRED`. Do not add optional panels or call its
conditional Stage 2 a replication.

**Decision:** failure localizes an interface; pass justifies designing the next
world. Neither outcome is paper evidence.

### Rung 1 — transition-path compiled-text action canary

Use the already proposed transition preflight shape: fixed public action--
outcome source deck, target-blind transition atoms, bounded dependent reverse
reads, and a physically executed four-move old/recent path. Include target-only,
crossed twin, lagged snapshot, flat bag, exact graph, decisive cuts, and matched
shams. Six calibration pairs remain calibration; calls are not `n`.

**Gate:** proceed only if authentic text causes valid path execution and the
directional twin/lag/cut controls behave as registered. This earns only text
semantic-use feasibility, not connected learning.

### Rung 2 — text-first PCFL-Stream post-native falsifier

Build one factorized multi-cohort stream where every checkpoint introduces new
independent mappings. Derive the exact native envelope from tokenizer plus all
visible state. Use one pre-native reference and at least three powered strictly
post-native checkpoints chosen for information growth and feasibility, not
marketing powers of two. Preseal all lives, targets, depths, age cohorts, and
failures before support or compilation is observed.

First run only explicit compiled text plus the decisive nonparametric controls:
no memory, honest native context/truncation, exact sufficient-statistic graph,
raw RAG, linked/A-MEM-style memory, and summary/reflection. Score depth 1 and 4
as primaries, and score new acquisition, old retention, and cross-era
composition separately under constant query/reason/action caps.

**Gate:** stop if compiled text has no causal action value, if unique supported
knowledge does not grow, if old value collapses, if cross-era behavior survives
complete memory cuts, or if the reader scans the whole life. A positive here is
the first material evidence for the architecture's post-native action premise.

### Rung 3 — same-corpus LoRA transport sentinel

At one preregistered post-native checkpoint, take the exact causally useful
frozen corpus from Rung 2 and cross substrate with reader: explicit text versus
LoRA, each under matched direct and recognition-assisted access where
executable. Add candidate-only/no-adapter, wrong-life and twin-life adapters,
direct trajectory-to-QA LoRA, raw-history LoRA, authentic/twin binding, and a
small decisive-versus-sham substrate-cut panel. Match initialization, example
order, exposure, training seed, resolver, target sets, and source lives.

**Gate:** if LoRA does not preserve the text result under the registered
interface, stop the parametric claim. If only recognition-assisted LoRA works,
claim transport through that named reader, not unaided parametric recall.

### Rung 4 — locked powered fixed-source Paper-1 confirmation

Only after Rungs 2--3 identify healthy cells, freeze a new confirmation split
and one feasible primary surface. Use whole world-life/twin pairs, blinded or
conservative power, failure-inclusive intention-to-run accounting, and
simultaneous inference. Confirm:

- post-native new acquisition;
- old retention;
- cross-era fixed-budget action composition;
- authentic binding causality;
- same-corpus text-versus-LoRA transport;
- exact named-baseline comparisons;
- retained-state and compute frontiers.

The highest honest claim from this rung is a finite-range, fixed-source/off-
policy synthetic result: compiled per-life memory improved action over the
tested post-native PCFL range. It is paper-ready only with held-out lives,
strong baselines, exact resource disclosure, and independent reproduction. It
is still not the complete self-learning flywheel.

### Rung 5 — prospective schema/compression study

Run this only if compression is scientifically central. Give cohort descriptors
a hidden reusable regime law, require the dreamer to commit later-cohort
predictions before confirmation, and evaluate a fresh sparsely observed cohort
where local atoms leave one decisive mapping unknown. Compare atomic-only,
explicit symbolic schema, leaked post-target schema, descriptor/mapping twins,
binding shuffle, text, and LoRA. Count exact incremental information-bearing
state and require noninferior action value.

**Gate:** if atomic memory matches the schema, or total state grows linearly,
claim storage/transport rather than compression.

### Rung 6 — on-policy action--experience--memory flywheel

Create randomized memory-assignment clones at a state where the next action
chooses which evidence to acquire. Authentic, null, shuffled, and twin memory
must induce measurably different information-seeking actions; those actions
must cause different public observations; the observations must alter later
compiled memory; and that memory must improve a later held-out action or return.
Then distill the successful prompted/exhaustive trajectories into the slow LOOP
policy and test it on unseen lives against the frozen prompt/controller ceiling.

This is the first rung that can support the full self-improving-agent thesis.

## “Run v2” versus “paper-ready”

| Standard | Run v2 | Paper-ready |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific purpose | Kill dead chronology/revision/action interfaces early. | Establish a distinctive, reproducible lifetime-action result. |
| Knowledge scale | Tiny, fixed, pre-context. | Genuinely new causal information at multiple powered post-native checkpoints. |
| Memory | External opaque text plus mechanical store/index. | Target-blind compiled text and matched per-life LoRA, with explicit attribution. |
| Learning | Frozen prompted capability. | At minimum per-life parametric write; learned controller claims require a later isolated study. |
| Action | Selected deterministic DEV action. | New/old/cross-era fixed-budget action value, causal cuts, and eventually action-selected evidence. |
| Baselines | Local target/corpus/null diagnostics. | Exact context, raw/RAG, linked memory, reflection, explicit graph, direct LoRA, and batch-posttraining policies. |
| Statistics | Exact-root descriptive gate; calls are nested. | Powered world-life/twin units, held-out lives, separate estimands, simultaneous uncertainty. |
| Compression | Not tested. | Only a complete information frontier or prospective schema earns the term. |
| Claim | Non-headline DEV descriptor. | Finite-range fixed-source paper at Rung 4; full flywheel only at Rung 6. |

## Stop rules for scientific value

- Do not spend another full model roster merely to improve v2's DEV success
  rate. Once it gives a clean yes/no, its marginal paper value is exhausted.
- Do not train LoRA on a corpus that has not first caused useful action as text.
- Do not call increasing tokens, replays, or calls a growing lifetime unless
  unique supported causal information also grows.
- Do not call a post-context endpoint a scaling curve; require at least three
  powered strictly post-native points.
- Do not call fixed rank compression without total information-state accounting
  and noninferior value.
- Do not call fixed-source later-action improvement self-improvement; the full
  flywheel requires memory-caused evidence acquisition.
- Do not infer a learned world model from opaque-note behavior alone.
- Do not let a prompted/exhaustive controller silently become the claimed
  learned dream policy.
