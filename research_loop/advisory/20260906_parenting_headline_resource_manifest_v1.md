# Parenting headline resource/statistics manifest v1

Date: 2026-09-06

Status: **design-only resource advisory**. This document authorizes no
architecture change, implementation, task or target enumeration, model call,
adapter fit, GPU use, SSH/external access, or scientific claim. Every number
below is a proposed bound for the next exact architecture-change artifact. It
must still pass the complete `AGENTS.md` deliberation, exact-byte human
ratification, implementation/review, and pre-GPU gates.

## Executive recommendation

Use the one-parent/one-child, parent-absent deployment experiment as the sole
headline and cost it as one matched four-cell root:

| childhood | deployment parametric writes off | deployment parametric writes on |
|---|---|---|
| unparented matched practice | `U0` | `U1` |
| one-to-one parented practice | `P0` | `P1` |

The paper-facing pair is `P1` versus `U0`; the scientific primary endpoint is
the within-root interaction

```text
D_r = [gAUC(P1) - gAUC(P0)] - [gAUC(U1) - gAUC(U0)].
```

Use the following proposed minimum protocol:

- `J=4` one-to-one parenting cycles;
- two target-blind hidden-rule practice tasks per cycle and childhood path;
- `E=48` unique-within-life CompilerGym deployment programs in three eras of
  16;
- parametric deployment writes after programs 16, 32, and 48 (`K=3`);
- clean parent-absent probes at deployment entry and those three cuts;
- `Q=8` sealed forward-transfer probe programs per cut;
- rank-8, all-layer, response-only LoRA, clean-base cumulative rebuilds;
- one fixed regular-resource layer in every deployment cell: the same tools,
  skills, files, active textual/external memory interface, context limit,
  clock, task opportunities, and public outcome contract; and
- equal generated-token allocations, not fixed action counts. Actions remain
  available until the token allocation is exhausted, subject only to a high
  fail-safe operation maximum.

Run two development roots, then four spending-pilot roots that are forever
excluded from confirmation. Begin confirmation with 12 fresh roots under a
sealed variance-only reducer. Without exposing arm means or signs, choose a
total confirmation size from `{20,32,48}`. Use 20 only for exceptionally low
root-interaction variance, 32 for moderate variance, and 48 only with a lease
extension and a measured fast all-in runtime. If the variance is too high for
48, do not call a smaller sample powered.

At `J=4`, `K=3`, the recommended matched-practice design costs exactly 14
adapter fits per headline root. Including three treatment-neutral heat fits,
a four-root writer canary with four trained cells per root, two development
roots, and four excluded spending roots gives:

| confirmation roots | total fits | headline logical samples, dev+pilot+confirmation | maximum generated output tokens, dev+pilot+confirmation |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 20 | 383 | 92,352 | 14,656,512 |
| 32 | 551 | 134,976 | 21,421,056 |
| 48 | 775 | 191,808 | 30,440,448 |

The call/token values exclude the small writer/heat canaries, which add a
proposed maximum of 500 logical samples and 97,024 output tokens. The input
token numbers below are hard safety ceilings, not expected consumption.

The submission-critical resource gate is not “rank 8 should be fast.” It is a
measured four-cell root wall time. On eight A40s, scheduling two four-GPU roots
per wave, require all-in p95 no greater than six hours/root and no greater than
24 occupied A40-hours/root. That makes 20 confirmation roots approximately ten
waves or 60 wall hours after confirmation starts. Thirty-two roots cross the
present September 14 lease boundary under that conservative bound; 48 roots
cannot produce a September 16 result unless the p95 falls to four hours and
compute is bound through at least September 18.

## 1. What this manifest does and does not buy

A passing interaction can support the bounded statement that, under one
frozen one-to-one process-parent policy, parenting improved how much a child
benefited from later personal parametric writes on fresh CompilerGym programs.
It does not by itself establish:

- a population of parents or a classroom;
- general human parenting;
- connected or compressed internal knowledge;
- novel strategy invention;
- improvement across arbitrary environments;
- asymptotic self-improvement; or
- a LoRA advantage over every active textual-memory or batch-training method.

Connectedness/compression remains a separate terminal controlled-world
carrier assay. It must not consume the parenting roots or delay the headline.
A final LEAFE-style batch write or same-corpus textual carrier may be added as
a terminal diagnostic only after the four-cell headline artifacts are safe.

## 2. Independent root and anti-pseudoreplication contract

One independent root contains all of the following:

1. one base/tokenizer/tool-template revision and one root-keyed random stream;
2. two isolated childhood branches from matched initialization: parented `P`
   and unparented `U`;
3. each branch's complete practice trajectory, writer corpus, adapter
   optimization, and resulting childhood checkpoint;
4. a byte-identical fork of the `P` checkpoint into `P0/P1` and of the `U`
   checkpoint into `U0/U1`;
5. four isolated on-policy deployment trajectories over the same assigned
   program opportunities and probe panels; and
6. one root-level interaction value `D_r`.

The following are repeated measurements and **never increase `n`**:

- the two hidden-rule tasks inside one parenting cycle;
- parent messages, child restatements, or admitted rows;
- CompilerGym programs, actions, tool calls, or public outcomes;
- the four deployment checkpoints;
- repeated decoding samples;
- adapter-off probes; and
- the four cells themselves.

Programs may be reused as fixed blocked items across roots if the available
offline CompilerGym pool is finite, but then inference is conditional on that
frozen program panel. Prefer a hash-bound program universe large enough to
sample a fresh 48-program wake deck and eight-program probe deck per root,
without replacement within a life. All four cells of a root receive the same
assigned deck and counter-keyed RNG opportunities; realized actions and
outcomes may diverge on-policy.

The parent is a fixed policy, not a sampled parent population. Multiple roots
generalize over separately raised child/task/writer realizations under that
one parent policy. If Rohin supplies the parent responses, the claim remains
conditional on Rohin's frozen protocol. If only one Rohin-parented child is
run, it is a case study regardless of its number of programs or probes.

### RNG pairing without false identity

Use random counters keyed by
`(root, childhood, deployment-write, phase, task, opportunity, sample)`.
Corresponding cells receive the same random uniform opportunity where the
call exists. Do not use one sequential global RNG: treatment-induced extra
calls would shift every later draw. The current `VLLMBackend` ignores its
offered seed, so it cannot execute this design unchanged.

## 3. The nursery: exact proposed scale

Use `J=4` cycles. Four cycles are the smallest plausible childhood that can
practice more than one reasoning disposition and still finish before the
deadline. One cycle has:

1. an initial target-blind hidden-rule task;
2. one process review: a parent correction in `P`, a neutral child-only
   self-review in `U`;
3. one child restatement/reflection;
4. one separate homologous application task;
5. one child-authored successor/dream state;
6. public-world admission of supported continuations; and
7. one cumulative clean-base sleep fit per childhood path.

The fixed parent may adaptively select one correction from a frozen
process-only vocabulary after seeing the child's public trace. That selection
is part of the treatment and the leakage audit. It may not emit a compiler
term, pass, program identity, action argument, hidden score, or reference
answer. The unparented self-review gets no parent text and no dummy semantic
lesson. Child generated-token allocation is matched; parent tokens are an
additional teaching resource and are reported separately.

Candidate dispositions should be frozen before source generation: prediction,
surprise-triggered discrimination, scope, thought-to-action conversion,
recall-before-rederivation, reversible context reconciliation, and stopping
when marginal progress per token collapses. Four cycles do not guarantee four
different dispositions; the parent policy chooses from this vocabulary and
the realized selection histogram is descriptive.

### Why the unparented childhood should also have `J` fits

There are two scientifically different factorials:

**A. Matched-practice/writer control (recommended).** Both `P` and `U` receive
the same task opportunities, dream opportunities, write cadence, optimizer
budget, and ordinary rehearsal. `P` receives the one-to-one correction; `U`
must assign credit from its own trace. Fits per root are

```text
F_root = 2J + 2K.
```

At `J=4`, `K=3`, `F_root=14`. This isolates the added information and credit
assignment supplied by the parent. `U0` is a strong matched unparented control,
not a pristine never-practiced base. In the paper it should be called the
“matched unparented ordinary agent,” not silently described as untouched.

**B. Untouched ordinary control.** Only the parented child practices/writes
before deployment. Fits per root are

```text
F_root = J + 2K = 10.
```

This produces the simplest visual contrast against a cold ordinary model, but
it bundles parent information with eight extra practice tasks, `J` extra LoRA
optimizations, and an inherited external-learning history. Its interaction
identifies a developmental package, not parenting. Under the present four-cell
limit there is no cell that removes those confounds.

Therefore use A for the confirmatory interaction. An untouched base may be
reported as a no-fit descriptive floor, but it is not another inferential arm
and cannot replace `U0`. This recommendation explains why the fit arithmetic
contains `2J`, even though the paper-facing story has one parented learner and
one ordinary agent.

## 4. Deployment: exact proposed scale

For each root and all four cells:

- `E=48` wake programs, unique within that life;
- three eras of 16 programs;
- personal-write decisions after 16, 32, and 48 programs;
- probes at `t={0,16,32,48}`;
- `Q=8` sealed forward-transfer programs at every probe;
- no probe response, score, external-memory row, or model output returns to
  the life; and
- all four cells retain their ordinary active textual/external memory and
  context-management affordances throughout deployment.

At a cut, all four cells receive the same opportunity to dream/compile their
own realized public experience. `P0/U0` may use the validated successor state
and ordinary external memory, but their proposed parametric rows are sealed
and discarded. `P1/U1` train a cumulative rank-8 adapter from the clean base
using their own accepted corpus plus the frozen rehearsal packet. This makes
“writes off” mean personal parametric writes off, not thought, dream, context
management, files, or memory off.

`P0` and `P1` must enter deployment from identical parented-checkpoint bytes;
`U0` and `U1` must enter from identical unparented-checkpoint bytes. Entry
probes happen before any deployment write. The two members of each pair should
therefore agree at entry up to a registered deterministic-serving tolerance.

## 5. Generated-token and logical-call manifest

The allocation below is deliberately finite. A “logical sample” is one model
completion for one prompt/item. Batching changes engine dispatches, not logical
samples or token accounting. Early `DONE` burns the remainder of that task's
allocation for fairness accounting; it does not trigger dummy generation.
Actual generated tokens, input tokens, tool calls, engine dispatches, occupied
GPU time, and wall time must all be reported.

### Per-task bounds

| request class | instances per root | max logical samples/instance | aggregate output allocation/instance | input cap/sample |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| nursery interactive task | 16 | 12 | 1,536 | 6,144 |
| child restatement/self-review | 8 | 1 | 192 | 6,144 |
| nursery successor/dream state | 8 | 1 | 384 | 12,288 |
| parent correction (`P` only) | 4 | 1 | 256 | 8,192 |
| deployment wake program | 192 | 12 | 2,048 | 6,144 |
| deployment dream/compile | 12 | 1 | 768 | 12,288 |
| clean probe program | 128 | 8 | 1,024 | 6,144 |

The nursery count is `2 childhood paths * 4 cycles * 2 tasks = 16`.
Deployment wake is `4 cells * 48 programs = 192`. Probe is
`4 cells * 4 cuts * 8 programs = 128`.

### Per-root maxima

| class | logical samples | generated output tokens | input tokens, hard ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| nursery tasks | 192 | 24,576 | 1,179,648 |
| child review/restatement | 8 | 1,536 | 49,152 |
| nursery dream state | 8 | 3,072 | 98,304 |
| parent correction | 4 | 1,024 | 32,768 |
| deployment wake | 2,304 | 393,216 | 14,155,776 |
| deployment dream/compile | 12 | 9,216 | 147,456 |
| probes | 1,024 | 131,072 | 6,291,456 |
| **total/root** | **3,552** | **563,712** | **21,954,560** |

The tool-dispatch fail-safe maxima are 192 nursery actions, 2,304 deployment
wake actions, and 1,024 probe actions per root. These are termination/safety
bounds, not equal-action budgets. The scientific comparison is value under an
equal token allocation; valid/distinct/informative actions per actual generated
token are secondary outcomes.

The input ceiling is intentionally loose because later calls may carry active
state and retrieved text. It is not a GPU-hour estimate. An all-in canary must
measure p50/p95 input length and prefill/decode time separately for every
request class. If the terminal dream or ordinary active-memory renderer needs
more than the bound, the protocol must be re-deliberated rather than silently
truncated.

### Writer and optimizer ceiling per fit

Proposed resource closure for every rank-8 fit:

- response-only native chat/tool targets;
- sequence length at most 2,048 tokens;
- response target suffix at most 512 tokens;
- microbatch one with gradient accumulation bound in the exact runner;
- no more than 128 optimizer positions;
- no more than 262,144 attended train tokens and 65,536 labeled response
  tokens;
- bf16, all `q/k/v/o` and MLP projections, fixed adapter initialization law;
- one of `{1e-6,3e-5,1e-4}` selected by the treatment-neutral writer canary,
  then frozen; and
- cumulative clean-base rebuild, never an unregistered in-place continuation.

The 128-position ceiling is a resource bound, not evidence that 128 is the
right dose. The neutral canary may select a smaller exact number before target
generation. It may never enlarge 128. Missing treatment-specific rows are not
filled with invented successes; use only the fixed generic rehearsal packet
to equalize optimizer work, and report treatment-specific accepted/labeled
tokens separately.

The legacy rank-16 fits measured `0:42` at sleep 32, `3:47` at sleep 256, and
`9:35` at sleep 736 from `COMPILED` to `DONE`. They do not measure this writer,
rank, mask, or fixed-step schedule. Require a fresh nursery-size, cut-16, and
cut-48 rank-8 timing canary. Proposed gates are:

- fit process start through hashed `DONE`: p95 at most eight minutes and no
  fit above ten minutes;
- serving shutdown through fit, save, fresh mount, and canary response: p95 at
  most 12 minutes and no lifecycle above 15 minutes; and
- nonfinite loss, zero-label rows, missing EOS, action-schema damage, or a
  failed mount is a stop, not a treatment-specific retry.

## 6. Exact fit arithmetic

Let:

- `H=3` treatment-neutral heat fits;
- `W=4` writer-canary roots with four trained writer cells each (`16` fits);
- `D=2` full-surface development roots;
- `B=4` full-surface spending roots, excluded from confirmation;
- `N in {20,32,48}` confirmation roots;
- `J=4` nursery write cycles; and
- `K=3` deployment write cuts.

For the recommended matched-practice design:

```text
fits/root = 2J + 2K = 14
F_total(N) = H + 4W + (D+B+N)(2J+2K)
           = 19 + 14(N+6).
```

| phase | fits |
|---|---:|
| treatment-neutral heat | 3 |
| writer floor, four roots x four trained cells | 16 |
| two development roots | 28 |
| four excluded spending roots | 56 |
| 20-root confirmation | 280 |
| **total through N=20** | **383** |

The totals are 551 fits at `N=32` and 775 at `N=48`.

If Rohin instead ratifies the scientifically weaker untouched-ordinary
childhood, the formula is:

```text
fits/root = J + 2K = 10
F_total_untouched(N) = 19 + 10(N+6),
```

or 279, 399, and 559 total fits at `N=20,32,48`. The saved fits buy a simpler
headline but lose identification of parent information versus extra nursery
practice/optimization. Do not choose this variant merely because it is
cheaper.

At an arithmetic 3--10 minutes per fit, the matched totals correspond to:

| N | fit-only A40-hours at 3 min | fit-only A40-hours at 10 min |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 20 | 19.2 | 63.8 |
| 32 | 27.6 | 91.8 |
| 48 | 38.8 | 129.2 |

Three minutes is not established for the proposed writer; ten minutes is a

## 7. Endpoint and interaction statistics

For probe program `q`, define value as the best nonnegative relative LLVM IR
instruction reduction reached under the fixed probe token allocation:

```text
v_q = max(0, (I_base - I_best) / I_base).
```

Let `V_c(t)` be the mean over the root's eight sealed probe programs in cell
`c` at deployment cut `t`. Define gain from that cell's own entry:

```text
G_c(t) = V_c(t) - V_c(0).
```

At equally spaced cuts `0,16,32,48`, normalized trapezoidal gain-AUC is:

```text
gAUC(c) = [2G_c(16) + 2G_c(32) + G_c(48)] / 6.
```

The primary root value is the single interaction `D_r` at the top of this
document. This baseline adjustment prevents a higher parented entry score from
being mislabeled as a higher learning rate. It does not completely remove
floor/ceiling effects. Before confirmation, choose a target-blind program panel
whose development entry values leave material headroom, then retain every
assigned confirmation root. Report a predeclared headroom-normalized
sensitivity analysis; never exclude a root because parenting moved its entry.

Primary success requires:

1. the two-sided 95% root-level Student interval for mean `D_r` excludes zero
   on the positive side;
2. the point estimate is at least the registered SESOI `0.05`; and
3. typed free-action compliance and typed-forced proposal value remain within
   their registered non-erasure margins.

This wording means “statistically positive with a point estimate of at least
0.05,” not “the entire confidence interval exceeds 0.05.” The SESOI is five
percentage points of average held-out IR reduction over the finite deployed
lifetime and should be rechecked against development oracle-minus-floor span
before target sealing.

Secondary, not co-primary:

- early interaction at program 16;
- `gAUC(P1)-gAUC(U0)` system contrast;
- `gAUC(U1)-gAUC(U0)` unparented online-write effect;
- `gAUC(P1)-gAUC(P0)` parented online-write effect;
- terminal forward transfer and backward retention;
- calibration and surprise-to-discriminating-action latency; and
- valid, distinct, informative actions per generated token.

Do not promote a favorable secondary endpoint after a failed primary. A
positive system contrast without positive `D` is a useful architecture result,
not evidence that parenting improved the ability to learn.

## 8. Blinded variance rule and sample-size options

The four spending roots test plumbing, direction, and cost only. They are too
few to estimate interaction variance and are never pooled into confirmation.

Prebind 48 confirmation root identities and their order. Run the first 12 as
an internal blinded variance tranche. A hash-frozen reducer may read cell
labels internally to compute `D_r`, but releases only:

- root count and missingness;
- the centered sum of squares or `s_D`;
- an 80% one-sided upper confidence bound `U80(sigma_D)`;
- resource totals; and
- one of the three predeclared sample-size buckets.

It must not release any cell mean, interaction mean/sign, root value, task
value, learning curve, or behavior trace until `N` is locked. Under the usual
variance approximation,

```text
U80(sigma_D) = s_D * sqrt((n0-1) / chi2_0.20,n0-1),  n0=12.
```

Choose:

| variance-only result | final N | interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| `U80 <= 0.075` | 20 | about 80% sensitivity to `delta=0.05` under the planning approximation |
| `0.075 < U80 <= 0.100` | 32 | moderate-variance confirmation |
| `0.100 < U80 <= 0.120` | 48 | high-variance expansion, compute permitting |
| `U80 > 0.120` | stop powered interaction claim | 48 is unlikely to resolve the registered SESOI; do not pretend 20/32 is enough |

These thresholds are planning approximations, not guarantees. They inherit
the small-tranche normal/variance assumptions; publish the final sensitivity
curve. Since the rule uses only variance and missingness, the first 12 roots
may remain in the final analysis. Any unblinded look at effect direction before
the bucket is locked invalidates that reuse.

For reference, prior resource work found that at `N=20`, a true 0.05 effect
has roughly 80% power only near `SD(D)=0.075`; `N=32` is near 79--80% at
`SD(D)=0.10`. A four-cell difference-of-differences can easily be noisier than
either simple contrast. The three-life v6 record—fading, plateau, and action-
dialect collapse—is reason not to assume a small SD.

No program, checkpoint, twin, action, model sample, or adapter restart may be
counted as an additional child. If the fixed parent is stochastic, its
root-specific response is part of the child-life realization; it is not a
second experimental unit.

### Missing data and restarts

- Invalid or malformed actions receive their registered public failure value;
  they are outcomes, not missing data.
- A root with a post-dispatch process/integrity failure remains assigned and
  missing; it is not replaced after outcomes are visible.
- A process may resume idempotently only from a pre-request committed boundary
  proving no model response or tool action was produced for that opportunity.
- More than one missing root in the first 12 variance tranche stops sample-size
  selection; more than 5% root missingness blocks a general interaction claim.
- Technical reserve is time/storage reserve, not permission to rerun a bad
  scientific trajectory.

## 9. Pilot spending gate

After implementation review and the writer/timing canaries, run exactly two
development roots. Their results may tune implementation and prompts only
inside the already ratified development scope. Seal the final protocol and
then run exactly four new spending roots.

The four-root spending pilot passes only if:

1. all four cells complete for all four roots with zero provenance, split,
   target-leakage, adapter-mount, action-routing, or external-resource parity
   failure;
2. at least three of four `D_r` values are positive and the mean `D` is
   positive;
3. the parent is absent by byte audit at every deployment call;
4. `P0/P1` and `U0/U1` entry checkpoints and probe opportunities match as
   specified;
5. free typed-action compliance and typed-forced proposal quality clear their
   preregistered guards; and
6. the all-in p95/cost projection fits the selected confirmation path and
   lease.

This is a spending decision, not evidence. Do not require mean `D>=0.05` in
four roots; that threshold would select a lucky pilot. The original
adjudication's stronger 0.05 pilot threshold should be retired in the later
normative proposal for this reason. Pilot roots remain public development
artifacts and are never pooled into a confirmatory estimate.

## 10. GPU schedule and lease gates

The existing A40 script names eight A40s and a lease through 2026-09-14, but
the exact end timestamp/timezone and any extension are not bound. Existing
v6.1 jobs must not be preempted or treated as available capacity.

### Safe scheduling topology

- Nursery: two GPUs/root, one for each isolated childhood branch; the fixed
  parent may share the `P` engine if the model/checkpoint permits.
- Deployment: four GPUs/root, one isolated cell life/GPU.
- Node: two roots concurrently across eight GPUs.
- Adapter fitting occurs on the owning cell GPU after its serving engine is
  transactionally released and before a fresh mount; do not leave orphaned
  engine children.
- Batching may combine tasks within one cell/phase, never ledgers or external
  memory across roots/cells.

At the proposed six-hour p95/root bound, confirmation requires approximately:

| N | two-root waves | confirmation wall bound | approximate finish from Sep 11 00:00 PT |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 20 | 10 | 60 h | Sep 13 12:00 PT |
| 32 | 16 | 96 h | Sep 15 00:00 PT |
| 48 | 24 | 144 h | Sep 17 00:00 PT |

These are queue-free continuous bounds after confirmation begins and exclude
paper analysis. The 48-root path finishes by September 15 only if all-in p95
falls to four hours/root. Critical-path root wall, aggregate occupied A40
hours, and fit-only A40 hours are separate receipts.

### Calendar gates

1. **By Sep 8:** exact architecture/resource bytes ratified; code reviewed;
   local cached model and all program assets verified; exact GPU UUIDs,
   competing jobs, lease end timestamp/timezone, preemption policy, and owner
   recorded.
2. **By Sep 9:** neutral writer and early/terminal timing canaries pass; two
   development roots finish; no scientific root has been exposed before the
   frozen manifest.
3. **By Sep 10:** four spending roots finish and the protocol/reducer hashes
   seal. A failure here kills the ambitious interaction run for the current
   deadline.
4. **Sep 11:** start the prebound confirmation root order. No efficacy look
   before the 12-root blinded variance bucket locks.
5. **By Sep 14:** under the current lease, require a 12-hour safety buffer
   before the exact lease end. No path may rely on “lease through Sep 14”
   without its timestamp.
6. **By Sep 16:** a positive central result, or an honest narrower/null result,
   must be immutable for the genuine September 18 abstract.

The `N=20` path is the only conservative path compatible with the current
lease, and only if confirmation starts by Sep 11 and the six-hour p95 gate
passes. `N=32` requires an extension/replacement through at least Sep 16.
`N=48` requires bound compute through at least Sep 18 plus p95 no greater than
four hours/root if the result is needed by Sep 16.

### Resource promotion inequality

Before launch, for selected `N`, require:

```text
ceil(N/2) * p95_root_wall
  + queued_preconfirmation_wall
  + 20% contingency
  <= bound_lease_time_remaining - 12 hours.
```

Also require:

```text
(N + D + B) * p95_root_A40_hours
  + writer/heat canary A40 hours
  <= 0.8 * bound_remaining_A40_hours.
```

The 20%/0.8 factors are scheduling reserve, not scientific retry authority.

## 12. Parent identity resource fork

For cost closure, the tables above assume one frozen, reproducible local model
parent policy with one correction call per parenting cycle. Its exact model,
revision, prompt bytes, decoding, and visibility remain a human-owned protocol
decision.

At `J=4`, confirmation needs `4N` parent interventions: 80, 128, or 192 for
`N=20,32,48`, plus 24 across development and pilot. If Rohin is the parent and
each review/correction takes 5--15 minutes, confirmation alone requires about
6.7--20, 10.7--32, or 16--48 human hours respectively. A single Rohin-parented
child is feasible as an additional mechanistic case; it cannot replace the
repeated fixed-parent confirmation.

This fork must be ratified before nursery source generation. Switching from a
model parent to Rohin after viewing results creates a different treatment and
requires a new labeled study.

## 13. Kill gates and claim-preserving fallbacks

1. **Writer floor:** no native writer moves a parent-absent interface habit
   while preserving typed-forced proposal quality -> stop; do not launch the
   reasoning nursery.
2. **Reasoning teachability:** no process disposition moves on target-blind
   application tasks after parent removal -> do not claim parenting; the
   writer result remains only an interface result.
3. **Resource closure:** rank-8 terminal fit/lifecycle or full-root p95 exceeds
   the selected path -> reduce `N` only if the blinded variance bucket permits
   it; otherwise stop, never weaken the statistics.
4. **Spending pilot:** any provenance/resource parity failure, or nonpositive
   direction in two or more roots -> stop confirmation and debug only on new
   development identities.
5. **Interaction:** `D` misses -> do not claim parenting improved learning.
   If `P1>U0`, report a bounded system win; if `U1>U0`, report per-life online
   write efficacy; if only `P0>U0`, report static parent-absent process
   transfer.
6. **Carrier mechanism:** execute only after headline artifacts are immutable.
   Its failure removes connected/compressed language but does not rewrite the
   parenting result.

## Final disposition

The revised headline is resource-plausible at 20--32 independent roots, but
only after the writer floor and one all-in root show that generation, fitting,
mounting, active external memory, and probes fit the proposed six-hour p95.
The statistic to protect is the child-root interaction, not the number of
programs, checkpoints, or actions. The scientifically preferred control pays
for `J` unparented nursery fits: that extra cost is what prevents “parenting”
from meaning “parent plus extra practice plus extra optimization.”

Freeze those exact integers and identities through the architecture workflow
before any target or confirmation output exists. If the result arrives, it is
a clean one-parent/one-child meta-learning claim. If it does not, the same
design decomposes the failure into writer, parent-absent transfer, online-write
efficacy, system performance, and interaction without inventing a success.
