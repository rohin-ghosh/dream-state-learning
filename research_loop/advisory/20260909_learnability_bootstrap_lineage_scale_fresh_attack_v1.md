# Learnability-bootstrap, lineage, and scale fresh attack v1

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: **source-only advisory and falsification map**. This is not an
architecture proposal, a selection of scaled-THINK topology, a curriculum or
bootstrap-content specification, a ratification request, an implementation
plan, or authorization for model/tokenizer/benchmark/data/adapter/GPU/network
execution. Direct Fable collaboration on scaled THINK remains pending. Every
material choice described here would still require the deliberation and exact
human-ratification path in `AGENTS.md`.

## Verdict

An initial LoRA could, in principle, amortize a useful policy for invoking
reflection across states before adaptive parenting begins. But every such LoRA
is literally post-training a policy. The scientifically separable question is
not whether it is post-training; it is whether the installed policy changes
the child's **subsequent rate or durability of learning from new, disjoint,
grounded experience and parenting**, rather than merely raising entry
performance, producing reflective prose, or copying a preferred ritual.

The minimum defensible distinction is therefore temporal and causal:

```text
installed-policy effect
    = bootstrap changes behavior before any new learning opportunity

learnability effect
    = bootstrap changes entry-adjusted gain from later new experience

parent-receptivity effect
    = bootstrap changes the incremental gain from the exact parent

personal-write mediation
    = a later child-authored SLEEP commit carries a delayed effect that is
      absent in a matched no-new-commit descendant
```

Only the second and third quantities bear on a learnability bootstrap. The
fourth is additionally needed to attribute durable transfer to subsequent
personal SLEEP rather than continued in-context instruction. A direct
bootstrap effect is not a defect, but it must be named **installed policy** and
not promoted to learning-to-learn.

The present repository does not yet support any positive bootstrap claim. E0
is open: the long-lifetime runner remains connected to the legacy bare-text
writer, and rejected candidate corpus can re-enter later cumulative builds.
The V9 repair is still a draft. The current classroom supplies only immediate
parent feedback, does not feed delayed transfer back to the parent, and does
not strictly preserve the previous child. Fable's scaled-THINK design response
is pending. These are blockers to execution, not reasons to infer a topology
or make a longer run.

## 1. Observable construct, not an internal-state story

Call a candidate bootstrap `META` and its dose/source/dialect-matched control
`SHAM`. The control must match permissible source class, unique grounded
evidence count, rendered-token dose, adapter capacity, compiler/trainer,
instruction-preservation mix, and style statistics while omitting the
candidate cross-state reflection relation. A raw-base `NONE` anchor is useful
for diagnosing generic adapter effects, but crossing all three bootstrap
levels with parenting is not the smallest causal test.

The object being tested is a **state-conditioned public operation policy**:
whether the child appropriately predicts, compares alternatives, checks a
plan, interprets an outcome, revises a named belief, preserves a prospective
intention, or stops reflecting and acts. Private chain-of-thought length and
self-descriptions are not endpoints. Public typed operations, released
actions, public outcomes, and generated-token cost are auditable endpoints.

“Amortized reflection” requires a cost claim, not simply more reflection. At
minimum `META` must move the action-value/generated-token frontier outward on
fresh states: higher action value at matched generated tokens, or no lower
value with fewer generated tokens/workspace operations. It must also avoid
excess hesitation and know when not to recurse. Without that frontier result,
the maximum phrase is “changed use of the measured reflection operations,”
not “amortized thinking.” This behavioral result still would not reveal or
prove a private internal mechanism.

“Multi-state” must mean prospectively distinct public state classes, not many
paraphrases of one prompt. The smallest meaningful set spans:

- pre-action uncertainty or route selection;
- post-outcome belief revision; and
- a later context-reset state in which a relevant intention or lesson is used
  without the original cue.

A gain confined to one of those classes is local policy tuning. Cross-family
transfer remains necessary before using even the bounded phrase “tested
metacognitive disposition.”

## 2. Smallest causal factorial and staged falsifiers

### Stage 0 — source and negative-control closure

Before any fit, bind `META`, `SHAM`, and `NONE`; their immutable birth, source
roots, semantic identities, unique-evidence counts, derivative-view counts,
tokens, writer, mask, rank/capacity, dose, and disjoint evaluation namespaces.
Reject any direct deployment answer, hidden task identifier, final-family
outcome, parent target, DREAM output, or descendant-exam artifact. Run literal,
semantic, binding-shuffle/wrong-life, and format/ritual checks. Failure ends the
candidate; it cannot be repaired by more roots.

This stage also has to state honestly what the bootstrap is. Data written by a
donor child or teacher is inherited post-training, not the newborn child's own
experience. If teacher text or teacher-produced solutions are targets, the
intervention is teacher distillation. If task-family content appears, it is a
skill bootstrap. Neither may be relabeled metacognitive receptivity.

### Stage 1 — writer and interface falsifier

Certify each candidate against its immediately preceding child or birth state
on identical seeded representative real contexts. Require exact grounded
provenance, response-only supervision in the deployment dialect, free-routing
quality, typed-forced proposal quality, retention/no-material-harm, adapter-off,
wrong-life/binding, transactional commit/rollback, and separate evidence- and
adapter-promotion decisions. Zero critical provenance, target-channel, or
lineage-binding violations are tolerable.

Loss reduction, increased probability of training rows, parseable syntax, or a
successful mount is only an upstream writer signal. It is not behavioral
extraction, reflection, transfer, or learnability.

### Stage 2 — no-learning installed-policy assay

Compare `META`, `SHAM`, and descriptive `NONE` before parenting and with later
personal writes disabled. Use unseen, non-answer-bearing states from the
allowed development namespace and score the public operation/cost frontier,
action rate, calibration, format, and ritualization.

- `META > SHAM` here estimates installed-policy content.
- `META = SHAM` does not disprove later learnability.
- Fluent reflective wording without better released actions is a failure.
- Any bootstrap-template repetition across irrelevant states is a failure of
  selectivity, even if the prose looks metacognitive.

This stage maps the entry shift that all later gain estimates must print. It
cannot establish learnability.

### Stage 3 — minimum causal development block

The smallest defensible causal block is a randomized `2 x 2` over independently
continued child roots:

| birth bootstrap | development parent |
|---|---|
| `SHAM` | absent |
| `META` | absent |
| `SHAM` | exact adaptive parent |
| `META` | exact adaptive parent |

All four cells use the same child model, THINK/DREAM/SLEEP mechanism, writer,
tools, task-opportunity distribution, total generated-token envelope, SLEEP
opportunities, promotion rule, saved ages, and final opportunities. “Parent
absent” means no parent intervention, not a weaker hidden teacher. The
parent-present cells use the same bound parent spec and adaptive transition
law; private child records are root-local. The estimand is the total exact
parent package, not correction-only causality.

For bootstrap `b` and parent condition `p`, let:

```text
E[b,p] = parent-absent score at developmental entry
G[b,p] = entry-adjusted acquisition gain/AUC on new personal experience

L_boot = G[META,absent] - G[SHAM,absent]

I_boot_parent
  = (G[META,present] - G[META,absent])
  - (G[SHAM,present] - G[SHAM,absent])
```

Interpretation is deliberately asymmetric:

- positive `E` contrast alone: installed policy or inherited competence;
- positive `L_boot`: bootstrap improved later parent-free acquisition for the
  exact mechanism and task distribution;
- positive `I_boot_parent`: bootstrap increased benefit from the exact adaptive
  parent;
- positive parent-present terminal value without positive gain: higher entry
  competence, tutoring value, or both—not learning-to-learn.

Report the full mediator chain by cell and cut: valid opportunities, child
actions/outcomes, eligible and accepted evidence identities, unique rows,
derivative views, target-type mix, labeled tokens, optimizer slots, fit/mount/
rollback outcomes, public reflection operations, and action value. A bootstrap
that merely causes more writer-admissible text may improve this exact system
through that pathway; that is a legitimate mediated package effect, not proof
of a domain-general disposition.

### Stage 4 — nested write and persistence falsifiers

At a prospectively selected pre-SLEEP cut, make paired **disposable**
descendants of each continuing child:

1. `NEW_COMMIT`: construct and mount the candidate from the already acquired,
   independently eligible personal evidence;
2. `NO_NEW_COMMIT`: perform the same rebuild/evaluation opportunity without
   the new parameter commit.

Both descendants immediately lose the parent and original waking context.
Evaluate once on fresh homologous states, then after unrelated work and a
second context reset, then on a genuinely fresh family. No result returns to
the ancestor. These paired descendants diagnose whether the latest personal
write carried the change; they are not independent population roots.

If the eventual adapter topology cannot remove only the new personal write
without also removing the birth bootstrap, then adapter-off does not identify
personal-write mediation. That limitation must be solved in the later
architecture deliberation or the personal-write claim must be withheld. This
advisory does not choose stacking, merging, composition, or any other adapter
topology.

Required downstream ordering is:

```text
new personal action/outcome
 -> independently eligible child-authored evidence
 -> candidate construction and valid commit
 -> parent-absent, context-reset changed action
 -> delayed retention
 -> fresh-family acquisition/transfer
```

Immediate post-advice improvement before a write is in-context uptake.
Immediate post-write improvement with no delayed persistence is short-lived
write transfer. Neither establishes a developmental bootstrap.

### Stage 5 — confirmation or stop

Only after Stages 0–4 survive should a separately powered, fixed-N confirmation
start from new clean independent roots and untouched task identities. The
calibration children, every age panel, and every deployment descendant are
consumed selection evidence. They cannot be counted again as confirmation.

## 3. Three lineage classes that must never be merged

### A. Canonical developmental children

A canonical child is one longitudinal learner under one exact mechanism hash.
Changing subject matter, practice instances, or governed parenting within the
bound treatment continues that child. Its append-only public evidence and
committed ages remain attributable to the same root. Promotion compares the
candidate with the **previous committed child**, never only with raw base.

The child never consumes developmental-exam or deployment-gym outcomes. A
valid failed lesson may remain grounded evidence under the frozen eligibility
rule; a rejected candidate's derived targets, dose artifacts, or ungrounded
claims do not silently re-enter later cumulative builds. Adapter-head
promotion and canonical-evidence promotion are distinct decisions.

### B. Disposable deployment-gym descendants

An exam/deployment descendant is a one-way fork from an immutable age snapshot.
Its prompts, actions, outcomes, ledgers, writes, scores, failure analyses, and
parent responses may be used only for their preregistered aggregate evaluation
purpose. They cannot update:

- the continuing child or its SLEEP corpus;
- that child's private parent record or reusable pedagogy core, except any
  explicitly preregistered aggregate development code allowed before freeze;
- bootstrap data, writer calibration, thresholds, task generation, or future
  root selection; or
- any sibling's evidence or adapter.

Repeated descendants or ages from one root are correlated measurements of one
lineage. Treating them as independent `n` is pseudoreplication.

### C. Mechanism-change sibling forks

A changed THINK, DREAM, SLEEP, compiler/trainer, response mask, eligibility
rule, rank/capacity semantics, dose law, replay law, SLEEP cadence, promotion
law, parent transition law, token/route allocation rule, or route
opening/merging/stopping rule creates a new mechanism sibling from the last
common immutable snapshot. It is not the next age of the same child.

Sibling curves may support paired mechanism comparisons because they share an
ancestor. They may not be stitched into one growth curve, pool evidence, teach
one another, share root-local parent state, or replace independent birth roots.
After parameter selection, confirmation restarts with the selected mechanism
on new roots; it does not continue the winning calibration sibling.

## 4. Scale and horizon gates

Define before observing outcomes:

- `delta_T`: smallest practically meaningful entry-adjusted downstream
  transfer effect in the primary units;
- `h_j`: noninferiority/harm margin for each protected endpoint;
- one root-level estimator and uncertainty procedure; and
- the exact immediate, delayed, and fresh-family cuts.

No universal numeric episode count follows from the sources. A defensible
scale rule is precision-based, not “try 1,024 episodes” or “write until it
works.” Use independent lineage roots as the sampling unit.

At each permitted stage, classify the primary downstream contrast using its
prospectively chosen 95% interval and the following ordered rules (the first
matching row controls):

| result | allowed next step |
|---|---|
| any protected endpoint crosses its harm rule | stop that candidate/mechanism; upstream writer success cannot rescue it |
| upper bound `< delta_T` | stop for practical futility at this scale |
| lower bound `> 0`, point estimate `>= delta_T`, all protected endpoints pass, and immediate plus delayed directions agree | eligible for one next horizon or fresh-family stage, subject to deliberation and resource gate |
| upper bound `>= delta_T` but the advance rule above is not met | add independent roots at the **same horizon** until the planned interval half-width is at most `delta_T / 2`; do not lengthen lives |
| entry effect positive but `L_boot` and `I_boot_parent` fail the transfer gate | report installed policy only; do not scale a learnability claim |

Thus, when writer provenance, mounting, absorption, or row likelihood works but
downstream transfer is absent, **the default scale authorization is zero**.
More epochs, rank, roots, parent turns, task families, model size, or lifetime
are not interchangeable repairs. If the interval is merely imprecise, only
same-horizon root replication is licensed by the rule above. If its upper bound
excludes `delta_T`, the candidate is practically futile at that scale.

At most one prospectively specified diagnostic horizon extension is
scientifically interpretable before a positive transfer signal, and only when
all of the following hold: the writer passes E0; admitted unique evidence and
valid commits increase as intended; the latest two preregistered transfer cuts
move in the same favorable direction; no protected endpoint worsens; and the
extension boundary was fixed without looking at descendant tasks. Otherwise a
longer life is horizon fishing. That diagnostic extension authorizes no claim
and is not a substitute for new independent roots.

Long-lifetime/E6 scale is a separate gate. It requires a prospectively
certified strong active-text comparator to meet its own registered local
plateau, followed by at least three fixed later checkpoints. Only root-level
positive learner late slope, slope advantage, terminal practical superiority,
and uncertainty can support the bounded statement that improvement persisted
beyond that comparator's local plateau on the tested distribution. Bootstrap
or writer signals alone never open E6.

## 5. Calibration surface, in dependency order

Calibration is developmental selection, not confirmation. Each changed update
law is a mechanism sibling, and every selected value must later be frozen.

| parameter | quantity that must be bound and reported | falsifying readout / selection rule |
|---|---|---|
| rank/capacity | exact target modules, rank semantics, tensor/artifact bytes, initialization, seed | certify representative real contexts and downstream extraction; choose the smallest passing rank, ties to smaller; a larger rank is not justified by lower training loss alone |
| dose | unique evidence identities, derivative views per identity, supervised response tokens, epochs/steps, optimizer slots, preservation mix | seek downstream transfer per unique grounded identity; stop when transfer plateaus or ritual/harm rises; never count paraphrases as new evidence |
| replay | exact old:new unique-evidence mixture, recency/coverage strata, deduplication rule, views and per-row exposures | require retention plus fresh acquisition; more replay that only raises old-row reproduction or dilutes new extraction fails |
| SLEEP cadence | evidence accrued per boundary, number of commits, cumulative-corpus identity, total labeled tokens and optimizer work | compare cadence at matched total eligible evidence and training work; frequent writes that amplify a ritual or one final batch that matches them gives no cadence benefit |
| thought budget | total generated tokens per state/action, public-operation counts, latency/workspace proxy | compare action value and information gained at matched tokens before any generous-budget package; longer traces are not success |
| routes/depth/reflection | route count, allowed sequential depth, post-outcome allocation, opening/merge/stop behavior | **deferred pending Fable**; later tests must separate breadth, depth, and post-outcome reflection and score learned stopping, revision quality, action value, and information per token |

Rank and dose can interact. The smallest informative capacity screen is two
bracketing ranks by two bracketing doses on mechanism siblings after E0, not a
one-dimensional rank race. Only if that local screen shows a passing region
should replay be varied while rank/dose stay fixed. Only then should cadence be
tested at matched cumulative evidence and optimizer work. If an interaction is
suspected, use one local two-by-two falsifier; do not launch an unconstrained
grid.

Thought allocation is last because topology remains unadjudicated. A future
design needs two different questions: a matched-total-token comparison to
identify topology, and a generous-budget package comparison to estimate
total-system usefulness. This advisory chooses no number of routes, branch
independence scheme, aggregation rule, verifier, depth, or stopping controller.

## 6. Contamination and visibility rules

1. **Ancestry:** bind immutable base, bootstrap, mechanism, parent, task split,
   and cumulative-evidence roots. An adapter from a donor or wrong life is a
   negative control, never a continuation.
2. **Bootstrap provenance:** keep ancestral bootstrap data in its own namespace
   and accounting column. It never counts as the child's personal evidence or
   as an independently discovered operation.
3. **Target boundary:** parent text may condition waking behavior but is
   loss-masked. Parent text, repetition-controller state, DREAM output, tool
   prose, raw ledgers, unsupported claims, and answer keys are not positive
   child targets, compiler conditioning, dose, replay, or gradients.
4. **Evidence versus rendering:** one public action/outcome semantic identity
   remains one evidence item. Paraphrases, replays, contrasts, or other views
   may change dose but never sample size or evidence count.
5. **Failures:** valid failed actions/outcomes are not deleted because teaching
   failed. Unsupported interpretations and derivatives of rejected candidate
   construction are quarantined; independently grounded rows follow only the
   frozen eligibility decision.
6. **Parent state:** teacher weights stay fixed. Root-local child records never
   cross children. A shared pedagogy core used in confirmation must be frozen
   from development-only evidence, and both parent-present cells receive the
   same core and exact parent permissions.
7. **Exam firewall:** descendant experiences never flow backward. Aggregate
   exam codes may update a development parent only if fixed before the exam;
   after confirmation freeze, even that path is closed or the complete
   adaptive law is predeclared.
8. **Selection firewall:** any task, age, root, parameter, stopping time, or
   parent hypothesis inspected during calibration is consumed. Confirmation
   uses new identities and new independently grown roots.
9. **Opportunity matching:** match generated tokens, tool permissions, task
   opportunities, SLEEP opportunities, labeled tokens/optimizer work where the
   causal contrast requires it, and report residual differences as mediators.
10. **No hidden topology:** temporary thought branches are not child lineages;
    data-parallel classrooms are not parallel thoughts; deployment descendants
    are not development children.

## 7. Precise maximum claims

| passing evidence | maximum defensible statement |
|---|---|
| E0/writer gates only | “The exact writer preserves the tested interface and can write supported child continuations.” |
| `META > SHAM` only before new experience | “The initial LoRA installs the tested public behavior policy.” |
| better matched-token value/cost frontier across the three registered state classes | “The initial LoRA amortizes the measured public reflection operations on these state classes.” |
| positive `L_boot` with entry printed and parent absent | “The bootstrap improves later acquisition from personal experience for this mechanism and task distribution.” |
| positive `I_boot_parent` | “The bootstrap increases the later benefit of this exact adaptive-parenting package.” |
| `NEW_COMMIT > NO_NEW_COMMIT`, parent/context absent, with delayed retention | “A subsequent personal SLEEP commit carries part of the measured downstream effect.” |
| fresh-family transfer across independently grown roots | “The effect transfers across the registered families and sampled lineages.” |

None of these outcomes alone supports connected experiential representation,
compression, goal-conditioned traversal, an expansion flywheel, universal
learning-to-learn, general intelligence, phenomenology, sentience, intrinsic
self-improvement, or superiority at increasing lifetime. Those remain the
separate E2–E6 rungs and claim gates. One selected sibling pair licenses only a
conditional case statement; general developmental effectiveness requires
multiple independently grown sibling pairs and a separately powered
confirmation.

## 8. Principal failure modes

- **Policy relabeling:** entry competence or preferred reflective prose is
  called learnability despite null entry-adjusted gain.
- **Ritual bootstrap:** the candidate creates a new repetitive template,
  excessive self-checking, indecision, or delayed action.
- **Storage/extraction substitution:** training rows become likely while new
  cues fail to recover or use the lesson.
- **Parent echo:** immediate restatement or post-advice improvement vanishes
  after context reset or a later SLEEP.
- **Task inheritance:** deployment-relevant content in the bootstrap makes
  transfer an answer/skill leak.
- **Admission mediation hidden:** `META` wins only because it emits more rows
  preferred by the writer; the mediator is suppressed and the result is called
  domain-general metacognition.
- **Rollback illusion:** a rejected adapter head is skipped but its derived
  corpus contaminates the next build.
- **Continuity laundering:** changed rank, dose, replay, cadence, or THINK rule
  is resumed under the old child's name.
- **Descendant backflow:** exam/deployment outcomes train the ancestor, parent,
  bootstrap, task selector, or stopping rule.
- **Pseudoreplication:** ages, classroom branches, episodes, or disposable
  descendants are counted as independent children.
- **Budget/topology confounding:** more routes receive more tokens and the gain
  is attributed to parallel thought; or longer traces are scored as better.
- **Horizon fishing:** lives are lengthened after each null until a favorable
  age appears.
- **Capacity/dose confounding:** a higher rank also receives more views,
  optimizer work, or replay and is credited for the combined package.
- **Frequent-write amplification:** cadence repeatedly consolidates a harmful
  habit while syntax and mount canaries continue to pass.
- **Parent adaptation leakage:** delayed exam data or another child's private
  record improves the tested parent.
- **Comparator evasion:** long-life superiority is claimed without a certified
  strong active-text baseline at a prospectively registered local plateau.

## 9. Advisory disposition

The bootstrap idea is scientifically coherent only as a staged causal
classification problem. Start by proving writer truth and mapping the direct
policy effect; then use the `META/SHAM x parent absent/present` development
block, nested disposable commit descendants, and delayed fresh-family tests.
Do not buy lifetime, model, rank, or thought scale with upstream writer signals
alone. Do not allow any calibration descendant or mechanism sibling to become
the canonical adult. Do not formalize scaled THINK until the requested Fable
collaboration is returned and separately adjudicated.
