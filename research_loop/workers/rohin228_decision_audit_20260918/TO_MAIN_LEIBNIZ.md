read-only observers remain unchanged and are not substitutes for this target.

and joining actual scorer receipt origins to those exact RESPONSE identities.

semantics: scored means ok, boolean accepted, not replayed; acceptance and

not zero. Counts are per logical journal cycle, not presumed equivalent to

retains live game feedback delivery. This worker performs no feedback relay,
inbox writes, generation, model calls, learner signals, or parent messages.

publishable report will contain hashes/record IDs/counts, not paths, hosts,
caption text or private judge/reference material. The bounded observer and
