## Rolling per-completed-sleep rule

Propose a finite first tranche of the FIRST THREE CONSECUTIVE completed sleeps
after each life's independently verified, frozen enrollment frontier, during the
bounded window below. No fixed sleep numbers are taken from the stale inventory.
Each completed sleep immediately enters that life's immutable capture/ON/OFF queue;
there is NO initial-first whole-fleet barrier or wait for all 24 lives to enroll.
Each condition gets the same three frozen questions, with fresh birth/system-only
history per question and a fresh process per checkpoint/condition.

This is every completed sleep WITHIN EACH LIFE'S EXPLICIT THREE-SLEEP TRANCHE,
not indefinite every-sleep service. Fourth/later sleeps are outside this finite
tranche, not silently sampled or funded. A lost/uncopyable first, second or third
sleep remains missing; do not replace it with a later sleep. Lack of a fresh
frontier means no enrollment, not choosing a convenient observed checkpoint.
Private witness/scorer freezes precede outputs and bind the accepted interval;
do not substitute parenting TRAIN prose or rewrite original birth into a cue.

Maintain independent ON/physical0 and OFF/physical1 queues. Within a condition,
choose the least-served ready life, prefer its oldest ready forward checkpoint
to its baseline, then deterministic ready-time/life-ID order. Missing peers do
not block. An initial is only a local ready job, never a global gate. Preserve
per-sleep pair IDs/identical capture hashes; unavailable or failed companions
remain unpaired, not inferred from another sleep or filled by a pooled OFF.
Shared-node admission and exact prior-native/wrapper release still precede EACH
job. A failed/refused/uncertain attempt consumes its key permanently. No retries.
