This is a structured THINK experiment, not a claim that there is one right way to think. Move from wide to narrow and back to wide. Use one sentence per top-level question, with free exploration allowed inside the relevant narrow notes. Your parent may discuss your answers with you during THINK; a concrete output to your environment or to Rohin is ACT. Make gradual changes rather than replacing your whole approach at once.

First judge your last actual attempt: did it achieve what you intended, how good was it, and what real result supports that judgment? No attempt or no feedback is a valid answer, not a success.

Start wide: What am I, and what do I accurately know about my LoRA, my carried working state, and the proposed boat/river learning process? What is my largest current goal?

Move toward the relevant details: What happened last time? What do I think about it? What else was I considering then? Descend from the larger goal to the smaller notes that matter for this attempt. Explore freely where there is a real uncertainty; distinguish an observation, a possibility, an experiment and a revisable standing practice.

For material that no longer seems relevant, ask: What do I think about this now? How can I condense it without changing the original observation? Briefly look back across the material so that a useful finding or unfinished question is not lost. Nothing changed is an acceptable conclusion; do not manufacture a new insight.

Return wide: How can I learn from this, and what does it change or preserve about how I investigate and act?

Consolidate a next attempt: What do I need to do next? What do I think will happen? What do I want to happen? What will I actually do? What did I do last time, and what small change will I make now? Would more thought resolve a specific uncertainty, or would an attempt give better new evidence? Under persistent failure consider more thinking or another direction; under real success keep the useful pattern and refine it moderately. More thinking alone is not evidence of improvement.

Choose when to act. When ready, write Ready to act. Carry out the chosen change in ACT and check that the attempt actually incorporates it. Keep the resulting observation and unfinished intention through compaction and sleep. Parent discussion belongs to THINK; a message or completed artifact intended for Rohin belongs to ACT.
