## Priority gaps and what is already implemented

1. **Community floor:** all C1–C5 live configs select `cadence_responses=3`, `schedule_on=response`, low reasoning effort. `gpu/orch_r153_community_parents.py:292` waits for verified rendering of an outstanding turn, then requires three new responses; silence and provider failure can consume opportunities without delivery. Neither this condition nor “parent alive” guarantees one rendered parent turn per two boundaries. The exact boundary unit and delivery/deadline requirement belong to Main's intake/Mendel's future policy, not an inference that three responses equal two boundaries.
2. **Community object budget already real:** configs fix `object_turn_limit=3`; current prompt requires stable object IDs across sleeps/retries and third-turn release. `decision` rejects exhausted IDs and requires set-aside/release on the third turn; ledger reconstructs verified rendered-parent counts. The policy says not to rename an object to reset its budget. This inspection is not a proof that semantic object relabeling is impossible. Do not replace this with an attempt counter or count tool/Rohin turns against the parent budget.
3. **English is uneven, not absent everywhere:** C1–C5 actual instructions explicitly require English for speech and rationale; their policy expressly disclaims that a script classifier proves English. Existing release-phrase validation is not a comprehensive language detector. Matched TRAIN parents say “Speak English.” Run1/pilot only “Encourage English for Rohin.” Other legacy actual prompts inspected have no explicit English-only rule. No current-language-output rate was measured.
4. **Legacy object limit missing:** generic principles recommend persistent repetition and all legacy parents allow useful silence, but inspected legacy instructions/configs have no stable-object three-delivered-turn mechanism. Community-specific limits do not silently apply to classroom, creative, support, controls, run1, pilot, or repo_reader. Changing these policies is Mendel's scope.
5. **Concrete perception teaching already present:** teach_perception birth explicitly distinguishes observations, interpretations and predictions; its contrastive parent policy asks for discriminating details and counterexamples. Support_free birth explicitly names uncertainty, perception and self-reflection. Brain_free asks the learner to reflect on actual observations rather than equate human-neuroscience vocabulary with model mechanisms. The common principles broaden P/R teaching to every inspected parent. These facts refute a blanket “never taught” statement, not the historical critique that teaching failed to transfer into behavior.
6. **Coverage/grammar remain unproven:** no new delivered-turn coverage or same-object-streak audit was run. The historical claim of community silence cannot be converted into current compliance/noncompliance from process metadata. Current parent policies do not establish that every community child received the requested one-time exact-JSON grammar lesson. Birth affordance descriptions and tools' rejection receipts are not that parent-delivery proof.
