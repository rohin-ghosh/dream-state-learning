## Independence and evidence still missing

The R232 observation fork has one explicitly bounded, completed withdrawal
cycle: THINK913 → ACT920 → LEARN932 → COMPLETE993. No new human, peer or parent
input arrived in that cycle; prior assistance remained in context. It emitted
three related caption variants; the manual outcome was no verified correction.
This is a real short withdrawal observation, not clean context or gradual
tapering. Runtime outages elsewhere do not add withdrawal samples.

The evidence supports testing concrete checking and single-object feedback,
but not declaring them the cause of a developmental improvement. For the
replications, retain distinct endpoints: delivered → rendered in THINK →
rendered/carry-through in ACT → correct requested artifact → completed sleep
with exact row eligibility → fresh unreminded transfer. Preselect checkpoints,
match tasks/exposure, count invalid transport separately, and compare trained
and frozen controls. These are audit implications, not a changed protocol or
an experiment launched by this workstream.
