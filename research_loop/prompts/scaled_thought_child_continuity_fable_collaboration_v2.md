# Fable collaboration v2 -- state trees, clean child, bootstrap, and scale

This is a direct Codex--Fable design conversation requested by Rohin. It is
not a fresh-independent review, architecture proposal, implementation request,
or execution request.

The earlier request was invalidated when Fable legitimately updated
`research_notes/IDEAS.md` and `research_loop/COORDINATION.md` with Rohin's new
directions. Read the newly hash-bound files here as one current snapshot. Do
not reuse the stale v1 source state.

Return a joint-design analysis that directly adjudicates all of the following.

1. **Two different parallelisms.** Distinguish thought-tree parallelism inside
   one state (one child forks several possible continuations and later joins)
   from data-parallel classrooms (one immutable child snapshot receives
   different experiences whose grounded evidence is pooled into one next
   child). State why neither is population replication or multiple children.
2. **State tree.** Define the minimum semantics of a state, branch, persisted
   unresolved branch, join, and return to an earlier state. Decide what the
   head child sees; what evidence/disagreement survives DREAM; what SLEEP may
   compile; and how speculative or unchosen routes avoid becoming false
   autobiographical facts.
3. **Depth, breadth, reflection, and stopping.** Decide whether sequential
   depth, parallel breadth, and post-outcome meta-review are separately
   allocated; how the child learns when to open, extend, revisit, merge,
   suspend, or terminate a route; and how guessing/estimated paths under
   uncertainty can be encouraged without training confident fabrication.
4. **Continuous thinking.** Attack the proposed no-DONE continual stream. Does
   removing a terminal token add useful quiet-wake reflection or merely
   unlimited ritual/noise? Give the narrowest safe exploratory interpretation
   and the public endpoints that distinguish it.
5. **One-child continuity and contamination.** Preserve the canonical
   developmental child across subjects and parenting changes only while the
   mechanism is unchanged. Define mechanism-change sibling forks. Treat every
   deployment/final-gym test as a one-way disposable descendant: no weights,
   memories, DREAM/SLEEP rows, outcomes, summaries, parent observations, or
   selection signals may return to the clean child. Explain what development
   exams may lawfully update before confirmation freeze.
6. **Head-node parent.** The parent has fixed weights but improves teaching
   through bounded context/files/skills/playbook and both immediate and delayed
   outcomes. Decide how one parent/head tracks concurrent classrooms for one
   child without crossing child/root boundaries or leaking final evaluation.
7. **Bootstrap pipeline.** Adjudicate the three stages: target-blind adapter
   post-training at birth -> adaptive parenting -> personal continual
   learning. Compare a structure-heavy metacognitive-flow bootstrap with a
   lived-heavy bootstrap and an active non-metacognitive control. State how a
   bootstrap can amortize state-to-state reflection without being mislabeled
   as parenting or the newborn's own experience, and what distinguishes an
   installed style/policy from improved later learnability.
8. **Scale gate.** Reconcile Rohin's “scale aligned proximal change before
   downstream emergence” principle with the counterattack's bounded rule:
   writer-only/ritual/harm gets no scale; imprecision gets more independent
   roots at the same horizon; delayed binding-sensitive personal-write change
   may buy exactly one prebound longer developmental runway; passing transfer
   moves to fresh-root confirmation. Attack whether this is too strict or too
   permissive.
9. **Plasticity and parameters.** Do not choose numeric rank, learning rate,
   dose, replay, cadence, token budget, route count, or episode count. Decide
   which are mechanism-defining (thus fork a sibling when changed), which may
   adapt within a prospectively frozen law, what evidence should reduce
   plasticity, and why “more thoughts” and “more unique experience” must be
   separately counted.
10. **Existing capability versus teaching.** Identify what chain-of-thought,
    revision, planning, emotional/social priors, and self-critique are probably
    already available in the base. Narrow the teaching target to state-to-state
    regulation, persistence, causal reflection, alternative generation,
    prospective intention, and learned stopping rather than teaching reasoning
    from scratch.
11. **Personal perspective.** Propose high-dimensional parent questions and
    causal tests that distinguish durable experience-grounded perspective from
    parent echo, lexical ritual, generic pretrained opinion, or confident
    self-description. Direct parent or DREAM text may not become positive
    personal SLEEP targets.
12. **Freeze boundary.** State what can honestly be mechanism-frozen within
    three days, what must remain developmental pedagogy, and which exact
    unresolved issue would make an expensive continuous child scientifically
    premature.

Preserve exactly THINK, DREAM, and SLEEP. The parent, head/principal, branch
router, ledger, compiler, evaluator, and merge procedure are roles or
laboratory infrastructure, not additional cognitive organs. Separate
exploratory development from confirmation and public claims.

Do not select any bootstrap corpus rows/content, model, tokenizer, benchmark,
adapter topology, rank, dose, resource count, route count, or execution plan.
The response is analysis only and authorizes no architecture proposal,
implementation, model/tokenizer execution, benchmark/data generation,
adapter/checkpoint operation, CPU/GPU/resource use, result evaluation,
scientific claim, publication, push, release, or transfer.
