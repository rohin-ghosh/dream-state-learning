# CompilerGym bootstrap quarantine

Date: 2026-09-10

Status: immediate contamination stop requested by Rohin. This is a provenance
and promotion restriction, not authorization to train, launch, or alter a
scientific protocol.

## Rule

Any bootstrap corpus, adapter, parent lesson, child state, or selection result
derived from a child that has already played CompilerGym is contaminated for a
later CompilerGym deployment or parenting comparison.

In particular, artifacts generated from `R2_B_seed3` / seed 303 may be kept as
development diagnostics, but they are **not eligible** to:

- initialize a clean parented child;
- enter a clean child's SLEEP corpus;
- train or select a bootstrap intended for the CompilerGym final;
- update the clean parent's playbook or teaching policy using CompilerGym
  evaluation results; or
- support a from-scratch parenting or deployment claim on CompilerGym.

No information returns from a disposable gym descendant to the clean child:
weights, adapters, rows, summaries, DREAM/SLEEP products, parent observations,
outcomes, rankings, selection decisions, or hyperparameter choices conditioned
on its held-out performance.

## Clean replacement

A clean Stage-1 bootstrap must be target-blind to the final deployment gym. Its
worked states may come from unrelated generated classroom microdomains and may
teach general state-to-state metacognitive structure. They must not contain
CompilerGym programs, passes, outcomes, task identifiers, or transformations
derived from them.

Before parenting or final evaluation, a birth manifest must identify the exact
base, bootstrap sources, parent history, mechanism version, and zero exposure
to the final gym. Comparisons fork matched children from that same clean birth.

The final gym may be used during development only through one-way disposable
descendants. The confirmatory experiment starts again from the sealed clean
stage after all mechanisms and policies freeze.
