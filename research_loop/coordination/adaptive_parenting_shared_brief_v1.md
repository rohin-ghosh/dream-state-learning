# Shared brief: adaptive-parent development, then frozen final evaluation

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: proposal and coordination surface only. This supersedes the scientific
intent of the unexecuted `one_parent_child_headline_v4` proposal; the old bytes
remain intact as audit history. Nothing here authorizes implementation, model
or tokenizer execution, benchmark generation, adapter work, external
scientific execution, or GPU use.

## The system in one view

```text
THINK     continuous thought <-> action <-> public outcome
DREAM     rebuild the finite active context; no parameter write
SLEEP     compile grounded experience, write a child LoRA, gate commit/rollback
PARENT    improve teaching through persistent nonparametric state; parent
          weights remain fixed; the child is the only parametric learner
```

## Three phases

### A. Writer/mechanism certification

Freeze a good-enough SLEEP recipe by 2026-09-11. The development bar is
absorption, retention across writes, exact action-interface preservation, and
no material harm with transactional rollback. Broad parent-absent transfer is
a long-horizon outcome, not a four-lesson writer gate.

The candidate compiler enriches but does not multiply evidence: one public
action/outcome has one immutable evidence identity; paraphrases, replay views,
contrasts, scoped lessons, and connections may increase training dose while
retaining the source identity. The writer uses native child continuations,
response-only loss, replay/anchor mixing, conservative training, and an exact
commit canary. Rank is capacity; learning rate, steps, sampling mixture, and
sleep cadence are plasticity controls; temperature is behavioral exploration.

### B. Adaptive developmental parenting

Use the strongest permitted teacher model that fits the development budget.
The parent model's weights do not update. The teaching system retains:

- exact child lineage and developmental stage;
- prior lessons, interventions, and child restatements;
- public actions/outcomes and whether the lesson helped;
- misconceptions, mastered dispositions, open teaching hypotheses;
- curriculum coverage, difficulty, and independent-work schedule;
- Codex/Fable/Rohin teaching proposals and their measured consequences.

The parent may know answers in development tasks but withholds direct answers,
using them to diagnose process. Teaching includes conceptual instruction,
process correction, intermediate feedback, independent struggle, varied tasks,
world outcomes, reflection, and play. Parent text is provenance-recorded and
normally optimizer-visible but loss-masked; the child learns from its own
grounded corrected continuations rather than by imitating several teacher
dialects.

Developmental exams occur at saved child ages and may alter later teaching.
They are not final evidence. Clean child checkpoints never exposed to the
deployment gym are kept separately from longitudinal adults. A mechanism
change forks lineage; it never splices two mechanisms into one growth curve.

Parallelism uses shallow synchronized fan-out: clone one child snapshot into
isolated classrooms, collect short disjoint experiences without local weight
updates, pool the exact set-union of world-admitted evidence, perform one
cumulative write, then distribute the new shared child. Merge corpora, not
independently learned adapters. Branch horizon, not raw branch count, governs
policy divergence.

### C. Frozen final evaluation

After development, freeze the adult child, SLEEP/DREAM/THINK mechanism, mature
teaching state, exact teacher model/configuration, task split, budgets, and
analysis. Clone that mature parent state at the start of each independent root
and keep it isolated but longitudinal within the root.

Run the practical 2x2 on sealed tasks:

| child | parent absent | parent present |
|---|---|---|
| regular agent | ordinary baseline | temporary tutoring |
| taught learning adult | autonomous experiential learning | experiential learning plus tutoring |

The answer-aware parent receives the same permitted answer/process visibility
for both parent-present cells and withholds direct answers. Immediate
parent-present gain measures tutoring. Parent-removed retention and fresh-task
learning measure durable learning. The final test must distinguish assistance,
retention, and faster subsequent acquisition.
