accepted-constructible recipient-bundle law of a versioned generator G:

   and TWIN plus one-shot and open-loop schedules, while every edge-indexed
   oracle-preserving SHAM remains noninferior by the same population criterion?
3. **D1C frozen DREAM context policy:** does one shared frozen-model DREAM view
   exceed the paired action margin more often than pi0 against prospective
   recency and recipient-local hash-permuted views?
4. **D1D DREAM-to-SLEEP total policy:** does DREAM-selected evidence-root
   assignment exceed the paired selection and action margins more often than
   pi0 against total recency and hash-permuted root policies, inclusive of all
   policy-induced evidence dose, training, publication, inference, failure,
   and resource consequences?

Both computational arms are instantiated from each identical pre-treatment
life and common-random seed bundle. No unobserved counterfactual or randomized
