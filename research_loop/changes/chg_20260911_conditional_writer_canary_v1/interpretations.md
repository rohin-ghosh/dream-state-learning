# Independent interpretations — conditional writer canary v1

Date: 2026-09-11

Three fresh read-only agents independently audited the current evidence. None
edited files, ran a model, trained an adapter, or used a GPU.

## Scientific-estimand interpretation

The proposed parenting factorial has the right eventual causal skeleton, but
no writer has yet passed absorption, specificity, retention, interface, and
non-harm together. The next expensive clean childhood should therefore wait
for a small, matched writer test. Existing mounted-adapter effects are effects
inside selected realised histories, not evidence that a writer policy or
parenting works.

## Skeptical paper-review interpretation

The strongest present paper result is narrower and mostly negative: sleep
updates fit records and alter behaviour, but commonly install a marginal
routine or broad completion habit rather than a selective conditional memory.
A new test must require situation-dependent action, compare true association
against a marginal-preserving semantic shuffle, show that the task is usable
from text, and keep training geometry exactly matched. Parenting should remain
exploratory until the carrier is qualified.

## Lineage/systems interpretation

Existing v6 lives and standalone pretests may finish as development evidence,
but the current runner is not a clean v7 persistent-child implementation. It
lacks a write-once birth manifest, fail-closed ancestry, exact transactional
resume, nonblocking writer/parent publication, and an immutable active-adapter
pointer. No existing compiler-exposed child may become a clean parenting root.
The full C11 guard does not repair these separate lineage issues and remains
parked.
