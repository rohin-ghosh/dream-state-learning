# PPC5r4 — generator-population public four-assay instrument

Status: proposal only. No implementation, static sealer, model/tokenizer/
embedding call, training, canary, behavioral dispatch, GPU action, or scientific
claim is authorized. PPC5r3 consensus is binding provenance; all earlier PPC
artifacts and exploratory organism runs provide no evidence or authority.

## 1. Registered scientific questions

PPC5r4 is the smallest controlled precursor to the Experience Model program.
It does not test a long-lived improving agent. It asks four separate questions
over independently sampled recipient-life/seed bundles from a versioned
generator distribution G:

1. **D1A supported selection:** with the complete candidate table supplied to
   one common direct provider, does an evidence-gated READ-LoRA exceed the
   registered paired margin more often than threshold pi0 against adapter-off,
   an exactly dose/opportunity-matched wrong-life adapter, and an exactly
   dose-matched binding derangement, while satisfying recipient-life-level
   false-selection and NOT_FOUND safety ceilings?
2. **D1B observable path dependence:** on a presealed decisive length-two or
   length-three path, does authentic interleaved response use exceed the
   registered paired margin more often than pi0 against every edge-indexed CUT
   and TWIN plus one-shot and open-loop schedules, while every edge-indexed
   oracle-preserving SHAM remains noninferior by the same population criterion?
3. **D1C frozen DREAM context policy:** does one shared frozen-model DREAM view
   exceed the paired action margin more often than pi0 against prospective
   recency and recipient-local hash-permuted views?
4. **D1D DREAM-to-SLEEP total policy:** does DREAM-selected evidence-root
   assignment exceed the paired selection and action margins more often than
   pi0 against total recency and hash-permuted root policies, inclusive of all
   policy-induced evidence dose, training, publication, inference, failure,
   and resource consequences?

Both computational arms are instantiated from each identical pre-treatment
life and common-random seed bundle. No unobserved counterfactual or randomized
arm-label estimand is claimed. The primary estimand for paired gate g is
pi_g = Pr_G[d_g > margin_g]. Arithmetic mean, quantiles, and magnitude
intervals are descriptive only.

D1A is external candidate-conditioned discrimination, not closed-book storage.
D1B observes public behavioral dependence, not private reasoning. D1C and D1D
are total policy effects. The four assays are non-substitutable and their only
conjunction is non-mediational.

## 2. Frozen cognition and isolated learned object

THINK and ACT use one byte-identical frozen model. D1B permits only READ,
PREDICT, ACT, or STOP under three exact schedule controllers. D1C and D1D use
the same prospective `R=behavior_read_slots` one-attempt READ sequence followed
by one-attempt PREDICT and ACT; every error advances its slot, and `R=0` is
valid. D1D additionally executes every sealed provider endpoint regardless of
root/training status. D1A invokes no cognitive model.
DREAM is one separately scheduled no-retry call to the same frozen model.

SLEEP alone trains one clean-base READ-scoring LoRA. The adapter is mounted
only through an opaque artifact handle inside the common candidate provider;
it may change only candidate scores. THINK, DREAM, ACT, prompts, public state,
writer, and trainer never receive treatment-semantic labels.

The public ledger is append-only. DREAM selects only a reversible view of
existing public record IDs and focus; it creates no evidence. Positive SLEEP
rows arise only from direct, closed, pre-boundary public ACQUIRE outcomes.
PROBE, future, target-derived, cognitive, DREAM, scorer, and policy-target
material are permanently sterile.

## 3. Population, sampling, pairing, and no replacement

The run lock names a versioned finite-code generator G, its parameter law,
fixed pre-entropy gate membership, path-template/edge registry, life count N,
pi0 and margins, and an auditable entropy source. One independent draw emits a
complete recipient bundle including its own recipient-disjoint, exactly matched
D1A wrong-life donor sub-bundle; global cross-life donor matching is forbidden.
A sample receipt binds the entropy bytes before outcomes and the exact
rejection-free generator draw for every life. If the generator uses rejection,
every proposal, predicate, and rejection is retained and the maximum proposal
count is predeclared; exhaustion is CONSTRUCTION_FAIL, never resampling.

Every sampled life is included in all prospectively assigned gates. Each life
plus its common-random seed bundle is one IID unit. Lower queries, items,
clones, endpoints, and edge positions are repeated measures aggregated in
canonical order. No outcome-dependent exclusion, resampling, seed shift, or
replacement is permitted.

## 4. Assay isolation and controls

Every arm starts from the same byte-identical pre-treatment snapshot. D1A
varies only the opaque mounted artifact or null and makes no policy call. D1B
varies only the precomputed condition-free response projection and schedule.
D1C varies only the installed context after one shared DREAM. D1D varies only
the root policy and all of its natural downstream consequences while every arm
uses the same recency context and clean base. Any unlisted difference is
RUN_INVALID.

The D1A binding shuffle is a target-blind perfect derangement preserving the
request and donor-binding multisets, row/update counts, and ordered per-row
token/byte classes. The wrong-life donor is emitted inside the same independent
generator draw, is recipient-disjoint within that bundle, and matches
base, tokenizer, shape, recipe, mount interface, rank, rows, ordered row token
and byte classes, total tokens, updates, candidate-table cardinality, eligible
equivalence counts, support-multiplicity histogram, and row-class histogram.
It is present and hash-valid before assignment; no replacement exists.

D1B constructs one CUT, directional TWIN, and action-preserving SHAM at every
ordered edge. The full path alone is action-sufficient; every canonical proper
subset is insufficient; no alternative sufficient simple path exists inside
the declared universe; and source events are pairwise disjoint according to
the typed edge-source relation.

## 5. Execution and status semantics

The canonical machine uses an acyclic chain:

pre_state_receipt -> post_state_body -> event -> post_state_receipt,

where the post-state body excludes the new ledger head, the event binds its
body hash, and every terminal or nonterminal state receipt binds event_hash as
ledger_head. No object commits to its own digest.

An exhaustive assay/arm/stage/raw-result table assigns exactly one continuation
and exactly one observation, missing failure, construction blocker, or runtime
integrity blocker. Any integrity-clean assigned path that takes no action is an
observed normalized action value of zero, not missing. Missing model/provider
calls never retry, count as failures for paired exceedance, and count against
the missingness ceiling. Required artifacts are checked before assignment:
absence is CONSTRUCTION_FAIL; post-seal disappearance or mismatch is
RUN_INVALID. Assigned DREAM empty/abstain/invalid, no roots, no admitted roots,
and deterministic TRAIN_FAILED continue through their declared no-view,
no-adapter, or adapter-off consequences and retain observed downstream value.

Every schedule-complete ACT terminates as SCHEDULE_COMPLETE. Required-slot
STOP is SCHEDULE_VIOLATION. Queued controls bind every originating envelope,
request, provider/intervention receipt, controller before/after receipt,
public flush event, and discard receipt. No implicit transitions exist.

## 6. Analysis and claims

For paired gate g and life i, derive d_i after canonical within-life
aggregation. Z_i=1 exactly when d_i > margin_g and every required arm is
present; otherwise Z_i=0. Test H0: pi_g <= pi0_g with the exact upper-tail
Binomial(N,pi0_g) probability and report the one-sided Clopper-Pearson lower
bound. Equality counts against release.

D1A designates exactly one primary false-selection safety trial and one primary
NOT_FOUND safety trial per life. Each is a Bernoulli adverse event. Test
H0: p_event >= ceiling using the exact lower-tail Binomial(N,ceiling)
probability; require observed rate strictly below the ceiling and report the
one-sided Clopper-Pearson upper bound. After integrity blockers, a missing
required model, provider, or DREAM call dominates the affected endpoint even
if a later branch produces an action: its raw value is null, its registered
adverse analysis bound is used, and it enters missingness. An arm that does
not consume the missing shared dependency is unaffected.

Every named gate is conjunctive. Each assay p-value is the maximum of all its
gate p-values, expanding per-edge CUT, TWIN, and SHAM gates separately. Holm
step-down controls the four assay hypotheses. The intersection adds no fifth
test. Power uses the exact same critical counts/tails for every paired, safety,
and missingness gate, then a Bonferroni union lower bound for the sufficient
event that every individual gate p <= alpha/4 and every nonstatistical gate
passes. No independence assumption is used.

Resource normalization and semantic overlap are descriptive only. Every
claim/export receipt nevertheless binds the presealed overlap manifest and a
fixed qualification: results concern the locked generator and target-informed
constructed benchmark and establish neither novelty, zero-overlap
generalization, nor naturalistic path structure.
