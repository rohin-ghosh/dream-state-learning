## 1. Preserve the simple organism

The organism remains exactly three functions:

- **THINK**: the continuing child's waking thought, action, and public-outcome
  stream.
- **DREAM**: disposable management of finite waking context only.
- **SLEEP**: compile grounded propositions already authored by the child and
  write the personal LoRA.

The birth bootstrap, parent controllers, support resolver, candidate stager,
preservation evaluator, promotion store, exams, auditors, capability runtime,
action gate, authority verifier, and release controller are laboratory
infrastructure, not cognitive organs.

Parenting and repetition remain waking interventions. The only permitted
causal route is:

`parent/repetition -> waking context -> child thought -> child action -> public outcome -> committed child trace -> independent eligibility -> later SLEEP`

Parent/repetition bytes, semantics, IDs, counts, hashes, ordering, timing,
cache state, filenames, and error shapes are forbidden from evidence
membership, compilation, replay/dose, candidate construction, and gradients.

## 2. Scientific labels and factorial

The primary mechanism experiment remains birth `{D,T}` by parent
`{absent,fixed}`. `M0` remains a separate entry-competence/bootstrap-damage
diagnostic, not a third primary birth level. The adaptive-parent package is a
separately randomized extension.

- `Delta_R` remains immediate selective advice receptivity before personal
  SLEEP.
- `Delta_F` is the birth-by-fixed-regime interaction over entry-normalized
  developmental AUC at ages `0, 8, 16, 24, 32`.
- `Delta_E` is the separately reported birth-by-adaptive-regime interaction
  over the same entry-normalized developmental AUC.

V7 must call `Delta_F` and `Delta_E` **entry-normalized developmental AUC
interactions** or **total-regime performance-curve acceleration**. It must not
call them intrinsic or latent learning-rate changes unless a later,
prospectively frozen measurement model distinguishes ceiling, threshold,
transfer, persistent entry-policy, corpus, and promotion alternatives.

## 5. Pure candidate construction, then postclosure staging

Replace the combined E4 writer/stager with two principals and epochs:

1. `E4_CANDIDATE_CONSTRUCTOR` receives only immutable birth, a fresh
   zero-effective-delta initializer, full cumulative eligible child evidence,
   frozen writer/support/coverage-dose contracts, and the candidate seed. It
   emits sealed candidate bytes and a sealed construction trace. It has no
   active-head, prior-weight, CAS, verdict, evaluation, parent, repetition,

2. A sealed-candidate bridge issues `E4_CANDIDATE_STAGER` a
   non-dereferenceable candidate descriptor and an equality-only committed
   snapshot handle. The stager may stage or reject that already closed
   candidate; it cannot alter candidate bytes.

Prior-active-head mutation may change only staging status. Holding immutable
birth, initializer, cumulative eligible evidence, contracts, and seed fixed,
it must not change constructor inputs, branches, errors, sampling, dose,
gradients, bytes, serialization, or trace.

## 9. Acceptance-test receipts and noncircular ordering

Every acceptance test is split into the earliest constructible phases:

1. `STATIC_SCHEMA` — proposal/schema/registry/DAG checks only;
2. `G0_FAKE_RUNTIME` — deterministic fake artifact/transaction/fault checks;
3. `G1_IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW` — exact implementation inspection only;
4. `G2_CPU_DOMAIN` — real CPU domain fixtures after implementation;
5. `G3_GPU_SCIENCE` — separately authorized GPU/scientific execution;
6. `G4_CLAIM` — powered claim-specific evidence and terminal authority.

Each phase emits a typed receipt binding test ID, phase ID, subject and input
roots, fixture/implementation/result roots, pass/fail, reviewer where
applicable, expiry/replay policy, and exact downstream action IDs. A test phase
is required only before the next action it can actually guard. No test may be
required before the implementation needed to run it.

Target-blindness, bootstrap-control semantic audit, and static/runtime
noninterference receipts are explicit GPU-dispatch prerequisites. Powered
root-level endpoints, intervals, multiplicity, missingness, safety,
child-authorship, extraction/native-use, and claim-specific receipts are
explicit scientific-claim prerequisites. Human authority never substitutes
for evidentiary validity.

## 10. Finite cumulative coverage and dose

Before any writer execution, freeze a finite
`FROZEN_CUMULATIVE_COVERAGE_DOSE_CONTRACT` binding:

- eligible proposition closure and deterministic order;
- at least one loss-bearing inclusion per eligible proposition;
- meaning-preserving deduplication/paraphrase/replay/filler rules;
- response-mask, tokenizer, truncation, batch, update, optimizer, rank, and
  supervised-token accounting;
- comparison-specific equality rules;
- maximum corpus/token/update/capacity ceilings;
- fail-closed overflow behavior.

If full cumulative coverage exceeds a frozen ceiling, the life stops before a
writer call and is reported as capacity-limited under the prospective
missingness/stopping rule. V7 must not silently drop eligible propositions,
compress them into new semantics, continue incrementally from prior weights,
increase budgets post hoc, or pretend unequal opportunities remain a matched
comparison.
