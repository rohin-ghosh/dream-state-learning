# Fresh interpretation B: developmental and final evaluation

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: read-only interpretation. No implementation or scientific execution.

## Causal object

The fixed object is the parent's weights, not a stateless parent agent. The
developmental treatment includes the persistent teaching ledger, curriculum
policy, update rule, and stopping rule. The mature parent can be cloned for
final roots, with each clone evolving only inside its isolated root under a
frozen transition policy.

## Developmental exams

Use disposable exam clones at saved child ages. Exam experiences never enter
the continuing child or SLEEP corpus; only preregistered aggregate skill/error
codes may update the parent's teaching state.

1. Age-zero parent-absent baseline on development-only tasks.
2. Teaching unit: solo attempt, public outcome, answer-aware process
   correction without the answer, then retry or fresh isomorphic practice.
3. Immediate parent-absent absorption on a fresh homologous item after reload.
4. Delayed retention after unrelated work and context reset.
5. Future-acquisition exam on a disposable clone and genuinely novel family,
   measuring entry level separately from gain-AUC/time to threshold.
6. Teaching-responsiveness exam from another identical clone, comparing
   parent-present and parent-absent matched items before a child write.
7. Graduation under a preregistered development budget/stopping rule, followed
   by sealing the adult, mechanism, exact parent model/config, mature initial
   teaching state, state transition, task split, budgets, and analysis.

Repeated ages from one lineage are correlated observations, not independent
replicates. Every viewed development panel is consumed selection data.

## Final design

Primary 2x2:

| | parent absent | parent present |
|---|---|---|
| matched non-parented adult | ordinary autonomous lifecycle | tutoring control |
| taught adult | autonomous experiential learning | experiential learning plus tutoring |

The primary non-parented adult has the same base child, tools, context budget,
THINK/DREAM/SLEEP mechanism, writer settings, and final experience budget; it
differs only in developmental parenting. A frozen product-style baseline may
be reported separately but cannot isolate parenting.

Each independent root is a randomized block with four isolated clones and
matched task randomness. Both parent-present cells receive the same permitted
answer/process visibility, token/turn limits, and mature parent starting state.
Root-local parent/child evolution is allowed under the sealed transition rule,
discarded after the root, and never shared.

Measure, separately:

- retained developmental effect on a solo entry deck;
- immediate tutoring before writes;
- differential use of tutoring across adult types;
- future learning rate via entry-adjusted parent-absent gain-AUC;
- autonomous lifetime improvement on fresh/context-reset tasks;
- optional post-tutoring retention after parent removal.

Writer-specific causality is not identified by this 2x2. It requires an
auxiliary matched commit-on/commit-off fork with identical experience.

## Leakage and adaptation controls

An answer-aware natural-language teacher can leak through strategy choice,
evaluation, ordering, message length, punctuation, and curriculum selection.
Record every parent message; keep it loss-masked; scan child/compiler rows for
task literals and answer reconstruction; use a fixed hint budget; run
counterfactual answer-swap and decoder/red-team audits. The defensible claim is
"answer-aware tutoring under a prespecified, audited withholding channel."
Claiming zero answer information requires a mechanically restricted,
counterfactual-invariant process channel.

Final tasks and keys remain unavailable until the full freeze. Every viewed
development exam is consumed. No final result changes the adult, parent,
curriculum, stopping rule, metric, subset, or later root.

## Claim boundary

Tasks, episodes, compiler rows, replay views, paraphrases, and parent turns are
not independent units; the root is the sealed-environment analysis unit. If
all roots clone one selected mature dyad, inference is conditional on that
dyad. General developmental-procedure claims require multiple independent
full developmental lineages.

The final can support tutoring, retention and faster acquisition for the exact
mature dyad, and autonomous full-lifecycle improvement. It cannot alone prove
general teacher effectiveness, SLEEP/LoRA/DREAM necessity, connected knowledge,
general intelligence, sentience, or asymptotic self-improvement.

## Smallest spend-licensing canary

From an identical prelesson child fork, compare one genuine process correction
against an equal-budget sham/no-correction fork. After writing, reload, remove
parent/nursery state, and require the targeted process plus improved public
behavior on a fresh homologous item without interface regression. Add an
intervening task and delayed fresh probe to call it retention. This licenses
larger spend; it is not a general teaching claim.
