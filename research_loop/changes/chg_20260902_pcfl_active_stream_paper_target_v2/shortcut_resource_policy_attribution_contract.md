# Shortcut, native-resource, and policy-attribution contract

Status: proposal only. This artifact defines scientific repair requirements
for a successor proposal. It authorizes no implementation, fixture execution,
model/provider/network call, GPU work, training, LoRA, result, claim,
publication, or stage transition.

## 1. Scope and inheritance

This contract resolves `CONS_D06_SEMANTIC_RENDERING_SHORTCUT_COVERAGE`,
`CONS_D07_NATIVE_RESOURCE_NORMALIZATION`, and
`CONS_D08_POLICY_PRIOR_VERSUS_LIFE_MEMORY`. It is additive to every valid V1
finite-packet, payload-age, OLD-embargo, physical AS-CTX, legal-action D4/A4,
AUTH/TWIN/NULL, binding, same-event, common-history, model-owned compiler,
representation-neutral scoring, native-interface, full-resource,
nonadaptive-versus-recurrent, failure-inclusion, visibility/reset/sterility,
premodel-freeze, and eight-root de-scope obligation.

Nothing below changes the exact B3 independent-unit rule, eight-root roster,
908 target-evaluation rows per root, 7,264 total target rows, 18 exploration
clones per root, or six binding diagnostic actions per root. New attribution
checks must be registered as pre-B3 A0--A2 fixtures or as nested B1/B2 gate
diagnostics on the already assigned two gate roots. They add no independent
unit and no B3 target-evaluation row. Every added call, action, byte, latency,
failure, and retry is nevertheless charged in the resource ledger. A
successor registry must assign new acceptance-test IDs; this contract does not
invent them.

## 2. Independently held-out semantic/rendering shortcut red-team

### 2.1 Freeze and role separation

Before B0 or any scientific model call, Stage A2 must hash-bind the production
renderer, every public packet/status schema, label alphabet and morphology,
option ordering, serializer, method/family prompt scaffold, target allocation,
shortcut hypotheses, evaluator, stop/narrow rules, and the exact split between
builder-visible development strata and independently held-out audit strata.

The shortcut red-team must be independent of the generator, renderer,
compiler, policy, and paper-result authors. It receives the sealed bytes and
the least privileged audit view needed to construct attacks, but receives no
author conclusions or B-stage outcomes. The held-out audit strata remain
unavailable to prompt, renderer, policy, baseline, threshold, or narrative
tuning. Their commitments and later opening receipts are published. Any
post-opening cognition or renderer change invalidates DEV and requires a new
freeze, review, and exact human execution decision.

### 2.2 Mandatory held-out strata

The independent audit must cover all of the following while holding the
latent world, legal actions, target proof, costs, and scorer fixed:

1. unseen semantically equivalent renderer and template variants, including
   field order, delimiter, whitespace, option order, serialization position,
   sentence order, and status-placement permutations;
2. unseen label alphabets and morphology, within-cohort relabelings, handle
   renamings, and family-name permutations that preserve the same latent
   involution and public semantics;
3. descriptor-neutral skins and independently authored adversarial
   paraphrases, including paraphrases designed to remove lexical cues and
   others designed to tempt pretrained stereotypes;
4. packet-shape, length, omission, overflow, fixed-status-frequency, retry,
   error, timing, candidate-order, and passive-signature canaries, with fixed
   cognition-facing shapes wherever the inherited contract requires them;
5. method/family/system/task prompt-scaffold swaps and ablations that preserve
   the allowed task instructions while detecting condition-specific hints;
6. target-only, target-plus-current-state, current-state-only,
   renderer/label-only, packet-shape-only, and passive-signature-only
   predictors with no life memory and no arm-owned action--outcome prefix; and
7. the registered nonadaptive witnessed-atom policy, exact-program construct
   ceiling, target-only/twin-byte Bayes checks, and recurrent-reader positive
   control.

No-memory signature models may be trained only on the committed development
partition and must be evaluated on held-out roots, label systems, and renderer
families. Their features, hyperparameters, selection rule, failure mapping,
and resource use freeze before the held-out partition opens. Exhaustive legal
packet enumeration bounds engine behavior but is not represented as an
exhaustive proof about pretrained semantic priors.

### 2.3 Exact disposition

A **hard shortcut** exists if a target-only, current-state,
renderer/label/shape/passive-signature, nonadaptive independent-atom, or single
memory-return route reaches the registered excluded solution condition under
the legal action cap—for example, it identifies the unique informative probe,
recovers the decisive hidden binding, or executes an integrative target that
the construct requires to depend on recurrent life-memory use. A hard shortcut
is `STOP_TEXT_ORGAN_INVALID`, not a covariate, subgroup, null, or unfavorable
SELF result. Affected efficacy and causal-credit comparisons are
uninterpretable.

If semantically equivalent held-out renderings change performance but no
excluded no-memory/nonadaptive solution route opens, the proposal may proceed
only if the pre-result paper contract already permits the exact
`RENDERER_BOUNDARY` disposition. The paper must name the exact frozen renderer,
show every held-out stratum separately, and make no rendering-invariant or
general semantic claim. Otherwise it stops. No result may choose between stop
and narrowing after B-stage outcomes are seen.

Recurrent KV, linked memory, rolling memory, raw RAG, or MECH success is not by
itself a shortcut. Those are legitimate alternative organisms/backends under
representation-neutral scoring. Their success may defeat model-owned-memory
advantage, but it does not invalidate the finite target unless an independently
excluded nonadaptive or nonmemory route also succeeds.

## 3. Policy-prior versus transformed-life-memory controls

### 3.1 Content- and shape-matched misbinding control

For each assigned authentic-memory diagnostic at the frozen B1/B2 gate roots,
construct one predeclared `MISBOUND_MEMORY` control by the first applicable
rule below; the choice rule freezes before any memory output or target result:

1. apply the sealed binding involution/permutation to the decisive opaque
   life-binding values while preserving record counts, record types,
   provenance/support flags, lifecycle/status distribution, IDs or
   ID-shaped surrogates, candidate order, serialized length, index entries,
   reader interface, call slots, return envelopes, and byte/operation/action
   maxima; or
2. if such an in-life permutation is not total, use a preassigned wrong-life
   donor matched on checkpoint, cohort exposure, support opportunity, record
   counts/types, lifecycle/status distribution, serialized lengths, catalog
   shape, and interface, then replace all donor world/side/root handles with
   frozen opaque shape-matched surrogates.

Donor or shuffle selection must be target-, result-, score-, treatment-label-,
and compiler-output-blind. The control must preserve content amount and shape
while breaking the authentic life-to-binding relation. It must not be a null
memory, a conspicuously malformed record, or a smaller catalog. It receives
the same actor model, task prompt, legal action menu, common online envelope,
sampling schedule, action cap, and failure rules as authentic memory. It is a
nested attribution diagnostic, not a new root or B3 row.

### 3.2 Equally provisioned raw-evidence policy

For the same gate items, run `RAW_EVIDENCE_POLICY` with every authentic public
action/state/outcome/provenance packet that was lawfully available to the
text compiler at that checkpoint. It receives a native raw-evidence store and
index, the same frozen actor/controller model and task scaffold, the same
query grammar, at most six reader calls, 4,096 aggregate UTF-8 query bytes,
16,384 aggregate UTF-8 returned bytes, twelve resolver operations, the same
sampling schedule and legal action cap, and the same retry/failure policy.

The raw policy may use iterative/recurrent retrieval and may return one native
canonical public-event envelope per read. It must not be forced through a
one-relation SELF parser, denied handle-based retrieval, deprived of a native
index, or charged compilation work that it does not perform. Conversely it
may not receive truth, target proof, scorer output, future allocation, or
evidence that SELF could not lawfully receive. All native preprocessing,
indexing, retrieval, model, storage, and online work are measured rather than
assumed equal. “Equally provisioned” means equal opportunity and the common
online envelope with legitimate native infrastructure, not byte-identical
representations or artificially equal compute.

### 3.3 No single target-complete recommendation

No admitted SELF record and no single reader return may contain a
target-complete probe or action recommendation. This condition is executable,
not a prose judgment. For every assigned gate item, a frozen audit removes all
other memory returns and asks the exact typed controller whether the candidate
record/return plus the public current goal/state alone determines:

- the unique registered informative probe and all required action parameters;
  or
- the complete legal terminal trajectory, including its binding choices and
  terminal commit.

The static audit also rejects explicit target handles, future target IDs,
optimal/correct labels, imperative action fields, answer keys, or serialized
equivalents forbidden by the Stage-A2 DSL. Paraphrase and renderer variants of
the executable audit are included in the held-out red-team. A violating record
or return is retained in the failure-inclusive audit trail; it is never
silently deleted or repaired after outputs are seen.

When a single-return recommendation supplies an excluded solution route, the
construct is `STOP_TEXT_ORGAN_INVALID`. When it changes behavior without
solving the excluded route, raw terminal behavior remains reportable but all
model-owned-memory/path credit for the affected item is withheld. Provenance
alone does not turn a recommendation into transformed-life-memory evidence.

### 3.4 Separate path-use and terminal-action scoring

Retain the inherited chain variables and report them separately:

```text
TERMINAL_ACTION_SUCCESS = h6
  = 1 iff the registered legal target trajectory executes within cap
    and earns the terminal world return; otherwise 0.

MEMORY_DEPENDENT_PATH_USE = h5
  = 1 iff the sealed pre-output path predicate shows that the executed
    certified working path uses a returned branch-event descendant and at
    least one later query or legal-action parameter is a function of an
    earlier return; otherwise 0.

MODEL_OWNED_PATH_USE
  = 1 iff MEMORY_DEPENDENT_PATH_USE=1, the decisive used descendant is an
    admitted target-blind model-owned semantic record, the single-return
    recommendation audit passes, and authentic use is directionally separated
    from MISBOUND_MEMORY and RAW_EVIDENCE_POLICY under the registered control;
    otherwise 0.
```

`TERMINAL_ACTION_SUCCESS` is primary behavioral value and remains
representation-neutral. It is reported even when path attribution fails or a
generic policy succeeds. `MEMORY_DEPENDENT_PATH_USE` and
`MODEL_OWNED_PATH_USE` are attribution diagnostics only; neither enters target
correctness or converts the eight roots into population evidence. A lucky
terminal action cannot restore an earlier failed cumulative link. Generic
policy, raw evidence, recurrent KV, linked, rolling, or MECH success remains
valid behavioral success while model-owned-memory credit is withheld whenever
the matched controls or recommendation audit fail.

## 4. Two separate native-backend estimands

### 4.1 Estimand A: common-envelope capability

`COMMON_ENVELOPE_CAPABILITY` asks:

> On the exact finite assigned targets, what failure-inclusive legal terminal
> action value and registered P1--P4 component/path profile does each frozen
> backend achieve when every backend keeps a legitimate native representation
> and implementation but shares the outer online read/query-byte/return-byte/
> resolver-operation/action envelope?

Report each backend/root/checkpoint/component separately. The common envelope
is at most six reader calls, 4,096 aggregate UTF-8 query bytes, 16,384
aggregate UTF-8 return bytes, twelve resolver operations, and the presealed
legal action cap. It does not require byte-identical catalogs or returns across
representations; byte identity remains required only for inherited matched
interventions and the WITNESS-KV-NA/REC pair. It does not equalize or hide
offline compilation, indexing, storage, model calls, or latency. Therefore
this estimand supports only an observed capability comparison at the chosen
envelope, never the phrase `resource-normalized advantage`.

### 4.2 Estimand B: cost/resource Pareto frontier

`COST_RESOURCE_PARETO_FRONTIER` asks:

> Which frozen backend configurations are nondominated on the complete finite
> failure-inclusive capability vector and the complete prospectively declared
> offline-plus-online resource vector, both in total and per registered later
> target decision over which reusable work is amortized?

The frontier is separate from P1--P4 and from the root-wise strongest
capability comparator. It may not select a baseline for B3 cognition, rewrite
the common envelope, combine resources with post-result weights, or create an
independent winner claim. Report both external-memory candidates even if a
post-eight-root reporting/planning selector names one AS-EXT series.

## 5. Complete resource vector and dominance

For every backend, root, side, checkpoint, target, and applicable shared job,
record raw totals and the declared allocation/amortization rule for:

1. offline and online model calls, separated by Dream-1, Dream-2, resolver,
   reader-policy, summary/reflection, embedding, compilation, and any other
   model role;
2. offline and online input tokens, output tokens, aggregate query bytes,
   aggregate returned bytes, and public raw-evidence bytes;
3. persistent corpus, record, view, index, embedding, candidate, manifest,
   cache, adapter, optimizer, and checkpoint bytes, plus peak transient bytes
   and transferred bytes;
4. indexing, embedding, enumeration, comparison, compiler, resolver, and
   serialization operations; FLOPs where measurable;
5. CPU time, occupied accelerator/device time by device type, end-to-end wall
   time, per-decision latency, and measured concurrency;
6. provider and hardware dollars under the exact accounting boundary, and
   energy when available (unavailable energy is `NOT_MEASURED`, never zero);
7. ordinary and diagnostic environment actions, reader calls, resolver
   operations, retries, timeouts, malformed outputs, failures, and unexecuted
   assigned work; and
8. the number of later target decisions over which each reusable offline cost
   is amortized, with both unamortized total and amortized-per-decision values.

Missing mandatory coordinates make that configuration
`RESOURCE_INCOMPLETE` and ineligible for a Pareto or efficiency statement;
they are not imputed as zero. Failure, retry, cache construction, hidden
candidate work, index building, enumeration, preprocessing, and transfer may
not be omitted because they are “offline” or shared. Equal visible response
bytes never imply equal compute.

For the finite observed surface, configuration `a` Pareto-dominates
configuration `b` if and only if:

1. `a` is no worse than `b` on every prospectively registered
   failure-inclusive capability coordinate assigned to both configurations,
   including raw terminal value and every pairwise-applicable conjunctive
   component;
2. `a` consumes no more than `b` on every mandatory resource coordinate under
   the same total or amortized accounting view;
3. at least one capability coordinate is strictly better or at least one
   resource coordinate is strictly lower; and
4. neither configuration is invalid, starved, `RESOURCE_INCOMPLETE`,
   selectively completed, or evaluated on a different assigned surface.

Dominance is partial. If one method trades a capability gain for any resource
increase, or resource dimensions cross, neither dominates unless a separate
prospectively human-ratified utility function exists; none is authorized here.
The pairwise capability-coordinate applicability map freezes at A2. A poor or
failed observation cannot be relabeled not applicable, and no configuration
may enter a frontier unless the common assigned capability surface is
nonempty and complete.
Do not scalarize tokens, bytes, latency, device time, dollars, or energy with
post-result weights. Say only “empirically Pareto-dominates on the registered
eight-root DEV surface,” name the two exact configurations and accounting
view, show all coordinates, and preserve the paper nonclaim boundary.

## 6. Non-starvation and native-backend fairness

Every registered backend—including AS-NONE, AS-CTX, raw iterative RAG,
WITNESS-KV-NA, WITNESS-KV-REC, MECH, linked memory, rolling summary, both
external-memory candidates, generic SELF, class-informed SELF, and the exact
program where applicable—must be frozen before B0 with:

- its faithful native write/update, record, serializer, catalog, index,
  candidate-generation, retrieval, recurrent-query, and failure behavior;
- lawful access to the same public evidence available for its registered role;
- the common online envelope and the same target/action cap, without a
  SELF-specific query grammar, hidden scaffold, larger return reserve, or
  favored retry rule;
- adequate storage/index/candidate capacity for the full assigned prefix and
  a preflight showing that the implementation, rather than an accidental
  wrapper cap, can exercise its registered native path;
- all offline and online work charged under the resource vector above; and
- an independent A2 fairness review covering interface fidelity, budget
  headroom, failure shapes, and method-specific expertise.

No baseline may be forced through a SELF schema, one-relation reader, batch
policy, truncated store, missing index, smaller candidate pool, lower model
budget, fewer legal actions, weaker controller, or artificial common-compute
cap merely to manufacture a SELF advantage. The nonadaptive witness condition
must remain nonadaptive by design, but its paired recurrent condition receives
the identical known-good atoms and maximum read count and may condition later
queries on earlier returns. Raw RAG retains iterative native retrieval.

If a legitimate backend hits an undeclared wrapper, capacity, storage,
latency, or provider cap; lacks required native infrastructure; or cannot
finish for a method-specific resource reason that was not prospectively part
of the comparison, set `BASELINE_STARVED`. Keep its assigned failures, but
invalidate the affected common-envelope and Pareto advantage interpretation;
do not call SELF superior. A prospectively declared global cap applying to all
methods remains a real chosen-envelope limit, must be reported, and still does
not justify general superiority.

The two AS-EXT candidates execute and are reported under the same immutable
premodel configuration. Any selector runs only after every B3 cell seals and
may choose an already executed series for reporting or future planning. Its
choice cannot enter B3 cognition, targets, scores, completion, Pareto
dominance, or an independent-winner claim.

## 7. Gate dispositions and claim boundary

The successor proposal must register independent gates that fail closed on:

- an unsealed or nonindependent held-out red-team;
- a hard no-memory, passive-signature, nonadaptive, one-return, or renderer
  shortcut;
- failure to apply the predeclared stop-or-`RENDERER_BOUNDARY` rule;
- an unmatched misbinding/wrong-life control;
- a starved or unfair raw-evidence/native backend;
- a target-complete SELF record or single return;
- conflation of path-use with terminal success;
- an incomplete resource vector, invalid amortization, or invalid dominance
  statement; or
- missing frozen bytes, independent review, or exact human scientific-call
  authority.

These gates are prospective specifications, not present evidence. A pass may
support only the exact finite construct/protocol and observed eight-root
descriptive attribution/resource statements allowed by
`paper_result_and_successor_contract.md`. It cannot establish population
efficacy, general resource efficiency, model-owned-memory superiority, formal
mediation, online learning, whole-organism necessity, plateau, external
validity, or associative-LoRA value. A fail never authorizes a repair run,
extra B3 cell, baseline removal, threshold change, LoRA rescue, or claim
narrowing that was not frozen before results.
