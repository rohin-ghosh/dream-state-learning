## 4. Valid completion and classification order

`VALID_B1_B3_PROCEDURAL_COMPLETION` is true only when:

1. B0 has sealed all inherited canary, reader, reducer, cadence, lifecycle,
   cursor, paired-RNG, intervention, failure, and blindness requirements;
2. B1 and B2 have both validly passed their frozen pre-B3 spending/construct
   gates on their assigned roots. An early B1 or B2 stop is not completion;
3. B3 has sealed all eight assigned roots and all 908 assigned rows per root,
   with no replacement, pruning, missing denominator, post-result cognition
   change, or unreviewed retry;
4. every construct, target, shortcut, visibility, reset, sterility, assignment,
   resource non-starvation, scoring, namespace, and reproduction validity gate
   passes; and
5. all failures remain in the reported vectors and the independent reviewer

Favorable B3 efficacy signs are not part of procedural validity. Once B3 was
properly dispatched and completely sealed, absence of one or more favorable

completed rows. In contrast, a shortcut, invalid construct, leakage,
starvation, missing cell, unreviewed cognition change, or B1/B2 early stop is
not a null and cannot become procedural completion.
