## 1. Preserve the simple organism and causal boundary

The organism still has exactly three functions:

- **THINK**: the continuing child's waking thought, action, public outcome,
  judgment, and prospective-intention stream.
- **DREAM**: disposable management of finite waking context.
- **SLEEP**: noncreative compilation of independently eligible, grounded
  child-authored experience followed by a personal LoRA write.

The birth intervention, fixed/adaptive parents, evaluators, support resolver,
writer constructor, stager, promotion store, auditors, capability system, and
release controller are experimental infrastructure, not cognitive organs.

The sole parenting route remains:

`parent/repetition -> waking child context -> child thought -> child action -> public outcome -> complete committed child trace -> independent eligibility -> later SLEEP`

Parent, repetition-controller, and DREAM bytes or direct/derived observables
must never be loss-bearing personal SLEEP evidence. Repetition is tested as
repeated meaning enacted across varied situations, not exact-string copying.

## 2. Narrow scientific object and labels

The birth factor is `{D,T}` and the parenting factor is `{absent,fixed}` for
the primary mechanism experiment. `M0` is a separate entry-damage/headroom
diagnostic. The adaptive-parent package is a separately randomized extension.

- `D` receives the matched direct child continuation/control rendering.
- `T` receives the matched feedback-to-child-computation rendering.
- Both receive the same task state, committed child action, public outcome,
  supervised-token opportunity, model/optimizer opportunity, truncation
  policy, and environment randomness under a separately frozen exact
  bootstrap contract.
- Neither rendering may contain the deployment target, target vocabulary,
  answer, hidden rule, or target-specific causal proxy.

The formal estimands are only:

- `Delta_R`: the T-versus-D incremental immediate selective-advice-use
  interaction before personal SLEEP;
- `Delta_F`: the birth-by-fixed-parent total-regime interaction over
  entry-normalized developmental performance curves at ages
  `0, 8, 16, 24, 32`;
- `Delta_E`: the separately randomized birth-by-adaptive-parent total-regime
  interaction over the same ages.

“Receptivity bootstrap,” “teachability,” and “learning readiness” are
intervention nicknames only. No result may be described as intrinsic learning
rate, latent plasticity, mediation, or a uniquely identified teachability
mechanism. Before any “conferred by T” language, the exact statistics contract
must prospectively freeze and satisfy powered D/T/M0 entry competence,
nondamage, ceiling, comparable-headroom, and equivalence/noninferiority gates.
Otherwise only the complete T-versus-D rendering contrast may be reported.
