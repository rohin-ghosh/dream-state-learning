Requested scope for this deliberation:

Define the smallest exact executable PCFL benchmark core and clean-child
boundary that can support the paper's M (connected experience, goal traversal,
information-seeking expansion, grounded write, delayed old-plus-new use), L
(increasing-lifetime improvement beyond one certified evolving active-text
memory baseline's prospectively registered local plateau), and C (separate
semantic-code rate--distortion) experiments without pooling their independent
units or claims.

The benchmark core must bind canonical public events, experiential atoms and
links, goals, actions, outcomes, evidence identities, a deterministic root and
twin generator, exact compilers, a finite visibility-safe reader, a phase
machine, necessary cuts/redirections/shams, root-level reducers, and CPU
shortcut/identifiability gates. It must establish the complete explicit-text
relay before any LoRA carrier is interpreted. E0 remains an independently
gated prerequisite; ACTIVE_TEXT_FIXED must be executable and certified before
L. Physical LoRA compression remains out of scope unless the measured byte
crossover is actually exceeded.

The clean-child boundary must fail closed. Parenting and target-blind
classroom work may affect only a verified clean developmental lineage. Any
deployment/final-gym visit begins from a read-only clean snapshot in a one-way
disposable descendant. Nothing from that descendant—including weights,
optimizer state, rows, summaries, DREAM/SLEEP products, parent state, outcomes,
rankings, schedules, or selection decisions—may return to the canonical child
or parent. Missing or unverifiable provenance is ineligible. Known
CompilerGym-derived lives and bootstraps remain quarantined development-only.

The proposal must distinguish OS-enforced read/write isolation from
tamper-evident provenance, and must not pretend that a manifest proves every
human or remote-model causal input. It must state what is blinded or prebound
for confirmation and what remains development-only. Prefer a compact
implementation boundary over a universal information-flow platform.

This is proposal deliberation only. It authorizes no implementation, model or
tokenizer execution, benchmark/data generation, adapter/checkpoint work, GPU
or resource use, scientific execution, claim, release, or transfer before
Rohin ratifies the exact adjudicated bytes and scope.
