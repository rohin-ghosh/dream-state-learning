# Cross-critique and adjudication

The lived gateway is weaker than the scripted six-bank assay in four ways:
one paired world root provides no repeatability estimate; opposite outcomes
make the child trajectories semantically different; selecting jointly correct
final actions produces a success-filtered corpus; and supervising the final
`ACT` can transport an endpoint policy without proving that the outcome token
or reasoning path was causally necessary.

The scripted assay is weaker for the project's immediate decision in three
ways: its experiences and correct actions are supplied, it retests identities
seen during training, and its full initial-plus-retention design costs much
more before we know whether a grounded native trajectory can move action at
all.

Adjudication: run one result-blind development preflight, then the lived
two-world gateway. Its only scientific claim is child-grounded endpoint-policy
carriage in one paired micro-world. A pass unlocks the independently scoped
six-bank true/permuted qualification and retention test. A failure stops the
larger writer spend until the localized failure is repaired. This ordering
optimizes information per GPU-hour without promoting the gateway into a writer
qualification or paper root.
