# V3 blocker resolution for extractable SLEEP + teachability bootstrap

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: successor-proposal input only. No implementation, model/tokenizer
execution, benchmark generation, adapter work, GPU use, resource acquisition,
external scientific execution, claim, or publication authority.

## 1. Live parent text has exactly one route

Static bootstrap conditioning is attended by the birth writer and
response-loss-masked. Longitudinal parent prose is different: it is attended
only by the waking child, recorded in a restricted audit ledger, and removed
before any personal SLEEP render. It is absent from both personal SLEEP prompts
and targets. The compiler may receive a parent-message ID solely to prove
exclusion; it cannot dereference the bytes. Only later grounded child-authored
thought/action/outcome evidence can enter SLEEP.

## 2. Development evidence and evaluation observations are different types

`development.public_observation` is an ordinary on-policy world response. It
may enter the committed life ledger and later SLEEP through the support
resolver.

`evaluation.public_observation` covers standardized-advice clones, disposable
development exams, and sealed final evaluation. It is reporting-only. It
cannot enter the continuing child or parent context, life/evidence ledger,
compiler inputs or selection, caches, replay, later roots, or any update. Exam
clones and evaluators can see it; the continuing wake child and SLEEP compiler
cannot.

## 3. Fixed teaching uses an exogenous schedule

The fixed-policy assay sends the byte-identical lesson assigned to a block at
the same preregistered ages in every paired birth: before lessons 1, 9, 17, and
25 in a 32-lesson screen. It cannot inspect behavior, acquisition, relapse,
scores, or SLEEP decisions. Every lineage therefore receives four reminders
at identical times and opportunities. Acquisition, selective enactment, and
relapse are measured only as outcomes.

Behavior-triggered fading and restoration exist only in the adaptive-parent
package and are labeled part of that package. The mock-child fixture includes
births with deliberately different acquisition/relapse times and proves the
fixed schedule remains byte- and time-identical.

## 4. Fresh-from-birth SLEEP has replay dependence, not unreplayed retention

Every replacement personal LoRA is trained fresh from the immutable birth.
Therefore information omitted from the new cumulative corpus has no legal
weight path into the new adapter. Replace the old unreplayed-retention claim
with a replay-dependence control:

1. Write a grounded personal sentinel and probe it immediately.
2. At the next replacement SLEEP, construct two disposable candidates from the
   same birth and exact writer seed: one from the canonical cumulative corpus
   including the sentinel, and one after removing the sentinel plus every
   descendant, paraphrase, correlated cue, and support bundle that contains it.
3. Probe both under fresh held-out cues and native-use cases.
4. Neither candidate changes the continuing lineage.

Survival only in the cumulative candidate supports replay-dependent retention;
survival in the fully ablated candidate is evidence of prior/base knowledge,
leakage, or an incomplete descendant audit. This does not claim continuous
weight inheritance or human-like retention without rehearsal.

## 5. Rejected SLEEP preserves life but rejects the write

The lineage head contains separately typed roots:

- `active_weight_root`: immutable birth plus previous committed personal LoRA;
- `life_data_root`: committed wake action/outcome evidence and eligible child
  notes;
- `parent_audit_root`: parent messages, teacher playbook/context, effort, and
  intervention receipts, never a compiler input;
- `repetition_state_root`: reminder schedule, relevant/irrelevant opportunities,
  acquisition observations, fade/relapse state;
- `context_state_root`: DREAM output derived only from committed allowed roots.

Each wake action/outcome and its eligible child trace commits to the life-data
root before SLEEP. Parent audit and repetition state commit under their own
typed rules. A candidate SLEEP snapshots the current head and may promote only
if compare-and-swap still sees that exact head.

If compilation, training, or preservation fails:

- `active_weight_root` remains the previous child;
- already committed wake evidence, parent audit, and repetition observations
  survive;
- candidate views/corpus, optimizer state, adapter, probes, and candidate DREAM
  are quarantined and never become discoverable inputs;
- context is rebuilt from the surviving committed roots and previous weights;
- an operational crash may retry the exact transaction once from its bound
  inputs; a behavioral/preservation rejection is not retried on the same data
  and waits for the next preregistered evidence threshold.

The adaptive parent cannot observe private candidate scores or gate verdicts.
Its playbook can change only from public committed child behavior and its own
prior messages. A rejected write therefore does not secretly train the parent.

## Consensus-only wording repairs

- “response tokens match” means supervised response-token **counts and
  positions** match; token identities differ only in declared treatment spans.
- Fixed-policy arms share writer/schedule/opportunity/gates, not realized
  ledgers, gradients, candidates, or commit decisions.
- `Delta_F` is fixed-curriculum-plus-personal-SLEEP acceleration, not advice
  alone or a uniquely weight-mediated effect.
- “Target-blind” is shorthand only for no incremental downstream-derived
  information entering bootstrap construction.
- Two-root margins license spending only. They cannot release a scientific
  claim without the separately frozen six-new-root replication and authority.
- The four proposed microdomains are candidates until the structural audit
  passes; they are not presumed unrelated.

## Required successor tests

1. Taint live-parent bytes and evaluation observations and prove they cannot
   reach personal SLEEP inputs/labels, continuing state, ledgers, caches, or
   later roots; prove development observations remain eligible.
2. Use differing mock acquisition/relapse times to prove fixed-policy reminder
   bytes/timing never adapt.
3. Run the cumulative-versus-fully-ablated sentinel replay-dependence assay and
   descendant-support audit.
4. Inject rejection/crash at every boundary and prove exact typed-root survival,
   quarantine, retry, context rebuild, and next-head behavior.
