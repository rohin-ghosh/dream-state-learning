## 2. DEV baseline selection

The external contest contains exactly:

- a faithful native linked/A-MEM-style streaming memory; and
- one target-blind rolling summary/reflection memory.

Both receive their own legitimate native streaming write, update, and target-
time read policies. They do not consume SELF claims or hidden graph paths. On
DEV, choose the arm with the higher mean cross-era D4 post-native AUC subject
to complete resource reporting. A difference below `0.02` selects the lower
amortized model/read-token arm; a remaining tie selects the linked-memory arm.
The contest, tie rule, and implementations hash-freeze at Stage A2 before any
DEV model call.

Raw episodic RAG remains a separate full-surface baseline even if linked
memory wins. The exact public program is a construct ceiling expected to score
best; SELF is not required to beat it. Ordinary D4 is explicitly finite
causal-map acquisition/retention, so bounded recurrent KV or linked success is
allowed and is not a construct stop. On integrative targets, success by the
nonadaptive independent-atom reader is `STOP_NONADAPTIVE_CONSTRUCT`; success by
recurrent KV, linked/rolling memory, raw RAG, or MECH is an honest comparator.
If the strongest such condition matches or beats SELF, stop only the
model-owned offline-compilation/reconsolidation advantage; report the finite
causal-memory result and all offline plus online resources.

The descriptive B3 P4 comparator is not `AS-EXT` alone. At each DEV root it is
the root-wise maximum post-native CROSS AUC across `AS-RAG`, frozen `AS-EXT`,
and `AS-MECH`, exactly as defined in the statistics contract. All three
component contrasts are reported without population inference. The
common-history compiler component likewise uses the root-wise strongest
non-SELF representation condition rather than a DEV-selected weak comparator.

## 4. Per-root resource ledger

Every arm, root, side, checkpoint, and target reports:

- ordinary opportunity episodes, actions, public outcomes, and raw tokens;
- introduced, opportunity-supported, actually supported, compiled, readable,
  and action-usable mappings;
- Dream-1/Dream-2 calls, samples, malformed outputs, input/output tokens,
  latency, and cost;
- canonical corpus, views, index, embedding, candidate, cache, adapter,
  optimizer, and manifest bytes;
- reader calls, comparisons, tokens, hidden candidate work, and latency;
- resolver calls, operations, tokens, FLOPs where measurable, and wall time;
- environment actions, diagnostic actions, probe information gain, returns,
  failures, and retries;
- compilation/training calls, examples, repeats, steps, FLOPs, energy where
  available, and wall time; and
- the number of later target decisions over which compilation/training cost is
  amortized.

Equal visible response bytes do not imply equal compute. The paper reports a
resource vector and Pareto plot. It makes no fixed-memory, fixed-compute,
efficiency, or scaling-complexity claim unless separately earned.

## 5. Hard scientific/resource stops

- Fewer than three strict post-native checkpoints or non-growing unique causal
  information: stop lifetime claims.
- Exact program below itemwise value one or no-memory headroom too high: stop
  as an invalid construct.
- Nonadaptive independent witnessed-atom reads solve the integrative target:
  stop as an invalid adaptive/compilation construct. Recurrent KV/linked/raw/
  MECH success is allowed; matching or beating SELF stops the model-owned
  offline-compilation advantage, not the finite causal-map benchmark.
- Text compiler lacks chain-complete binding, same-event incremental value,
  outcome necessity, or causal-cascade effects on DEV: report the failure and
  do not request a confirmation preregistration.
- Stage C is always `DEFERRED_NEW_PREREGISTRATION` in this protocol. Any
  population rejection, 80%-power statement, fixed confirmation roster/cap,
  or C1--C4 scientific claim is forbidden. A future proposal that cannot bind
  untouched roots, a finite-sample-valid or explicitly model-dependent rule,
  strict variance/assumption margin, end-to-end gate-passage power, and a
  complete measured resource envelope stops rather than prunes.
- No text confirmation or LoRA authority exists here: do not run LoRA. A future
  associative-LoRA proposal must be independently reviewed and human-ratified,
  and cannot rescue failed text organs.
