# Compiler, sleep, and memory ownership contract

Status: proposal only; no implementation or execution authority.

## 1. Chosen compiler ownership

The primary compiler is deliberately **model-owned prospective/revision**:

- a frozen prompted resolver proposes bounded local causal claims and public
  predictions before their decisive outcomes;
- later ordinary public outcomes are shown without correctness labels;
- the same resolver owns `RETAIN`, `REVISE`, `ABSTAIN`, or a local addition;
- a deterministic sleeper may validate syntax, provenance, chronology,
  literal normalization, semantic-equivalence indexing, supersession, and
  realization only; and
- no exact solver, hidden truth, target, scorer verdict, or mechanical
  correctness filter selects positive SELF memory.

This choice carries the architecture-facing claim. This architecture proposal
does not contain executable cognition bytes, but the later implementation and
execution proposal must hash-freeze and publish the exact generic
world-family-blind prompt, separately labeled PCFL-class-informed ceiling
prompt, DSL/schema, canonical serializer, parser, model/sampling configuration,
sample cap, operation cap, exact multi-sample aggregation/reducer/admission
rule including unique instance/equivalence and Dream-2-addition semantics,
presealed opportunity-block/event-slot IDs, tokenizer-exact constant-shape
scheduling-envelope slots, the minimal unpadded public-payload schema and
finite reachable packet catalog, the dual envelope/payload clocks, ordinary
Dream-1/Dream-2/snapshot cadence, checkpoint
cutoff/tie rules, checkpoint-specific SELF lifecycle/readability policy,
fixed-shape status-slot serialization, deterministic cursor/exhaustion,
index/reader/query policy, retrieval and returned-byte caps,
candidate construction, retry policy, stopping rule, failure mapping, and
semantic-cut algorithm at Stage A2 **before the first Stage-B model call**.
The same freeze includes exact target/truth/scorer/comparator/DEV-result
blindness assertions and mutation/golden fixtures for those three mandatory
treatment-defining policies.
No DEV output may tune those bytes. A change after that boundary invalidates
the eight-root DEV roster and requires a new precommitted run; it cannot be
called a repair within the sealed evidence.

Stage A2 also freezes the canonical `P1_COMPILER_CRN_V1` seed function,
length-prefix/endianness rules, digest-to-provider conversion, the preassigned
`MAIN_FORK` and `COMMON_HISTORY` pair groups, sample-index schedule, and golden
seed vectors. Every paired P1 compiler draw is common across its paired lanes
and excludes branch/treatment/opaque-label/lane/method/assignment metadata.
The intact SELF delta and same-probe twin-outcome recompile share compiler
draws exactly; semantic cut/sham lanes deterministically transform one intact
delta and cannot resample. Independently seeded descriptive compiler repeats
are barred from P1. After unmount, only the ordinary public branch event may
vary by branch at the delta compiler boundary.

## 2. Supplied event extraction

A deterministic extractor converts an ordinary public action, before-state,
after-state, outcome, and public handles into canonical witnessed transition
atoms. It may normalize public syntax and compute literal before/after trait
differences. It may not infer an unwitnessed preparation mapping, site
predicate, route rule, exception, target path, importance score, or hidden
family. It is applied identically to all applicable arms and is reported as
supplied preprocessing, not DREAM intelligence.

The mechanical compiler control admits only witnessed atoms and relations
licensed by a frozen public separating-panel rule. It may never consult hidden
truth or future targets. It is an alternative/control, not a ceiling unless a
separate proof establishes a true upper-bound property. If it dominates SELF,
the result is a deterministic causal-memory baseline, not evidence for
model-owned compilation.

## 3. Claim lifecycle

All semantic claims and status events are append-only. Identity is always
lineage-local:

```text
ALLOCATED_DREAM1_PROPOSAL_SLOT -> PROPOSED | ABSTAINED | MALFORMED | FAILED
PROPOSED -> PROVISIONAL
PROVISIONAL | SELF_RETAINED | SELF_REVISED
  -> SELF_RETAINED | MODEL_CONTRADICTED | ABSTAINED
  -> SUPERSEDED_BY_MODEL + child SELF_REVISED
ALLOCATED_DREAM2_ADDITION_SLOT
  -> SELF_RETAINED | ABSTAINED | MALFORMED | FAILED
```

Every preallocated sample/proposal, revision, and Dream-2 addition slot has a
unique immutable `record_instance_id`, including a status-only abstained,
malformed, failed, or missing slot and including two slots with byte-identical
semantic payloads. Stage A2 freezes its collision-checked derivation from the
protocol hash and lineage-local allocation coordinates
`(call_id,slot_kind,sample_index,proposal_index,version_index)`; no semantic
payload, target, truth, result, assignment, confidence, or utility byte enters
the ID. A revision receives a new instance ID in the same immutable
`lineage_id` and names exactly one prior active instance as parent. A Dream-2
local addition receives a new instance ID and begins a new lineage at that
Dream-2 commit.

The separate `semantic_equivalence_key` is a byte-literal key over the
canonical semantic payload. It may support literal grouping, equality audit,
and index lookup only. It never merges record instances or lineages, transfers
or votes lifecycle state, adds support, changes capacity, suppresses a
candidate, selects a winner, or chooses a reader return. Schema aliases may be
normalized within one payload before this key is computed; equivalence across
instances never collapses them.

Offline scorer labels such as `CORRECT`, `INCORRECT`, `SUPPORTED_BY_HIDDEN`
or target utility never enter this lifecycle. Direct witnessed atoms carry
`PUBLIC_WITNESS` status. Synthetic views, paraphrases, repetitions, model
samples, shared ancestors, and proposal multiplicity add zero evidentiary
support.

Every object records its record instance and lineage IDs, source public-event
IDs, parent record-instance IDs, semantic-equivalence key, commit
time, target observable, deadline/world version where applicable, compiler
mode, and taint. Literal schema aliases normalize within an instance before
cycle checking. Self,
descendant, realization, target, evaluation, scorer, and assignment-derived
citations fail closed.

### Mandatory Stage-A2 multi-sample reducer

Stage A2 MUST contain one hash-bound executable
`SELF_AGGREGATION_V1` artifact, not an implementation-default parser choice.
For every scheduled Dream-1 and Dream-2 invocation it binds the sample cap,
per-sample proposal cap, source-slot count, canonical field order, literal
normalizations, exact-duplicate key, disagreement-group key, admission order,
overflow behavior, retry use, status transition, and failure receipt. It maps
the complete ordered tuple `(call_id, sample_index, proposal_index,
raw_output_or_failure)` to the canonical SELF snapshot as follows:

1. every allocated source slot defines an independent immutable lineage,
   receives a unique lineage-local `record_instance_id`, and is represented
   exactly once; a syntactically
   and provenance-valid proposal is admitted, while an explicit abstention,
   malformed parse, timeout, exhausted fixed retry, or missing proposal becomes
   an `ABSTAINED`, `MALFORMED`, or `FAILED` status-only slot;
2. only byte-literal schema normalization and computation of the exact
   canonical-payload `semantic_equivalence_key` are permitted. Exact duplicates
   remain separate record instances and separate lineages; the shared
   equivalence key is only a literal group/index key and never a lifecycle,
   voting, support, admission, capacity, or reader-selection key;
3. nonidentical claims that disagree on a canonical subject/relation/time key
   are all admitted as separate `PROVISIONAL` records with the same
   deterministic disagreement-group ID. Incompatible Dream-2 retain/revise/
   abstain decisions and differing revisions likewise remain in their separate
   source-slot lineages; no cross-lineage winner is selected. Disagreement
   cannot invoke voting, majority selection, confidence ranking, support
   weighting, or extra calls;
4. the fixed sample-by-proposal allocation is large enough for every legal DSL
   output under the bound caps. Any excess proposal is a schema violation: the
   scheduled call emits its preallocated `FAILED` slots and admits no
   truncated subset. The reducer may never discard a legal proposal to satisfy
   a semantic, utility, correctness, or convenience ranking; and
5. Dream-2 model outputs alone may retain, revise, abstain from, or declare a
   model-observed contradiction to a named active record instance after
   receiving the scheduled raw public outcome. Each update names exactly one
   lineage-local parent. `RETAIN` changes that instance to `SELF_RETAINED`;
   `REVISE` commits a separately preallocated child instance as `SELF_REVISED`
   and marks only the named parent `SUPERSEDED_BY_MODEL`; contradiction or
   abstention makes only the named lineage instance payload-ineligible. Missing,
   duplicate, cross-lineage, or unavailable parent references fail the
   preallocated decision slot; and
6. every scheduled Dream-2 call has a distinct fixed bank of addition slots.
   A valid local `ADD` fills exactly one such slot, receives its own new
   lineage and instance ID, and enters `SELF_RETAINED` at that Dream-2 commit;
   it is not attached to or voted with an equivalent Dream-1 lineage. An
   unused, abstained, malformed, timed-out, overflowed, or failed addition slot
   receives its frozen status-only receipt. The deterministic reducer validates
   and records these model-owned decisions but never infers whether one is
   true.

No hidden truth, target allocation or bytes, evaluation goal, scorer verdict,
comparator output, downstream action value, DEV metric, prior success rate, or
human correctness label may enter the reducer, admission order, disagreement
handling, retry, or status transition. Raw ordinary public outcomes are allowed
only at the already scheduled Dream-2 input. Thus neither deterministic
correctness selection nor performance-conditioned canonicalization is an
admissible implementation.

## 4. Sleep permissions

Sleep may:

- select from the new ordinary public block, prior SELF memory, and public
  outcomes using the presealed target-blind cadence below;
- invoke bounded Dream-1 and Dream-2 calls;
- canonicalize model-owned output and construct literal semantic-equivalence
  index groups without merging instances or lifecycle;
- append supersession links;
- produce fixed forward, reverse where valid, cloze, contrast, and provenance
  views; and
- seal text snapshots.

Sleep may not:

- see evaluation goals or target allocation;
- use an exact comparator verdict to retain or repair SELF content;
- enumerate the hidden family, choose a correct candidate, or add a missing
  relation;
- silently drop false, abstained, malformed, or failed assignments from
  coverage accounting;
- create a target-complete plan or goal-specific shortcut; or
- change prompts, budgets, retries, or sampling after DEV freeze.

Here `DEV freeze` means the pre-model-call Stage-A2 hash freeze, not a choice
made after observing DEV output. The trusted selector may choose only the
already frozen external baseline and future spending disposition; it cannot
rewrite cognition.

### Mandatory Stage-A2 ordinary cadence

Stage A2 MUST contain one hash-bound executable
`ORDINARY_SLEEP_CADENCE_V1` table covering every ordinary opportunity exactly
once. Before B0 it enumerates, in immutable public-deck order, every
`opportunity_block_id`; every preassigned `event_slot_id` in that block; the
single Dream-1 call ID and block-open trigger; the single Dream-2 call ID and
block-close trigger; the snapshot-seal ID; and its relation to the `0.75L`,
`1.5L`, `3L`, and `6L` tokenizer-exact cutoffs. An event-slot ID exists before
the ordinary actor acts and receives exactly one fixed-shape public packet:
the legal rendered action/outcome, or the registered no-action/failure/
overflow packet. Dream-1 fires exactly at the named block-open boundary before
any included outcome exists. Dream-2 fires exactly after the table's final
event-slot ID is sealed and receives the exact Dream-1 bytes plus all included
fixed-shape event packets in ascending slot order. The snapshot then seals
exactly once even if an action fails, a renderer overflows, or either model
call abstains, is malformed, times out, or exhausts its fixed retries.

The same artifact freezes a tokenizer-exact **scheduling envelope** for every
event slot: immutable header fields, a slot-specific fixed token count,
canonical payload encoding, neutral padding token sequence, and fixed-shape
no-action, failure, and overflow packets. A legal realized action/outcome is
placed inside its assigned envelope and padded to exactly that presealed token
count. If it does not fit, the entire slot becomes the registered fixed-shape
overflow packet and the affected scientific row fails; truncation, spillover,
a larger slot, or a shifted boundary is forbidden. Thus action choice, outcome
text, failure text, and their realized lengths can never change a later
envelope offset, sleep trigger, snapshot, or checkpoint.

The scheduling envelope is not an information-age clock. Stage A2 freezes one
minimal canonical public-payload schema containing only the agent-visible
public state delta, executed-action fields, outcome fields, and a short fixed
status code. `NO_ACTION`, `FAIL`, and `OVERFLOW` have exact short fixed payloads;
they cannot contain explanations, repeated headers, padding, reserve text, or
free-form diagnostics. The bytes placed in `PUBLIC_PAYLOAD_STREAM` are exactly
the public bytes shown to every eligible ordinary actor. Any hidden extra byte,
verbose or repeated boilerplate, semantically null token-age filler, free-form
or unbounded packet, or unequal agent-visible serialization is
`STOP_PAYLOAD_LAUNDERING_INVALID_CONSTRUCT`; it cannot be repaired by lengthening
a status or envelope.

Each slot separately serializes that unpadded canonical public action/outcome
payload; concatenating and retokenizing those payloads in slot order defines
`PUBLIC_PAYLOAD_STREAM`. Neutral pad/header/empty-reserve tokens add no public
evidence, native-context age, OLD age, or unique causal information and never
enter a cognitive prompt. `AS-CTX` is constructed by first physically
concatenating and retokenizing only `PUBLIC_PAYLOAD_STREAM`, then taking its
target-independent final `L_native` tokens. Truncating an envelope stream and
then stripping or attention-masking neutral bytes is forbidden because those
bytes consume positions/KV and could evict evidence. Stage A2 freezes, for
every slot, the exact minimum and maximum payload-token length over the complete
finite set of reachable legal action/outcome/no-action/failure/overflow packets.
If complete enumeration is infeasible, the construct fails closed. Target
allocation is valid only when
the sum of per-slot maxima before the `0.75L` reference cutoff is strictly less
than `L_native` and the sum of per-slot minima before each declared post-native
cutoff is strictly greater than `L_native`. A0 enumerates that legal packet set,
retokenizes every canonical payload, verifies these bounds, and separately
verifies realized payload ages. Thus no action/outcome choice or neutral padding
can manufacture pre-/post-native status.

OLD targets are preassigned against the latest **possible** supporting
opportunity across every reachable history, not the realized last support.
Every later opportunity capable of supporting that target is embargoed, and
the worst-case minimum unpadded payload distance from the latest possible
support opportunity to the target must be strictly greater than `L_native`.
Realized acquisition failure remains a zero-valued failure and never relabels
or drops the OLD target.

Block boundaries and triggers use only the presealed opportunity-block and
event-slot IDs. The table also contains the fixed cumulative **envelope**
tokenizer offset after every slot. It MUST place a block-close snapshot exactly
at each registered envelope-age `q * L_native` cutoff for
`q in {0.75,1.5,3,6}`. `L_native` MUST be divisible by four; otherwise A0 fails
closed before implementation expansion or a scientific model call. A0 independently retokenizes the full constant-shape
scheduling-envelope stream and certifies exact integer equality, otherwise the
construct fails closed. Envelope age schedules evaluation but cannot establish
native-context overflow, OLD age, retention, or information growth; those use
the separately certified unpadded `PUBLIC_PAYLOAD_STREAM`. At a coincident boundary the
total order is: seal the final event slot at the cutoff; run the already
scheduled Dream-2; seal that block's snapshot; bind the checkpoint evaluation
to that snapshot; only then may the next block's Dream-1 fire. A slot whose end
offset equals the cutoff is included; a slot whose start is at the cutoff is
excluded. No cutoff may bisect a slot, and no nearest-boundary or tolerance
rule is allowed.

Every figure, response curve, table, and claim-level lifetime coordinate uses
realized `PAYLOAD_AGE/L_native`, never envelope `q`, as its x-axis. The envelope
label may be reported separately only as a scheduling-audit field. A0 also
reports useful unique causal information per payload token and fails the
construct if null/repeated/status boilerplate rather than new public causal
information drives a post-native label or AS-CTX loss.

Cadence cannot depend on event bytes or meaning, realized action/outcome
length, contradiction detection, model output, support/correctness,
target/truth, scorer/comparator results, DEV metrics, failures, latency, or
resource pressure. There is no contradiction-triggered,
performance-triggered, early, skipped, or extra ordinary Dream/snapshot call.
Each checkpoint's current snapshot is the table-named snapshot at the exact
cutoff; every lagged or first-post-acquisition snapshot ID is enumerated in the
same table. A missed trigger consumes its preassigned slot and applies the
frozen failure mapping without moving any later trigger.

No materialized shortcut is part of the primary paper surface. Local
connections present in model-owned claims may be measured, but cross-era
success is called resolver composition over compiled local causal memory
unless a separately registered endpoint-held edge intervention proves edge
necessity.

## 5. Memory backends

The primary text snapshot contains immutable SELF records, witnessed atoms,
the exact checkpoint status view below, and a counted target-independent index.
Witness atoms and SELF semantic records are distinct typed objects with
separate bytes, provenance, index entries, read receipts, and cut eligibility.
No model-owned record may relabel a direct witness as an abstraction or hide
the witness inside an opaque combined record.

Stage A2 MUST contain one hash-bound executable
`SELF_LIFECYCLE_READER_V1` artifact. For each named checkpoint/snapshot it
enumerates every scheduled SELF proposal, revision, addition, and status-only
record instance committed by that cutoff; its unique `record_instance_id`,
lineage and parent; semantic-equivalence key if any; canonical candidate
position; active-version rule; readable-status predicate; fixed
`SELF_SLOT_BYTES` and `SELF_RETURN_BYTES` envelopes; neutral padding bytes;
literal match keys; cursor transition; and reader return. Historical snapshots
are immutable: a later revision or supersession never rewrites an earlier
snapshot.

At every checkpoint, only an active `SELF_RETAINED` instance or the active
latest `SELF_REVISED` instance within one lineage as of that snapshot is
payload-readable. A valid Dream-2 addition is an active `SELF_RETAINED`
instance in its own lineage beginning at its commit snapshot. Exact-duplicate
and disagreeing lineages may therefore coexist independently. Their
reader-visible status is the single normalized token `READABLE_SELF`.
`PROPOSED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `MODEL_CONTRADICTED`, `ABSTAINED`,
`MALFORMED`, `FAILED`, and `SUPERSEDED_BY_MODEL` are payload-ineligible.
Every such committed slot remains at the same candidate position and occupies
the same `SELF_SLOT_BYTES` envelope as its readable counterfactual; it returns
the one normalized padded `SELF_SLOT_UNAVAILABLE` envelope without exposing
which ineligible status caused it. A superseded slot becomes unavailable only
in snapshots at or after its model-authored supersession time; its active
revision uses its separately preallocated revision slot. Future unscheduled
slots do not exist yet, but no committed slot may be merged by semantic
equivalence, omitted, compacted, reordered, length-changed, or removed from
candidate accounting because of its status. Witness records remain a separate
typed surface.

Within a matched SELF intervention pair—intact versus semantic cut/sham,
lifecycle mutation, or another explicitly registered SELF counterfactual—the
checkpoint catalog, candidate positions, native serializer, cursor, return
shape, index work, candidate work, and charged read slots are identical except
for the intended payload/tombstone bytes. `WITNESS-KV-NA` and
`WITNESS-KV-REC` likewise use one byte-identical witness catalog and native
return policy. This identity requirement does **not** cross native
representation baselines. RAW/RAG, WITNESS-KV, MECH, linked/rolling AS-EXT,
and SELF share the declared query grammar, resolver/read/returned-byte/action
budgets, source public history, and scorer, but retain their frozen
backend-native records, catalogs, indexes, serializers, and reader interfaces.
Those native surfaces and all construction, enumeration, storage, compute,
latency, and returned bytes are fully reported. No cross-representation
byte-identical-catalog claim is permitted. Target success remains
world/action-defined; this policy creates no requirement that any SELF record
be readable or exist.

SELF catalog order is frozen before outputs by ascending allocation tuple
`(call_ordinal,slot_kind_ordinal,sample_index,proposal_index,version_index)`;
neither semantic payload nor status participates. For each normalized literal
query key, an evaluation-local cursor begins before the first position and
advances monotonically to the next catalog instance matching the frozen
literal index predicate. Each call returns exactly that one instance's padded
`READABLE_SELF` or `SELF_SLOT_UNAVAILABLE` envelope and advances past it. If no
matching instance remains, it returns the padded `NOT_FOUND_EXHAUSTED`
envelope; repeated exhausted calls return the same envelope and consume their
preassigned read slots. Conflicting and semantically equivalent readable
instances are therefore all exposed, one per call, in the same pre-output
order. Confidence, recency, support count, multiplicity, lifecycle reason,
utility, correctness, target, result, or adaptive ranking may not select,
skip, merge, or reorder an instance. Cursors and query bytes are sterile
evaluation-local state and are destroyed at target teardown.

One reader call returns exactly one fixed-shape backend-native record or status
envelope, including `NOT_FOUND_EXHAUSTED`; it never returns a target-complete
plan, correct action, hidden
status, backend name, rank, score, latency, or uncharged candidate alternative.
Target correctness is wholly world/action-defined and never tests for a
`SELF_SEMANTIC` record. Every target-time condition uses the same frozen
resolver and exact online cap: six reader calls, 4,096 aggregate UTF-8 query
bytes, 16,384 aggregate UTF-8 returned bytes, twelve resolver operations, and
the target's presealed four-to-six action cap. Raw event, witness atom,
mechanical relation, linked record, rolling-summary chunk, or SELF record may
all support a legal successful action within that envelope. Offline compiler
calls/tokens/latency/cost, storage, indexing, enumeration, embeddings, and all
online work are separately and jointly reported.

The registered integrative targets depend in the world on the branch outcome,
at least two other provenance-distinct public events, and four life-specific
bindings. The canonical raw witnessed-atom controller needs a read-dependent
query/path DAG; its nonadaptive independent-read version must fail, while its
recurrent version and faithful linked/raw/mechanical comparators are permitted
to succeed. Their success is the comparison SELF must beat, not a construct
contradiction.

The common-history reader sentinel installs one byte-identical known-good
witness-atom store behind two policies. `WITNESS-KV-NA` commits the complete
ordered query list, read count, and stopping rule before receiving its first
return. Its terminal action may use the returned fixed batch, but no query
token or read/stop choice may depend on a return. `WITNESS-KV-REC` uses the
identical atoms, index, candidate catalog,
maximum calls, returned-byte cap, and resolver cap, but query `q_i` for
`i>1` may depend on prior returns. The manifest stores the precommit hash,
ordered read receipts, and the certified read-dependent query/path DAG. An
integrative target solved by `WITNESS-KV-NA` fires the
nonadaptive/mere-recall construct stop.

At the `3L` same-event sentinel, the compiler emits separately sealed
`RAW-AO`, `ACTION-ONLY`, `WITNESS/MECH`, and `SELF-DELTA` lanes as specified in
`mediation_contract.md`. The executable SELF semantic cut is frozen at Stage
A2 before any SELF output. For branch event ID `e`, `CUT_ELIGIBLE(e)` is
exactly every canonical `SELF_SEMANTIC` record emitted or revised by that
branch's Dream-2 whose validated provenance set contains `e` and at least one
earlier, distinct ordinary public-event ID. `SELF-SEM-CUT` removes all such
records, subject only to the already frozen DSL output cap; zero eligible
records makes the assigned cut pair a zero-valued failure for P1 rather than
triggering a new rule. This can withhold the offline-compilation claim, but it
does not alter the representation-neutral target construct or erase ordinary
world/action success.

The matched sham removes the same record count from SELF semantic records that
do not cite `e`. Order cut records by ascending
`SHA256(protocol_hash || record_instance_id || "SEM_CUT_ORDER")`; for each in that
order, choose the unused sham candidate minimizing the lexicographic tuple
`(absolute serialized-length difference, relation-type mismatch indicator,
absolute provenance-count difference,
SHA256(protocol_hash || candidate_record_instance_id || "SEM_SHAM"))`. Insufficient
matches are a zero-valued failure. Constant-shape tombstones and neutral
padding preserve each removed slot's serialized byte envelope. Both lanes hold
witness objects and their actual returns, reader/index/candidate shape, call
slots and maxima, operation caps, evaluation seeds, and charged resource slots
fixed. They do **not** hold the removed SELF semantic bytes or return-dependent
query bytes fixed: a query resolving a cut slot receives
`SEMANTIC_WITHHELD`, and later sterile queries/actions may consequently differ.
The canonical eligibility predicate, serialization, tie rule, cap, tombstone,
and failure behavior are part of the Stage-A2 hash.

Outcome-drop and actually executed same-probe twin-outcome lanes hold probe
identity fixed and replace the complete public outcome packet—after-state,
observation, cost, and terminal flags—while every other Dream-2 input byte and
input order is common. No future target, comparator output, scorer result, or
hidden correctness value selects records for any lane or cut.

## 6. Failure-inclusive attribution

- Exact typed ceiling failure: invalid world or interface.
- Mechanical compiler fails despite sufficient public evidence: extractor or
  support-rule defect.
- Mechanical succeeds and SELF adds no representation-neutral executed-action
  value: model-owned compiler failure.
- Recurrent witnessed KV, raw RAG, MECH, linked memory, or rolling summary
  matches or exceeds generic SELF on byte-identical histories under the common
  online cap: no model-owned offline-compilation or reconsolidation advantage;
  report the winning finite causal-memory method and total resources.
- SELF fails to exceed raw, action-only, or witness/mechanical same-event
  deltas, or survives decisive SELF-semantic removal: incremental model-owned
  reconsolidation is not established; at most public-event memory is shown.
- Same-probe outcome-drop/twin-outcome does not move the SELF delta and later
  action in the certified direction: the outcome-bearing evidence link is not
  established.
- Known-good text reads fail: reader/index failure.
- Reads succeed and action fails: resolver/planning failure.
- Common-memory plus acquired delta fails after informative evidence:
  reconsolidation/use failure.
- Text succeeds and LoRA fails: parametric transport failure; no LoRA claim.
- Candidate-only or wrong adapter matches LoRA: reader/index owns the result.
- Direct-QA LoRA matches compiled LoRA: explicit compiler adds no parametric
  value.
- New acquisition succeeds while old value degrades: retention failure.
- Twins/cuts do not redirect behavior: no authentic life-binding credit or an
  invalid/leaky construct.
