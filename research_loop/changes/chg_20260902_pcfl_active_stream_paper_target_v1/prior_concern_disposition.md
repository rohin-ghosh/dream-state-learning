## 3. Finite causal-map and nonadaptive-read falsifier

Ordinary NEW/OLD/CROSS D4 is prospectively called acquisition, retention, and
use of a finite per-life causal map. Bounded recurrent witnessed KV, linked
memory, raw RAG, rolling summary, MECH, or SELF may all solve it under the same
six-read/4,096-query-byte/16,384-return-byte/twelve-operation/action envelope.
Their success is not an invalid construct and does not support higher-order
schema, compression, or unbounded-reasoning language.

The integrative construct fails only if the canonical witnessed-atom reader
solves with one event, independent lookups, or queries/read count/stopping
committed before the first return. A recurrent atomic route is allowed and is
an honest strongest comparator. If recurrent KV, linked/rolling, raw, or MECH
matches or beats generic SELF on byte-identical history, report the winning
finite causal-memory method and withhold model-owned offline-compilation or
reconsolidation advantage. Twinned binding/outcome and semantic cuts still
test the narrower causal cascade. The exact public program is a ceiling and is
never weakened to manufacture a system win.
