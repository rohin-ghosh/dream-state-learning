# PCFL-Active-Stream paper-target experiment contract

Status: proposal only. This file binds a scientific design for deliberation. It
does not authorize implementation, CPU work, scientific model/provider/network
calls, training, GPU work, ratification, confirmation, or a claim. The only
model/provider calls permitted by this proposal are the configured
non-scientific architecture-deliberation roles in the bound workflow, ending
at `human_required`; they create no scientific or execution authority.

## 1. Scientific hypothesis

The primary hypothesis is:

> Under fixed action, query, and resolver budgets, a frozen controller with
> target-blind model-owned prospective/revision text memory will use its
> growing public action--outcome life to acquire more decision-relevant causal
> evidence, retain early causal knowledge, and compose old and recent
> knowledge into successful unseen D4 actions after raw history exceeds the
> exact native context envelope. At registered diagnostic forks, authentic
> versus twin or null memory will change the probe action; after that assigned
> memory is removed, the different public evidence and its target-blind
> reconsolidated delta will yield a chain-complete causal-cascade contrast in
> later sterile action value. On byte-identical histories and the same fixed
> online resolver/read/query-byte/return-byte/action budget, generic SELF will
> add value beyond recurrent witnessed KV, linked/rolling memory, raw RAG, and
> MECH after all offline and online resources are counted.

This is a finite-range, controlled, synthetic, action-conditioned continual
learning and offline-compilation claim over finite causal-map content. Ordinary
D4 measures acquisition, retention, and use of that finite map; it is not
higher-order schema learning or an escape from bounded recurrent KV. This is
not a claim about generic SOTA systems, naturalistic or human development,
autonomous world discovery, semantic compression, unbounded lifetime, formal
causal mediation, or a learned controller.

## 2. PCFL-Active-Stream world

Each world-life contains ordered causal cohorts. Cohort `c` introduces thirteen
fresh persistent public family labels:

```text
P[c,0..5]  six preparation families, permuted over six stable trait transforms
S[c,0..3]  four site families, permuted over four stable acceptance predicates
R[c,0..2]  three route families, permuted over three stable preconditions
```

The base latent family is the PCFL field-lab family in notes 46--48: four
binary functional traits, six stable preparation transforms, four bounded
site predicates, three route requirements, and sparse presealed exception
opportunities. Each cohort contributes thirteen genuinely new mappings. Labels
persist for the rest of the life; entity, specimen, site, route, and mission
handles are fresh per episode. A counterfactual twin preserves pre-action
public bytes, skins, opportunity schedule, action menus, counts, and target
bytes while applying a fixed within-cohort involution to decisive mappings.

Natural-language descriptors may make actions readable but are sampled so
they do not identify the within-life mapping. Both meaningful-skin and
descriptor-neutral shortcut probes are certified offline. No model-visible
field names the hidden mapping, twin side, optimal action, target proof, or
future target allocation.

## 3. Ordinary on-policy life

The generator pre-seals an **opportunity deck** before latent outcomes are
realized. Every method receives the same sequence of public episode starts,
goals, legal action menus, costs, and caps on the same world root. The deck is
not a fixed source trajectory: each method selects its own legal actions and
therefore obtains its own public observations and outcomes.

Each ordinary episode contains:

1. a fresh mission requiring a preparation/site/route decision;
2. one or two diagnostic-action opportunities chosen from at least four
   plausible `SURVEY`, `ASSAY`, `PREPARE`, or `INTERVENE` probes;
3. a four-to-six-action execution cap after the diagnostic allowance;
4. a certified target dependence on at least four life-specific causal
   bindings, with no source episode containing the exact state--goal solution;
5. insufficient budget to reacquire every decisive relation; and
6. an ordinary public success, failure, state transition, and action cost.

Episode reset fixes the next public opportunity start, so a failed action does
not make later opportunities incomparable. Only memory and the public history
obtained by the arm persist. The fixed resolver may retrieve, update a bounded
workspace, act, observe, backtrack, defer, and stop. Its prompted operation
policy; multi-sample SELF reducer/admission with unique record instances,
literal-equivalence-only groups, and exact Dream-2 additions; presealed
opportunity-block/event-slot cadence and tokenizer-exact constant-shape
renderer; exact Dream-1/Dream-2/snapshot cutoff/tie order; and checkpoint
lifecycle/readability/deterministic-cursor/exhaustion/constant-shape slot
policy are hash-frozen at Stage A2 before DEV and never trained or rewritten
within any B0--B3 life.

The on-policy life is the paper treatment. A byte-identical scripted common
deck may exist only as a diagnostic representation/use sentinel and may not be
substituted for the action-conditioned headline.

## 4. Constructive targets

All target allocations are total and presealed before source outcomes,
support, compiler output, or method results. The `0.75 L_native` checkpoint is
a **pre-native NEW/reference checkpoint**. It contains six
`PRE_NATIVE_NEW_REFERENCE_D4` targets per twin side: each target uses four
decisive mappings introduced in the prefix, and none is labeled old,
retention, or cross-era. No greater-than-`L_native` age condition applies at
this checkpoint.

Each of the three post-native checkpoints contains six targets per twin side
in each of three D4 cohorts:

- `NEW_D4`: four decisive mappings introduced since the previous checkpoint;
- `OLD_D4`: four decisive mappings from the earliest eligible quartile. Before
  outcomes, the target allocator finds the latest possible support opportunity
  over every reachable history, embargoes every later supporting opportunity,
  and requires the worst-case minimum unpadded `PUBLIC_PAYLOAD_STREAM` distance
  from that opportunity to target to exceed `L_native`; realized acquisition
  failure remains zero and never relabels the row; and
- `CROSS_D4`: at least two decisive `OLD_D4` mappings and two decisive recent
  mappings in one four-to-six-action mission.

If a post-native checkpoint has no eligible old mappings, its preassigned
`OLD_D4` and `CROSS_D4` rows score `NOT_ELIGIBLE_INVALID_CONSTRUCT`; the age
rule is never relaxed. Thus the fixed D4 allocation per twin side is six
pre-native reference targets plus `3 checkpoints x 18` post-native targets.

Every diagnostic fork additionally preassigns an `INTEGRATIVE_MED_D4` target
set. Target success is defined only by the latent world, legal executed action
trajectory, action cap, and terminal return; no record type, SELF object,
reader receipt, memory backend, or compiler output is part of correctness.
Each target's world-optimal trajectory depends on the branch outcome plus at
least two other provenance-distinct public events and four life-specific
bindings. Against the canonical raw witnessed-atom store, an exact controller
certificate must reject every one-event, four-independent-lookup,
nonadaptive-precommitted-query, and lower-depth route. The certified atomic
route has an explicit read-dependent query/path DAG: at least one later query
or legal action parameter is a deterministic function of a prior reader return
rather than of target bytes alone. A recurrent KV, linked, rolling, raw, MECH,
or SELF backend may solve under the common online budget; those successes are
honest comparator outcomes, not construct violations. These are nested
targets, not independent units.

Every D4 target has fresh handles, an unseen state--goal combination, a unique
successful trajectory under the registered cap, graph-theoretic minimum depth
four over life-specific bindings, exhaustive rejection of lower-depth alternate
proofs, target-only/twin-byte shortcut certificates, and an exact typed public
sufficient-statistic controller with itemwise value one.

All target-time memory conditions use the identical frozen resolver, query
grammar, and exact online envelope: at most six reader calls, at most 4,096
UTF-8 query bytes in aggregate, at most 16,384 UTF-8 returned bytes in
aggregate, at most twelve resolver operations, and the target's presealed
four-to-six environment-action cap. A reader call returns one fixed-shape
backend-native record or status envelope, including exhausted `NOT_FOUND`; it
may not return a target-complete plan or correct action. Catalog/return bytes
are identical within registered matched interventions and between
WITNESS-KV-NA/REC, not across representation baselines. Native records,
catalogs, interfaces, offline compilation, storage, indexing, enumeration, and
model calls may differ but are frozen and fully reported together with online
work. No backend is starved to force a SELF win.

At `6 L_native`, four presealed `A4_REVISION` targets per twin side introduce one
previously witnessed exception or versioned local change. The ordinary life
must expose a contradiction only through an executed action. For every arm,
primary A4 target value `V` is one only when the arm executes the registered
legal world/action trajectory within its cap and earns the terminal world
return; otherwise `V=0`. No contradiction label, SELF object, revision status,
memory record, reader receipt, or unrelated-retention result enters target
correctness. Contradiction detection, any model-owned retain/revise/abstain
status, later analogous recovery, and unrelated old-mapping retention are
recorded separately as descriptive mechanism diagnostics. A4 remains a
secondary sentinel inside each 908-row B3 roster, cannot gate or establish deferred C1--C3 or
substitute for D4, and requires a new separately powered and authorized
proposal before any A4-specific efficacy claim.

## 5. Lifetime axis

`L_native` is the pinned resolver's tokenizer-exact maximum useful public
payload history after subtracting the frozen system/task prompt, goal and
state, workspace, operation history, reader-return reserve, and output
reserve. The exact renderer and tokenizer determine it; a model-card number
does not. Two clocks are reported. `ENVELOPE_AGE` is the concatenation of
presealed tokenizer-exact constant-shape scheduling envelopes; it alone
selects Dream/snapshot/checkpoint cadence. `PAYLOAD_AGE` is the concatenation
and tokenizer-exact retokenization of realized unpadded canonical public
action/outcome payloads; it alone determines native-context eligibility,
post-native status, OLD age, and AS-CTX useful history. The minimal canonical
payload contains only the agent-visible public state delta, executed action,
outcome, and an exact short status. Agent-visible payload bytes equal
`PUBLIC_PAYLOAD_STREAM` bytes. Neutral headers, padding, unused reserve,
explanations, and diagnostics never enter any cognitive prompt. AS-CTX
physically concatenates and retokenizes the unpadded payload stream first and
then takes its target-independent final `L_native` tokens; envelope-first
truncation followed by stripping or masking is forbidden.

Before outcomes, A0 completely enumerates the finite set of reachable legal
packets per slot and certifies exact tokenizer minima/maxima. Free-form or
unbounded packets fail closed. The maximum possible payload age at the `0.75L`
reference boundary must be `<L_native`; the minimum possible payload age at
each `1.5L`, `3L`, and `6L` post-native boundary must be `>L_native`.
Each OLD target is preassigned using the latest possible support opportunity
over every reachable history, every later supporting opportunity is embargoed,
and the worst-case minimum unpadded distance from that opportunity to target
must be `>L_native`. Realized acquisition failure remains zero and never
relabels or drops the target. Realized payload ages are retokenized and must
satisfy the same strict labels. A0 also
certifies that block-close snapshots land at exact integer
`ENVELOPE_AGE=q*L_native`; `L_native` must be divisible by four, and no envelope cutoff may bisect a slot or use a
tolerance. Exact envelope equality is a cadence certificate only, never
evidence of context overflow.

The nested checkpoint names refer to the presealed `ENVELOPE_AGE` schedule;
their pre-/post-native labels additionally require the separate payload-age
certificates above:

```text
0.75 L_native   pre-native reference
1.5  L_native   post-native 1
3.0  L_native   post-native 2
6.0  L_native   post-native 3
```

Each interval must introduce new cohorts and unique hidden mappings. Repeated
events, paraphrases, replay views, synthetic training examples, distractors,
verbose/repeated status boilerplate, or neutral slot padding add no source
information, mapping credit, or payload-age legitimacy. No-action/failure/
overflow use exact short fixed statuses. A filler/information-density audit
fails closed if semantically null or repeated bytes create a post-native label
or AS-CTX eviction; it cannot be repaired by lengthening boilerplate.
Report exact source tokens, envelope age, realized payload age, payload
minima/maxima, padding tokens, actions,
introduced mappings, support opportunities, supported mappings, compiled
mappings, readable mappings, and action-usable mappings. If complete cohorts
cannot produce all three post-native points at the exact presealed slot-aligned
cutoffs under the resource envelope, no growth, crossover, saturation, or
continual-learning claim is eligible.

Every figure, response curve, table, and claim-facing lifetime coordinate uses
realized `PAYLOAD_AGE/L_native` on its x-axis. Envelope `q` may appear only as
a separately labeled scheduling-audit coordinate.

At each checkpoint, evaluation processes receive a sealed read-only memory
snapshot and fresh target process. Evaluation outcomes, failures, thoughts,
and scorer records are sterile and cannot alter the life, compiler, memory,
candidate store, cache, later opportunity, or later checkpoint.
The snapshot ID, last included ordinary block/event slot and its exact
cumulative tokenizer offset, SELF record instances committed by the cutoff,
readable-status predicate, active-version rule, deterministic cursor/exhaustion
rule, and constant-shape unavailable returns are exactly the Stage-A2
`ORDINARY_SLEEP_CADENCE_V1` and `SELF_LIFECYCLE_READER_V1` bytes. They
cannot depend on target allocation, hidden truth, scorer/comparator or
downstream results, DEV output, or a later checkpoint.

## 6. Registered arms and full-surface core

All full-surface arms receive the same world root, opportunity deck, episode
starts, action/query/resolver caps, target manifest, and scorer, but produce
their own on-policy action--outcome histories:

| ID | Condition | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `AS-NONE` | no persistent state beyond within-episode workspace | frozen-agent floor |
| `AS-CTX` | complete native raw context while it fits, then a frozen target-independent recent suffix | honest context/truncation |
| `AS-RAG` | raw episodic iterative RAG with Stage-A2-frozen native retrieval | raw external memory |
| `AS-EXT` | one strongest native linked-memory or rolling-summary implementation selected and frozen on DEV | strongest learned external baseline |
| `AS-PROG` | exact public sufficient-statistic program and typed controller | certified construct ceiling, never a learned baseline to beat or a model-run arm |
| `AS-MECH` | deterministic public-event extractor plus mechanical supported-atom compiler | alternative/control; ceiling only if separately proved |
| `AS-SELF-TEXT` | deterministic event extraction plus model-owned prospective/revision compiler under the exact generic world-family-blind prompt and target-blind text sleep | primary system |

`AS-EXT` is selected using only the eight DEV roots from a predeclared contest
between a faithful native linked/A-MEM-style memory and one rolling
summary/reflection implementation. The selection metric, resource frontier,
tie rule, candidate implementations, and failure handling freeze at Stage A2
before any DEV output. The losing candidate is reported on DEV. This protocol
authorizes no later confirmation run.

The mechanical event extractor maps public action/state/outcome bytes to
canonical witnessed transition atoms. It is supplied preprocessing in every
applicable arm and is never credited to DREAM. `AS-MECH` deterministically
admits public witnessed atoms and registered separating contrasts; it does not
use hidden truth, future targets, or scorer output.

No schema/compression arm, second backbone, broad benchmark suite, learned
LOOP adapter, recurrent-versus-batch cadence grid, or rank sweep enters the
full lifetime surface.

The eight-root B3 descriptive surface executes six full-surface arms: `AS-NONE`,
`AS-CTX`, `AS-RAG`, frozen `AS-EXT`, `AS-MECH`, and `AS-SELF-TEXT`.
`AS-PROG` is computed and certified from the sealed world/target manifests as
the itemwise-value-one construct ceiling; it does not consume model calls or
replace the mechanical public-evidence control.

`AS-MECH` is an alternative/control unless its exact public-evidence rule is
separately proved to upper-bound every admissible target-blind compiler. It is
not called a ceiling merely because it is deterministic.

At the `3 L_native` representation sentinel, byte-identical public
action--outcome history is supplied to eight separately sealed conditions:
native raw iterative RAG, typed witness KV with nonadaptive precommitted reads,
the same typed witness KV with recurrent reads, `AS-MECH`, the frozen linked
memory candidate, the frozen rolling-summary candidate, generic
world-family-blind `AS-SELF-TEXT`, and a separately labeled
PCFL-class-informed SELF scaffold ceiling. Each condition receives the same
one ordinary and one `INTEGRATIVE_MED_D4` target per twin side, candidate
surface, reader-call/returned-byte/action caps, and common evaluation seeds;
native preprocessing and all work remain charged. The nonadaptive KV actor
must commit its complete ordered query list, read count, and stopping rule
before its first return. It may form its terminal action from the resulting
fixed batch but may not issue another query. The recurrent KV actor uses the
identical known-good atoms and maximum read count, but later queries may depend
on earlier returns. No on-policy arm difference is used to claim compiler
quality. Generic SELF must add executed integrative value beyond the strongest
non-SELF common-history condition under the exact common online envelope;
class-informed SELF is a scaffold ceiling, never the primary system. Recurrent
KV or linked/rolling success is allowed; if the strongest such condition
matches or beats generic SELF, no model-owned offline-compilation or
reconsolidation advantage is available.

At the `3 L_native` causal-cascade sentinel, the same sealed branch event and common
authentic prior feed a bounded delta factorial: `RAW-AO`, `ACTION-ONLY`,
`WITNESS/MECH`, and `SELF-DELTA`. The intact SELF condition is also paired with
outcome-drop and twin-outcome interventions that hold the probe identity
fixed, plus decisive-SELF-semantic and equal-byte sham cuts that hold witness
atoms, reader interface, candidate catalog, operation/query caps, and charged
resource envelope fixed. This factorial is part of the B3 descriptive roster
but does not create new roots or multiply the six ordinary lifetime arms.

## 7. Deferred associative-LoRA successor target

Stage D is not requested by or applicable to deferred text C1--C3. Under
`claim_test_applicability.json`, `NOT_REQUESTED_STAGE_D` is a passing
not-applicable disposition and requires no LoRA artifact. This protocol
terminates at B3 and grants no adapter, training, model-call, or claim authority.

If and only if the frozen B1/B2/B3 text gates are sealed, a **new**, independently
reviewed and human-ratified `DEFERRED_ASSOCIATIVE_LORA_PREREGISTRATION` may be
proposed. Favorable B3 efficacy is not a powered prerequisite; the prerequisite
is that the text organs and causal plumbing were validly exercised. Failed text
organs cannot be rescued or reinterpreted by LoRA.

The conceptual successor target is associative learned memory, not the old
transport sentinel: compare connected DREAM traces with matched isolated atoms,
shuffled connections, and direct-QA data; require partial- and reverse-cue
THINK without an external answer catalog; then test action, a second public
SLEEP/rebuild, and later action. The former matched text/LoRA reader and
authentic/twin/sham transport sentinel may remain only as a component control
inside that future proposal, not its headline. This paragraph freezes no model,
dataset, statistic, roster, resource, or execution contract for the successor.

## 8. Current claim ceiling

No positive C1--C5 scientific claim is available under this protocol. B3 may
report only the eight failure-inclusive descriptive root vectors, named
mechanism diagnostics, complete 908-row/root ledger, and construct/falsification
dispositions. Even uniformly favorable values terminate at
`DEFERRED_NEW_PREREGISTRATION`; they are not population rejection, powered
evidence, or confirmation.

A future text claim would require a new post-B3 human-ratified design using
untouched roots, measured B distributions/costs, finite-sample-valid analysis
or explicitly model-dependent assumptions with a strict margin, and
end-to-end gate-passage power. A future associative-LoRA claim separately
requires its own ratified proposal. Neither could establish generic SOTA,
naturalistic external validity, compression, autonomous discovery, human-like
or unbounded development, a learned compiler/controller, formal mediation, or
a universal self-improving agent.
