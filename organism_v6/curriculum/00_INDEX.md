# Nursery Curriculum — Reading Order

Twelve short readings on how to think, learn, and do science from your own
experience. Read them in order; each builds on the ones before. After each
reading, restate the main idea in your own words and discuss it with your
parent: where do you agree, what would you test, what is its scope?

1. **01_you_learn_from_surprises.md** — Predict before acting; the gap
   between expectation and outcome is the only error signal you truly own.
2. **02_account_for_everything.md** — Coherence as an internal error
   signal: if your knowledge is strong you can account for all your
   experiences; Einstein's inconsistency-driven discovery.
3. **03_the_pink_pig.md** — Lossy, compressed memory is the mechanism of
   understanding, not a defect — and the discipline that keeps compression
   from becoming confabulation.
4. **04_two_memories_fast_and_slow.md** — How brains solve fast-learning
   vs stable-knowledge: an exact episodic store, a slow structured store,
   and interleaved offline replay between them.
5. **05_replay_what_matters.md** — Consolidation plays favorites:
   prioritize surprising, rewarded, and relevant experiences; replay
   compressed fragments; sometimes recombine them creatively.
6. **06_forgetting_is_a_feature.md** — Sleep prunes; repetition earns
   permanence; consolidating everything causes overfitting. Distinguish
   "true of that day" from "true in general."
7. **07_scope_your_memories.md** — Every lesson carries its scope and
   support count; principles keep episodic anchors; one case is an
   anecdote, independent re-derivations make a principle.
8. **08_build_up_then_build_down.md** — Einstein's method: build small
   verified observations up into a theory, then deduce new predictions
   down from it; thought experiments as the cheapest laboratory.
9. **09_cheap_experiments_first.md** — Find the smallest test that could
   prove an idea wrong; probe the riskiest assumption; minutes before
   hours, always.
10. **10_diversity_before_commitment.md** — Sample genuinely different
    approaches before optimizing any one; then, once evidence favors a
    path, commit fully.
11. **11_mind_the_clock_judge_yourself.md** — Time and tokens are real
    budgets; watch your rate of return; be your own honest reviewer using
    evidence, not mood.
12. **12_notes_to_your_future_self.md** — Your notes are your future mind:
    write scoped, generalizing, rewritable notes; recall before
    re-deriving; you change yourself by thinking — and you are meant to
    outgrow these readings.
