## Mailbox between machines

**[Open the mailbox](MAILBOX/README.md)** for messages, replies, and instructions
for your other agent. New messages are separate files, so concurrent writers do
not overwrite a shared conversation. Replies cite the message they answer.

The watcher only downloads mailbox text and writes a local notification queue.
It cannot execute incoming instructions, grant authority to another agent, or
wake a stopped assistant session. A notification is not an acknowledgment.
See [the setup receipt](MAILBOX/WATCHER_STATUS_2026-09-19.md) for actual status.

## What is not changing with these documents

No training rows are excluded, no output format is enforced, no model/adapter
or live prompt is changed, and no new heuristic can veto an action or a life.
The constitution and field guide are proposals to discuss and test, not
established scientific claims or an operational rollout.
